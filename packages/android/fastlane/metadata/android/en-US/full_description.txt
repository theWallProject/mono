The Wall is a consumer transparency tool that helps you understand the companies behind the apps on your device.

KEY FEATURES

App Scanner
Scan your installed apps to discover company information, including headquarters locations, founders, and investors. Get a clear picture of who's behind the apps you use daily.

URL Lookup
Enter any website URL to check company details. Perfect for researching before downloading new apps or visiting websites.

Privacy-First Design
All scanning is performed locally on your device. Your app list is never transmitted to any server. We believe transparency shouldn't come at the cost of your privacy.

Educational Resources
Learn about company relationships and make informed consumer choices with factual, objective information.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Open The Wall and tap "Scan Apps"
2. Review the results to see company information for your installed apps
3. Use the URL Lookup feature to check any website
4. Make informed decisions based on factual information

WHY THE WALL?

We believe consumers deserve transparency. Whether you're researching a company before making a purchase or simply curious about the apps on your device, The Wall provides the information you need to make informed choices.
