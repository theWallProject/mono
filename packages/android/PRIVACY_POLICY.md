# Privacy Policy

**The Wall Project**
Last updated: February 23, 2026

This policy covers The Wall browser extension and The Wall Android app.

---

## Browser Extension

To provide the best service, we ONLY track a very limited number of events.

**WE WILL NEVER COLLECT USER IDENTIFYING DATA NOR NAVIGATION HISTORY!**

Those are the events we track:

- When the Wall appears (to ensure the addon works as expected, and to count the number for encouraging others in the future)
- When the Wall's share, continue, or report buttons are clicked (to study the performance of design changes etc.)

---

## Android App

The Android app collects **NO data whatsoever**. Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero events.

### What the App Accesses Locally

- **Your list of installed apps** — compared against an embedded database entirely on your device. Your app list never leaves your phone.
- **Local preferences** — language, notification settings, and scan timing stored on your device only via Android SharedPreferences:
  - Language preference
  - Notification settings (ignored apps, snoozed apps, scan timing)
  - Onboarding completion status
  - Share prompt cooldowns

### What the App Does NOT Collect

- No personal information (name, email, phone number)
- No device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID)
- No location data
- No usage analytics or telemetry
- No crash reports
- No browsing history
- No data is transmitted to any server

### Network Communication

The App does not have the INTERNET permission and makes **zero network calls** from its own code. The only network activity occurs through the Google Play Billing Library when processing optional subscription purchases, which is handled entirely by Google Play infrastructure under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional $1/month supporter subscription through Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information.

### Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

### Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences mechanism. SharedPreferences are excluded from cloud backup (configured via Android backup rules). Data may be transferred during device-to-device migration.

### Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. Since the App collects no user data at all, no special provisions are necessary.

## Shared Principles

- Both products use an offline database that ships with the install. No server lookups required.
- Both products are open source. Don't trust us — verify it yourself on GitHub.
- We will never sell, share, or monetize user data.
