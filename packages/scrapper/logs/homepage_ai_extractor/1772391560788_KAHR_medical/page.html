DSP107: First-in-class T-cell Engager

A novel bispecific fusion protein that converts the tumor's immune evasion mechanism into a T-cell activation signal

The Challenge

Chemotherapy induces overexpressed CD47 on tumors and liver metastases, delivering a "don't eat me" signal that inhibits innate immunity. DSP107 uniquely converts this immune evasion signal into a powerful T-cell activation mechanism

CD47 Anchor

DSP107 utilizes CD47 overexpression as a docking site for binding

CD47 used by cancer cells to hide from the immune system is used by DSP107 to anchor to the target cancer cells

4-1BB Activation

Enforces cancer cells to cross-present 4-1BB ligands

When bound, DSP107 engages and activates cytotoxic T cells through 4-1BB costimulatory signaling

Innate Immunity

Activates innate immunity by blocking CD47

Innate immune cells present cancer antigens, amplifying adaptive immune response

T-cell Engagement

4-1BB is a costimulatory receptor that attracts and activates T-cells

Leads to T-cell activation, proliferation, and tumor environment modulation

Dual Mechanism

  • Blocks CD47: Activates innate immunity by removing the "don't eat me" signal
  • Activates 4-1BB: Engages and amplifies adaptive T-cell response
  • Tumor-Specific: Activity limited to CD47-overexpressing cancer cells

4-1BB Benefits

  • T-cell Activation: Powerful costimulatory signal for T cells
  • T-cell Proliferation: Amplifies anti-tumor T-cell populations
  • Tumor Modulation: Reshapes the tumor microenvironment

No Hepatotoxicity

Unlike other 4-1BB agents, DSP107 shows conditional activity limited to the tumor

No RBC Binding

Unlike other CD47 agents, DSP107 spares RBCs and does not cause anemia
