ClickSoftware to Salesforce Field Service Migration

Organizations using ClickSoftware for their field service management need to find an alternative solution since end of life was announces for Dec. 2023.

Having gone through numerous ClickSoftware implementations, we strongly believe that the smart, efficient and cost-effective solution is a full migration to Salesforce Field Service.

Possessing unique knowledge of both ClickSoftware and Salesforce Field Service, we are singularly positioned to lead end-to-end migration projects, allowing organizations to retain their investment while optimizing existing functionality and business processes.
