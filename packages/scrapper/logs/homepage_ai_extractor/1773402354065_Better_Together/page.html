FAQ

How does a weight loss challenge work?

You create or join a group challenge with friends, family, or coworkers, set a shared goal and timeframe, then everyone checks in and tracks progress inside the app. The group keeps each other accountable with updates and friendly competition.

What can we track in the app?

You can track your weight and habits, and follow your progress over time in the group. The app is built around progress tracking plus accountability, so you’re not doing it alone.

Can I do this with friends and family who live far away?

Yes. Challenges are designed to work whether people are nearby or remote, as long as everyone is in the same group challenge in the app.

Can coworkers join a workplace challenge?

Yes. You can run a team challenge the same way you would with friends or family, create the group, set the goal, and keep everyone engaged with check-ins and progress updates.

Is it free?

You can download the app and start a challenge. Pricing and what’s included can vary by plan, so check the app store listing or in-app plan screen for the current options.

What makes BetterTogether different from other weight loss apps?

Most weight loss apps focus on solo tracking. BetterTogether is built around group challenges, accountability, and motivation, so you stick with it longer and keep it fun.

Do we need to compete to use it?

No. You can use it collaboratively, competitive, or both. Some groups use leaderboards and prizes, others just use shared goals and support.
