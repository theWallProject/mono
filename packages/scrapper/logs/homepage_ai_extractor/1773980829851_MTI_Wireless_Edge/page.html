About Us

Fifty Years of Proven Performance

Founded in 1972, MTI Wireless Edge is a premier designer, developer, and manufacturer of state-of-the-art antennas and antenna systems.

Headquartered in Israel and part of the MTI Group, MTI Wireless Edge is an internationally recognized producer of commercial off-the-shelf and custom-developed antenna solutions in a broad frequency range of HF to 170 GHz for commercial, RFID, and military applications.
