Frequently asked questions

Why is Napo different?

At Napo, we help you care confidently for your pet by removing the worry of unexpected vet bills. Our comprehensive policy covers all your pet’s needs, with a straightforward £99 excess per condition each year.
But we don’t stop at insurance. You get free, unlimited access to expert vet advice and health support tools designed to keep your pet well and reduce costly vet visits. And when it’s time to claim, our award-winning team works quickly—paying vets directly—so you’re never left managing bills alone.
With Napo, you receive real support from quote to claim, at every stage of your pet’s life.

Can Napo pay my vet directly?

Yes. We can pay you or the vets, whichever you prefer. If you want us to pay your vets directly, it's up to the vet practice whether they would be happy to do this.

Can I speak to someone over the phone?

Yes, you can chat with us over the phone using our callback service. To book a call, tell us what you'd like to talk about in a chat or email to us. So we can help you as best we can on the call, please give as much detail about your query as you can in your message. Then one of our customer champions will send you a calendar where you can book a call at a time that suits you. 😊
We don’t have an inbound phone line. So if you want to chat with us over the phone, please book a callback.
Just a heads up, we can not make any changes to payments or Direct Debits over the phone.
But, the quickest and easiest way to speak to us is through our chat. Customer champions are available 10am-5:30pm on weekdays. 💬

Does Napo do multipet discounts?

Yes, Napo offers 5% multipet discount when you insure more than one pet with us. To get your multipet discount, just get a quote and add another pet during the quote.
If you’re already a customer and want to add another pet, please contact us.

How can the excess be only £99 per year?

High excesses make claiming difficult and stressful. Insurers set them high to avoid paying up. We want to make claiming as stress-free as possible.
We don’t need to wriggle out of paying when you need us the most. The low £99 excess is our proof to you that we mean it.
We know claiming always comes at tough times, and think our free pet services will help you to prevent the need to claim in the first place.

Will my policy price increase if I claim?

Some insurers boast they’ve got “no penalty for claiming” but they don’t show you their fineprint. Here’s the truth...
Pet insurance policies, including Napo’s, go up each year as your pet gets older and less healthy.
When you claim, this also affects your premium. The increase will depend on the type of claim you make. For isolated accidents, the increase in premiums will likely be lower when compared to if you claim for something that might lead to long-term consequences like heart issues or diabetes.

Why do I need pet insurance?

Illnesses, dental work, behavioural treatment, even ‘missing pet’ rewards... The costs of a pet over its lifetime can stack up.
For example, if you own a medium dog, the PDSA estimates you’ll pay between £7,500 - £11,800 over their lifetime. Unless you have insurance. 
Our comprehensive lifetime cover makes sure that if you ever need expensive help, you can afford the very best!
