ServiceNow to Acquire Pyramid Analytics to expand Data-Driven AI

Learn More

Possible isn’t enough,

it has to be easy.

In a reality of ever-growing analytics needs, Pyramid prevents the exponential growth in complexity. Possible isn't enough, it has to be easy for data teams and business users to keep up with the pace of the modern business

PLATFORM OVERVIEW

Data prep & science

Pyramid includes hundreds of built-in data connectors, and a super-fast direct query engine that ensures flexibility and high performance without data extractions. From data wrangling to advanced machine learning models, Pyramid enables sophistication without complexity.

Semantics & logic

Virtual semantic models hide database complexities while enhancing the analytical experience for querying and analysis. Sophisticated, complex functions and hierarchies can be easily built. Define and share custom formulas, business logic, and dynamic calculations.

Business analytics

Pyramid streamlines data discovery by enabling users to interactively query and analyze data directly from live sources using AI, drag and drop, or a spreadsheet-like interface. Create interactive visualizations using customized charts, infographics, and real-time dashboards. Enrich your analysis further with contextual commentary, dynamic slicers, external content, images, and interactive data-driven components.

Collaborate & distribute

Transform reports and dashboards into collaborative workspaces with built-in annotations, real-time commenting, tagging, and data snapshots, to help teams maintain context and streamline decision-making. Robust reporting capabilities support both dynamic embedded dashboards and customized, pixel-perfect static reports that can be scheduled or exported as multiple formats.

Fully governed & secure

Pyramid provides robust governance and enterprise-grade security, offering granular, role-based access control. Features like Tabulate, Master Flow, and a centralized business logic library ensure data consistency, version control, and standardized metrics, while built-in auditing and usage monitoring support compliance and informed resource management.

Advanced analytics on an enterprise scale with zero complexity

Pyramid’s all-in-one platform for intelligent analytics drives smarter, faster data-backed decisions at every level of your organization
