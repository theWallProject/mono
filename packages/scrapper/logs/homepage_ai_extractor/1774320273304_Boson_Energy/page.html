Technology

Cracking the code of 'non-recyclable' waste

Non-recyclable solid waste is mostly a mix of complex hydrocarbons. Boson's integrated gasification-based technology and system solution cracks the hydrocarbons into the fundamental molecules of Hydrogen and captured CO2. Boson has created a process environment that unleashes the full power of chemistry to ‘do its thing' – completely and efficiently.

Solutions

Sector coupling defined: Waste-to-X

Local waste offers unique possibilities for sector coupling, to deliver local energy security, flexible heat and cooling, zero emission transportation, sustainable chemicals, and even clean water – without drawing a single kWhe from the grid. The wide range of revenues allows for profitability without subsidies – at fossil-parity pricing.

Impact

Local energy security, health, and prosperity – from Your waste

1 ton of non-recyclable waste transformed into heavy duty EV charging equals 300 litres of diesel. There is enough waste in any city to shift all commercial transport in that city to zero emission. Waste ‘done right’ represents an incredible potential for energy security and de-pollution at fossil parity – globally.
