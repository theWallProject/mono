About Us

  • Kudan’s spatial perception technology is a core enabler for the evolution of artificial intelligence into Physical AI—intelligence that can perceive, act, and learn within the real world.
  • Spatial perception is indispensable across a wide range of industries, forming the foundation for digital-twin–driven transformation and productivity gains, as well as for achieving labor reduction and full autonomy through intelligent robots.
