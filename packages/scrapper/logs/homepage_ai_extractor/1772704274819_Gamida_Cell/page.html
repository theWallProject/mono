Future of malignant and non-malignant hematological conditions

Our proprietary nicotinamide (NAM) technology leverages the properties of NAM to enhance and expand cells, creating allogeneic cell therapy products and candidates that are potentially curative for patients with hematologic and non-hematological conditions.
