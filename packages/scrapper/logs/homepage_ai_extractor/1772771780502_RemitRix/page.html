    Reports Insights Agent

    Turns dense reports into human-ready explanations. The Reports Insights Agent reads your ALM, ESG, VaR and Solvency II outputs and surfaces the 'why' behind every number — in seconds.

    Summarizes long reports into executive-ready briefs

    Highlights drivers of change vs previous run (by risk type, line of business, scenario)

    Flags anomalies, data quality issues, or parameter shifts

    Exports explanations into Word/PDF / email summaries

    Portfolio Analyzer Agent

    Answers portfolio questions with deep context — exposure, concentrations, sensitivities — connected directly to the underlying positions and scenarios.

    Natural-language queries over positions, cash flows and risk factors

    Instant breakdowns by asset class, credit quality, currency, maturity bucket

    Scenario comparison: 'How does this portfolio behave under Stress A vs Stress B?'

    Detects concentration risk by issuer, sector, geography

    Solvency II Agent

    Explains, validates & narrates regulatory capital logic. The Solvency II Agent understands standard formula logic and your calibrated parameters, helping you communicate solvency capital results.

    Explains SCR components (market, life, non-life, health, operational, etc.)

    Links calculations back to Edge+ parameters and correlation matrices

    Answers 'what if' questions on yield curves, stresses and correlations

    Generates narrative explanations for ORSA, board packs, and regulators

    User Guide Agent

    Onboarding & troubleshooting agent for your platform. The User Guide Agent knows the RemitRix platform, from Edge and Horizon screens to data templates and export flows, so new users become productive quickly.

    Answers 'how do I...?' questions about any screen or feature

    Generates step-by-step walkthroughs with highlighted UI elements

    Learns from your internal documentation & FAQs

    Scenario Generator Agent

    Build, modify & explore stress scenarios. From historical crises to bespoke stress tests, the Scenario Generator Agent lets you create and re-run scenarios by describing them in natural language.

    Create scenarios from historical episodes (e.g., 2008, COVID crash), customized for your portfolio

    Describe new stresses: 'steepening yield curve + 20% equity drop + 200 bps credit spread widening'

    Preview impact on solvency ratios, capital needs, and P&L before running full calculations

    Save scenario templates and replay them automatically across future portfolios

    Portfolio Impact Radar Agent

    Portfolio-aware early warning. Scans macro, credit, FX and geopolitical events, maps them to your exposures, and generates ready-to-run stress scenarios — delivered as a CRO-ready email briefing with a one-click 'Run Scenario' action.

    Scans global events daily and filters out noise

    Ranks relevance based on your exposures (duration, currency, sector, rating)

    Auto-generates stress scenarios (curve shifts, spread widening, FX moves, equity shocks)

    Email briefing includes a one-click 'Run Scenario' link into RemitRix

    Market Data Agent

    Dedicated to curves, spreads & time-series insights. Ask about market movements and see how they affect your risk and solvency results.

    Answers questions on yield curves, credit spreads, equity indices, FX rates

    Shows time series charts and compares dates or regimes

    Connects market moves with model inputs (ESG, discount curves, shocks)

    Uses the same market data feeding your risk and solvency models for consistency

    Asset Pricing Agent

    Price financial instruments quickly and interactively — options, bonds, FX forwards, swaps, and more. Adjust inputs, explore sensitivities, and export the logic to Excel when you're done.

    Price European options and view Greeks in real-time

    Calculate FX forwards with customizable tenors and notionals

    Analyze bond prices, duration, and DV01

    Export formulas to Excel that replicate the calculation
