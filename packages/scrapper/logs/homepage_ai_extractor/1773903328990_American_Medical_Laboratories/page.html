    American Medical Laboratories

    American Medical Laboratories (AML) is Israel’s largest, private full-service clinical laboratory
    The core laboratory in “Beit Noy” provides clinical laboratory services (private blood tests, urine and microbiology) in the disciplines of Biochemistry, Hematology, Coagulation, Hormonal Analysis, Serology (including HIV test), Microbiology, Cancer Markers, Esoteric testing, etc. The STAT laboratory in the Raphael Hospital is providing the hospital with laboratory services, including STAT testing and blood banking.
    For your convenience, we offer private blood and urine tests at home with our “Nurse at Home” service.
    For more information and to schedule a visit, contact us today.

    Quality Assured

    AML successfully participates in multiple International Quality Control Schemes-Proficiency Testing. It has an Independent Quality Assurance Department with a proprietary quality manual document, version controlled and written Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
    AML  central laboratory in located  Beit Noy is Accredited by the Israeli Laboratory Accreditation Authority for the analytes in the scope of accreditation.
    AML also participates in the NGSP International Standardization Program for Hemoglobin A1C Testing.

    Clinical Laboratory Testing

    AML Israel additional provides clinical laboratory testing services to Israeli medical institutions, private walk-patients, and comprehensive central laboratory services for clinical trials to the international and Israeli pharmaceutical and biotechnology industries. AML has global alliances with major international central labs including,  LabConnect, and Synlab for standardized central lab services on multi-national clinical trials.
