Frequently Asked Questions

What is CropX?

CropX is the world’s leading digital farm management system for precision agriculture, known for its ease-of-use, comprehensive agronomic features, and powerful integrations. CropX products include easy to install sensors (soil, rain gauge, weather station, ET, telemetry), complete farm management software (FMS), and specialized tools for dealers and agribusinesses for managing farm operations at scale. We are intentionally focused on all things agronomy – soil and plant health, irrigation, disease, nutrition, weather, crop monitoring, and more.

Who is it for?

CropX is a complete agronomy solution for farmers, dealers, agronomic advisors and agribusinesses who are serious about yields, profits, efficiency, and sustainability. Unlike others, our hardware and software is super easy to learn and use, has been in production for 10+ years, is trusted by 20,000+ users worldwide, operates in 70+ countries, and serves 100+ different crop types. We think you’ll love it!

How exactly does it work?

We continuously collect and analyze data from a variety of in-field sensors (such as soil sensors, ET sensors, weather stations – both our own and third party), satellites, and farm machinery (almost any brand)

We apply our proprietory, research-backed agronomic models, data science, and AI tailored to your specific crop, field and weather conditions to track the past and predict the future of things like water use, crop stress, disease risk, nitrate leaching, salinity levels, vegetative growth, root and canopy development, and 

We transform this wealth of agronomic data and insights into up-to-date, accurate, and actionable recommendations that you use to guide your day-to-day farming operations! Like when and how much to irrigate, what type of fungicide to apply to protect your crop, and whether your roots and canopy are developing on track or falling behind. 

While behind the scenes, our hardware, software and services runs on a high-performance, scalable and robust, enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure, backed by 24/7 tech and grower support, a deep trove of training and knowledge resources, and a passionate, global team of agronomists, techies, and active farmers!

What makes CropX unique?

We are unlike many point solutions out there with limited features or regional relevance, or dated stagnant platforms with poorly integrated tools, or immature offerings from new agtech upstarts. 

CropX is a truly all-in-one, fully integrated, and globally scaled DIGITAL AGRONOMY platform that includes

  • Sensors – robust, accurate, easy to self install, and research-backed in-field instruments designed from the ground up by CropX to meet the highest standards of farming
    • Soil sensors (SV line) at multiple depths that measure soil moisture, temperature, electroconducitivity and salinity
    • Evapotranspiration sensors (ET line) that measure actual ET, canopy growth and water stress
    • Weather stations (WS line) and rain guages (RG line) to monitor local weather conditions
    • Telemetry / communication gateway devices (TD line) with built-in cellular connection to connect all these devices to the cloud
    • New real-time in-field nitrate sensor is also in development for 2025/26
  • Software – a complete farm management software (FMS) suite with modules for
    • Managing farm, field, crop, and soil data
    • managing installs, status, health of field devices and sensors, both CropX and third party supported
    • Making seasons plans and tracking crop rotation history
    • Monitoring field and crop health, root and canopy growth and development milestones
    • Monitoring crop water use and scheduling irrigations
    • Monitoring nutritition, salinity levels and leaching
    • Monitoring disease risk and crop protection applications
    • Monitoring effluent systems and pumps
    • Managing machine data and partner connections with a variety of farm machines, irrigation systems, third party services and sensors
    • Communicating and receiving dealer advice and recommendations
    • Managing farm tasks, activities, schedules, and work assignments
    • Creating reports for seasonal review, audit and compliance
    • Additionally in EU: financials and cost tracking, soil test results, crop recording, crop registration, inventory and stock levels, contracts and agreements, data sharing with processors & agribusiness partners
    • And more features added every week
  • Science – Deep agronomic science, the latest field research, and an R&D team of in-house agronomists and active farmers informs the development and testing of all our agronomic and AI models, that include
    • Crop growth models, that track phenological stages, canopy size, root depth, etc based on GDD and days-since-planting
    • Sensor auto-calibration and auto-set-point detection models, for field capacity, wilting point, refill points, etc
    • Disease lifecycle and risk models
    • Irrigation & water use models
    • Leaching detection models
    • Satellite image processing, indices calculation and cloud detection models
    • And more
  • Support
    • A global technical and grower support team based in 4 timezones that provides 24/7 coverage
    • Broad online self-serve knowledgebase with articles, FAQs, how-tos and videos
    • Pre-, in- and post-season training, and on-demand agronomy focused webinars throughout the year
    • Reachable via email, phone, web or in-app in a variety of languages
    • Seamless returns and exchange program when hardware issues arise
