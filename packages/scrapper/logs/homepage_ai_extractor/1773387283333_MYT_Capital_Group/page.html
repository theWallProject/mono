What We Do

With a substantial background in investments, financing and technologies, coupled with our global network of relations we help our customers with solving acute strategic, fund raising and internal or external growth issues. Together we decide on the directions, make the connections and jointly strive for the right solution.

Capital

We invest in innovative technology companies with a clear expansion plan to global markets, especially to Asia.Our current investments are in the areas of IOT, robotics, aviation, education AI, medical, food tech and blockchain.

  • Investments and M&A Advisory

Investments and M&A Advisory

We source investment and acquisition opportunities for multinational companies and funds.

From macro insight on each sector to companies screening, we leverage our network to analyse the most updated data and information on each target company, including the founders’ credibility, investors opinion, market positioning and uniqueness of the technology.

Never the less, we verify how and if the management can leverage our investors’ strategic value for the company expansion in different markets and pointing out the execution of the plan post the investment.

We have partnered with the leading Israeli banks, law firms and accountants to provide end to end solution under our umbrella.

  • Funding Advisory

Funding Advisory

We have tremendous network of global investors, from angels, family offices, VCs, PEs and buy-out funds to government funds, strategic investors and large corporate investments division.

We help start ups and funds to map out their financial roadmap and to evaluate the go-to-market strategy, strengths, market traction and positioning for the up coming round.

  • Business Consulting

Business Consulting

We help customers with the continuing challenges of cutting costs, increasing revenue, improving financial performance, preparing and achieving strategic plans and goals, as well as support them before, during and after mergers.
