POINT: The Ultimate Workspace by Azrieli Group

Beyond a workspace
Welcome to POINT, the signature workspace brand by the Azrieli Group Israel’s leading real estate company. More than just a place to work, POINT is designed to elevate your workday experience.
Our spaces blend breathtaking architecture, an inspiring community, and best-in-class services - giving you the freedom to focus on growth while we take care of everything else.
Our Vision

At POINT, we create environments where businesses can thrive.
Our offering includes private offices of varying sizes, executive boardrooms, and elegant business lounges.
With a people-first approach, our team takes care of every detail to support a seamless and productive workday.

Legacy of Excellence
As part of the Azrieli Group, POINT is backed by Israel’s most prestigious real estate company. From iconic towers to integrated mixed-use destinations, our legacy is built on excellence and innovation.
Our flexible workspaces are designed to grow with you - whether you need a single office or a fully customized suite, POINT adapts to your business needs.

POINT Azrieli Center Tel Aviv

Located in the Round Tower at Azrieli Center, one of Tel Aviv’s most iconic landmarks, POINT Azrieli Center places your business at the heart of the city’s main business hub. From beautifully designed business lounges and private offices to advanced meeting rooms and a versatile event space with a private balcony, every space reflects thoughtful design and attention to detail.

Located on floors 10–11, POINT Azrieli Center offers a work environment defined by convenience, design, and quality. The space includes private offices, business lounges, breakout areas, meeting rooms, and a versatile event space. Members also enjoy ongoing community events, Happy Hours, and a variety of wellness sessions.

  • Floors: 10th-11th, Azrieli Center TLV

POINT Azrieli Sarona

Flooded with natural light and framed by panoramic views of the Tel Aviv skyline, every detail at POINT Sarona is designed to inspire. From beautifully designed business lounges and private offices to the executive boardroom on the 61st floor, every space reflects thoughtful design and attention to detail.

Located on the top floors (58–61) of the iconic Azrieli Sarona Tower, one of Tel Aviv’s most prestigious office buildings,
POINT Sarona offers a work environment defined by quality, comfort, and design. The space includes private offices, business lounges, breakout areas, fully equipped cafeterias, meeting rooms, and a private gym with showers. Members also enjoy ongoing community events, wellness activities, and an on-site bakery.

  • 58th - 61st floor
  • Top floors
