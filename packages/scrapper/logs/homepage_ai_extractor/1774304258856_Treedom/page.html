Widget AccessiWeb

The AccessiWeb widget adds tools, adjustments and quick settings designed to personalize the browsing experience and use of content according to the specific needs of users.

Accessibility is fundamental to ensure that all users, regardless of their ability, disability or limitations, can navigate and interact with the contents with maximum effectiveness.

The widget provides 8 navigation profiles dedicated to specific needs; 10 content adjustments to improve accessibility and visual comfort; 12 perceptual, visual and auditory accessibility settings and 15 color adjustments to customize visual comfort and readability.
