Fundraising

Distributions

Only Covercy makes distribution payments this simple. Thanks to customizable waterfall models fully integrated with Excel, you can make one-click distributions, track and reconcile all payments down to the cent, and instantly integrate transaction data with third-party accounting systems. All from a single platform.

Payments

Tailored for real estate transactions, Covercy facilitates the rapid movement of large sums needed to close deals, make distribution payments, or accept capital contributions, offering services like electronic verification and real-time tracking. Our focus on the real estate sector ensures that funds are transferred swiftly and securely, crucial for timely deal closures.
