    Our Story

    Dalumi Group is a leading global family-owned diamond company, renowned for its commitment to quality, reliability, and integrity. 

    With a factory in Botswana and offices in key locations including New York, Los Angeles, Hong Kong, and India, Dalumi specializes in manufacturing, polishing, and marketing finest quality natural diamonds and diamond jewelry globally.

    Dalumi transforms rough diamonds into brilliance with integrity and excellence. Ethically sourced and globally renowned.

    Company

    Discover Dalumi’s legacy of excellence — from humble beginnings in 1960 to global leadership in yellow diamonds, built on integrity, innovation, and a timeless family vision.

    Heritage

    Values
