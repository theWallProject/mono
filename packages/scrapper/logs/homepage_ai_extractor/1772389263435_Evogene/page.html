Real-World Innovation Pioneering chemical Discovery with Generative AI

Harnessing computational chemistry and generative AI to meet real-world commercial product needs in pharma and agriculture.

AI INNOVATION IN PHARMA AND AGRICULTURE MEANS A BETTER WORLD FOR ALL

PHARMA | DRUG DISCOVERY

Increasing novel therapeutics’ probability of success with AI-driven molecule design

AGRICULTURE | AG CHEMICALS

Proven results in shaping sustainable agriculture with next-generation crop protection solutions

Over 90% small-molecule failure rate in reaching commercial phase, demands
a disruptive approach

Evogene bridges the gap between commercial product needs of the pharmaceutical and ag-chemical industries and specifically tailored computational innovation. Our unique approach has the potential to translate generative AI discoveries into practical, commercially viable products.

ChemPass AI™: A Proprietary AI-First TECH-ENGINE for Optimized Multi-Parameter Novel Small Molecule Design

At the core of Evogene is ChemPass AI™, a proprietary generative AI tech-engine – creating highly potent, unique small molecules optimized across multiple critical parameters – increasing the probability of success while reducing time and costs.

Joint Creation

By leveraging strategic partnerships or our own in-house expertise, with ChemPass AI’s capabilities for small molecule design, we facilitate the development of market-ready products for both drug discovery and agricultural chemicals. This flexible business approach enables us to explore a wider range of product development opportunities and maximize our impact in the real world.

professor ehud gazit a world leader in the field of Molecular self-assembly

“We are excited to join forces with Evogene in this groundbreaking endeavor.
