Kemphos 2001 CTSP

COLOR INDICATOR

Kemphos 2001 CTSP Pink contains a built-in indicator which can act as a “private laboratory” for beer equipment applications. The solution’s color changes from pink to milky white when residues still exist in the system or equipment. A pink solution at the end of cleaning can help indicate the completion of the cleaning process.
