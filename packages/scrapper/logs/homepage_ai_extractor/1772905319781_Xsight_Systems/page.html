             REDEFINING
            RUNWAY SAFETY             Xsight Systems’ cutting-edge remote Sensing and AI provide airports with automated detection, classification, and assessment of hazards that pose a risk to aircraft operations during take-offs & landings.

            Half Of All Accidents Occur During Takeoff & Landing

            Runway safety has always been a top priority at airports, and it becomes increasingly more challenging due to the continuous growth of air traffic. Each and every day, aircraft face various runway threats from various origins, including foreign object debris (FOD), birds and wildlife and accumulated contaminants (snow, ice, etc.).

            One of the main challenges airports faces is the sheer enormity of the airside. Sizable areas such as these require continuous monitoring to detect and classify hazards that pose safety and operational risks, leading to disruptions in air traffic activity.

            Our Technology

            Xsight’s approach to runway risk management is to start from the issue and develop a solution for our customers’ needs. The primary goal is to automate the management of runway risks. To do this, we specialize in remote sensing.

            Automation starts with the ability to analyze appropriate data using both machine learning and AI technologies. Xsight uses state-of-the-art neural networks as well as machine intelligence algorithms that have learned utilizing data that we have collected over our twelve-year history of airport operations. As a leader in the field, we understand what kinds of sensing equipment are required and have developed camera and radar systems in-house to tackle the issues in the most efficient forms.

            Xsight systems developed an in-house camera module that withstands outdoor environments and has a long-range in both day and night situations. We also developed a radar system that has complementary capabilities to the camera.
            With our sensing equipment, we have developed the only hybrid radar-optical system on the market.

            Our system uses AI in the form of both machine learning and deep learning in every stage of our system.  It is key in the detection process for both detecting objects and classifying them.

            Our solutions solve airport operation issues such as runway capacity and safety.  Our solutions reduce FOD retrieval downtime, are key in bird and wildlife management, and keep airports up to regulation in any runway condition.
