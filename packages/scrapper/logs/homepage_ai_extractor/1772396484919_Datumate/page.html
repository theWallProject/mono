The Way We Build Big Projects is Broken

Heavy civil construction projects are under constant pressure—tight timelines, massive budgets, and countless moving parts. Yet most infrastructure teams still rely on outdated reporting methods that are siloed, reactive, and often inaccurate. The result? Missed deadlines, costly rework, and frustrated stakeholders.

 

Lack of construction site data and project visibility 

Shortage of professional workers 

No single source of truth

Little or no collaboration between stakeholders 

What’s missing is a shared brain for the project—one that connects teams, tracks progress in real-time, and ensures everyone is working from the same clear, accurate picture.

Purpose-Built for Heavy Civil

Most construction tech is designed for vertical builds like high-rises. DatuBIM is built from the ground up for infrastructure projects—roads, rail, bridges, and earthworks. It understands the terrain, scale, and workflows unique to heavy civil, not just buildings.

Visual-First Source of Truth

Progress isn’t buried in spreadsheets—it’s right in front of you. DatuBIM provides visual, measurable insights that everyone can understand, from the field to the boardroom.

Clear ROI from Day One

From reducing rework to streamlining documentation, DatuBIM drives value fast. It’s easy to deploy, easy to scale, and delivers results in days, not months.

Real World Validation

Proven by major infrastructure owners and GCs. Trusted by those who build and maintain the world's most critical assets.

Owners

Monitor your assets more effectively and achieve optimal outcomes by connecting the right people with the right information at all times. 

General Contractors

Win more bids, take on more projects, gain better visibility into project progress, and streamline your processes to deliver on time and on budget. 

Engineering & Consulting firms 

Easily track projects from design to completion, share information with all project stakeholders, and gain full transparency into multiple sites at any time and from anywhere. 

Flawless Execution Starts with Better Visibility and Smarter Coordination
