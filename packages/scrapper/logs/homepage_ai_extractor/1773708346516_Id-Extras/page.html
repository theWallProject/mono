We have add-ons and scripts to

  • easily create rich, interactive, accessible PDF forms
  • automate multi-column footnotes
  • add line numbers to InDesign documents
  • link master pages to paragraph and object styles
  • create interactive indexes

and many more.

Custom Scripts

A custom InDesign script can save you time and lower costs. In many cases, running a script is also less error prone, so you save on editorial corrections.

Unique problems require original solutions. Sometimes it is impossible to find a ready-made InDesign add-on or script. Commissioning a script can be an inexpensive and convenient way to deal with workflow issues that are causing delays or consuming resources.

Or perhaps one of the ready-made scripts in the Id-Extras.com library is almost perfect but is missing a crucial feature? We can customize any of our scripts to suit your needs.
