Driving Extraordinary Care Home

Hospitals face a growing capacity crisis. myLaurel solves this by delivering acute and transitional care at home. Our hybrid model - hands-on clinical care supported by virtual technology - prevents unnecessary hospitalizations, frees critical bed space, improves outcomes, and significantly lowers the cost of care for our health systems and payers.
