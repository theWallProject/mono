    What Are Calming Stickers?

    At NeuroPet, we believe every pet deserves a calm and stress-free life. Our NeuroPet calming stickers are designed to support pets during stressful situations such as travel, vet visits, thunderstorms, fireworks, and separation anxiety. Studies suggest that over 72% of dogs experience anxiety-related behaviors, including trembling, barking, pacing, or hiding during stressful events like loud noises or unfamiliar environments. Simple wellness solutions can help reduce this stress and improve a pet’s daily comfort. Unlike supplements or medications, NeuroPet calming stickers are non-invasive and easy to use. Simply place the sticker on your pet’s collar, harness, crate, or bedding. This allows pets to receive gentle calming support throughout the day without changing feeding routines or introducing new substances
