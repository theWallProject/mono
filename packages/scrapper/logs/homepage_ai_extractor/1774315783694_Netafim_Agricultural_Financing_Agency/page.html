Making Agriculture Climate-Resilient

Agriculture has always been full of uncertainties, and now climate-change is adding more complexities to it by draughts, floods, uneven distribution of rainfall and resultant water-stress. Farmers are bearing the brunt of these difficulties and are finding it difficult to manage their crops. Challenge is to make Agriculture climate-resilient and profitable. Our financing solutions supports farmers in adopting such technologies which are resilient to climate change & helps in mitigating the risk of crop losses.

Supporting mechanisation & automation in Agri & Allied sector

In addition to growing urbanization and rising population, the shortage of farm labours is also becoming a real issue. Hence, a significant boost is required in adoption of agri-machinery and farm technologies, that allow optimum usage of land & resources. We believe farm mechanization is a key tool for increasing the productivity of our farms. Similar challenges have been faced by Indian dairy sector, where the productivity level needs to improve across the dairy value chain. Automation in dairy sector helps in improving profitability, animal health, lifestyle & milk quality. Being the world’s largest milk producer, but having very low productivity per cattle, it is essential now to invest in dairy technologies for increasing the overall efficiency in the value chain. Our financing solutions supports farmers & relevant stakeholders engaged in mechanisation & automation of agriculture & allied activities. Agriculture must become a thriving business, not just a subsistence activity.

Helping farmers in increasing production

India’s population stands more than 1.3+ bn and is estimated to rise at a steady pace to reach 1.6 bn by the year 2050 (According to the World Bank estimates). Even though food grain production has increased significantly over the years, there is a need for the production to increase even further in order to meet the ever-growing demand created due to this population increase. Micro irrigation (Drip/Sprinkler) systems play a key role in increasing crop productivity while saving water. Our financing solutions, thus, enable the farmers to ‘Grow More, With Less’.

Providing Access to Finance to Farmers & MSMEs

Access to adequate, timely and efficient financial solution continues to remain the biggest challenge for farmers & agri-MSMEs and has restricted their growth. Given their significant contribution in the Indian economy and vast potential to drive India’s growth, ensuring access to finance to these under-served segments is of immense importance. Our impact financing intends to provide the required growth impetus to farmers and agri-MSMEs for increasing the overall agricultural growth.

What Our Growers Have to Say!

Vinayak Patil

Sangli, Maharashtra

Due to continuous flood, irrigation and waterlogging conditions, salts started accumulating on the soil's surface. To improve decreasing fertility, we installed drip irrigation with the help of NAFA. The results were incredible, with almost doubled per acre yield even after using 40% less water.

Dharamraj -Geeta Estate

Hassan, Karnataka

Previously about 10-12 years back, I installed a local drip irrigation system; it was a complete failure, and the pipes were affected within 2 years. Recently we decided to install, with the help of Netafim agriculture financing agency, drip irrigation for our coffee plantation. The yield was drastically increased from 25-30 bags to 40 bags this year.

Dharmraj Patil

Satara, Maharashtra

Netafim Agriculture Financing Agency (NAFA) has the best in class technology; their agronomists provide regular guidance on crop management. We are getting 20-25% more yield compared to the flood irrigation we used to follow earlier. Consistent use of drip irrigation resulted in fewer infestations of weeds and reduced labour costs.

S.Iyappan

Theni, Tamil Nadu

The traditional system's water was used to irrigate one acre of land with drip irrigation through channel financing; it is now enough for 5 acres. We can now fix one valve and leave it for 3 to 5 hours without needing a human to stay on site. Uniform water distribution has improved the quality and shine of our produce.
