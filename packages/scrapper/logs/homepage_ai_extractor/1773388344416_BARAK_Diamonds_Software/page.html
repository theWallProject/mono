Our Solutions

Our Solutions

BARAK solution is a robust software platform designed to meet the diamonds and jewelry industry’s business requirements.

BARAK solution controls every stage of the business, reduces business risks, operational inefficiences and total cost of ownership (TCO) and increases revenues and profitability.

BARAK can be purchased either as SaaS (Software as a Service) or as on-premises software.

BARAK for diamonds

The BARAK for diamonds solution provides the following features and benefits:

  • Full and efficient inventory management for certified diamonds and fixed assortment parcels

  • Reporting tools to analyze your business performance

  • Branch management

  • Accounting management unique to the diamond industry

  • Automatic uploads to B2B and B2C websites (Rapnet, IDEX, BlueNile etc)

  • Advanced interfaces with special hardware (scales, barcodes, etc.)

  • Sophisticated and easy-to-use reports-generator function enabling you to create any report 

BARAK for jewelry

The BARAK for jewelry provides functionality required for managing jewelry inventory through the entire process flow – from diamond jewelry catalog design, control raw materials and job bags through tracking production to sales of ready jewelry items.

Diamond Point

Diamond Point solution allows your current and future customers and partners viewing your diamonds inventory online. 

This module can be easily integrated with your company website.

By using Diamond Point you can efficiently define your available online inventory.
