A Step into the future

[ 001 ]

Danit Peleg, the founder of the innovative 3D Printed Fashion Lab, is committed to revolutionizing the fashion industry through innovation, technology, and sustainability. Her mission is multifaceted, aiming to redefine traditional garment production methods, promote accessibility in fashion, and inspire creativity in design.


At the heart of their operations is a dedication to leveraging 3D printing technology to create customized, on-demand clothing that are wearable, environmentally friendly, and circular.

By pushing the boundaries of what's possible in 3D printed fashion design production, Danit and her team seek to empower the industry and individuals to express their unique identities and embrace bodies without limitations.


Through ongoing research, collaboration, and outreach, Danit Peleg strives to pioneer a more inclusive and sustainable future for the fashion industry, where creativity and innovation intersect to create positive change.


Danit Peleg has received numerous accolades and was recognized as one of Europe’s Top 50 Women in Tech by Forbes. She also appeared on the BBC's list of 100 inspiring and influential women from around the world.

3D-Printed fashion collection
for Nouns DAO

The collaboration between Nouns DAO and Danit Peleg merges blockchain technology with innovative fashion design.


Nouns DAO, a decentralized autonomous organization focused on digital art, funded Peleg to explore the intersection of digital creativity and craftsmanship.


Leveraging her 3D printing expertise, Peleg crafted a unique collection reflecting Nouns DAO's ethos: experimental, boundary-pushing, and decentralized. Each garment in the collection was created using a different 3D printing method, showcasing the versatility of the technology.


By merging their domains, Nouns DAO and Peleg redefined the possibilities of digital art and fashion.

3D-printed dress for the

Paralympic Rio 2016

Opening Ceremony

At the opening ceremony of the 2016 Paralympics in Rio de Janeiro, we had the opportunity to introduce pioneering 3D-printed clothing designs. Tailored specifically for Paralympic medalist and dancer Amy Purdy's body, our garments showcased the intersection of fashion and technology, aiming to inspire inclusivity and accessibility. Through meticulous research and experimentation, we crafted personalized pieces using advanced scanning technology, aiming to provide both functionality and style.

Danit Peleg at the BBC 100 Women

At the BBC 100 Women conference,
Danit shared how the fashion industry's vast waste and poor labor conditions demand radical change. With only 1% of garments recycled, the need for innovation is clear. Danit advocate for 3D printing as a revolutionary solution, offering a sustainable and ethical future for fashion.

Danit Peleg's TED talk +2M views

Downloadable, printable clothing may be coming to a closet near you. What started as designer Danit Peleg's fashion school project turned into a collection of 3D-printed designs that have the strength and flexibility for everyday wear. "Fashion is a very physical thing," she says. "I wonder what our world will look like when our clothes will be digital."

3D-printed Fashion For Beginners
Online Course

Danit Peleg offers an innovative online course through her platform on Teachable, focusing on the future of fashion with 3D printing technology. The course is designed for anyone interested in merging cutting-edge technology with fashion design, providing insights into the tools, materials, and techniques needed to start creating 3D-printed garments. Students have the opportunity to learn from an industry pioneer and gain hands-on experience in this emerging field.

European Union’s Horizon 2020- 2021

CloudiFacturing, funded by the EU's Horizon2020 program, enhances SME competitiveness through cloud-based simulations. The research optimizes 3D fabrics printing to reduce costs and time. By upgraded Modax3D printer allows faster, 4 dual-head printing. Fashion designer Danit Peleg uses this technology for customized 3D-printed garments.

Gerber Technology 2016- 2019

Working with Gerber Technologies on an innovative research project has significantly impacted our use of AccuMark 3D. This tool enables fashion designers to visualize garments in 3D, unleashing creative vision and accelerating the design process. By integrating AccuMark 3D into our 3D printing workflow, we have been able to create a more professional and efficient workflow.
