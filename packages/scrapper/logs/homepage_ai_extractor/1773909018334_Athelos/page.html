About Company

ATHELOS LTD is a private company that deals with projects management and provides engineering consultant and design services.
The company has vast experience in multidisciplinary / integrated engineering / R&D projects and cost reduction designs in the fields of electro-mechanics / Industrial control development.

The company has strong connections with Israel manufactures and other engineering / operational service providers.
