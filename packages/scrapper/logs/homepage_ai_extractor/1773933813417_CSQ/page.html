CSQ is a dynamic, solution-oriented firm of chartered construction professionals.

We delight our clients and achieve truly satisfying results.

CSQ is a leading Project Development Consultancy in the MENA region. We utilise the latest technology in delivery of our projects and optimise our services through insight, innovation, and data driven intelligence.

CSQ is regulated by the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS). We reduce the inherent risk associated with real estate, construction, development, and investment by providing a broad range of strategic and customised professional services.

Services
Development Management and Monitoring

CSQ delivers multi-billion-dollar projects of every typology including high-rise, hotel, commercial, retail and residential.

Our team consists of architects, engineers, quantity surveyors, construction managers, project managers, planners, and financial analysts. Together, we create the ideal partnership to successfully deliver complex projects and safeguard the interests of financiers, investors, and developers.

Project Management

Our customised project management service combines world-class leadership and administrative skills with extensive local experience to deliver your project as planned.

Quantity Surveying

We appreciate that every project is different. We customise procurement strategies and provide strategic commercial advice tailored to our clients’ specific project goals.

Our commercial expertise and local construction intelligence allows us to evaluate and reduce costs to achieve completion according to your planned quality, budget, and programme.

Management Contracting

We offer complete turnkey project delivery solutions, directly engaging with designers, engineers, sub-contractors, and suppliers on behalf of the client. We manage all aspects of the project, including construction, IT/AV, furniture, equipment, move management, and set-up.

Building Surveying

Enhance property decisions with our Building Surveying expertise, offering comprehensive insights into property condition and potential risks.

We provide Technical Due Diligence surveys, Dilapidation Assessments, Reinstatement Works, Schedule of Condition, Reserve Fund Study, Measured Survey and Handover Survey.

Fit-Out Management
