Basic machining, such as drilling, milling and turning

Schleisner Flame and Plasma Cutting Ltd. is a long established Israeli company for flame and plasma cutting, offering a large inventory of plates in a wide range of steels and thicknesses – some of them exclusively. In addition, the company offers import services by special order.

MERCHANT STEEL AND GASES

At K. Schleisner Metal & Gas Ltd., we market professional iron, pipes, profiles, standard plates, thin black and galvanized plates, and fence posts in various lengths, and screens. We also offer insulating panels for light construction . The work that we perform includes cutting, drilling, bending, and customer shipping.
