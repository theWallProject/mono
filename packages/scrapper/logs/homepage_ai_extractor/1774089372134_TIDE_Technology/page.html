The VersaTide Platform

TIDE Technology develops and operates the VersaTide platform.

VersaTide powers enterprise systems designed to support complex organizational operations.

These systems include modern applications that combine governed workflows, automation, analytics, integrations, and user facing interfaces, allowing teams to coordinate activities across facilities and enterprise platforms.

VersaTide is used extensively in Healthcare and Skilled Nursing environments, where these systems support regulatory reporting, revenue cycle management, clinical compliance monitoring, and cross-facility oversight.

Extending enterprise Systems 

Most organizations rely on core enterprise platforms such as EMR, ERP, CRM, and billing systems. While these systems manage critical data, they are often difficult to extend and are not designed to coordinate activities across teams, facilities, and departments.

As organizations grow, this can lead to fragmented workflows, limited cross-facility visibility, and processes that depend on spreadsheets or manual coordination.

VersaTide enables organizations to benefit from modern applications while extending the capabilities of their existing enterprise systems rather than replacing them.

These applications connect workflows, data, and integrations across enterprise platforms. They operate within a shared framework for identity, governance, data context, operational standards, and user experience, allowing organizations to maintain consistent oversight as their systems evolve.

Who Uses VersaTide 

Healthcare & Skilled Nursing

Organizations operating multi-facility healthcare environments, where systems must support clinical workflows, regulatory reporting, revenue management, and cross-facility visibility.

Suppliers & Logistics

Organizations coordinating vendor operations, equipment tracking, logistics workflows, and billing processes across distributed service environments.

Enterprise Operations

Organizations managing complex internal workflows, operational data, and decision processes across departments, teams, and facilities.

Regulated Environment

Organizations operating under regulatory oversight where governance, auditability, and operational visibility are essential..
