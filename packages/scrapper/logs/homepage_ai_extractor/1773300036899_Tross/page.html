FAQ

  • Can you make any campaign successful?
    No. A good campaign starts with an amazing product at a great price with a healthy margin.

  • Can you work with my campaign even if it already launched?
    Sometimes, depending on your metrics. We’ll primarily focus on the performance advertising side of things. Drop us a line below.

  • What types of products do you work with?
    We mainly work with physical mass-market B2C products. This means that if your product is a mobile app, a film or B2B hardware, we’re probably not going to partner up. There are some exceptions though, so contact us if you’re not sure.

  • How much money do I need?
    Our services are geared towards funded or established companies. Having said that, we do want to help any creator with a great product - that’s why we’ve created our own fund. Visit crowdfundersfund.com to learn about it.

  • Will you work with any project?
    Over 100 campaigns approach us every month, and we usually end up validating 4 and working with 2. We have a two-step validation process for every campaign: first, we evaluate the product and prices internally, then we create a live validation website, drive traffic to it and measure the results. Finally, we study the results - conversions, costs and demographics - if the data looks good we move forward with creating the campaign.

  • What countries do you work with?
    We live in an amazing age of global business. Mostly, our clients are based in the US or Israel, but we’ll work with clients from any country if the product makes sense. We mostly communicate via video calls, which we’ve found to be the most efficient communication method.

  • What’s your business model? Do you work for revshare only?
    Our business model always involves revshare and an upfront payment. If you’re looking for a solution with no out-of-pocket money, you should read about our fund at crowdfundersfund.com.

  • Can I license your software to run my campaign on my own?
    No, we don’t license our software. However, you’re welcome to use the public version of it - it's called BiggerCake and it's awesome. Go to BiggerCake.com.

  • Can you do just the video for my campaign?
    No. We only offer performance advertising or consulting as separate services. Other than that - it's all or nothing.

  • Are you guys wizards?
    Yes Harry, we are.
