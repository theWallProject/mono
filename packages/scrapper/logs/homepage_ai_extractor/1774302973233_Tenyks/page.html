FAQ

Your questions, answered

Do I need new cameras?

No. Tenyks connects to your existing infrastructure. No extra hardware is required.

When will I see results?

Most customers see actionable insights within 48 hours of installation. Revenue improvements typically begin within the first 3 months. Our average customer achieves ROI within 21 days.

What about data privacy & security?

We take data protection extremely seriously. Our system is fully compliant with the EU AI Act (the world’s most stringent AI regulatory framework), as well as GDPR and CCPA. It has been designed from the ground up to respect privacy and eliminate personal identification risks.

We only collect aggregate, anonymised metrics such as dwell time, overall visits, and broad demographic trends. The platform is not intended or permitted for individual tracking or customer profiling.

For example, it cannot tell you that a specific person visited your store three times in a week.

Our technology does not use facial recognition or any biometric identification. As a result, there is no capture of personally identifiable or biometric data of any kind.

In practice, our approach is significantly less intrusive than common industry practices, such as the use of credit-card datasets or mobile location data.

On the security front, our platform meets rigorous enterprise standards. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, and we conduct regular external penetration tests and independent security audits to ensure continuous protection and operational integrity.

What does setup cost?

Setup is completely FREE. We provide white-glove onboarding experience and FREE 1 week trial.

Which types of cameras, NVRs, or VMS platforms do you support?

We support any camera that provides RTSP or RTSPS stream access, including those connected via NVRs and VMS software. Our system is designed to slot into existing infrastructure with minimal fuss, making integration straightforward for most modern deployments.

We also maintain partnerships with many of the major camera and VMS providers, ensuring reliable performance, optimised compatibility, and smooth onboarding for enterprise environments.
