Product

SpotLight™ Duo is the first dedicated cardiovascular and thoracic CT scanner designed to provide comprehensive clinical information for diagnosis and therapy planning. SpotLight™ Duo is optimized for imaging the entire heart and cardiovascular system with 140 mm coverage at a rotation speed of 0.24 sec per rotation.

0.24sec rot speed, 120msec temporal resolution

14cm whole heart coverage, 560 slices

0.28mm spatial resolution w/ 0.5mm detector

139kW effective power for SNR

Novel Stereo CT design

Technology

Arineta’s innovative Stereo CT technology consists of two rapidly alternating sources of radiation that create dual overlapping beams. Combined with high rotation speed of 0.24 sec per rotation, it provides a sharp, motionless CT image of the heart in one heartbeat.

Unique Stereo CT® technology

Focused field-of-view (FOV)

Fast gantry rotation

Ultra-short gantry geometry

Small footprint

About Us

Arineta is an Israeli company that develops and produces innovative cardiovascular imaging solutions. Arineta’s flagship product is SpotLight™ Duo, the world first dedicated single-heartbeat, whole heart Cardiovascular and Thoracic CT, based on the Company’s proprietary Stereo CT technology. The company is part of a long tradition of medical imaging innovation that began in Israel in the 1970s. Among Arineta’s scientists are the inventors of the multi-slice spiral CT technology, still the cornerstone of CT imaging, and several other key technologies.
