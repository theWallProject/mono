Our Story

It All Started With a Goal...

Back in 2007, Pavel Israelsky set out on a mission – to help digital marketers and managers in organizations. No fancy funding, just a determination to make a difference. Despite all odds, his blog “AskPavel” struck a chord, attracting over 15K readers. Then, in 2013, Angora Media was born with the same goal. Fast forward over a decade, and we’re still at it – supporting leading brands, making their marketing dreams a reality.
