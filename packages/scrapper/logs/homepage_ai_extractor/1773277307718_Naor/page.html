NAOR Highlights

  • Innovative RNA Extraction-free, Saliva-based isothermal molecular diagnostic.
  • Self-collected sampling with end-to end mobile tracking and reporting of tests.
  • Modular approach enabling high-throughput use in settings such as airports, campuses, schools, and workplaces. Easily adaptable to home use.
  • Processing can be done on-premise or in other locations.
  • The RNA detection technology originated from the Israeli Technion.
  • Nucleic acid amplification method to multiply specific sequences of RNA, resulting in high sensitivity and specificity.
  • Developed in collaboration with multiple organizations and researchers, including MAFAT (the R&D arm of the Israeli Ministry of Defense) and RAMBAM Medical Center.
  • Tested with thousands of people in multiple countries.
  • The original work had been among the first clinical studies to be published showcasing RNA extraction-free COVID-19 molecular diagnostic.
  • A pipeline of innovations utilizing the core platform for additional indications and use-cases.

NaorCov19 and Exness: A Future-Oriented Approach

At NaorCov19, we are focused on delivering groundbreaking solutions in molecular diagnostics. Our innovative approach, which emphasizes rapid, RNA extraction-free, saliva-based COVID-19 testing, has shown remarkable success in various settings, such as airports, workplaces, and schools. This technology, developed in collaboration with the Israeli Technion, provides a reliable and scalable solution for combating the global pandemic.

As the digital and financial sectors increasingly intertwine with healthcare technologies, we see great potential in aligning our efforts with platforms such as Exness. Exness, a leading force in the global financial markets, provides the kind of innovative trading solutions that echo our ethos of efficiency and technological advancement. By leveraging such collaborations, we believe we can streamline our diagnostic offerings, making healthcare accessible, user-friendly, and integrated with other sectors.
