Securing the Edge:
From Growing Risk to Immediate Protection

Organizations face a rapidly expanding threat landscape as the edge becomes increasingly central to enterprise operations. Countless edge devices and applications are now deployed both within the IT perimeter and in exposed, out-of-perimeter environments, creating a broader more vulnerable attack surface.

This evolution introduces significant challenges: ensuring compliance with data privacy and cybersecurity regulations, safeguarding sensitive data and credentials, and preventing service disruptions or AI model poisoning at the edge. In this environment, the risks, from lateral movement and downtime to data breaches, can severely impact operations and trust.

With ShieldEdge, enterprises and SMBs gain immediate visibility, security, and compliance across their connected edge networks, without disrupting existing operations or requiring any deployment effort.

PLATFORM HIGHLIGHTS

Enterprise-Grade AI Security for the Connected Edge

ShieldEdge agent-less cybersecurity AI SaaS/cloud platform delivers businesses with operational visibility, threat management, and compliance, across any IoT / modern OT / IT connected device or application, at a scale of billions.

With over 1 million connected devices secured, ShieldEdge protects IP-based networks across key sectors such as payments, transportation, energy, healthcare, enterprise IT, office IoT, smart cities, government, and defense.

Powered by an agentic AI security expert, patented Coreset-AI technology, and the industry-first NVIDIA Morpheus-based AI edge security solution, the platform analyzes device-to-cloud network traffic statistics (metadata) to detect early signs of unknown cyberattacks and operational anomalies. It automatically safeguards devices’ sensitive data, operations, and critical services, while ensuring regulatory compliance.

The multi-tenant software platform is available as a SaaS service or can be deployed on any customer’s public or private cloud. Enterprises and SMBs can effortlessly onboard to the service within 1 day, with no device installation, network changes, integration, or configuration required.

Asset Intelligence

Get full visibility into your connected edge network, including asset risk, network behavior, link visualization maps, geo maps, usage trends, and Gen-AI-driven security and operational network-wide insights.

AI Threat Management

Protect your devices and prevent damage using multiple detection engines, including ML/DL-based network anomaly detection, 100+ threat intelligence feeds, and 30+ threat detection auto-configured rules.

Security & Privacy Compliance

Comply with data privacy and cybersecurity regulations, such as GDPR, CCPA, NIST 2.0, NIS2, and UN R155, with continuous edge device and network monitoring combined with automated reporting.
