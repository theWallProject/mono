CREATING OPPORTUNITIES FOR EARLY INTERVENTION

With truly accurate predictions of patient deterioration, up to eight hours before patient monitoring systems would suspect, CLEW gives hospitals and health systems the opportunity for early intervention. This leads to decreases in mortality and reductions in complications and readmissions.
