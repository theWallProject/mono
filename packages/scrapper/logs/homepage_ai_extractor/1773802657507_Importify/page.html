Frequently asked questions

What is Importify?

Imagine never worrying about inventory again. Importify is your all-in-one dropshipping assistant: it helps you find hot products, import them with one click, and automate order fulfillment—no upfront costs, no stock to manage.

How do I get started with Importify?

Ready to dive in?

Choose your platform. We support Shopify, Wix, WooCommerce, and Jumpseller. Install the app. Head to your platform's App Market, search "Importify," and hit "Add." Start your free trial. You'll instantly see trending products and import buttons in your browser. Easy, right? Let's explore this together.

Does Importify come with a trial?

Yes, all Importify plans include a 1-day free trial. You can test all features before committing, and cancel anytime if it's not the right fit for your business.

Can I use Importify as a beginner?

Importify is designed to be beginner-friendly. You don't need any prior experience with dropshipping. Our platform guides you step-by-step, from finding products to fulfilling orders.

Do you offer full-scale order management?

Yes, especially with our Gold plan which offers full automation for AliExpress orders. You can manage all your orders in one place.

Can I cancel anytime?

There's zero commitment—you're free to pause or stop your subscription whenever you want.

Can I manage more than one store?

Yes! you can use Importify on multiple online stores, but each store requires a separate subscription.

How does finding products work?

Importify makes product discovery simple. Browse any of our 30+ supported marketplaces, and when you find a product you like, just click the 'ADD' button that appears in your browser. The product details, images, and variants will be instantly imported to your store.

How much do I pay after a trial?

After your trial ends, you can choose from these plans:

  • Basic: $14.95/mo (or $11.95/mo billed yearly)
  • Premium: $27.95/mo (or $22.95/mo billed yearly)
  • Gold: $37.95/mo (or $29.95/mo billed yearly)

Billing starts only once your trial expires, and you can change or cancel at any time.

Which websites can I import products from?

Using Importify, you can import products from marketplace such as Aliexpress, Alibaba.com, Etsy.com, Banggood, Dhgate, Walmart, Taobao and 1688. For the full list, visit https://importify.com/suppliers
