About Career Israel

Career Israel enables you to jumpstart your career and enhance your resume on short or long-term internship programs. Students, graduates, and career changers from across the globe have the opportunity to network and build professional skills at leading companies and organizations in Israel. Our team supports you in the internship placement process and provides housing, Hebrew learning programs, trips, and the chance to fully immerse in Israeli life.
