Welcome to The Ilan & Gavish Company

The Ilan & Gavish Company Ltd. was established in 1973. The Company provides solutions for the supply of components, planning, consulting and implementation of automation and controls and profiles systems for a wide spectrum of industrial sectors. The Ilan & Gavish Company has three branches located in Petah Tikvah, Yokneam Illit and Ashdod. In addition, the Company has two production centers located in Yokneam Illit and Petah Tikva. The Company is comprised of four departments: Automation, Control and Robotics, Aluminum Profiles and Linear Motion, and a plant for production of non-standard equipment.
