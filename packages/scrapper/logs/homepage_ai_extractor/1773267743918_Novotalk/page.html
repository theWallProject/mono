Why Novotalk?

Here are the key advantages Novotalk offers:

It works

All our programs are based on the clinically-validated methods and on the experience of top clinicians and feedback we got from patients. Want proof? Take a look at the testimonials – they’re all from real people like you.

Learn anytime, anywhere

No need to go anywhere or visit a speech clinician. Accessible on your mobile and web, you can work on relieving your stuttering whenever it is convenient for you, 24/7.

It’s personalized to you

As you practice, our intelligent technology (Emily’s “brain”) analyzes your specific speech patterns and gives you immediate, actionable and personalized feedback. Additionally, the structure of the program, the types of exercises, and the emphasis all adapt based on your speech performance. Pretty cool, right?

What do you get?

Emily: Your own dedicated virtual clinician

Always staying current with the latest science, Emily continuously learns your unique patterns and adjusts treatment to your needs. Always there. Always patient.

Clinical Support: A network of speech clinicians

Working with Emily involves a networked team of highly skilled and experienced speech clinicians who supervise the programs and intervene when necessary to offer advice, mentoring, tips, or any other support you might need.

Emotional Support: From people who understand

The program provides dedicated, validated emotional support tools offered by our caring, trained staff. Emotions might arise during the program as you practice the techniques and incorporate them into your daily life — we provide assistance to help you manage them. Feel confident in your progress and everyday life.

Community: You are not alone

Novotalk has created an internal social network for people with similar conditions to share, practice together, and connect with each other.
