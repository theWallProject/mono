Heart failure remains one of the largest unmet needs in medicine, affecting 6.7 million people in the United States and 64 million worldwide. Patients face a relentless cycle of hospital readmissions, with 25 percent readmitted within 30 days of discharge.

Heart failure is the leading cause of hospitalization among U.S. Medicare beneficiaries, creating an economic burden exceeding $30 billion annually and projected to reach $70 billion by 2030.

Levron Medical introduces a novel and fresh approach to the treatment of Heart Failure, with elegance and simplicity like no other.
