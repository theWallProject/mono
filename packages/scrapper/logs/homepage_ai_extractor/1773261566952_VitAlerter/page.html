Proactive Care Benefits

Fall Prevention

The system provides early warnings on bed exit to prevent falls before they happen

Posture change

VITALERTER tracks bed-time and turn movements and provides real-time turn reminders and reports

Respiration rate

Continuously monitors respiratory rate trends and alerts the caregivers on duty when relevant events occur

Heart rate

Monitors heart-rate trends in real-time and alerts when relevant events occur

Improve Sleep Quality

Tracks movements and avoid useless turns by caregivers during sleep time

Body temperature

Continuously monitor body temperature. Alerts in situations of abnormal trends

SPo2 trends

Continuously monitor SpO2 levels and alerts in case of low oxygen levels

 

Simplicity By Design

VITALERTER is designed to get you from zero to continues monitoring in just a few hours

  • Seamless & Low Maintenance

    Positioned under the bed/chair with no loss of comfort.
    Powered by mains or long living batteries.

  • Wireless

    Connects via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi to standard or existing network infrastructure

  • Secure Cloud

    The platform is offered in secure cloud service, so there is no need for expensive IT investments. On premise deployments are also optional.

  • Integrated

    The platform integrates with the most common nursing home management software, so settings and monitor data are synced automatically.

  • Plug-and-play

    The sensors can be connected to your nurse-call infrastructure and will activate alarms in the same way as your existing call buttons.
