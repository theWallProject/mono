OUR MISSION

Our patented technology harnesses the wisdom of nature to develop and manufacture next generation cell-based products across a range of industries.

Our cutting-edge technology speaks the native language of cells, understanding at a microscopic level how cells grow and expand. Our platforms are then able to harness cells to develop the products and solutions of the future.

READ MORE

OUR TECHNOLOGY

Pluri’s three-dimensional (3D) cell expansion platforms grow cells in an efficient and cost-effective process. This process can be applied to many different cell types, from plant to animal and human cells.

Pluri is a global leader in 3D cell-expansion technology, developing platforms to manufacture the products and solutions of the future. Since 2003, we’ve been expanding cells to accelerate the development of pharmaceutical product candidates. Our cell expansion technology is not limited to the pharma industry and can potentially create solutions for additional industries: food tech, agri-tech and beyond.
