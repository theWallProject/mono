ABOUT THE AZRIELI GROUP

The Azrieli Group is Israel’s leading real estate company.
We operate in the real estate market by promoting, building, developing, and managing retail centers, shopping malls, office buildings, senior homes, residences and hotels.
We are also active in the income-producing property market abroad, mainly in the U.S. We recently entered the growing data centers market through an investment in a North American company and the acquisition of a Norwegian company operating in this field.
We focus on building experiential mixed-use real estate projects which combine offices, retail, malls, residences and hotels.

our properties

Malls and retail centers

Starting with Israel’s first-ever mall, the Azrieli Group has continued to develop, build, acquire, lease-out and manage premier retail centers and shopping malls across Israel. Management services are provided either directly by the Group and/or through dedicated Group-owned management companies, at the highest international standard and with uncompromising professionalism.

Office buildings

In this area of business, we engage in the development, acquisition, leasing-out, management and maintenance of office towers and business centers. Many of the Group’s income-producing properties are multi-use projects which integrate retail space and offices. Our office towers are populated by leading local and global companies from a variety of business areas.

Palace senior housing

We develop, build, operate and manage top-tier senior homes, featuring high-quality finishings and luxury services. The Group owns four senior homes designed for independent living, which also offer medical and assisted living facilities as part of the range of services available to residents.

Data centers

The rapidly growing global demand for data storage has led to a surge in the data center industry. As part of our strategic efforts to enter areas of business synergetic to income-producing property, we have made a huge investment in this industry through the Norwegian company Green Mountain, which provides services to the world’s largest cloud computing companies.

Hotels

We are developing several hotels as part of mixed-use projects. As part of the expansion of the Azrieli Center Tel Aviv, the Spiral Tower, set to be the tallest in Israel, is a mixed-use development that will include a hotel. In the Azrieli Modi’in Center, West Complex, the city’s first hotel has opened, featuring 85 rooms and suites. In addition, the Mount Zion Hotel in Jerusalem is currently undergoing renovation and construction.

Rental housing

In the heart of the business metropolis of the most vibrant city in the world, a new standard of long-term rental housing has emerged – Azrieli Town Tel Aviv – featuring new, beautifully designed and furnished apartments, as well as a variety of distinctive services for more comfortable living, all at the Azrieli Group’s high standards of construction, reliability, and responsibility. Rental housing available in Azrieli Town Tel Aviv, and now also in the new Azrieli Center Modi’in!

key figures
1

5 Million Monthly Visitors

five million monthly visitors frequent our properties. The Azrieli experience is enjoyed throughout our properties – in our landmark office spaces, shopping malls, senior homes, data centers, mixed-use properties, hotels, and long-term rental residences, both across Israel and abroad.
2

GLA OF 1.4 MILLION SQM

Our properties for rent span 1.4 million sqm, and include shopping malls, offices, senior homes, data centers, mixed-use properties, hotels, and long-term rental residences across Israel and abroad. All of our properties are managed in-house, allowing us to offer the highest standards for our tenants and visitors.
3

DEVELOPMENT

Our properties currently under development include seventeen projects spanning 1 million sqm, the most prominent of which is the Spiral Tower, a mixed-use tower and mall spread out over 13,000 sqm at the tower's base, which will form an extension of the Azrieli Mall Tel Aviv. Another standout project under development is Azrieli Holon 3, the largest office and commercial project in Israel.
4

green building standards

Our goal is for 100% of our properties to be certified according to green building standards. 4 of our properties are LEED certified, 9 of our in-development projects are planned to be LEED certified, and 3 of our in-development projects are planned to be Israeli standard 5281- certified

corporate responsibility

We aim to establish the Azrieli Group and its brand to be a model for commitment to the environment and the community, both locally and nationally, for all of our stakeholders: our tenants, visitors, residents, employees, suppliers, community, and the environment.

We uphold Zionist values by building and strengthening the national economy, committing to bettering society, and raising the bar in eco-friendly practices.
