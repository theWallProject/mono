Our products

Insights you can trust, at the speed you need

Boost

Boost augments low-incidence samples with statistical precision, delivering robust, reliable data for niche audiences. In a fragmented market, it gives you the confidence to make sharper, more targeted go-to-market decisions.

Learn more

Check

New!

Check uses agentic AI to detect fraud, bots, and inattentive responses—then lets you review flagged cases before cleaning. It blends automation with control, protecting data integrity while keeping you in command.

Learn more

Impute

Beta

Impute fills missing survey responses using AI-driven modeling, turning partial datasets into complete, analysis-ready insights. As attention spans shrink and surveys shorten, it uncovers the full story without more fieldwork.

Twins

Coming soon

Fairgen's simulated audiences compress research timelines from months to days by replacing traditional research with fully synthetic respondents. Built on premium behavioral data, they deliver real-world accuracy without the cost or delay. Test unlimited ideas, validate strategy faster, and reduce go-to-market risk.
