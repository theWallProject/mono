Frequently Asked Questions

How does Contact Sync Work? Expand

Basic Plan: Contacts+ pulls changes once per day from your accounts like Google, iCloud, and Exchange/Office 365.

Premium and Team Plan: Changes are pulled from your account in real-time.  Under Premium plans, any changes made in Contacts+ will immediately push back to connected accounts.

For the Basic plan, changes will push back once per day. Pricing 

What are Contact Updates? Expand

Contacts+ searches for publicly available information to add to your contacts, including photos, job titles, locations, social media profiles, and more. Basic accounts receive updates each month. Premium accounts receive updates each day.

How does Business Card Transcription Work? Expand

Contacts+ uses AI to transcribe business cards in approximately 15–20 minutes, adding accurate contact information to your address book.
