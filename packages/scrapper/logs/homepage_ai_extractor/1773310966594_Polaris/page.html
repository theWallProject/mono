OUR STORY

During the Second Lebanon War in 2006, CEO/ CO Founder Asaf
Picciotto and CTO/ CO Founder Asaf Miller, then members of an elite
IDF unit, realized the need for enhanced protection against enemy
thermal imaging and night-vision equipment. Leveraging their
combat experience, they set out to develop the ultimate survivability
technologies.

Polaris Solutions LTD was founded in 2010 and since then has
radically transformed the field of advanced survivability solutions.

OUR STORY

During the Second Lebanon War in 2006, CEO/ CO Founder Asaf Picciotto and CTO/ CO Founder Asaf Miller, then members of an elite IDF unit, realized the need for enhanced protection against enemy thermal imaging and night-vision equipment. Leveraging their combat experience, they set out to develop the ultimate survivability technologies. Polaris Solutions LTD was founded in 2010 and since then has radically transformed the field of advanced survivability solutions.
We pride ourselves on our collaborations with top industry entities like ELTA Systems LTD and Stolero Group. These partnerships enable us to expand our range of technological solutions, ensuring we remain at the forefront of defense technology.
