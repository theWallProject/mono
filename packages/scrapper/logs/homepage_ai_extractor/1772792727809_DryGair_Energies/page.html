DryGair Main Features

Large-Scale Dehumidification for Horticulture

DryGair provides high water extraction rates, using efficient, condensation-based dehumidification. Designed for optimal performance under greenhouse and grow room conditions.

Read More
Patented Air Circulation for Uniform Climate

DryGair’s patented air circulation module and powerful fans spread the treated air in all directions, 360°, to ensure homogenous, optimal climate conditions throughout your growing facility.

Read More
Integrated Heating & Cooling - All-In-One

DryGair units can be equipped with a heating and cooling system to provide additional temperature control functions. Controlling humidity and temperature together provides complete and efficient control over climate conditions.

Save Energy

DryGair is designed for maximum energy efficiency. Save as much as 50% on energy, compared to traditional humidity control methods.

Uniform Climate

The patented air circulation module disperses dry air to all corners of the growing space. Air circulation ensures a uniform climate, providing all plants with the ideal conditions.

Developed by climate and agronomy experts, DryGair provides energy-saving humidity control exclusively for horticulture.
