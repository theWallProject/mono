What is DormoVision X™?

DormoVision X™ is an FDA-cleared, AI powered, next-generation polysomnography platform that captures comprehensive Type I, II, and III sleep study data at home, matching the precision of gold-standard in-lab testing. Discover seamless online management of unlimited in-lab and home PSG studies.

It offers real-time streaming of high-quality physiological data, measuring 29 direct channels and 9 derived signals, including EEG, EOG, Chin EMG, SpO₂, Heart Rate, Nasal/Oral Airflow, Respiratory Effort, Snoring/Breathing Sounds, Leg EMG, and Body/Head Position.

Compatible With All Sleep Study Types (1,2,3)

DormoVision X™ supports Type I–III sleep studies in both home and in-lab settings, and is optimized for seamless integration with new hPSG CPT codes, ensuring compliance and smoother reimbursement.

Unlimited Multi-Night Studies

Capture richer insights with unlimited multi-night studies at home or in-lab, revealing night-to-night variability for more accurate diagnoses and personalized care – without added complexity or cost.

Scalable, Efficient, and Cost-Effective

Wireless, cloud-based, and ready to scale. A usage-based model removes upfront costs, and centralized management streamlines workflows across unlimited locations.

Improved Patient Experience

A comfortable, wireless experience at home or in-lab. Soft materials and child-friendly designs improve compliance, resulting in more positive experiences and more reliable study outcomes.
