FAQ

Your questions answered

Do I need separate phone or computer?
No, ElliQ is an all-in-one voice activated device that can communicate with you independently
Is it hard to set up?
Not at all. ElliQ arrives pre-configured. Just plug it in and connect to Wifi. If there are any issues, our support is there for you.
Can more than one person use ElliQ?
ElliQ is designed and personalized for one older adult, but ElliQ can interact with other people in the household too.
Are my conversations with ElliQ safe and private?
ElliQ adheres to the highest privacy and data standards, ensuring users’ information is always safe and protected.
Is the AI safe and private?
Guided by a state-of-the-art code of ethics, ElliQ builds trust and integrity into every interaction
Can ElliQ help during emergencies?
ElliQ is not a medical device, but encourages members to contact their caregiver and trusted contacts.
How long will it take for my ElliQ device to ship after I place an order?
Once your order is confirmed, your ElliQ device will typically ship within 3 to 5 business days. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking information once it has shipped.
