Infiniplex Technology

We use qPCR (quantitative Real Time PCR) technology and novel primers that selectively amplify mutated DNA fragments enables the detection of dozens of mutations in various cancer types in a single test. Those primes are being designed by a propriety AI-based system  that was developed by our multidisciplinary teams of scientists and software engineers.

Infiniplex Product

Our product utilizes DNA that freely circulates in the bloodstream (cell-free DNA, cfDNA) as a non-invasive and real-time biomarker of cancer.  Using a multiplexing method, it can identify dozens of mutations in various cancer types in a single test.
It provides the solution for increasing efficiency and specificity to detect mutations in cfDNA and selectively amplify the mutated DNA fragment, identifying its presence in the sample.
