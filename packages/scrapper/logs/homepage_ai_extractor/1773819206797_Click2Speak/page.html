The Story of Gal

Gal Sont

Click2Speak was born from the vision of Gal Sont, a developer who refused to let ALS limit his ability to create and connect. When Gal found existing communication tools too slow and expensive, he decided to build a better solution himself.

By integrating advanced prediction technology into a high performance on screen keyboard, he created a tool that allows users to type faster and more accurately using only a mouse, head tracker, or eye gaze system.

Gal's journey was about more than just software. It was about reclaiming independence. Today, we carry his mission forward by providing a professional communication bridge for anyone facing physical challenges, ensuring their voices are always heard.
