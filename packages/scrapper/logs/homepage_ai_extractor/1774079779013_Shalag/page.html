Weaving trust,
bonding excellence.

 

Shalag Nonwovens is a world leader in Carded Thermo Bonded and Air Through Bonded Nonwoven technologies.
With more than 40 years of experience and proven expertise, Shalag has developed a global reputation as cutting-edge developer and producer of numerous innovative Nonwovens marketed all over the world.

01
ABOUT SHALAG

Shalag’s nonwovens and materials are the product of 40 years of experience and a team of dedicated and passionate people.

02
INNOVATION

We put emphasis on developing products that offer better performance, comfort, and durability by understanding the unique properties of our materials and how they can be tailored to meet your unique requirements.

03
TECHNOLOGIES

We use Air Through bonded, Carded Thermo Bonded and a variety of other technologies in order to create products that have specific and unique properties tailored for each specific application.

04
SUSTAINABILITY

We recognize the importance of promoting social equity and well-being for all individuals as fundamental for achieving a sustainable future.
