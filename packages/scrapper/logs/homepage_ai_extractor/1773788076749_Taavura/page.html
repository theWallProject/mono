Taavura Group is one of Israel’s leading companies in the fields of transportation, logistics, infrastructure, energy, and services. With more than 76 years of activity, the group stands at the forefront of industry and innovation.

Over the years, Taavura has grown and expanded, building a solid reputation and strong market position, thanks to its dedicated employees. The group’s success is driven by a customer-centric approach, reliable service, pursuit of excellence, and the highest standards of professionalism.

Since its establishment, Taavura has been a pioneer in transportation and earthworks in Israel, later expanding into the import of trucks, buses, heavy equipment, and advanced logistics services.

Today, Taavura has also become a major international player, with a significant share of its operations focused on the import of passenger cars and light commercial vehicles to Central Europe. In parallel, the group is active in infrastructure, energy, and environmental projects, while fostering innovation across all its divisions through its dedicated Innovation Center.

Combining the strength of its legacy with a forward-looking vision, the group continues to lead growth and success across multiple sectors, while unlocking unique opportunities for the future. Read more>
