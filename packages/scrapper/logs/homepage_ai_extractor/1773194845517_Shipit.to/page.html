2. THE QUALITY OF TRANSPARENCY OR PURITY.

Making Logistics Crystal Clear

Our bottom-up approach turns the most basic building blocks of your supply chain – your cargo – from passive to interactive. When deployed across your supply chain this gives you access to insights previously unavailable to your organisation.


Track and Trace

For businesses with a high volume of shipping whose customers require 24/7 visibility, typically: online stores (e-commerce), postal operators, and couriers.


Cargo Monitoring

For businesses shipping expensive, sensitive, unique or time-critical cargo, typically: luxury brands, time-critical providers, freight forwarders and exporters/importers.


Supply Chain Analytics

For businesses with an extensive supply chain & inbound logistics, typically: logistics warehouses and manufacturers.
