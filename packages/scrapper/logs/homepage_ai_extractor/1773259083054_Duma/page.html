Instruments of Light
for Light Measurements

Discover the Total Station Autocollimator — an “all for one and one for all” solution that unites beam profiling, alignment, and optical metrology into a single compact system, replacing a full optical lab and ready for mass-production environments.

Duma Optronics line-up includes laser beam positioning, beam divergence measurement, M2, multi-wavelength including near-IR, laser beam profiling, high power laser beam profiling, alignment and all in one multi-tasking Total Station Autocollimator.

Electronic Autocollimators

Detects minute optical reflections and can also measure laser beam direction and divergence in specialized versions.

Alignment Sensing

Simultaneous position and angle measurements for various applications.
AlignMeter: Measures both position and angle.
AngleMeter: Measures incoming angles only.

Position
Sensing

Measures optical beam position on a detector surface and provides absolute power readings.

Beam
Profiling

Measures beam sizes from <0.5µm to 15mm with power levels from picoWatts to several Watts. Special versions are available for solar energy applications.

High Power
Beam Analysis

Measures beams up to 8 kWatt, including profiles, beam size, position, and real-time power. Wavelength range: 190–1550 nm and beyond.

Our Core Solutions

High-Power Laser Beam Profiling

Safely profile industrial lasers at unprecedented power levels:

  • Current solutions measure beams up to 30 kW average power.
  • Next-generation systems under development extend capability to 150 kW for heavy industry and defense-grade applications.
 

Digital Autocollimators & Precision Alignment

Sub-arcsecond electronic autocollimators and micro-aligner systems for angular alignment, tilt control, and optical positioning.

Beam Divergence & M² Measurement

Integrated solutions for divergence analysis and M² determination, ensuring precise laser characterization for quality and compliance.

Multi-Wavelength & Infrared Beam Profiling

Broadband coverage from UV through visible to IR (SWIR, MWIR, LWIR) — enabling multi-wavelength beam diagnostics without changing systems.

 

Proprietary Know-How & Unique Advantage

At Duma Optronics, innovation is built on exclusive proprietary designs, patented methods, and advanced algorithms developed over decades.

Our flagship Total Station is a multi-tasking solution that combines the capabilities of several standalone instruments — beam profiler, digital autocollimator, divergence analyzer, M² meter, and alignment system — into one integrated device.

  • Full Optical Laboratory Replacement – perform the work of multiple instruments in one compact platform.
  • Mass Production Ready – automated operation, repeatable results, and robust hardware for high-throughput manufacturing lines.
  • Multi-Wavelength Capability – cover UV to IR in a single solution.
  • High-Power Ready – beam profilers designed for 30 kW continuous lasers, with next-generation 150 kW systems in development.
  • Real-Time Micro-Alignment – motorized stages and feedback ensure precise and dynamic control.
 

Featured Products

  • Total Station Beam Analysis System
    Multi-function instrument replacing multiple lab devices with integrated beam profiling, autocollimation, alignment, divergence, and M² analysis.
  • High-Power Beam Profiler
    Industrial-grade system for real-time profiling of lasers up to 30 kW, with development paths to 150 kW.
  • Digital Autocollimator + Micro-Aligner
    Compact system for precise tilt measurement and micro-scale optical alignment.
 

Applications

  • Laser Welding & Additive Manufacturing – stabilize and monitor high-power beams in production lines.
  • Fiber Coupling & Delivery Systems – optimize alignment and reduce losses.
  • Micromachining & Processing – achieve precise divergence and spot size control.
  • Aerospace & Defense – autocollimation and beam stabilization for high-energy laser systems.
  • Research Laboratories – consolidate multiple instruments into one advanced platform.
