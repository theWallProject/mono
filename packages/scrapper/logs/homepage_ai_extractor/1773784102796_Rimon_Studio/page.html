Work. Play. Learn.

Rimon Studio, a division of Dweck Consultants, creates games, toys, and stories that connect the digital and physical worlds—delivering meaningful experiences for players and users alike.

What We Do

At Rimon Studio, we support a wide range of projects—from educational games to interactive exhibitions—bringing our unique perspective and expertise to each one.

Our goal isn’t just to create outstanding play experiences, but to make a meaningful impact on society and the world. We harness cutting-edge technologies—from AR to IoT—to develop innovative products that bridge the gap between how we play and how we learn.
