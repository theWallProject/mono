WELCOME....... WE MEAN IT


 By nature, as Palestinians we are warm people. If our first dealing is business like, our second is friendly and sincere. Try us once, and you become our ambassador!! This is how we developed our customer base.

Our agency has about 95 years of experience in the travel industry distributed among management and staff. It goes back to 1955 when our General Manager, Wassef Daher, joined Swissair. Nine years later he moved to Scandinavian Airlines (SAS). He remained with SAS until 1995 when he was offered its General Sales Agency (GSA) for the area. In this same year our agency was offered another airline, Iberia (Airlines of Spain).

The agency kept its professional experienced SAS staff and hired more to keep up with the market demand.

The Sales Manager, Yusef Daher, joined the agency with 10 years of experience in tourism and so started the Tourism Department.

This is our 10th year. We today offer a range of services from airline tickets to full travel and tour packages in the Middle East area and Spain.
