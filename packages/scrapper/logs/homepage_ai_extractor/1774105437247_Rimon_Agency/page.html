Helen Wexler is an award winning "archi-tech", working at the intersection of architecture and technology. Helen holds a degree in Architecture from the Bezalel Academy of Art and design and has recently received an award from NASA for her design of Martian housing. After working as a technology analyst for a Tel Aviv based investment firm, she moved from the private sector to the public one. There she currently manages the Jnext program at the Jerusalem Development Authority, where she does economic development to support startups and technology companies in the city of Jerusalem.

Oded Turgeman

 Founder of Rimon, a consulting firm that bridges entertainment and tech, with strong ties to Hollywood, Israel and China. Oded also serves as the N. American Director of Business Development for the Jerusalem Development Authority, and aids with government affairs for studios and production companies considering physical production in Israel.  He is President of The OR, a production company focused on identifying, developing, and creating IP for animation with the potential to become universal mega-brands. He is a graduate of The AFI, and the Sam Spiegel Film &T elevision School in Israel.

Guy Ben-Dov

Guy is an interactive entertainment serial entrepreneur with over 25 years of experience. Guy works with innovative teams in fortune 500 companies as well as startups all around the world bridging interactive entertainment and new platforms. Through studios and agencies Guy led, he works with clients such as Oculus, Google, Intel, Rovio, DeNA, LeapMotion and Primesense. Guy co founded Side-kick games, Double Fusion, a leader in the in-game advertising space, Cellular Magic and Click! Online, a casual games studio. In 2007, Guy co-founded GameIS, the Israeli game developers association and serves as its chairman.
