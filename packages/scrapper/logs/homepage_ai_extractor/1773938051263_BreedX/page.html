BREEDX

ABOUT US

Breedx is a global specialty vegetable development company dedicated to developing, promoting, and commercializing specialty vegetable varieties that deliver unique consumer benefits. We focus on creating high yielding premium products that add value to growers, to retailers and to consumers— driving innovation, quality and sustainability throughout the fresh produce supply chain.

More About Our Plant Breeding Company

OUR PRODUCTS

PEPPERS

Pepperito®, our seedless and snack-size sweet pepper (grown from seeds), in three colors (red, orange, yellow) offers high yields, unbeatable convenience, crunch and flavor — without the hassle of seeds. It’s perfect for snacking, stuffing, or cooking, delivering a low waste convenient experience for consumers and a differentiated premium product for consumers, growers and retailers.
Our Pepper Varieties

OUR PRODUCTS

TOMATOES

Our specialty tomatoes bring together vibrant colors, diverse shapes and sizes, and are rich in flavors — from Mini-Plum to Midi-Plum and Roma-type tomatoes to unique bi-colored varieties under our Specialties. Our varieties are bred for exceptional taste, texture and visual appeal, plus resistance to TOBRFV and long shelf-life. A tasty treat, the seeds of which are produced under GSPP certification for consumers around the world.
