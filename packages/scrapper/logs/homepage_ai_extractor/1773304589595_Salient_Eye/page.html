SALIENT EYE – SECURITY AT HAND

Home security should be a right, not a privilege. That’s why Salient Eye enables anyone to turn any smartphone or tablet into a portable burglar alarm system. The world’s most affordable security camera may already be in your drawer.

TURN A SMARTPHONE INTO A HOME SECURITY ALARM SYSTEM

Salient Eye turns your spare smartphone or tablet into a motion detector burglar alarm system. The app uses the device’s camera as a motion sensor, capturing photos of intruders and any suspicious activity and instantly sends them to you. You can set the app to also sound a loud alarm when motion is detected.

Salient Eye is offered for free on Android, and is only $1.99 on iOS devices (iPhone or iPad).



REMOTE CONTROL APP

With “SalientEye Remote Control” app, you can arm and disarm Salient Eye remotely without needing to touch or be near the device it runs on.
You can leave a device with Salient Eye security system installed at the
position you want, connected to charger and no longer touch it. You can
control the alarm from your primary device.
Main features include:

  1. Push notifications on motion detection directly to your primary phone
  2. Remotely arming and disarming Salient Eye other devices
  3. Taking live pictures from Salient Eye on the other devices
  4. Browsing your user’s motion event pages and pictures
