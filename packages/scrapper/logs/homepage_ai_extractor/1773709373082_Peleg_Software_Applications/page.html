Who we are

Peleg develops advance back-office systems for tour operators and travel agencies since 1983.
After it acquired many years of experience it became specialized in giving innovative and advanced options to travel agencies -
wholesales and retails.

Flights Seats Control

The flight management module allows the agency easy and fully manage of seats across individual flights or a series of flights. The module includes full monitoring and control (Seats Control) where it's possible to monitor the flight and sales status in general, by class / department, and by partners.
Also, the module includes an advanced prices rates management system for seats, extras, luggage, prices per agency and interfaces to the various distribution system.
Read more

Hotel Rooms Management

The hotel module allows the agency to fully room inventory management from the level of quantity management to the level of passenger assignment to specific rooms. Allocation management can be done according to a variety of parameters - room types, suppliers, agents / organizations, etc, while there is control and release (return) of rooms automatically.
The module also supports the management of price lists / agreements with a wide range of functions and conditions according with the needs of each agency, including the management of promotional / campaigns and distribution to the various websites.
In addition, there is the ability for online reservations to hotels through interfaces and even receive booking from various distribution systems.

Read more

Reservation Management

The reservation management system includes full wholesale booking management (home products) and retail booking (purchase from other suppliers) including automatic pricing system from price lists, manual pricing, discounts, complete document management, invoices, receipts, cost subsidy, and the etc.
Also, the system enables to create vouchers for suppliers, on the one hand, and the manual / automatic import of agencies vouchers, on the other hand, while it automatically releasing the travel documents.
Each order has an archive of documents that allows for full organization and documentation, in addition to documenting all the changes that have taken place in the order.
Also, the reservation module allow to receive external booking (Pnr2Docket) from the GDS systems (Amadeus, Sibre, Wordspan), websites, online vendor interfaces, LDS (Alpha, Sibre) and more.
Read more

Package Management

The package management module provides a solution for managing, selling and distributing all types of packages - vacations, organized, fly and drive, flights only, ski, cruise, events, and more.
In a single package you can manage a large number of flights, from several airlines and at the same time a number of hotels and room levels, on different dates
The module allows dynamic and flexible prices management which can also be exported to various websites and distribution systems. Those prices can be pre-set according to the booking dates and departure dates / seasons, while each price can be set according dynamic parameters of the tour level.

Read more

Cruise Cabins Management

Peleg Systems fully supports the wholesale and retail management of cruise products, from the entire ship management level and the allocations of the various rooms types, including assignment of cabin passengers, to the retail management of the cruise booking documentation in the system.
The system enables companies engaged in the management and sale of cruise products to receive variety of tools which necessary for the proper operation of the cruise department and to receive, in real time, information about the status of inventories, sales, profitability, and more.
Read more

Groups / Organized Tours

Many resources have been invested in the Peleg systems in order to create dedicated module for managing groups and organized tours, both with open and closed registration. We have also created customized reports to monitor the progress of registration for trips, examining and forecasts the profitability at any given moment.
We have also created online systems that distributed the content of trips to information and registration websites.

Read more

FIT / Individuals

All Peleg's system allows each agent to book services quickly and easily with a wide range of additions. The process contain automated modules that shorten the need for the long operational preoccupation with the booking, thus provide him more time to make sales.
The system included B2B and B2C that allow receiving reservations from external systems (PNR2Docket), automatic release of travel documents, independent flight seat selection, personal customer zone access, and more
