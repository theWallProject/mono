STORYTELLING AT ITS BOLDEST

Faraway Road Productions is the global premium content studio founded by award-winning creator, writer and actor Lior Raz and award-winning creator, writer and journalist Avi Issacharoff.  

We believe storytelling transcends borders, inspires and connects.

We focus on high impact, propulsive, character driven narratives, and are committed to bringing authentic voices from across the globe.

TV & FILMS

OFF ROAD

Lior Raz (“Fauda”) and Rotem Sela (“The Baker & The Beauty”) star in “Off Road,” a Netflix unscripted reality series produced by Faraway Road Productions and Keshet International.The series follows Raz and Sela, two famous Israeli actors who are real-life best friends, across a six-part road trip across Central Asia,while challenging their relationship and their personal beliefs. The six-part series will launch globally on Netflix on July 10.

Trailer

Fauda season 5 sets production start and early 2026 premiere.

The fifth season of Fauda will guest star Melanie Laurent and is directed by Omri Givon (Hostages) and written by Omri Shenhar (Tehran, Magpie). The fifth season will follow the crew two years after October 7th, with the plot straddling Europe and Israel.

Trailer

GHOSTS OF BEIRUT 

A four-part spy drama based on one of the greatest espionage stories of modern times: the manhunt for Imad Mughniyeh, the elusive Lebanese terrorist who outwitted his adversaries in the CIA and Mossad for over two decades. The limited series features an innovative narrative approach augmented by deep journalistic research and documentary elements.

Trailer

FAUDA

Named as the #8 best international TV show of the decade by The New York Times, Fauda Season 4 launched globally on Netflix in January 2023. This action-packed portrait about the impacts of terrorism on the lives of secret service agents, their colleagues, and family members, offers adventurous, nail-biting stories that keep viewers asking for more.

Trailer

HIT & RUN

Starring Lior Raz and Sanaa Latham, a happily married man’s life is turned upside down when his wife is killed in a mysterious hit-and-run accident in Tel Aviv. Created by Avi Issacharoff and Lior Raz and inspired by actual events, the series follows a highstakes global operation by the CIA and Israel’s central intelligence agency Mossad to track down and kill one of the world’s most wanted terrorists
