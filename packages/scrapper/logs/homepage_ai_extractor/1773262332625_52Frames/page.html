How it Works

  • 1. Look!

    "Frame your world" isn't just our tagline. Carve out a slice of time each week to really look around you. You will start to see your world differently.

  • 2. Shoot!

    I mean, we are a photography project. Everyone has the same 7 days for the challenge. The point is to press that shutter button each week, and TAKE THAT PHOTO.

  • 3. Submit!

    Share with the world. Be OK with imperfection. Get helpful feedback from our community and give your thoughts on others.

  • 4. Repeat!

    Don't break the chain. Let the consistency of creativity build week after week and discover new creative breakthroughs you never knew you had.
