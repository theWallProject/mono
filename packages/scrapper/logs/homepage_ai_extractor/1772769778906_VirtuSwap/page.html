VirtuSwap has closed its exchange operations

Important Announcement: Closure of Exchange Operations

Dear VirtuSwap Community,

We regret to inform you that VirtuSwap.io has closed its exchange operations.

After much deliberation, we have made the difficult decision to sunset the VirtuSwap platform. We are immensely grateful for the trust, support, and contributions of our users, community members, developers, and especially our liquidity providers.

To facilitate remaining liquidity removals, please contact support@virtuswap.io

Thank you for being part of the VirtuSwap journey. While this chapter is closing, we remain proud of what we built together and grateful for your role in making it possible.

With appreciation,

The VirtuSwap Team
