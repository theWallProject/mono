2key was built on a simple but powerful idea: A SmartLink — a link that could understand how it spread, reward those who shared it, and create a fair, transparent sharing economy for everyone.

Our product was originally developed on the Ethereum network, pioneering new models for incentivized sharing, layered analytics, and smart-contract-based rewards.

However, as the Ethereum ecosystem evolved and costs increased, maintaining the system became increasingly challenging. As a result, the platform is currently offline.

The Next Chapter

The team behind 2key, DcentraLab, is planning to rebuild the product in the future —
with better technology, improved scalability, and renewed commitment to the same vision: empowering people to earn their fair share for sharing information online.

If you’d like to be notified when 2key returns, we’d love to keep you updated.

Stay informed as we prepare for the next version of SmartLinks.
