Donating for Israel

At Give Israel, our primary mission in these challenging times following the recent attack on Israel is to work closely with verified voluntary organizations within the country. Our devoted team of volunteers has meticulously validated each of these organizations to ensure they are credible and effectively supporting the nation through crucial initiatives. These validated groups actively supply soldiers with essential items like boots and warm food, purchase vital equipment for hospitals, and offer comprehensive support to attack survivors — providing food, clothing, essentials, and much-needed mental support.

American Friends of Unit 669
The American Friends of Unit 669 (AFU 669) is a 501 (c) (3) non-profit organization established to support the alumni and welfare of the soldiers of the IDF elite Unit 669—many of whom will be the future leaders of Israeli society. We want to provide the infrastructure to allow alumni of Unit 669 to maximize their humanitarian potential, benefitting themselves, the unit, and all of Israel’s citizens.Our efforts focus on promoting Tikkun Olam, the act of repairing the world, beyond their active-duty service.We are doing our best to keep up the good work of “Tikkun Olam”.

Brothers And Sisters For Israel
Brothers and Sisters for Israel is currently the largest aid organization in Israel. We are a 100% civilian voluntary based operation, so that every dollar donated goes directly and entirely to the actual goods and services that are needed.  Since the onset of this war, we have been running an unprecedented Civil Emergency Headquarters. Our teams collect requirements both bottoms up as well as tops down, to make sure real needs are met. Accordingly, we mobilize critical resources, including food, water, clothing, medical equipment and aid, provide accommodation for displaced families, evacuate those in war zones, locate missing individuals, procure vital equipment, and much more. We are committed to leaving no request unanswered.  Help us help our brothers and sisters

Duvdevan Foundation
The Duvdevan Foundation is the only Foundation that represents graduates of the Duvdevan Undercover Unit of the IDF. The Netflix TV hit show Fauda is based upon the work of this Elite Unit. The Foundation involves 150 alumni volunteering in various departments and with our associates embedded in the Israeli society, as well as two full-time employees. The goal of the Duvdevan Foundation is to be the mainstay of past, present, and future Duvdevan Unit soldiers and supports them in several different areas. We work on providing access to education, empowerment, leadership workshops, coaching, mentoring programs, as well as organized events dedicated to strengthen our Heritage. This Foundation has been listed by federal law, registered as “Friends of Duvdevan.”

Lotar Association
The Counter-Terror Unit of the Israeli Defense Forces. At this critical moment, Lotar, the Counter-Terror Unit of the Israeli Defense Forces, needs our urgent support. Our soldiers face unimaginable challenges and are in dire need of equipment, food, and other resources as they defend Israel and respond to the recent Hamas attack. For those looking for ways to help, your donation to the Lotar Association will directly support our soldiers currently on the front lines defending the people of Israel. Your contribution will go towards providing immediate assistance with: * Equipping and sustaining actively deployed teams * Supporting the families of the unit’s fallen soldiers * Essential care for the unit’s hospitalized soldiers The Lotar Unit is an elite unit that specializes in rapid response to terrorist attacks and hostage intervention. The Lotar Association is a nonprofit organization that is operated by the unit's graduates and functions as a legally registered institution with the Registrar of Associations in Israel. During the current situation, the Association led by Avichai Magen, the CEO, is committed to assisting all the unit’s immediate needs.

Orev Paratrooper Fund
The Orev paratrooper family is a non-profit organization working to preserve the well being of the current and reserved soldiers in the Orev unit - Paratroopers Brigade, alongside their families. The organization aims to send constant support for the soldiers' families and stand closely by bereaved families. During times of peace, the organization works to promise the welfare of the current and reserved Orev Paratrooper soldiers, raise funds and scholarships, build a network of mentorship programs and lectures for reserved soldiers, provide constant job offers and opportunities for the community, initiate projects with the reserved soldiers, and keep an ongoing connection with the Orev Paratrooper society. This past weekend, the IDF has mobilized all reserved soldiers in the unit for emergency duty over the weekend to fight within Israel. However, due to the rapid escalation and the unexpected nature of the attack, the IDF is not able to provide the reserved soldiers with the most basic combat equipment, including clothing, hydration packs, backpacks, flashlights, and bulletproof vests. Therefore, we are raising funds to donate the necessary equipment for our reserved soldiers, who are bravely putting their lives on the line to prevent Hamas from taking additional children / elderly hostages and the brutal murder of Israeli civilians.  100% of donations will go towards food and supplies for soldiers and bereaved families.  Additional information: - The Orev Unit, whose name means "Raven" in English, is an all-volunteer segment of the 'Israeli Defense Forces' Paratrooper Brigade.
