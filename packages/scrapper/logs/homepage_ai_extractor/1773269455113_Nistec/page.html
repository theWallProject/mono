Industry Impact

Our comprehensive services cover the full product lifecycle, from initial design and manufacturing to systems integration, order fulfilment, and after-market support to over 500 clients across multiple sectors. We are a valued ally to top-tier electronics and technology firms in a broad range of industries.

Communications
As a trusted partner in the communications industry, we offer comprehensive design and manufacturing services for carrier- and enterprise-class equipment, ensuring seamless execution and allowing clients to focus on core business activities.
Defense & Aerospace
Advancing global safety through high-reliability electronics systems, we prioritize quality, reliability, and cost-effectiveness, certified by the Israeli Ministry of Defense and Israel Aerospace Industries.
Medical
Manufacturing high-quality medical products for OEMs, our capabilities meet and exceed stringent industry standards. We support the unique demands of medical manufacturing with ISO 13485:2003 certification.
Automotive
Delivering top-tier automotive solutions with a keen focus on quality and performance, our “end-to-end” approach, backed by 16949 IATF certification, helps clients navigate the complexities of the global automotive market.
Agriculture
Leading in manufacturing robust and efficient solutions for the agricultural sector, our high-quality products enhance productivity and sustainability in farming, meeting and exceeding industry standards through advanced technology and rigorous testing.
Clean Tech
Nistec offers tailored electronic manufacturing services for the Clean Tech industry, focusing on energy efficiency, renewable energy, and environmental sustainability. Their expertise includes the design and production of advanced electronic systems that support clean energy technologies, such as solar power, electric vehicles, and energy storage solutions.
