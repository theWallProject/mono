Senior Primary Care, Powered by Relationships

Hopscotch Primary Care is bringing a new level of primary care to rural communities that goes above and beyond, with an initial focus on expanding access and care for Medicare and Medicare/Medicaid patients.

We Deliver Care That Goes Above and Beyond

About Us

Our mission is to transform lives in rural communities through accessible and proactive value-based care. We are committed to delivering exceptional care for seniors, both in our clinics and beyond. We support our patients with more resources, time, and the attention of a full care team.

Learn More

Your Partner in Senior Healthcare

Who We Serve

Hopscotch specializes on serving the needs of patients with Medicare, including most Medicare Advantage plans, Original Medicare Part B, Medicare Supplement or Medigap plans, and Medicare/Medicaid Plans.

At Hopscotch, we know the importance of building a community of care centered around the needs of our patients, and we strive to support our patients, care team members, and other partners within the healthcare community.

Patients

Our patients are at the heart of everything we do. We offer high-quality, comprehensive care for seniors with Medicare or Medicare/Medicaid. Our physicians and care teams focus on building strong relationships, taking the time to get to know you, and listening to your questions and concerns.

Learn More

Partners

We support partnerships that power possibilities. At Hopscotch, we believe in the importance of working with both local and broader groups to ensure patients get the care and support they need, where and when they need it most.

Careers

At Hopscotch Primary Care, we are bringing better access and high-quality care to rural communities across America. To make this happen, we need a world-class team. If you are team-first, mission-driven, and bring skill, passion, and dedication to everything you do, we’d love to meet you.

The Hopscotch Primary Care Difference

Our Values

At Hopscotch Primary Care, our true north is serving our patients and the care teams who serve them. Our culture is what unifies us, underpins everything we do, and ultimately lays the foundation for us to achieve our mission and vision.

We believe that when you invest in culture, commit to values, and hand good people the possibility, great things happen.
