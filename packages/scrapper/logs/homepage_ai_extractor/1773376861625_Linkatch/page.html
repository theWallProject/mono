Awesome Features

Online Employee Recruitment Has Never Been So Easy!

The Linkatch platform is very intuitive and simple to operate, and it is the ultimate method for recruitment campaigns, social media and channels and sourcing through closed communities.

Linkatch allows you to collect and analyze data at the very beginning of the recruitment process, and optimizes recruitment for maximum results.

Why Linkatch?

Leverage the Power of Sharing!

Using WhatsApp, email and any other digital channel, Linkatch allows you to spread the word to your employees and let them share job offers with their friends. This allows you to reach a wide variety of audiences and spread your net wider, so you get a more diversified range of job candidates.

Stand Out

Use Linkatch to make your employer’s brand look unique and attractive. By posting a job offer with Linkatch, you can create a more noticeable advertisement that brands your organization exactly the way you want it.

Better Communication by Leveraging Each and Every Ad

By reading a single job ad, candidates learn a lot about the employer's branding, through the use of images, a description of the company, and the recruiter's personalization of corporate messaging.

Control, Manage, and Measure How Effective Your Process Is

The ability to track and improve the process is available at your fingertips. Linkatch shows you exactly how much you get out of each job post - the number of job candidates, views, and shares. The shares can be by anyone on any media, at any group, and any time.
The recruitment team gets a data report in real time about your published job offers and the candidates you receive, enabling you to adjust and improve the recruitment process as you go.
