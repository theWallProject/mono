Brightman-Gil Real Estate Investments manages a real estate portfolio through private equity funds. Since its establishment in 2013, Brightman-Gil has managed assets, totaling $300 million of assets of management.

We believe that the best way to manage our investments is by having "boots on the ground" in our local office in Atlanta in addition to our Tel Aviv office. We create value for our investors by actively managing our properties and building a diversified investment portfolio.

Brightman-Gil Real Estate Investment Management builds a diversified investment portfolio by focusing on growth-oriented locations and carefully defining the optimal investment for any given time.

Brightman-Gil's second fund (B&G II) will focus on creating a diversified asset portfolio that will enable investors to enjoy surplus returns. Our success has and will continue to come from focusing on geographic locations that are economically robust, identifying the right investment opportunities for any given period, and professionally managing the assets in the portfolio.

We continue to believe that the United States is a preferred destination for real estate investments. Furthermore, we believe that the southeastern region of the United States has both the economic and population growth that leads to investment opportunities.
