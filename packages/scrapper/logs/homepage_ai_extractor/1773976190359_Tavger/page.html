How it works
Affinity Gentle Jet System uses pressurized tiny solution streams to clean and remove debris and foreign matter from the skin. It also allows the filling of skin appendages with various medical aesthetics and antiaging liquid products.

The safety delivery confuses the skin's immune system, activates its homeostasis and stimulates all skin's system apparatus resulting in skin getting, healthier fresher, and inevitably younger.
How it works
Affinity Gentle Jet System uses pressurized tiny solution streams to clean and remove debris and foreign matter from the skin. It also allows the filling of skin appendages with various medical aesthetics and antiaging liquid products.

The safety delivery confuses the skin's immune system, activates its homeostasis and stimulates all skin's system apparatus resulting in skin getting, healthier fresher, and inevitably younger.
The "heart" of the system is plastic tips, having two micro-nozzles of 50 μm diameter each. The tip directly interacts with the skin surface, installed on the ergonomically designed handpiece. The negative pressure links the skin surface with the nozzle outlets. While moving the handpiece along the skin surface, the follicular space is filled with the solution when the alignment between the pore orifice and jets nozzles occurs.

The pores function as a reservoir and portals of entry to the viable skin. Their walls represent the expansion of the epidermis, which significantly increases the total skin surface area available for penetration.
