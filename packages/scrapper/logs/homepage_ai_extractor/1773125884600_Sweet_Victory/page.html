What is Sweet Victory Gum?

Sweet Victory is a delicious proprietary chewing gum, made with the sugar-busting power of organic gymnema, a natural plant leaf. Sweet Victory stops your desire for sugar right in its tracks simply by blocking the sugar receptors*. This effect can last up to 2 hours.

What kind of proof has Sweet Victory gum gained?

In 2024, a clinical pilot study was conducted at Tel Aviv Sourasky Medical Center, led by Dr. Roy Eldor, Director of the Metabolic Syndrome Unit. This double-blind, randomized clinical trial with Type 1 Diabetes patients showed a significant decrease in daily sweet food consumption in the Sweet Victory group compared to the placebo group. These findings reinforce results from a consumer study conducted in 2023, which revealed that 80% of participants significantly reduced their sugar intake with Sweet Victory, and 90% experienced weight loss. For more details on the trials, please click here.

How long does the effect of the gum last?

The duration of the effect differs from person to person. While the effect lasts for an average of one hour, some people experience the effect for up to two hours.

People also report that even after the effect has lessened or completely stopped, they often still have no desire for sweets or carbohydrate for a few more hours.

After chewing this gum for just a minute or two, eating other foods will not taste the same. While they will maintain their texture but they won’t taste sweet. If you eat chocolate after chewing the gum, it will just taste like butter.

When should I use the Sweet Victory gum?

 We recommend chewing one piece of gum after every meal for maximum effectiveness. 

How does blocking the sweet taste prevent me from eating sugar?

According to previous studies, blocking the receptors for sweet taste on the tongue decreases sugar cravings*


The gum works on two levels – physiological and psychological.

  1. Physically: This SOS product has an immediate effect. After chewing the gum, eating sugary foods does not have the sweet taste we expect and are used to. As a result, we won’t have a craving for sweets for at least one hour.
  2. Psychologically: Most people crave something sweet at certain times of the day, such as immediately after having lunch. Over time, this craving becomes automatic and is very hard to change. Yet chewing Sweet Victory Gum at those most challenging times may slowly break our bad habits and help us rebuild better and healthier habits.

Is there research to support the effect of the Gymnema Sylvestre plant ?

Since the 1920s, studies have been conducted on the Gymnema Sylvestre, mainly among animals and more recently among humans. Preliminary evidence suggests that Gymnema may be efficacious for the management of serum glucose levels in type 1 and type 2 diabetes, in addition to conventional drug therapy. Gymnema also appears to lower serum glucose and glycosylated hemoglobin (HbA1c) levels (Baskaran et al., 1990).

One major side effect of Gymnema is taste alteration. Studies show that Gymnema reduces our perception of sweetness inside the mouth (Brala & Hagen,1983; Lawless,1979; Meiselman&Halperin,1970; Meiselman&Halpern, 1970; Min&Sakamoto,1998; Simons, O’Mahony & Carstens,2002; Warren, Warren & Weninger, 1969).

Our product focuses specifically on this positive side effect that reduces human craving for sweets, rather than on the plant’s main effect of balancing sugar level among diabetics.

What is the Gymnema Sylvestre plant?

The “Gymnema Sylvestre” originates in India. Also known as “Gurmar”, it means “sugar destroyer” in Hindi. This plant, considered to have unique virtues, has been used in ayurvedic medicine for over two thousand years*. Ancient medicine uses the plant for a number of health-related problems, including high blood sugar levels, high cholesterol and triglyceride levels, heart diseases and excess weight.
