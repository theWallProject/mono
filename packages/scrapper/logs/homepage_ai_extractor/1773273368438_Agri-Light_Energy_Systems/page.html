What we do?

AGRILIGHT develops a patented Agrivoltaics solution to improve orchard and vineyard yield, while generating 15-20% more solar energy than other systems on the same land.

AGRILIGHT mission

Optimizing plant growth according to real-time needs while maximizing solar energy generation.

Maximizing solar energy generation, 15-20% more than competitors

Improving crop quality and production in 10-20%

Savings in water for irrigation up to 20-30%

Protection of crops from extreme weather conditions

Increases farmer’s income from dual revenue sources

The challenge

Other Agrivoltaic systems (single-axis tracker) can’t do both: either crop yields decline or energy production is compromised

Crop yields decline

Energy production compromised

less photosynthesis

Single-axis tracking can maximize energy but casts shadow, disrupting crop photosynthesis

less solar energy generation

Aligning panels with the sun reduces shade on crops but cuts solar energy generation.

Our solution

Maximum Yield. Maximum Power.
Zero Compromise.

AGRILIGHT allows both  simultaneously! enabling solar energy generation and crop optimization to work in parallel, without compromise or interference, based on our patented dual-axis and ML algorithms, delivering 10-20% more energy than others while providing the plant exactly what it needs, all the time.
