Our Mission

Deep-Tech Showcase is an active ecosystem that supports the growth and development of deep-tech & dual-use technologies. Amongst our members are founders of novel technologies, government stakeholders, corporate decision makers and venture capital firms looking to make strategic investments. Our program provides startups with strategic consulting sessions, pitching opportunities, guided introductions, dedicated events spotlighting their companies, along with marketing support for awareness and lead generation. 

Deep-Tech Showcase is proudly supported by Eagle Point Funding, world leading grant consultants who assists companies in identifying federal funding opportunities, and crafting winning grant proposals.

Strategy

Strategic Government-oriented consulting: with former DoD stakeholders, investment strategists, and others to help with DoD procurement, use cases, targeting end users, overall research. This allows us to make sure that your goal, messaging, alignment and technology are all perfect before putting yourself out there.

Guided Introductions

As an ongoing effort to maximize the potential within our network, we are constantly learning the needs and pain points of our members, and actively working to solve them through relationships. When we find the right fit, a warm introduction is made. For startups, this means opening new doors that were previously unapproachable. For stakeholders, this means access to constant deal flow of relevant next-gen technology.

Finding an Air-Force Customer

The MoU Club is an accelerator program designed to help founders get a signed “Memorandum of Understanding” from a U.S Air Force stakeholder. The end goal is a signed MoU and high chances of winning Phase 2 funding. This program is run in partnership with our sister company, Eagle Point Funding, who can than write the Phase 2 grant application for the applicable companies. Throughout the process, we not only create a support network, but also offer coaching sessions, feedback from ex-Air Force personnel, warm introductions and dedicated pitch events to panels of Air Force stakeholders.
