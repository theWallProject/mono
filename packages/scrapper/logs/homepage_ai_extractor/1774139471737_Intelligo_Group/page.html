FAQs

Intelligo provides due diligence and background check reports based on publicly available information and data from premium sources. Our reports are designed to give you a comprehensive view of any red flags or risks associated with entities or individuals with whom you seek to partner, invest, or engage in a business relationship. Our services can be used to vet risks before you engage in a business relationship and to monitor ongoing relationships.

We have a variety of reports that provide insights into the legal, regulatory, traditional media, social media, financial, and professional history profiles of entities and individuals. Our suite of automated reports are mainly compiled by our AI platform, which rules out false positives and focuses on information relevant to our clients’ needs. Our Advantage reports combine the time and cost savings provided by AI-guided research with a human analyst who is then able to go a level deeper to connect the dots and provide a more comprehensive review.

Intelligo is the first platform to train AI’s ability to collect and analyze data with the mindset of a human due diligence researcher. Our technology has gone through years of training and has been given tens of thousands of points of feedback to learn from to provide relevant insights while reducing false positives for the user. The relevant algorithms continue to improve as they receive continuous feedback from our research and product teams.
