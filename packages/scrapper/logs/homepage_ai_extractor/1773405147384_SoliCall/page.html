SoliCall develops patented noise cancelling technology that improves audio quality in telephony. Our unique noise cancelling software includes an innovative NOISE FIREWALL™ for contact centers and crowded workspace, a versatile echo cancellation software and an advanced noise cancelling technology. Using our noise cancelling software, you can easily cancel noise, voice and echo and improve call quality regardless the phone system you have in your organization. SoliCall’s noise cancellation app is powerful, lightweight and it can be used in almost any telephony environment. Our noise cancelling app is utilizing both a powerful AI engine and an advanced analytic engine.

Our worldwide customer base includes companies of all sizes from big blue-chip companies to small start-up companies. Among our customers you can find:

• Call Centers and companies that use our plug-and-play products to cancel noise, improve customer experience, increase productivity and improve the accuracy of voice analytic.

• Developers of communication software and devices that use our OEM products to  embed our technology in their solution.

• Education centers, conference bridge providers, VoIP operators,  and companies around the world that use our technology to remove echo and noise from their calls.

• Developers and users of voice analytic solutions looking to improve the accuracy of the analytics.
