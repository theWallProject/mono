We give crops a voice.

CropVoice uses the power of plants that communicate to take the guesswork out of soybean fungicide decisions by sending alerts when there is confirmed disease near specific fields.

Discover

InnerPlant creates plants that communicate so farmers can make the best management decisions possible with data tied directly to plant physiology

Signal

We code crops to communicate within hours when stressed by pathogens, NPK or drought

Detect

Our Living Sensor plants emit optical signals detectable by tractors in the field or by satellites in space

Act

Our platform translates signals into actionable recommendations that help farmers grow more productively, profitably and sustainably

Seeing Is Believing

Move the slider to see a fluorescing Sensor Plant and a non-sensor plant through an optical filter

Early Warnings. Bigger Yields. Lower Costs.

More Bushels, Less Guesswork.

CropVoice can confirm disease from the most harmful above-ground fungal pathogens - like white mold and frogeye leaf spot - before symptoms are visible in the field.

InnerPlant's technology is backed by a local field team with over 60 years of combined agronomy experience and 8 PhDs spanning functional genomics to infrastructure systems engineering

Once the plants in a sensor plot fluoresce, that information is validated with laboratory analysis, boots-on-the-ground scouting, local agronomic expertise, weather data, and advanced modeling to confirm fungal disease in the network.
