Our Approach

Explore insights into our investment strategies and management processes.

Market Driven

Copperline continually seeks properties in metropolitan areas that reflect strong job and population growth with favorable demographic trends. Within those overall markets, we target submarkets that generate high absorption rates and opportunity for increasing demand.

Barrier To Entry

Copperline has built a portfolio of irreplaceable assets by investing in prime communities and neighborhoods with few remaining buildable sites, but which offer strong in-fill locations that fall within projected growth paths.

Creative Vision

Whether through new construction or extensive renovation and rebranding of current structures, Copperline draws upon its creative vision and expertise to create unique, in-demand destinations in highly regarded markets.

Experienced Management

Since its inception, Copperline has self-managed every one of our assets. Our decades of experience combined with the on the ground, daily involvement of our executive team is Copperline’s guiding differentiator.

Executive Team

The core of our success is our people – the management and staff who comprise the Copperline family. Copperline prides itself on the stability of its staff, many of whom have been with the company for more than 25 years. This dedicated and loyal team has enabled us to maintain strong performance in spite of market fluctuations and generates the insight and enthusiasm that continues propelling us forward.
