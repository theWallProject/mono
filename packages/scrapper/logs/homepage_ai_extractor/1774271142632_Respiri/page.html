Our approach is evidence-based

A recent systematic review of RPM determined that RPM interventions can reduce acute care use7.

Recommendations for Best Practice RPM program design1

Thomas et al., 2021 in their qualitative systematic review of 91 studies found that RPM interventions which successfully reduced acute care use, were those that:

Target high risk populations
Accurately detect a decline in health
Responsive / timely care
Personalized care
Enhanced self-management
Collaborative & coordinated care

To realize these benefits our team will leverage this evidence base for best-in-class program design:

Use our strong data analytics capability to identify at risk patients and stratify them into programs

Collaborative protocol setting is integrated into existing workflows to ensure timely action

Continuity of care is guaranteed with one care advocate assigned to each patient to drive adherence and support self-management

Partnership between patient and multi-disciplinary team; including the PCP and a dedicated single professional monitoring & motivating the patient

Chronic Care Management has been shown to deliver reductions in total medical expenditure

In a white paper, CMS documented how Chronic Care Management saved an average of $74/month per beneficiary over an 18-month period in a 2017 study. With results repeated in 20198.
