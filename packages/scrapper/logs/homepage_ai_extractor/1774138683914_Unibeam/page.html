Trusted Hardware. Never Been Hacked.

SIM and eSIM chips are secure elements with built-in cryptography. By binding them to your device’s unique hardware ID, Unibeam turns the most proven security layer in mobile history into a modern authentication engine.

Nothing to hack

There’s not much to hack on Unibeam’s’ SIM applets. Not much code. No personal data. That’s why even if the SIM-based Unibeam applet was compromised, there’s just nothing to steal. Kind of like breaking into a safe…and finding it empty.

No More SIM Swap or Spoofing.

Unibeam cryptographically binds each SIM (or eSIM/iSIM) to its device’s serial number. Any attempt to replace or clone the SIM instantly breaks the link, stopping account takeovers before they start.
