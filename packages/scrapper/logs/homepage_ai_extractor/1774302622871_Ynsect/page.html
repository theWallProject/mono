Who We Are

We are one of the world leaders in insect ingredient production for people, animals, and plants. Our goal? To make the way we eat more nutritious and sustainable.

We need a food system revolution

Our food systems are at a breaking point. Sustainable feed, food, and fertilizer alternatives are essential, but we cannot afford to compromise on quality.

Mealworms, a superfood for people and planet

Our mealworms reconcile the wellbeing of both our society and ecosystems. With a positive impact on cholesterol and microbiota, and packed with high-quality protein, vitamins, and minerals, they create sustainable, healthy products for feed and food while enabling the use of all materials – even their dejections.

Elevated by technology

We cultivate our mealworms in state-of-the-art vertical farms powered by advanced robotics, computer vision and cutting-edge AI. Our pioneering work has led to 440+ patents and ensures every part of our process and products is optimized for maximum health, growth and productivity.

The Result

A functional, scalable solution to help create global food systems that serve people and planet.
