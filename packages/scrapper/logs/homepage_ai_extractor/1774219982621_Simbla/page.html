What is an AI-powered CRM?

An AI-powered CRM is a Customer Relationship Management system that integrates Artificial Intelligence. This system employs AI not only to customize itself to the unique requirements of the customer but also to facilitate a wide array of functionalities. These functionalities may include automated processes driven by AI, AI-enabled customer support chat, and beyond.
