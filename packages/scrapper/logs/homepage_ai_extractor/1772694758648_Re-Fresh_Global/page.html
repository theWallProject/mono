Re-Fresh Global brings a cutting-edge approach to the growing problem of textile waste. We’re proud to be driving innovation together with other visionary partners across the globe.
OUR biotechnology
process
We convert textile waste into fibers which are then bioprocessed to create premium-quality nanocellulose and bioethanol. The waste from this process is used to produce a multifunctional, purified pulp. We’re pioneering a truly zero-waste solution by turning piles of textile waste into industrial quantities of new resources. Our solution rescues waste, reduces greenhouse gas emissions, and saves water.
