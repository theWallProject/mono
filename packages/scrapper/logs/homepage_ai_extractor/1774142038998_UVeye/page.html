How It Works

Our systems scan the full exterior, undercarriage, and tires of every vehicle in seconds. Using a combination of high-resolution imaging, advanced sensors, and patented AI algorithms, UVeye detects issues invisible to the naked eye.

What It Detects

UVeye’s systems detect everything from tire wear and misalignment to leaks, rust, and underbody damage. Using high-resolution imaging and advanced sensors, each scan identifies cracks, scratches, and safety risks, providing instant, visual insights that keep vehicles road-ready and customers informed.

Why It’s Trusted

Trusted by industry leaders like General Motors, Volvo, Toyota, and Amazon, UVeye is built on proven AI technology refined through millions of vehicle scans worldwide. Each inspection delivers consistent results, giving OEMs, fleets, and dealerships complete confidence in every decision.

Seamless Integration

UVeye connects effortlessly with your existing systems and processes, embedding automated inspections into daily operations without disruption. Real-time data flows seamlessly across departments, providing instant, visual insights that improve accuracy, simplify workflows, and create a unified, transparent experience from start to finish.
