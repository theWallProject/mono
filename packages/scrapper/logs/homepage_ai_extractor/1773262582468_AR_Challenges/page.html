ABOUT

The company was formed exclusively to deal with advanced W2E systems. AR Challenges Ltd owns various proprietary designs, Patents and IS (Industry Secrets) that have been exclusively licensed to the company by its suppliers.

THINGS TO DO

We provide end-to-end, complete solutions to ALL waste problems (Except Hazardous and C&D). Our systems are based on the Circular Economy principals together with Zero Waste to landfills.
We also provide distributed energy so that the power and energy generation can be detached from the waste sorting facility, eliminating the need for expensive electrical grid infrastructure. We completely support Micro-Grid technologies.
We provide entire energy solutions to: Hospitals, Hotels, Industrial complexes and campuses.
