Jacobs-Yaniv Architects is a leading Israeli design practice with considerable project running experience and a portfolio which includes high end houses and apartments, residential buildings, commercial spaces and public projects.

The practice also specializes in a ‘one stop shop’ service for overseas clients wishing to build a holiday home or a temporary residence in Israel.

Jacobs-Yaniv Architects was founded in 2004. Practice founders Tamar Jacobs and Oshri Yaniv returned to Israel having spent a decade living and working in London: graduating from the Architectural Association school of Architecture and working for some of the world’s leading architects – Zaha Hadid, David Chipperfield and Wilkinson Eyre Architects.
