CAPABILITIES

Pioneering two new capabilities for natural human antibodies

We leverage our platform to engineer epitope specific antibodies, such as agonists, antagonists, conformation-specific or conditional binders; or multibodies, which are multi-specific symmetrical IgG binding to more than one target on the same Fab.

Biolojic’s multibodies will solve key challenges in treating autoimmune diseases​and cancers.

PLATFORM

A Revolutionary New Approach

Using machine learning, we create superior drugs by programming antibodies to become dynamic functional switches that affect biology in new ways.

Biolojic’s AI-designed antibodies provide a precise molecular effect, in the desired micro-environment, at the right time, leading to greater efficacy and lower toxicity, while maintaining impeccable developability, half-life, stability and safety of human antibodies.

PIPELINE

Bringing novel technology to well-established biology

We’re on a mission to cure disease.

Our pipeline of computationally designed programmable antibodies focuses on autoimmune diseases and immuno-oncology.

We target well-understood, validated biological pathways. We use our technology to design smart antibodies that affect these pathways more precisely and can succeed where traditional drugs have failed.
