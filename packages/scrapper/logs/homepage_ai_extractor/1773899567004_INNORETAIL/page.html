STRATEGIC FOCUS:
Retail Tech is one of most attractive markets for VC
Market situation: Nearly all retailers worldwide are private companies operating in competitive environments and driven by the pursuit of efficiency.

Industry transformation: Retail is undergoing significant changes and is in dire need of new technologies.

Scalability: Technological solutions for the retail sector can be relatively easily scaled to international markets (in contrast to the products and designs of the stores themselves).
