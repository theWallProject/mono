    How It Works

    A unique electrochemical process that generates cleaning agents on-site, eliminating external detergents.

    Water

    Ordinary tap water is the base ingredient

    Electricity

    Controlled electrolysis separates water into active streams

    Salt

    Simple salt completes the electrochemical process

    Clean Laundry

    Two powerful solutions replace all detergents

    HIGH PH

    Alkaline Solution

    Removes dirt and grease, replacing traditional laundry detergents. Generated on-site with precise dosing.

    LOW PH

    Acidic Solution

    Handles disinfection and neutralization, replacing bleaches and fabric softeners. Biodegradable wastewater.

    The AS-System

    An industrial production unit installed at your site, integrating seamlessly with all existing washing machines.

    Target Markets

    Modular Design

    The system is designed modularly, allowing precise customization. Additional units can be connected in parallel to increase production capacity.

    Example: A system producing 1,000 L/hr (1 control unit + 4 additional units) can serve a facility processing ~10 tons of laundry per day.

    Easy Integration

    No infrastructure modifications required. The AS-System connects directly to your existing washing machines and water supply.
