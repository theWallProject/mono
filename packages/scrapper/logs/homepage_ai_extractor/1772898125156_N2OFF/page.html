MitoCareX Bio

Redefining Cancer Drug Discovery Through Mitochondrial Science

MitoCareX Bio is an innovative drug discovery company dedicated to developing first-in-class therapeutics for hard-to-treat cancers by targeting a previously underexploited drug targets in cancer biology: mitochondrial transport proteins. The company focuses on members belonging to the SLC25A transport family, which play a central role in cellular energy metabolism and cancer cell survival yet have historically been considered difficult or impossible to drug due to their membrane localization and structural complexity.

At the core of MitoCareX Bio’s discovery engine is MITOLINE™, a proprietary computational platform designed to allow proper homology modelling of mitochondrial membrane proteins, particularly transport proteins. By enabling 3D structural modelling and large-scale virtual screening, MITOLINE™ opens access to a novel and largely untapped target space, allowing MitoCareX to identify small-molecule modulators that directly disrupt cancer metabolism with the potential of minimizing systemic toxicity.

MitoCareX Bio integrates advanced in-silico discovery with rigorous experimental validation, creating a capital-efficient pipeline that rapidly translates computational insights into biologically validated drug candidates. This approach is designed not only to accelerate the discovery of high-quality oncology assets but also to generate significant long-term value through partnerships, licensing opportunities, and scalable platform applications. With a strong translational focus and a clear path toward clinical relevance, MitoCareX Bio aims to unlock mitochondrial biology to deliver transformative cancer therapies.

As part of the N2OFF Inc. ecosystem, MitoCareX Bio represents a powerful convergence of proprietary technology, deep scientific expertise, and commercial potential - positioning it at the forefront of the next generation of oncology drug discovery.

Solar Energy

Israel-based N2OFF has entered the Solar Energy by establishing a JV with Solterra Renewable Energy. Solterra is currently active in three primary target markets: Italy, Poland, and Germany, managing a portfolio of at various stages of solar PV development. N2OFF provides financial support to Solterra, including a loan installments.

The JV focus is accelerating project development to ensure its timely progression to market demand.

The Melz project is one of Solterra’s flagship developments, representing its dedication to create renewable energy facilities with a total portfolio capacity of approximately 300 MW across Germany, Italy, and Poland.
