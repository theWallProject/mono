    Scientific Approach

    Our Target

    Macrophages are central orchestrators of inflammation, immunity, and tissue repair. In chronic inflammatory, infectious, and fibrotic diseases, macrophages transition into pathogenic activated states, including both pro-inflammatory M1 and alternatively activated M2 phenotypes, driving cytokine dysregulation, tissue damage, viral or parasitic persistence, and fibrosis.

    Across this spectrum of activation, CD206 (the mannose receptor) is upregulated on disease-associated macrophages, serving as a characteristic marker of macrophage activation and providing a selective gateway for targeted therapeutic delivery. Targeting CD206 enables precise engagement of the macrophages most responsible for sustaining inflammation and tissue pathology, while minimizing effects on resting immune cells.

    Working in conjunction with the HPA system rather than against it enables maximal effect, repair of the HPA system, reduces GC resistance, and allows natural just-in-time cortisol release to treat ongoing inflammation.

    Our Platform

    101-PGC-005, a mannose-linked, pH-sensitive dexamethasone prodrug designed to:

    • Release active dexamethasone in the low-pH lysosomal environment
    • Induce apoptosis of pathogenic macrophages
    • Eliminate systemic steroid exposure, enabling higher anti-inflammatory dosing with reduced off-target effects

    Disease Targets

    Our platform is disease-agnostic and applicable to any condition driven by a dysregulated HPA axis. We are initially prioritizing high-value therapeutic areas including rare diseases, autoimmune conditions, neuroinflammation, infectious disease, metabolic diseases, and oncology.

    Our platform is disease-agnostic and applicable to any condition driven by pathogenic macrophage populations.

    Rare Diseases

    Targeting macrophage-driven rare diseases with RPDD granted for sJIA. Applicable to both genetic and acquired macrophage-driven conditions.

    Autoimmune & Chronic Inflammatory

    Addressing joint destruction, tissue inflammation, and chronic pain. Works alongside biologics to improve efficacy and restore HPA axis control.

    Neuroinflammation

    Targeting CD206⁺ microglia and perivascular macrophages to restore BBB integrity and reduce CNS inflammation.

    Metabolic Diseases

    Reducing macrophage-driven inflammation in obesity, insulin resistance, and NAFLD/NASH. Complementary to GLP-1 therapies.

    Infectious Disease

    Efficacy demonstrated in COVID-19, Dengue, and Leishmaniasis models. A platform for pandemic preparedness.

    Oncology & TAMs

    Depleting immunosuppressive tumor-associated macrophages to reprogram the tumor microenvironment and enhance checkpoint inhibitor efficacy.
