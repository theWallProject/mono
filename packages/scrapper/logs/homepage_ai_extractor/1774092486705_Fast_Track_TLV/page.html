Enjoy VIP assistance at the airport, from the plane to the hotel, avoiding queues for a relaxing journey. All airport services you need. Fast response to any request.

Arrival Fast Track Service

When you land at Ben Gurion Airport, one of our agents will meet you at the plane’s exit with a signboard. They will escort you through fast-track passport control, assist you with your luggage, and help with customs. Additionally, the agent will escort you to your awaiting transportation.

Details

Departure Fast Track Service

To save time, arrive up to 2 hours before your flight. Meet our agent at the entrance of Terminal 3, Ben Gurion Airport. Enjoy fast-track security screening and priority check-in. Get help with hand luggage and a VAT refund if needed. Our agent will escort you to the boarding hall.

Details

Private Terminal - Arrival

Our agent will meet you outside the aircraft with a signboard. Then, they will drive you to the VIP terminal, where you can relax with beverages and snacks. An agent will bring your luggage. A border officer will assist you with entry documents in the VIP Lounge. Your driver will wait next to the VIP Lounge to take you to your destination.

Details

Private Terminal - Departure

To save time, arrive up to 2 hours before your flight. Your driver will deliver you to the Fattal VIP Terminal. In the exclusive VIP lounge, complete all departure formalities. Our agent will check in your luggage, and an official will process your travel documents. Enjoy VIP transport by car directly to the aircraft.

Prices 2026

Fast Track service at Terminal 3  is  $295 for a group of 1 to 5 people, with an additional $35 charge per person for larger groups.

In Private Terminal Fattal, the price for the first person is $650 (including VAT), and for the second person, it is $490 (including VAT). Any orders made less than 24 hours in advance incur a $35 last-minute fee.  Cancellation Policy
