Our Mission

Reduced emissions, increased revenue

CarbonBlue develops technologies which increase industrial efficiency, productivity and profits while removing CO2 from the atmosphere, enabling the adaptation of global industries to a sustainable, profitable, and carbon-neutral economy.

Learn More

Our Solutions

EcoLime

CarbonBlue’s proprietary technology is able to produce lime at low temperatures, using renewable energy and with zero emissions, fully unlocking lime’s potential for environmental rehabilitation, and providing a  sustainable, carbon-negative source for one of the most highly utilized materials on the planet.

Learn More

AquaFix

To rapidly scale up and reach the volumes of CO2 removal required to mitigate climate change, we turn to some of the world’s largest industries, providing technologies to create carbon-negative materials, which leverage the industries’ existing operations and core business for positive environmental transformation, unlocking sustainable transformation for continued growth.
