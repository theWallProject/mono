What's under the hood?

Inventory Management

Whether you work in biology, chemistry, or any other area of life science research, LabSuit has you covered. LabSuit is a complete solution for managing all aspects of your lab inventory - from chemicals, antibodies, and plasmids to any custom item types you define to fit your lab's needs. Easily map your physical lab into virtual locations and sub-locations, ensuring you never lose track of an item again. With MSDS attachments, custom tags, and many other handy tools, everything you need is right at your fingertips.

Learn more

Requests module

Every lab has its own ordering workflow - and LabSuit makes it effortless.
When lab requests are managed in one place, the entire research group stays in sync. Everyone can see who ordered what, when it was ordered, and at what price. This transparency helps prevent mistakes, avoid duplicate orders, and quickly spot delays.

Learn more

LabSuit Shop

LabSuit offers an integrated LabSuit Shop, where labs can easily purchase the products they need. The Shop works seamlessly with the Requests and Inventory modules, making it even simpler to manage day-to-day lab operations.
LabSuit Shop also supports PunchOut integration with selected institutional procurement systems and is currently available in Israel only.

Why is LabSuit free?

As our founding team comes from a life sciences research background, we understand the importance of research tools that are both accessible and easy to use.
That's why we set ourselves the goal of providing LabSuit free of charge.
To support this mission, LabSuit includes an integrated LabSuit Shop*, where lab products can be purchased and seamlessly added to an inventory and requests module.
* LabSuit Shop is available at selected locations
