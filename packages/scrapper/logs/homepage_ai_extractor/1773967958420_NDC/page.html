Our Strengths

Smart Presentation

To get the right answer you need to ask the right question. Using a Problem- Oriented approach, checking charts and a case moderator, we make sure it is presented in a way that allows solving it quickly. From years, to days!

Intelligent Choice

Many rare disease diagnoses come from unexpected sources. To eliminate the pressure of consensus, doctors submit their diagnostic suggestion discreetly. Then we use collective intelligence and AI techniques to choose the best diagnostic suggestions for further testing.

Tailored Case Moderation

Each case has a physician Moderator supervising the entire case discussion. On top of helping with presentation and diagnostic choice, the moderator aids with applying for testing in expert medical facilities specializing in exclusive medical fields.

Diagnostic Talent

Do you have a diagnostic talent? Test your skills with real challenging cases. Win points and go up the website’s rank by solving case challenges. We scout doctors who excel at diagnosing to form an All-Star team of diagnostic experts that can offer their services to patients.

What is NDC Medicine

Neglected Diseases Collaboration (NDC) is an international network of doctors helping each other diagnose challenging cases quickly and accurately using a smart, web-based crowdsourcing method combining AI techniques. NDC Medicine platform allows physicians to present patients of all ages with unusual symptoms, discuss the case with their peers and submit diagnostic suggestions. NDC Medicine also helps to connect with expert medical centers to test the best diagnostic suggestions, and scouts for diagnostic talents.
