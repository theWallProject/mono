  • Teach Science with einstein™!

For over more than three decades, Fourier Systems (also known as  Fourier Education) is a worldwide leader in the field of STEM education technology for students from primary schools through high school, as well as teachers’ training colleges and universities.

“One of the World’s Top EdTech Companies of 2025 TIME magazine in partnership with Statista”

In today’s world, science education needs to evolve with the teachers’ and students’ expectations for dynamic stimulation and technology innovation in science education methods and tools.

Fourier’s ecosystem, the einstein™ connected lab platform, is a holistic science education solution that enables performing hands-on science experiments in a computerized lab from everywhere.

Fourier focus on promoting out-of-the-box science experiences with the einstein™connected lab and encourages educators all around the world to teach science as a tool for developing 21st-century skills in students, critical thinking, creativity, innovation, and problem-solving.

software

MiLABEx is a powerful app that collects, displays, and analyzes experiment data. MiLABEx must be paired with the einstein™ Tablet or the einstein™ LabMate, transforming any screen device into a digital lab, and works with over 60 einstein™ sensors.

The MiLABEx contains 3 sub-apps:
Lab – Start an experiment
Workbook – Create and share experiments
Weather Station – Monitoring climate parameters
