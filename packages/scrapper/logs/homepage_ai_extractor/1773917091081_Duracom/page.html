About Us :

Building Strong Connections Since 1998

Duracom Contracting and Telecommunications Co. has been a trusted name in Palestine since 1998, providing reliable solutions in telecommunications and electrical infrastructure. Founded with the vision of creating a strong Palestinian alternative in the telecom industry, Duracom has grown into a recognized and authorized company registered with all relevant authorities.

Headquartered in Nablus, we have successfully delivered major projects across the West Bank, partnering with leading companies and clients. Our commitment to quality workmanship, service excellence, and innovative solutions sets us apart. With deep expertise in both telecommunications and electrical systems, we manage every project with hands-on dedication—from planning to execution—ensuring reliability and long-term value for our partners.

Our Services

What We Master...

We provide cutting-edge telecom infrastructure services that power connectivity, drive innovation, and build the digital backbone of tomorrow’s world.

  • 01.

    Telecom Networks supply and installation

    Duracom provides end-to-end telecom network solutions, from design and supply to full installation. We deliver reliable, scalable, and efficient networks that meet international standards.

  • 02.

    Fiber Optics Cabling

    We specialize in the installation of high-performance fiber optic cabling, ensuring fast, secure, and uninterrupted data transmission for businesses and communities.

  • 03.

    Supply of Fiber Optics accessories

    Duracom supplies a complete range of fiber optic accessories and components, offering durable and high-quality products to support advanced telecom infrastructures.

  • 04.

    Fiber to the Home (FTTH)

    We deliver cutting-edge FTTH solutions, bringing high-speed internet and modern digital services directly to homes and businesses across Palestine.

  • 05

    GSM and Microwave Towers

    Our expertise includes the construction and installation of GSM and microwave towers, providing reliable mobile coverage and connectivity across diverse regions.

  • 06

    GSM Solutions for High Traffic

    Duracom offers advanced GSM solutions designed to handle high-traffic demands, ensuring seamless communication and network stability even in densely populated areas.

  • 07

    Power and Lighting system

    We design, supply, and install complete power and lighting systems, supporting telecom and infrastructure projects with safe, efficient, and sustainable energy solutions.
