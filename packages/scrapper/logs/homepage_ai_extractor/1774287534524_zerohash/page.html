FAQs
Is zerohash regulated?
zerohash operates regulated entities and licensing frameworks across multiple jurisdictions. The platform is designed for banks, brokerages, and fintech companies that require compliance with financial regulations and operational controls. zerohash provides built-in compliance infrastructure, custody solutions, and monitoring systems so partners can launch digital asset products within regulated environments.
Does zerohash support tokenization of financial assets?
zerohash provides infrastructure that allows institutions to create and manage tokenized financial assets. Tokenization represents traditional assets such as funds, securities, or deposits as blockchain-based tokens. zerohash helps companies issue and manage these assets while supporting compliance, custody, settlement, and blockchain integration.
Can zerohash help companies launch crypto trading?
Yes. zerohash provides regulated infrastructure that enables brokerages, fintech platforms, and financial institutions to offer crypto trading directly inside their products. The platform handles liquidity sourcing, execution, custody, and settlement so companies can launch digital asset trading without building exchange infrastructure or managing blockchain operations themselves.
What are stablecoins and how does zerohash support them?
Stablecoins are digital currencies designed to maintain a stable value, typically pegged to fiat currencies like the U.S. dollar. Financial platforms use stablecoins to enable faster payments, cross-border transfers, and 24/7 settlement. zerohash infrastructure allows companies to accept, send, and convert stablecoins while managing compliance, liquidity, and blockchain connectivity behind the scenes.
Who uses zerohash infrastructure?
zerohash is used by banks, brokerages, fintech platforms, payment companies, marketplaces, and other platforms that want to embed digital asset capabilities. These companies use zerohash to power services such as crypto trading, stablecoin payments, cross-border payouts, and tokenized asset issuance without building complex blockchain infrastructure internally.
How is zerohash different from crypto exchanges or wallets?
zerohash is not a consumer crypto exchange. Instead, it provides infrastructure that powers digital asset products inside other companies' platforms. Banks, brokerages, and fintech apps integrate zerohash technology so their users can trade crypto, move money using stablecoins, or access tokenized assets within familiar financial products.
What is digital asset infrastructure?
Digital asset infrastructure refers to the technology systems that allow financial institutions to interact with blockchain networks and digital assets. This includes custody, liquidity access, regulatory compliance tools, transaction monitoring, settlement, and blockchain connectivity. zerohash provides these capabilities through APIs so companies can launch crypto and stablecoin products without building their own blockchain systems.
What products and capabilities does zerohash provide?
zerohash provides infrastructure across three core areas: trading, payments, and tokenization. Companies can enable digital asset trading, stablecoin-powered money movement, global payouts, on-ramps and off-ramps between crypto and fiat, and tokenized financial assets. These capabilities run on a single infrastructure platform with built-in custody, compliance controls, and settlement systems.
