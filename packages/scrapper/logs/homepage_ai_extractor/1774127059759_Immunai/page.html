Solutions

Our solutions support decision-making along the drug discovery & development journey to bring better therapies to patients.

Our platform helps pharma companies make smarter choices, reduce risks, and accelerate progress with three key solutions:

  • Target DiscoveryIdentify novel high impact targets linked to disease
  • Preclinical EvaluationPrioritize the most promising drug candidates for development
  • Clinical Trial OptimizationBetter understand drug mechanism of action, identify the right groups of patients and the best treatment strategies to maximize trial success

Drug development is a complex journey that requires critical decisions at every step.

platform

Our platform transforms complex therapeutic questions into actionable recommendations. With our end-to-end workflow, we optimize drug development strategies.

Therapeutic Query

For example: What patient population should receive the drug? How does the drug’s mechanism of action compare to the standard of care?

01 Generate

We produce high-quality, multiomic data from clinical and preclinical samples using cutting-edge wet lab and computational pipelines. These are transformed into a uniform single-cell data foundation with unmatched resolution.

02 Augment

Generated data is enriched with AMICA, the world’s largest immune-focused, harmonized single-cell database. By integrating our vast data and immune knowledge, we enhance analytical power to deliver deeper, more precise insights.

03 Compute

Leveraging our advanced machine learning platform, we compute novel immune features, linking immune mechanisms to treatment responses and outcomes.

04 Validate

Utilizing functional genomics, we validate ML-driven hypotheses in the lab to ensure robust and actionable insights.

05 Recommend

Our recommendation engine provides clear, actionable paths forward – how to proceed and why, in order to increase confidence in decision making.
