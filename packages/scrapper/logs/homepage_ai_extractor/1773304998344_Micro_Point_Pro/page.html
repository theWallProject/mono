About

Micro Point Pro Ltd

Micro Point Pro Ltd (MPP) was founded in 2010 following the acquisition of Kulicke & Soffa’s bonding tools and other back-end consumables production lines, along with the associated knowledge and expertise of highly experienced employees boasting over 40 years of industry experience. Since then, MPP has become a recognized global leader in the Semiconductor market across both Backend and Frontend sectors. We have further strengthened our capabilities by incorporating additional manufacturing processes, enabling us to deliver comprehensive solutions that address our customers’ expanding and evolving needs.
