faq

What does a crypto PR agency do?

A crypto PR agency helps blockchain and Web3 projects gain visibility, credibility, and trust by securing media coverage, shaping narratives, and engaging the right audiences. At MarketAcross, this includes media pitching, thought leadership content, and community‑driven strategies that turn complex ideas into stories people understand — often integrated with broader blockchain marketing initiatives.

How is blockchain marketing different from traditional marketing?

Blockchain marketing requires a completely different approach than traditional industries. The Web3 ecosystem moves faster, is community‑driven, and demands trust from highly engaged audiences on platforms like Twitter, Discord, and Reddit. Our blockchain marketing strategies combine media outreach, storytelling, and grassroots engagement to cut through the noise and connect with your audience.

Why is Web3 PR critical for growing a project?

In Web3, trust is everything. Web3 PR goes beyond basic publicity — it establishes credibility, educates users, and attracts partners, investors, and communities. Our team specializes in building narratives that resonate with both crypto‑native audiences and mainstream stakeholders, helping projects scale with confidence as part of a holistic crypto marketing approach.

How is MarketAcross different from other crypto PR and marketing agencies?

We’re a crypto‑native team trusted by industry leaders like Binance, Polygon, and Polkadot. Unlike general agencies, we focus exclusively on blockchain PR, Web3, and crypto marketing, blending deep industry relationships with hands‑on strategy to deliver campaigns that get noticed and drive real results. One key difference is our results‑based retainer model — instead of charging blind monthly fees, we define exactly what deliverables you’ll receive and ensure you see tangible outcomes. In our world, finishing a month with no meaningful media hits simply doesn’t exist.

Do you also provide advisory on blockchain PR strategy and positioning?

Absolutely. Beyond execution, we help refine your messaging, sharpen your positioning, and craft a compelling blockchain PR strategy that aligns with your project’s goals. From early‑stage startups to leading protocols, we advise clients on how to communicate effectively in the Web3 space.

What should I look for when choosing a blockchain PR agency?

Look for proven experience in the crypto space, strong relationships with tier‑1 publications, and a track record of successful campaigns for top blockchain projects. At MarketAcross, we’ve partnered with leading names like Binance, Polygon, and Polkadot, helping them achieve meaningful media coverage and lasting growth through tailored PR and crypto marketing strategies.

Do you work with early‑stage startups or only established blockchain companies?

We work with projects at all stages — from startups preparing for their first token launch to established blockchain enterprises scaling globally. Our crypto marketing and PR solutions are tailored to your size, milestones, and growth objectives.

How can I get started with MarketAcross?

Getting started is simple. Book a strategy call with our team to share your goals and upcoming milestones. We’ll create a tailored plan to help your project gain visibility, build credibility, and grow within the Web3 ecosystem.
