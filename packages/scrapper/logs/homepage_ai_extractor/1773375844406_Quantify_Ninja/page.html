The honest reason

Why we are closing

We believe our users deserve a clear, honest explanation – not a vague announcement. Here is what led us to this decision.

01
Amazon SP-API ecosystem changes

Amazon’s Selling Partner API has gone through significant changes in its architecture, permissions, and terms of use. Keeping up with these changes requires deep ongoing engineering investment – and not all capabilities we relied on remain available to third-party platforms in the same way they once were.

02
PII restrictions for invoicing

Amazon has restricted how personally identifiable information (PII) – including buyer details – can be accessed through the API. This directly affects our invoicing service, which depended on that data to generate compliant invoices on behalf of sellers. Without it, we can no longer offer this feature in a meaningful way.

03
Pricing and maintenance reality

The cost of maintaining a multi-feature platform that stays current with Amazon’s evolving ecosystem has grown considerably. We reached a point where the pricing required to sustain QuantifyNinja responsibly would no longer be accessible or fair to the sellers we built it for. That is not a trade-off we are willing to make.
