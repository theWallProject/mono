Building a Single-Chip Quantum Processor
Creating a Quantum Moore’s Law

Single chip, thousands of qubits

Today’s quantum solutions demand significant cooling and power, yet they still fall short of unlocking quantum computing’s full potential.

Quantum Transistors is building the solution the world needs— a scalable, accessible, and affordable quantum processor to fully realize quantum computing’s promise.

Pioneering Quantum Integration

Our quantum technology leverages decades of investment and innovation in the semiconductor industry.

At Quantum Transistors, we’ve built our own diamond fabrication facility, enabling advanced R&D and small-scale production. By combining solid-state natural qubits with photonic interconnects, we achieve integration levels previously unimagined, bringing quantum computing into a practical and universal single-chip form.
