A LEADING MANUFACTURER OF COMPOSITE MATERIALS

Custom Composite Manufacturing in Israel

"Precision-built composite solutions for aerospace, defense, medical, and industrial applications".

Founded in 2005, Comparts specialises in custom composite manufacturing, delivering high-performance parts, assemblies, and full systems to meet the most demanding requirements. Based in Israel and trusted by global OEMs and Tier 1 contractors, our solutions combine engineering excellence with agile production capabilities.

Accomplishments

Build-to-Spec. Build-to-Print. Built to Perform.

Whether you need build-to-print components, build-to-spec assemblies, or turnkey composite systems, Comparts provides end-to-end support—from design and prototyping to production and integration.

  • Reverse engineering & 3D measurement

  • Short-run and serial production capabilities

OUR TEAM

Comparts’ operational infrastructure comprises experienced industry managers, engineers, QA, and logistics. We provide our customers with a complete range of services, from computer-aided design, analysis, testing, and tool manufacturing to developing prototypes and serial production.

The R&D division of Comparts’ is dynamic, rapidly adapting to the evolving industry requirements. Our engineering experts identify existing deficiencies and failures and adjust and develop superior solutions that provide our customers with the ultimate parts and components.
