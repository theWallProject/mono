The Shift to Bio-Based Alternatives

The global push for sustainability is accelerating the demand for alternatives to fossil-based feedstock. Regulatory initiatives and corporate ESG commitments are driving the adoption of renewable biopolymers and biofuels, while consumer demand for eco-friendly products continues to rise.

The Castor Bean Advantage – Nature’s Sustainable Powerhouse

Castor has emerged as a leading veg-oil crop, thriving in semi-arid, marginal soils while delivering high-yield, cost-effective production. Its resilience supports rural economies and conserves fertile farmland for food cultivation, ensuring a sustainable and reliable supply chain.

However, challenges remain. Traditional small-scale farming and outdated cultivation practices limit scalability, while non-optimized strains reduce efficiency. These barriers prevent the industry from fully meeting the growing demand for bio-based solutions – an opportunity primed for transformative innovation.

The castor seed embodies the perfect balance of performance and sustainability.

Bridging Supply and Demand

At the core of our technology is GeneRator AI, a cutting-edge computational biology engine that accelerates and accurates breeding, cutting traditional development time. Expanding Catalog of High-Performance Commercial Varieties

With R&D sites in Israel, Kenya, and Brazil, Casterra has developed elite castor varieties tailored for commercial-scale farming. Optimized for high oil yields and rapid growth cycles of under 150 days.

Casterra has successfully commercialized these high-performing varieties and established robust seed production operations in Africa and Brazil. By leveraging AI-driven breeding and extensive genomic and phenotypic data, our elite seeds are engineered for superior results.
