Welcome to Savransky Partners LLC

 

We are an international CPA firm specializing in the tax needs of dual citizens residing in Israel and American expats worldwide. Our firm also offers comprehensive tax and accounting services to Israeli investors in the US and American investors in Israel, making it a first-choice for companies with cross-border operations. We provide our clients with expert services in the following areas:

  • US & Israeli tax preparation (individual & corporate)
  • Child tax credit
  • Helping clients get back to compliance penalty-free (IRS streamlined program)
  • Representation before the IRS and the Israeli income tax authority
  • International tax planning
  • US & Israeli Bookkeeping and Payroll
