I.Sherman Industries: Quality, Service, Professionalism

1. Product Development (NPI)

Characterization and guidance from the concept stage to the feasibility stage, support through the CDR/PDR/SDR stages, and the transition to mass production.
2. Configuration Management

A structured plan for the transition from development to production is a necessary step in moving from single prototype production to serial production of any new product.
3. Mechanical Production

Design and construction of machines intended to perform a series of actions to transform raw materials into a finished product.
4. Supply Chain Management

Maintaining efficiency and effectiveness in today’s competitive and dynamic market through optimal infrastructure and management tools to maximize performance throughout the product lifecycle.
5. Global Procurement

Extensive experience and necessary expertise in global procurement to meet the challenges of the modern era.

6. Turnkey System Assembly

Providing development and production services under the Turnkey model, offering clients complete end-to-end solutions under one roof.
7. Quality Control System

Adherence to the highest standards of quality, reliability, and dependability in production. All manufacturing systems are subjected to rigorous examination by leading certification bodies in Israel and globally.

About the Company

With over 40 years of experience and expertise in all supply chain components and product lifecycle, I. Sherman has become the chosen development and manufacturing partner for dozens of clients across various industries and economic sectors. The company provides a comprehensive, end-to-end range of development and manufacturing services. Companies and organizations in numerous industries rely on Sherman's advanced development and manufacturing services to manage all their product development activities and product lines, from the concept stage through development, design, engineering, electrical and electronic planning to production and market logistics. Under one roof, I. Sherman offers its clients the full range of services they need for continuous product development, fast market entry with excellent products that meet all international standards, and efficient management of the company’s entire product portfolio.
