The World’s Leading BioAg Fungi
& Soil Carbon Removal Company

Groundwork BioAg, a global bioagriculture company, produces highly-concentrated, cost-effective mycorrhizal inoculants that address the dual challenges of food security and climate change. Grounded in decades of R&D, we were the first to crack the code on the scalable production of mycorrhizal inoculants for both agriculture and durable carbon dioxide removal (CDR).

Our Rootella product line is already at work on millions of hectares worldwide, enhancing crop yields, improving soil fertility and accelerating permanent carbon sequestration. Groundwork BioAg envisions cropland restored to its role as a net carbon retainer, enabling farmers to increase yields, optimize fertilizer use, and earn premium carbon credit revenue.

The Rootella product line and the Rootella Carbon Program are making that vision a reality, today.

Start Growing with Rootella

  • Nature's Hidden Carbon Sink

    In uncultivated soil, mycorrhizal fungi sequester 13.1 gT of carbon annually

    Fungi have been sequestering carbon for over 400M years, fueling the carbon cycle on which life depends

    Today, soil remains one of the largest carbon sinks on the planet

  • The Unraveling of the Underground Carbon Network

    On farmland, industrial practices have destroyed most mycorrhizal fungi, releasing carbon into the atmosphere and degrading soil

    Since the Industrial Revolution, land-use changes have unleashed 785 GtCO₂eq, nearly as much as burning fossil fuels.

    Cropland soils have lost 50-70% of their carbon

    Today’s cropland topsoil is only 31% carbon-saturated, meaning it could still absorb 674 GtCO₂eq if restored

  • Agriculture: the Key to Soil Carbon Removal

    Groundwork BioAg restores mycorrhizae to croplands, helping agriculture meet its powerful carbon sink potential.

    Mycorrhizae are the main pathway of carbon into the soil.

    Applying Rootella® across the world’s 4B acres of croplands could sequester 81 GtCO₂eq in just 15 years
