Welcome to Palad

Palad H.Y Industries Ltd. is a leading Israeli manufacturer of plastic piping - PVC and polyethylene in infrastructure applications for the transportation of most types of fluids. In addition, the plant manufactures pipes for the transportation and distribution of natural gas, as well as pipes used as conduits for communication and power cables. As a large, long-standing and leading manufacturer in its field in Israel, the plant operates with a continuous effort to maintain the highest level of technology and product quality, and also provides a variety of products while meeting all the requirements of local and international standards.

Palad is ISO 9001:2015 certified and produces according to the requirements of international and local standards.

The plant is located in the Southern Industrial Zone of Migdal HaEmek and has over 40 years of experience.
