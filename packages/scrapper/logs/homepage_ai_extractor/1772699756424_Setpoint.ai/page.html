CLIMATE AS A SERVICE

Setpoint’s mission is to optimize indoor climate in commercial buildings, lowering energy consumption and peak shaving demand, without compromising on climate comfort. Setpoint’s solution is offered on a Climate-as-a-Service basis, allowing our clients the benefit of a robust and innovative solution with the 360 support and care of a dedicated customer success team.

AUTOMATING INDOOR CLIMATE
BEYOND CLIMATE CONTROL

Setpoint’s Climate Intelligence solution is revolutionizing the way climate is being controlled in multi-room, multi-user commercial buildings around the globe. Applying sophisticated data models and deep learning algorithms, the fully integrated system allows commercial building operators to monitor and control the climate in every room from any place, for personalized climate comfort.

SMART, SIMPLE PLUG & PLAY SYSTEM

Designed for adaptability, enables control of any air conditioning type or brand, regardless of thermostat terminals available.
