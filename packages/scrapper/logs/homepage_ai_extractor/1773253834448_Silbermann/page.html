SILBERMANN Technologies Ltd.

Silbermann is a leading manufacturer and worldwide supplier of Medical Gas Pipeline Equipment and Medical Gas Systems. Offering a comprehensive range of high-quality & innovative products and integrations.

We design & manufacture: central supply systems, medical gas pendants/booms, medical gas alarm panels, automatic gas manifolds, power columns, bed head units, medical gas outlets (including AGSS), medical gas oxygen regulators, medical flowmeters and more.
