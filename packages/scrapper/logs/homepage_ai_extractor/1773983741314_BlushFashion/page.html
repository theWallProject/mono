About us

Sheryll is the creative force and dedicated owner behind Blush, a brand known for its minimalist and heartfelt wedding designs. With an eye for detail and a deep passion for intimate celebrations, she specializes in transforming simple ideas into unforgettable moments. Sheryll’s commitment to sustainability and personalized touches has made Blush a favorite among modern brides seeking eco-friendly, budget-conscious wedding inspirations. Her innovative vision and thoughtful approach ensure every celebration is as unique as the love story it represents.
