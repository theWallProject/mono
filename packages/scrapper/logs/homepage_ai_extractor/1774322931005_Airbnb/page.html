Displaced families need a safe place to stay.

When a disaster happens, Airbnb.org provides free, emergency housing in Airbnbs for families and first responders in need.
Make a donation

We believe emergency housing should be in a home, not a shelter.

After a disaster, a free stay in a home gives security, dignity, and normalcy — for adults, kids, and pets.

Donations only fund housing. Nothing else.

Our model is unique. Operating costs are covered by Airbnb, so all public donations are used to fund free, emergency stays.

Get involved

There are two main ways to give to help Airbnb.org provide free, emergency housing.

Make a donation

Give a one-time donation or make a monthly contribution that Airbnb.org can use to fund emergency housing.

Host a stay

If you’re an Airbnb host, you can choose to offer your Airbnb at a discounted rate for people impacted by disasters or other crises.
