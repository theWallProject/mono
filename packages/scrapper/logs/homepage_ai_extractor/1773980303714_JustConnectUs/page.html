How justconnectus works

Fast. Free for customers. And 100% private

Looking for a graphic artist, web developer, virtual office assistant, hairdresser, or lawyer – and don't know where to look, or who to ask? JustConnectUs is the community referral app that helps customers find trustworthy local service providers in less than 1 minute!

Here's how you do it:

1. Find

Local service providers recently used by someone you know –
and whose opinion you trust.

2. Ask

Your contact for a recommendation based on their knowledge of you and their experience with the provider.

3. Text

Your service provider privately through the app and get the help you need ASAP.

Read more about how it works

for customers and service providers

For customers

No more searching for local providers online

Get word-of-mouth recommendations about local service providers, from people you know and trust.

  • Find the providers you need without fielding tens of calls, emails, and social media messages.
  • No ads or fake reviews mean no time wasted figuring out who's really good – and who just sounds good.
  • Communicate discreetly with contacts and providers through direct messages, not community forums.

For service providers

No more energy wasted on online advertising

Get access to a steady stream of word-of-mouth referrals from loyal customers.

  • No more spending hours – or thousands of dollars – every month on SEO, digital ads, and social media.
  • No more chasing after poor-fit leads. Just focus on what you do best: good work for good people.
  • Close new business quickly through private texts instead of multiple emails, calls, and online forums.
