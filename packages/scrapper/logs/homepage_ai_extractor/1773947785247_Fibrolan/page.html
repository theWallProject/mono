Welcome to Fibrolan

Providing Carrier Ethernet and Timing Solutions since 1996

Fibrolan specializes in the development and manufacturing of Timing & Synchronization systems and Carrier Ethernet switches. The company's unique framework allows a rapid adjustment to the demands of this ever-changing industry. Client-specific needs can be met efficiently, thanks to a close and immediate connection between management, product development, and production teams.

 

Fibrolan Products

Fibrolan delivers integrated access systems and solutions for carriers, service providers and mobile operators.
Fibrolan ‘s wide product portfolio offers a comprehensive toolbox for various Mobile Backhaul/Fronthaul and Carrier Ethernet Access applications through a wide range of  EDDs and Agreggators for up to multiple 10Gbps rates and strong set of dedicated OAM tools.

Timing & Synchronization. Most of Fibrolan’s access, aggregation and transmission product lines support the most essential SyncE, BITS and PTP protocols with superb accuracy and stability, making them prime building blocks for modern networks and variety of applications.  

Fibrolan  became, over the last few years, a key player in the Timing & Synchronization space. These are augmented by a series of Grandmasters coming at a variety of capacities, protocols, performance levels and costs.

The CES (Circuit Emulation Services) enabled devices seamlessly integrate legacy networks (PDH and SONET/SDH) with modern packet networks permitting extended life of legacy networks while minimizing OPEX.

Last but certainly not least: Simple xWDM transport systems allow operators of data centers, small cells, pipelines, utilities and others utmost simplicity and capacity over existing or minimal new cable plants.
