Why use the CISOteria platform for controlling your cyber strategy?

CISOs face a barrage of threats, security tools, policies, processes and regulations they must control. They need a single knowledge base with a single view and proactive guidance, not just another tool.

CISOteria gives CISOs an efficient way to manage cybersecurity so they gain three things.

Minimize cyber risk

With visibility, governance, and automation CISOs can manage cybersecurity efficiently. This results in more informed and prioritized decisions that reduce cyber business risk.

Focus on people and processes

Most cyber breaches are a result of failed processes. CISOteria’s built-in process recommendations help to establish and enforce stronger processes.

Cost-effective compliance

CISOteria automates the gap analysis stage, recommends tasks and recurring activities, and monitors their completeness to ensure full compliance.

How CISOteria works

Knowledge is power! CISOteria collects cybersecurity data 24/7 from external and internal sources, calculates the cyber business risk, and leverages experienced CISOs and trained LLM to recommend activities that reduce risk and enforce cyber resilience.

24/7 data collection

CISOteria knowledge base gets data 24/7 from industry i.e., vulnerabilities, news, announcements, regulations, etc. as well as company-implemented products, policies, and processes.

Calculate the cyber business risk

With its patented approach, CISOteria calculates the business risk for each business asset and critical process. It identifies the root cause of changes to the risk and recommends risk reduction activities.

Track enforcement and cyber risk

The root cause of breaches is failed processes. CISOteria tracks and detects flawed processes. It then alerts stakeholders and provides a plan of action to mitigate and reduce the cyber business risk.
