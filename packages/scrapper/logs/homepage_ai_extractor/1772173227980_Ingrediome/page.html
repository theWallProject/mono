MISSION

NATURE IDENTICAL TASTE & TEXTURE

MEAT CONFIDENCE. We produce meat made from actual muscle proteins, delivering the same authentic eating experience as traditional meat. Unlike other companies that create meat-like products, our approach uses real muscle proteins for a truly genuine taste and texture.

ZERO
SACRIFICE

A solution with ZERO compromise of the epicurean experience, introducing the sun fed Meat Protein known by connoisseurs and chefs alike, to satisfy the savory sensation complexity worthy of an exceptional meat experience.

The Process

HARVESTING THE SUN

Nutrients from the most pure energy source on the planet.We kraft the leanest and cleanest meat and fish products without sacrifice, using sunlight and CO2 (direct from the air) to make nature-identical meat.

WHY

We’ll save 500 million tons of CO2 each year.

By producing meat decoupled from animal agriculture and exploitation, we empower billions of meat consumers to make healthier, more sustainable choices for both themselves and the planet.
