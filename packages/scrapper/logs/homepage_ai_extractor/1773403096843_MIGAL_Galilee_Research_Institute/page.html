RegenUp: A New Model for Agricultural Innovation in Northern Israel

RegenUp, launched in 2024 by MIGAL Galilee Research Institute and regional partners, is a new implementation-driven model designed to bridge the agri-tech adoption gap by placing farmers at the center of technology development and on-farm validation. What began as a wartime initiative supporting seven farmers in northern Israel is now expanding into a national model for regenerative, climate-smart agriculture
