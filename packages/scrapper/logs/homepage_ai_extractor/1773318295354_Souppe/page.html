Our Philosophy

The name Souppe reflects our belief that building strong investment portfolios is like composing a refined system, where each element has a role and strength emerges from how they work together.

Each position is an ingredient within a broader composition. Too much of one element can distort the whole. Too little variety weakens its resilience. The craft lies in knowing what to add, in what proportion, and for what purpose.

Research Foundation

Grounded in Decades
of Academic Research

There is no artificial intelligence in Souppe. The entire system is built on statistical analysis: factor decomposition, regime-conditional measurement and correlation analysis. We chose this approach because portfolio risk is quantifiable, and the mathematics that measure it are transparent, reproducible and decades old.

Souppe's methodology is grounded in peer-reviewed factor research spanning decades of financial economics. Our research paper explores the principles and methodologies of portfolio management since the beginning of the 20th century, forming the basis of Souppe's innovation.
