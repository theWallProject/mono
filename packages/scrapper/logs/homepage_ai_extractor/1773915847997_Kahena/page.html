Who We Are

Kahena is a search marketing agency that’s driven by a never-ending ambition to help our clients get discovered on the web. We’re extraordinarily passionate and our team of technical marketers believes in fostering deeply personal, meaningful partnerships in order to deliver better performance.

Our world-class skills, know-how, and SEO + PPC killer instincts don’t hurt, either.

What We Do

SEO

Best-in-class search engine optimization services focused on sustainable, data-driven growth. Beyond SERP rankings – business driven KPIs & ROI.

PPC

Complete industry leading online advertising strategy and execution: paid search, retargeting, programmatic display, and paid social.

Ecommerce

From daily organic & paid search optimization to the most advanced replatformings, expect enterprise-level eCommerce experience.

B2B Lead Gen

Targeted B2B search marketing alongside deep integrated feedback loops with marketing & sales teams. MQLs & ROI are our north stars.
