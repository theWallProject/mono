HARNESSING TECHNOLOGY TO IMPROVE SPINE CARE

The Dreal® is a nonlinear, high speed, high torque power tool designed for spine surgeries. It is the first and only commercially available power tool with a curved tip, shielded to protect nearby nerves and tissues. FDA-approved and protected by over 20 registered patents, the Dreal® sets a new standard in spinal surgery innovation.

ABOUT CAREVATURE

Carevature Technologies Ltd. was founded with a primary goal to improve the tools available to spine surgeons for spinal decompression.

Decompression of the neural elements and spinal canal is the most critical aspect of surgical intervention for degenerative spinal conditions, and yet for decades there has been little to no innovation in improving the tools for spinal decompression. Carevature Technologies is changing that!

Quality and Regulation
Carevature Technologies is ISO 13485:2016 certified. It manufactures its products under the highest standards, with each unit functionally tested before being released for packaging and sterilization.

The Dreal® is FDA Approved.
