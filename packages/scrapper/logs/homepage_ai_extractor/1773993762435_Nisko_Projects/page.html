Our Products

Active

Nisko Projects has the distribution technologies to cost-effectively deliver products from the world´s leading solutions suppliers for: Analog, RF, Power, Lighting, Medical, Military Industrial & Communication.

Passive

Nisko Projects is the largest Israeli distributor of passive components. The combination of inventory and the vendors we represent allows us to provide you practically all your passive needs.

RF/Communications

Nisko Projects represents world class suppliers with products addressing a wide variety of unique application requirements including IC's and SoC's for Broadband Access, Digital Home and High Speed Optical Networks, Optical Transceivers & Modules, TE 4G/3G small cell & RF cards solutions. RF power devices: LDMOS, GaN for various applications we offer the whole spectrum of network solutions through market leadership in: POTS, VoIP, xDSL Broadband, Gigabit Ethernet, Wi-Fi, GPON ,T/E Carrier, ISDN and Fiber Optic Transceivers and Modules.

EM/Power Supply

The Power supply & Electro Mechanical Group provides a wide range of solutions for Power & control systems, components and assemblies to the Israeli electronics industry. Our experienced application engineering team´s main objective is finding the best technical solutions for board designers and system developers, covering a wide range of applications (both general and customized) in different market segments: telecommunications, military and security, solar energy, industrial automation and consumer electronics products. Our customers include a long list of leading companies in Israel.
