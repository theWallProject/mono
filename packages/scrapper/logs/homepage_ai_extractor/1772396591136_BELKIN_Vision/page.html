Evolving First-Line Treatment Paradigm

 

The Laser in Glaucoma and Ocular Hypertension (LiGHT) study found that SLT is efficacious as a primary procedure for the treatment of OAG and OHT.1

Recommends that SLT should be considered as an initial treatment option for mild or moderate OAG and OHT patients3

 

Recommends 360o SLT as first-line treatment for people with newly diagnosed OHT and COAG4
