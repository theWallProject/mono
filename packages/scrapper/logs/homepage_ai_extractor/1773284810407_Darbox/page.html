Some Facts About Darbox

Environmentally Friendly

Darbox containers are designed to enable you to get closer to this goal

UN Packaging

UN packaging is certified after successfully passing tests including drop tests and stacking tests. 

100 % Recyclable

Darbox containers are made of 100% mono-materials. Both HDPE and PP allow easy recycling.

Ground Water Neutral

Water Neutrality has impacts for the Local and Regional Plans as well as Development Management.

Reusable

Heavy-duty, open-top, all-plastic containers are resistant to forklift impacts and related performance incidents.

Design for Sustainability

Injection-moulded plastic packaging is designed precisely to specifications, so the package can fit the product contents without extra waste and bulk.

The Sky's the Limit

MAXIMUM FIT TO PALLET

Cost-efficient storage, logistics and transport.

Improved sustainability performance.

  • Save valuable space with compact, nestable packs when empty -- Save up to 70%
  • Maximize product packed per load when filled. Certified to stack up to 3 meters high.
    Save up to 30-45% outbound costs.
  • Save time and labor in production and in warehouse. User-friendly and secure press-on closure with integral tamper evidence.

WE ARE ECO-FRIENDLY

Today’s market demands a safe, cost-effective and eco-efficient use of all resources.

It is not unusual for transport, handling and storage requirements to represent a significant part (>35%) of the overall packaging cost. Darbox containers are designed to minimize these requirements by reducing costs by up to 70% inbound (when empty) and 30-45% outbound (when full) when compared to traditional round drums. This further reduces both the total cost throughout the supply chain and also their overall eco-footprint. 

More efficient logistics and supply chain = less energy and eco-resources used.
