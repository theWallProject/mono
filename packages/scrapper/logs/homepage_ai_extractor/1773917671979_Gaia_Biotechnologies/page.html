Gaia Hypothesis
The Gaia Hypothesis proposed by James Lovelock suggests that living organisms on the planet interact with their surrounding inorganic environment to form a synergetic and self-regulating system that created, and now maintains, the climate and biochemical conditions that make life on Earth possible
We believe that humanity is a part of this self-regulating system and has the power to revert its destructive impact on the global ecosystem to create a sustainable future for all of us

Synthetic Biology as a solution
Synthetic biology is a field of science that involves redesigning organisms for useful purposes by engineering them to have new abilities. Synthetic biology researchers and companies around the world are harnessing the power of nature to solve problems in medicine, manufacturing and agriculture

We believe that with the tools of Synthetic Biology we will be able to redesign the way we build stuff, starting from basic materials all the way to complex, novel solutions most of major industries
