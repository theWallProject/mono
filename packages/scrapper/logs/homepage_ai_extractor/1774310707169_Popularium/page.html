Meet the chaos legends

The universe of Chaos Agents, known as the Firmament, is vast and varied in its planets, species, cultures, and their motivations. Chaos Shards govern and shape many aspects of the socio-political and economic events of the Firmament, making Chaos Agents and their Brokers—such as you—key players in this ongoing cosmic theater.
