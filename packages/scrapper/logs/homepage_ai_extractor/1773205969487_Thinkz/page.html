Thinkz is Solving Urban Struggles

Meeting 21‑st‑century expectations isn’t just hard—it’s mission‑critical.

From mobility and tourism to sustainability and public health, today’s cities must excel on every front—yet five persistent hurdles keep getting in the way :

  • Gridlock & Overcrowding – traffic jams, packed buses and endless parking hunts sap time and patience.

  • Pollution & Poor Air Quality – exhaust and particulate matter undermine public health and climate goals.

  • Urban Stress & Low Satisfaction – noise, unpredictability and long queues erode residents’ wellbeing.

  • Crowded Public Spaces – popular squares and attractions risk becoming unusable at peak times.

  • Tough Climate Targets – Fit for 55 and COP 2030/2050 demand tangible emissions cuts, fast.

Thinkz FAQ

Covering Urban Experience, Smart Tourism, and Sustainability / CO₂ Reduction

CONTACT US

What exactly is Thinkz?

Thinkz is a cloud-based smart-city platform that ingests data from any IoT sensor or open feed, applies patented Live Data Verification (LDV) to ensure accuracy, and serves real-time insights to municipal teams, citizen apps and tourist services—all in one place.

How does Thinkz improve the day-to-day urban experience for residents?

By publishing live information on parking availability, bus arrivals, micromobility, air quality and crowd levels, Thinkz lets citizens plan journeys in seconds, cut wasted time and avoid stress hotspots—turning reactive commutes into proactive choices.

What value does Thinkz bring to smart-tourism programmes?

Visitors gain instant access to transport options, attraction status and crowd-free routes. Cities, in turn, spread footfall more evenly, protect heritage sites and boost local spending through seamless, app-ready data feeds.

How much CO₂ can Thinkz really save?

Our calculator shows that a city of 100 000 residents can avoid up to 31,183 t CO₂ each year through Solutions like parking-search optimisation and real-time public-transport insights—worth roughly €2.5 m at today’s EU carbon prices.

Which sensors and data sources does the platform support?

Thinkz’s universal collector accepts barrier-gate feeds, kerb-side parking sensors, cameras, GBFS, GTFS-RT, OCPI charging data, environmental probes, and more—any protocol, any vendor.

How fast can we deploy and see impact?

Because Thinkz is SaaS and leverages existing infrastructure, most mid-sized cities go live in ≤ 5 weeks. Tangible benefits—reduced cruising, shorter queues, cleaner routes—begin as soon as citizens access the live feeds.

Is the data truly reliable and GDPR-compliant?

Yes. LDV cross-checks every datapoint against historical patterns and neighbouring sensors, flagging anomalies in real time. All data are processed and hosted within the EU under strict GDPR controls.

    What procurement paths are available?

    In France, Thinkz is accessible via the UGAP Territoires de Demain catalogue through our partner SCC, eliminating the need for a tender. Elsewhere, we support standard RFPs or pilot agreements tailored to local rules.
