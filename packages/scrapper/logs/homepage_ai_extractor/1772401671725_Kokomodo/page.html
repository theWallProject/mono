Cacao’s future  — is in danger
Cacao, the ‘food of the gods’, faces a risk of extinction as our planet gets warmer. Kokomodo is born from a profound passion for preserving the supply of cacao.

Unstable Supply
Unstable weather, diseases, and dry seasons lower cacao yields.

INCONSISTENT QUALITY
Diverse farming and handling practices, along with traces of heavy metals & pesticides, affect bean quality and safety.

Complex Supply Chain
Logistically complex supply chains & high carbon emissions from transport.

Solving the industrie's pains:
Ensure Climate proof Production gain Stability
Our controlled environment ensures resilience against extreme weather and climate change, maintaining stable year-round yields. This innovation stabilizes supply and prices, benefiting both producers and consumers.
Solving the industrie's pains:
Guarantee  quality earn CUSTOMER trust
By cultivating cacao in a controlled environment, we ensure a high-quality, safe, and consistent product, maintaining customer trust.
Solving the industrie's pains:
Short  supply logistics to minimize costs & Emissions
By localizing production we minimize carbon emissions from transport, a considerable sustainability concern within the industry. We also tackle economic issues such as supply chain delays, ensuring fresher, higher-quality products.
