WHAT’S PREVENTING YOUR COMPANY’S GROWTH?

Picking & Delivering Mistakes

Errors in the picking and delivering lead to expensive returns, additional repackaging, shipping, warehouse labor costs, customer service complaints and sometimes even loss of sales.

Long Delivery Time

Online shopping and home delivery are at an all-time high. However, if delayed systems do not meet customer demands, they will be lost to your competitors.

Inability To Meet Demand

Traditional picking and delivery machines require lots of space, resulting in high overheads. That’s why warehouses are usually located outside of town, far from customers, often resulting in longer delivery times, poor customer service and, eventually, loss of profits.

Long Down Time

Events such as planned maintenance and unplanned circumstances tend to impact efficiency, slowing down order fulfillment processes, throughput and revenue, ultimately impacting your bottom line.
