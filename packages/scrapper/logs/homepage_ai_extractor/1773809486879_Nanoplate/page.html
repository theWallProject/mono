The nanotechnology company Nanoplate Itd. was established as a spinoff of the Israeli company Interplate Itd. by two cofounders Gabriel Schonwald and Ricardo Burstein, both senior chemical engineers by trade.

The Company deals with the research and development of innovative and economical plating nanotechnology based on scientifically proven results. The company focuses on the great potential such applications might have in the field of nanotechnology with emphasis on – environmental protection, health and hygiene, green construction, medical applications, tracking and detection technology and geometric plating by way of developing unique products such as: self-cleaning surfaces, air purification spraying methods, energy efficient plating and more.
