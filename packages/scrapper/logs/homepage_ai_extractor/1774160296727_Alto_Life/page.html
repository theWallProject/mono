ABOUT ALTO LIFE

AMERICA’S FIRST ADVANTAGE CLUB FOR PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES AND THEIR FAMILIES
ALTO IS A REAL STORY

Hello! My name is Moshe, and I’d like to personally welcome you to Alto, America’s first Advantage Club for people with disabilities and their families.

Before I explain exactly what Alto is, there’s something I’d like to share with you. There is a saying that behind every idea there is a story. Well, here is mine:

In July 2008, my brother Yoav and his wife Ruthi welcomed the arrival of their youngest baby boy – Erez. You may have already heard about Erez. Maybe not. I have to tell you, he really is a wonderful kid. So very special, and so passionate about life.

Erez was born with a rare disease and a complex cognitive and physical disability. However, it is his smile and his challenging journey that has helped our family to learn about never giving up. Erez is inspiring. He is a fighter. And with his incredible zest for life and his endless inner strength, he enriches and empowers the lives of every person that he has ever met.

More than 1 billion people in the world live with some form of disability, 57 million in the U.S alone. Their struggles and challenges are seen daily. Their journey’s have made them and us what we are today and in most cases closer and stronger.

Joining Alto means that together, a huge group of unique individuals can become an amazing buying power of shared needs and interest. A big family that works as one to benefit all!

We hope that you and your family enjoy Alto and we can’t wait for you to join the club.

Alto, Your Advantage For Life!

Moshe Gaon
CEO & Co-Founder
