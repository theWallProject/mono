Duda platform FAQ

  • Who is Duda for?

    Duda is a website builder catering exclusively to professionals; freelance website builders, digital marketing agencies, SaaS companies, etc. We create products tailored specifically to these workflows, designed to help you and businesses like you build and offer better sites faster. Agencies who switch to Duda consistently tell us they’re building websites in a fraction of the time, and are saving even more time managing their catalog of client websites after they’ve been built. You can learn more about our existing customers by visiting our success stories library.

  • Does Duda offer a free trial?

    Duda offers a 14-day free trial of the “White Label plan,” which includes some of the platform’s best features like a fully end-to-end white label editor, extensive team and client collaboration, Duda’s AI Stack, and more. You can get started today for free, no credit card necessary.

  • What AI features does Duda offer?

    Duda offers a comprehensive suite of AI features designed to accelerate professional workflows. Duda’s AI Stack is informed by various aspects of your clients’ sites including the content of the pages, the business name and description of the client, and more. Builders can generate and modify text, improve their SEO, recommend additional pages, improve page design, create custom widgets, enhance client onboarding, spin up entire multi-page sites, connect to third-party tools via MCP, and more. Discover every AI feature Duda has to offer.

  • How does Duda improve my SEO & AEO?

    Websites built on Duda are born SEO- & AEO-ready. They’re lightning fast by design, with the highest average Core Web Vital scores in the industry, and are easily read by bots and crawlers thanks to auto-generated schema markup and sitemaps. IndexNow support means any changes to your site are communicated to search engines and AI platforms directly, while technical features like server-side rendering increases the odds those changes get indexed as quickly as possible. Duda’s Site Audit dashboard furthers your ranking odds with smart advice to improve your pages, like detection systems for broken links and missing metadata. This dashboard provides one-click solutions and AI-tools to help further optimize your sites for search engines, AI tools, voice assistants, and everything in between. Learn more about how Duda helps your sites get discovered.


  • Is Duda right for my business?

    Duda is built for businesses that sell and manage websites at scale. If your growth depends on launching high-quality sites quickly, managing them efficiently, and protecting your margins as you expand, Duda is designed for you. We support digital marketing agencies, freelancers, and SEO professionals managing dozens- or thousands- of clients, as well as SaaS platforms, point-of-sale systems, media companies, hosting providers, and domain registrars that embed websites into their core offering. If websites are part of your revenue model, Duda gives you the tools to standardize delivery, collaborate across teams, integrate with your systems, and scale with control- all in a predictable, scalable, and easily maintainable way.

  • Can I use custom code with Duda?

    Yes. Duda offers extensive support for custom code, as well as built-in ways to fully integrate your custom code with Duda’s intuitive drag-and-drop editing canvas. Dev mode lifts the hood on your sites, revealing the frontend code Duda’s editor generates to create your site, while the native HTML widget allows you to place code from within the visual editor. Duda’s Custom Widget builder allows you to package your custom code into reusable elements with content and design customization options similar to the platform’s out of the box widgets. The snippets API endpoint enables power users to inject code into existing sites programmatically from beyond the editor. Explore all of the ways Duda empowers developers to build truly unique experiences.


  • How does Duda help businesses scale?

    Duda knocks down the most common barriers professionals face when scaling their website business. Features like “Connected Data” and “Global Themes” enable pros to change text, colors, and fonts once everywhere on their site, while “Dynamic Pages” and Duda’s AI Assistant make creating new pages entirely automatic. Duda even provides tools to accelerate agency workflows outside of the editor, like the integrated “Client Content Collection form” built to accelerate onboarding. The biggest time savers, though, are the things professionals don’t have to do. Duda eliminates the need for ongoing technical maintenance by centralizing the infrastructure. In short, Duda handles hosting and security, bar any custom code, so that you can worry less about your old sites and more about your next site. Customers tell us they spend 50% less time building websites and 75% less time maintaining them compared to their previous website building platform. Learn more about Duda’s website builder.

  • Does Duda offer any training or support?

    Duda proudly offers an award-winning support team and ever-growing “Duda University” product education portal, in addition to more business-oriented and advice-based training like free webinars and blog articles. Educational materials also exist within the platform, and are available to your customers as well as white labeled assets. For enterprise customers with more boutique needs, dedicated account managers and technical account managers are available to provide white-glove support.

  • How does Duda integrate with SaaS platforms?

    SaaS companies leverage many features of Duda’s platform, like the fully end-to-end white label capabilities, extensive REST API, and injectable code to create a seamless website building experience that nestles thoughtfully into their solution. Features like SSO and bi-directional data synchronization make for a totally frictionless experience for end-users, while Duda’s Custom Widget Builder and Custom Templates makes integrating your core technology exceptionally easy for your customers. SaaS companies who offer websites with Duda report 25% lower customer churn rates, higher product adoption rates, 29% higher monthly recurring revenue, and greater customer satisfaction scores. Speak to a representative to learn more specifically how Duda would fit into your platform.
