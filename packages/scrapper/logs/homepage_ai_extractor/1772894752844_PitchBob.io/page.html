PitchBob is designed for aspiring entrepreneurs, helping them iteratively create a large number of necessary documents at the beginning of their business idea development. It saves a lot of time and helps overcome the blank page problem.

How does PitchBob work?

The product is a step-by-step (question-answer) AI chatbot with its own methodology and dialogue structure. It’s important to follow this structure to achieve a quality result.

Why is the quality of the generated documents important?
