ABOUT US

Deldent was founded by the joint CEO’s Drs. Alan and Julian Edel who immigrated to Israel from the UK with their respective families in 1975. The aim of the company is to produce a quality range of products with unique features and benefits for the esthetic and preventive dentistry market.

DENTAL SOLUTIONS

Deldent Company develops and markets advanced and high-quality equipment for the dental medicine sphere, hence prompting the emergence of progressed dentistry solutions for all dental patients and clinicians alike.
