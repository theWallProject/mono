Our Products?

Agriculture

At Ginegar, we partner growers and developers with the perfect solution for their crops, climate, and terrain conditions, finding the right solution for every need. We offer innovative solutions for optimizing and managing light, climate, growing conditions and resources, ensuring proven production & yields for growers all over the world. All while promoting sustainability with our environmentally conscious pesticide alternatives and biodegradable mulch films. All of our products use top-of-the-line materials and have unique mechanical, optical, and thermal characteristics. They ensure greater durability, better dust resistance, and excellent resistance to weather conditions.
