What data sources do you use?

We aggregate data from verified nonprofit registries, government databases, and direct organizational partnerships. Our sources are updated regularly to ensure maximum accuracy and relevance.

How fresh is the data?

Our platform refreshes data continuously, with most sources updated weekly. Critical information like funding status and organizational changes are processed within 24 hours of detection.

What's the difference between free and premium?

Free accounts provide basic organizational profiles and limited search capabilities. Premium unlocks full research tools, deep analytics, and comprehensive export features for serious impact professionals.

Can I export research?

Premium users can generate professional PDF reports, export data in multiple formats, and create custom research packages with our advanced export tools.

How do you protect privacy?

We follow strict data governance protocols, anonymizing sensitive information and complying with international data protection regulations. Your research remains confidential and secure.
