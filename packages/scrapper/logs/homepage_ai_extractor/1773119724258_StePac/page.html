Display Is In The Details

Every aspect about your produce and packaging processes matters to us.
To determine the best post-harvest packaging solution for you, we look at:

  • Produce type
  • Produce weight
  • Produce quality
  • Harvest maturity/ripeness
  • Business model: Bulk, food service, or retail
  • Packaging format and dimensions
  • Postharvest handling and packing
  • Cold chain conditions (post-harvest protocol)
  • Supply chain length
  • Printed/non-printed
