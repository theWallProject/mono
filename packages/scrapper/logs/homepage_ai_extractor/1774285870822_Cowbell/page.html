About Cowbell

In a world of unpredictable risks, Cowbell equips organizations with cyber insurance solutions to overcome the inevitable. We make digital risks manageable—helping businesses understand, prevent, and recover with certainty.

At Cowbell, we believe protection should create confidence, not complexity. That’s why we act with urgency, turn setbacks into strengths, and empower brokers and businesses alike.

Because strength isn’t about making noise. It’s the steady hum of protection—always present, always ready.

What we do

Cyber insurance

Adaptive cyber liability insurance that moves at the speed of today’s threats, built to protect businesses from cyber risks before, during, and after an incident.

Professional indemnity

Errors & Omissions coverage for technology professionals who provide expertise, covering liabilities tied to the advice, design, and services they deliver.

Management liability

Insurance protection for leaders and small to medium-sized organizations, addressing exposures that come with critical decision-making in a fast-changing world.

The Cowbell advantage

Cowbell doesn’t just provide insurance; we make businesses stronger.

Our closed-loop approach—assess, insure, respond, and improve—creates a cycle of continuous protection so your resilience is constantly evolving.

Assess

Using Cowbell Factors™, quantify your risk exposure, benchmark it against millions of accounts, and highlight vulnerabilities with early detection signals from Cowbell Insights.

Insure

With your broker, translate risks into tailored coverage. Our AI-assisted underwriting and API-driven quoting deliver custom cyber policies that reflect your unique profile.

Respond

When incidents happen, rely on our cyber-specialist claims team to act fast—delivering rapid recovery services and expert guidance, backed by real-world insights.

Improve

Enhance your resilience with continuous assessments, actionable recommendations, and consultative support from Cowbell Risk Engineering and our Cowbell Rx partner network.
