Our Services
Our Platform provides access to the absolute leaders of the private markets universe - today’s rising stars and tomorrow’s industry leaders.
Direct Secondary Transactions
We identify sellers (mainly former employees/executives) and investors for direct share transactions.
SPV Investment Programs (Single & Basket)
Provides exposure to Primary and Secondary opportunities by pooling capital from accredited investors to meet minimum thresholds.
Participation in Primary & Tender Rounds
We provide investors with access to primary and tender rounds led by major private tech companies.
Liquidity Programs for Israeli Companies
We design structured liquidity programs in collaboration with company management, enabling organized employee and early investor share sales.
Our Innovative Product
Index-Based Fund

We’re proud to have established Israel’s first index-based fund in partnership with Harel Finance, investing in the 20 largest private tech companies worldwide (exclusive in Israel).

The fund is based on an innovative index developed by Morningstar, offering investors lower barriers to entry, diversified exposure, and a benchmark-driven strategy.

Elephant serves as both initiator and professional fund manager.
