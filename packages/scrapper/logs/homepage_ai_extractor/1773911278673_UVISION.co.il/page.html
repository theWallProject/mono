Services

Comprehensive Software Solutions

Since 2012, UVISION has delivered custom software from architecture to deployment. Today, we combine proven development practices with AI-enhanced workflows and agent-based automation to help you move faster, reduce overhead, and build smarter from day one.

Modernizing Legacy Systems

We don’t just rewrite, we revitalize. UVISION brings modern tools and AI-powered logic into existing systems, upgrading functionality, automating repetitive tasks, and preparing your stack for scalable agent integration, all with minimal disruption.

Integrated Development Teams

Our development teams are AI-ready. Whether leading full-stack delivery or supporting internal dev efforts, we help you adopt AI tools, integrate custom agents, and streamline development using human and machine strengths aligned to your goals.

Specializations

Pioneering Vertical Solutions with UVISION

Ad Tech

We build intelligent advertising platforms that use large language models (LLMs) and AI agents to streamline content generation, automate campaign adjustments, and deliver data-driven optimization — improving ROI with less manual effort.

Health Tech

Our healthcare platforms integrate AI assistants to support clinical workflows, automate documentation, and enhance patient engagement. With secure, scalable infrastructure, we help organizations deliver better care with smarter tools.

Edtech

We develop adaptive learning solutions powered by AI agents and LLMs, enabling personalized learning paths, automated tutoring, and real-time feedback — supporting educators and improving learner outcomes.

Travel Tech

From smart booking flows to multilingual customer support agents, we bring automation and personalization into the travel experience. Our solutions reduce operational complexity and enhance customer satisfaction.

Tech Renewal

We modernize legacy systems with minimal disruption, embedding AI agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and workflow automation to help organizations scale capabilities without replacing their entire tech stack.

InsurTech

Our insurance solutions use predictive analytics, LLM-based customer interfaces, and AI-driven process automation to improve risk assessment, streamline claims, and deliver more responsive service.

Gaming & VR/AR:

We combine creative design with AI-powered interactivity — from intelligent NPCs to procedural storytelling. Our solutions enhance gameplay while optimizing performance for cross-platform experiences.

CyberSecurity

We embed AI into cybersecurity frameworks to detect anomalies, automate responses, and adapt to evolving threats. Our systems combine traditional best practices with intelligent agents for proactive protection.
