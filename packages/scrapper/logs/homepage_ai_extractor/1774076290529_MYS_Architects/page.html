Gindi TLV, Israel

Gindi TLV is an unusual, dense urban project in the Tel Aviv skyline, consisting of 4 towers totaling 1,540 residential units in the historic Wholesale Market area. The towers, a unique composition of three rounded volumes connected by a prominent architectural design in the shape of the clover leaf, provide the functional programmatic and design qualities of the building.

The rounded and dynamic envelope consists of repetitive horizontal elements that emphasize the curves of the building and differentiate the structures from the surrounding low-rise buildings.

Ofer Ramat Aviv Mall, Tel Aviv, Israel

This elegant and prestigious shopping center in North Tel Aviv combines a mall including a selection of luxury brand stores and restaurants, with an office building containing clinics and parking.

The office building emerges above the dominant horizontal lines of the two-story mall, creating a vertical emphasis and an urban landmark.

Suzuki Tower, Tel Aviv, Israel

This slim and elegant office tower is a composition of two shifting shapes highlighted by balconies at each floor. The ground floor contains a large and impressive exhibition space, coffee shops and restaurants, covered by a large, landscaped urban roof used for events by the Suzuki company and the building's residents.

Project Type

MYS Architects specializes in planning projects with a wide range of programs and designations. In all these projects, the firm outlines architectural design that is timeless and elegant. As planners and designers, we offer a wide range of architectural solutions based on extensive experience and knowledge accumulated in the firm over decades.

Our project portfolio includes residential towers, commercial buildings, offices, mixed-use ,leisure and hotel projects, public buildings and interior design stemming from the architectural concept. In addition, the firm specializes in urban planning and design, including town planning, policy plans and environmental analyses.

Sustainability is part of the professional and social fabric of the firm, and we are among the founders of the Israeli Green Building Council (ILGBC). In order to maintain our high standards in this field, we incorporate LEED-certified sustainability experts who specialize in integrating green standardization into our projects.

What we bring to every project

We bring to each project an unprecedented pool of experience, accumulated knowledge, flexibility of thought and creative freshness. Our professional and dedicated staff is our foremost asset and is the main key to our success. Our portfolio includes a long list of award-winning projects, master plans and designs in office, commercial, residential, medical and health buildings, the hospitality and hotel industry, public buildings, and academic university buildings. These projects span the length and breadth of Israel and 32 countries around the world.

MYS Architects is an international Israeli architectural firm, founded following a merger between Yasky & Co. Architects (founded in 1965 by the late Prof. Arch. Avraham Yasky, winner of the Israel Prize for Architecture) and Mor Architects (founded in 1950 by the late Arch. Yitzhak Mor) and considered one of Israel's leading architectural firms.

The firm is responsible for planning some of the most prominent and important projects in Israel, many of which have become architectural icons and synonymous with excellence and innovation. The spirit of the firm's founders and partners is reflected in almost every junction in Israel and the firm's roots reach back to the days of the establishment of the State.
