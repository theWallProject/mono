The Fastest and Most Reliable Software RAID

High-performance RAID solution for the new generation of storage. Your partner in NVMe® performance.

EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE ACHIEVED WITH xiRAID

xiRAID is a high-performance software RAID engine that works with all storage devices and excels when deployed alongside NVMe® or NVMe-oF™ devices. It is the only software solution on the market to drive up to 97% of raw device performance in a computationally heavy RAID5 configuration with a low load on the host CPU and memory footprint.

#3 in global io500

xiRAID has earned a prestigious #3 ranking worldwide in the IO500 Production category and #1 among all Lustre-based systems, powering MEGWARE’s storage system at the Erlangen National HPC Center and outperforming many established storage solutions.

The achievement shows xiRAID’s unmatched speed, resilience, and efficiency in real‑world, production‑grade environments.
