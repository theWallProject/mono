September 2024

First EFS Trillium™ Patient in the USA

Innoventric is thrilled to announce the enrollment of its first Trillium™ EFS patient.
The case was performed at TriStar Centennial Medical Center, in Nashville, TN.
We congratulate Dr. Samuel Horr, Dr. Andrew Goodman, and the exceptional Tristar Centennial Medical Center team. Thank you for your outstanding commitment, professionalism, and teamwork!
The implantation procedure for the experimental Trillium™ system took only 5 minutes (device implantation time), with immediate and significant hemodynamic improvements, such as V-wave CVP reduction of 12 mmHg and a cardiac output increase from 6 L/min to 9.6 L/min.

News
February 2024

First Patients in North America

Innoventric is excited to share the successful treatment of our first two North American patients!
The credit goes to the incredible St. Michael's Hospital team in Toronto, led by Dr. Neil Fam, Dr. Sami Alnasser, and Dr. Geraldine Ong. Thank you for the great professionalism and collaboration!
Both patients had been turned down by many other interventional treatments, including TEER, TTVR, and bi-caval valve replacement. However, with our Unica™, heterotopic cross-caval TR solution, they were successfully treated.
Both procedures were short and straightforward, taking only 5 minutes for device implantation and 10 minutes for skin-to-skin time.
Both procedures did not require general anesthesia and were performed under conscious sedation. Also, echo guidance is not required with Innoventric’s cross-caval technologies, so TEE was not performed.
The post-procedural effect was marked- a significant cardiac output increase and venous pressure reduction.
Both patients had an uneventful procedure and recovery and were discharged from the hospital the next day.
