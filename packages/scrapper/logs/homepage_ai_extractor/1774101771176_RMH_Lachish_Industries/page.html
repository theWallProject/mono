RMH MIXERS

Over 60 Years of Excellence

Founded in 1956, RMH has built a strong legacy of over 60 years in manufacturing high-quality feed mixers for dairy farms worldwide. Committed to excellence, outstanding service, and maximum performance, RMH continues to set industry standards.

With two state-of-the-art production sites in Israel (Sderot) and Poland (AndoTech), along with a European subsidiary (RMH France) serving as a key marketing and operational hub, RMH ensures seamless global reach and support.

In 2023, RMH was recognized for its remarkable achievement of increasing share yield by more than 100% on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange. As a pioneer in its field, RMH is dedicated to continuous innovation, developing advanced models that drive its sustained success.

The company invests heavily in cutting-edge technologies such as Galileo, Dairycs, and AgTech, reinforcing its leadership in the evolving agricultural landscape.
