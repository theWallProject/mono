Privacy Policy

The Company respects the privacy of users of the Website and places great importance on protecting personal information provided to it or collected through the use of the Website. This Privacy Policy is intended to explain, in a transparent manner and in accordance with the provisions of the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 1981, including Amendment No. 13 thereto, how information is collected, used, stored, and the rights available to users in connection with such information.

Use of the Website, including browsing and/or providing information through it, constitutes consent to this Privacy Policy, as updated from time to time.

The information collected through the Website consists of information voluntarily provided by users and information collected automatically. Information provided voluntarily may include, inter alia, full name, email address, telephone number, and any additional information provided by the user when submitting a contact form or otherwise communicating with the Website Management. Users are not legally required to provide such information; however, failure to do so may prevent the Company from responding to inquiries or providing certain services.

In addition, during use of the Website, technical and statistical information is collected automatically, including IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, pages viewed, and other usage data. This information is collected for operational, security, statistical, and analytical purposes, and in order to improve the user experience and the functionality of the Website.

The information collected through the Website is used for the following purposes: contacting users who have initiated contact; responding to inquiries and requests; operating, maintaining, and securing the Website; improving services and content offered through the Website; analyzing usage patterns; and complying with legal obligations and requirements imposed by competent authorities.

Processing of information is carried out based on the user’s consent, the legitimate interests of the Company, and/or a legal obligation applicable to the Company, all in accordance with applicable law.

The Company will not transfer personal information to third parties, except where such transfer is necessary for the operation of the Website and the provision of services (including hosting providers, analytics services, and technical support providers), or where required by law or by a competent authority, or for the purpose of protecting the Company’s legal rights. Such third parties are required to maintain the confidentiality of the information and to use it solely for the purpose for which it was disclosed.

The Website uses cookies and similar technologies for ongoing operation, security, statistical analysis, and improvement of user experience. Some cookies are essential for the proper functioning of the Website, while others are used for analytical or marketing purposes. Users may manage or block cookies through their browser settings or through the cookie management mechanism available on the Website, if applicable.

In accordance with the Protection of Privacy Law, users are entitled to review personal information held about them in the Company’s databases and to request correction or deletion of information that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear, or outdated. Requests in this regard may be submitted by email to: info@tyco.co.il, and will be handled in accordance with applicable law and within a reasonable time.

The Company implements reasonable and accepted technical and organizational security measures to protect personal information in its possession, in order to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

The Company reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time, in accordance with technological, business, or legal developments. The version published on the Website at the time of use shall be the binding version.
