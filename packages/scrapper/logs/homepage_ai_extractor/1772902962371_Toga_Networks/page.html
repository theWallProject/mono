SOMETHING EXTRAORDINARY

Welcome to the dynamic world of Toga Networks, Huawei’s state-of-the-art R&D center in Israel where we don’t just adapt to change but ignite it. Toga Networks provides advanced technology research and high-level design for information and communications technology (ICT) infrastructure and smart devices. Our goal is to create a truly interconnected world that embraces both the present and the future. Here, you’re not just a small piece of a large system – you are an essential player in our mission to shape the future of communications and smart devices. With a team of over 450 talented experts, a relentless commitment to innovation, and access to the latest advancements in technology, we are paving the way for a better-connected future.

What we do

WE’RE DEFINING THE FUTURE
OF CONNECTIVITY

Our innovative solutions across various fields of technology related to hardware, software, cloud, and AI, cater to a wide range of markets, including enterprise, telecom, devices, smart cars and homes. As one of Huawei’s top research and development centers globally, Toga Networks has an impressive track record in designing the groundbreaking products of tomorrow.

INTELLIGENT CLOUD

Our intelligent cloud teams play a significant role in designing the future of the intelligent cloud computing system, empowered by artificial intelligence (AI) for every type of intelligent application and system.

SMART INTELLIGENT NETWORK

Our teams take part in innovating the intelligent network & network services including relevant solutions, whether AI-based or other advanced solutions relating to data security, load balancing, intelligent connectivity, self-managed and self-adapting networks.

LLM AND GENERATIVE AI

Our teams use LLM and generative AI to help develop ICT components and applications including video to text generators, and other next generation solutions for everyday products such as smart cars, optical solutions, cameras, IoT, and more.

ADVANCED SILICON

We take part in designing the next level components of future generation chipsets and ASIC & SOC processors, requiring out-of-the-box thinking to cater for a wide variety of uses and technological products.
