A Simple Path to Superior Cognitive Assessment

Cognitive health is complex. Accurate assessment shouldn’t be.

With NeuroTrax, you can precisely measure cognitive function across seven major domains for stronger clinical care plans or research protocols in four straightforward steps:

  • 1 PREPARE

    Create an optimal testing environment.

    NeuroTrax is built for fast administration in clinical offices or research facilities. All that’s required to get started is a quiet room and a computer.

  • 2 PERFORM

    Administer interactive tests in under an hour.

    Examinees complete a series of engaging, adaptive tasks that thoroughly evaluate multiple cognitive domains.

  • 3 PROFILE

    Generate quantified cognitive measurements across domains.

    NeuroTrax provides objective data showing cognitive test performance so clinicians can better evaluate patients and researchers can go deeper with their analysis.

  • 4 PLAN

    Support clinician-directed care plans with objective data.

    Detailed test results and longitudinal tracking features, combined with patient-friendly reports, give clinicians the tools to develop individualized care decisions based on measured outcomes.

From test prep to care plan to tracking over time, NeuroTrax makes comprehensive cognitive assessment fast and easy
