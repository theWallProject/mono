The Grid Was Built for Yesterday

Our current electrical grid was designed for a world of predictable, fixed loads of homes, office buildings and factories. However, the surge in electric mobility presents an entirely new challenge, demanding a fundamental rethink of how we distribute and manage power: higher power demands, out in the streets and unpredictable.

Unpredictable Demand

Electric vehicles introduce 'spiky' and unpredictable energy demands, taxing a grid built for steady consumption. This creates imbalances and can strain local infrastructure.

High Operational Costs

Manual charging, battery swapping, and the logistics of traditional charging solutions lead to significant operational expenses, reducing fleet availability and battery lifespan.

Downtime & Safety Risks

Vehicles spend valuable time offline for charging. Battery swapping requires charging hubs with high power capacity and double the number of batteries. Fast charging poses fire risks.
