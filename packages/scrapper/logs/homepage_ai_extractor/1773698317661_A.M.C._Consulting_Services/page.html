Sport clubs solutions

A.M.C. has a profound understanding of sport clubs marketing and season tickets sales gathered in over 25 years of working in the sport sector. During the years, we gathered high experience in many aspects of the sport club's needs for information and activities. Our team supports fan's data collection, data cleansing, synchronizing and coordinating all PR, marketing, social media and sales. A.M.C. has developed methodologies of enhancing communication with club fans and is also involved in improving efficiency of season ticket renewal and selling.
Our Services Include:
  • Helping our clients in creating a club organic season tickets sales infrastructure
  • Setting guidelines for pricing season tickets and drafting season tickets selling strategies
  • Drafting selling terms and pricing logic in collaboration with our clients
  • Helping in preparation of calling scenarios and training salesman in conducting dialogs with fans
  • Gathering and processing fans' data in all its aspects
  • Targeted email and SMS campaigns
We have developed several packages that are being offered as off the shelf platforms for sport club management is various areas, such as:
  • FRM cloud based platform for sport clubs, that was especially designed in tight collaboration with Maccabi Tel-Aviv basketball club. The platform can be easily customized to any club special needs and implemented within a short time. It creates a fan profile from his interactivity at club touch points
  • Sport club academy operational system, that was built to fit the need of sport clubs to manage their sport academies. The system collects the information of the students and keeps track of their learning, presence in practices and attendance in activities. It also monitors youth league players, practices and payments
  • Sport club players monitoring system is responsible to collect the information of club players training activities, game statistics, doctors injury reports, physiotherapy treatments and rehab processes.
 

Diamonds industry solutions

For the diamonds industry we have developed two solutions:
  • Rough diamonds price-lists is a unique software solution, designed especially to fit the needs of a large rough diamonds trading corporates in accurate valuation of rough diamonds prices for selling or buying.
  • Mine stock monitoring system is a package of software solutions designed especially to fit the needs of a diamonds mining company. The package monitoring covers the whole diamond stock flow from the mine, though the sorting site and up to the viewing site.
 

Linear asset management solutions

Our company has developed several solutions for linear asset management, such as:
  • Solution to monitor all of the construction and financing for Israeli sewage administration.
  • A financing monitoring and billing management solution for water infrastructure projects that include maintenance and repair water pipes in local municipalities.
  • Management of the software development department for the Israeli road company.
