SUSTAINABILITY

We are committed to be among the leaders for a better tomorrow in Israeli fashion from both a social perspective and an environmental one.
We are dedicated to finding methods of improvement when it comes to best practices for sustainability practices across all aspects of our business. We firmly believe that climate change and equality should be a major focal point at every fashion organization as this industry is one of the most diverse and global in the world.
