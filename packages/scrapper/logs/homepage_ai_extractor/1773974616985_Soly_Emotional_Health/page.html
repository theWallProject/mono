Questions & Answers on Your Journey to Recovery

All the Answers You Need to Know About Soly. 

Can someone who is already in therapy use Soly?

Yes. Soly works best as a complement to professional treatment. Therapists and clients use it between sessions to track emotions, practice regulation tools, and gather insights that deepen the therapeutic process.

Do I need a mental health diagnosis to use Soly?

Not at all. Soly is designed for anyone who wants to manage stress, build resilience, and better understand their emotions. Many users simply use it as part of their daily well-being routine.

Who is Soly for?

Soly supports individual users, therapists, clinics, and organizations. Whether you’re seeking personal growth, managing a high-stress environment, or running a healthcare system, Soly adapts to your needs.

What makes Soly different from other mental health apps?

Unlike traditional apps, Soly combines multiple AIs: language, voice, and computer vision—with trauma-informed design and physiological intelligence. It connects both sides of care: the therapist’s insight and the user’s daily experience.

How does Soly ensure privacy and data protection?

Soly’s AI platform is built with strict compliance in mind: HIPAA posture, SOC2 standards, and full data encryption. All emotional and biometric data remain private and are never shared without consent.

What results can users expect?

Many users report reduced stress and improved emotional awareness within days of consistent use. For professionals, Soly offers measurable insights into progress, engagement, and resilience growth.

Is Soly available now?

Yes. Soly’s WhatsApp version is already live and used by over 24,000 people. Our advanced AI platform – with language, vision, and voice capabilities – is currently under development and will soon be available for clinics, therapists, and organizations.

Does Soly replace professional therapy?

No. Soly is not a substitute for diagnosis, treatment, or professional care. It’s a supportive tool designed to complement therapy and help users practice emotional regulation between sessions. We always recommend consulting a qualified mental health professional for any clinical or diagnostic concerns.
