PATIENT ENGAGEMENT IS DIFFICULT

Behavior change is challenging for everyone.

The one thing that works? Human interaction.

However, human interaction requires available paramedical staff, an expansive and scarce resource:

2.1M

Projected nursing shortage in the US by 2025

65%

Job burnout among nurses

Introducing AI Patient Relationship

XOLTAR provides an AI accountability partner platform that emulates the one-on-one interactions nurses conduct with patients. Through hyper-personalized encounters, the accountability partners lead patients to embrace the healthy habits required to manage their medical conditions.

Each AI accountability partner can be customized by gender, race and language, promote different goals and behaviors, monitor patients, collect and report video & audio RWE, and offer an emotionally engaged, hand-holding experience.

As a staff augmentation solution, the AI accountability partner platform empowers the current staff by serving as co-pilots, enabling infinite scale with zero marginal cost.
