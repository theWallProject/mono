Biotic envisions a world where
plastic is no longer a concern

We are commodifying bio-based & biodegradable materials to offer a circular economy solution to plastics. Our scalable, bio-based, biodegradable polymers serve as a ‘drop-in’ solution for plastics.

Learn more

Biotic’s technology uses diverse feedstocks, including naturally grown sea algae and agricultural waste, to create an optimal plastic alternative material with numerous applications.

Plastic Crisis ​

The plastic pollution crisis affects every being on earth.​

Out of 400M tons of plastic wasteproduced annually, ONLY 9% is recycled, leaving the rest to degrade over hundreds of years. The resulting plastic pollution touches every ecosystem and every being on earth.

Microplastics are released from disposed plastics, and can be found everywhere from oceans, rivers, landfill, rain water, marine life habitat, and in the last years has been discovered in mothers’ breast milk.

Studies show we consume approximately FIVE GRAMS (a spoonful) of plastics on a weekly basis.

Biotic’s Solution

Biotic’s PHBV polymers enable the transition from conventional plastics. Our scalable production process uses no synthetic chemicals and a circular economy approach aiming to produce zero waste.

PHBV Polymers

Made from a diverse range of feedstocks including macroalgae and agricultural waste.
Fits a wide range of applications.
Seamlessly integrates into current production lines.

Biodegradable

Fully breaks down into natural compounds with no toxic residue.

Eliminates costs of and limits to recycling.

Circular

Derive high value plastics from feedstocks.

Low waste production process.
