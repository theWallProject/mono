What
is Niio?

Niio is an art streaming service that allows you to turn screens into digital art canvases at up to 8K quality. Works of more than 7,500 artists, galleries and museums – NFTs included - available for purchase and streaming on any screen. Create your own playlist, enjoy curated ones. Support artists, live with creative energy around you.

Learn more >

How does
it work?

Download the Niio app to discover exclusive art, follow curated collections, and easily stream them to any screen of your choice up to 8K quality. Access more than 30,000 artworks from a global network of artists & galleries.

Learn more >

How can I display Niio on my screen?

You can seamlessly display Niio art on your screen or streaming box, up to 8K quality:

(1) Samsung TV: Install the Niio art Player App from the Samsung AppStore directly on your TV.

(2) Apple TV Streaming Box: Install the Niio app directly from the App Store on Apple TV.

(3) Smart TVs with Android: Install the Niio app via Google Play on any Android-powered smart TV, including SONY, B&O, Hisense, Sharp, Philips, and more.

(4) Android TV & Fire TV Media Streaming Boxes: Download the Niio app from Google Play or the Amazon Appstore.

(5) Stream directly from your mobile phone (iOS/Android) to any Smart TV: Cast artwork using AirPlay (iOS) or Chromecast (Android).
