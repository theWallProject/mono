Core Capabilities

Our services are built on four key pillars that allow us to provide seamless, full-service support from concept to commercialization:

Computational Design & Cell Engineering

Alagene applies advanced computational tools and genome engineering techniques to design innovative biological systems that meet a wide range of scientific challenges, fostering breakthroughs in biotechnology.

Precision Fermentation & Process Design

Our fermentation processes are optimized from lab-scale to commercial production, ensuring smooth transitions and scalability to meet rigorous industry standards.

High-Throughput Screening (HTS)
& Bio-Assay Development

We offer state-of-the-art HTS and bioassay platforms that accelerate research through automation, enabling fast, efficient screenings tailored to your project's needs.

Downstream Process & Analytics

Alagene specializes in advanced downstream process development, utilizing techniques like chromatography and analytics to ensure high purity and activity in proteins and molecules, ready for commercial production.
