RED SEA GROUP

In today’s ever-changing global landscape of real estate development and entrepreneurship, the Red Sea Group is a collective of real estate companies operating worldwide in the development, construction and management of a variety of projects on all scales; specializing in the hotel and residential real estate and data centres markets, as well as complementary services in other upmarket sectors.

The cumulative experience of the people and companies in our group, combined with our vast web of international connections, empowers us with a comprehensive understanding of the relevant sectors, a rare eye for opportunity, and the ability to cover the entire profit-generation cycle from the initial development including planning and financing, to the final stage of the completed construction and management.

Our rich and distinguished record of successful projects and notable accomplishments is a testament to the Red Sea Group’s many years of bold and dedicated work, earning us the reputation of being a powerful and trustworthy force in the global real estate market.

All the Real Estate Development rivers…  flow to The RED SEA Group.
