How does the CalmiGo Device Work?
CalmiGo uses 3 proven techniques to decrease stress hormone levels by enabling your body to quiet the “anxious” nervous system (sympathetic system) and activating the “calming” nervous system (parasympathetic system)—providing support during moments of anxiety, panic, or PTSD.

CalmiGo’s patented technology guides you to effortlessly regulate your breath according to your unique breathing pattern. After just a few breaths your parasympathetic nervous system is activated, helping to lower stress hormones and ease feelings of anxiety or panic.

CalmiGo detaches you from an anxious state by activating four of your senses as a grounding technique: you see the lights, smell the calming scent, feel the vibration and the sensation of the device in your hand, and hear the vibration sound.

CalmiGo uses pure oil to activate parts of your nervous system that promote relaxation and a sense of calm. This can be especially helpful during moments of anxiety, panic, or PTSD. Our scented plates are made using a unique technology that solidifies pure oils, ensuring a completely drug-free experience.
