Storage Drop Technologies

Making green energy available for All.

about us

Storage Drop is an innovative company for I-CAES (“Isothermal Compressed Air Energy Storage”) and LDES (“Long Duration Energy Storage”) systems.

Storage Drop operates in the field of Energy Storage and develops several products with a potential for significant ecological advantage in the energy industry to address a wide range of applications including but not limited to power generation (“HyDrop”), air compression (“DropX”), heat and cold generation (“CoolDrop”).

The systems are based on hydraulic piston technology and are highly efficient, with extended life expectancy and low maintenance costs.

The company has filed 6 patent applications for its unique technology and gained the rights to commercialize a patented technology developed in a US DOE laboratory.

Hydrop - Energy Storage System for Power
Generation

Where energy can be stored at a time when electricity is available from renewable sources or when electricity tariffs are low.
Storage Drop uses compressed air technology. This technology exists in the market for decades but was not economically viable due to the use of conventional compressors (during the charging phase) and the heating of air (during the discharge phase) by pollutant sources.
Storage Drop uses a unique Hydraulic “green” isothermal compressor. The use of our proprietary unique technology solves the problem of air compressors high cost and low efficiency.

CoolDrop - Cooling Generation

Energy Storage System for cooling generation – including the development of a liquid piston compressor. This technology uses natural materials and is energy efficient compared to standard cooling cycles.

Storage Drop is developing a unique technology– storing high pressure coolant for cooling and air conditioning purposes. Storage Drop CoolDrop solution will allow the storage of cooling energy at a price lower than the one of competitors – Thermal Ice Storage.
