Why CommU?

What makes us unique

Integrating Extensive Cultural Research

CommU leverages a unique database, compiled over decades by medical anthropologists, to help doctors understand patients better and prevent costly cultural misunderstandings.

Optimal Translation Experience

CommU uses a combination of AI models, each optimized for a specific task to ensure the best translation experience overall.

Accurate Translations

In healthcare, there is no place for translation errors. That's why CommU is fine-tuned using an extensive database of medical interactions in various languages and is continuously tested against professional human interpreters to ensure high accuracy.
