Custom built kitchen design

Our planning process is similar to luxury indoor kitchen design.

Interview with the customers or designers to grasp their vision and type of hosts they are.
Learning the site architecture and structure, walls, pillars, planters, flooring, shades, water, electricity, drainage and gas infrastructure, general layout vision against wall or free standing, single strip, dual strips, L-shape, C-shape, integrating Seating & Dinning area combines or separated, prep area, cooking area, cooling area, drinks area, storage requirements, shelving. Drawers, trash bins, etc…Discussion about the appliances involved, define brand and models. Finally, picking up your door style panels and discuss about decoration options: Single Tone or Two Tone, frame decoration only or horizontal slat inserts for our segmented style panels your choice of selective pattern or full pattern. You may add internal led light system in cabinets as well as ambient led light at Toe-Kick.
Seating area is part of your hospitality experience planning. Your guests may seat along-side the kitchen to face the view or opposite the cooking area to enable the host or chef to communicate while cooking, it can be an elevated bar or around two wings, face to face seating on floating bar and more…
Outopia offers same panels design for you to go further behind the kitchen and cover your outdoor surrounding with same style such as: Back splash, Pillar cover, Planters, Benches, Fire Pits, Walls, Suffix, Vent covers, etc…

About

Luxury Outdoor Kitchens

Outdoor kitchens are where memories are made. Enjoy cooking, dining, and entertaining with loved ones in a relaxed atmosphere. Picture good food, wine, music, laughter, and the aroma of herbs. Outopia outdoor kitchens enhance this experience with their ambiance and visual appeal. Developed and manufactured in Israel by Emka Klein, Outopia combines design, manufacturing, and engineering expertise. Our kitchens meet interior designer standards and seamlessly blend interior and outdoor spaces.
Our team continually develops new features to enhance your outdoor kitchen experience. Outopia outdoor kitchens are for those with a true passion for life.

Elevating Outdoor Kitchen

Outopia all-aluminum cabinetry is perfect for outdoor projects. With patented design profiles, 0.4” thin panels, concealed adapters, high-quality hardware, and multi-layer near ocean color coating, our cabinets are lightweight yet strong. Features include rubber-gasket-sealed doors, silent slamming drawers, extra storage, improved heat removal, no sleeve required, all-weather durability, ambient light, anti slop toe-kick solution, door latches for stormy weather . Ideal for rooftops & balconies.
