Prevent the next respiratory attack

About

An estimated 300 million people worldwide suffer from asthma.

This number will grow by more than 100 million by 2025.

More than 11 million people have been diagnosed with COPD in US, but millions more may have the disease without even knowing it.

 

Introducing Peak.me

Improve quality of life for asthma and COPD patients by providing early warning of respiratory attacks

  • Predict events before they occur
  • Generate personalized insights
  • Increase awareness to triggers
  • Improve patient adherence to treatment

and… it’s affordable for everyone!

World’s first affordable smart spirometry

  • Entirely mechanical device attached to smartphone
  • Easy to use – no wires, no power needed
  • Innovative device converts exhaled air into an acoustic signal
  • Proprietary algorithm extracts spirometric values
  • Disease management platform for both patient and care provider

Benefits

Meaningful insights

Data analysis enables smart prediction for better management and prevention

  • Predictive alerts based on location, time and user data
  • Personal insights tailored for each individual
  • Enhanced awareness of exacerbation triggers
  • Incorporated environmental data such as weather, pollution and pollen
  • Enable patients to self-monitor their condition
  • Innovative whistle converts exhaled air-flow rate into an acoustic signal
  • Proprietary algorithm analyzes the signal and gives immediate feedback to the user
  • Data is uploaded to cloud service and can be shared with doctor
  • Doctor analyzes results and sends feedback to patient
  • Feedback from the doctor can be viewed within the app

Features

  • Guidance & feedback

    Ensure proper use and correct test exhalation maneuver

  • Notifications

    Alert patient, caregivers and clinicians to provide timely interventions

  • Trend analysis

    Enable actionable early recognition of exacerbation and improvement

  • Diary

    Record lung function measurements, symptoms, triggers, and medications

  • Reminders

    Remind users to take medications and perform tests

  • Cloud and EMR

    Enable integration with electronic medical record and Cloud backup
