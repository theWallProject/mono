For over 60 years, Palram has continually pushed the limits of thermoplastic technology to solve emerging market challenges. Today, our products are used in countless applications around the world – from stadiums and airports to agricultural facilities, commercial buildings, and private residences.

Whether you’re a builder, fabricator, architect, printer, or even a homeowner interested in DIY projects, Palram’s range of polycarbonate and PVC solutions will help make your vision a reality.

Palram Industries Ltd

Palram is a leading global manufacturer of semi-finished extruded thermoplastic sheets, panel systems and finished products, made primarily from Polycarbonate and PVC.

Palram products are designed to suit a diverse range of applications in various markets, which include DIY, construction, architectural projects, advertising, agricultural, glazing, and fabrication.
