דלג לתוכן

In a universe of sparkling startups,
amazing opportunities and extreme threats,
let us consigliare you.

We are the go-to advisory firm for today's fast-track tech companies, for all of their strategic transactions and processes.

From launch to exit

Consiglieri's advisory services address the needs of high-tech companies at all phases of their life-cycle.
These services include initial setup packages to build the companies’ business infrastructure; Seed, Early and Growth capital round management; Investor relationship management, high level CEO consultancy and M&A advisory; as well as creation and management of controlled secondary markets, internal innovation systems and more.

Service as a Product

Most of Consiglieri’s advisory services are productized: each service product is defined by specific goals, concrete deliverables, a timeline and price. Each service has a clear methodology, operating manual, templates and pre-defined contracts – to be tailored to the special circumstances, as much as needed.

Your business confidant

Consiglieri is neither an investment Finder nor an investment Bank. The assigned Consigliere is working closely with the management team as an integral part of that team (often as part of its advisory board), for the duration of the service. Each client will be assisted by Consiglieri's exceptional pool of resources and know-how, assisted per need by Consiglieri partners, a group of top professionals in their respective fields who are called to help serve our clients.

RAISING THE BAR

When approaching a strategic event or a process, which is not a routine challenge in the startup trajectory, you often see a major disruption to the
management team in their primary activities, while the transactions and processes are often handled inefficiently, lack professional support and
eventually cost too much. With Consiglieri advisory services, management can stay focused on its productive activities, essential to the company's
growth, while Consiglieri takes responsibility for the strategic mission, employing its core expertise in this space.

WE ARE ON A MISSION

The planets in our ecosystem, the large public or private corporations, have their own strategic challenges. When they face them – they will approach the McKinsey advisory group or the likes. These advisory companies are actually serving as these corporates' consiglieri. High-tech startups, have as many, if not more, critical strategic challenges, and they deserve no less of quality services. Enter Consiglieri.

When launching a project with Consiglieri, each client is assigned a Consigliere to lead the service, with the wealth of experience and expertise to match the client's needs. That Consigliere could be one of our in-house experienced team members or a Consigliere in Residence (CIR) assigned from a pool of the most experienced high-tech veterans for that domain. Either way, the Consigliere will be working for the duration of this project, hand-in-hand with the client's management team and leading the process as an integral part of that team. The Consigliere will utilize Consiglieri's comprehensive infrastructure, its tools and resources, always with the backup and guidance of Consiglieri superstar team.

THE PRODUCT LAUNCH PAD

Consiglieri has productized its services, so they are ready for customization and fine-tuning when applied to new clients, rather than starting each project from scratch.
To that end we are building a massive, ever-growing infrastructure of powerful tools and accessories to turn advisory services into products.
As a result, every service-product is well defined. It has a start and a finish, it has set goals, a systematic methodology, it defines deliverables and timelines.

Capital Round First/Business Class

With First Class, we take over the complete capital fund raising process: company readiness analysis, positioning formation, strategizing on all fronts (product, go-to-market, etc.), definition of round structure and terms, investor document preparation and leading negotiations. With Business Class, our client prepares and controls most of the master documents involved in the process. We provide lower touch tiered investor sourcing, while relying on edited company documentation. We help to define terms of the round, are involved during all process negotiations, and providee feedback on all aspects of the fundraising process

A-Z Venture Lending

Let us help you find the best venture debt deal. This is end-to-end process management from board decision to raising money, including term sheet sourcing and optimization, investor docs preparation, deal documents, perfection certificates (globally), post deal reporting and negative covenant compliance

Exit Ready Process

We get you ready for the big event. We assist with two main aspects of preparing for an exit process: – Exit preparation seminar (including guest speakers). – Self-DD, ensuring that company will be ready for all forms of DD, for when the opportunity arises

Company Sale

We can represent the buyer or seller in an M&A transaction. We provide turn-key internal investment banking: leading the preparations and the complete process of M&A sourcing and negotiations, hand-in-hand with the company’s management team

Friendly Separation

We help make this process as painless as possible for all parties involved. This product provides a proprietary path to an amicable separation between founders, securing the company from irreversible damage- including shelf product agreement and methodology to reduce dead equity with minimum friction

Stakeholder Binder

Let us help you maintain your investor relationships through technology, by analyzing information and reporting requirements: Provide insights on where investor relations can be strengthened; Risk Analysis on investor relations front; Calendar Dashboard- Execution and follow-up

Moshe Levin, Chairman, is one of the most experienced veterans in the Israeli high-tech market. An entrepreneur by himself (co-founded and led a startup through a complete life cycle), CEO of 3 leading Israeli tech corporations – all traded on Nasdaq, a Partner and Managing Partner in 5 Israeli Venture funds (Pitango, DFJ network funds), Chairman of a dozen of companies in a variety of industries and domains.

Paz Eliav, Consigliere (Senior Partner), As head of accelerator at MindCET and as Fintech sector lead at Startup Nation Central in the past 7 years, worked with dozens of startups, assisting them in forming their business plans and product market fit, as well as supporting their business development, global expansion and fundraising, primarily in the Edtech and Fintech spaces.

Roy Sivan, CFO & COO, Experienced American CPA (Deloitte USA and EY Israel) with knowledge of business issues encountered by startups. Strong network of investors in Israel and abroad. Enjoys working with companies on their financial model, and in general, solving problems.

Aviv Yovel, CTO
Has accumulated experience as both a tech lead and CTO in several startup companies, as well as serving as a team lead in a classified intelligence unit within the IDF's Intelligence Forces. Anna Saltykova, Social media and communications manager
