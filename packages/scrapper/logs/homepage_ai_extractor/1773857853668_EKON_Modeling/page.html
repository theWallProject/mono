DISASTROPEDIA a product of B-Prepared

We deliver the Disastropedia knowledge hub

Disastropedia is not a scientific platform. It is for people, everyday people. It works similarly to well-known Wiki platforms, just in plain language, (even) more readily understandable. One reason for this is that in disaster or crisis situations, the human mind more easily remembers simple advice, tasks or information; coming from this, it is more likely that the person will remember that information and act accordingly. Moreover, different ages, languages, and special accessibility needs require further versions of the exact text to put through the same amount of information.

WELDGALAXY PROJECT

WELDGALAXY DIGITAL B2B PLATFORM,

based on DKM

WeldGalaxy developed a secure, scalable, user-friendly and transparent digital B2B platform that connects end-users with equipment and consumables manufacturers, suppliers, distributors and technology providers. Based on a DKM (Dynamic Knowledge Management), the WeldGalaxy platform integrates a dynamic evolving database with relevant data from equipment and consumables manufacturers that will dynamically evolve as the usage of the platform grows.

The platform includes:

  • Transparency of product features, capabilities, resource use, associated add-on services and price;
  • Customer feedback, real-time use feedback (anonymised as needed) and associated options;
  • Scalability with respect to technological development and manufacturing application domains;
  • Information about standards and regulatory compliance (e.g. the facilitation of re- and de-manufacturing) as well as security requirements;

This project has received funding from the European Union's Horizon 2020 research and innovation programme. Grant agreement 822106.
