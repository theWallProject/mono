Individual Backlinks, Not Just Metrics

Aggregate scores are useful for quick filtering, but they are not a full backlink audit. Providers have crawl gaps, and score models can overvalue spam while missing truly strong links.

What aggregate metrics show

  • •Snapshot metrics like Trust Flow (TF), Domain Rating (DR), and Domain Authority (DA)
  • •Total referring domains and IP counts from each provider's own index
  • •A simplified score that compresses a complex backlink profile into one number

What they don't show

  • •Important backlinks missing from a single tool's crawl, even when links are real and valuable
  • •Which sites are actually linking and whether they are legitimate
  • •Whether links are still live, indexed, and in natural placements
  • •Whether low-quality or spam sources are inflating the totals and distorting the score

Backlinks from Trusted Sources

Such as major news sites, .edu/.gov domains, business directories, and other recognized organizations. Every listing shows its verified backlinks.

Clean Domain History

Every domain is verified for single-owner use and legitimate purpose. Spam, PBN, or manipulative history means automatic exclusion.

Metrics as Context Only

TF, DR, and Ref IP counts are shown for reference, but they do not tell you which sites are actually linking. Our backlink lists do.
