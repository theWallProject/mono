60 Years of Leadership

From a small kibbutz in the Negev to a global agricultural revolution — for 60 years, Orbia Netafim has led with bold vision, resilience, and innovation.

We’ve transformed challenges into victories, delivering precision irrigation to every farmer, every crop, everywhere.

With purpose-driven leadership, we’ve helped reshape farming, empower communities, and secure food for future generations.

As the world changes faster than ever, we’re proud to be trailblazers, committed to empowering our farmers every step of the way — because when they thrive, we all do.

Precision irrigation changes the economics of agriculture.

In the past, if you wanted to grow more, you needed more fertilizer, labor or land. Precision irrigation is about reversing this age-old relationship between cost and output. It’s about delivering the perfect amount of water and nutrients straight to the roots of each plant – not the soil. So you grow bigger, better yields every season – with fewer resources.
