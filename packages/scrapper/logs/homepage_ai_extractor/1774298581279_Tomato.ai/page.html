Clearer Pronunciation

Tomato.ai softens the accent of agents in real-time as they speak. The solution works for heavily and lightly accented agents. The accent heard by customers is set based on their geography.

Learn More

Noise Cancellation

Tomato.ai removes background noises that are common on sales and support calls. Noise removal covers work from home noises like kids or pets, and call center noises like other agents in the background.

Learn More

Improve Voice Quality

Tomato.ai clears up the voice of an agent. If the agent is hard to hear, or breathy, or choppy, the solution using AI enhances the voice. Customers then are less annoyed by such issues, which are common on calls.

Learn More

Preserve Voices

Tomato.ai softens the accent while preserving the voice and rhythm of the speaker. So agents still sound like themselves but it’s as if they took classes to reduce the level of accent that they have.

Learn More
