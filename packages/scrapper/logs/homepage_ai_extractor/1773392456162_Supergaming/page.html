Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get paid?

Payouts are processed weekly (every Monday) via PayPal or bank transfer. Minimum payout is $50 - amounts under that roll over to the next week.

What's the revenue share?

You earn 30% of the house edge from all bets in your world. With a 15% average house edge, that's 4.5% of total betting volume directly to you.

Can I customize payouts?

Yes! You can set multipliers and win chances for each block type within our balanced limits. This lets you create unique risk/reward experiences.

How long until my world is live?

After you submit your world, our team reviews it within 24-48 hours. Once approved, it's immediately published and earning revenue.

How To Play
Break blocks, win rewards!
1️⃣ Basic Gameplay
  • Set your bet amount using the controls at the bottom
  • Click on blocks in the world to break them
  • Each block has a chance to pay out 0.5x to 100x your bet
  • Different blocks have different volatility levels
2️⃣ Block Volatility
🟢 Low Volatility
~16-18% win rate, frequent wins
🔵 Medium Volatility
~13-14% win rate, balanced odds
🟠 High Volatility
~8-10% win rate, chase big wins
💎 rules.jackpotHunter
rules.jackpotHunterDesc
💡 Pro Tips
  • Check the Paytable to see exact odds for each block type
  • Low volatility blocks are great for steady play
  • High volatility blocks are for jackpot hunting
  • Shards expire in 30 seconds - collect them fast and find a Collector!
  • Free Breaks and Shards are mutually exclusive - only one bonus at a time
3️⃣ Bonus Features
  • Mystery Blocks - Rare special blocks (0.5% spawn) trigger Slot Machine or TNT Explosion with 50x average payout! Always rewards!
  • Multiplier Shards - Purple crystals spawn randomly (15% RTP). Collect them before they expire! Find a 🪙 Collector block to cash in all shards at once
  • Scatter Symbols - Golden scatter blocks appear rarely (18% RTP). Collect 3+ within 150 blocks to trigger Free Breaks!
  • Free Breaks Mode - 15-40 free breaks at your locked bet with +2x bonus multiplier. Can retrigger for more!
  • World Depletion - When only 10% of blocks remain, the entire world resets with fresh blocks for everyone!
4️⃣ Fair Play & RTP
  • Total RTP: 40% base + 18% scatter + 15% shards + 23% mystery = 96% total
  • RNG is server-authoritative and provably fair
  • Results are calculated before animation - what you see is what you get
  • Bet is locked during bonus modes to prevent exploitation
