ABOUT NAZARENE TOURS

Incoming department
The incoming department of Nazarene Tours Ltd. has been offering customized tours in the Middle East for the past 45 years. We arrange tours tailor-made for your needs, whether for pilgrimage groups or independent travelers, those seeking a fun-and-sun vacation, a country exploration or a historical tour, and those planning conferences and incentive tours.

We have established long-term partnerships with major international travel agencies, acting as their handling agent in Israel. Our professional staff are available to offer round-the-clock help, providing a travel experience of unsurpassed authenticity and comfort. Our reputation has been built on a commitment to quality and customer service, coupled with attention to detail.

WHAT WE OFFER

Excursion & Activities

We look after the needs of individual travelers (FITs), tailor-making itineraries, as well as arranging excursions, accommodation and guides.

Guiding Services

Our guides are highly trained, speak a wide range of languages and are committed to providing the best holiday experience possible.

Accommodation

We have strong, long-term relationships with the best hotels in the region, in addition to owning two prestige hotels in Jerusalem and Nazareth.

Transfer & Transport

We have a large fleet of buses and can arrange transportation between any of the destinations included in your itinerary.
