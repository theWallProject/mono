Send incident alerts to all stakeholders

Send an alert to any number of recipients – both those responsible for fixing the fault and other users affected by it. Ensure that critical incidents are never missed and that all stakeholders are kept up to date, without worrying about extra costs.

For example, upon a database failure, alert the DBAs responsible for fixing the problem and simultaneously notify all users affected by the failure.

The smartest alert logic you can imagine

SNS++ analyzes your entire system, allowing you to define sophisticated alert logic. Send notification when X errors occurred within a week that affected your CRM.  Alert when multiple disk errors from several servers have reached a weekly threshold. Identify alert flooding. Monitor your monitoring servers' heartbeat. And much more.
