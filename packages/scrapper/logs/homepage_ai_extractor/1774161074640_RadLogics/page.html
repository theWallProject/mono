Uncover Unidentified Cancers

Mission Statement

RADLogics helps cancer centers uncover unidentified cancers by leveraging Al-Powered image analysis applied to archived medical imaging data – improving patient outcomes, mitigating healthcare providers’ malpractice risk, and creating previously untapped revenue potential.

Transforming Oncology
Care

At RADLogics, we created Al-powered imaging solutions that help oncologists uncover unidentified cancers by reviewing historical imaging data to surface overlooked cancer patients without adding burden to existing workflows.

Retrospective Insights and Seamless Integration

RADLogics’ patented Al platform is designed for quick deployment whether on-premise or via the cloud using Microsoft Azure and Amazon Web Services (AWS). Our workflow engine enables retrospective cancer detection across millions of archived imaging scans, surfacing untreated patients.
