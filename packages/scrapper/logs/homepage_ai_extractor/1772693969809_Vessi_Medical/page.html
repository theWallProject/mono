    the opportunity

    THE NEED

    Current treatments are costly, invasive surgical procedures that must be performed in a hospital under general anesthesia and are associated with significant complications.

     

    The market demands an effective treatment for urological tissue that reduces side effects, improves patient outcomes, and lowers healthcare costs.

    The current standard of care has numerous limitations

    High incidence of Bleeding

    Repeated Perforations

    Decreased Quality of Life 

    our solution

    Unique Cryo Spray
    for Hollow & Humid Organs

    Vessi Medical offers a minimally invasive cryo spray specifically designed for hollow and humid organs. This innovative, bladder-specific cryotherapy provides a new therapeutic alternative to surgery, reducing both costs and complications associated with traditional treatments.

    • ​Easy to Use

    • Office procedure

    • No Anesthesia required in some cases

    Reduces Complications: Minimizes the risk of complications compared to traditional surgical methods.

    Non-Contact Ablation: Utilizes a non-contact technique for ablating tissues.

    Effective Treatment:

    Easily treats the targeted tissue and its surrounding areas.

    THE VESSI SYSTEM

    THE VESSI SYSTEM

    The Vessi system comprises a standalone console and a disposable device designed for transurethral insertion into the bladder.​

    Vessi's advantages

    Hollow Organ Visualization: Provides effective visualization inside hollow organs during surface cryotherapy.

    Visualization: Enables clear visualization in mixed liquid and gas environments during surface cryotherapy.

    Flexible Design: Designed for use without anesthesia, making it suitable for office procedures.

    Optimized Cooling: Balances the ideal cooling temperature and pressure for effective treatment.
