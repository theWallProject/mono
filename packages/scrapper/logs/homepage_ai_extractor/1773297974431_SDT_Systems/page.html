REAL UNREALITY

Interactive Simulation and Training Solution for practical and tactical shooting training

Modern

We design and develop the most ultimate simulators, training devices and equipment based on modern cutting edge technologies and best word simulation practices.

Application of these solutions during training proceses significantly increasses training effectivenes and helpes to learn air, marine and ground based complex equipment and improves safety.

During simulation education trainees are given the most valuable thing — operational experience and emergency cases drilling.

Realistic

Quality of simulation training depends significantly on visualization system and image generation system of external environment. «SKYLIFER» image generation system — provides unprecedented true to live fidelity of virtual environment, simulates different weather conditions, atmospheric effects, restricted visibility conditions and creates best environment for training process.

Efficient

Utilization of modern full operational simulators and training devices in trainig process enables to deliver trainee in-deep knowledge of the trainig course supported by operational experience during short period of time. It also provides experince of emergency situation which cannot be simulated in reality because of economic or safety reasons. Simulation based education also saves equipment and material resource and operational expenses and still provides high efficience of learning proces while expenses remains much more lower in comparison with education on real equipment.
