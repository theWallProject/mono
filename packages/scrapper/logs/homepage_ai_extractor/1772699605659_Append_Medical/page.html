X5

LAA – Left Atrial Appendage    LA – Left Atrium AF – Atrial Fibrillation

LAA occlusion solutions aim to decrease stroke risks and are progressively replacing other stroke prevention therapies
DRT and PDL are
inherent risks in current
LAA occlusion solutions

Device Related
Thromboembolism (DRT)
Caused by foreign material left
in the LA increases risk of stroke
by 5 fold

Peri Device Leakage (PDL)
Evidence shows even small
leaks are significant and
increase risk of stroke by 2 fold

The Append Transcatheter System

A sophisticated yet simple vacuum solution for
inversion and ligation of the LAA tissue
Without implanting any device in the heart, Append is
poised to disrupt the LAA occlusion market with a
proprietary vacuum system, eradicating DRT and PDL risks
while providing a transcatheter solution that universally fits
the widest range of anatomies.
