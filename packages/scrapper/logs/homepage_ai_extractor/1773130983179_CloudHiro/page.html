The Problem

Cloud Spend Is Fragmented. Financial Control Is Not.

Multi-cloud environments create billing silos, commitment risk, and inconsistent allocation models. Without consolidation, optimization becomes reactive and accountability is blurred.

Finance

Sees too much detail.

"Who approved this new service and what business outcome is it tied to?"

Granular billing with no allocation context. Every line item is a question without an answer.

Engineering

Sees usage, not answers.

"Usage is up 40%. Is that customer growth, an anomaly, or something we simply cannot explain?"

Resource data without business context makes it impossible to distinguish healthy growth from waste.

Leadership

Sees neither clearly.

"Revenue grew 100%. Cloud costs grew 50%. Are we efficient or just lucky?"

Without cost per user, cost per transaction, or unit economics, there is no way to know.

CloudHiro transforms fragmented cloud data into a structured financial operating model, giving Finance, Engineering, and Leadership a single source of truth.
