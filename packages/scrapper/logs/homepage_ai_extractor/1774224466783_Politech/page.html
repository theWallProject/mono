What we do

We consult with organizations of all types and sizes to design, plan and build web applications and software technology. Our process is centered around taking a deep dive into your unique situation, the problem you are trying to solve and collaborating with you to come up with a solution tailored to your needs.

Our team specializes in backend development in Elixir and frontend development with React, though we can work with many other modern web technologies.

Process-driven and flexible

We believe that the majority of software development agencies are simply too rigid. We are eager to work with you and take your unique situation into account.
