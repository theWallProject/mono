The Milken Innovation Center at the Van Leer Jerusalem Institute  focuses on developing market-based solutions to Israel’s greatest challenges as it transitions from a startup nation to a global nation.  Our goal is to accelerate economic growth, build human capital, and cement Israel’s role as a pioneer in addressing global challenges in water, food, education, health, and energy with solutions that others can replicate.

About the Fellowship

Milken Innovation Center Fellowship Programs

The Milken Innovation Center has two fellowship programs – a policy fellowship in key ministries in the Israeli Government and local and regional authorities and a project fellowship focused on the design of high impact projects and programs in Israel and developing economies.

Each of these fellowships follows a graduate-level course in Financial Innovations offered at the Hebrew University and the University of California/Berkeley.
