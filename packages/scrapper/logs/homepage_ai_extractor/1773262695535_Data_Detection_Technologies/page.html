TECHNOLOGIES

Image Counting Inside

Our counting machines use the most advanced counting technology in the world.

In conventional counting systems, the items to be counted need to be separated over different lines, spaced at specific intervals and then fed to the counter one by one. Contrary to them, in DATA’s ICI-based systems items are, in fact, counted in bulk as they free-fall vertically through a detection unit at a continuous and uniform rate.

A DATA ICI counting unit counts small objects at a speed that is virtually the same as the speed of weighing. Our counting machines (counters) have counting rates of up to 360,000 objects per minute. This can be achieved for objects as small as 0.5mm and a single system can be configured to handle a wide range of object shapes. Advanced mathematical algorithms ensure 100% accuracy by analyzing multiple views of the falling objects. These counting machines are suited to metal and plastic molds, medical parts, electronic components, o-rings, automotive aerospace, and many more.

Image Counting Unit

To date, the pharma industry uses tablet and capsule counting machines in which items are carefully aligned at specific intervals, and then counted off, one by one. These systems typically have a large footprint and require high maintenance in order to combat the dust generated in packaging non-coated tablets.

DATA’s ICU-based systems are the first to bring a vision-based bulk tablet and capsule counting technology to the pharma industry. DATA uses cutting-edge, real-time image processing to count multiple objects in bulk on one wide track. Because the counting unit and the tablet-carrying module are separate, ICU-based systems are remarkably durable, insensitive to dust and easy to upkeep.

ICU bulk counting is 100% accurate, based on an advanced algorithm developed by a multi-disciplinary team of experts in electronics, optics, mechanics and computer sciences.
