Empowering Tomorrow, Ensuring Today

At OFIL Systems, we provide smart inspection and diagnostic solutions that empower the reliability of electrical assets. Our advanced solar-blind UV technology enables precise, real-time detection of corona partial discharges and arcing, ensuring proactive maintenance in high-voltage environments.

Designed for electrical utilities and industrial applications, our innovative Gridnostic platform enhances grid reliability through image intelligence. By harnessing AI-driven analytics and multi-sensor technologies, Gridnostic converts complex data into actionable insights, improving operational efficiency and supporting predictive maintenance.

Experience the power of early fault detection and data-driven decision-making with OFIL Systems, where innovation meets reliability.
