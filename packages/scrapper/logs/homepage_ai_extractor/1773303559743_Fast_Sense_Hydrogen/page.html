Nanoscale Sensor Structures Integrated into CMOS Layers for Advanced Gas Monitoring

At Fast Sense, your safety and the reliability of your equipment are our top priorities. Our innovative solution enables precise monitoring, detection, and analysis of complex gas mixtures with advanced nanoscale sensors embedded within CMOS layers. These sensors are engineered to minimize cross-sensitivity, delivering accurate readings across multi-component gas blends. Our technology provides a cost-effective, rapid, and reliable approach to multi-gas analysis, ensuring dependable data for safer operations.
