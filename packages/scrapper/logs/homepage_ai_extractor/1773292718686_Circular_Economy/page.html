WELCOME TO THE ISRAELI CIRCULAR ECONOMY STAKEHOLDERS PLATFORM

Circular Economy IL

The Israeli Circular Economy IL Platform is uniquely recognized by the official European Circular Economy Stakeholder Platform (A joint initiative by the European Commission and the European Economic and Social Committee) as an Israeli actor on national, regional and international levels to integrate new resource strategies and circular based business models with Israeli innovation capabilities.

Recognizing the importance of adopting a sustainable way of human behavior, the circular economy model aims to achieve a sustainable economic growth, by breaking the link between economic development and non-efficient consumption of resources.

By structural changes such as redesign, reuse and re-production, using innovation and new business models, materials and their value remain in the circle of production & consumption instead of ending up in the garbage, such as is the linear model.

Our goal

Acceleration of the necessary change towards an economy based on a sustainable circular economy model by combining the well-known Israeli innovation with new resource strategies & business models. 

In doing so, we wish to contribute the world’s effort to create new and sustainable economic and social values on national, regional and international levels.

 

Our goal

Acceleration of the necessary change towards an economy based on sustainable circular model, and a proposal of new resource strategies & business models, combined with the well-known Israeli innovation. In doing so, we contribute to the world’s effort to create new and sustainable economic and social values on national and international levels.
