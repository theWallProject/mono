    About GTI

    GTI Medical Ltd. is a privately owned company established in 2012 and headquartered in Netanya, Israel. Its team of engineers and QA professionals specialize in manufacturing, assembly and packaging for Tier 1 medical device companies around the world.

    GTI’s R&D facility is strategically located in one of the world’s major centers for medical technology, where Israeli scientists and engineers have created a unique entrepreneurial ecosystem.
