FAQ

Got Any More Questions?‍

How Does Superstream Optimize My Data Transfer?

When you connect the Superstream local agent — it continuously analyzes each topic’s workload by sampling payloads.
Using this real-time analysis, it determines and outputs the optimal configuration properties for every payload type. When applications using the embedded Superstream package restart and begin producing messages, their initial configurations are automatically updated with these optimized settings.
These may include a more efficient compression algorithm, adjusted batch sizes, and optimized linger intervals. The impact of these optimizations can be observed through the Superstream dashboard or any third-party monitoring tool that tracks Kafka bytes in and out.

How Does Superstream Optimize My Kafka Clusters?

Superstream automatically analyzes your Kafka usage patterns—including metrics, configurations, and throughput—and applies real-time cluster-level optimizations. It dynamically adjusts retention policies, reduces partition counts, tunes consumer group settings, resizes infrastructure, and more—all to lower costs and boost performance, without downtime or manual intervention.

Is There A Free Trial Available For New Users?

Yes. Superstream offers a Free Plan for both SuperClient and SuperCluster. You can optimize 1 cluster, with full access to daily health reports, idle topic detection, and network footprint optimization—no credit card required.

Can I Control What Superstream Auto-Optimizes?

Yes. You can set granular policies to define what’s in scope for optimization—by topic, clusters, or workload type. You can choose full automation, manual approval, or read-only mode depending on your comfort level.

How Does Superstream Handle Security And Compliance?

Superstream runs with least-privilege access, encrypts all data in transit and at rest, and logs every action. It integrates with your IAM policies, supports audit trails, and never moves data outside your environment. We’re SOC 2 Type II, ISO27001, and GDPR compliant.
