TERRAFLEX COMPOUNDS

Terraflex develops and produces a wide range of standard as well as customized PVC compounds.
All Terraflex compounds are lead free and are made of food-approved ingredients. The large variety
of both soft and rigid PVC compounds allows for a large gamut of applications such as cables,
seals, food grade bottles, injection molding, bath mats, hoses, cable tunnels, medical devices and more.
