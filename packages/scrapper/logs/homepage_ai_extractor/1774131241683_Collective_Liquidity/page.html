Why the Collective Exchange Fund?

Reduce your risk

Holding all of your wealth in a single, illiquid stock is risky. Exchanging some of your shares for an interest in Collective's diversified portfolio can greatly reduce your risk, effectively putting a floor under your net worth,

Defer your taxes

Unlike a stock sale, exchanges into Collective's fund don't trigger taxes. The tax savings compound over time and potentially more than double your wealth over the long-term.

Invest alongside renowned VCs

Collective's portfolio of private growth companies is comprised of the "winners" of the most successful VCs. The fund targets rates of returns equivalent to those of top quartile venture capital firms over time.

Access liquidity

Investors in the Collective Exchange Fund can redeem their fund interests for cash or access immediate liquidity through Collective's LP BuyBack program or loans from Collective's partner banks.

How it works

Complete your exchange in 3 easy steps

Employees holding shares or options in eligible companies may exchange them for a limited partnership interest in the Exchange Fund of equal value. So, for each $100,000 worth of shares contributed to the fund, the employee receives a $100,000 limited partnership interest in the fund.

To start your Exchange, follow these simple steps:

1

See if your company's shares are eligible for exchange into Collective's fund here

2

See the per share value Collective assigns to the shares you are exchanging into the fund here

3

Use Collective easy online process to review and e-sign the paperwork

That's all you need to do. From there, Collective's friendly operations team works with your company and expertly manages your exchange into the fund.
