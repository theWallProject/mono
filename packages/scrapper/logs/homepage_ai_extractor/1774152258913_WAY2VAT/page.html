FAQs

What is VAT/GST reclaim, and how do domestic and foreign reclaims differ?

VAT/GST reclaim is the process of recovering taxes paid on business-related expenses. Domestic VAT reclaim focuses on local expenses, while foreign VAT reclaim applies to cross-border expenses, requiring compliance with specific international regulations. Way2VAT's automated platform simplifies both processes across 40+ countries.

Why is outsourcing VAT/GST reclaim better than doing it in-house?

In-house VAT reclaim is complex, with country-specific regulations, language barriers, and high potential for manual errors. Way2VAT eliminates these challenges with automated compliance checks, AI-powered validation, and direct communication with tax authorities—ensuring maximum refunds while your team focuses on core business activities.

Where do the biggest VAT or GST reclaim opportunities often hide in a business?

Hidden opportunities include Accounts Payable invoices, employee travel expenses, capital expenditures, and foreign VAT transactions. Way2VAT's AI platform identifies reclaimable expenses across all categories, capturing up to 30% more recovery than traditional methods.

How does VAT/GST reclaim improve cash flow and financial planning?

Reclaiming VAT or GST improves cash flow by recovering funds spent on eligible expenses and reducing operational costs. Regular VAT refunds, especially for international operations, provide consistent financial benefits that support strategic planning and growth.

Why shouldn't my business delay VAT/GST reclaim?

Strict deadlines for VAT/GST reclaim vary by country, typically ranging from 1 to 5 years. Missing deadlines forfeits refund opportunities. Way2VAT tracks and manages these deadlines automatically, ensuring timely claims and maximized cash flow.

What is APAI and how does it protect my business?

APAI (Accounts Payable AI) is Way2VAT's real-time invoice compliance validation technology. It automatically checks every AP invoice against country-specific VAT regulations before payment—catching invalid VAT IDs, incorrect rates, and compliance errors that could trigger penalties, denied tax credits, or audit issues.

What VAT consultancy services does RBCVAT provide?

RBCVAT, now part of Way2VAT, offers strategic VAT advisory services including VAT planning and strategy, transaction structuring, cross-border compliance guidance, supply chain optimization, and VAT health checks. With over 20 years of experience and Big Four-caliber expertise, RBCVAT helps businesses navigate complex VAT regulations across UK and EU markets.

How does RBCVAT's compliance management service work?

RBCVAT manages your multi-jurisdiction VAT compliance including registrations, periodic VAT returns, EC Sales Lists, Intrastat declarations, and ongoing reporting obligations across UK and EU countries. You get a single point of contact managing your entire European VAT portfolio with expert oversight and timely filings.

Can Way2VAT help with e-invoicing requirements?

Yes. As e-invoicing mandates roll out across Europe (EU ViDA by 2030, with many countries implementing earlier), Way2VAT's platform is designed to support digital reporting requirements. Combined with RBCVAT's regulatory expertise, we help businesses prepare for and comply with mandatory e-invoicing standards.

What's the difference between automated VAT reclaim and VAT consultancy services?

Automated VAT reclaim focuses on recovering taxes already paid on eligible expenses through AI-powered processing. VAT consultancy provides strategic advisory on how to structure transactions, manage compliance obligations, and optimize your VAT position going forward. Together, they offer complete indirect tax management—recovering past opportunities while preventing future issues.

Do I need both automation and consultancy services?

It depends on your business complexity. Companies with straightforward expense reclaim may only need automation. However, businesses with cross-border operations, complex supply chains, or significant regulatory exposure benefit from combining automated processing with expert advisory to maximize recovery, ensure compliance, and minimize risk.
