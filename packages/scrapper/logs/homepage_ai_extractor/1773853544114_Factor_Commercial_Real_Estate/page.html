OUR SERVICES

Tenant Representation

We create a strategy for each tenant’s future real estate needs based on known and existing risks and future business expectations and execute this strategy according to relevant requirements, including relocation, consolidation, renewal, expansion, space disposal, sale & lease back, and more.

Investment Properties

We maintain strong business relationships with almost all the main, active investors in the Israeli market. We dominant all aspects of the transaction, from first driving interest in the property and up until ensuring the exact details of the agreement, all along applying our diverse knowledge in law, accounting, finance, and taxation.

Landlord Leasing

We advise real estate developers from the initial stages of project planning, customizing the project for target audiences, and developing and executing a marketing plan to ensure maximum project occupancy.

industrial & Logistics

We specialize in leasing and buying/selling property nationwide, including land, logistics buildings, industrial buildings, and laboratories.

Research & Analysis

We offer research and analysis services, which include cash flow, buying vs. leasing, commuting/ transportation factors, quality assurance, market assessments, and more. This is provided as a component in all of our services or can be provided independently as well.

Project Management

We provide a professional project management team to lead your project life cycle, From strategic planning and Design, through construction and completion. A one-stop shop that covers all client’s project needs, In combination with the most advanced technologies and work methodologies in the industry.
