Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you move in.

I want to stay for several months, how do I book?

Yes, we offer temporary rentals for months, all inclusive and hassle free. Send a booking request through the form and our team will contact you within 72 hours to proceed with your booking.

I only need to stay for a few days, how do I book?

You can book your flat for a few days in the same way as you would a hotel, from our website.

Is this a hotel or a flat?

Neither and both. Flipco is Flex Living: spaces with the soul of a home and the services of a hotel. Flats and rooms for rent with services included.

What’s included in the price?

Everything is included. WiFi, utilities such as water, electricity and electricity, cleaning, access to all the common areas… No extras or surprises.
