ABOUT US

Lahat Technologies (part of Agentek) is a well-known distributor for some of the world’s leading manufacturers in the fields of Lasers, Optics, Electronics Test & Measurement, Motion Control and more.
Our aim is to provide our customers top-quality, efficient technologies and best support and service.
Lahat’s sales and service team are qualified certified engineers, who are highly experienced in the fields of Physics, Electro-optics, Electronics and Mechanics.
