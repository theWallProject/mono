In the future, TBI and concussion patients will have a valid diagnostic tool and treatment.

Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) diagnosis and treatment are unmet needs in the medical field.

No diagnostic tools for assessing the extent and severity of a TBI exist.

Therapy development is at the core of Medicortex R&D plan.

About Us

Medicortex Finland is dedicated to improving the diagnostics, treatment and prevention of acute neurodegenerative conditions and is currently focused on developing a diagnostic kit for the detection of Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI).
