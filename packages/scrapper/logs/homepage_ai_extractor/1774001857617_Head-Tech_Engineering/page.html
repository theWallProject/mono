About

Head-Tech brings to Israel the most innovative technological solutions from across the globe.  As key partners of our customers, we offer an array of solutions for a variety of companies across the Israeli tech industry, such as PCB assembly, electronics, defense, medical, optics, solar, metal, and finishing.
