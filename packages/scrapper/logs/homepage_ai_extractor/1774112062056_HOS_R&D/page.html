Established in 2010, HOS Technology R&D LTD. Specializes in engineering & mechanical design, electrical engineering, and the design of vehicles and military modifications. The company integrates design engineers with experts in the field of metallurgy, welding, AC, electricity and control.

HOS is centered in Israel, with offices and installment centers in Kibbutz Beit Alfa, and in the Kokhav Yair Manufacturing Plant, which also serves as the headquarters for the company.

Technology in action

HOS provides Leading Technology Solutions for automotive platforms, of all size, shape and tasks. The pillar of our activities, is the full understanding of the automotive platform. This understanding allows us to modify, add or even change original set-ups without damaging the integrity of the platform.

After more than a hundred different projects, where platforms such as tracked APCs military vehicles, wheeled APCs military vehicles, multi axels military trucks, Fire Fighting trucks, Riot control vehicles, civilian pick-ups and vans and additional many more types where involved;
we can declare without hesitation that there is no task deemed unachievable.

The many years of service in the IDF and the extensive professional experience of the company’s personnel in the field, allow HOS to support projects carried out with the IDF and institutional Identities.
