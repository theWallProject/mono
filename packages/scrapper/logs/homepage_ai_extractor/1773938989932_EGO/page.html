Mashu AI

The Operating System for AI Agents.
Enterprise-grade orchestration platform for AI automation.

Mashu AI helps organizations build and manage intelligent agents that automate workflows, connect data silos and orchestrate enterprise operations in real time. Secured by IBM. Built on IBM Cloud technology, it's now used by global teams to deploy multi-agent systems with absolute security and compliance.

Shipper Global

AI-powered logistics and shipping automation system.

Originally developed for global e-commerce networks, Shipper Global integrates carriers like DHL, FedEx, Aramex, Israel Postal, Gett and UPS through a unified API. It automates routing, customs, pricing and tracking across 220+ countries - reducing human error, speeding up delivery and cutting operational costs by up to 60%.

NeuroLab

Intelligent AI orchestration for healthcare communication.

We built a multi-agent system connecting patients, doctors and clinics through real-time AI orchestration - streamlining communication, diagnostics and follow-ups via a unified data dashboard. NeuroLab now powers pilot projects for smart hospitals and research centers - transforming how healthcare systems share, analyze and act on medical information.

ETGAR

AI-native regulatory document automation for Wall Street and U.S. financial markets.

ETGAR is an intelligent SaaS platform built for financial institutions, investment firms and compliance teams to automate the creation, management and filing of complex regulatory and disclosure documents. It replaces manual, error-prone workflows with a secure, AI-assisted collaboration system - covering everything from SEC filings to investor communications with full auditability and compliance alignment.
