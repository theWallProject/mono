#JOESWORLD

Joe's is proud to support the Lapierre Zipp Collective & the Lapierre Overvolt teams

The Lapierre Zipp Collective is made up by Isabeau Courdurier who is the 2019 & 2022 EWS World Champion, Adrien Dailly 2017 EWS Vice World Champion and Lisadru Bertini who won the last round of the 2022 EWS in Under 21 category. In 2022 the Lapierre Zipp Collective finished 3rd on the team global ranking of the EWS.The Lapierre Overvolt team is made up by Nicolas Vouilloz (X10 DH World Champion), Antoine Rogge who finished in the Top 30th of the DH WC in 2022 with 2 EWS-E podiums as well and Kevin Marry who is one of the most talented rider in the EWS-E championship. In 2021 the Lapierre Overvolt finished 1st on the team global ranking of the EWS-E.

ABOUT US

It's our love of bicycling and fresh air that makes us free. Free to ride, travel, explore, commute or compete. This is our story:

Joe's No-Flats provides the bicycle market with the world’s most effective anti-puncture award-winning sealant. Our product line includes both latex based and latex and ammonia free sealants, with tubeless solutions and accessories, self-sealing tubes, tubeless conversion kits, lubricants based on the most advanced Nano technology, excellent cleaners and bio-degreasers and more.

The Joe's No-Flats spirit of being a reliable, leading and innovative manufacturer is reflected in our products, and can be seen in the new and unique line of products that serve faithfully the professional bicycle riders worldwide.
