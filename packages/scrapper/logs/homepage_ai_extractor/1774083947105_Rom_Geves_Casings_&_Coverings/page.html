Our Story

Rom Geves Casings & Coverings (1997) Ltd. is the primary contracting arm of the Amos Luzon Development and Energy Ltd. Group in the construction industry. ROM is one of Israel’s veteran and leading companies in the field of construction finishing works, and was established for this purpose in 1991. With the goal of growing and expanding, the company underwent reorganization in 1997, and joined the A. Dori Group (later renamed the Amos Luzon Group) as a partner. It was given a new name - Rom Geves Casings &Coverings (1997) Ltd.

EXPERIENCE

With solid foundations developed since 1997,at  ROM we bring a wealth of industry knowledge and expertise to every project we undertake. Our experienced team of professionals has honed their skills over the years, producing exceptional art and flawless workmanship.

STABILITY

Our rich history is a solid foundation for our stability throughout many years of operation. In a market saturated with building projects as well as upheavals, ROM was and continues to be a stable and reliable company that guarantees the construction of a successful project. Our unwavering commitment to reliability has earned us a solid reputation in the industry, making us the most trusted choice for clients seeking quality and expertise.

INNOVATION

PROFESSIONALISM

SAFETY

Safety at work is our top priority. ROM company is uncompromisingly committed to guaranteeing a safe working environment for its employees and partners. We always strive to improve, grow stronger and adapt safer and proper work methods.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem solving is at the core of what we do. Our team of skilled workers excels at identifying challenges in advance, at developing innovative and effective solutions, and at implementing them smoothly to ensure project progress and timely delivery.

RESPONSIBILITY

From the skeleton to the final touches, ROM takes full responsibility for all aspects of a project.
