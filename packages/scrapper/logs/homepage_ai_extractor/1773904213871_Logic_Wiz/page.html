About

We are two friends who are passionate about sudoku and Logic games. We believe that logic games are a great way to challenge your mind and improve your problem-solving skills. In 2020, we decided to quit our high-paying jobs to create the ultimate sudoku app. We wanted to create an app that would be both challenging and fun to play. We also wanted to create an app that would be accessible to everyone, regardless of their skill level. We are proud to say that we have achieved our goal. Our apps are now used by millions of people around the world. We are constantly adding apps, new features and puzzles to our apps. We are grateful for the feedback we receive from our players. It is your feedback that drives us to make the app even better. Thank you for playing with our app!
