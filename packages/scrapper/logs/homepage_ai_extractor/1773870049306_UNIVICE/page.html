    About us

    About Us

    UNIVICE is a sustainable energy group that design, develop and deliver comprehensive solutions from concept to completion.  

    ​

    We are great believers in Circular Economy and thrive to keep resources in use for as long as possible, extract the maximum value from them whilst in use, recover and regenerate products and materials at the end of each service life and generate multiple benefits for our suppliers and clients.

    ​

    We monitor renewable energy opportunities closely and take a hands-on approach at every stage of the project value chain, from the bidding process, tender preparation, feasibility study, environmental impact assessment study, permits, tender design, procurement and project financing, to construction and operations. Our projects are designed for optimal long-term operations. 

    ​

    We seek innovative solutions and long term relationships with technology providers, local partners and customers.

    What we Value

    At UNIVICE, we look for people who share our values and who have the intention and ability to help accomplish our vision. This requires honesty, good judgement, communication skills and a desire and passion to make a positive impact, through curiosity, innovation and courage, for fellow co-workers and for stakeholders at large. 

     

    For UNIVICE it is paramount that licenses and ownership rights to develop and operate assets are obtained through transparent processes. We have zero tolerance for corruption. 

    ​
