The End-to-End AI Data Refinery

A continuous refinement loop that turns raw data into production-grade models. 

Ingest & Decomposition

We take your raw data and break complex problems into specific micro-tasks. 

Orchestrated Validation

Tasks are dynamically routed to the right layer of human judgment: crowd for general consensus, or domain experts for specialized context. 

Continuous Refinement

Feedback flows back into the model, closing the accuracy gap and adapting to new edge cases in real-time.

Where We Win

Tasq.ai orchestrates the ML and GenAI lifecycle by combing world-class models and seamless human guidance for leading AI practitioners.

AI Data Labs (GenAI & LLMs) 

Powering the next generation of models. 

  • RLHF at Scale: We provide the massive-scale human feedback required to fine-tune Large Language Models (LLMs) and reduce hallucinations.
     
  • Synthetic Data Validation: Our crowd validates and ranks synthetic datasets to ensure they mirror real-world complexity before they enter your training pipeline.

  • Model Benchmarking: Create “Golden Sets” of ground truth data to rigorously test model performance against human baselines. 

Fintech & Payments 

Zero tolerance for error. 

  • Fraud & Anomaly Detection: Ambiguous transaction patterns are routed to expert validators to distinguish between complex fraud and legitimate user behavior.

  • KYC & Document Verification: We handle the edge cases in identity verification that automated OCR misses, ensuring compliance without slowing down user onboarding. 

E-Commerce & Retail 

Turning search into conversion. 

  • Visual Search & Discovery: We refine product tagging to ensure that “visual search” results are actually relevant, driving higher conversion rates.

  • Catalog Standardization: Clean up messy supplier data and categorize millions of SKUs with >98% accuracy to fix broken search filters and improve user experience. 

Social Networks 

Context-aware safety at global scale. 

  • Nuanced Content Moderation: Automated filters struggle with sarcasm, cultural nuance, and bullying. Our global network provides local context to keep communities safe. 

  • Fake News & Misinformation: Rapidly deploy task forces to verify claims and label misinformation in real-time during critical events.
