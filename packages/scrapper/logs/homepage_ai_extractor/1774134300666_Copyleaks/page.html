Industry-leading AI Detection & Content Integrity Tools for Individuals and Businesses

In the age of generative AI, content integrity isn’t a nice-to-have. It’s essential. It means protecting all forms of digital content—text, code, intellectual property (IP)— from alteration, duplication, plagiarism, and unauthorized AI manipulation. At its core, it’s about ensuring your work stays original, accurate, and proprietary. 

Since 2015, Copyleaks has been a trusted leader in maintaining content integrity. What began as a mission to help organizations detect plagiarism has evolved into a comprehensive platform of AI detection and compliance tools for today’s most critical use cases. 

Now, with AI reshaping how content is created and shared, we’re helping enterprises and educators stay one step ahead. Designed to support content integrity across all business sectors, our enterprise solutions can help support academic integrity, AI model training, IP and copyright compliance, GenAI governance, code governance, unauthorized LLM usage, and more.

In addition to our enterprise-grade solutions, we offer free, AI-powered tools for individuals, including our award-winning AI Detector and Plagiarism Checker, making content integrity accessible to everyone.

Whether you’re a student writing a term paper or a Fortune 500 chief information officer navigating AI’s impact on your organization, Copyleaks has a solution for you.
