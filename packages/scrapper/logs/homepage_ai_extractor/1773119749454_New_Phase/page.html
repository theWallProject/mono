Nanotechnology brings new hope for metastatic cancer patients

For people facing stage IV cancer, treatments can be limited, with low response rate, and severe side effects.  At New Phase, we’re offering hope. Our approach focuses on prolonging patients’ lives while preserving the quality that makes life meaningful.

Precision Cancer Treatment with SARAH Nanoparticles (SaNP)

The technology combines nanoparticles containing multicore superparamagnetic iron oxide coated by a biocompatible polymer which are delivered to the cancer tissues and are heated to 50°C using an Electromagnetic Induction Systems (EIS) which generates an Alternating Magnetic Field (AMF) and applies electromagnetic hyperthermia, causing destruction to the cancer cells since they are more sensitive to heat, leaving healthy cells unharmed

The Treatment Process: Step-by-Step

Nanoparticles (SaNP) containing super paramagnetic iron oxide cores
SaNP injected intravenously into patient
SaNP preferentially delivered to cancer cells via the Enhanced Permeability and Retention (EPR) Effect
SaNP accumulate in malignant tissue
Apply Alternating Magnetic Field (AMF) at 290 kHz using New Phase EIS
SaNP heat malignant cells to 50°C causing cell death
