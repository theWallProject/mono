FAQs

What are legal outsourcing services?

Legal Outsourcing Services are services where law firms, and companies, hire external providers to handle certain legal tasks instead of doing everything in-house. Typical areas where outsourcing is used is overflow support where the legal team is too busy,  jurisdictional expertise, and temporary gaps such as maternity leave cover.

Who needs legal outsourcing services?

In-house legal teams, both small and large and law firms.  In-house legal teams at companies need outsourcing services to help them reduce their work load and flexibly grow and shrink their teams in a cost effective way.    Law firms typically use outsourcing when assisting their clients with cross jurisdictional needs and for large scale projects where they temporarily want to bring more lawyers on to their teams.  

What are Alternative Legal Service Providers (ALSPs)?

Any company that delivers legal services to clients using a different model to the typical law firm model. Now, the word Alternative is typically dropped as LSPs have become main stream and are no longer considered an alternative model.

Why use Lawflex legal outsourcing services?

LawFlex offers B2B legal services and covers all the corporate, commercial, regulatory and litigation needs to a company.
