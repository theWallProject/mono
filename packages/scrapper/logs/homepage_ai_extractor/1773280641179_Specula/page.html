In every organization, there is an exponential growth in the number of objects and components that the IT team is responsible for. These objects produce an endless growing stream of events, logs and alerts. 

The level of interconnectivity between the objects creates ‘’blind spots’’ which makes the discovery of an IT failure root cause a difficult task requiring lots of specific knowledge. As a result, and regardless of the size of the IT team, MTTR constantly grows YoY.
