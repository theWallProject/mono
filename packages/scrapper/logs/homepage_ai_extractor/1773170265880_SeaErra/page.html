A NEW ERA IN UNDERWATER VISION

SeaErra offers innovative underwater vision enhancement solutions based on state-of-the-art algorithms and technologies. We develop  AI-based solutions, enabling enhanced underwater vision improving the visibility range, contrast, and colors in real-time. In addition we develop AI applications for automatic detection and segmentation. Our Solutions are compatible with a variety of cameras, very easy and intuitive to use, and can be installed on a standard PC.

Suitable for divers, ROVs, AUVs, fixed camera systems and applicable for multiple use cases in the blue economy.

OUR SOLUTIONS

SeaErra’s products and solutions are based on patented and proprietary technology in the field of image enhancement and restoration in scattering media. Methods are based on analyzing the physical image formation model and developing algorithms which reverse the optical effect.

The patented algorithms are applicable in any type of water or light conditions, they have low complexity and require minimum calibration. These unique features allow us to provide our technology is 3 software products:

SeaErra EP

Enhancment player

SeaErra RTS

Real time server

SeaErra AI

AI real time detection
