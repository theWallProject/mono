Chronic Conditions can be Traced Back to Dental Issues

Inflammation is a major reason for chronic illness. While modern medicine intricately understands it, one of the most overlooked sources of chronic inflammation comes from the mouth. We’ve long known how gum disease leads to systemic health issues, and most can identify, treat, and manage it relatively easily. What’s difficult to diagnose is chronic inflammation found around old or failed dental treatments, as well as extraction sites such as wisdom teeth. Our immune system fights off most infections created in our mouths, but often cannot eliminate them completely. These create low-grade chronic infection “hotspots” which consequently enter the bloodstream producing cytokines, or enter the nervous system creating massive systemic health disruption. Unfortunately, as it usually doesn’t cause local symptoms, it may be missed during regular checkups. Moreover, depending on the training of your regular dentist, they may not have a comprehensive understanding of such issues or lack the technology to correctly diagnose the problem in the first place. The good news is that with proper diagnosis, any well-trained dentist or oral surgeon can help by cleaning out inflamed or infected sites with modern dental therapies, restoring you to your full potential. It's imperative all problematic areas are treated to eliminate all low-grade infections and inflammation to gain the best systemic benefits.
