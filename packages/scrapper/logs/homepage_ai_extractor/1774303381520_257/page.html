Flexibility is the solution to growing grid strain

The energy grid is under pressure as demand rises faster than supply. Flexible demand, enabled by EVs, solar, batteries, smart thermostats, heat pumps, and energy efficiency programs, offers a practical path forward.

But adoption remains the challenge

Solar installers, HVAC contractors, energy suppliers, and utilities lack the precise data and marketing tools to efficiently target high potential households.

A Digital Energy Twin for Every Home

257 uses AI and massive data sets to profile every U.S. home, helping providers identify the households most likely to benefit from new energy products and services. We lower acquisition costs, improve customer engagement, and enable a stronger grid.
