Continuous Wellness

Healthy habits for those finding themselves in front of a display all day.

Toni Tone's for-each wellness platform integrates wellness efforts into organizations' workflows, issuing goal-aligned reminders at natural break points when it makes the most sense, even across roles and teams. A configurable tone ensures that coaching fits every individual's vibe, and messaging seamlessly spans timer, tracker and reminder applications. Competitive or cooperative challenges facilitates team-building. We believe tune-ups aren't just for the garage, and improving employee wellbeing isn't just for 5-to-9.
