    OUR INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGY

    InSight Acoustics HyperMic™ Technology is based on a synergistic fusion of optical and acoustic sensors into a single optically guided acoustic beamforming device. HyperMic™ can optically track multiple people or any user selected sound sources, estimate a relative direction to each source and concurrently steer independent acoustic beamformers. The output of each acoustic beamformer is a digital audio stream whose audio content is predominantly comprised of sounds arriving form the direction of the selected source.

     

    An extended version of the HyperMic™ Technology (AKA: CrossBeam™) utilizes two or more synchronized HyperMic™ Modules in order to direct multiple beamformers from different direction towards the same sound source. CrossBeam™ digital audio stream outputs are time-aligned and cross-correlated to provide for an additional speech capture performance boost.

    HYPERMIC™ FUNCTIONAL CAPABILITIES

    Multi-Beamformer Synchronization

    HyperMic™  provides for precision location specific speech capture at extended ranges within noise filled environments

    Source Specific Sound and Speech Capture  
    With High Level of Background Noise Rejection

    HyperMic™ performs on-chip source specific spatial filtration (using beamforming) for one or more selected sound sources, rejecting sounds arriving at the HyperMic™ from all other directions

    Real-Time Sound Source Tracking & Sound Capture

    HyperMic™ tracks the physical location of the selected sounds sources, as either the source or the HyperMic™ move relative to one another, and dynamically steers a beamformer in the tracked direction.

    Concurrent Multi-Source Tracking & Sound Capture

    HyperMic™ concurrently tracks and dynamically steers acoustic beamformers at each of the selected sounds sources

    PLACES WHERE HYPERMIC ™ CAN BE INSTALLED

     AIR AND SEAPORT SECURITY

    Airports, malls, train stations

    Airports, malls, train stations
    In each place you can install several HyperMic ™ units and thus cover large areas
    When connecting several units, you can listen to a single person even if he has moved to different rooms
    HyperMic ™ has auxiliary cameras such as an HD camera and a flyer, which can be used to track one or more people and to receive pulse and body heat

    CONFERENCE ROOMS

    Meeting rooms, meetings, courtrooms, cars


    In each of these places today several people sit and need to hear each separately
    Today, the system is deployed in a variety of different microphones, with HyperMic ™ you can put one single in each room and take everyone in the room or car with no problem and without the need for extra wires

    PRISON

    Prison, security places, secret service


    The ability to monitor each person in one of these places and to hear everything that everyone says is very problematic.
    With HyperMic ™ it is possible to put every cell in jail and fidget very quickly if someone plans a rebellion, suicide or continues his activity inside the prison.
