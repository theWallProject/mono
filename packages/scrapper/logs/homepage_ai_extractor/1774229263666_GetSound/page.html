The Novelty Effect

The novelty effect refers to the improved focus, interest, and performance users often experience when engaging with new technology or features. With GetSound.AI, this novelty isn't fleeting. Thanks to our innovative RTS technology, you receive a fresh, one-of-a-kind soundscape every time you use the app. This constant variation ensures the novelty persists, providing ongoing enrichment to your focus, relaxation, or mindfulness sessions.
