About MidLink

 MidLink is an AWS Advanced Consulting Partner, serving as a one-stop-shop for all your Cloud migration and modernization needs. 

We design well-architected cloud blueprints and provide you with accurate methodology and tools for Cloud enablement and transformation. 

 Owing to our All-in-AWS Cloud Experts, including AWS Architects, DevOps Professionals, Data Engineers, Security Specialists, FinOps Masters and Project Managers, we provide and deliver solutions that reduce infrastructure management overhead, shorten time-to-market, optimize costs, and focus on our clients’ business goals. 

 Moreover, as a subsidiary of EMET Technologies, MidLink benefits from the strength and investment of one of the largest technology integrators in Israel, specializing in both on-prem and cloud IT technologies, along with comprehensive professional services and software development. 

Make it cost effective

Onboarding to AWS Cloud may be perceived as a costly migration, whereas in practice – it can lower your overall spend. We invite you to rely on our knowledge and relationship with AWS, and our ability to navigate, recommend and facilitate the appropriate AWS program tailored for your needs; either AWS migration acceleration program (MAP), AWS Enterprise Discount Program (EDP) for large consumption customers or AWS partner opportunity acceleration (POA) for funding new technology experimenting and proof of concepts, and more.

  • Well Architected

 MidLink leads your journey to the cloud; starting from migration assessment, designing your Cloud architecture, building your Cloud environments, and migrating your application and data workloads, either from on-prem or other cloud platforms to AWS, all based on AWS modern compute and data services, while focusing on all required security, network, logging, monitoring and additional Day-2 operational needs at the highest standards.

 Adopting a Cloud DevOps culture across your tech organization is not an easy journey. MidLink enables our clients to focus on their product development and core business, while assuming the responsibilities for their cloud infrastructure and DevOps tasks. These services include implementation of cloud services, security, and the ability to automate all aspects of CI/CD pipelines focusing on “Shift-Left” methodologies, architecture modernization, IaC (Infra as code), monitoring, logging and more. 

 MidLink assists our clients to unlock the value of their data, using all of AWS modern Cloud Data services. This service includes modernizing your data pipelines, from data ingestion, over to storing data in relational, no-SQL, and other purpose-built managed databases, building your Cloud DataLake / DWH strategy, and through implementing Data visualization, Analytics and ML/AI, all for achieving your business goals. 

 MidLink enables our clients to implement a personalized cloud cost management strategy, aligned with your business growth. It always starts with establishing cost visibility (using the most advanced tools available on the market, at your choice). Thereafter, your allocated FinOps expert will conduct periodical meetings to review your cost optimization tasks, monitor, alert and implement budget controls and Supplemental technical and business-related cost optimization strategies. 

MidLink serves as a Cloud-First one-stop-shop for our clients, allowing to define architectures and blueprints comprising both AWS native services and complementing best of breed SaaS offerings in selected pillars, including Security, Observability, Cost optimization, Data, DevOps, Storage and more. 

 We extend financial benefits through our AWS Marketplace private offers, and – just as importantly, we provide you with local technical assistance (Cloud and ISVs), unified invoicing, and single point of accountability that spans your entire Cloud/SaaS technology stack. 

The AWS Well-Architected Framework provides a comprehensive approach to designing, building, and running high-performance, secure, and cost-effective cloud applications on AWS.
