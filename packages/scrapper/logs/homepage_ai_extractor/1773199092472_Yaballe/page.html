    Frequently asked questions

    • What is Yaballe and how can it help my eCommerce business?
      Yaballe is a comprehensive, all-in-one eCommerce dropshipping platform designed to automate and scale your online business. We provide advanced tools powered by data and AI, including real-time stock monitoring, dynamic repricing, and automated order fulfillment. Our mission is to eliminate the manual, day-to-day operational burden, allowing you to focus on strategic growth, product research, and maximizing your profit margins.
    • Can I sell in global marketplaces even if I’m not located the US or China?
      Of course. Yaballe can help you sell in multiple global marketplaces from a single interface while connecting you to over 10K top-tier active online sellers.
    • Can I start a free trial today?
      Yes. Just click the “Start Free Trial” button, sign-up, connect your online store and you’re ready to go! Start listing and monitoring your store in minutes!
    • How do I get help from Yaballe?
      Our support team operates 24/7,and your online business is our top priority. At any hour, ask us anything; how to use the platform, status of your orders, or assistance with cancellations. Just contact us via our support chat, let us know what you need, and our team of experts will take it from there...
    • How do I migrate to Yaballe from another monitor?
      Simply open the Yaballe monitor, define your break even, install the Yaballe Chrome extension, and start migrating - up tp 500 products with a single click.
    • Can Yaballe help me improve sales and hit-rates in my store?
      We can do better than help. Yaballe can create more demand, bring more traffic to your store, generate more sales, and increase your profits with our smart store management services and automation.
    • What marketplaces and platforms does Yaballe support?
      Yaballe is a versatile platform built to support successful online players across multiple global marketplaces. While we have a strong foundation in eBay dropshipping, our technology is designed for integration and scalability across various eCommerce platforms. Our goal is to empower you to expand and globalize your business, regardless of your primary sales channel.
    • How does Yaballe’s automation compare to manual dropshipping?
      Manual dropshipping is labor-intensive, error-prone, and difficult to scale. Yaballe's automation transforms this by handling critical tasks like Auto-Ordering and Auto-Tracking, Real-Time Stock Monitoring, and Auto-Repricing 24/7. This hands-free approach drastically reduces operational errors, ensures compliance, and allows you to manage a high volume of SKUs and orders effortlessly, making your business remarkably rewarding and scalable.
    • Is Yaballe suitable for both new sellers and large enterprises?
      Yes, Yaballe is built for growth at every stage. New sellers benefit from our streamlined tools and compliant solutions like the MIP (Multi-Channel Integration Platform) to kick-off their business successfully. For large enterprises and high-scale operations, we offer specialized services like 'Fulfilled-by-Yaballe' and comprehensive 'Automate Business Supply Chain' solutions, designed to handle millions of SKUs and complex fulfillment demands with stability and efficiency.
    • How does Yaballe ensure my business remains profitable?
      Profitability is a core focus of Yaballe. Our Auto-Repricer constantly monitors competitor pricing and adjusts your listings dynamically to ensure you remain competitive while protecting your desired profit margin. Furthermore, by automating fulfillment and stock monitoring, we eliminate costly errors like out-of-stock cancellations and manual processing mistakes, directly contributing to a healthier bottom line and a higher return on investment (ROI).
    • How do I get started with Yaballe?
      Getting started with Yaballe is simple. You can join our platform to begin automating your dropshipping business immediately. Our resources and support team are available to guide you through the onboarding process, connecting your store, and utilizing our powerful tools to start scaling your sales and maximizing your efficiency.
    • Does Yaballe provide specialized solutions for high-volume or enterprise-level eCommerce operations?
      Absolutely. Yaballe is equipped to support high-volume and enterprise-level eCommerce businesses with specialized solutions. Our offerings include the 'Fulfilled-by-Yaballe' service and advanced 'Automate Business Supply Chain' solutions, both meticulously designed to meet the rigorous demands of large-scale online retail and complex supply chain management.
