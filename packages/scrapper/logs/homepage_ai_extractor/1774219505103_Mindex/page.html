FEATURED CLIENT SUCCESS

State of New Jersey Chapter 78 Insurance Benefit Calculation Automation

The State of New Jersey's health benefits reform law, called Chapter 78, requires public sector employees to contribute a % of their paycheck to their health benefits. Many organizations participating in State-administered retirement systems and health benefits programs, have had to resort to significant manual payroll efforts to comply with the state’s legislation. There was a growing need to automate this process in order to free up critical resources, and eliminate the risk of legal ramifications that could result from errors due to manual calculations and data entry.
