Firering Strategic Minerals

Firering Strategic Minerals plc (AIM: FRG) is an emerging producer of quicklime and explorer of critical minerals with its primary focus on Limeco, a significant quicklime project in Zambia.  Having started production in early 2025, Limeco is now scaling up operations with the goal of producing 600-800 tonnes of quicklime per day along with a range of ancillary products. With over US$100 million in historical investment, the project is strategically positioned to support the expanding copper producers in the Central African Copperbelt, which are currently reliant on imported quicklime from South Africa. 

Further Details about Limeco can be found by visiting their website HERE

Additionally, Firering is advancing the Atex Project in northern Côte d'Ivoire, an exploration project rich in lithium and tantalum-niobium, with drilling results indicating significant resource potential in this established and stable mining jurisdiction.
