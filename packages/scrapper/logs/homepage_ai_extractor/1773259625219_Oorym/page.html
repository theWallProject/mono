How it Works?

Oorym’s waveguide is a revolutionary optical element that separates the two main functions of coupling the light waves out of the substrate and combining the light waves from the external scene with the virtual image which is projected from the display source. While in all other existing technologies for AR, a single complicated device is utilized for those two functions, resulting in an inefficient and complex solution, Oorym’s waveguide is composed of two simple components that accomplish independently the required functions.
