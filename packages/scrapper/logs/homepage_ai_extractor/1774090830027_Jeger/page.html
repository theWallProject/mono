    About Us

    Welcome to JEGER, a leading boutique company in worldwide exhibitions design founded and managed by the renowned designer Orit Jeger (B.Sc.T.E.). With over three decades of experience, we specialize in providing creative and unique solutions that are tailor-made to meet our customer's vision and needs. At JEGER, we have a diverse, dynamic, and creative team consisting of industrial and interior designers, marketing communication experts, photography professionals, and graphic designers. Our team works together to deliver high-quality, innovative, and personalized designs that meet our client's expectations. Our mission is to offer our customers the best exhibition design experience, from conceptualization to execution. We pride ourselves on our long-term knowledge and experience in the exhibition design industry, and we are committed to continuously innovating our designs and services to ensure we exceed our customer's expectations.

    Why choose us?

    • One “Stop Shop" - a wide variety of solutions in the design, production, supervision, accompaniment of custom-made stands, as well as marketing tools: multimedia for simple and quick implementation of any event. Leave it to us, we provide logistic management services throughout the chain of contact with event organizers and managers.

    • We keep close relationships with our customers and provide management systems based on personal accompaniment, mutual trust and professional acquaintance.
