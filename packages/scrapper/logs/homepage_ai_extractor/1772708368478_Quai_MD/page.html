Turning Patient Care Into Best Care

Quai.MD is an Al-powered clinical workflow platform that elevates clinical judgment, reduces variability, and automates documentation - helping physicians deliver better outcomes with less effort.

Designed for real-world emergency care, Quai.MD embeds intelligence directly into the clinical workflow - supporting patient assessment, differential diagnosis, workup, and documentation inside the EHR. By aligning medical knowledge, patient data, and best practices at the point of care, the platform helps clinicians practice the best practice, every time.

  • Integrated best-practices
  • Better patient outcomes
  • Reduced clerical burden
  • Improved documentation
  • Higher Efficiency 
  • Faster time-to-bill

The Emergency Department Challenge

The ER represents the perfect storm of clinical complexity. Physicians must navigate patient care in a chaotic, high-pressure environment with minimal digital support - relying solely on memory, attention, and fragmented systems. This contributes to inefficiency, variability, and elevated clinical and financial risk.

The Critical Gap in Healthcare

Clinical Gap

What physicians should do versus what actually happens.

  • Impacts patient outcomes
  • Reduces care efficiency
  • Increases total cost of care

Documentation Gap

What physicians do versus what is documented.

  • Causes claim denials
  • Results in revenue loss
  • Delays reimbursement

Healthcare systems face a gap between optimal care and real-world practice, leaving clinicians without adequate workflow support and resulting in avoidable variation and poorer outcomes, resulting in $50M-$150M annual loss per hospital, adding up to $100B annual loss nationwide
