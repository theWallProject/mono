MODULAR TRAINING INFRASTRUCTRE

Modular Training Solutions for Urban Warfare

Built on real combat experience, not classroom theory. 

Trango Systems delivers the structures your training runs on: modular shoot houses, CQB lanes, street fronts, and tunnel networks that scale from small drills to full mission rehearsal. 

These training arenas reset in minutes, so instructors swap layouts fast and keep teams solving new problems, not running the same hallway again and again. Furniture kits, vehicle models, and Friend & Foe targets then load each setup with clutter, cover, and tough shoot / no‑shoot calls.

QUICK FACTS

100% Modular

Lego-like modularity allows for fast and simple layout changes. Supports multitude of training scenarios.

27 lbs / 12 kg

Extremely lightweight: one wall is only 27 lbs (12 kg). Easy to move, transport, and reinstall.

>12,000 hits

Withstands over 12,000 hits with 5.56 mm or over 3,000 hits with 9mm – and still standing. 

Tool-Free

No tools required to assemble the entire structure. No special skills, no ground preparation.

No Ricochet

No risk of ricochet. Bullets go through with no splinter. Suitable for both – live fire and simunition.

Highly adaptable training systems designed by veterans to provide military and law enforcement personnel with a realistic and safe environment for tactical drills. Lightweight, interlocking panels require no specialized tools, allowing teams to rapidly configure and scale custom floor plans for close-quarters combat training. Engineered for longevity, the materials are exceptionally durable against both harsh weather and thousands of live rounds while eliminating the hazard of ricochets. Ultimately, the system offers a cost-effective mobile solution for professional units needing a versatile, mission-specific facility that can be deployed or stored with minimal effort.

Explore Shoot House Kits

Ricochet‑free, true‑to‑life CQB environments that assemble fast and reconfigure on demand. Weather-resistant, durable, and lightweight.

Fully Modular and extremely light weight. Construct in hours, reconstruct in minutes by 2-4 people.

No tools, no special ground preparation. Safe for live fire and simulation rounds.

Almost zero maintenance.

From basic 2-room shoot house of 323 sq. feet to massive 2884 sq. feet structures – choose your modular training system and start training with no delays.

Weight of one Panel: 27 lbs (12 kg)

Weather-proof: rain, snow, extreme temperatures, UV radiation.
