RDV Systems brings your
 Designs Data Projects to life!

Visualization and safety analysis are traditionally time-consuming and cumbersome processes, made all the more inconvenient by outdated tools. RDV Systems, works side-by-side with US State DOT’s, consultants, private engineering firms and public agencies to provide the opportunity for safer roads. We also create the industry’s most realistic, eye-catching, effective and versatile 3D models for visualization on regional roads or highways, urban roads and streets and also for rural or low-volume roads.

RDV Systems focuses on 2 major initiatives for US roadways. Remaining laser focused on safety, RDV Systems offers a 3D Road Safety Audit Tool and a Visualization Technology called 123BIM. We invite you to join us online on LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram.

ROAD SAFETY AUDIT

3D TOOL

Road Safety Audit 3D

This revolutionary tool uses a systemic approach for the road safety audit processt to identify safety deficiencies in 3D environments. It uses this tool for both new and existing roads and it provides very accurate data and insight into potentially hazardous sections of the road.

123BIM

123BIM was designed to seamlessly convert actual, complex project designs created in any industry-standard CAD platform into 3D digital models, which can then be uploaded to the cloud for collaboration and public engagement on the 123BIM platform.
