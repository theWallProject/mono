Core Functions

How it works.

Arbiter's platform is powered by the Record-Action-Alignment model - a continuous engine that turns fragmentation into flow  

Record

Unifies clinical, financial, and policy data into a single longitudinal source of truth

Action

Agentic Ai recommends and automates the next best step, in real-time

Alignment

Monitors outcomes and keeps everyone in sync, learning continuously to improve performance
The Arbiter Platform

Gap Detection & Clinical Triggers

Detect care opportunities in real time.

Arbiter surfaces referral opportunities based on quality gaps and risk adjustment data - missed screenings, overdue labs, or preventive exams - directly in the EMR. This ensures every referral starts with a structured, clinically and financially aligned trigger.

Provider Graph & Routing Engine

Match every patient to the right provider, every time.

Routing intelligence optimizes referrals for cost, quality,  and availability, backed by a dynamic, validated directory of in-network providers - reducing leakage, improving match quality, and powering site-of-care optimization.

Automated Prior Authorizations

End the delays that derail care.

Arbiter streamlines delegated and standard prior authorizations by structuring criteria directly from payers and codifying raw policy documents. The result: faster approvals, fewer cancellations, and less administrative burden for both payers and providers.

Patient Engagement & Scheduling

Close the loop with every patient.

Arbiter engages patients the moment a referral is created - offering multi-channel outreach, real-time scheduling, reminders, and follow-up. By guiding patients all the way through to booked appointments, Arbiter increases completion rates, reduces no-shows, and improves patient experience.
