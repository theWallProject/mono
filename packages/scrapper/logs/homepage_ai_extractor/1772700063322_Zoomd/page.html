FAQs

  • How does Zoomd Drive Better Performance?

    Zoomd enhances performance through a data-driven approach, leveraging AI and vast media reach to optimize campaigns in real time. Our unified platform allows brands to manage campaigns across hundreds of media sources from a single smart dashboard, while AI-powered bidding and optimization maximize ROAS. With scalable growth strategies, from programmatic ads to influencer collaborations, Zoomd ensures brands achieve efficient, high-impact results.

  • What is a DSP (Demand Side Platform)?

    A DSP (Demand Side Platform) is an automated advertising platform that optimizes campaign performance in real-time. It connects advertisers to multiple ad exchanges, networks, and SSPs, making split-second decisions to deliver your ads to the right audience at the best price. Zoomd’s advanced mobile DSP technology processes millions of ad opportunities daily across 70+ markets.

  • Why is Zoomd’s Influencer Marketing community so effective?

    Zoomd’s influencer marketing community is highly effective due to its data-driven matching, performance-based approach, and global network of creators in various languages. Our team carefully selects influencers based on audience demographics, engagement levels, and campaign goals to ensure maximum impact. Whether working with micro or top-tier influencers, Zoomd scales campaigns efficiently, delivering authentic, high-engagement content that feels native, builds trust, and drives real actions.

  • What is CGC (Creator-Generated Content)?

    Creator-Generated Content (CGC) is authentic, influencer-driven content created by micro-creators to promote brands and apps in an organic, engaging way. Unlike traditional ads, CGC seamlessly blends into social feeds, making it more relatable and impactful. It includes user testimonials, unboxing videos, in-app experiences, and branded storytelling—designed to captivate audiences and drive real engagement.

  • What makes Zoomd different from other performance marketing platforms?

    One unified platform for all your performance marketing needs. Instead of juggling multiple dashboards and vendors, Zoomd gives you complete control through a single interface. Our proprietary technology manages and optimizes campaigns across 600+ media sources while providing real-time insights for better decision-making.

  • What performance marketing services does Zoomd provide?

    Zoomd provides various performance marketing solutions to help brands and mobile apps acquire high-quality users efficiently. Our services include multi-channel user acquisition across platforms like Meta, Google, TikTok, and programmatic networks, performance-based influencer marketing, and both managed and self-serve advertising solutions. With proprietary AI-powered optimization and a centralized analytics dashboard, we continuously refine campaigns in real-time, maximizing efficiency and ROI while offering deep data insights for smarter marketing decisions.

  • Where Does Zoomd Operate?

    Zoomd operates globally, with a strong presence across North America, Latin America, Europe, and Asia. We work with advertisers and publishers in over 80 countries, delivering performance-driven user acquisition and media buying solutions tailored to local markets.

  • How does Zoomd ensure quality across the open internet?

    Zoomd works only with a rigorously vetted safelist of exchanges and partners, enforcing strict fraud-prevention standards and ensuring brand-safe environments. Combined with our real-time bidding engine and measurement integrations, marketers get the reach of the open internet with the stability and accountability they expect from premium platforms.

  • Is Zoomd’s tech stack suitable for in-house teams?

    Yes. Zoomd’s tech UA stack, including our fully self-serve DSP, is designed to empower in-house teams that want full control with zero operational friction. Marketers can plan, launch, and optimize campaigns independently through an intuitive interface, tap into vetted global inventory, and make smarter decisions with real-time insights and 3rd-party measurement integrations. And whenever deeper strategic guidance is needed, Zoomd’s experts are there to support and elevate performance without taking ownership away from your team.
