Backup Payment Method

FlyCode’s Backup Payment Method uses intelligent automation to seamlessly retry failed payments using alternate valid cards already stored on file. No manual workflows. No service disruption. It’s a simple toggle—no code required—that unlocks higher recovery and better retention.

Learn more

Failed Payment Recovery

FlyCode helps subscription businesses recover lost revenue and prevent involuntary churn with AI-powered smart retries and payment optimization—working behind the scenes to turn failed transactions into recovered revenue.

Learn more

Reduce involuntary churn

Involuntary churn happens when subscribers unintentionally lose access due to payment failures—not because they chose to cancel. In most cases, customers don’t even realize their payment failed.

Learn more

Email outreach and communication

We also have robust models powering failed payments emails and communications. It means we will coordinate emails with retries to reduce involuntary churn. We send emails at the user’s local time with transactional emails and with the best sender score and deliverability.

Learn more

Billing workflows

Our models go further—analyzing failure reasons, adapting retry strategies dynamically (vs. fixed), expanded retry capabilities and leveraging alternative payment methods to recover revenue. We optimize the billing lifecycle and workflows of customers.

Learn more

Intelligent routing

We built decisioning models that take into account 100’s of datapoints, such as customer and payment metadata as well as internal classifiers of error codes and messages. Frequently Asked Questions

How is FlyCode different from traditional payment-recovery tools?

FlyCode is AI-native from day one. Its models learn from every transaction and issuer response, continuously refining retry timing, routing, and messaging, far beyond the static, rule-based schedules used by legacy solutions and PSP’s.

What pricing model does FlyCode use?

FlyCode charges only on dollars it actually recovers. There are no seats or minimums, our incentives are perfectly aligned with your

Why do Visa & Stripe partnerships matter?

Direct partnership with Visa and Stripe gives FlyCode network insights and payment meta data, boosting recovery rates while preserving compliance-grade safeguards.

How fast can we launch FlyCode?

Most customers go live in hours with a Stripe or Shopify apps or API keys.

What ROI should we expect?

Live case studies with leading SaaS and eCom brands show a 16-25 % lift in recovery rate with ARR boost of 5% - 9%.

How does FlyCode decide when to retry a payment?

FlyCode uses machine learning to find the best moment to retry a failed payment.

Does FlyCode do more than simple retries?

Yes. FlyCode prevents churn by automatically charging alternate cards on file, sending communication, finding revenue anomalies, orchestrating smart dunning flows, and surfacing granular analytics your team can act on.

How do I fix failed payments if I’m using Stripe?

Simply enable FlyCode’s Stripe app. Our AI reads your Billing webhooks, predicts the best retry window, and routes each attempt through the highest-approval path—often recovering the charge before the customer even notices.

How can I solve involuntary churn?

Involuntary churn is mostly payment-related. FlyCode attacks it on three fronts: (1) AI-optimized retries, (2) alternate-card-on-file routing, and (3) predictive dunning that reaches customers before the subscription lapses. The result is fewer cancellations and higher lifetime value.

Do you provide email templates for failed-payment outreach?

Yes. At FlyCode, payment recovery doesn't stop with retries. Our advanced email communication models coordinate perfectly with payment retries to recover revenue and reduce involuntary churn.
