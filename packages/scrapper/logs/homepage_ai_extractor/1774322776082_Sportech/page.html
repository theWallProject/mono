Our Business

Sportech is a licensed operator offering responsible gaming entertainment in the State of Connecticut. The Group’s B2B divisions deliver fully licensed lottery solutions and managed venue services to clients in highly regulated markets.

Venues

Holder of exclusive licences in the State of Connecticut, USA to conduct pari-mutuel betting through Sportech’s Winners branded venues, telephone and online. Also licensed by the Connecticut Lottery Corporation to offer sports betting at Sportech’s venues under the Playsugarhouse brand.

Read More

Digital

Provider of award-winning Lottery games and iLottery software whose partners include some of the worlds’ biggest regulated gaming companies, as well as being the owner of 123Bet.com as pan-USA pari-mutuel online retail platform.

Read More

Investors

Sportech is an innovator in the highly-regulated global gaming technology sector, holding exclusive licences in Connecticut, USA and offering world class lottery technology solutions. The Group seeks to achieve long-term shareholder returns by leveraging Sportech’s heritage, gaming licences, technologies, client relationships, investor engagement and smart capital deployment.
