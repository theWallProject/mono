What is CC submit?

CC-submit stands for Credit Card Submit which is a type of offer where a conversion is counted when the customer leaves the credit card information in a form. These types of offers are often connected with free trial campaigns or nearly free trial campaigns where a small sum of money is written off from the card. With Credit Card Submit Offers (СС Submit offers), the conversion is considered to be completed when the card functions.

How to run CC-submit offers properly?

The main thing for this user flow: the user should be prepared to share their credit card information. We recommend not to offer it on the first page and lead to it gradually. By no means should misleading advertising be used.

What are sweepstakes?

Sweepstakes are a type of promotion that often involve incentives to enter and win prizes. Sweepstake winners receive their prize immediately after they compete against each other. Our IQ-games-based sweepstakes allow the user time based on how fast their mind works. Don’t worry if this sounds daunting – we’ll make sure everyone has an opportunity regardless whether they’re quick-minded or slow at solving problems.

What is VOD streaming?

Video on Demand, or VOD for short means that viewers can watch videos at their convenience. There are many different types of services which provide this service including Netflix and Disney+. The convenience of Video on Demand is hard to beat. VOD services offer videos, TV shows and movies at the user’s own pace with the ability to choose when available!

What is PC security software?

A computer’s best friend!
Software that protects your system, keeps it running smoothly and securely with the latest security updates. The goal of this software is to provide a safe haven for the user’s computing devices. It helps you manage access control, protect data from viruses and network/Internet-based intrusions with its antivirus features – all while defending against other potential security risks such as physical attacks on the server or malware injections within processes themselves .
