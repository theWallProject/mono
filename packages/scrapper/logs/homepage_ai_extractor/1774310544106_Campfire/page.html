Today's collaboration tools weren't built for 3D

Teams have spent years trying to force 3D pegs into 2D holes, and it's no secret that we're well past the breaking point.

We shoehorn 3D models
into 2D documents

From slide decks to CAD screenshares, even simple communication takes more time and effort than it should.

We send files into a black hole of fragmented tools

Where's that email? Is it the latest deck? Comments live in doc, drives, chats, and everywhere except on the model.

And we keep hitting the same frustrating walls

The process is so painful, most people can't weigh in until it’s too late — when every change comes at a steep price.

No engineering degree required

Not everyone can use CAD, but everyone can use Campfire. It’s secure, powerful, and engineer-approved — yet so intuitive, anyone can join in.

EASY TO SHARE

Share scenes as easily as Google Docs

Drop 3D models into scenes and lay them out however you want. Then just set the right permissions, and share securely with a link.

EASY TO ANNOTATE

Attach annotations directly to 3D models

Put comments where they belong: on the model. That way, all your conversations live in one place and nothing gets lost in translation.

EASY TO COMMUNICATE

Explain with lasers (literally)

Unlike screensharing, Campfire gives everyone control — move around, grab parts, or point a laser. It's natural and instantly clear.

Make scenes, not slides

Slides are flat, but scenes represent physical space. Add CAD, scans, or any 3D format — then easily copy, paste, move, rotate, or resize models to tell any story you need.
