WHO WE ARE

SMARTECH is a global provider of smart manufacturing solutions that help industrial manufacturers to improve their profitability by increasing raw material efficiency and transforming critical processes into autonomous manufacturing.

SMARTECH is a hi-tech company made up of industry veterans with backgrounds in process engineering, control systems, algorithms, artificial intelligence and physical sciences.

SMARTECH has received global awards for innovation in technology. Recently, SMARTECH received an IOT Breakthrough Award in the category Smart Manufacturing Company of the Year for its Autonomous Manufacturing Platform.

WHAT WE DO

We provide smart material systems and artificial intelligence solutions to the traditional manufacturing sector spurring industries to upgrade their facilities to smart factories by transforming the production lines into high-performing, autonomous, data-driven and sustainable assets.

WE IMPROVE PROFITABILITY

WE GROW MARGINS

We help generate significant value via both directly decreasing the quantity of raw materials needed and increasing production capacity.

WE REDUCE COSTS

Our systems transform raw materials via an in-line process in the production line, just-in-time prior to use.

WE INCREASE CAPACITY

Our software drives autonomous operation of critical production processes to increase capacity, while improving quality.
