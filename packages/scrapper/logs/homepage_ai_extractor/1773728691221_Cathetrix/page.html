Foley-Safe™ is a catheter stabilizer for the prevention of Catheter-Associated Urinary Tract Infections (CAUTI)

and unintended and accidental Foley catheter extractions.

First-of-its-kind stabilizing and securing device Reducing the chance of urinary tract infections due to a Dwelling catheter,

protecting against accidental urinary catheter extraction by severing the catheter and preventing potential damage to the bladder and urethra.

Testimonials

Having a catheter felt embarrassing at first. I avoided going out or even moving much. When I started using Foley-Safe 2.0, I felt secure again. It holds everything firmly, so I can walk, sit, and even go for short trips without fear. It’s discreet, comfortable, and made me feel like myself again. We, as Doctors, are aware of many cases in which unconscious patients are pulling out the catheter because the uncomfortable feeling it causes. By doing so they are harming themselves, causing sometime irreversible damage. FoleySafe certainly avoids such damages. Moreover, FoleySafe avoids dislodgment, and by doing so reduces drastically the danger of Catheter Associated Urinary Tract Infection (CAUTI).
Dr. Aaron (Roni) Manes Urologist Surgeon
I examined FoleySafe carefully. Because Foley catheter is stabilized in the bladder with a balloon, the unstable balloon position may cause an uncomfortable sensation to the patient. Furthermore, in many cases, the patients have a tendency of pulling the balloon inflated catheter out of the body. The pulling out maneuver either causing damage (sometime irreversible) to the bladder and/or to the urethra. Foley-Safe can avoid these uneven situations. Since I have very good experience with FoleySafe apparatus, I can certainly recommend the usage of the FoleySafe at hospital setting for both children and adults' applications.
Haim Bibi Professor of Pediatric Pediatric intensive care
