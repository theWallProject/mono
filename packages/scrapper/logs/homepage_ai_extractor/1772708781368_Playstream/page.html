FAQ

Is Playstream.GG accessible to any streamer?
Playstream is accessible to PC and console players as well, however for now, console players will have to verify and manually complete Playstream challenges as well.
What streaming platform does Playstream.GG support?
Whether you are streaming on Twitch, YouTube, Facebook or any other streaming platform, we’ve got you covered! And remember. we require no third-party platform to stream!
Is the service free?
One of our main goals is to increase your revenue, we have made the tool free to download and our service's are free. We do have a commission fee on every completed challenge which depends on your Playstream XP and progression, "Playstream Streamer's" receive 80% of the challenge donation, "Affiliate's" receive 85% and "Partner's" receive 90%. If by the end of the stream the challenge is not completed, the viewer does not get charged. To keep transactions safe and protected, the payment method is through PayPal. PayPal's external fees are currently 4.4% and 0.30$ on each transaction.
What games does Playstream.GG support?
Playstream.GG’s A.I. technology supports multiple games like Warzone, Fortnite, Apex and Fallguys. In these games you will be able to be challenged automatic challenges that Playstream will track and complete it when the task is done. You can see the full list of supported games on our homepage. If you have a game that you would like us to support, send us a mail, until then you can create your own challenge using contract cards for any game that will have to manually be completed by you!
