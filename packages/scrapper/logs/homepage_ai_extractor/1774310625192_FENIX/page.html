Riding right

Sure, Fenix pushes the boundaries, but we won’t compromise when it comes to safety.

  • Ride responsibly on sidewalks
  • Protect your smarts - wear a helmet
  • Watch out for walkers
  • Don't double up, one person per scooter
  • Use on low speed roads (under 40km/h)
  • Riders must be 18+
  • If your route has a bike lane, then that's the place to be
  • The best riders park carefully. Be like them
