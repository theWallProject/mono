Experience our products in operation

Our avian monitoring system for wind farms is just one example of our technology in operation. Using 3D radar and AI-powered cameras, it tracks birds with extraordinary accuracy, identifies species, and predicts high-risk situations in real time.

We use cookies

Like many other websites, ART uses cookies. Cookies' are small pieces of information sent by an organisation to your computer and stored on your hard drive to enable websites to recognise you when you visit them. They collect statistical data about your browsing actions and patterns and do not identify you as an individual. For example, we use cookies to store your language preference. This helps us to improve our website and provide a better and more personalised service. In general, you can always configure your browser settings to stop receiving cookies but we also include a Cookie Consent Manager in our website so you can decide which cookies you allow.
