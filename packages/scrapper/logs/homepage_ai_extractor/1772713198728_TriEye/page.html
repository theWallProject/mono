Seeing Beyond the Visible

TriEye offers SWIR sensing capabilities to mass-market applications. Our unique technology provides mission-critical imaging and ranging information in all weather and lighting conditions. We enable the implementation of SWIR sensors at scale; Vision systems can now utilize a new layer of critical information, reshaping how they operate and enhancing their capabilities tremendously.

SWIR For The Masses

Reduce the expenditure up to 100x using a deep-tech approach, overcoming the obstacle of InGaAs-based SWIR sensors

SCALABILITY

Analysis: Increased Safety Demands in ADAS Features

The global demand for increased safety in ADAS features is well reflected in the new version of UN regulation R157 and the planned AEB night tests. Specifically, the need to reduce pedestrian injuries (day and night) as well as avoid highway collisions due to small, fallen objects (AKA ‘lost cargo’) is imminent.
