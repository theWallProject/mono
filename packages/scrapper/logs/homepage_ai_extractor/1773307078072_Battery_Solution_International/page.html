About Us!

Battery Solution International Ltd.
(BSI) considers itself as a global pioneer in the field of battery life extension
and battery refurbishment for various types of lead acid batteries.

BSI has developed an end-to-end, innovative and unique solution utilizing state-of-the-art technology.
Our solution is designed to significantly reduce the costs of battery maintenance for especially large traction
forklift fleets and telecom operators. The entire process is supported by a green, environmental approach.

During its years of operation, BSI has established a successful track record
in its international chain of battery life extension and battery restoration facilities.

Beyond Industrial Batteries:
Introducing BSI Micromobility Battery Refurbishment

Battery Solutions International leads the industry in reducing downtime and costs while extending the battery life
of industrial material movers like forklifts, scissor lifts, and pallet stackers. 

We now offer specialized services for micromobility batteries tailored to the unique requirements
of compact, high-performance batteries used in personal transportation devices like electric scooters,
golf carts, rickshaws, and bikes. Through battery refurbishment, we prolong the lifespan of micromobility batteries,
aiding waste reductionin the growing electric micro-transportation sector.
