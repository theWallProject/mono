Services

Our experienced team offers a wide variety of software development services, all focused on Machine Learning and Deep Learning

  • Vision

    With Deep Learning it is possible to create new technologies that tackle even the most difficult concepts. Object detection, video analysis, scene classification and object recognition are just a few of the projects we can assist with.

  • Cyber-Security

    Our engineers can design and develop high-quality systems to recognize network endpoints by their communication patterns, recognize anomalies and detect suspicious behavior.

  • Design

    Image generation, 3D modeling, knitting models, architecture and construction are all going through an automation phase with assistance from convolutional and generative neural networks.

  • Language and Audio

    Advancements in recurrent networks, such as LSTM, have provided improvements in audio and natural language processing, enabling increased automatic translation, speech to text, sentiment analysis, context understanding and more.

  • Healthcare

    AI and Deep Learning are critical in automatically profiling medical conditions and treatments based on medical records. For example, they can be useful in radiology for advanced image processing.

  • Data analytics

    Using advanced statistical methodologies, it is possible to generate predictive models for sales cycles, inventory management and detect repeat purchasing and behavioral patterns.

    Are We?

  • Why
    Us?

    We utilise our extensive experience in Software Development and Machine Learning to assist companies raise POCs, develop new projects from the ground up, update, enhance and modernize existing products and deliver business-critical projects quickly.

    Work Modes
