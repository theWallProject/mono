ES RUBBER GROUP

ES RUBBER conforms to the highest international standards of quality

INFRASTRUCTURE

Exceptional infrastructure starts with ES RUBBER profiles for buildings, roads, bridges, tunnels, water supplies, sewers, and more. Utilized in various industries and synonymous with quality, reliability, and performance, our wide range of profiles and seals give our customers an impressive array of products for a range of applications.

TRANSPORTATION

ES RUBBER profiles and seals play an essential part in numerous sealing applications for the transportation industry. We offer a wide range of profiles for vehicles such as buses, trucks, passenger vehicles, trains, and railways. All products comply with IATF 16949:2016 and EN 45545 requirements.

HOSES

ES RUBBER manufactures high-quality, textile-reinforced industrial hoses for a variety of applications: water, air, gas, and fuel, welding, air, and vacuum brakes, hydraulics, automotive heating, and cooling.

INDUSTRY

ES RUBBER operates at the forefront of technology and manufactures products from advanced elastomers such as FKM, HNBR, NBR.
We manufacture gaskets and components for milking systems, rubber parts for machines, control bodies, filtration systems, and more.

SECURITY

ES RUBBER Industries has developed technology and production lines that meet the strict standards of the defense industry.
These products are carefully manufactured, to ensure durability over time and proper operation in all field conditions.

CONSTRUCTION

Constructing and building the future with ES RUBBER sealing profiles.
ES RUBBER is generating innovative solutions for civil engineering and tunneling applications and many other construction needs.
