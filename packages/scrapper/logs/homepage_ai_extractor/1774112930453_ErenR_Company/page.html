Our Partners

We have today the ability to source any type of product, innovation and new technology in Israel.

Founded in 1999 by Irene Feder, French Foreign Trade Advisor, VP Chamber of Commerce France Israel HDF. EREN. R Company is a leading sourcing firm in Israel, collaborating and working with the strongest retailers in the world and  promoting the industrial consumer products filed abroad.

Eren R. Company is present in more than 25 countries and 4 different continents. We partner with the factories on various aspects of the export field such as consulting and risk management on their international export activity, on-demand sourcing products, guiding them through the steps of becoming global sellers, preparing the organizations to social audits and quality controls, supporting them in all the specifics call for tender and customizing the offered value-proposition to your needs.

Our Services

Confidence, Partnership and Savoir-Faire towards success.

Strategy Consulting

We provide comprehensive business strategy support, tailored to the unique challenges of each company. Our approach combines industry expertise, competitive intelligence, and pragmatic solutions to drive growth and maximize performance. Whether you’re a startup, an SME, or a large corporation, we help you define a clear and effective roadmap to achieve your goals. Strategy, innovation, expansion – together, let’s take your business to the next level.

Back Office & Administrative Support

We provide comprehensive back-office solutions to support companies in their business development, procurement, logistics, and operational management in Europe. Our mission is to ensure smooth, efficient, and cost-effective operations, allowing businesses to focus on their core growth objectives.

Business Development

We specialize in supporting businesses looking to expand and establish a strong presence in the European market. With extensive experience in international trade, retail partnerships, and strategic market positioning, we provide tailored solutions to help companies successfully navigate the complexities of European business environments.

Quality Control & Special Audits

We assist factories and manufacturers in preparing for social and quality audits, ensuring they meet international standards and the requirements of global buyers.

Sourcing

We specialize in sourcing high-quality consumer goods and groundbreaking innovations to meet the evolving needs of our clients. Whether you are a retailer, distributor, or corporate buyer, we act as your trusted partner, identifying the best products from global markets and ensuring seamless integration into your business.

Business Tours

We offer fully managed business tours for companies and investors seeking to explore Israel, meet potential partners, and visit key industrial sites. From the moment you land to your departure, we ensure a seamless, productive, and well-organized experience, allowing you to focus on building strategic business relationships.
