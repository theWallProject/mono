About Pixoner

We recognize people and objects in mass real life photos fast and accurate. We offer a variety of services including personal photo albums, personal video clips, online sale galleries and API for any photo platform. Pixoner's photo automation technology is used by major operations worldwide, offering a cost effective solution to maximize brand engagement, social reach, fan acquisition and sales.
