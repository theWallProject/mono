A Revolutionary Gravity-Powered Micro-Irrigation Solution

N-Drip transforms agriculture with disruptive, precise irrigation technology that conserves water, reduces energy and fertilizer use, and enhances yield potential – all without requiring pumps or costly water filtration.

 

 

As the most cost-effective solution for converting flood-irrigated fields into water-efficient systems, N-Drip provides an exceptionally efficient way of replenishing water used in different industries while helping to transform agriculture to become a sustainable one.

The World Needs Alternatives to Flood Irrigation

Agriculture consumes 70 percent of global water resources, and much of it is wasted. As water scarcity intensifies, one of the most critical challenges is replacing the outdated practice of flood irrigation.

Globally, 85 percent of irrigated fields—600 million acres—rely on flood irrigation. Even in the U.S., nearly 40 percent of all irrigation is flood-based. This unsustainable method wastes nearly half of the water used, suppresses yields, depletes soil and minerals, and contributes to water contamination.

N-Drip is the most cost-effective alternative to flood irrigation.

 

A Game-Changing Innovation in Agriculture

N-Drip’s Gravity-Powered Micro-Irrigation Solution utilizes always-available gravity to deliver precise, efficient irrigation to a wide array of crops regardless of water source or quality or soil conditions. No external energy is needed.

This is made possible by a proprietary dripper, uniquely designed with a multidimensional flow structure and developed through years of research led by Prof. Uri Shani and a team of experts.

A Comprehensive Solution

N-Drip’s team of agronomists and irrigation specialists works closely with farmers to ensure water savings are achieved, alongside additional benefits including lower energy costs for groundwater pumping, reduced fertilizer use, lower labor costs, and other input savings.

The N-Drip Connect – a decision support system – continuously monitors fields, measures water savings, and provides accurate data on water usage and fertilizer application.

Additionally, N-Drip offers an innovative solution for water replenishment initiatives, ensuring sustainable water management not only for N-Drip-irrigated fields but also for fields using other irrigation methods through N-Drip Connect.
