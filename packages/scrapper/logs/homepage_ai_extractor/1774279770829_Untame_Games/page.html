About

Untame is an Upstate NY based award-winning games studio, and the alter-ego of designer and software engineer Itay Keren. Since its founding in 2008, Untame has created dozens of games and interactive products for various clients, as well as its highly acclaimed original titles.

We approach every project with deep passion and excitement, as we enjoy the process of creating games just as much as we do playing them.
