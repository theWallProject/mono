The RxWare Operating System

The RxWare Operating System (RxWare OS) is the secure, flexible foundation behind our AI-enabled patient engagement and navigation tools. Designed for specialty pharma programs and clinical trials, RxWare OS integrates seamlessly with EHRs, CRMs, and clinical trial systems to accelerate onboarding, improve adherence, optimize workflows, and safeguard sensitive healthcare data.

Seamless EHR Integration: Ensure smooth prescription processing and patient management.

Cross-Platform Compatibility: RxWare connects with your existing tools—regardless of platform—to create a consistent and unified patient experience.

Vendor-Agnostic: Our software easily integrates with third-party systems without costly disruptions or vendor lock-in.
