Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

  • What is Bitcoin mining?

    Bitcoin mining is the process of generating cryptocurrency by performing complex calculations to validate transactions and maintain the network. In return for this work, miners receive rewards in BTC. The ECOS bitcoin mining platform enables remote Bitcoin mining without the need for users to independently configure hardware or software.

  • How do I start Bitcoin mining?

    To start mining with ECOS, simply register on the platform, launch the application, and choose a suitable solution. Users can rent mining capacity or purchase ASICs and host them in ECOS data centers. The entire process is managed through the ECOS bitcoin mining app.

  • Can I try Bitcoin mining for free?

    Yes, ECOS offers a demo mode that allows users to explore the platform's functionality. This is a convenient way to understand how crypto mining tools work and how the mining management process is structured.

  • How will I receive Bitcoin rewards?

    Mining rewards are automatically credited to your internal ECOS balance. You can track earnings in real time through your personal account on the mining platform and withdraw funds to an external wallet.

  • When can I withdraw my earnings?

    Withdrawals are available once the minimum payout threshold is reached. After submitting a withdrawal request, funds are sent to the user's wallet within 24 hours.

  • How can I monitor the mining process?

    ECOS mining software provides detailed analytics, including hashrate, profitability, and reward history. All data is available in the user's personal dashboard.

  • What payment methods do you accept?

    Mining contracts can be paid for using multiple payment methods, including bank cards and cryptocurrencies.

  • Are there limits to purchases?

    Minimum and maximum limits depend on the selected product. ECOS offers flexible conditions for individual users and scalable solutions for those operating at higher volumes on cryptocurrency mining sites. Each solution is tailored to specific user needs.

  • Can I cancel my purchase?

    Cancellation and refund conditions depend on the product type and activation stage. Full terms are published and available on the ECOS mining site, allowing users to evaluate all aspects before purchasing a mining contract. In addition, ECOS operates a marketplace where users can buy ASICs below market price, launch mining, and resell equipment directly within the application.
