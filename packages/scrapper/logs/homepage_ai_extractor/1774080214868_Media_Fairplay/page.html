WEB AND MOBILE DEVELOPMENT

Web development is a broad term for the work involved in developing a web site, it can range from developing the simplest static single page of plain text to the most complex web-based applications, electronic businesses, and social network services. Most recently Web development has come to mean the creation of content management systems or CMS. In broad terms the CMS acts as middleware between the database and the user through the browser. A principle benefit of a CMS is that it allows non-technical people to make changes to their Web site without having technical knowledge. ANALYTICS AND TESTING

Marketing analytics is the practice of measuring, handling and analyzing marketing performance in order to maximize its effectiveness and optimize return on investment (ROI). The importance of marketing analytics is obvious: if something costs more than it returns, it’s not a good business strategy. In a 2008 study, the Lenskold Group found that ‘companies making improvements in their measurement and ROI capabilities were more likely to report outgrowing competitors and a higher level of effectiveness and efficiency in their marketing.’ Our take on analytics and testing: Simply put: Knowledge is power. We feel that in order to provide you with the best results – it is necessary to regularly analyze and test our campaigns, so that we can improve and make changes as we go, without wasting any part of your budget.
