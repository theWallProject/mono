Get to Know Us

Unique Differentiators of Hydrolite’s AEM Technology

Unmatched Efficiency and Performance

AEM technology delivers high energy efficiency, requiring less than 45 kWh per kilogram of hydrogen and achieving up to 3x the current density of traditional electrolyzers.

Scalable Megawatt Solutions

Featuring a compact 250 kW stack, AEM technology is designed for scalable hydrogen production, targeting megawatt-level applications.

Sustainability at Its Core

Hydrolite’s AEM membranes and electrodes assembly are PFAS-free, recyclable, and built with non-precious and non-critical raw materials, ensuring minimal environmental impact.

Revolutionary Cost Efficiency

Hydrolite’s AEM membrane reduces costs by over 90%, making green hydrogen production more affordable than ever.

Broad Water Compatibility

Unlike PEM systems that require deionized water, Hydrolite’s AEM technology works efficiently with tap water and even brine, enabling wider adoption in diverse locations.

Decades of Expertise

With over 15 years of dedicated research and successful field testing, Hydrolite’s AEM technology represents a proven and reliable solution in the hydrogen market.

Introducing Our AEM Technology

Making Green Hydrogen Practical

Our solutions focus on transforming energy systems: fuel cells providing zero-emission backup power, hydrogen generators producing clean fuel from water and electricity, and technologies enabling industries to reduce their carbon footprint. 

We are dedicated to investing in innovative, scalable green energy sources for a more sustainable future.
