Smart Science. Simple Solutions. SUPERB™ Results.

  • Non-Invasive

    Experience a true non-invasive treatment with our needle-free next-generation ultrasound treatment. Our ultrasound technology precisely passes through the skin’s surface, heating the mid-dermal tissue at just the right depth to revitalize the skin.

  • Minimal Downtime

    Live life uninterrupted. Skip the downtime and get right back to your everyday activities immediately following treatment. With Sofwave™, there’s no aftercare - you’re free to workout, apply makeup, go to dinner, and get back to your life!

  • Safe For All Skin Types, All Year Round

    Sofwave is safe and effective for all skin types and colors, particularly for individuals with darker skin tones who are more vulnerable to burns.

  • Quick and Effective

    Sofwave treatment is quick easy to perform, and patients love that it fits into their active lifestyle at any time of the day.

  • Natural Results

    Sofwave relies on your body's regenerative reaction by stimulating collagen, elastin, and hyaluronic acid production for a natural appearance.

  • Body Contouring

    Pure Impact™ is a first-of-its-kind whole body bio-electrical muscle stimulation treatment that can be customized to your unique needs for a lifted, stronger, more toned body.
