JET TECHNOLOGY

Exclusive to TavTech, Jet pressure energy technology is a world-first breakthrough approach to skincare and to wound care.
TavTech’s novel Jet technology utilizes pressurized air, accelerating it to manipulate liquid into fine misty jet streams using JetPeel and JetOx handpieces. These wand-like instruments, available in a range of configurations, deliver jet streams to the skin travelling at a speed of over 200 m/sec. (660 ft./sec), providing versatile and precise means for performing a wide variety of treatments covering specific aesthetic and wound conditions.
Based on technologies adapted from the aviation industry, TavTech takes non-invasive yet effective skin treatments to new heights of excellence, providing outstanding clinical results and patient comfort.

World-class capabilities
Jet technology by TavTech has excelled over more than two decades and continues to lead the industry under the guidance of a dedicated team with industry-unique jet-pressure energy in-depth knowledge matched with aesthetics, dermatological and wound care expertise. Training is a key component of the unique Jet systems. Building on the accumulated experience of our staff, we provide thorough training in the use of Jet devices to our customers, to ensure they achieve the best results for their customers.
Our products are continuously tested and improved in real-world conditions, so we can offer the latest recommendations for techniques to use Jet pressure energy technology in the optimal way.
As a world-leading medical company, TavTech adheres to strict national and international standards and accreditations, including FDA and EU regulations.

OUR SOLUTIONS

TavTech offers a combination of cutting-edge jet pressure energy trans-epidermal systems and a rich variety of liquid nutrients and solutions for the treatment of an array of medical and aesthetic conditions.
