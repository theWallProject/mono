Our Organization

National Computer Saksharta Mission (NCSM) is a beacon of digital empowerment in India, passionately committed to fostering a future where everyone thrives in the digital age. We're not just an organization; we're a movement, igniting potential through cutting-edge vocational training and expert educational consulting. By forging powerful alliances with Central and State Governments, NCSM stands as a vital force, providing ingenious solutions to fuel India's burgeoning digital economy.

Beginning of the journey
Since 2009, our mission has been to promote computer education and skill development, seamlessly integrating it into the daily lives of all Indians. This endeavor seeks to unlock opportunities for personal growth, career advancement, and societal development. Over the years, our journey has resulted in the expansion of our team, with over 400 centers and team members spread across India, reinforcing our dedication to empower individuals through digital education.
Together, we’ll accomplish great things
United by a shared vision and a spirit of collaboration, together, we will accomplish great things. In the tapestry of our combined efforts, each thread contributes to the creation of something extraordinary. It's the synergy of diverse skills, perspectives, and determination that propels us forward. As a team, we embrace challenges, celebrate victories, and consistently strive for excellence. With a collective commitment to our goals, we are not just individuals working side by side; we are a force, a community poised to make a significant impact. Through collaboration, we pave the way for innovation, success, and a journey marked by shared achievements.

Supriyo Shekhar Kundu — Chairman, NCSM

Committed to Digital Literacy & Empowerment
“Digital literacy is not just a skill — it is the foundation of an inclusive and progressive nation.”

At NCSM, our mission is to make digital education accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or circumstance. We believe that knowledge has the power to transform lives, and we work each day to help individuals gain the skills they need to grow and succeed in a changing world.

With the collective efforts of our dedicated team, NCSM has been able to reach and support over 500,000 learners through various initiatives in education, training, and employment. Through Pragati Private ITIs and our vocational programs, we continue to nurture a future-ready workforce equipped for new opportunities.

Our journey is guided by a simple goal — to empower youth and women, bridge the digital divide, and contribute, in our own small way, to building a self-reliant and digitally empowered India.

Fostering Inclusive Growth Through Innovation

At NCSM, we believe that true progress is achieved when innovation is inclusive and benefits every section of society. From the very beginning, our approach has focused on addressing present challenges while preparing individuals and communities for the future.
