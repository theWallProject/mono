Rimipharm Medical Products Ltd.

Rimipharm Medical Products Ltd, a private leading company, specializes in distribution of diagnostic healthcare solutions that can lead to improve patient outcomes and provide economic benefits to healthcare system and companies. We promote Faster, easy to use and accurate Rapid tests and medical devices for the use of laboratories, doctors, pharmacies, hospitals and other health organizations. Rimipharm is ISO 9001 certified and meets the quality standards dictated by regulatory authorities around the globe and Israel.
