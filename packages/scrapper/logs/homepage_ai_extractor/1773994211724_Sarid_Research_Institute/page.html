PerMed Case Study
Sarid Research recruited 4000 individuals as ‘Specialized Research Communities’ (SRC) for an IoT-based PERsonalized MEDicine (PerMed) academic research. Subjects carried a smartwatch and reported daily health questionnaires, which was then cross-referenced and analyzed for predictive modelling using smartwatches.

The Joint Case Study
Sarid Research partnered with Joint-Ashalim to develop a pioneering Social Mobility survey within the fields of healthcare and digital access. Through targeted quantitative research methods, Sarid gathered and analyzed data from over 1,000 participants, yielding insights into socioeconomic factors influencing social-mobility in health literacy, digital literacy, and quality of life.
