About Us

Our success is in the creation of universal methodologies for advertising and estimation of efficiency based on ROI, CPA, etc. We are always staying on top and following the latest marketing trends. ActionAds offers a wide variety of innovative advertising solutions and helps our partners build their online marketing campaigns with maximum efficiency in two ways: SaaS and Managed Service.

We are here to help you more effectively and profitably manage your advertising campaigns and scale your businesses. By providing universal tracking and promotion software for your Facebook, Google and Bing marketing campaigns, we aim to lift all cares from your shoulders and make digital advertising simple for you.

Features

  • Big data segmentation algorithm
    Anticipate users and make predictions about their future behaviors using their behavioral, geographic and demographic data.
  • Multivariate A/B testing
    Find the best audiences for your ads by auto splitting targeting in granular segments and getting a side-by-side comparison.
  • Automatic ad optimization
    Automatically promote the best performing ads based on your rules.
  • Comprehensive daily and monthly reports
    Daily and hourly statistics allow you to see exactly how your campaign is performing and determine the strong sides of your ads.
  • Single dashboard
    Allows merging of stats from different campaigns and accounts into one project.
  • Unique metrics
    Create your own metrics for further addition to reports.
