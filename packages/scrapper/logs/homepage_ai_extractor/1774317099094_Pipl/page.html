Connected fraud intelligence for enterprise risk decisions

Built for the complexity of enterprise fraud, Pipl connects identity, device, and behavior signals into traceable intelligence for complex systems and fragmented environments. At its core is an industry-leading identity graph of more than 5 billion profiles, powering Pipl’s AI-native model to help enterprises detect, prevent, and investigate fraud with confidence. That’s why some of the the world’s largest payment networks, ecommerce marketplaces, and digital wallet platforms trust Pipl.

Confident fraud decisions need more than just signals

More signals don't automatically lead to better fraud decisions. Teams need evidence they can interpret, trust, and use across complex systems. Pipl turns fragmented signals into intelligence that teams can carry across systems, decisions, and reviews without losing meaning. 

Coherence

Fragmented identity, device, and behavioral signals resolve into intelligence that teams can interpret and use in fraud decisions.

Explainability

Intelligence retains its meaning across systems, teams, and contexts, so decisions don't break down under review or repeated use.

Compliant

Apply intelligence across complex systems at scale with confidence, supported by privacy protections and responsible data practices.

Apply adaptive scoring across real-time payment workflows
