About Digital Care Pathways
Crossing the Interoperability Chasm

Redefining how healthcare is
measured and delivered

At The Clinician, we enable the management of patient-generated health data outside traditional clinical settings and help replace manual, inefficient processes with digitally-enabled care pathways.

 

We developed our digital platform to support the delivery of more proactive, personalised and value-based healthcare. By enabling timely exchange of health outcomes, experiences and educational information, The Clinician provides care teams and patients with real-time, actionable information to improve health outcomes while reducing costs and inefficiencies.

Our Solutions

 

Digital Care Pathways

We help healthcare organisations digitally transform their care delivery by replacing manual, costly processes with automated and streamlined digital care pathways. Digital care pathways monitor, support and engage patients along their entire care journey, improving convenience and health outcomes while reducing waste and administrative burden.

Learn more
Pathway library

Patient Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs)

Moving beyond inefficient paper-based forms and siloed electronic systems, our platform automates the collection and analysis of medically validated patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs). PROMs give you a 360° view of your patients' health so that you can focus on delivering the outcomes that matter most.

Learn more

Patient Reported Experience Measures (PREMs)

Supporting the complete management of patient-reported experience measures (PREMs), our platform helps you capture and analyse data on key domains such as staff communication, care coordination, and empathy. Understand the strengths and weaknesses in your care delivery so that you can improve the healthcare experience and create happier, healthier patients.

Learn more

Remote Monitoring

Launch one complete digital solution for monitoring and managing patients outside the hospital walls with our remote monitoring solution. Built for patient engagement, we can help make your patients feel supported and cared for from the comfort of their own homes.

Learn more

We partner with leading organisations

ICHOM certified partner

We are proud to be the official technology and implementation partner for ICHOM (International Consortium for Health Outcomes Measurement) in Asia Pacific and the Middle East.

Supporting ICHOM’s standard sets, we help health organisations to capture and analyse outcomes data at scale so they can unlock the potential of value-based care.

 
 
 

Amazon technology partner
