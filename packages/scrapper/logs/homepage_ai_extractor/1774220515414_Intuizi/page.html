Our Platform

Secure Data, Powerful Insights:

Our signal processing platform and the Anonymized Knowledge Graph (AKG) bridge data consent owners and providers, enabling compliant, addressable insights into consumer behavior across the digital and the physical. The AKG offers a versatile way to query vast amounts of de-identified data with easy integration and diverse use cases.

Unlock Insights, Build Smarter Solutions:

Signal Providers de-identify consumer-derived data using advanced encryption, salting, and hashing—ensuring consumers remain completely de-identified. In return, they gain access to valuable insights that drive ad sales, enhance consumer understanding, and licensing revenue.
