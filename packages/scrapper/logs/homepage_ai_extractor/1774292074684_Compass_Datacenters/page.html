Data Centers
Done Differently

We deliver hyperscale campuses built to last. Our industrialized model brings predictability, consistency and reliable performance to every market – supported by long-standing partners and a commitment to the communities we’re part of.

A Manufacturing Mindset For
Hyperscale Growth

Standardized, efficient construction that scales with demand.

Rather than treating each build as a one-off project, Compass uses a consistent design across all campuses. This standardization ensures control over the variables that matter most: cost, quality, schedule and long-term operability – while enabling fungibility across regions. Components, crews and capacity can be shifted as needed to keep hyperscaler roadmaps on track.

With ˜85% of each data center manufactured off-site using a standard kit-of-parts, Compass removes the risks of stick-built construction. The result: faster, more predictable delivery with fewer surprises and the ability to redirect resources between markets when demand spikes.

Factory environments mean fewer errors, less rework and delays on-site.  Every component arrives tested, refined and ready – driving consistent quality and more cost-efficient outcomes at scale.

By shifting complex, high-risk tasks into controlled factory settings, Compass minimizes heavy lifting and elevated work on-site – making our campuses safer from day one. This approach also creates predictable schedules and steady work for local crews, contributing to a more stable and inclusive workforce.

A Supply Chain Built for
Speed, Certainty and Scale

We treat suppliers as true partners, not vendors. From design through operations, this model drives continuous innovation, dedicated capacity and long term reliability across every campus.

Dedicated partner capacity and factory lines give Compass an unmatched ability to deliver standardized components — like power centers, cooling systems, and MEP skids — faster and with schedule certainty.

Because every component is standardized and every partner is integrated in real time, Compass can dynamically allocate resources across markets and adapt to customer needs — even in volatile conditions.

Supporting the Communities
We Build In

Learn about our work with Communities Learn about our work with Communities

Our data centers are designed to prioritize the protection of natural resources and reduce environmental impacts, both during build-out and operations.

By shifting the majority of construction off-site, we significantly reduce traffic, noise and on-site disruptions – designing campuses that respect the communities around them.

We collaborate with community partners to support long-term economic growth, education pathways and organizations that strengthen local families and the future workforce, ensuring our presence creates lasting benefits for the people who live and work nearby.
