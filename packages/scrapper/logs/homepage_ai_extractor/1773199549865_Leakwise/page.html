FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What industries benefit from LEAKWISE’s oil spill detection systems?

LEAKWISE systems are widely used in oil and petrochemical industries, power generation and distribution, transportation, manufacturing, water supply, and environmental monitoring. Our technology provides essential protection for any operation that handles hydrocarbons.

How can LEAKWISE help reduce operational costs?

LEAKWISE systems detect oil spills early, minimizing the size and impact of spills, which directly reduces cleanup and recovery costs. For example, a spill of 100 m³ in a remote area near the shoreline can cost up to $20M in damages. By identifying spills at the earliest stage, LEAKWISE helps prevent large-scale environmental incidents, cutting down on expensive cleanup efforts, regulatory fines, and operational downtime.

What kind of support does LEAKWISE offer after installation?

We offer comprehensive post-installation support, including training, technical assistance, and ongoing maintenance services to ensure your system operates optimally. Our global network of distributors provides local support wherever needed.

Are LEAKWISE sensors suitable for hazardous environments?

Yes, our sensors are certified for use in hazardous areas, including ATEX, IECEx, and ETL. We offer intrinsically safe and explosion-proof options to meet the needs of challenging environments.

What maintenance is required for LEAKWISE sensors?

LEAKWISE sensors are designed for long-term use with minimal maintenance. They are highly durable, ensuring reliable performance with minimal downtime.

How accurate are LEAKWISE systems in detecting oil spills?

LEAKWISE systems detect oil sheens as thin as 0.3 microns— a precision unmatched by any other system. This ensures the earliest possible spill detection, helping prevent environmental damage and ensuring compliance with safety regulations.

What sets LEAKWISE technology apart from competitors?

LEAKWISE’s Electromagnetic Energy Absorption Technology offers exceptional sensitivity, detecting even the thinnest layers of hydrocarbons on water. Our proven reliability and precision make us the top choice for early spill detection.

How customizable are LEAKWISE solutions for different industries?

LEAKWISE solutions are highly customizable, with flexible configurations that can be adapted to meet the specific needs of different industries. From oil refineries to power plants, we offer tailored solutions that fit your operational requirements.

Can LEAKWISE systems integrate with existing monitoring infrastructure?

Absolutely. LEAKWISE controllers and sensors are designed to integrate seamlessly with your existing systems. Our solutions support various communication interfaces for local or remote monitoring and reporting.

How can I purchase or get more information about LEAKWISE products?

You can contact our global network of distributors or reach out to us directly for more information. We’re here to help you find the right solution for your needs.
