Why should I outsource my R&D team instead of hiring in-house?

Outsourcing your R&D gives you access to a wider talent pool and allows you to scale your team quickly without the overhead of recruitment and training. With YozmaTech, you get dedicated experts who integrate seamlessly with your existing team, ensuring fast results and reduced costs.

How does the process of building a dedicated offshore team work?

At YozmaTech, we work closely with you to understand your needs, select the right specialists, and create a team that aligns with your business goals. From recruitment to onboarding, we handle every step so you can focus on innovation and growth.

What makes YozmaTech different from other outstaffing providers?

We offer a personalized approach that emphasizes quality, transparency, and speed. With access to our global network of pre-vetted talent and an efficient recruitment process, we help you build high-performing teams faster and at a lower cost.

How do I ensure the quality of the developers you provide?

We source only the top 1% of talent from around the world, rigorously vetting each candidate for skills and cultural fit. You’re involved in the selection process to ensure we match the best developers to your project.

How quickly can I hire an offshore team through YozmaTech?

Our average time to hire is just 3 weeks. With our pre-vetted talent pool and efficient recruitment process, you can quickly scale your team and start delivering results faster.

How do I manage a remote offshore team effectively?

We ensure smooth communication and seamless integration with your in-house team by setting clear expectations, using the right tools, and offering continuous support. Your dedicated Success Manager will ensure everything stays on track.
