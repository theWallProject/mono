BREAKING: WORLD’S FIRST MEMS SPEAKER-MICROPHONE SOLUTION

SonicEdge LTD launched SonicTwin in Las Vegas at CES 2025. SonicTwin is the world’s smallest integrated speaker-microphone in a single package for True Wireless Stereo (TWS) earbuds and headphones. Measuring just 8 x 4 x 1 mm, SonicTwin combines cuttingedge sound generation and sensing capabilities, setting a new benchmark in compact audio innovation.
