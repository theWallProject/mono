Florasonic

This vegetation serves as a nesting place for Quintetrium stars and is a food source for Glandolls and Ducteels. But the main advantage of these plants reveals itself at night, when their colonies transform themselves into the best party of the deep sea, and that is because at night they emit a sound frequency that makes all the creatures in the vicinity drop everything they’re doing and start to dance.

Mushmush

Any place you happen to run into these fossilized mushrooms, know that there used to be a lively colony here which has disappeared due to the consumption of mushmush, a highly poisonous fungi. If you’ve stumbled upon one of these, you must never, under any circumstances, take them out of the water, because exposure to oxygen reanimates the fossil and the mushroom begins to spread immediately at an accelerated pace, poisoning its environment. You’ve been warned.

Solo

Solo is the cutest creature in the underwater world and has a terrific voice, but it’s unlikely that anyone’s aware of it and that’s because Solo likes to stay in his shell and be alone, think all kinds of thoughts, make up stories for himself, and mainly take power naps. If you wake Solo he won’t get mad, he’ll just turn to the other side and keep being by himself. The only creature he’s ever happy to see is the quiet Glandoll who always takes on his colors and therefore he feels comfortable hanging out together with a Glandoll in silence.
From the Hidden Archives of Professor Krömpblaut

Glandolls

Packs of glandolls can only be spotted in the deep waters of the northern hemisphere. The first person to have observed them was Captain Marmelados, who in his book described them as: “colorful and mysterious sea bells”. It took an entire fifty years after that to discover that the glandoll is a kind of dancing clam that changes colors according to its environment, not unlike chameleons on land. They like to nimble on florasonic, communicate through telepathy, and have a great sense of humor.
From the Hidden Archives of Professor Krömpblaut

Hypnoticat

The uncontested king of the deep. The Hypnoticat (or Noti as he’s sometimes affectionately called) has the power to hypnotize, and he can convince all the other creatures to do as he pleases. For instance, he’s made the quintetrium stars broadcast contradictory messages to their mother planet, thus for years now preventing the invasion of Earth. In fact, the Hypnoticat is the guardian of our world. But sometimes, when he gets bored, he hypnotizes Solo who in turn forgets his loner habits and bursts into an awesome solo song to the great pleasure of all the ocean dwellers.
From the Hidden Archives of Professor Krömpblaut

Ducteel

Ducteels are peaceful and happy creatures. They charge themselves through their own motion, and their most unique characteristic is that they can glue things thanks to a substance they secrete from their bodies. The problem is that they are not blessed with a whole lot of brain power, and sometimes they end up gluing themselves to rocks, remaining immobilized for several hours...

Quintetrium

The Quintetrium stars came from the Zyrcosium planet in the Milky Way. They live on the sea floor in groups of 3-84 and have high photographic abilities. They have been observing the ocean for hundreds of years. Their job is to collect data and pass it on to the command station on the planet Mercury, in the aim of one day taking over the Earth.

Jumping Seasun

Living in burrows under the sea floor, jumping seasuns spends the whole year in hibernation. Once a month a seasun will jump out and light up an area up to a quarter of a mile in diameter. With every passing hour its circle of light grows smaller, until it goes completely dark after 24 hours. During this time the photosynthesis process takes place, and the sonic plants grow ears.
