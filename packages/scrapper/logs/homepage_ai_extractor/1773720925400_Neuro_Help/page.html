The Challenge: Neurological Disorders

Epilepsy

Epilepsy affects over 65 million people globally, and for nearly one-third of them, medications fail to control their seizures. These patients live with constant uncertainty — never knowing when the next seizure will strike.

The unpredictability of epilepsy has a profound impact on daily life:

  • Persistent anxiety and social withdrawal
  • Limitations on driving, swimming, working or parenting
  • Increased risk of severe injury from falls or accidents
  • SUDEP (Sudden Unexpected Death in Epilepsy), a fatal risk with no warning signs

While existing devices — such as seizure detection watches or video monitors — help some patients, they fall short in detecting subtle or non-motor seizures, and their high false alarm rates often erode trust and usefulness.

EpiNess™ – Our Integrated Solution

EpiNess™ is more than a seizure predictor — it's a full-spectrum, AI-powered platform for brain health monitoring at home.

Combining wearable EEG, intelligent sensors, and proprietary machine learning, EpiNess™ empowers proactive care for epilepsy, cognitive decline, and sleep disorders through one unified solution.

Wearable EEG Headband

Soft, discreet, and designed for comfort — even during sleep. With 8 dry-contact EEG electrodes, it captures high-fidelity brain signals continuously, day and night.

Multimodal Sensing

Tracks heart rate, motion, and temperature alongside EEG to provide a holistic view of neurological function — ideal for seizure context, sleep scoring, and cognitive rhythms.

AI-Powered Intelligence

Our proprietary algorithms don't just detect — they predict. EpiNess™ achieves high sensitivity with an industry-leading low false alarm rate by learning each user's unique brain signature.

Real-Time Alerts & Insights

When abnormal activity is detected — or a seizure is predicted — the system notifies users and caregivers immediately. Data is securely synced to a mobile app and clinician dashboard.

Beyond Seizures

Supports most of the known neurological disorders, including early detection of cognitive decline through analysis of EEG slowing and brain activity shifts, accurate sleep staging for long-term brain health tracking and of course epileptic seizure detection and prediction.

Secure Cloud Platform

All data is encrypted and accessible to users and clinicians for ongoing care and research.
