MRC- Laboratory Equipment

For over 30 years, MRC has been developing, manufacturing, and supplying laboratory equipment to over 70 countries worldwide. Since 2001 MRC quality management system is officially certified in accordance with ISO 9001 fulfilling internationally accepted quality requirements. Our expertise in the supply of laboratory products is backed by our qualified and experienced service team.


A Wide Range Of Laboratory Equipment

MRC laboratory technology offers a wide range of innovative equipment for numerous applications in research and development. Industry leaders trust in MRC proven technology for our specially developed heating apparatus and temperature control products such as hotplates, circulators, baths and chillers used in mixing, shaking, growing, testing, and crushing applications. MRC has also gained an industry-leading position in the world market thanks to our innovative Shaking Incubators, Ovens, Humidity chambers, Chillers, shakers, homogenizers, mills,Refrigerators, rotary evaporators, centrifuges, laboratory balances.

Our warehouses are extensively stocked for immediate delivery. We offer a wide variety of over 3,000 quality products, unmatched customer service, and provide a unique level of after-sales support and maintenance advice.
