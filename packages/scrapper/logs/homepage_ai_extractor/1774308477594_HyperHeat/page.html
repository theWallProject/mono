Industrial Heat Unlimited
Hotter. Cheaper. Built to Last.

  • Electric gas heater

    HyperHeat’s electric gas heaters replace fossil-fuel burners, delivering clean, efficient heat with outlet temperatures of up to 2000°C – ideal for industries requiring high thermal performance and retrofit solutions.

  • Radiative heater

    HyperHeat’s radiative heating solutions are engineered for exceptional durability in challenging, high-temperature environments. They deliver high heat flux, enhancing process efficiency, reliability, and thermal performance.

  • Running on medium voltage

    HyperHeat’s systems connect directly to standard industrial medium-voltage grids, scaling from hundreds of kilowatts to multi-megawatt capacities, ensuring effortless integration and maximum efficiency.
