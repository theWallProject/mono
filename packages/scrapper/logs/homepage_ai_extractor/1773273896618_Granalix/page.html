Innovative Groundbreaking Technology

GranaGard Nano-Omega 5 is a food supplement based on patent-protected Nanotechnology for optimal absorption.

The picture illustrates the conversion of GranaGard Nano-Omega 5 into nano-drops in water, versus plain Pomegranate Seed Oil which floats on the water. This advantage allows the oil found in GranaGard to achieve optimal absorption and high bioavailability.

Technology and Research

Granalix aims to provide its customers with products based on science and natural ingredients. The company’s labs are run by Prof. Ruth Gabizon from Hadassah Medical Center and Prof. Shlomo Magdassi from the Hebrew University.  The company’s laboratories conduct research on various models to assess the safety and effects of the company’s products. These studies have been published in scientific peer-reviewed journals.

Relevant studies on the company’s products are also implemented in clinical trials with humans, in close collaboration with the neurology departments at Hadassah Hospital and Ichilov Hospital, as well as the neuropsychology department at Rambam Hospital and a research institute in Mexico.
