Left Ventricle Under Pressure

In CHF, the left ventricle stiffens or weakens — causing dangerous pressure buildup inside the chamber. As the LV strains to maintain output, its walls progressively dilate and scar. Without mechanical intervention, ejection fraction falls and prognosis worsens year over year.
