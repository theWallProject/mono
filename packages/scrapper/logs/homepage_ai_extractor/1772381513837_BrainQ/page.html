Reversing
stroke
disability
requires
the
brain's
neural
networks
to
relearn
lost
functions.

BRAIN.Q relies on emerging science suggesting the use of targeted stimulation of network activity as a key for maximizing the brain's learning capabilities.

Our breakthrough investigational therapy has been designed using AI to distill neural activity patterns by analyzing datasets of network activity.

These predefine patterns are then applied to patients through tailored, low-intensity, low frequency electromagnetic stimulation that aims to enhance and accelerate the brain's recovery process after stroke.
