NEPAL - YOUR PREFERRED OFFSHORE DESTINATION

Kavadev is all about helping you provide top quality software development services while reducing Total Cost of Ownership.

We know that your business requires the ﬂexibility to scale your team and we have carefully recruited a network of software experts based in Nepal to enable you to do just that. Our services include customized, enterprise grade products & services for startups and mid and large businesses. Whether you are looking to outsource a single role, a team in your business or an entire business function, we can provide a tailored solution through our professional teams based in the Kathmandu Valley.

WHY OUTSOURCE TO NEPAL?

There are numerous alternatives on the market for scaling your in-house team via offshoring, so choosing the right partner is paramount.

With 20+ years of experience in utilizing international offshore resources, we have found Nepal to an attractive destination for offshore software development. We’ve developed a deep understanding of the culture of Nepali employees and a strong professional  local network of software developers. This network is committed to the highest coding guidelines and standards and timely project delivery.
