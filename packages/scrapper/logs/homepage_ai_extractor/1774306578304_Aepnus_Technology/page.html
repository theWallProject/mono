Demonstration-Scale Plant

Our planned demonstration-scale plant will process over 500 tonnes of sodium sulfate annually, showcasing Aepnus' complete sulfate recycling process on a commercial scale. This project eliminates technology risk, delivering a fully validated, ready-to-deploy solution. We’re inviting forward-thinking partners to join us on this journey—offering them a first-mover advantage in cost-effective, on-site chemical regeneration and the chance to lead their industry in sustainable waste valorization.

Testbed in Oakland, California

Our Commercial Cell pilot project, based at our Oakland headquarters, is the proving ground for Aepnus' electrolysis technology. Here, we are fine-tuning every aspect of the system—maximizing performance, enhancing durability, and ensuring it’s ready for commercial deployment. This facility also serves as a hands-on testbed for customers, allowing them to send in samples for testing and validation. At Aepnus, we’re turning cutting-edge innovation into commercial reality.

Pilot in Bécancour, Quebec

Our pilot project in Bécancour, Quebec, marks a significant milestone in the development of Aepnus' electrochemical technology platform. Supported by industry leaders Ultium CAM (GM x Posco), Vale, and Nemaska Lithium, the system is operated with sodium sulfate sourced directly from these partners' plants. This pilot project is a critical step toward scaling up to our first commercial plant. By validating our technology with real-world inputs, we’re paving the way for the next phase of deployment, bringing cost-effective, sustainable, and scalable solutions to the chemical industry.
