About us

Genesis REIT Socimi, a Spanish-Israeli real estate company, was founded in 2016 and has been listed on the European market (EURONEXT) since 2024

We engage in the purchase, improvement, and rental of real estate assets mainly located within the M30 road, Madrid.

Assets

Genesis REIT Socimi operates in highly demanded areas of Madrid. Our investment strategy focuses on the improvement of our projects by renovating the apartments and public areas, adding elevators, and increasing accessibility, increasing construction permits, dividing the building from one unit into several apartments in the Spanish taboo.

Genesis enjoys higher than average rents and has a very high occupancy due to the location, modern design, and the management services of our projects.

Our projects are mainly rented to students, therefore most of the assets are located close to leading universities.

Areas of investment

Commercial real estate
This refers to properties that are used for business purposes, such as offices, retail stores, hotels, and warehouses. Investing in commercial real estate can provide stable rental income and long-term capital appreciation.
Residential real estate
This includes properties such as single-family homes, apartments, and condominiums that are used for residential purposes. Residential real estate investment can provide rental income, potential for capital appreciation, and tax benefits.
Industrial buildings
These are large-scale projects that involve the construction or maintenance of physical structures such as roads, bridges, airports, and public transportation systems. Investing in infrastructure projects can offer long-term stable returns and is often backed by governments.
