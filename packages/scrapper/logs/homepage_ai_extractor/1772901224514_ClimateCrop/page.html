It’s in the leaves.

Even as we push our planet to its limits, global demand continues to increase for essential crops. We need more from plants than ever before – and we need it right away. ClimateCrop upgrades plants by tapping into the power hidden in their leaves.

We increase the daily starch storage in plant leaves using a simple, non-GMO, precisely targeted gene edit. This improves photosynthetic efficiency in an indirect but powerful way, enabling plants to perform better.

The result: better yields, improved tolerance to drought and heat waves, reduced strain on existing resources, and more.

ClimateCrop-upgraded
plants do more with less.

Greater Climate Resilience

Increased starch storage also means more energy is available for respiration to keep plants healthy in stress conditions. Plants are stronger against heat waves and drought stresses – with faster recovery from the stress.

Reduced Strain on Resources

Efficient plants produce more without draining additional resources. Increase yields with the same resources inputs – water, land, nutrients, and energy.

Larger Yield, Same Quality

By storing more energy as transitory starch during photosynthesis, plants can use that extra energy to support better performance. This improves yield and plant health while retaining nutrient quality.

Farm-Ready

Our technology does not require any further investment in new agricultural practices, mechanization, machinery, or tools. Farmers can continue to plant, grow, and harvest as they see best. 

Broad Application:
Upgrades for Food, Feed, Fiber, & Fuel

Our proprietary gene exists in all vascular plants. The energy metabolism regulation (transitory starch biosynthesis and its transformation to sugar) is generic, independent of plant species or use.

We have the potential to upgrade 391,000 different species of vascular plants, providing broad application and opportunity across plant-based industries and agriculture – including food, animal feed, fiber, fuel and more.
