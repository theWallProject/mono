Let’s Talk

DOTS, founded by a team of research scientists, is dedicated to developing continuous, real-time nitrate monitoring to combat fertilizer overdose, prevent waste, and protect the environment. Based on years of research, trials, and four registered patents, we developed a unique water siphoning mechanism, combined with electro-optical sensors and analysis algorithms, to enable affordable real-time nitrate monitoring.

Farmers can now get continuous and precise information to support decisions for irrigation and fertilization, saving up to 40% of applied fertilizers every season.
