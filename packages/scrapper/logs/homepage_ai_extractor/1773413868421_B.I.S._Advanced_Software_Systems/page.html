Overview

CloudIUS is a secure cloud file sync and share solution used by enterprises on their private cloud and offered as a white-label VAS by telecom and cloud service providers, enabling their customers to become connected, synchronized and productive anytime, anywhere.

CloudIUS enables users to enjoy a complete “take your PC with you” experience with automatic backup, advanced scheduling, selective restore and file versioning features. Media streaming direct from the cloud coupled with advanced sync and sharing enhance the user's experience and enable easy and productive collaboration.

Offering secure online and offline access to content using any mobile or stationary device, CloudIUS is a productivity enhancing cloud business enabler.

Business Benefits

  • Innovative and secure cloud services for your customers
  • A powerful, white-label platform for managing and sharing content in the cloud
  • Improved customer stickiness and innovation levels
  • A quick return on investment with minimal upfront investment and expenses
  • Added value that differentiates you from your competitors

User Benefits

  • Online access, backup and sync of all files and data in the cloud, coupled with advanced sharing and streaming capabilities
  • Access to all of their files and folders from anywhere using mobile apps for iPhone, iPad or Android, or any web browser
  • Complete “take the PC with you” capabilities and secure backup features for all of their content, across all their devices
  • The useful and enjoyable experience of being able to access and share documents anytime and anywhere
  • The Value Added Service (VAS) of innovation, through access to and sharing of their content anytime and anywhere

Mobility

  • Cloudius enables secure mobile access to your content anytime, anywhere
  • A true “take the PC with you” experience allows you to work from anywhere without compromising security
  • Native iOS and Android apps enable a smooth, intuitive user experience on any device
  • Experience the benefits of a roaming office with access to any file, wherever you are
  • Stay synced with your team’s latest files and documents while on the road

Productivity

  • Collaborate easily and securely with anyone, including those who don’t have an account
  • File versioning capabilities allow for instant restore of any previous document version, anytime
  • Selective sync features and bandwidth throttling enable you to control use during peak hours or in limited bandwidth environments
  • Automatic schedule and manual sync options allow you to define sync intervals at preferred time slots
  • Mobile apps for iPad, iPhone and Android allow you to be productive anytime and anywhere
  • Offload large file email attachments by sharing files or links via the secure cloud

Security

  • Full support of HTTPs for connectivity between the client and cloud, and between components of the cloud
  • Enhanced WebDAV protocol connectivity for on-premises private, hybrid, and public clouds
  • Enterprise grade security features include AES 256 bit encryption to ensure data security
  • Maintain control over access permissions for groups and individuals
  • Stay informed with activity reports, backup status reports and online notifications

Collaboration

  • Invite collaborators, team members and partners to shared online workspaces while maintaining tight access controls
  • Collaborate without limit: create, sync and share files with no size limitation
  • Offload heavy e-mail file attachments and share securely via the cloud
  • Share files securely with anyone including users who don’t have an account, without requiring signup
  • Control the content you share with expiration dates, versioning and notification features
  • Use social connectors to directly share your files and folders on Facebook, Twitter, Youtube and Picasa

Platform Administration

  • Enjoy a fully featured set of tools for user administration and group management
  • Employ automatic configurations to get people on board quickly and easily
  • Set user storage quotas and backup plan configurations
  • Use bandwidth throttling to control the available bandwidth for backups
