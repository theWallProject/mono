Fresh produce

Fresh produce

Fresh produce, including fruits, vegetables, and herbs, continues to respire even after harvest. By reducing their respiration rate, maintaining optimal humidity levels, and implementing appropriate post-harvest treatments, we help keep them fresh for a longer period. This ensures the maintenance of their quality, including taste, aroma, texture, appearance, and prevents weight loss.

Bread and pastry

Bread and pastry

Our bags, designed for bread and pastry packaging, maintain optimal air circulation, prevent excessive drying and inhibiting the growth of molds. These attributes ensure safe transportation, and contribute to extend the shelf life of baked goods.

Lidding films

Lidding films

From convenient stand-up pouches to reliable top films, vacuum bags, and versatile lidding options, we provide packaging solutions that meet your specific needs. Our products ensure freshness, maximize shelf life, and offer convenience for both manufacturers and consumers.

Medical packaging

Medical packaging

Pharmaceutical manufacturers, biotech firms, and medical equipment companies require specialized solutions to ensure the sterility and safety of their packaged products. Our films and bags designed for such applications meet quality and safety standards, providing reliable protection.

Special Packaging Solutions

Special Packaging Solutions

Polyethylene and polypropylene films are ideal for protecting the integrity of packed products. They provide mechanical barrier, preventing contamination, moisture, and external elements from compromising the quality of the packaged goods, ensuring end customer satisfaction.

R.O.P specializes in the production and distribution of high-quality cast film and blown flexible packaging, made of polyethylene and polypropylene. The products are designed for a variety of applications and industries, such as fresh produce, food, medical, agriculture and more.
