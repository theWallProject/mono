Mehadrin is A LEADING GLOBAL GROWER

Mehadrin is a leading Global Grower of Citrus and Avocado fully vertically integrated.

Mehadrin is Israel’s largest grower and exporter of citrus, avocado, dates, and other fruits and vegetables. The company has annual sales of approximately $350 million, with over 70% of the produce exported globally to all continents. Our fresh produce comes in daily from more than 4000 hectares of agricultural holdings across Israel and other countries. We are fully vertically integrated, controlling the entire supply chain of our produce.

Growers at heart

Agriculture is the core of our business as well as our passion.
Being close to the earth and nature taught us that we always have a lot to learn and improve. We strive for professional, innovative agriculture, which is carried out in harmony with nature and produces natural, nutritious, and healthy food.
It also taught us to carry on with dignity and respect to people, both our cherished employees, some of whom have been with us for tens of years, as well as our valued customers. We create trusting relationships with our customers and partners based on uncompromising integrity.

OUR EUROPEAN MARKET

We are not just growers; we pack, export, and distribute through an extensive network of branch offices in Europe.
In order to provide the best service to our customers, Mehadrin has moved as close to its customers as possible.
To this end, we have set up an extensive network of branch offices in Europe. These offices, operated by local employees, handle the import, storing, marketing, and distributing of Mehadrin’s produce in Europe.
