Galil Chemicals Ltd (Galil Detergents Ltd) founded in 2000, as a part of the TROPICAL-GALIL group, operates according to the systems required by ISO 9001: 2008. Galil Chemicals Ltd holds expertise in manufacturing custom formulations, and in the supply of a wide range of cleaning detergent products for private labeling.

Products include: laundry washing powders, laundry liquid detergents, Laundry softeners, dishwashing liquid detergents, floor cleaners, glass cleaners, toilet-bowl cleaners, dishwasher powders, and more…

The products can be packed in various kinds of packages, such as cardboard boxes, plastic boxes, poly bags, plastic containers - all in different volumes according to customer requirements.

Products conform to the strictest standards with advanced technologies and internal and external quality control. All these for the purpose of supplying products of the highest quality and technical service to the complete satisfaction of our customers.

Galil Chemicals is located in Kiryat Shmona in northern Israel.
