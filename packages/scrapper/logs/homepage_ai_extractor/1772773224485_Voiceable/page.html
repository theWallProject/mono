Who in my company can use Voiceable?

Your existing sales team can use Voiceable easily to engage with customers – your existing agents won’t need any further training. Today Voiceable is used in call centers, offices, and in brick & mortar stores. 

What happens in peak hours when agents are busy?

Voiceable has built in-routing so whoever is free can take the incoming call. If nobody answers, the customer is prompted to leave their details so an agent can get back to them.

What if we already use a chat service on our site?

Voiceable integrates with chat. Some of our retailers choose to keep their existing chat for post-order support and use Voiceable for proactive presale. It’s up to you.
