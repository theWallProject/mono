Formula Group

Formula Systems (1985) Ltd. ("Formula"), a member of Asseco Group, we are a global information technology company which together with our subsidiaries provide software consulting services and computer-based business solutions. We design and implement IT solutions and software systems which improve the productivity of our customers’ existing IT assets, enable them to effectively manage their operations and reduce their business risks in the face of changing business environments. 

 

We are present everywhere where technology and business impact every day's life. We use our competencies and experience to create software which has a real impact on the present and the future. Our software solutions and services are used in Israel and around the world by banks and Insurance companies, hospitals, energy and telecommunication companies, the public, defense, high-tech, and healthcare sectors.

 

We have been constantly improving our competencies and investing in research and development for the success of our customers and this approach has enabled our subsidiaries to become leaders in their field of expertise .
