Check out our pricing FAQs

What do I receive in the free trial?

We provide a 14-day free trial to experience our platform. This includes unlimited access to the platform. Note that the trial does not include Calls/SMS Terminations, Custom Domain SSL Certifications or any customization.

What does the pricing include?

Pricing includes capacity usage based on concurrent calls/faxes and messages. Note that pricing does not include Calls/SMS Terminations.

Do you provide professional services?

Yes, we do provide professional services (i.e., consulting) as an additional service. Our experience with various CRM and software platforms can be beneficial to you.

What is the process of on-prem installation?

First, we break down the various requirements. Next, we analyze the entire project that will be provided as part of voipappz's SOW. We try to incorporate all features needed in a Communications Platform-as-a-service (cPaaS).

Do you provide Call Termination?

Our main focus and core business is software development and implementation. Nevertheless, we can provide call termination via certificated providers on cloud or let you use your own providers using a dedicated IP address on-prem.

How is scaling performed on cloud?

Resources are made available as and when you use them; therefore, there are no delays when expanding capacity or any waste of unused capacity. Resources are updated automatically via the self-care portal or by contacting the Support team.

Can I integrate your platform with our legacy product?

Yes, our agile platform can be easily integrated with legacy call centers, technologies, and protocols.
