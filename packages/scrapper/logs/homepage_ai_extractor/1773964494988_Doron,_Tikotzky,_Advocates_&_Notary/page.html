Doron, Tikotzky & Co.

Since its establishment in 1997, Doron, Tikotzky & Co. has been providing quality legal consultancy intertwined with uncompromising professional and personal attention. We quickly became one of Israel’s largest and leading law firms, known for excellence and innovation, with an impeccable service orientation and dedicated teamwork.
Our firm is consistently ranked in a prominent position by the legal ranking guides in the areas of companies law, taxation, litigation and dispute resolution, real estate and urban renewal, insolvency and financial rehabilitation, mergers and acquisitions, intellectual property, employment and labor law, immigration and relocation, white collar crime, banking, torts, family wealth management, trusts and estates, third sector, and numerous additional areas of legal expertise.

Our clients enjoy access to a select team of lawyers, some of whom hold a foreign license to practice law in addition to being Members of the Israeli Bar Association, accountants, economists, former Ministry of Justice staff, and government and tax authorities. Thanks to the firm’s staff, who integrates diverse knowledge areas, we succeed in providing our clients with an interdisciplinary perspective for each and every case we manage.
Challenging and fascinating cases, and excellent outcomes for our clients, clearly indicate our expertise combined with professional dependable service, confidentiality, and high-level availability of attorneys and partners guiding the legal issue to its optimal resolution. As our client, you benefit from a comprehensive legal package for your business activities under one roof based on in-depth understanding of your legal needs combined with innovative, practical solutions.
A sizable part of our legal practice involves international activities. Our firm’s offices are situated in central Israel and Haifa, France, Austria, Romania and Cyprus, as well as strong collaboration with law firms in the USA.
