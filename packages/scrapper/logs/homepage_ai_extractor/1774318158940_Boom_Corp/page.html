team

Boom Corp is a Tampere-based mobile games company founded in 2020.

We are building a company to bring awesome game visions to life. We are looking for people who can bring their own uniqueness and skills to bring impact to the projects we are working on.

The Boom corp vision

We value communities, social gameplay and self expression.

Our most cherished memories within games are the ones that go beyond the virtual realm to create valuable connections between people.

Our goal is to bring these experiences to the most accessible gaming platform – mobile.

To do this we are striving for a workplace that nurtures creativity and gives strong independence to teams to create the best possible games.
