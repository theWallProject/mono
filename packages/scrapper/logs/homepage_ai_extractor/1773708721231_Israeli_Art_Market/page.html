Special Announcement:
The Israeli Art Market Gallery is now for sale.

“I founded the gallery in 2013 with all of my love, and have faced ongoing threats from the Israeli Mossad because I privately own the gallery and serve as head curator, showing art from around the world, including Arab countries.
In May 2025, the threats became so serious that my life and my daughter’s life were at risk.
I have decided to sell the gallery and all related domains, including those with the name Israel.
To all the artists who have exhibited with us, I wish you continued success.
Thank you for your support. May God bless each of you. May we all have the creative freedom we deserve! “

  • Dafna Navarro, Art Market – Global Media Company

    You are more than welcome to visit Art Market Magazine and Lens Magazine

Long before genetic testing revealed an almost 97 percent DNA overlap between orangutans and humans, the similarities between the two species were noticed. “Orangutan” comes from the Malay word orang (people) and hutan (forest), hence the title of this book. The Book “The People of the Forest” by Mark Edward Harris. Signed by the artist.
