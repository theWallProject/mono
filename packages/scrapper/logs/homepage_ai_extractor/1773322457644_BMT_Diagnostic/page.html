About us

BMT has been focused on bringing innovative products to the point-of-care and self-use diagnosis markets, with an emphasis on developing customer-friendly rapid diagnostic tests. By combining reliable diagnostic technologies and innovative product design with novel commercialization routes, BMT has pioneered and expanded previously under-served diagnostic market segments.

We continuously strive to improve our products offering, paying close attention to our customers’ needs and feedback. We take pride in participating in the ongoing point-of-care diagnostics revolution, making rapid tests more accurate, affordable, and simpler to use and supporting the transition of diagnostic tests from central labs to the physician’s office and from there to patients’ homes.

We Have 18 Years of Experience

Founded in 2004, BMT Diagnostics is an Israeli company focusing on providing rapid diagnostic solutions to point-of-care (POC) and self-testing.
BMT Diagnostics was the first company to introduce rapid tests for Strep A diagnosis to Israeli pharmacies, allowing patients to purchase these tests over-the-counter (OTC) before visiting their primary care physician.

This strategy improves the process of patient screening at the physician`s office, thereby reducing ibioticant abuse by preventing their administration to patients testednegative for Strep A. An additional goal for this strategy was to shift Strep A screening from centralized labs to point-of-care, improving HMO service at times the centralized lab was inoperative (e.g., weekends) or overloaded.
