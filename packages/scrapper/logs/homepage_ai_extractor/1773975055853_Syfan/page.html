ABOUT SYFAN

SYFAN’s polyolefin shrink films have been used worldwide for over 30 years for a wide variety of packaging applications. Improvements in technology, raw materials, and packaging machinery challenge Syfan to continually upgrade and innovate our shrink film products to meet new market requirements. SYFAN offers a variety of shrink films including cross-linked, soft shrink, antifog, poultry film and more, all of which are food grade and user-friendly.
