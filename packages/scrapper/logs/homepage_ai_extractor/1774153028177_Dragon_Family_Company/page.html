Our Mission
To help kids become responsible, organized, and develop positive habits through play.

Together with parents, we seek ways to better understand and support their kids, guided by smart AI tips. Our big goal is to create an AI-powered parenting assistant. By leveraging cutting-edge Large Language Models (LLMs) and the unique insights from the Dragon Family Edutainment platform, we're crafting the ultimate helping hand for modern parents.

Inspiregon AI
A helper to parents and a friend for their children
Family Knowledge Base
We possess extensive data about children and their parents. We continuously analyze this information, gaining deeper understanding, and transforming it into valuable knowledge within a secure database.
Next-Level Knowledge Enrichment Tools
We can generate new knowledge by implementing innovative educational approaches, gamifying professional tools for psychological diagnostics of intelligence, skills, and personality, and creating new game mechanics. This allows us to effectively enrich our database.
Personal AI Assistant
Using family data, we create an AI friend for your child and a personalized AI assistant for parents — ready with spot-on advice for every twist and turn of family life.
