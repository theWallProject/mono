• LIBERTY® Endovascular ROBOTIC SYSTEM

Breaking Down Barriers to Access

Microbot Medical Inc. (NASDAQ: MBOT) is an innovative medical device company focused on transforming endovascular procedures through advanced robotic technology. Microbot’s LIBERTY Endovascular Robotic System is the first single-use, remotely operated robotic solution designed for precision, efficiency and safety. Microbot is driving the future of endovascular care.

LIBERTY® Endovascular Robotic System

BACKED BY TOP CLINICAL LEADERS

Experienced Pioneers

LIBERTY was founded by visionary robotics pioneers with a proven track record of launching and commercializing cutting-edge robotics companies, in collaboration with leading experts in Interventional Radiology, Neuroradiology, and Cardiology. Together we believe that access to quality care begins with the right solution.
