Retorno – Addiction Prevention & Rehabilitation

Retorno is the largest Jewish organization in the world for the prevention and treatment of addictions. Our detox centers and residential rehabilitation community in Israel support sustained recovery from alcohol and drug abuse.

Detox Centers
Rehab Community
Recovery

Reset • Restart • Recover

Residential Rehab in Israel

Highly structured therapeutic community in Israel. Go beyond the twelve steps with holistic treatment for sustained recovery from addiction. Group and individual therapy, horseback riding,  animal therapy, and more.

Rehab Community
Detox Programs

Go beyond withdrawal towards healthy living and sustained recovery. Led by medical and emotional health professionals. Graduates can continue to the residential community. Separate detox programs for men and women.

Detox Centers
Support for Women

Our Elah program is a solution for women using drugs, abusing alcohol, and engaging in dangerous behaviors. Elah removes the young woman from her environment, provides supportive detox, and can continue to full recovery.

Detox for Women
Holistic Mental Health
