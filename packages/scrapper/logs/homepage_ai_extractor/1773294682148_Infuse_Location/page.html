Indoor Location

 

Indoor location enables an accurate positioning of smartphones inside buildings.
While knowing the indoor location of people would change the way we interact with our smartphones forever, this is a challenging problem, since GPS is unavailable inside buildings.
Understanding that a working solution would be very rewarding, many competitive companies have tried to address this challenge. Nevertheless, indoor location remains a global challenge. 

 

Highlight video


Highly Advanced Sensor Fusion

 

Infuse is a software only solution based on smartphone sensor fusion.
This means that only sensors available today in every smartphone are used in generating accurate indoor location, achieving:

  • Fast and easy deployment.
  • Pin-point accuracy.
  • Low battery consumption.
  • Background operation- from inside the user's pocket or purse.
