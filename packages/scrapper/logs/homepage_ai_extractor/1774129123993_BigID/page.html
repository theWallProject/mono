Data Management for the AI Era

Traditional data management was built for structured analytics — not AI. BigID is the first data management and governance platform designed for AI data workloads: built to classify, catalog, and contextualize unstructured genAI data at scale. With BigID, organizations can derisk and control data in RAG pipelines and vector databases, automate stewardship with AI agents, and ensure AI data is accurate, compliant, and ready for use.

  • Catalog Unstructured genAI Data: Index, discover, and curate unstructured AI training data with semantic search and context-aware insights.
  • Agentic-Powered Metadata Management: Extract, infer, and enrich metadata automatically using AI to improve accuracy, lineage, and ownership context.
  • Labeling for Unstructured & Structured Data: Apply flexible, automated labeling for sensitivity, taxonomy, or business context to streamline management and compliance.
  • Simplified Compliance & Control for genAI Data: Validate, test, and report on AI data compliance with emerging frameworks — ensuring safe, governed AI data pipelines.
