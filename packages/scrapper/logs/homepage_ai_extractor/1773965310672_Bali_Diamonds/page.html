Our
expertise

Bali is an exceptional diamond manufacturing company that specializes in a unique niche area of custom made precisely calibrated diamonds as requested by leading watch and jewelry brands, and designers.

Bali’s cut-to-measure diamonds are inspired by the freedom of the designer’s imagination and enable their ideas to become reality. In other words, we make the impossible possible.

 

precise
perfection

For 15 years, we have been creating very special shapes which cannot be found in any other manufacturers’ stocks.

Our main target is to provide extremely precisely cut diamonds as required in the detailed design plans of our customers.

As you can see – our signature key is the art of cutting.

MGI Creation

As part of the company’s expansion and in light of growing demand, we have established a brand-new factory, MGI CREATION, in Chiangmai, Thailand, for cutting and polishing Custom Made Precisely Calibrated diamonds at the highest quality with the latest technologies that is adapted to strict quality as requested by leading watch and jewelry brands, and designers.
