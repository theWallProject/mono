Effortless LFG

01

Download GameTree

Build your universal gamer profile (games, platforms, personality). Invite friends.
02

Join the GameTree Discord server

Hop into the GameTree Discord server and type "/connect". This links your profile, friends, and games so you’re ready to play.
03

/lfg to Play

Game time! Type "/sessions" to find games or "/lfg" to host. GameTree sets up a space and brings the right friends (or new ones) to you.

What is LFG?
+
-

Gamers say "LFG", or looking for group, when they want to group up with other players to tackle a hard challenge or just have more fun playing together.

How to find people to play games with?
+
-

Finding people to play with is very easy using GameTree. Tell us as much about your tastes in games, genres and play styles as you wish, and match you with the your best potential teammates and friends. This can be done through the Match tab in the mobile app, or via /lfg when creating game sessions.

Is GameTree free to use?
+
-

Yes, GameTree is free! There will be premium options for those who want to take their social gaming experience to the next level, but anyone can use GameTree free of charge.

Which gaming platforms are supported for LFG gamers?
+
-

GameTree supports game across all the major platforms, such as PC, mobile, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, and Wii, and tabletop games

Does GameTree offer voice chat for gaming?
+
-

GameTree creates for you private group voice and text chats in GameTree's Discord server, then automatically cleans them up after the session.

How do I play without toxicity?
+
-

Despite having the ability to report players, GameTree has a reputation as a friendly gamer community. The best way to play without toxicity though, is really to just play with the right people for YOU - and GameTree group finder helps! You can also check people’s levels and achievements as a sign of their reputation in the community.
