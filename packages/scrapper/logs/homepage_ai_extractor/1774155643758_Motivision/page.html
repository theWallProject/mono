    Programming, VBT, athlete testing and more

    Workout 

    Programming

    Easily create and deliver training programs through the Motivision Trainer’s Dashboard — for groups or individual athletes.

    Real-Time Bar Speed

    and Tempo Feedback

    Keep athletes locked in on every lift. Real-time feedback on eccentric and concentric velocity or tempo goals drives accountability, effort, and consistency.

    Max

    Tracking

    Motivision automatically updates each athlete’s estimated 1RM from logged performance, enabling 1RM percentage programming and delivering precise target weights to athletes.

    Trainers Live

    Workout Tablet

    Simultaneously monitor every athlete live from the palm of your hands - view set results, effort levels, and workout progress from one central screen.

    Lift Performance

    Video Playback

    Playback set videos to review technique, assess movement quality, and provide targeted feedback — all within the trainers dashboard.

    Athletic

    Assessments 

    Quickly run key tests like vertical jump, mobility, and stability — track results over time without extra equipment or complicated setup.

    How it Works

    /01

    Program

    Workouts

    Strength coaches create training plans through the Motivision Trainer’s Dashboard — for individuals, positions, or the entire team.

    /02

    Athletes Train

    with Feedback

    Athletes log in on the rack tablet, follow their workout, and receive real-time feedback on bar speed, tempo, and movement quality.

    /03

    Coaches Monitor Live Results

    Coaches track every athlete in real time — see set results, effort levels, and get instant safety alerts across the room.

    /04

    Review Progress and Testing

    Post-workout data, video playback, and athletic test results help coaches track progress, hold athletes accountable, and adjust programs.
