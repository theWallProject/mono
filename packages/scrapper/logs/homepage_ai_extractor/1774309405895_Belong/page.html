Being long

The stock market goes up and down every day, but 100 years of history shows that a good way to make money is to invest and hold.

Picking stocks can be exciting, but 'buying the market' is a clever way to diversify and anchor your savings... no matter who you are.

The Power of Compounding

Einstein called compounding the 8th wonder of the world. It’s a simple fact, the more money you invest and the longer you leave it, the faster it grows.

Belong Fees:
For customers who open an account on or following 29 January 2025, Belong charges £1.99 per month + a yearly platform fee of 0.79% of your investment value, of which 0.1% is paid out to partners for servicing. This 0.79% fee is calculated and billed on a monthly basis.
For customers who opened an account before January 29 2025, please see the Belong Terms & Conditions document in your Belong app for more information about your fees.
Any additional fund management fee is charged directly to your investment by the fund manager and depends on the fund you select; this fee is outlined in the Key Investor Information Document when you select a fund on the Belong platform. We do not charge any fees for top ups or withdrawals, however if you choose to do an in-specie transfer out of your Belong ISA, the ISA manager may charge a £20 fee. If you choose to add a Boost loan to your investment, associated fees will be presented clearly to you before you apply, and then outlined, separately, in your loan agreement.
