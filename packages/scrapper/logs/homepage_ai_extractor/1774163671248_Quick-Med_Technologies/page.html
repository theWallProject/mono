Commitment to Fighting Antimicrobial Resistance

With increaing usage of antimicrobials and growing incidence of conditions where resistant organisms can form, a phenominon called antimicrobial resistsnce, or AMR, has emerged as a lethal global problem with a high human and economic cost. Featured Products Provide Health and Safety

Quick-Med Technologies collaborated with the US Air Force. Check out this video about the project!

The patented technology is applied to airplane seats that are in use with the US Air Force. For this product, a biomimetic textile coating kills antibacterial-resistant pathogens such as MRSA to 75+ launderings, mitigating the spread of infectious disease while facilitating cost savings and human safety from toxic chemicals.
