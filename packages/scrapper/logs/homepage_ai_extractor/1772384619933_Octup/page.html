FAQs

Wondering if Octup is right for you?
How does Octup help 3PLs?

Octup is an AI-powered analytics and billing automation platform for 3PLs, built to improve warehouse efficiency, client visibility, and financial performance. 

It unifies data from your WMS, ERP, OMS, and ecommerce systems into one intelligent dashboard, giving operators real-time visibility into orders, labor, and SLAs. 

With Octup, 3PLs can track fulfillment KPIs, automate billing, and share branded performance dashboards that reduce churn and strengthen client relationships.

How does Octup help brands?

Octup provides a dashboard & login to each client(brand) of the 3pl. The 3pl can customize and control what data their customer sees.  

Brands can monitor on-time shipping, order accuracy, storage and value-added service (VAS) charges, and live billing data directly from their 3PL partner’s Octup dashboard. 

This transparency improves collaboration, reduces CS tickets, shortens billing cycles, and builds trust between brands and their fulfillment partners.

How does Octup collect data for both brands and 3PLs?

Octup connects directly to existing warehouse management systems (WMS), enterprise resource planning (ERP) software, transportation management systems (TMS), and ecommerce platforms like Shopify.  

Octup is able to connect to each 3PLs tech stack quickly and easily because we use a third party that already has every integration pre-built.

 It automatically cleans, normalizes, and syncs operational and financial data across systems, eliminating manual exports and data silos.

Then this data is interpreted with our propietary AI tool and used to power real-time dashboards, predictive insights, and automated billing, helping both brands and 3PLs run on a single source of truth.

What kind of metrics does Octup report on for 3PLs?

Octup reports on the performance, profitability, and reliability metrics that matter most to third-party logistics providers. For example, 3PLs can monitor:

  • On-time fulfillment rate (SLA compliance) by brand, warehouse, or order type.
  • Average fulfillment time and units per labor hour (UPH).
  • Revenue capture and billing accuracy, including missed charges and value-added services.
  • Client-level profitability and cost-to-serve visibility for better pricing and planning.
  • Churn and escalation trends to improve retention and client satisfaction.
  • And many more!

With real-time analytics and AI insights, Octup helps 3PLs protect margins, prove reliability, and scale profitably.

What kind of metrics does Octup report on for brands?

Octup gives brands a look into their fulfillment operations through easy-to-use dashboards that centralize every key metric. Brands can track:

  • Order and delivery performance: monitor delays, stuck orders, and any issues with an order, instead of waiting on customer service tickets to stay updated.
  • Cost visibility: view automated invoice breakdowns and track spending across storage, shipping, and value-added services.
  • Unit economics: understand cost and profitability by SKU, enabling smarter pricing, discounting, and product strategy.
  • Marketing performance: see campaign results and fulfillment impact in one place to connect marketing and logistics outcomes.

With Octup, brands and their 3PL partners share a single source of truth that combines operations, billing, and marketing analytics, turning transparency into stronger decisions, trust, and customer retention.
