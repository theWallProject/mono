INNOVATING SOLUTIONS, ELEVATING EXPERIENCES

SalePoint provides solutions for businesses to engage with their customers.  Whether it is a point of sale solution or a healthcare patient self-service registration kiosk, we understand the need for innovation to better serve your customers.  Our solutions are industry specific and allow customers to quickly and easily complete their transactions. We look forward to learning more about your needs.

Retail & Hospitality

Our retail and hospitality solutions range from point of sale and enterprise applications to eCommerce solutions for specific retail and restaurant verticals. If you are looking for new systems to better serve your customers and manage your business, we can help.

Healthcare

Our healthcare industry solutions include patient self-service check in kiosks, mobile patient booking of appointments and check in, patient queue management, patient messaging, and more.  Our solutions integrate in real time with leading EMR and practice management applications.

 HIGHER EDUCATION

We provide payment processing solutions to colleges and universities.  The payment processing application is integrated with leading student information systems to provide fast, simple to use and economical payment processing.

Public Sector

Solutions for payment processing for public sector entities.  The solution integrates with your billing systems to provide a wide range of payment options.  Payment of invoices and customer bills can be done quickly and efficiently.

Superior Customer Support

SalePoint provides excellent customer support. We have a team with years of experience implementing technology solutions. We understand the complex issues involved in systems innovation, best practices, and the demands on our customer's resources. Let us guide you through a project to automate your front end processes and allow us to provide fast and responsive service after the go live.
