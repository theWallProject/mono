About Us

Established in 1994, Barnoam offers training solutions for unmanned, robotics and command & control (C²) systems. With over 200,000 training hours Barnoam has positioned itself as a leading company both in the defense & aerospace and civilian industries by providing training services, products and systems implementation. At Barnoam we specialize in developing various training products including e-learning modules, 3D simulations, LMS and screening tools. Our company offers end-2-end training solutions, based on our broad experience, high quality personnel and unique training methodology. We are committed to providing high quality and innovative methods for delivering knowledge and providing professional, reliable and creative services.

Learning Technologies

Integrating technology into learning enhances training processes, allowing content to be delivered in a creative, engaging, and interactive way through digital tools. At Barnoam, we utilize a variety of technologies to develop advanced learning products. Our solutions include e-learning modules, videos, games, online courses (MOOCs), and more. These tools enable the creation of diverse, personalized learning experiences that boost learner engagement and significantly improve training outcomes.

Our team at Barnoam is composed of experts in training, design, and development, and we support our clients throughout the entire training process—from content development to learning experience design, all the way to solution development and implementation.
