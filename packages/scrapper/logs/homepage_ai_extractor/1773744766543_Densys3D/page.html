MIA3d

Affordable Israeli technology
for the dentist

MIA3d is its intra oral 3d scanner, working in dental real time. Accurate, fast, affordable and easy to use, using regular visible light, MIA3d relies upon sophisticated algorithms to real time calibrate its input, and output an open 3D true color file of the teeth and adjacent soft tissues
