Our Mission: Cure the Loneliness Epidemic at Scale.

Wisdo Health is a Clinically-Proven and AI-Driven Social Health Platform.
We empower Health Plans and Providers to Address the Costs of Loneliness and Social Isolation and Improve Mental Health Outcomes and Engagement.
