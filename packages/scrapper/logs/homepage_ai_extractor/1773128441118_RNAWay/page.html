RNAway Applications

Agriculture Challenges

The agricultural industry faces ongoing threats caused by climate changes, environmental conditions and pathogens.
Since current solutions fail to meet industry’s demands, there’s an urgent need for solutions that will improve crop yield and resilience, reduce reliance on harmful chemicals, improve farmers’ profitability and ensure global food security.

RNA: a Novel Approach

RNA-based solutions hold the potential to be highly effective in solving those current and future threats faced by the agricultural industry
High Specificity
+
RNA molecules specifically alter plant characteristics, enabling them to effectively confront a wide array of agricultural challenges
Eco-friendly
+
RNA molecules don't harm local environment and biodiversity
Non-GMO
+
RNA molecules change the plant’s characteristics without permanently modifying its genome

Despite these clear benefits, effective insertion of RNA molecules into plants has been unfeasible mainly due to RNA sensitivity, low penetration and uptake

Until Now.

RNAway is a Gamechanger

Our cutting-edge delivery platform carries RNA molecules, protects them, and releases them inside the plant. RNAway based treatments can be implemented both by spray or by irrigation, making it easy for farmers to integrate them into their normal operational activities.

Stable RNA
+
RNAway's delivery platform carries RNA molecules and protects them from degradation, regardless of the sequence
Eco-friendly
+
Delivery platform encapsulating the RNA is biodegradable, which ensures no harm to wildlife and the environment
Simple Application
+
Fits easily into existing spray or irrigation practices for fast and easy application
High Efficacy
+
Enables effective uptake of RNA molecules into plant cells

Proof of Concept

Crop
Tomato
Pathogen
Tomato Brown Rugose Fruit Virus (ToBRFV)

Challenge: Tomato Brown Rugose Fruit Virus (ToBRFV) is a destructive virus infecting up to 100% of the crop. With no cure, the only solution is to terminate the infected plants.

Solution: RNAway based treatment is a highly effective preventive treatment for ToBRFV, reducing yield loss and providing marketable fruit.

Learn More
Crop
Grapevine
Pathogen
Grapevine Leafroll associated Virus 3 (GLRaV-3)

Challenge: Grapevine Leafroll Disease (GLD), a destructive viral disease with no cure other than uprooting infected vines, causes significant economic damage for growers.

Solution: RNAway based treatment effectively treats GLD by reducing disease incidence from 80% to 30% and recovering berry parameters.

Challenge: Cucumber Green Mottle Mosaic Virus (CGMMV) significantly reduces yields up to 25%. With no cure for CGMMV, the only solution is to terminate the infected plants.
