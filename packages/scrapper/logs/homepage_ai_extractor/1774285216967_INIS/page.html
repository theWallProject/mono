About Us

  • Home

INIS Marketing Services Pvt. Ltd., is a service provider cum consultant. It was established in February 2010 by Mr. Harel Cohen with it’s headquarter in New Delhi.

INIS is a group of talented, professional, experienced Indian entrepreneurs with one goal: to assist Israeli companies with mature products, to effectively penetrate the rapidly growing Indian market.

We offer proven advantages by reducing the barriers of entry to the Indian market, while substantially controlling risk, reducing time, resources and financial costs.

INIS shares its existing and successful platform to provide a cost effective local sales representative office for its clients. Our platform includes all required sales and marketing support to achieve a smooth, effective and quicker market penetration.

Our clients enjoy the expert advice of sales and business consultants, project and support managers, as well as legal and tax consultants to assist at every stage from planning to implementation of the commercial plan.

INIS years of experience in a wide range of Israeli industries ranging from financial solutions to Sales and Marketing combined with an outstanding group of professionals and a winning approach broadens our proved capabilities and enhance our ability to identity, confront and resolve any commercial challenge for our clients.

Our clients need the support experts who provide on time, comprehensive and efficient answers to help their business to move forward rapidly. We provide our clients with such a service.

We believe in one relevant business result: Profitable Sales!

Harel Cohen

Harel Cohen Adv. Cohen, the founder of INIS Marketing Services Pvt. Ltd., has established the company as a visionary and leader in the Indian market. Based on his 13 years of commercial experience in India, Mr. Cohen has directed INIS to become a market leader.
Former to INIS, Mr. Cohen had founded FTK Technologies in 2005, with the vision of being the first to deliver unique and comprehensive Indian languages virtual keyboard.
Mr. Cohen has lead the successful commercial penetration of FTK and its flagship product LooKeys into the Indian market, established profitable partnerships with firms such as HP, Educomp, Wipro, HCL, Microsoft and Sify and attracted equity investments. 
Prior to his position in INIS, Mr. Cohen has gained extensive experience in sales, marketing and business development activities in the Indian market, as marketing manager within leading Israeli firms such as Israel Air Industries (IAI)
Mr. Cohen is a certified attorney in Israel, a partnr at Harel Cohen & Co. Law firm, and holds a Law degree (LL.B.) as well as Business Administration, both from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. 
Mr. Cohen specialized in commercial, corporate and property law, and has acquired an extensive knowledge in these fields

Devajani Atom 

Devajani Atom is the CEO of INIS Marketing Services Pvt. Ltd.

She has vast knowledge in the fields of operation management, marketing and human resources. Prior to her position in INIS, she has gained extensive experience in organising conferences and events within different international and local organizations, and customers’ relations management using a variety of project management, communication & organizational qualities. She started her career as an accountant in a non profitable organisation based at Agra, U.P. and proved herself as an outstanding account manager. She has been a part of several programs on women empowerment organised by non profitable organisations of Uttar Pradesh, India. 

She also served as one of the board members of BioBee Biological Systems Pvt. Ltd. , TOP Greenhouse Technologies Pvt. Ltd. & EWAVEMD Services Pvt. Ltd.

Devajani holds a M.Sc. degree in Botany from Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University, Agra, Uttar Pradesh.
