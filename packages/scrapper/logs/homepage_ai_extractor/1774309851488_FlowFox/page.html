Flowfox eliminates the need for manual email coordination by fully automating container release, drop-off depot assignments, and container reuse processes.

All importer requests will be replied to in real-time, allowing ocean carriers to provide service quality promises never before possible.
Global Standardization
Flowfox integrates seamlessly with local security protocols like CPU, SCR or Secure Chain to create a standardized, reliable process across carriers and countries, removing regional inconsistencies.
