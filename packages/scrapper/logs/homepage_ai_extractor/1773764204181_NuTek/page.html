Real-time verification that the cavity surface is free of cancerous tissue.

Precision surgical guidance.

 

Any cancer surgery
ends up with one critical  question on the surgeon’s mind:

Did I remove all the cancer?

Surgeons currently lack real-time information to confirm complete tumor removal

leading to repeated surgeries and billions $  healthcare costs each year

 NUTEK’s SOLUTION

Pioneering precision in surgical oncology, NUTEK develops an AI-powered, handheld hyperspectral imaging scanner that enables real-time detection of residual cancerous tissue during surgery.
Its technology transforms tumor removal from subjective estimation to objective, data-driven precision, reducing re-operations and improving patient outcomes.
Backed by a multidisciplinary leadership team and proven clinical validation, NUTEK is positioned to disrupt the multi-billion-dollar guided surgery market.

Did you know?

Cancer tissue left undetected in the patient’s body results in:

Roughly 1,000,000 cases require repeat surgery

$10 billion per year in excess cost worldwide

Increased aggressive treatment, patient post-op pain, recovery time, and risk of cancer reoccurrence

Introducing:
The Rainbow Probe

Samples live tissue directly on the patient's body, during surgery, to alert surgeons to the existence of residual cancerous tissue, and provides guidance for their further removal.

Our technology

Combining analysis of very high-resolution 2D image with precise spectral tissue classification

The Rainbow scanner uses an advanced miniaturized hyperspectral imaging device and a unique optical system to provide surgeons with real-time data on the live tissue scanned.
