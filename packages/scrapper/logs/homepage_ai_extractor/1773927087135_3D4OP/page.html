    Patient Specific Instrument

    PSIs are used to execute the 3D surgical plan. Instruments are printed in biocompatible materials and they guide surgeons where and how to accurately cut, drill, or fix bones during surgery.

    ​

    These instruments are widely used in orthopedics, maxillofacial, and spine surgeries. The technology helps in bone and joint preservation, accuracy of osteotomies and reconstruction accuracy

    Customized Titanium Implants

    Customized implants are designed exactly to match patient specifications, matching their anatomy perfectly and printed in medical titanium.

    Customized Plates, Orthopedic Implants, and more.

    Customized Anatomical Models

    Patient specific models based on CT/MRI scans, enable surgeons to practice surgeries prior to entering the operating room.

    The models are provides medical staff with new insights and potentially new surgical approaches. It also helps to improve decision-making processes and communication between surgeons.

    ​

    Anatomical models based on CT/MRI scan helps in Research and Development of medical devices and more.

    3D Surgical Planning

    With pre-surgical 3D planning, the surgeon is presented with a virtual three-dimensional segmented model based on CT/MRI scan. This significantly improves the ability to prepare for surgery which, of course, helps to improve final surgical results.
