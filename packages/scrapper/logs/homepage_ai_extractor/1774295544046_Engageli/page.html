Bringing back the human connection in online learning

 

Classroom tables support inclusive, student-centered learning, enabling instructors to foster deeper engagement, connection, and comprehension. Engageli brings back the human connection to the virtual classroom with it’s unique table structure that cultivates classroom community and makes large classes feel personal.

More than

just meetings;

Real classrooms

If outcomes matter, a meeting tool isn't enough. Most virtual learning platforms rely on video conferencing, but that doesn’t always translate to meaningful learning. Learners have fewer opportunities to interact and stay engaged, and instructors struggle to see who is participating and who is falling behind.

​Engagement directly impacts performance. Without visibility into learner interaction, instructors cannot adapt their teaching, and learners are less likely to stay involved in their learning. This gap has led to lower outcomes. That’s why we built a market leading virtual classroom with analytics: to bring interaction to virtual learning and turn the active learning experience into measurable results.

While Engageli is built for the classroom, it goes far beyond teaching and learning pedagogies. Engageli delivers engagement insights and plug-and-play technology that help institutions transform their business models and operations - improving performance, strengthening competitiveness, and driving sustainable growth.

Dynamic virtual tables replace isolating breakout rooms

Tables create supportive spaces where learners ask questions, solve problems, and receive personalized guidance. Small groups strengthen collaboration, critical thinking, and 21st-century skills while enabling instructors to monitor peer learning and guide discussion. Tables foster collaboration, making them ideal for active learning in modern virtual classrooms.

Market leading class controls stop classroom disruptions

Well-managed virtual classrooms create safe learning environments, strengthen student engagement, and support social and emotional learning. Instructors have clear classroom controls: managing microphones, guiding chat channels, and limiting access to tools when needed, ensuring classes run smoothly and every student can participate safely.

Market differentiating asynchronous learning replaces passive recordings

Engageli’s interactive asynchronous learning tools keep learners engaged while giving instructors insight into understanding through quick knowledge checks. Learners also receive personalized AI tutoring in real time to address gaps and master content at their own pace.

Real-time engagement indicators provide visibility into engagement

Instructors can monitor student engagement in real time and provide timely support. These insights make it easier to track progress, identify learning gaps, and respond quickly to keep learners on track. Acting on engagement signals helps students feel valued, increasing motivation and strengthening trust.

Trusted by educators

A leading online university reports that when an instructor taught on Engageli, they awarded twice as many A grades than the same class taught on a live meeting platform.
