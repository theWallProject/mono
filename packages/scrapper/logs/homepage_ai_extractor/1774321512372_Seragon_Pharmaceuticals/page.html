Aging

Aging is a natural consequence of cells as they undergo replication. By understanding methods encompassing metabolism, genomics, and gene therapy, we aim to develop novel techniques toward slowing the human biological clock.

Metabolism

Metabolic disorders are a significant health burden to the world. By understanding the underlying mechanisms behind this group of disorders, we seek to develop novel methods to address these health challenges.

AI Bioinformatics

Artificial intelligence (AI) streamlines research through deep learning and data analysis. By optimising how to interpret biological data, the strength of AI-driven genomics guides the future of diagnostics of cancer treatment and genetic disorders.

Gene Therapy

As cells constantly process genes, there may be instances where a gene becomes defective and detrimental as it is unable to create proteins essential for normal body function. To repair their function, we utilise modified viruses to infiltrate cells in gene therapy as a strategy in repairing defective genes.

Immunology

The immune system is the central defence mechanism of the human body. By studying how immune dysfunction contributes to disease, we hope to find ways to mitigate these conditions.
