Israel XP
at Bar-Ilan University

Are you looking for a multi-faceted experience that combines university learning with Jewish growth, seeing the land of Israel, and connecting with amazing role models? Then Israel XP at Bar-Ilan University is the perfect program for you!

Apply Now!

University Courses

Stimulating general and Judaic classes at beautiful Bar-Ilan University, with credits that transfer to many North American universities, and a vibrant dorm life.

Learn More

Experience Israel

Amazing trips across the land of Israel, a range of exciting volunteer and leadership opportunities, and Shabbatons in breathtaking locations.

Learn More

Grow at Your Own Pace

Grow at your own pace, while learning to integrate Judaism into your daily life as a young adult. Warm, open environment with a loving and dedicated staff.

Learn More

Get ready for the most exciting and inspiring year of your life!

Israel XP at Bar-Ilan University is an independent entity that works with BIU to provide students with university classes and credits.

Israel XP at Bar-Ilan University is a MASA approved program.

Israel XP at Bar-Ilan University is a member of the Yeshiva University Joint Israel Program.
