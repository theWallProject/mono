Why Basepaws?

Top reasons to DNA test your pet:

Different breeds have different needs.

Your pet’s breed mix contributes to their temperament, personality traits, and genetic health predispositions. Learning their background empowers you to help give your pal the best care.

Their health markers can guide your health choices.

The sooner you know about health and breed risk factors, the sooner you can work with your veterinarian to address them and support a healthier, longer life for your pet.

It's fun, easy, & strengthens your bond.

All we need is an oral swab from your pet to uncover what makes them unique. With comprehensive results delivered to you digitally, you’ll soon know your fur baby like never before.
