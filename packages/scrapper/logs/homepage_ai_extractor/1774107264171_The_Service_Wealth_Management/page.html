The
Company

‘The Service’ group leads Israel’s Wealth Management services growing industry. Our most important task is leading our clients in fulfilling their life goals and dreams.

We do so by working with professional and multidisciplinary Wealth Managers entrusted with managing our clients’ family wealth over time.

Today, after a decade of activity, ‘The Service’ is the largest wealth manager in Israel, with over ILS 12 billion in managed assets for hundreds of wealthy families.

The
Services

We enable our clients to enjoy a variety of services that provide a complete and holistic solution for their needs and aspirations:

Family office services, financial planning, asset management, cash flow construction and management, tax planning, risk management, intergenerational wealth planning and transfer, retirement planning, and more.

The
Model

Achieving wealth is one thing, managing wealth is another! from our vast experience, High Net-Worth families generate high revenues, assets, and property, and yet find it challenging to achieve a sense of control, security, and peace of mind in their financial future.

We help our clients gain control and security in their future by focusing on four essential elements:

  • Revenues
  • Expenses
  • Assets
  • Liabilities

We actively adapt these to the goals and objectives we have set with our clients priorly.

The
Technology

Our proprietary management platform, which we have developed and perfected over the years, is the core of conduct with our clients.

This platform was built while assimilating the vast knowledge we accumulated over the years.

The up-to-date interface allows each client to see the entire household assets, risks, and liabilities as well as all the actions taken, all in one place.
