About Us

Turning data into knowledge

The ubiquitousness of data requires a new paradigm for knowledge management.
In an era of data explosion, Keotic’s mission is to automatically understand and organize vast amounts of raw, unstructured textual data and turn it into manageable knowledge.

Uncovers what you don’t know you need to know

Keotic sees the bigger picture, reveals hidden patterns and connections, and autonomously asks what you don’t know you need to ask. 

Route information to the right place and time

Keotic’s AI learns where each piece of information fits and can autonomously route information to the right place at the right time, improving decision making and revealing hidden opportunities.

Define nothing,
discover everything

Focusing on specific aspects limits the ability to assess real value and see the big picture. With Keotic’s AI there is no need to define anything in advance. The AI learns the data and tells you what it contains.
