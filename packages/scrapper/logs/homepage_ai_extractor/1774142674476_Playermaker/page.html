FAQS

Playermaker is designed for footballers of all ages starting from 8 years old. From pro players, to semi-pro, and grassroots, Playermaker supports them all. With straps sized for both kids and adults, it’s built to fit every player and help them track and improve their game. Notably, 40% of our users are female players, reflecting the company’s commitment to serving and empowering the entire football community.

Playermaker turns every session into measurable progress by tracking both your technical skills and physical performance. Each week, the system sets personalized Targets based on Optimal Actions — the high-intensity, high-quality movements that matter most, such as high-speed running, turns, and first touches. The principle is simple: as the volume of high-intensity actions performed with precise technique increases, consistent improvement follows.

Playermaker works both indoors and outdoors, so you can use it anywhere you train or play. Unlike GPS trackers that sit on your chest and mainly measure running distance and top speed, Playermaker is mounted directly on your shoes, capturing every touch, pass, turn, and kick with precision. By tracking data from the true source of motion - your feet - Playermaker doesn’t just measure the volume and intensity of your actions, it also evaluates your technique. This unique ability helps you not only understand how much and how hard you perform, but also how well you execute, giving you clear guidance to improve both your physical and technical game.

Playermaker tracks both technical and physical performance, providing a complete picture of your game. It records your ball touches, passing, shooting, dribbling, first touch, two-footed use, and kicking velocity, as well as distance covered, sprints, accelerations, decelerations, power, intensity, and more.

At the core of Playermaker’s AI technology is the ability to accurately measure the profile of every step you take, and to detect and classify every ball touch. This step-level and touch-level accuracy is what powers all of Playermaker’s data, making it possible to capture not just how much you do, but also how you perform each action. After every session, all of this data is sent to the app, where you can review your performance, track your progress over time, and benchmark yourself against players at your age and level.
