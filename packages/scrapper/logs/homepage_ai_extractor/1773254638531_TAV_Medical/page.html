Molds

A highly skilled workforce, state-of-the-art technology, and stringent quality standards positioned TAV Medical as leading manufacturer of high-end molds. Ranging from single to multi-cavity molds, over molds, insert molds etc. we provide all solutions according to customer needs.

Injection Molding

We are an industry-leading manufacturer of components and assemblies for complex medical products utilizing injection molding technology. All production is carried out in clean rooms in accordance with stringent ISO, CE and FDA standards demanded by market leaders worldwide.
READ MORE

Assembly & Packaging

Advanced ISO 7 & 8 cleanrooms boast robust assembly applications from flexible automation using robot integration to fully automated assembly. Manual stations, stand-alone operations, semi-automated lines, and fully automated cells prove expertise in producing low and high-volume solutions.
READ MORE

TAV Medical operates out of two plants of ~12,000 sq.m located in the town of Shlomi and at the Bar-Lev Industrial Park. These modern manufacturing environments comprise the most advanced laboratories, production and QA facilities, and state-of-the-art machinery including 1,500 sq.m   ISO 8 and ISO 7 clean rooms.
