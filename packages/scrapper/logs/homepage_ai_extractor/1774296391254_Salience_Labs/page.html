Built to scale AI

Salience Labs’ all-optical switches target a fundamental bottleneck in AI datacenters: data movement.

Significant investment has been made into optical pluggables and interconnects to enable next generation AI networking. However, for the efficiency and latency gains of optical connectivity to be realised, all-optical switches are required.

Salience Labs has developed fully integrated, silicon photonics based switches. Our switches scale network bandwidth and decrease latency, significantly improving AI workload performance.

Our Product

The OC-32M all-optical switch module eliminates the need for the costly and power-hungry optical transceivers required for today’s electrical switches.
