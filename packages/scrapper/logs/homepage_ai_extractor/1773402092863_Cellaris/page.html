Frequently Asked Questions

Cellaris is a distinguished marketplace revolutionising wine investment, collection, and appreciation. Our focus is on providing exclusive access to exceptional wines years before production. 

Through our curated selection of Grand Primeur wines, collectors can acquire the finest cuvées up to a decade in advance, securing unparalleled opportunities. Beyond investment, Cellaris is cultivating a robust wine trading ecosystem that fosters deeper connections between enthusiasts and producers. 

By empowering both wine producers and collectors, we aim to reshape the industry, enabling sustainable growth and meaningful engagement for the future.

To view pricing, simply create a complimentary account. Registration is straightforward via the login page, granting you immediate access to all pricing information.

Grand Primeur wines represent an innovative approach to acquiring future vintages, enabling collectors to purchase wines directly from producers up to 10 years before production and bottling. 

This concept expands upon the traditional En Primeur model, which originated in Bordeaux, offering a distinguished new avenue for wine investment and appreciation.

Whilst En Primeur enables the purchase of wines typically 8 to 18 months following the current vintage (depending on the region), Grand Primeur extends this considerably further. This model allows you to secure exceptional wines directly from producers up to 10 years in advance—before the grapes are even harvested.

Purchasing Grand Primeur wines carries the inherent risk that a vintage may not be produced. This can occur due to adverse weather conditions or when the producer determines that the wine does not meet their exacting quality standards. In such cases, both producers and Cellaris guarantee that buyers will automatically receive an equivalent allocation from the next available vintage produced by the same estate.

Acquiring Grand Primeur wines on Cellaris offers exceptional advantages:


Access to the world's finest producers: Discover exceptional wines from acclaimed and emerging producers worldwide, personally curated by Julien Boulard, Master of Wine. Our selection provides exclusive access to highly coveted wines available only in limited allocations.


Be part of the journey: Experience wine in an entirely new way by engaging with the winemaking process from inception. Support the vision of distinguished producers whilst witnessing your wine evolve. This unique aspect lies at the heart of Cellaris, and we remain committed to enhancing it for an increasingly immersive experience.


Intelligent investment pricing: Our Grand Primeur pricing model rewards early investment—the sooner you acquire, the more advantageous the price. For collectors building or expanding their wine portfolios, Grand Primeur wines offer unparalleled value.


Effortless collecting: Grand Primeur wine collecting proves seamless on our platform, eliminating the complexities of physical storage and logistics. Space, temperature, and preservation concerns become entirely obsolete.


Global wine trading: Our trading platform enables you to engage with buyers worldwide, unlocking opportunities and maximising growth potential across global markets, unencumbered by physical handling constraints.


Guaranteed authenticity: Every bottle originates directly from producers and ships from their own facilities, ensuring complete authenticity. Furthermore, our secure blockchain ledger guarantees the integrity and traceability of all assets and transactions.

Commission structure: Cellaris applies a 10% commission on successful sales, shared equally with the wine producer. This pioneering approach enables producers to benefit directly from their wine's appreciation—a transformative advancement in the industry. Buyers incur no fees, nor do unsold wines.


Storage fees: As Grand Primeur wines are yet to be produced, no storage fees apply. We are developing storage solutions and shall provide updates as plans advance.


Taxation: In most jurisdictions, wine qualifies as a "wasting chattel" for tax purposes, typically exempt from Capital Gains Tax. However, frequent wine sales may constitute taxable income under authorities such as HM Revenue & Customs. Tax legislation varies by jurisdiction and evolves over time; we strongly recommend consulting a tax adviser for specific guidance.

Notifications: Upon purchasing wine from Cellaris, you receive an estimated delivery date. Timely email reminders follow—first at three months, then at one month before availability—ensuring you remain informed and prepared. These communications prompt you to select your preferred delivery method and provide complete guidance on the process.


Delivery methods - Three convenient options are available:


1. Standard delivery: Ensure your delivery address remains current and accurate within your profile. Additionally, review local regulations regarding wine delivery and taxation to prevent complications or delays.


2. Storage facility delivery: We are developing global storage solutions to accommodate wine once released.


3. Estate collection: In certain circumstances, direct collection from the estate may be available.
