Traction
on the
Move

Meditrac’s revolutionary neck and back pain treatment protocol is founded on the theory that increased blood flow and optimal disc fluid mechanics are integral to a healthy spine.

Meditrac’s ambulatory decompression devices are uniquely designed to integrate dynamic rehabilitation with spinal traction. Activity and muscle contraction naturally result in increased blood flow, which accelerates tissue healing.

Vertical and horizontal traction forces increase intervertebral space, allowing for the reabsorption of escaped disc fluid and improved mechanics.
