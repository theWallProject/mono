Frequently Asked Questions

Get quick answers to your questions. To understand more, contact us.

What is Mosaic AI and who is it built for?

Mosaic AI is the first AI-native platform purpose-built for B2B SaaS support teams to automate workflows, unify knowledge, and improve resolution speed.

How does Mosaic AI make support faster and more accurate?

By connecting to your support stack, Mosaic AI automatically summarizes tickets, retrieves verified answers, and powers self-service—reducing time-to-resolution by up to 50%.

How does the Customer Context Model improve ticket quality?

It enriches every ticket with sentiment, product, and account data—ensuring AI-generated answers and workflows are contextual and brand-safe.

What ROI can SaaS support leaders expect?

Mosaic AI customers typically see 3x ROI within the first 90 days, through faster resolutions, fewer escalations, and reduced agent workload.

How is Mosaic different from Zendesk AI or Salesforce Agentforce?

Mosaic is AI-native, built from the ground up for SaaS support—not layered onto ticketing tools—making it faster, more accurate, and easier to deploy securely.
