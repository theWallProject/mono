Start A Fire is no longer available.

We built Start A Fire with a mission to enable those who discover and share great content to get the attribution they deserve.

While we have achieved this goal and encouraged tens of thousands of brands, bloggers, influencers and other individuals to share and distribute great content with millions of people, from a business perspective Start A Fire did not reach a point where it is sustainable as an independent product.

We strongly believe in focusing our resources on things that matters and that can be sustainable. Therefore, we are sorry, but we've decided to discontinue Start A Fire.

What will happen to links I created and published?

All the links you've created using Start A Fire will be redirected to their original source. This means that the Start A Fire links you've published on social media will continue to function but without the added Start A Fire badge and analytics.
