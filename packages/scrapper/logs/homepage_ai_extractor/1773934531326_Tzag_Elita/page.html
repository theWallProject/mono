Transform Your Goals into Reality with Our Expert Services

Maximize growth with tailored, expert business solutions.

clusters & Servers

Clusters and servers are vital for modern businesses, providing the computing power for R&D, IT scalability, and compute-driven business. A well-established cluster can improve efficiency, reduce pain points, and enhance time-to-market.

workstation

Workstations provide a dedicated, high-performance computing environment for individuals, improving productivity for complex workflows. Our state-of-the-art builds feature high-end hardware and pre-installed software for maximum performance and endurance.

hardware as a service

Unleash the power of your projects with our premium Hardware-as-a-Service solution. Rent remote access to our top-of-the-line GPU servers, with a straightforward retainer model. Enjoy a private, secure, and hassle-free experience, without any cloud provider overheads.

software & schedulers

Maximize cluster efficiency with our software and scheduler solutions for HPC/AI. Choose from a range of licensed and open-source options, tailored to meet your specific resource allocation needs. Our tools support R&D workflows, ensuring seamless performance.

DevOps

Our DevOps specialists deliver high-performance environment and virtual systems, with wide knowledge in HPC scheduling using Slurm, Kubernetes, Promethius, Grafana, RedHat, ESXi and many more. We excel in cluster deployment, monitoring dashboard development , ML tools and more.

HPC hosting

Our energy-efficient high consumption cabinets, built to Tier-3 standards, overcome the challenges of deploying high-performance servers. Expertise in Direct Liquid Cooling, immersion cooling & efficient air cooling methods ensures efficient power usage and effective cooling, minimizing noise and reducing hardware temperatures.

Behind the Scenes

Uncover the fascinating world of technology with a behind-the-scenes look at the installation process of the servers powering Israel’s biggest companies. Witness the inner workings of tremendous technology and gain a new appreciation for the innovative power behind these industry leaders.
