Yitran Technologies

Yitran is a fabless semiconductor company that designs, develops and markets highly robust, low cost Power Line Communications (PLC) integrated circuits (chips and solutions).

Yitran is a leading provider of cutting edge PLC communication technology for command and control applications including Energy Management, Automatic/Remote Meter Reading, Home/Building Automation, Switching and Lighting, HVAC Control, Street Light control and more.
