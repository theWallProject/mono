    WHY E-FOILS

    The Most Sustainable Way to Move on Water

    Conventional powered surfboards and watercraft consume a lot of energy to propel themselves. For example - a family-sized speedboat requires 15X more energy than a family car to travel the same distance.

    Hydrofoils, on the other hand, cut down ~80% of the drag, making them the most efficient choice for sustainable marine mobility.
