Your Success Hinges On Smarter Connectivity.

Traditional connectivity was never designed for today’s scale, speed, and governance needs, and to be the IoT leaders of tomorrow, you need to be continually ready for change.

Powered by a patented multi-IMSI over eUICC (eSIM) and a globally distributed, locally compliant core network, floLIVE built a global cellular network designed exclusively for IoT. Our mission is to keep you future-focused and optimize business impact, empowering you to expand into new markets, respond to evolving regulations, and meet changing performance demands, without rebuilding your connectivity stack.

Our full-stack solution is Seamless to operate, Secure by design, Adaptive as conditions change, and Crazy-smart in how it optimizes performance and cost. The result is global visibility, operational confidence, and connectivity that grows with your business, all backed by best-in-class support.
