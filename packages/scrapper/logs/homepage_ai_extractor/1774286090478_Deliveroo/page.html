Cookies

We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies to analyse traffic, tailor and improve our content and services (like showing you great restaurants and shops we’d think you’d like), serve relevant ads and measure their effectiveness on other apps and websites. To help make ads relevant we may also share your data with advertising partners. You can accept, reject or customise our use of such cookies for these purposes, or select ‘Cookies’ in our Sites footer at any time, to choose which cookies we can use. To learn more see our Cookie Policy.

Customise Cookies

When you visit our Sites, we and our partners may store or retrieve information on your browser, mostly in the form of cookies or similar technologies. This information might be about you, your preferences or your device. The information is mostly used to make our Sites work as you expect it to, but it can also give you a more personalised experience on our Sites and on other websites. You can choose not to allow some types of cookies by clicking on the different category headings to find out more and changing our default cookie settings. However, blocking some types of cookies may impact your experience on our Sites and the services we are able to offer.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Always Active

These cookies enable our Sites to work properly, so you can create your account, sign in, and place orders. They also remember your selected currency, language, past searches and other preferences. These technical tools must be enabled in order to use our Sites and services.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our Sites. They help us to know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around our Sites. If you do not allow these cookies we will not know be able to monitor such performance of our Sites.

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable our Sites to provide enhanced functionality and personalisation. If you do not allow these cookies then some or all of these services may not function properly.

Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used by us and our advertising partners on our Sites to build a profile of your interests based on your browsing history and use of our Sites and to show you relevant ads on our Sites and on other third party platforms (including social media). We may also share other data relating to you and/or your use of our services with such advertising partners. If you do not allow these cookies, you will experience less targeted advertising. For more information about how we and our advertising partners use your personal data to serve relevant ads, please see our Privacy Policy.
