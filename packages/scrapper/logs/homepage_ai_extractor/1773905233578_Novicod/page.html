Why Choose Novicod Solutions?

Novicod technology units integrate the manufacturing of RFID, NFC, and Bluetooth tags seamlessly with your machinery. As brand owners demand the digitalization of item processes, our company stands ready to fulfill this vision and mission.

Benefits of Novicod Solutions:

With embedded RF packaging and labels, brand owners benefit from wireless digital registration, tracking, and monitoring from production to disposal, ensuring efficiency and collaboration with brands, retailers, and consumers. The market size for this technology is projected to grow from $15.49 billion in 2024 to $37.71 billion by 2032, with a CAGR of 11.8%.

Key Benefits and Proposal:
Novicod, an Israeli startup with offices in Denmark, offers:

  • RF-Embedding Units: Customized for inline functionality with printable material manufacturing, converting, and printing.

  • Optimized Supply: Click supply of optimized conductive paste and microchips according to the highest global industry standards of HF and UHF RF communication.

  • RF Networking Protocol: Modified for item manufacturing, storing, delivering, vending, and usage areas.

We aim to establish a global item network through our large-scale development and collaboration with major machine producers, printable material manufacturers and converters, printers, and RF-converters leading the packaging and label industries.
