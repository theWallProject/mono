Mechano Deen: Contract Machining Company of Choice for Businesses in High-Tech Industries

  

As a contract machining specialist that serves many crucial industries, Mechano Deen tracks the market trends that affect our clients so we can adopt the technology and production processes that will best meet their needs. We utilize state-of-the-art manufacturing and inspection equipment, offer versatile precision machining services, and adhere to lean principles so we can produce a vast array of difficult-to-produce, close-tolerance parts with fast, dependable delivery schedules. As such, we’ve demonstrated our ability to meet the specialized contract machining needs of clients in crucial fields such as:

  • Aerospace/Aviation
  • Medical equipment and devices
  • Other high-technology industries

As part of the hand-in-hand relationship we form with our contract machining clients, we can actively work with them to satisfy their fulfilment needs, utilizing Kanban, minimum/maximum inventory management systems, and other forecasting techniques to ensure adequate product levels at all times. Our clients also appreciate how we have strong financial stability and continually invest in cutting-edge technology and training for our team, which helps ensure exceptional quality in all the work we do.

Plus, we dedicate a single salesperson to each client’s account. This person will monitor the contract’s production and inventory status in real-time, helping to maintain our high percent on-time delivery rate. This account representative also serves as a single point of contact at our company who clients can reach out to any time with questions or requests.

Precision Aircraft Components

Mechano Deen is a global market leader in many segments including air, flight and engine data, airframe, interior and cargo as well as a variety of aerospace system components.

Precision Medical Components

Mechano Deen is a major supplier of critical components of biomedical testing instrument systems and precision machined components for automated high end medical infusion devices.

Precision Industrial and Process Control Components

Mechano Deen provides precision and critical components for industrial applications and process control instruments for markets including Oil and Gas, High Technology and Heavy Equipment.

Expert Engineering and Systems

 Whether for new applications, product improvements or cost reduction, our customer’s projects often require engineering and design expertise.  Mechano Deen’s experienced engineering staff provides technical support and the ability to provide manufacturing design assistance for your project.  Utilizing multiple CAD/CAM platforms and tool path optimization software, our team works closely with our customers to efficiently provide technical and manufacturing solutions. 

Prototyping from Concept Through Market Production

With dedicated prototype capabilities, Mechano Deen can rapidly take you from concept through market production seamlessly getting your product to market with precision and speed.

Quality Assurance

  

Following our Quality Management System, our highly skilled team is dedicated to doing the job right, an attitude which has made us a vital and trusted business partner and has led to being a leading supplier to customers with even the most critical applications.

We bring quality consciousness to every part we machine, monitoring and measuring quality at all stages of production.  Our absolute conformance to specifications through our quality processes and systems are reinforced by our commitment to process improvements, investment in inspection equipment and employee development.

As an AS9100D and ISO 9001:2015 certified facility, Mechano Deen has a fully equipped Quality Assurance department and Quality Management System.  Mechano Deen’s measurement equipment include in process inspection probes, state of the art CMM’s and Vision systems that provide accurate and timely in process and final inspection.  We have integrated these systems at the point of manufacture to ensure quality is built into the manufacturing process.
