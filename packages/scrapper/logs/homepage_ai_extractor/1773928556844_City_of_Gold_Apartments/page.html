Vacation Home Management

Weekly inspections​

Enjoy peace of mind knowing your Jerusalem home is professionally cared for, even when you're away. We handle weekly inspections with a dynamic checklist covering plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and more, ensuring everything is in top condition. Our bill payment service takes care of utilities and expenses, even when foreign credit cards aren't accepted.

Maintenance team​

Our in-house maintenance team provides quick, cost-effective repairs, while trusted subcontractors handle larger projects. We also oversee renovations and home improvements, from waterproofing to pergola construction, ensuring quality work from start to finish.

Before your arrival​

We arrange detailed cleaning and grocery stocking, so your home is perfectly prepared for you or your guests. With 24/6 emergency support, our team is always on call, ready to assist. Relax and enjoy your home - we take care of everything else.

Vacation Rental Management

Earn rental income while keeping your home perfectly maintained for personal use. Our full-service short-term rental management covers everything: bi-weekly inspections, proactive repairs, bill management, and maintenance coordination. We handle staging, photography, and multi-platform bookings for seamless guest experiences. With personalized cleaning, stocked essentials, and 24/6 emergency support, your home stays a comfortable retreat for you and a profitable asset when you’re away.

Long Term Rental Management

For property owners who treat their property purely as an investment, our long-term rental management service is designed to deliver hassle-free, profitable returns. We take care of everything—from tenant screening and lease preparation with legal expertise, to transferring utility bills and addressing any issues that arise during the tenancy. When tenants depart, we ensure the property is thoroughly inspected, manage the return of security deposits, and prepare the space for the next renter seamlessly.
Our dedicated project management team oversees all property improvements—from waterproofing and painting to pergola construction—sourcing reliable professionals and managing each project to meet your quality, budget, and timeline requirements.
Plus, with 24/6 emergency support, your investment is protected around the clock. Enjoy the financial benefits of your rental property without any of the day-to-day hassles—we handle every detail so you can focus on growing your investment.
