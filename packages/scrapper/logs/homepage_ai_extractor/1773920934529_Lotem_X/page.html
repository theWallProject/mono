about company

Lotemx is an international digital agency

Lotemx Group is an international digital agency established in 2003 in Israel. We specialize in production of custom software solutions for businesses: web design, digital advertising (PPC), website development, site maintenance, mobile applications, SEO and all-things branding.

The company’s range of experience and successful projects have expanded significantly into numerous business areas such as: CRM-systems, financial trading and logistics applications, web solutions for the healthcare industry, exhibitions, social networks and many more. Led by an entrepreneurial spirit, we treat every client’s business as our own. Taking the time to fully immerse ourselves in their respective industry by identifying competition, trends, failures, and successes is the key when working with our clients to develop customized strategies and yield outstanding results.
