A modern database
Everything has changed. Today, our applications and services need to make immediate decisions on live data, however our traditional databases were simply not built for this future. RegattaDB rethinks the database to combine OLAP, OLTP and Vector processing so your agents and apps can learn, reason and transact.

Agentic AI: Allow your AI agents to reason and transact

RegattaDB allows AI applications to instantly run complex, company-wide ad-hoc queries across massive, live transactional datasets. Whether they need to calculate firm-wide risk in finance, optimize real-time supply chains in retail or enhance the player experience in gaming, RegattaDB ensures AI workloads and agents make the right, real-time decisions.

Data Architecture: Reduce complexity and modernize your data architecture

Tying operations (OLTP) and deep analysis (OLAP) systems together requires layers of costly, complex technology, which often slows down insights as batch or overnight processes prevent true real-time business analysis. RegattaDB efficiently manages both operational data and your analytics combining them into a single database which eliminates cost and complexity and delivers faster insights and complete context, without delays.

Scale transactions and analysis: Upgrade your workloads

Single CRUD databases are critical, but linear scale often requires complex sharding, and resilience dictates sub-optimal, costly active/passive configurations. RegattasDB provides high performance, distributed ACID transactions out of the box and can scale linearly and is naturally resilient to failure. And once it is successful as a transactional system, it can be extended with analytical queries that give the business and dashboards up to the second insights.
