About Us

Sentient Synthetics is at the forefront of generating synthetic scenarios, offering a cutting-edge platform for generating controllable and photorealistic synthetic data. Our mission is to enable businesses and researchers to overcome data scarcity and privacy concerns by providing high-quality synthetic data that can be tailored to specific needs and scenarios. With our advanced technology, we are revolutionizing data generation, making it faster, safer, and more efficient.
Sentient Synthetics Solutions offers a state-of-the-art synthetic data generation platform that produces photorealistic and highly controllable datasets. Our platform allows users to generate data that mimics real-world conditions and scenarios, ensuring comprehensive and diverse training sets for spectral sensors and medical images.


Sentient Spectral

Generating realistic data for spectral sensors while controlling the scene

Our solution transforms semantic information from simulators into photorealistic images for various spectral sensors. It allows transforming the synthetic data between different sensors and different environments

Sentient Med

Coping with the multiscale nature of tissues

Our technology can generate tailor-made synthetic images of tissues, across multiple modalities. The resulting datasets are unbiased, validated, labeled, free from privacy concerns, and have an on-demand composition of cases.
