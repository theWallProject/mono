The MetaVision clinical information system

MetaVision, iMDsoft’s robust, feature-rich clinical information system, streamlines the most complex workflows and improves clinical decision-making across acute and critical care environments. Our software helps clinicians and healthcare providers enhance patient safety and care, contributing to shorter hospital stays, lower mortality rates, and improved financial performance. MetaVision also fosters compliance with protocols and best practices, simplifies reporting, and supports clinical research.
