The Israeli CFD Center (ISCFDC) conducts research and development in the field of Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD).

The company develops advanced software and provides consultation services to major aerospace companies in Israel.

Code development includes, structured, unstructured, and Cartesian flow solvers. The company's leading CFD codes, EZNSS and EZAIR are extensively used by industry and government professionals.
State of the art research spans robust implementation of advanced turbulence models, multi-physics modeling, overset (Chimera) grid capabilities, convergence acceleration methods and computational aeroelasticity.

The company also specializes in aerodynamic analysis of modifications to aircraft, and store separation simulations.
