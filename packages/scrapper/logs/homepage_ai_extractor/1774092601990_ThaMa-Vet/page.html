Wide Range of Innovative Automatic
and Self-Refilling Veterinary Syringes

ThaMa-Vet of E. Nechmad Ltd specializes in the development and manufacture of state-of-the-art automatic syringe systems and injectors for veterinary and healthcare applications.

ThaMa-Vet offers a wide range of innovative self-refilling veterinary syringes designed for mass vaccination. The company supplies high-quality easy-to-use syringes and accessories to poultry, swine, livestock, fish and small animal producers, as well as healthcare professionals. The syringe product line includes fixed-dosages, variable-dosages and multi-purpose syringes
