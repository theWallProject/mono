Technical Integrator

We are looking for motivated pre-university candidates, preferably following a military service - who are eager to take their first step into the hi-tech industry.

This is a unique entry-level position for minimum commitment of 1 year, requiring only high school graduation (Bagrut) and we'll teach you all that you need to know. 

The role will combine hardware, software, and integration work within a dynamic, hands-on environment. You’ll join a young and collaborative team, contributing directly to real technological innovation.

What You’ll Do

  • Perform hardware and software integration tests, focusing on radar and RF-based systems.
  • Participate in system performance and functional testing.
  • Support debugging and troubleshooting of integration issues.
  • Collaborate closely with R&D, product, and algorithm teams.
  • Learn and grow through continuous on-the-job training.

What We’re Looking For?

  • High-school graduates with Mathematics and English (Bagrut) - Mandatory.
  • Advantage: Background in Physics or technological study tracks / military technological units.
  • Curiosity, analytical mindset, and a hands-on approach.
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills.
  • Full-time availability and ability to commit for at least one year.
  • Motivation to learn and grow

How to Apply

Send your CV to career@vayyar.com along with your Bagrut's Grades and mention “Integration Position” in the subject line
