Annotation
Types

01. Automatic Annotation

Machine-learning assisted annotation with quick and accurate data preparation. We use our streamlined data labeling platform to provide efficient high-quality annotation for superior model training.

get in touch

02. Bounding Box

This is the most common annotation type. Our team uses boxes around targeted objects to mark them for computer vision. It’s quick and effective, however, it doesn't provide as much detail as other methods.

get in touch

03. Oriented Bounding Box

A more accurate method of portraying objects that aren’t aligned with the horizontal or vertical axes of an image. This helps precisely mark the orientation of your object and creates accurate datasets.

get in touch

04. Cuboid

This method extrapolates three-dimensional objects from images, adding depth, width, and height to them. This is achieved with 3D bounding boxes, or cuboids, that create a nuanced understanding of scenes.

get in touch

05. Polygon

Used for outlining complex shapes, this method employs connecting small lines to precisely define the exact shape of your object. Often used for complex images where nuance matters, such as medical data.

get in touch

06. Semantic Segmentation

Separates pixels in an image into classes for accurate annotation. Objects are carefully outlined to capture their shape, then annotators label them along with backgrounds such as the sky, ground, and so on.

get in touch

07. Instance Segmentation

Further enriches training data by separately identifying each time an object occurs and highlighting its properties. So, every instance of an object will get a separate label and highlight color for accuracy.

get in touch

08. Skeletal

Commonly used for recognizing humans, we attach lines to images to mark limb positions and body types. Skeletal annotation trains AI to understand things like movement, shape, and posture.

get in touch

09. Key Points

This very granular method is most often used for important elements such as facial structures. Individual points are used for labeling features such as nose, lips, eyes, and so on - with specific characteristics for accuracy.

get in touch

10. Lane

Annotators trace lanes of specific lengths across an image to identify common elements such as roads, railway tracks, pipelines, and other linear structures. Often employed in logistics and autonomous driving.

get in touch

11. Bitmap

Links individual pixels to specific objects - allowing you to accurately mark objects in images that contain large gaps. For example, if your object is cut in half by something else in the foreground, you can accurately mark that.

get in touch

12. 3D Point Cloud

Some sources already provide more nuanced 3D data for processing. In this case, we are annotating data that helps us create 3D representations of objects and their relationships with each other in a reconstruction.

get in touch

13. Custom

We can combine different techniques to create unique datasets for specific computer vision applications. AI models are incredibly diverse, so we remain flexible and ready to create clean data for your needs.

get in touch
