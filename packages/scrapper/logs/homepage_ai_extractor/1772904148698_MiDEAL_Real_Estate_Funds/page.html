    MiDEAL Real Estate Funds

    MiDEAL Real Estate Group of Funds is an investment manager specializing in income-producing commercial properties across Europe. We serve institutional investors seeking stable, risk-adjusted returns through transparent and disciplined investment structures.

    Our investor base includes leading institutions, family offices, and HNW individuals. Over 70% of our capital is institutional, reflecting the trust and credibility we’ve built in the market.
