Fleet Safety Built On Real-Time Driver Coaching

How GreenRoad Improves Driver Safety

Safety improves when drivers get clear feedback during trips – and managers know where to focus coaching.

Detect risk as it happens

In-vehicle and in-app signals identify risky driving in real time.

Coach drivers immediately

Drivers self-correct with live feedback during every trip.

Act on clear insight

Managers focus coaching and planning where risk is highest.

Real-Time Coaching

Safer Driving, Trip by Trip

Drivers receive clear feedback during trips, helping them self-correct risky behaviour as it occurs. For managers, real-time coaching supports consistent driving standards across teams – reducing incidents without relying on retrospective intervention.

Driver Focused

Supporting professional drivers

GreenRoad supports professional drivers with clear, fair feedback focused on improvement – not punishment. Consistent coaching and transparent scoring build trust and reinforce safer driving habits over time.

Safety Management

Consistent, evidence-based safety management

GreenRoad gives safety managers clear visibility into driving behaviour across routes, depots, and teams. Consistent, behaviour-based insight supports fair driver scoring, targeted coaching, and early identification of risk – helping organisations manage safety proactively and defensibly.

Deep & Accurate Data

Clear, reliable insight into fleet risk

GreenRoad provides accurate, behaviour-based insight across drivers, routes, and depots. Clear data helps safety managers identify emerging risk, understand trends over time, and make informed, data-led decisions.

Easy to Engage. Fast to Deploy.

Designed for easy adoption and scalable rollout

GreenRoad fits into existing operations, supporting rollout across depots and diverse vehicle types. Clear driver engagement and minimal disruption help teams adopt the system without adding complexity to daily operations.

API Integration

Fits into your existing fleet ecosystem

GreenRoad integrates with existing telematics and operational systems, supporting deployment across mixed vehicles and depots. Data is consolidated into a single, consistent view – without disrupting established workflows.

Real-time Coaching

Real-Time Coaching That Reduces Risk

For managers, real-time coaching reduces the need for manual follow-up and helps prevent incidents before they escalate.

Driver-Focused

Driver Engagement Built for Daily Operations

Deep and Accurate Data
