About Hish

Hish is a leading engineering and manufacturing company in the Israeli economy, an old family company that owns a manufacturing plant, which until 2023 was located in Afula and is now located in the cement industries complex, Nesher (near Haifa).

The Hish company is now part of the AGH group (Arrow Israel – Arrow – Ganon Technologies – Ganon Metal) and with this merger, has increased its production capacity and expanded its services.
