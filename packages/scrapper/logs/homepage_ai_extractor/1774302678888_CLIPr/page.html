Automatically Review, Recap and Index BodyCam Video to Draft AI-generated Police Reports

01

Dock Your Device
Simply dock your BodyCam or DashCam device as usual, and CLIPr automatically receives the camera footage for processing. Police departments can specify which types of videos to auto-process so only the footage you need is used.

02

Receive Email
You will receive an email notification when your video is ready for review, ensuring timely access to the generated report.

03

Review Draft
Review, edit, and improve the generated police report draft. Check the detailed police report recap and the BodyCam notes, and if there are repeated errors, use the CLIPr dictionary to correct them for future reports.

04

Copy & Save
Copy and paste the finalized report into your records management system (RMS) to complete the documentation process.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Police Report Software, and how does CLIPr improve it?

CLIPr’s police report software automatically generates draft reports from Body Worn Camera (BWC) audio, saving time and ensuring accurate, timely reports.

How does CLIPr’s BodyCam Software work?

CLIPr’s bodycam software transcribes and analyzes audio from BWC footage to create detailed police reports, reducing manual work for officers.

What is BodyCam AI, and how does it assist law enforcement?

BodyCam AI powers CLIPr to convert BWC audio into report drafts, improving report automation and increasing officer efficiency.

How can Police Evidence Software enhance evidence management?

CLIPr integrates with your existing systems and police evidence software to streamline the management of digital evidence and automating report creation.

How does Report Automation benefit law enforcement officers?

Report automation through CLIPr generates accurate reports from bodycam audio, allowing officers to spend more time in the field and less on paperwork.
