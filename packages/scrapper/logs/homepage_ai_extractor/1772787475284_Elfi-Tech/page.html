Advancing care across the PAD continuum

The same validated engine powers the FDA-cleared Faro™ Research Kit and our integrated PAD product suite. Kuro™ enables fast, affordable early PAD diagnosis, while Azora™ delivers continuous peri-operative monitoring from the OR through recovery and home care. Together, these solutions cover the full PAD continuum – from cutting-edge research to early risk detection, ongoing monitoring, and informed clinical decision-making.

Faro™ 

Research

The Faro™ research kit accelerates vascular discovery with a plug-and-play, non-invasive mDLS sensor that attaches to any body site, unaffected by ambient light and motion noise, and streams validated perfusion waveforms, giving scientists high-quality blood-flow insights in any lab or pre-clinical model

Purchase now

Kuro™ 

Diagnosis

Kuro™ delivers rapid, point-of-care PAD screening in primary care with a single, non-invasive 5-minute test, providing reproducible results across all skin phototypes. It integrates seamlessly into routine consultations, enabling early detection, risk stratification, and timely referral for vascular evaluation.

Azora™ 

Monitoring

Intra-operation | Post-operation | Home care

Azora™ delivers continuous, real-time perioperative monitoring of peripheral perfusion during and after endovascular interventions, streaming validated indices to any standard monitor to enable early intervention, prevent complications, improve outcomes, and reduce costs.
