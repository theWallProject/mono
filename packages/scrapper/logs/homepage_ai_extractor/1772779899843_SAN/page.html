OUR MISSION

Challenging Engineering

SAN challenging engineering is an Israeli private company established in 1995.

SAN focuses on design, production, engineering and projects management. SAN is an ISO 9001-2015 company with strengths in the areas of hydraulic, mechanical and power systems (APU), electrical and systems integration.

SAN is a well-established supplier in Israel that Develop, designs, manufacture, sells and services machinery and technical equipment. 

SAN supplies heavy and light equipment to a variety of markets and to leading companies such as the Israeli Electric Corporation, Haifa Refineries, Israeli Railways and Israeli Chemical Cooperation.

SAN sells leading equipment manufacturers all over the world, such as ALTEC, HI-VAC, TESMEC, all characterized by high standards of quality and the excellence of their products and equipment.
