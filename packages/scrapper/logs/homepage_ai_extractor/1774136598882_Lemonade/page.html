How Lemonade Works

Lemonade is built around a simple idea: insurance should take care of people when they need it most, and do some good along the way. When you get your first policy, you choose a cause you care about, and Lemonade donates to nonprofits supporting those causes.

Good luck and responsible behavior can lead to fewer losses and larger Giveback donations, directing more support to the causes customers chose. Knowing that outcomes matter helps align us around honesty and fairness, especially at claims time.
