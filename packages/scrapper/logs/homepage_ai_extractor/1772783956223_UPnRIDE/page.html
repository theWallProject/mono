Navigate Life

More Videos

UPnRIDE is a unique self-balancing robotic mobility device that recreates the natural flow of standing and walking. Users experience safety, independence and freedom to navigate life.

Anywhere. Anyhow. Anytime

A n y w h e r e

Groundbreaking balancing technology ensures unmatched stability on any terrain. Indoor and outdoor, UPnRIDE adjusts to safely maintain balance.

A n y H O W

Robust set of features with the push of a button. Sitting, standing, or reclining, users can move in ways they never thought possible.

A n y T I M E

Every stage of life, every moment of each day and night, the robotic wheelchair delivers unmatched agility and enhanced visibility for users.
