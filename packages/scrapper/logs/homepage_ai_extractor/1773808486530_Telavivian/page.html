Discover Israel with us

Welcome to Telavivian Club, a tried and true connection to our Tel Aviv and Israel.

You might know us from Instagram, but here is where we gather the true gems of Tel Aviv – and all of Israel. Discover a picturesque winery near Zichron Yaakov, a legendary bar in Jerusalem, a humble olive oil maker in the Galilee, and a calming hotel in the heart of the Negev Desert. All this, and more, among 600+ businesses across the country.

As a member of our Club, you will always be up-to-date on everything new, cool, and worthwhile in Tel Aviv and in Israel.

Stay connected to Israel

Two years after the events that changed so much, and amid the ongoing news cycle both in Israel and abroad, Telavivian is a constant reminder of the dynamic beauty that surrounds us here.

Our magazine will connect you to Israel and also to other like-minded people. We are also occasionally hosting events to create an interactive meeting point for our vibrant community – both online and in real life.
