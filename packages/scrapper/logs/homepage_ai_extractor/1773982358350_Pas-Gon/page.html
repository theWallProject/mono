      Technology

      Pultrusion is a manufacturing process that produces continuous lengths of composite materials with a constant cross-section.

      Pas-Gon’s strong, durable, lightweight, high-performance, fiber-reinforced plastic (FRP), glass-reinforced plastic (GRP), and carbon fiber-reinforced polymer (CFRP) products are not only the perfect alternative to steel, aluminum, or wood, but the preferred choice for use in highly corrosive, extreme environments.

      FRP pultruded products are an excellent electrical insulator when mechanical strength is also required.

      Who Are We?

      Since 1973, Pas-Gon (1973 Ltd.) has been a leading manufacturer of high-quality composite products. Using an advanced pultrusion process, we manufacture hundreds of fiberglass products for a range of industry sectors from aerospace and defense and transportation to building materials, medical devices, and ladder manufacturers.

      We export most of our fiberglass products to customers around the world. Our vast exporting experience has led us to develop expertise in producing and shipping any length or order size, including partial containers.
