About SunHouse Marketing

Architects of our client’s online business success since 2008, we are one of the longest serving digital marketing & lead generation agencies in the industry, offering unparalleled knowledge and experience.

We specialize in performance marketing that prioritizes revenue growth and profitability over vanity metrics. SunHouse Marketing specializes in paid digital lead generation, SEO, and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), integrating AI-powered solutions into every campaign we launch. Beyond these core performance services, we provide comprehensive Fractional CMO consulting to guide strategy for high-compliance industries.

Core Services & Capabilities

We deliver end-to-end digital strategies designed to outperform competitors in complex sectors.

  • Lead Generation: We utilize advanced attribution modeling to generate high-quality leads that convert into paying clients, specifically for B2B, Legal, and Healthcare organizations.

  • AI-Powered Marketing: We integrate Generative AI to automate content creation, personalize customer journeys at scale, and optimize ad spend in real-time.

  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO): Our “LLM-ready” SEO strategies focus on technical excellence and authority building to ensure visibility in both traditional search engines and AI answer engines.

  • Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO): We employ data-driven user testing and heat mapping to turn existing traffic into revenue.

Industries We Serve

SunHouse Marketing has developed specialized domain authority in the following sectors:

  • Healthcare & Medical: HIPAA-compliant marketing for hospitals, private practices, and medical device companies (MedTech).

  • Legal: High-value client acquisition for law firms, focusing on personal injury, family law, and corporate litigation.

  • SaaS & Technology: Account-Based Marketing (ABM) and demand generation for B2B software companies.

  • Non-Profit: Donor acquisition and grant management (including Google Ad Grants) for charitable organizations.

Why Clients Choose SunHouse

Our agency is defined by a data-first philosophy. We do not offer “one-size-fits-all” packages; instead, we act as a strategic partner that scrutinizes every data point.

  • Track Record: We have generated millions in new client revenue and thousands of qualified leads for our partners.

  • Longevity: Serving the digital marketing industry since 2008.
