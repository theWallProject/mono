PLAYSTUDIOS ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

PLAYSTUDIOS is committed to facilitating the accessibility and usability of its website, playstudios.com, for everyone. PLAYSTUDIOS aims to comply with all applicable standards, including the World Wide Web Consortium's Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.0 up to Level AA (WCAG 2.0 AA). PLAYSTUDIOS is proud of the efforts that we have completed and that are in-progress to ensure that our website is accessible to everyone.
