Quality Control

The control mechanism will follow the customer’s AQL (Accepted Quality Level) standards. Daily communication with our manufacturers, ensures smooth production progress, a seamless supply chain, & ad hoc production management.
We provide on-site inspection, comparing the bulk production to the approval sample, and ensuring that the product was executed according to the customer’s expectation.

Quality Assurance

Quality Assurance is crucial to business success, especially for brands and private label programs. We have a specialized team of inspectors who are proficient in handling the supplier’s audits, product testing, and quality assurance. The process includes an extensive factory audit covering technical, quality processes, and compliance. When necessary, we also involve laboratory testing and certification. Product sample approval: the process includes a thorough sample evaluation covering structure, technical, quality, packaging, and in-house testing to meet customers’ needs.
