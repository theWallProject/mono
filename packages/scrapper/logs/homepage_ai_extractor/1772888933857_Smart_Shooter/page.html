How It Works

Mount it on
Any Rifle

Attach the SMASH system to any weapon. It takes only minutes to learn and master.

Select Your
Target

Through advanced image processing, SMASH recognizes the target and can predict its movements

It Tracks it
for You

SMASH will stay locked on the target even when you or the target move.

Makes Sure
You Hit

Once locked, pull the trigger, and SMASH will do the rest, making sure you hit the target

Handheld Operated

SMASH Handheld systems offer a decisive tactical advantage for individual dismounted operations. This easy-to-use, cost-effective solution can be integrated onto any assault rifle, operated with minimal training, and effectively eliminates targets in various conditions, reducing engagement time. 

Remotely Controlled

SMASH HOPPER is a modular Light Remote-Controlled Weapon Station (LRCWS) for rapid deployment that offers hard-kill elimination of static and moving targets, ground or aerial, while remotely controlled by an operator from a safe distance. Weighing around 15 kg, it’s ideal for deployment on various lightweight manned and unmanned vehicles and platforms.

Our Products

SMASH 2000L (3000)

The SMASH 2000L (3000) is the latest and most advanced fire control system by SMARTSHOOTER. All operational and technical insights from previous versions were incorporated into the system, making it even more effective while enabling significant weight and size reduction.

Our proprietary target acquisition and tracking algorithms are integrated with sophisticated image-processing software into a rugged hardware solution, providing an easy-to-use and cost-effective solution that creates the required overmatch.

SMASH Hopper 5000

Featuring SMASH Fire Control System (FCS) unique technology, the SMASH HOPPER is a modular, lightweight remote-controlled weapon station that delivers unmatched precision and lethality. Engineered for one-shot, one-hit accuracy, it allows operators to neutralize threats from a safe distance while maintaining superior performance in its weight class. Available in Standard and Enhanced Night Vision configurations, the system ensures 24/7 operational readiness, excelling in urban, border, and infrastructure protection. With combat-proven deployment options—including tripod, fixed mast, and lightweight vehicle mounting—the SMASH HOPPER redefines precision and versatility in remote-controlled weapon stations, effectively countering dismounted hostiles, vehicles, and small drones across diverse mission profiles.

SMASH X4

The SMASH X4 combines an x4 magnifying optic scope with SMARTSHOOTER’s fire control capabilities providing extended range detection, recognition & identification for the shooter to engage threats at longer distances. 

The SMASH X4 also includes an etched reticle to allow shooting without battery power. An optional integrated Laser Range Finder (LRF) enables range measurement both as a user-initiated capability as well as a system input for improved precision. The system integrates Lock-Track-Hit capabilities hitting static and dynamic targets under complicated shooting conditions and tactical scenarios.

What You Can do With SMASH

Military

Combat scenarios are complex, diverse, and dynamic. It is a significant challenge for soldiers at the battle to aim accurately under physical exertion, fatigue, stress, and mental pressure. SMASH overcomes these challenges and ensures shooter’s success.
Designed to help ordinary soldiers swiftly and accurately neutralize their targets, SMARTSHOOTER’s SMASH technology enhances mission effectiveness through the ability to accurately engage and eliminate ground, aerial, static or moving targets during both day and night operations.
With a unique “One Shot, One Hit” capability, the SMASH Family of Fire Control Systems increase assault rifle lethality while keeping friendly forces safe and reducing collateral damage.

Special Forces

Deny criminals and terrorists the ability to hide behind their hostages with the SMASH fire control system.
Calculating whether to shoot at a target hiding behind a human shield is one of the most stressful tasks for any shooter, however well trained they are. The space to take the shot is small, the timing is tight and the stakes are extremely high.
But with the help of SMASH, the target can be clearly selected in the weapon sight and if the shooter commits and pulls the trigger, they have the reassurance that the shot will only be taken when the advanced fire control computer makes the split-second decision on the optimum moment to fire.

Counter Drone

In recent years, drones and UAS have become a persistent threat, enabling hostile forces to gather critical tactical intelligence and even make direct attacks. These unmanned aircraft are surprisingly fast and nimble, making them very hard to counter even for skilled marksmen. SMASH family of Fire Control Solutions offers precise, swift, and simple hard-kill elimination of this threat.
SMASH fire control system put a precision anti-drone capability at the fingertips of its users, featuring built-in targeting algorithms that can track and hit even very small drones skimming along at high speed, at ranges of up to 250 meters.

HLS & Law Enforcement

SMARTSHOOTER’s fire control solutions are designed to give law enforcement officers a decisive tactical edge in almost every operational scenario, maximizing operational effectiveness throughout every engagement while keeping the force safe and ensuring that innocent civilians are not accidentally getting hit.
SMASH fire control systems allow the implementation of various software applications that improve performance in complicated law enforcement scenes, such as less-lethal applications.
Significantly reducing operating costs, training hours, and amounts of ammunition, SMASH is a cost-effective solution that brings all shooters to an expert level.
