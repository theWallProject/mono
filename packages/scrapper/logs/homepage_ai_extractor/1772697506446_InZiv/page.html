ABOUT THE COMPANY

Innovate. Inspire. InZiv.


Founded in 2019, InZiv has quickly become the market leader in high-resolution electro-optical testing and inspection, with a specialized focus on microLED and silicon photonics technologies.

Our clients include some of the world’s leading players in display manufacturing and consumer electronics.

Backed by deep expertise in physics, electro-optics, and advanced engineering, our team is pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in both display and silicon photonics metrology.

Trusted by global innovators and guided by industry veterans, InZiv is driving the future of advanced manufacturing – enabling the scaling of next-gen microLED displays and silicon photonic chips for AI.
