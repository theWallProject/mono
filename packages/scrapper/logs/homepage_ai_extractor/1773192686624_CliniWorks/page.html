AccelTeleHealth™

Advancing Healthcare Access

Anywhere - Anytime

60+ M Patients

30+ M Telemedicine visits & procedures

2.5+M Telemedicine Visits in 2019

Since 2002, CliniWorks AccelTeleHealth™ Technology Platform, has advanced
telemedicine services for millions of patients and thousands of healthcare providers
nationally as well as internationally.

AccelTeleHealth™ has been designed, customized and proven to be 100% functionally
compatible with healthcare networks , clinical departments and a variety of specialties

  • Seamlessly integrated with hundreds of existing medical and administrative
    information systems.
  • Complete data-integration with ANY EMR, including legacy and the newest
    technologies
  • Mobile patient access supported by numerous customized online apps and
    portals
  • Online Patient Video Encounters
  • Multiple Data Capture Tools including:
                      o Video
                      o Imaging: integrated with PACS and/or internal image archiving
                      o ANY device: full duplex, data capture
                      o Audio Recordings and Streaming
                      o ANY textual data in any format with natural language processing
  • HIPAA Compliant, Encrypted and Secure
  • Advanced Reporting & Analytics –Sophisticated reporting and analytics
    dashboards supporting make informed decisions
