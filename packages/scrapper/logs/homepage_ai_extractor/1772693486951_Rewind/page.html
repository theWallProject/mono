Scalable and natural carbon capture

Plants capture 50 gigatons of CO2 every year. But they eventually decompose, releasing all of that carbon back into the air. What if we could preserve it in an environmentally-safe way and stop it from cycling back into the atmosphere? 

Rewind achieves this in three steps:

1

Source

Sustainable biomass, such as residual wood from forestry and agriculture 
2

Store

The biomass in existing, oxygen-free environments for thousands of years where it won’t decompose or release carbon
3

Monitor

The sites to ensure permanence and validate carbon credits with leading certification standards

The Rewind Advantage

Inspired by nature

Our carbon removal mirrors and accelerates the Earth’s natural cycles.

Energy Efficient

We don’t use combustion or industrial processing, making Rewind’s method the most energy-efficient carbon removal approach.

Global Scale

With hundreds of thousands of disused mines and oxygen-free sedimentary zones in oceans, our approach is built to work in any geographical location.

Social Impact

From repurposing mines to supporting local ecosystems and creating jobs, every project drives positive change and restoration
