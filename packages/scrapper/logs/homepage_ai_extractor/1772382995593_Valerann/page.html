Valerann and Partners Earn Dual Industry Awards as Lanternn by Valerann™ Expands Nationwide in Ireland

LONDON, November 11, 2025, — Valerann, a global leader in intelligent transportation systems (ITS), announced today that its collaboration with Transport Infrastructure Ireland (TII) has been recognized with two major industry awards on both sides of the Atlantic. The honors coincide with the nationwide deployment of Lanternn by Valerann™, the company’s AI-powered road traffic monitoring and incident detection platform, across Ireland’s motorway network.

Data Fusion

Our platform continuously ingests, organizes, and analyzes large-scale data in real time. By leveraging AI, machine learning, and advanced algorithmic techniques, we generate actionable insights for complex infrastructure.
