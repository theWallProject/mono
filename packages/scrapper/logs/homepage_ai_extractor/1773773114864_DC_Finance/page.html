DC Finance


DC Finance’s Global Family Office and High Net Worth Individual Community builds an international network of high net worth individuals, family offices, and investors for mutual growth and support through first tier events worldwide. It is the largest family office community in the world today destination wise, with annual gatherings in New York, Miami, Houston, Dallas, Austin, Los Angeles, Silicon Valley, Boston, Chicago, Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary, Toronto, Abu Dhabi, Tel Aviv, London, Singapore and Hong Kong.

DC Finance is known for its highly regarded conferences, private dinners and online sessions. Recently DC Finance has founded the Family Office Circle – a private club for families chaired by Ms. Lisa Silverstein, Vice Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Silverstein Properties

Speakers include some of the world’s most known families, the world’s leading thought leaders, and entrepreneurs. From the SEC to the Federal Reserve, City Mayors and Senate members; founders of the world’s leading corporates and cutting edge tech companies to family members such as Silverstein, Disney, Pritzker, Crown, Desmarais, Brenninkmeijer, Spielberg, Arison, Hunt, Sawiris, Du Pont, Musk, Perdue, Mars, Rockefeller, Firestone to name but a few.
