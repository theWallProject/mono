Control Tower

 

Control Tower

Real-Time Control supporting operational decisions

The Logivice control tower, Unilog’s web-based solution, enables our clients to view and act on events using a single platform, empowering them to effectively meet modern digital logistical challenges. It contains all the technology, organizational tools, and processes needed to capture data from all stages of the supply chain, from the manufacturer to the consumer. The control tower gathers and analyzes realtime data throughout the supply chain.

Tracking & Tracing

Supply Chain Visibility

 

Supply Chain Visibility

Visibility as a key to our clients’ supply chain execution

The challenge is in managing agile, stable and rapid supply chains, supporting multiple inventory sites, sales channels, and distribution across an increasing number of operations and partners. Logivice provides deep E2E supply chain visibility across global locations, providing transparency into on-hand inventories, in transit, international and domestic shipments, order fulfilment status, deliveries, material flow and more. Full supply chain visibility enables our clients to provide high-level service wherever and whenever they operate.

Order Management

 

Order Management

Unilog manages various order types for our customers. All of the order types are opened on the Logivice system by an API/EDI connection to our customers CRM/ERP system and transmitted to the relevant warehouse for operation. Inbound/Outbound transactions are transmitted back to our customer’s ERP systems by an API/EDI connection. We manage sales orders, BO, PO, VMI, service parts, scrap, replenishment, WH transfer, repair and more.
