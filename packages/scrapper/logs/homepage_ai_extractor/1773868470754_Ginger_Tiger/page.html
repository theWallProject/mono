Let's Play

Where do you study and enjoy with Ginger Tiger?

In Schools

Ginger Tiger is a must-have for schools serving students with special needs.
Ginger Tiger is suitable for student with specific learning disabilities, multiple disabilities, autism, physical disabilities, blindness and visual impairments, developmental delays, deafness and hearing impairments, mental delays, and speech and language impairments.
We support all sorts of accessibility features including eye-gaze, switches, and touch screens.
Ginger Tiger helps students learn at their own pace, enjoy success and progress significantly.

Read more >

In Kindergartens

Kindergartens are a great environment to develop motoric and cognitive skills.

We have specific games designed for early childhood skills and all games are accessible.

All our games and activities are graded in escalating difficulty levels. This helps the pupil to practice the activities enjoyably according to his or her abilities.

With Ginger Tiger every child can play and develop these important skills.

Read more >

At Home

Ginger Tiger is the easiest way to allow your child to learn while playing at home. Ginger Tiger works on any internet-connected device. Just log in and play any of our hundreds of games.

Success and joy are guaranteed!

Read more >

At the Clinic

Speech and occupational therapists love Ginger Tiger. It allows them to interact with their patients and increases their motivation.

Ginger Tiger is a major part of the treatment protocol, enabling better care.

Ginger Tiger serves as a great diagnostic tool for many of our clients.
