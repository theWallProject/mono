About

The Mossad is Israel's intelligence agency, responsible for collecting intelligence to help Israel's decision-makers shape national security policies. In addition to intelligence collection, the Mossad uses its capabilities to conduct strategic operations designed to safeguard the Middle East and maintain international stability. The Mossad's ability to collect intelligence and perform covert operations is based on work done by a group of exceptional individuals around the world, who chose to take part in shaping history, fulfilling their potential, and being part of the best team the world has to offer.
