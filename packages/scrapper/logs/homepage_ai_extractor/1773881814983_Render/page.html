who are we
Render was founded by two die-hard design fanatics, Hofit Almog and Yael Moinester in 2002. Render is a design agency with an innovative marketing mind. The studio delivers full strategic design from A-Z., specializing in web, branding, online marketing and strategic design, Render creates mind-blowing experiences that inspire business, push boundaries and ensure that clients stay ahead of their competition.
