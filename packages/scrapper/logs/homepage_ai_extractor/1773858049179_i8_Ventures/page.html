MindLi

Let’s think… together

Learn More

Have too many misplaced ideas, tasks, and URLs across emails, WhatsApps, AI tools, feeds, and sporadic digital or paper notes that are difficult or impossible to find when needed? MindLi is the solution.

One tool (Web, iOS, and Android) to capture, store, process (with AI) and find all your thinking.
Simplify your AI thinking with MindLi.
