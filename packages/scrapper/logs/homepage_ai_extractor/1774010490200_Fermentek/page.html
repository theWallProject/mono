FOOD & WATER SAFETY LABS

Food and water safety is an important concern that includes handling, preparation and storage of food and water reservoirs in ways that prevent poisoning.

Fermentek supports customers globally with a wide range of food-safety and water-safety related products to ensure the integrity of customers’ needs. Our food and water safety testing materials can be used for determination of contaminants or toxins (Standard Solutions). Also, our test procedure can determine and quantify adulterants, hazardous chemicals or pathogens present in food or water that can have a dangerous influence on human health.
