How does that work?

What Sets Our Multiplayer Infrastructure Apart?

Host on-demand across a public network of community-run nodes. Sessions spin up when players connect, scale automatically, and shut down when idle, delivering low-latency performance and cost efficiency.

Who Is This Infrastructure Designed For?

For game developers — especially indie and Web3 teams — Alliance Games provides a frictionless path to multiplayer deployment, with scalable, usage-based infrastructure that grows with the game and not against it. For node providers — including individuals, communities, and data centers — the platform offers a structured way to allocate compute resources, contribute to session hosting, and participate in a decentralized ecosystem that rewards high performance and long-term reliability.

Why Do Developers Choose Alliance Games?

Upload your game executable through a simple UI or CLI — no DevOps setup required. Alliance Games handles the heavy lifting: matchmaking, auto-scaling, rollback support, and optional AI opponents for low-traffic queues. Choose flexible pay-per-session pricing during early adoption, or switch to a subscription plan for predictable costs as your player base grows.

How Does the Network Handle Global Gameplay Smoothly?

Alliance Games hosts sessions on a decentralized network of global nodes, dynamically selecting the best-performing machines closest to the players. This reduces latency and ensures responsive gameplay, no matter where users are located. The network auto-scales on demand without manual intervention, handling global traffic smoothly through smart session routing and lightweight coordination, all without the overhead of managing traditional infrastructure.

What Makes the Network Trustworthy and Resilient?

Sessions run on a fault-tolerant network where multiple nodes independently verify outcomes, ensuring trustless execution and protection against tampering. If a node fails mid-session, others seamlessly take over, keeping gameplay uninterrupted. Developers can deploy with confidence, knowing their game logic will run consistently and verifiably, even at global scale.

Ecosystem Partners – Building the Future Together

Whether you’re a game studio, an investor, or a platform partner, collaborating with Alliance Games means tapping into a vibrant, open ecosystem. Partners can integrate their services, reach new user bases, and benefit from the network effects of a unified gaming infrastructure.

Run a node and contribute compute to host real multiplayer sessions. Get started with our guided installer, stake tokens based on your hardware, and build reputation through uptime and performance. Developers – Focus on Features, Not Servers

Deploy multiplayer games in minutes with zero DevOps. Upload your executable via UI or CLI, and the network handles matchmaking, scaling, and rollback—no server management required. Choose pay-per-session or subscription-based pricing to align infrastructure costs with actual player demand.

Players – Always Live, Always Yours

Play anytime, everywhere. Games stay online—even if developers step back. Log in with email or wallet, play seamlessly, and participate in community-funded server wallets that keep your favorite titles alive indefinitely.
