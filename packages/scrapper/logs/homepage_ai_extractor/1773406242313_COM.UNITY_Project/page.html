The Basics of the Game Aviator

Time to understand what Aviator itself is. It’s a crash game that requires a combination of good luck, quick reactions, and some basic strategy. Here’s how to play it.

  1. When starting the round, you’ll see a plane. Place your bet and watch how this plane begins to move.
  2. The multiplier emerges and starts elevating your bet. The plane keeps flying higher and higher, and the stakes are rising, making the situation increasingly risky.
  3. You need to cash out at some point, and you must do it before the plane flies away from your view. If it happens and you failed to withdraw from the round, you’ll lose your bet, and the game will be over.
  4. If you get out before the plane vanishes, you’ll win the sum of your bet plus the multiplier. After that, all you’ll have to do is withdraw your money or try playing again.

The Aviator bot tells you when to cash out prior to the start of the round.

Strategies for Playing Aviator

There are some extra Aviator tricks you should learn if you want to fully enjoy your gaming experience.

Early Cash Outs

Try cashing out early every time you play. While the plane can disappear quickly, you can beat it by waiting for just a couple of seconds before withdrawing.

Yes, your winnings will be really small, but at least they’ll be consistent.

The Daredevil

In contrast, you can try a high-risk strategy. Place a small bet each round and let the multiplier hit truly astonishing numbers, like 8x or more.

Of course, you’ll be losing more often than not, but if you win, you’ll win big.

Double Bet

Some players like placing two bets at once, and it might be a good choice for those who want to risk and stay safe at the same time.

Bet on the same round twice. The first time, withdraw early — this will let you win a small prize. With the second bet, wait until the multiplier rises to a dangerous extent and try scoring a higher win.

Even if the second bet loses, you’ll have the first victory as a consolation prize.

Martingale Strategy

This is a riskier strategy. Place a smallish bet and try winning. If you lose, double your bet. Repeat this process again and again until you finally triumph. After this, go back to your initial stake.
