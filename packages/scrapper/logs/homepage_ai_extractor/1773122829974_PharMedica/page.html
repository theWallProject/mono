Novel Enabling Technologies

P-ODF uses a proprietary biological and chemical programming mechanism with breakthrough architectural designs that enable unprecedented control over drug delivery.

Swelling Patch Technology

Hygroscopic polymer swells with humidity exposure, forming a gel for controlled, sustained API release over extended periods.

Multilayer 3D Patch

A novel manufacturing process enabling ultra-thin laminated layers, each carrying distinct APIs or functional roles.

Micro-Reservoirs (Wells)

Perforated layers form reservoirs for distinct APIs, preventing problematic interactions and enabling complex drug combinations.

Flexible & Cost-Effective Manufacturing

Reliable hot melt extrusion process enabling scalable, cost-effective production with consistent quality and precision.
