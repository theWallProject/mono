    FAQs on Mygate

    What makes Mygate the most complete solution for gated communities?

    Mygate is a full-scale ERP built for residential communities. It handles everything from daily operations and finances to resident engagement, all on one platform.

    • Over 250 features, including billing, accounting, facility booking, helpdesk, visitor management, asset tracking, vendor control, communication, and IoT integration.
    • Trusted by 4 million+ homes across 50+ cities, making it India’s largest community platform.
    • Modular design lets societies start with essentials and scale without switching systems.
    • Consolidates multiple tools into a single dashboard, cutting complexity and costs.
    • Monthly feature updates based on user feedback.

    Unlike NoBrokerHood, Mygate covers the full spectrum of society management, so you don’t need multiple vendors. Mygate has grown into a full community ERP that handles the day-to-day load of large RWAs with far more depth than most people expect. Most large societies today tend to rely on its accounting, billing, helpdesk, facility bookings, and committee workflows for their primary operating system.

    If your community needs a platform that can manage scale, keep finances organised, maintain clean audit trails, and still handle resident operations without friction, Mygate generally fits that requirement better than Nobrokerhood. Communities focused only on basic administration may still find Noberokerhood adequate, but RWAs looking for a stronger, more mature ERP backbone usually lean toward Mygate.

    Can Mygate handle both small societies and massive townships?

    Yes. Scalability is built into Mygate. We support everything from 50-unit societies to townships with over 10,000 homes without any slowdowns.

    • Cloud-native setup with 99.9% uptime and high concurrency.
    • Separate workflows for blocks, phases, or zones, all managed centrally.
    • Multi-property management from one dashboard.
    • Syncs data across admin consoles, apps, and IoT devices seamlessly.
    • Supports large-scale hardware integrations like RFID gates, LPR cameras, and biometrics without lag.

    This makes Mygate the right choice for developers managing multiple properties, unlike platforms that struggle at scale.

    Is Mygate only a visitor management app?

     No. Visitor management is just one part of Mygate’s 20+ ERP modules.

    • Billing and accounting with GST compliance, TDS tracking, online payments, and bank reconciliation.
    • Facility booking for courts, halls, pools, and more.
    • Helpdesk ticketing with SLA tracking.
    • Community engagement tools like polls, events, and announcements.
    • Vendor and asset management with preventive maintenance scheduling.
    • IoT integration for access and security.

    Unlike competitors who focus mostly on visitor management, Mygate covers all community operations end-to-end.

    How does Mygate ensure smooth accounting and billing for RWAs?

     Mygate replaces separate accounting tools with a complete financial suite.

    • Automated invoices for maintenance, utilities, and bookings.
    • GST-ready billing with tax breakdowns.
    • Online payments via UPI, net banking, and cards, with instant reconciliation.
    • TDS computation and reporting for vendor payments.
    • Expense tracking and budget forecasting.
    • Bank integration for real-time transaction sync

    Unlike Nobrokerhood, Mygate’s comprehsnive accounting module is fully native, reducing cost and complexity.

     How does Mygate protect resident data and privacy?

    Mygate prioritizes data privacy, with user control, ISO 27001:2026 certification, GDPR guidelines, and industry best practices like SSL encryption.

    • End-to-end encryption of all resident and financial data.
    • Secure, certified cloud hosting with multiple backups.
    • Role-based access controls.
    • No sharing or selling of resident data.
    • Annual external audits and penetration tests.

    Competitors like NoBrokerHood have faced criticism for mixing resident data with property listings. There was a massive data breach reported in another community management app in 2025. Mygate keeps your data strictly within your community.
