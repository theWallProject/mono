Glass for the Future 

Glass as a transparent material is becoming an essential material surrounding us everywhere; its allowing us to connect with the outdoors and it allows light and joy to enter our lives. The usage of glass around us can also add color, allow quietness and protection from the sun and bad weather. We at Phoenicia believe that high quality of glass will result in high quality of life.

Phoenicia offers a variety of products and glass benefits that will fit every need, while keeping in mind the quality of glass needed to maintain the quality of life.

Clear Float Glass


Clear Float Glass

Phoenicia’s float glass is manufactured using the latest advances in glass-making technology, ensuring highest quality glass with a flat and smooth surface. Clear float glass made by Phoenicia is extremely convenient to work with thanks to Phoenicia’s  manufacturing process and quality control. 

Learn more

Low Iron - True Color®

Low Iron
True Color®

When envisioning the perfect design for an interior space or an architectural project, one can easily forget the slight color distortion in ordinary float glass. Phoenicia developed a low iron ultra-clear glass with high transparency and practically no color or shade. 

Learn more

Laminated Glass

Laminated Glass

Glazing is a major component of all types of buildings. Phoenicia’s laminated products are composed of two or more layers of high quality glass laminated with an interlayer of Polyvinyl butyral (PVB) to provide safety and beauty.
