Tikkun Olam Makers
TOM - Tikkun Olam Makers - is an Israeli-turned-global venture inspired by a bold vision calling for improving the lives of 250,000,000 people. ’Tikkun Olam’ ("to repair the world" in Hebrew) is a Jewish idea that encourages every person to contribute toward improving themselves, their community and the world.
