    ABOUT US

    ​

    The Oridian legacy advertising brand (formed in1998) was re-established in 2017 by leading industry veterans.

     

    Its renewed mission is cutting through the clutter, buzzwords, platforms, and acronyms that separate advertisers from audiences.

     

    Through a distinct and effective operation, Oridian is able to offer its partners the best of all possible worlds: Quality audiences at scale and premium brands seeking real contact.

    ​

    Oridian provides a full suite of advertising services for clients in North America, and other English-speaking geos. 

    ​

    Our display ad formats range from the classic banner sizes to exclusive high-impact ads. We pride ourselves on our range of large and medium video players and our premium inventory of pre-, mid-, and post-roll ad placements.

    OUR TECHNOLOGICAL EDGE
