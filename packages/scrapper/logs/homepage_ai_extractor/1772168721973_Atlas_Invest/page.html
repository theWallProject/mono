Tal Shahar

Co-Founder and CEO

Co-Founder and CEO of Atlas Invest, Tal Shahar leads the company in revolutionizing asset-backed debt investments. With over a decade of experience in fintech and financial strategy, Tal’s commitment to delivering impactful results is rooted in a deep passion for financial innovation. Prior to founding Atlas, he was a key member of the founding team at Deep Insight, a venture capital firm specializing in deep tech, where he continues to serve as a Venture Partner. Tal’s dedication to strategic execution and team development has solidified Atlas Invest as a trusted partner for those seeking secure, asset-backed debt opportunities.

Tal’s career has been defined by his ability to combine financial expertise with a forward-thinking approach to technology and markets. He launched his first fintech startup, Athoss, while pursuing degrees in Computer Science and Entrepreneurship. He also established the influential investment community HiveMind, further showcasing his talent for connecting vision with execution. These experiences laid the groundwork for the creation of Atlas Invest, where Tal integrates his knowledge of computer science, financial strategy, and market dynamics to deliver innovative solutions in real estate-backed debt investments.

Known for his results-driven leadership style, Tal places a strong emphasis on teamwork and shared purpose. His collaborative approach and relentless focus on delivering value have propelled Atlas Invest into a position of trust and excellence in the industry. Tal’s ability to bridge deep industry insights with cutting-edge technology continues to drive Atlas forward, offering secure and scalable opportunities in asset-backed debt markets.
