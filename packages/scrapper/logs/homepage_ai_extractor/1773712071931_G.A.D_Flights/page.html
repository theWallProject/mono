Our Services

Luxury Flight Services at the Highest Quality

G.A.D Flights provides the highest levels of flight quality and customer experience for any flight services you require

Book a Flight

Private Flights

We offer luxurious private flights with high standards of service, comfort, and discretion. Full VIP service is provided from arrival at the terminal through landing, with personal attention throughout the flight.
Our aircraft feature comfortable seating, spacious luggage compartments, and chef-prepared catering tailored to personal preferences.
Onboard Wi-Fi services are available during the flight.

Cargo Flights

We take care of your personal belonging and protect them in the best possible way. Here at G.A.D Flights we understand the importance of flying sensitive cargo responsibly and quickly without damaging it along the way. We offer cargo flight services to a variety of destinations with strict conditions – matching the aircraft model to the type of cargo (including sensitive cargo), most efficient flight path, close accompaniment of the flight team and all this while keeping complete discretion of until it arrives.

Air Ambulance

Many emergencies require a medical flight, that requires a short response time with top-level professionalism. Our ambulance flights are accompanied by a skilled and certified medical team of doctors and paramedics, with all the medical equipment required to execute these flights. In situations like these, we know the importance of professional care of the patient, maintaining their health, and bringing them to follow up treatment as quickly as possible. Our team has a lot of experience in flights like these.

Business Flights

Our business flights are suited for the changing needs of any organization and include top service levels in the business flights field – VIP service which accompanies you throughout the whole process, flight attendant on each flight, comfortable and luxury seats, large luggage compartment for maximum convenience and personal attention.
