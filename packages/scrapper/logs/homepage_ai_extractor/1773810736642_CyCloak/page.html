Why Phishing Awareness Just Isn't Enough

Compliance

Why Cyber Certifications Don't Mean Much

Compliance is overrated

Many companies showcase prestigious cyber certifications as proof of organizational resilience. But in reality – even certified organizations get breached.

Instead of pouring endless resources into chasing certifications, it's better to invest in real capabilities – strengthening architecture, hardening systems, and improving incident response.

Recovery

How to Recover Properly from a Cyber Incident?

Recovery best practices

A breach is not just a technical event – it's a business crisis. The first few hours are critical: you must stop the attack, investigate, recover carefully, and make sure the attacker has no way back in.

Organizations that recover correctly not only survive, but come out stronger – with upgraded defenses and restored customer trust. The key principle: don't wait until it happens. Build your recovery processes and business scenarios today.
