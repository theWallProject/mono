Resilient Yields Start with Resilient Seeds

Salicrop enhances seeds before they are planted. Our proprietary protocols induce a gentle, controlled stress at the seed stage, strengthening the plant's natural response mechanisms.

The result is crops that germinate better and emerge faster in hot, saline, or low-quality soils, develop stronger root systems with improved nutrient uptake, and maintain productivity under heat waves, drought periods, or brackish irrigation.
