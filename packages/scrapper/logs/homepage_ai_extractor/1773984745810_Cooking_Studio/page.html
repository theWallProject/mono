Join a Class

View our Classes Schedule

If you like either cooking or eating, we invite you to join our open-to-the-public classes, and enjoy the atmosphere, dine with delicious food and enrich yourself with new skills.
The length of each workshop is about 3 hours, and in addition to the food, you will be offered a variety of cold and hot drinks, and a variety of wines of Binyamina Winery. Every week we host at least 1 cooking class in English.
