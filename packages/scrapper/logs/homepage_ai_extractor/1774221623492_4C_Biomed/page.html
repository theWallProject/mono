Unleash the Immune System to Fight Cancer

We focus on immune targets – the key to unlocking new and effective cancer therapies. Our unique and innovative platform identifies novel immune targets we can modulate with new drugs.

Our Focus

Unlocking the Promise  of Immune Targets

We combine cutting-edge science, real-world clinical insights and unique autologous cell systems (i.e. cancer and immune cells from the same patient) to drive the development of immuno-oncology therapies. Our lead drug has completed IND-enabling studies and is planned to enter clinical Phase I in 2026.

Our Technology

Patient-derived,  Patient-inspired

We are committed to delivering pioneering solutions that directly influence patient care. Our discovery platform is patient-derived and able to discover new ways to fight cancer. Over 20% of the immune targets we have discovered*, have reached the stage of clinical trials by pharma companies.
