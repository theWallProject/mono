ABOUT THE FIRM

Kaufman Kaiser (KK), specializes in the fields of capital markets, corporate law and M&A, hi-tech & start-up.

Our lawyers’ long proven record demonstrated that different clients require different approaches, therefore we believe in providing tailor solutions based on the specific clients’ demands and needs.

KK provides legal services to public and private companies, start ups and individuals, in various fields of expertise.
Our team consists of lawyers with extensive experience, who operate collectively towards a single goal – providing our clients with top-tier legal service.

Our focus is on being approachable, responsive and offering high levels of specialist expertise.
