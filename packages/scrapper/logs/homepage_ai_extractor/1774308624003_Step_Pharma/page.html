Dencatistat (STP938) is entering clinical trials for the treatment of blood cancers with the potential for accelerated approval from a single arm Phase II study.

Dencatistat (STP938)

Our lead CTPS1 inhibitor dencatistat has the potential to become a key treatment for a range of different cancer types. Dencatistat is in clinical development for the treatment of lymphoma, solid tumours with the initial focus on ovarian cancer, and for essential thrombocythaemia. In the future dencatistat could also form the backbone of various treatment regimens for solid tumours.
