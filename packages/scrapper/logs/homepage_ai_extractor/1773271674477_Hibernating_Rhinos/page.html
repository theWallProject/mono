Keep latency compact as your application expands

A huge advantage is that your profiler works in real-time on your continuous integration system. New builds mean new and updated queries and new versions indicate lots of newly baked in requests to your system.

Any time you create a new query, the profiler will analyze it to see if there is an opportunity to serve the query with less latency or requiring minimal computing resources.

  • Your profiling tools analyze your code and data queries on a continuous basis
  • They ensure that new builds and new versions don’t mean new latency and old performance. A single bad query can double latency and crush performance!
  • Maintain lean DevOps by clicking directly to new code and fixing it immediately

As your application matures, it carries more information. The data you manage for each query expands. Over time, a single query requires more work. Even if your user base or traffic remains steady, the amount of data they are using gets denser.

Never go to production again with heavy queries that can unexpectedly slow down your application or produce surprises on your next month’s cloud bill.
