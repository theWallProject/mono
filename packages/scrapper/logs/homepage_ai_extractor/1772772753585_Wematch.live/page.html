Our Platform

Our platform provides three core functions for sell- and buy-side firms

Matching
  • Access multiple matching protocols, including inventory matching, price discovery, RFQs, and directed interests, all tailored to your specific requirements.
  • Seamlessly integrate with your existing in-house or third-party solutions via flexible API connectivity or dedicated trading venues.
Workflow Automation
  • Streamline your pre- and post-trade workflows with our bespoke lifecycle management tools.
  • Centralise your trade data and optimise collateral management and risk with our powerful suite of tools, including Smart Basket Builder, Collateral Optimisation, and intuitive API connectivity protocols.
Internal Solutions
  • Transform internal communications and streamline workflows across your organisation with Wematch’s Sales to Trader solution.
  • Connect diverse global teams, such as treasury, equities, and collateral, to optimise costs, improve return on equity, and reduce capital costs through our internal marketplace.
  • Enhance operational efficiency with simple, flexible API connectivity that integrates seamlessly with your existing systems.
