From source to sky - built to scale

The world must capture gigatons of CO₂ - from industrial emissions to direct air. Yet, existing solutions face prohibitive energy penalties at low concentrations (0–5%).

While other carbon capture solutions struggle to adapt high-concentration technologies to diluted sources, RepAir’s solid-state, electrochemical carbon capture technology is purpose-built to capture these emissions, breaking through the energy and cost barriers that limit current methods.

RepAir’s modular technology consumes 70% less energy while producing 98–99% pure CO₂ for permanent storage or utilization, deployable for both point-source and direct air capture.
