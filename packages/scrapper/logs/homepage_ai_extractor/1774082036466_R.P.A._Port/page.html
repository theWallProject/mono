            SPECIAL EXPERTISE IN THE CIS

            R.P.A. Port Ltd has been one of the foremost logistics companies operating within the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) since 1991. We handle any size project, including out-of-gauge and oversized equipment, bulk vessels and block trains. From freight forwarding and customs brokerage to logistics operation by rail, truck, air or multimodal transportation, R.P.A. Port Ltd offers door-to-door service between the innermost former Soviet Union countries and the rest of the world.

            R.P.A. Port Ltd specializes in high quality customs clearance. With three generations of experience in dealing with customs authorities, we provide solutions that enable the swift and easy passage of all cargo types.

            INTERNATIONAL FORWARDING

            R.P.A. Port Ltd ensures the safe, smooth and fast delivery of cargo anywhere in the world and finds creative solutions for each project’s specific needs. We handle shipments of every imaginable type of cargo, using standard and multimodal transport by air, sea, road and rail. Utilizing only best-of-breed carriers, and including FCL, LCL, FTL, LTL and bulk, we create tailor-made solutions to answer every shipment need.

            WAREHOUSING

            R.P.A. Port Ltd provides simplified solutions to storing shipments prior to and following transport. Our close connections to warehouse providers, throughout Israel and around the world, enable us to offer bonded and non-bonded warehouse services, both before and after customs clearance

            CONSULTATION

            R.P.A. Port Ltd is pleased to provide expert consultation regarding the myriad issues that arise in regards to international shipping and customs clearance.             SPECIALIZED CARGO

            R.P.A. Port offers point-to-point solutions for cargo of every imaginable type, weight and size, to and from locations throughout the world. Our tailor-made solutions ensure that your cargo reaches its destination safely and securely, including: out of gauge, oversized and overweight cargo, perishables that require temperature control and special containerized solutions, hazardous cargo, ammunitions, live animals and more.
