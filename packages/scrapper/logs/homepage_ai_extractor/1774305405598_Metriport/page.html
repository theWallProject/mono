Who can use Metriport?

Metriport currently serves organizations with a valid Treatment purpose of use under HIPAA. Only provider organizations with a valid NPI number, or healthcare IT vendors who sell to providers, are permitted to exchange data on the networks that Metriport participates in. If you're curious to know where your use case falls under, read this federal guide on Treatment-based exchange.
