Welcome to Mostech Ltd.

Mostech Ltd. is a trading company specializing in electronic components and sub-systems.
Founded in 1990, we are strategically located in the heart of Israel’s high-tech trading area, enabling us to provide exceptional service and efficient delivery to our customers.
We are ISO 9001 certified and proud members of the Israeli Chamber of Commerce.
Our mission is to deliver high-quality products with the shortest possible lead times, based on a seamless online interface between our customers and suppliers.
To achieve this, we maintain a permanent inventory in our warehouse and offer a wide range of custom-designed products.
Our carefully selected manufacturers are chosen for their competitive pricing, strong technical support, fast and reliable delivery, and deep understanding of the Israeli market’s unique requirements.
For more information, feel free to explore our suppliers’ websites by clicking on their logos, or contact us directly – we’re happy to assist you.
