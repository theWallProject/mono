EMS-PLUS: The Future of EMS is Here!

EMS-Plus: Redefining the Future of Electronics Manufacturing

Welcome to AY Group, founded in 1980 by Arik Rod and now led by his sons, Yaniv and Itay Rod. We specialize in supply chain management, from product development to serial production, focusing on customer needs, operational excellence, and strong engineering expertise.

AY Group is involved in over 100 projects across defense, medical, IoT, and HLS sectors. Our EMS-Plus services go beyond traditional EMS, integrating advanced resource planning (RAP) and leveraging our recent acquisitions—ShiraTech and ZT Technologies. This allows us to deliver AI-driven data analysis software, predictive monitoring, reballing, RMA, and turnkey manufacturing solutions.

Our commitment: comprehensive support, tailored solutions, and quality at every stage of production. Discover EMS-Plus, your partner for evolving market needs.

Our Vision

At AY Group, we aim to redefine global standards in EMS by producing high-end products and building strategic partnerships in component procurement, distribution, engineering support, global logistics, and resource planning (RAP). Our approach emphasizes precision, quality, and efficiency through JIT, one-piece flow, and OTD methodologies.

By leveraging cutting-edge technology and strong partnerships, we empower our clients to gain a competitive edge, driving mutual success across diverse industries.
