Frequently asked questions
What is Launchese?
Launchese is a service provider that works with entrepreneurs from all over the world. We specialise in solutions for businesses like yours that can’t access the best financial tools and scale their businesses because of where you live.

We solve these problems by moving your startup to the UK. This involves setting up a UK business entity, helping you find and introducing you to the right payment gateway, business banks and funding services.

We are different because we are online entrepreneurs and we know the problems you face.
Do I need to visit the UK to open a company?
No, you do not need to visit the UK to open a company here. Any “real person” who is at least 16 years of age from almost any country is eligible to apply. Launchese is fully integrated with the Companies House and will handle all the paperwork. Your company will be established from the comfort of your home.
Do I need to visit the UK to open a bank account?
Traditional Business Bank accounts are often not easy to access. High street banks like Barclays, HSBC, etc. may request that you visit a branch directly in the UK or in your country.

If this is not possible, Launchese is partnered with several online banks that understand the nature of our customers' businesses. These banks will work with non-resident company directors and generally only require two types of verification - proof of identity and proof of address.

Some online banks may require that you have a residence in the UK or EU, however, Launchese will always be here to offer you the best possible solution for your business needs.
Why should I start a company in the UK?
The Process is Very Fast
In some parts of the world, it can take weeks or months to set up a new company. With Launchese, we aim to set up your new UK Company within 2 business days so you can start trading right away.


Professional Image
Your new UK business will have a prestigious London address to use on your website, email signature and other company documents. This helps to build customer trust in your online business.


Less Government Bureaucracy
We are very experienced in dealing with the UK Government and have found that they make it easy to register a new company.


Lower Taxes
There are two certainties in life - Death and Taxes. So if you’re going to pay tax then it may as well be as little as possible, right? In the UK, you will only pay 19% tax on your profit.*


Online Shopping in The UK is #1
The online shopping industry in Europe is huge, but the United Kingdom is by far the largest and most developed when it comes to online shopping in Europe.
Why should I choose Launchese over other services?
Launchese is the only startup-friendly company on the market that provides more than a formation service for non-UK residents. Most formation services in the UK help you create your company and that is it. Launchese has years of experience and offers tailor-made consultancy services about payment gateways and business bank accounts.

Most of our competitors will send you an affiliate link to banks and payment gateways and if you have any problems they don’t offer support. What Launchese does is walk you through every process to reduce the amount of risk you take and support you even after a successful launch. Launchese has been created by seasoned e-commerce and online entrepreneurs and we know the problems you are facing and the right solution.

We offer support for the life-time of your UK company. You can count on us to provide you with a full start to finish service.
What about taxes?
Limited companies are charged a flat rate of 19% corporation tax on all taxable income (generally net profit). You may also be liable for VAT depending on the nature of your business. You will be responsible for ensuring that your UK entity complies with all tax regulations and files and pays company tax returns on time.

Launchese has full Bookkeeping and Accounting services available to help you understand your obligations and prepare your books and taxes.
Can I receive funding for my UK company?
There are many financial supports available to UK startups. After running your company for a minimum of 6 months, Launchese can help you to get Corporate Credit Cards, 0 equity financing for your stock or marketing or even investment. Even if you are a non-UK resident. Launchese works very closely with many financial institutions, Fintechs, Startup Clubs and Venture Capitalists. We can help you to get your business to the next level with funding.
Who are your partners?
Launchese works with the best financial institutions and tools in the world that are startup-friendly. We work with the companies we believe will provide benefits to our customers after testing their services ourselves.

We work with Payoneer, Tide, Wise, Stripe, Paypal, Jeeves, ClearCo, Virgin Startups, Xero, Juni, AWS, AOP+, LightFunnels and more.
Can I set up a company in the US with Launchese?
Launchese can help you set up your US company in Delaware and Wyoming to start your business in the US. Check here for more info: https://www.launchese.com/us-formation
Can I open Stripe and Paypal with my UK company?
When you have your LTD company in the UK (Which Launchese can provide), you can apply to any payment gateways, including Stripe and Paypal. You can do it by yourself with our Start-Up Package. If you want us to give you consultancy for Stripe or Paypal, you can choose the Growth or Advance package of Launchese. We will provide you with all the necessary information, and documents will be requested by payment gateways.

Most of our users can get a Stripe and Paypal account. But those companies have their own terms and conditions to accept new clients. Some certain businesses won’t be accepted by those companies. You can find the list on their website: https://stripe.com/en-gb/restricted-businesses
