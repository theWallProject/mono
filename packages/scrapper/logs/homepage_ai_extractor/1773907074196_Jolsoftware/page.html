OUR GROUP

One of the key advantages of our company is an integrated approach to the creation of IT products. Thanks to an ecosystem of marketing agencies, a venture studio and our own startups, we focus on launching turnkey projects. The first step is to conduct an in-depth analysis of the target market - we study consumer demand, the competitive environment, and monetization opportunities.

Then we create a unique selling proposition and develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with key features for early users. At the same time, we are prototyping designs, creating a brand and preparing a marketing strategy. Here we rely on the experience of our digital agencies.

After launching the MVP, we quickly iterate – finalizing the functionality, optimizing the UX and UI, and building a funnel for attracting users. Great emphasis is placed on content marketing and promotion on social networks. The venture studio also helps to attract the first investors in the early stages for scaling.

This full development cycle allows us to bring truly competitive IT products to the market. We are open to partnership!
