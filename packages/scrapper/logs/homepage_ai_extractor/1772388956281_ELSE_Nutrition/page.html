    Organic Tapioca

    Vitamin A

    Vitamin A

    Vitamin A is a powerful nutrient for your child’s health and development. Essential for vision, it helps babies and kids see better in low light and supports eye health as they grow. Vitamin A also plays a key role in strengthening the immune system, helping little ones ward off common illnesses. Plus, it’s vital for healthy skin and cell growth.

    Learn more

    Iron

    Iron

    Iron plays a key role in supporting the healthy development of babies and toddlers. It promotes the formation of red blood cells, which are essential for carrying oxygen throughout the body, boosting energy, and supporting brain development. Adequate iron intake is especially important in early childhood to prevent iron-deficiency anemia and support cognitive and immune function during growth.

    Learn more

    Riboflavin

    Riboflavin

    Riboflavin, also known as Vitamin B2, is a powerhouse ingredient in our formulas, supporting essential functions in your little one’s growth and development. This important vitamin helps convert food into energy, fueling active bodies and busy minds. It also plays a key role in maintaining healthy skin, eyes, and nervous system development. Since children’s bodies can’t store riboflavin, getting enough from a balanced diet is essential.

    Learn more
