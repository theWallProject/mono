Vectored has developed a people-centric, ubicomp technology that creates the Autonomous Room - AKA the "driverless"- Home or Office.﻿

 

Our inexpensive ﻿stationary room-robot i-Botler™ automatically provides room-occupants with optimal or individually-tailored, “learned”, services by surrounding them in safe, comfortable and energy-saving micro-environments. Using the latest advances in AI and machine-learning, the patented, hands-free, Virtual/Physical Assistant automatically detects a person's identity, their location, what activity they are performing, physiological information etc., and then automatically provides each room-resident with their detected needs on a personalized basis. – Furthering tech beyond Amazon's-Alexa® - you don’t even need to ask.﻿

Vectored is an artificial intelligence robotics startup; and while we have a big vision, that of replacing the waning smartphone platform with our ambient-intelligence EveryWare tech, we’re beginning in health-tech with a small, but high impact life-saving emergency alert system for the elderly.

Our team is passionate about bringing the autonomous-room’s™ socio-economic benefits from elderly well-being to sustainability to millions of users world over. The autonomous-room™ uses AI and machine learning in a robotic docking station for people-centric sensors, devices and appliances. It is capable of detecting each individual room occupant’s needs and automatically providing those needs on a “learned” personalized basis; – the way they like it and there is no need to ask. Our advanced sensing and "digital" delivery tech creates a more comfortable, yet energy saving, micro-environment around the user; similar to the personalization experienced in an airplane seat﻿.
