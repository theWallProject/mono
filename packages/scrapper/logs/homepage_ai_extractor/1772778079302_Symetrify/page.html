Chronic low back pain (CLBP) is often rooted in structural misalignment and postural dysfunction within the lumbo-pelvic region—the lower portion of the spine responsible for load transfer and stability. Asymmetries at the Lumbo-Pelvic Load Distribution Axis (LDA) can disrupt neuromechanical balance, altering load distribution and placing excessive strain on adjacent musculoskeletal structures, ultimately contributing to pain and long-term biomechanical deterioration.

The L-PACT introduces a novel approach to restoring proper load distribution across the lumbo-pelvic structures. First, utilizing AI-driven sensing and automated counter-compressive force application, it assesses dynamic postural imbalances during movement. Then, through brief, walk-based “train-to-treat” sessions, the system applies targeted counter-pressure to promote neuromuscular adaptation and brain retraining—supporting long-term postural correction and the prevention of chronic low back pain (CLBP).

About Us

Symetrify aims to establish a new standard of care in the assessment and improvement of neuro-musculoskeletal health.

In a proof-of-concept study involving 100 patients, 98% demonstrated measurable improvement, with 42% showing progress after just a single session.

Symetrify’s go-to-market strategy begins with the $34 billion U.S. physical therapy and chiropractic market, followed by entry into the sports, fitness, and workplace wellness sectors.

Symetrify’s proprietary technology platform can also be applied to numerous neuromuscular conditions, including neck pain, shoulder pain, and sports injuries, all multi-billion-dollar market opportunities.

The company is collaborating with eHealth Ventures, a digital health incubator providing access to world-class clinical, operational and business networks.
