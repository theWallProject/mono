PLAN BEE

The existing method of honey production endangers bees and results in escalating prices. Similarly, the honey produced may contain toxins, pesticides, and antibiotics. Bee-io introduces a ground-breaking, bee-free honey production technology. Utilizing novel, patent pending bio processes, Bee-io enables year-round supply of reasonably priced honey - allowing the bees to resume their traditional role of pollinating.

To Bee or
Not to Be

Bees are the world’s major pollinator, playing an active part in the production of our nutrition.
Yet, commercial beekeeping methods along with climate change, diminishing habitat, and exposure to pesticides, have made bees highly sensitive to parasites and viruses.

As food security and our global footprint are now matters of universal discussion, it’s time to recalculate the honey route.

Pollinators at risk

Local pollinators species are now at risk of extinction due to honeybee aggressiveness coupled with air, soil, and water pollution.

Ecological damage

Reduction in local pollinators species, leads to serious negative impacts on biodiversity, and to widespread ecological damage.

Limited honey production

Mass disappearance of bees soon follows due to multiple causes including deadly pathogens, parasites, chemical pesticides, herbicides, and global warming, which dramatically limits global honey production capacity.

The Bee-io Technology

The traditional honey production process is time-consuming, highly sensitive, depends on many unexpected factors, and has not been changed for decades. It is a process in which the use of natural nectar substitutes is a widely accepted practice, yielding low quality honey.
