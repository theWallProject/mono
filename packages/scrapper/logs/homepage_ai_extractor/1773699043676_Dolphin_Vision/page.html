At the beginning of 2020, we were among the first to recognise that COVID-19 spread via airborne particles. Later, we discovered that this is a common mechanism for almost all aerobic diseases such as influenza, Avian Flu/Bird Flu, Mpox and Tuberculosis.
After several years of research and development, we came to the ultimate solution, which we called Arothera.
Arothera prevents people from becoming infected with the above-mentioned diseases, but makes it a totally enjoyable and easy adventure through aromatherapy. 
We managed to break the stigma that the war against infections is really a war with quarantines, protective suits and a long list of prohibitions.
Instead, we use natural plant products that are beneficial to humans, but in a certain combination, destroy pathogens in the air.

Be healthy through joy, pleasant smells and natural oils!
