    ESA

    Get SAT expands its satellite communication solutions and offers new capabilities for the changing demands of the market. ESA (electronically steerable antenna) is a family of products that includes a fully electronic antenna, with no moving parts, that is low profile, lightweight, and provides reliable communication. With ESA, no calibration is required therefore providing long-term reliability and lower maintenance costs. The ESA family has options for both the Ka and KU band and L band and is optimized for connectivity in the Air, Land, or Sea. The ESA antennas provide government and commercial users with a reliable, high-performance, low-power communication solution without compromising bandwidth. Get SAT’s phased array antennas are designed for GEO/MEO/LEO constellations to enable a switch from GEO to MEO satellites in milliseconds.
