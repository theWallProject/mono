About us

The Call4life team has created an Electronic Doctor to help people in the most accessible and effective way. We have managed to combine the simplicity and accessibility of conventional screening tests with a large scientific base of European and American specialized associations.

By using artificial intelligence-based technologies and processing large amounts of data, our Electronic Doctors are able to determine the risks of certain diseases, make recommendations on clarifying the client's condition, and refer them to the right specialists for diagnosis.

Our use cases

  • Insurance companies

    Call4life's Electronic Doctors allow for the assessment of a patient's health condition to make a service price offer.

  • Clinics and hospitals

    Call4life solutions save staff time in selecting a doctor's specialty, initial consultations, directing for tests, and monitoring patients' well-being.

  • Financial organizations

    Electronic Doctors are an excellent VAS that can increase the LTV of your clients.
