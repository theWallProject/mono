    Ease the Flow with Minimally Invasive Nasal Trabeculostomy

    Ease the Flow

    MINT® Creates five drainage pathways directly within the trabecular meshwork. These pathways are designed to form an opening in the trabecular meshwork tissue, facilitating the flow of fluid from the anterior chamber to Schlemm's Canal.

    The procedure can be performed either as a standalone treatment or in conjunction with cataract surgery.

    Following MINT® surgery, post-operative complications are expected to be minimal and at a lower incidence.

    The Procedure at a Glance

    • 1. Preparation:

      Make a Corneal Incision, inject OVD and Insert the MINT® Surgical Device (Tool) into the Anterior Chamber

    • 2. Position

      Position the MINT® surgical device tip on the pigmented (functional) trabeculum band using a gonioscope

    • 3. Press

      Press the pedal to create a trabeculostomy opening in the pigmented Trabecular Meshwork

    • 4. Perform

      Perform four (4) additional openings at different sites in the nasal pigmented (functional) Trabecular Meshwork
