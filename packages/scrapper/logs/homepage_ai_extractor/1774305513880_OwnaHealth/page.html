What We Do

Transforming Chronic Care

Every day, patients across the country are breaking free from diabetes, obesity, hypertension, and depression — not with more medications, but with a personalized, high-touch, food-first program that works.

OwnaHealth is a clinically-proven, tech-enabled service that combines medical supervision, one-on-one coaching, continuous health tracking, and therapeutic nutrition to drive real, lasting results — even in the hardest-to-treat populations.

Keeping your health safe and secured

We Treat The Root Cause

Most chronic conditions stem from the same thing: what you eat. Our nutritional approach helps you reverse diabetes, reduce medications, and regain energy — without calorie counting and without being hungry.

Always here to protect

We Show Up For You

Our patients are never alone. You are paired with a dedicated Owna medical provider and a health coach or dietitian. You have daily access to your clinical team via the OwnaHealth app. You meet with them periodically using a video meeting. Whether it’s a tough day or a win worth celebrating, we’re just a message away.
