Welcome to HMC

Herzliya Medical Center is one of Israel’s leading private hospitals, dedicated to providing uncompromising excellence and the most advanced medical care available.
Each year, we perform tens of thousands of surgeries, consultations, diagnostic tests, imaging procedures, and treatments across a wide range of specialties, including neurosurgery, orthopedics, urology, general surgery, oncological surgery, ENT, in vitro fertilization, gastroenterology, and more.
Our team of expert physicians consists of hundreds of internationally recognized senior doctors, all of whom are constantly updated on the latest medical, scientific, and technological advancements.
Patients at Herzliya Medical Center benefit from state-of-the-art operating rooms, five luxurious and comfortable inpatient departments, and well-equipped recovery rooms. Throughout their stay, they receive personalized care and continuous monitoring from our dedicated medical, nursing, and administrative teams.
