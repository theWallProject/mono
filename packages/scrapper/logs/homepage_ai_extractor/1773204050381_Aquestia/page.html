Committed to Our Planet

In the last 50 years, demand for water in the urban, domestic, agricultural, and industrial sectors has more than tripled, outpacing population growth and exacerbating the impact of climate change on the world’s natural water resources.

In the face of a real risk of water scarcity, Aquestia is on a mission to create innovative, sustainable water management solutions that ensure consistent and efficient supply, while promoting responsible resource management across the board. From energy savings to safeguarding water resources, enabling a global water supply, and protecting systems and people from fires, sustainability guides every aspect of our work.
