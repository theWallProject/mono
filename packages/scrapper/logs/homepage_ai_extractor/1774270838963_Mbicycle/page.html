ShotTracker: Real-time Sports Data Tracking App

This project is for a well-known sports data-as-a-service provider for basketball associations, that operates primarily in the United States and today cooperates with a variety of universities, NABC coaches, and professional teams.

Our team made a sports analytics solution gathering real-time data on a variety of sports metrics via BLE-based sensors installed in athletes’ garments and around the court. This data stores and synchronizes in the cloud for further processing by athletes, coaches, team managers, betting companies, broadcasters, and other stakeholders.

MyTable: Mobile Apps for Experiential Food Delivery

MyTable is an Uber-like solution with a game-changing approach to food delivery services. The main idea of the project was to bring together professional chefs and people who wanted to get restaurant-grade food prepared at home for a reasonable price.

During the development process, Mbicycle’s experts defined the app user roles, added tools to edit role profiles and make in-app secure payments, and provided more features to matchmake professional chefs and at-home diners.

Who We Are

Specialists from Mbicycle are software development outsourcing enthusiasts working hard to deliver powerful software solutions. We founded Mbicycle in 2016 to help startups and SMEs bring their ambitious ideas to life.

Over the years, our professional software development company has accumulated skills and experience by providing robust custom software development services well-adjusted for various business verticals worldwide.

In late 2023 we became a part of Noventiq — a leading global solutions and services provider in digital transformation and cybersecurity, headquartered and listed in London.

Since 2023, Mbicycle has been operating under the legal entity Soft Development PL sp. z o.o., a strategic evolution that underscores our commitment to growth and excellence in the software development industry. This transition has not only reinforced our operational framework but also aligned our endeavors with broader business objectives, enabling us to deliver even more comprehensive and innovative solutions.
