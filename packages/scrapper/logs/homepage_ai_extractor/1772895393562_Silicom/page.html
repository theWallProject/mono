Silicom Connectivity Solutions

What We Do

Silicom Ltd. is the go-to firm for OEMs looking for a competitive edge. Silicom’s advanced multi-port/bypass adapters, smart adapters and appliance platforms increase throughput, reduce latency, and boost the performance of Cyber Security, Network Monitoring and Analytics, Traffic Management, Application Delivery, WAN Optimization, High Frequency Trading and other applications for the data center and enterprise networks. Our innovative solutions for high-density networking, high-speed fabric switching, offloading and acceleration, which utilize a range of cutting-edge silicon technologies as well as FPGA-based solutions, are ideal for scaling-up and scaling-out cloud infrastructures.

With over 300 products and infinite design capabilities, Silicom solutions solve some of the biggest connectivity bottlenecks for major players in virtualized cloud, big data, Internet of Things (IoT), and Network Function Virtualization (NFV) / Software Defined Networking (SDN) / SD-WAN environments. Our products are used by major Cloud players, service providers, telcos and OEMs as components of their infrastructure offerings, including both add-on adapters in the Data Center and stand-alone virtualized/universal CPE devices at the edge.
