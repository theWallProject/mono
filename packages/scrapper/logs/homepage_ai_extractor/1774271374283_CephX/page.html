Automated and Instant

CephX uses artificial intelligence (AI) technologies to automate diagnostic and analytical dental imaging tasks, such as cephalometric analyses, teeth segmentation and airway volume.

CephX is designed for practitioners of all experience levels and skill sets — providing the tools you need to increase case acceptance, boost productivity and save time.

Cephalometric analysis

Performed on digital 2D Ceph and 3D CBCT volumes

CephX provides instant cephalometric tracing and analysis to streamline the orthodontic pre-treatment process. Upload a 2D ceph or a 3D volume, and our AI will automatically construct a perfect ceph for tracing, giving you a complete analysis in seconds

Learn More

Teeth segmentation

3D diagnosis validation and treatment planning tool

CephX’s AI segmentation technology enables you to validate patient anatomy in 3D, export segmented DICOM files for case planning, and gain unmatched insight into bone and root positioning. Segment bone, individual teeth, and nerves on CBCT to make your treatment planning process faster and more accurate

Learn More

Airway volume analysis

From 3D volume CBCT data

CephX offers instant airway analysis services based on x-ray and CBCT imaging. DICOMs and 2D cephs uploaded for segmentation or cephalometric analysis are automatically analyzed for airway measurements and volume

Learn More

Segmentation of Intraoral Scans (IOS)

Advanced IOS management and editing tool

CephX introduces automated AI segmentation and editing for Intraoral Scans (IOS), enabling seamless integration into your workflow. Upload your IOS data, and our AI will segment and identify gingival margins automatically, streamlining your diagnostic and treatment planning processes.

Stitching of IOS and CBCT

Enhanced diagnosis with IOS and CBCT data integration

CephX’s IOS and CBCT stitching feature combines the accuracy of the IOS with the anatomic structures of the CBCT, for a comprehensive 3D view of the maxillofacial and dental structures. This integration ensures detailed and accurate diagnostic insights, precise treatment planning,  and improved patient outcomes.

An Advanced AI DICOM Viewer

Web-based DICOM viewing and annotation tool

CephX offers a web-based DICOM viewer and annotator, allowing you to access and interact with your DICOM files directly from your browser. Annotate images, highlight areas of interest, and collaborate with colleagues in real-time, enhancing the efficiency and accuracy of your diagnostic processes.

AI-based image gallery

Smart image management and analysis

CephX’s AI-based orthodontic image gallery leverages artificial intelligence to organize and analyze your patient’s images. Automatically sort, categorize, and highlight key features within your image library, making it easier to manage and utilize your visual data for diagnostics and treatment planning.
