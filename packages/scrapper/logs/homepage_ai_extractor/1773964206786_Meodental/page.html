100% Ecological - 100% Effective - Patented Formula


As a Dental Professional the challenge of teeth whitening procedures is to produce visible and lasting results for your patients through procedures that are safe, non-toxic and non-painful while still protecting and maintaining the integrity of the dentine and enamel.
