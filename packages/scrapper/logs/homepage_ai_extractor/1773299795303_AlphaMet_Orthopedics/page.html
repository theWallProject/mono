ABOUT US

AlphaMet Ltd. develops, manufactures, and markets the "CyclaPlex Correction System", an innovative orthopedic implants and unique surgery instruments to perform minimal invasive Hallux Valgus deformity correction. The company product received recently FDA 510K and is cleared to market the device in the US.

The company is located in the Bar Lev High Tech park in the northern part of Israel and distributes its innovative device in the US by Genesis Ortho Partners

CyclaPlex – innovative implant advancing Hallux Valgus surgery

CyclaPlex Correction System is a newly introduced implant device and surgical instruments intended for Hallux Valgus deformity correction. The CyclaPlex procedure enables deformity correction without the need to perform bone osteotomy.

As a result, post op healing and recovery are prompt. Immediate weight bearing enables patient to prompt back to normal life!
