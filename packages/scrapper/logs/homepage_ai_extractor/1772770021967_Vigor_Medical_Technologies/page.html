VIGOR Medical Technologies Ltd. at the forefront of developing life-saving devices and AI-driven solutions for chest trauma and thoracic/abdominal drainage. With over 20 million global chest trauma cases annually, it is a leading cause of accidental death (WHO). VIGOR’s C-Lant, a safe, quick and easy-to-use self-fixation device, prevents severe complications by ensuring immediate identification, treatment, and effective management. Our innovative technology sets a new standard for safety and efficiency in trauma care across medical settings.
