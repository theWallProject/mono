UrbanChange Protocol

The protocol designed to power real live communities approached us to provide the Discord infrastructure and in-depth blog content to support their “Local Hero” initiative. We organized and implemented a new structure on their Discord server, guiding the member experience with pinned content and featuring the articles we wrote to provide an in-depth perspective.

Kana Labs

Kana Labs reached out to rebrand their product shifting the website focus from their home-built web3 middleware to the trading platform and developer tools built on top of that middleware. We created a new website from scratch to embody their new marketing vision and drive awareness of their products.

Blue Kraken Online

BKO is an MMORPG Play and Earn game connected to a sustainable, inflation-proof ecosystem in which the meta-game world is connected in meaningful ways to the real world, designed to immerse players in an experience that constantly recycles value into the ecosystem. We created and designed their pitch deck.

Magic Square
