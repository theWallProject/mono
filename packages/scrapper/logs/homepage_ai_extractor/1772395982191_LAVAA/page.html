FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Explore how Lavaa Health helps healthcare teams work smarter, prevent hospitalizations, and improve outcomes with AI-powered tools.
What is Lavaa Health and how does it work?

Lavaa Health is an AI-powered clinical intelligence platform that connects directly to your EMR to deliver real-time insights. It identifies patient risks, care gaps, and billing opportunities, helping providers improve outcomes while maximizing operational and financial performance.

How does Lavaa help reduce hospital readmissions?

Lavaa's personalized care engines continuously monitor patients across chronic disease areas and flag early signs of deterioration. It empowers care teams to take preventive action before conditions escalate.

Is Lavaa Health secure and HIPAA-compliant?

Yes. Lavaa is fully compliant with HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, and ISO 27701. It uses encrypted data, role-based access, JWT authentication, and continuous security monitoring through Drata.

Can Lavaa integrate with our current EMR system?

Absolutely. Lavaa integrates directly into existing EMRs using secure APIs, making adoption seamless without disrupting existing workflows.

What types of organizations benefit from Lavaa?

Lavaa supports hospitals, primary care providers, SNFs, MSOs, ACOs, and payers—any organization focused on value-based care, chronic care management, or clinical trial recruitment.
