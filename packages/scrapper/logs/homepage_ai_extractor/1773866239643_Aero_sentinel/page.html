Payloads Options

Mission-Driven
Payload

Sentinel drones support a range of combat-ready EO/IR payloads, optimized for situational awareness, target tracking, and real-time intelligence gathering. From the ultra-light Dror camera-ideal for long-range reconnaissance without compromising agility-to the compact yet powerful Snunit, offering x26 zoom and triple-axis stabilization in under 200 grams.

For extended missions demanding superior optics, the Kfir turret delivers exceptional clarity and precision with a total x40 zoom and HD thermal resolution. All payloads are hot-swappable, stabilized, and engineered for low weight, rapid deployment, and operational flexibility across mission profiles.
