our technology

The Future of AI & Visual Data

Transform unstructured visual data into actionable insights.

Visual Layer's cutting-edge technology is powered by a highly efficient, CPU-only graph engine that uncovers hidden connections within visual data. Our engine processes images, videos, objects and semantic concepts, exposing intricate relationships across datasets of any size, from gigabytes to petabytes.

Data is first embedded into a multimodal vector space, and our unsupervised C++ engine constructs a comprehensive graph called the VL Index, exposing intricate connections across the dataset without requiring external metadata.
