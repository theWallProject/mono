IoTM Adds Support For T-Mobile USA

Revolutionizing IoT Connectivity Management: IoTM announces integration with T-Mobile

September 19, 2023 – IoTM Solutions, a world leading provider of IoT connectivity management solutions, today announces a new wholesale integration enabling T-Mobile’s IoT customers to access IoTM’s full portfolio of analytics-driven connectivity management services.

Dan Amir, IoTM CEO commented, “I am really excited about the integration with T-Mobile. Together we can enable T-Mobile IoT customers to gain a competitive edge around connectivity management, life cycle management, and automation in their business processes. Pursuing our joint value creation strategy will enable us to dominate the IoT industry and bring best in class networks and platforms together for large scale clients.”

 

“Managing global connectivity for scaled IoT deployments creates operational challenges and opportunities for OEMs and IoT solution providers. Together with IoTM, we unlock value-added services that allow our IoT customers to level up their product offerings and gain a competitive edge in servicing their clients,” said Dan Thygesen, Senior Vice President of T-Mobile Wholesale and head of T-Mobile’s growing wholesale business. “IoTM’s single pane of glass integrated with T-Mobile’s robust connectivity & network APIs are a force multiplier for any IoT company looking to scale.”

 

Customers exclusively utilizing T-Mobile’s native IoT platforms or leveraging multiple connectivity management platforms (CMPs) for mixed SIM deployments can now onboard and orchestrate seamlessly through IoTM’s single pane of glass product. IoTM’s Federated Connectivity Management product (GUI & API set) supports over 100 Mobile Network Operators (MNOs) out-of-the-box and provides unique critical functionalities, advanced SIM management capabilities, and a harmonized operational experience across disparate connectivity management platforms.
