​Who We Are and What We Offer

​Israel Startup Network (ISN) was established in 2008 by Arlene Marom, the Director of Tech River - a company that provides Marketing Communications and Business Development to Israeli high-tech and defense companies. She organizes the group's activities together with David Vaisman and Ori Ainy, Founder of Beam Global - a company that helps B2B software and SaaS startups secure their first international customers and channel partners. ISN is an informal, rapidly growing community of 6300+ startup entrepreneurs, as well as a variety of service providers and investors. 

​Among our activities are high-quality informative events that feature speakers and panels on topics of relevance to startups ‒ as well as extensive networking opportunities. We can organize pitchfests for investors from abroad and special events for visiting experts.

In addition to events, members are notified here, as well as via email and Meetup (https://www.meetup.com/IsraelStartupNetwork), regarding various opportunities ‒ including relevant exhibitions, competitions, pitchfests, webinars, investors seeking startups and distributors seeking representations.

We write blog posts about our members, - both startups and service providers - free of charge. Feel free to request our Questionnaire in order to be featured on our blog.
Our goal is to present you with as much important and relevant information as possible. A number of top professionals have graciously agreed to share their expertise with us. Please visit our Resources page for links to a wide range of informative articles. Our Blog is filled with important information from international experts as well as introductions to the Israeli high-tech ecosystem's best Service Providers - and we are always happy to feature startup companies. We have also gathered a selection of Special Offers to meet your various needs as you travel along your journey.
