About AVT

We Are Print Inspection. For over 30 years, we’ve focused on what we do best-delivering cutting-edge print inspection solutions for flexible packaging and label printers.

Backed by industry-leading innovation, patents, and awards, our technology ensures flawless quality, efficiency, and cost savings.
Now operating independently, we remain dedicated to advancing print inspection and empowering our customers with precision, automation, and expert-driven solutions.
