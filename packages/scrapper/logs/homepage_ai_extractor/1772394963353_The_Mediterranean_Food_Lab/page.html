FLAVOR CRAFTING: THE POWER OF SOLID-STATE FERMENTATION

Through our unique fermentation process, proteins are transformed into savory peptides and amino acids, carbohydrates are converted into various sugars, and fats are broken down into flavorful fatty acids. The result is a blend of flavor-packed metabolites.​​

Our Story

THE JOURNEY TO CULINARITY AT SCALE

From our Bistro to Breakthrough

MFL was founded by three cooks and a filmmaker, brought together by their obsession with deliciousness, commitment to sustainability, and love for real food. At their bistro, “Brut,” inspired by French and Northern Italian cooking, they served hearty, meat-centered dishes.

Frustrated by the lack of depth in meatless menu options, our co-founders set out to recreate that same richness using fermentation of plant-based, natural foods to unlock the depth of flavor found in meat. After years of study, the team discovered the power of Solid-State Fermentation.

Real Food Flavor Revolution

We knew that capturing such complex flavors was only possible with the resources of a fine-dining restaurant. We wanted to change that. We set out on a mission to become the first Real Food Flavor (RFF) company to bring culinarity at scale.
