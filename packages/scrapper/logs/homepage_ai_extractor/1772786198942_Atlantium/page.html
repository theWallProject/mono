    Purestream™ BWMS Successfully Completes All Testing for IMO and USCG Type Approval

    Atlantium Marine, a subsidiary of Atlantium Technologies, announces that it has successfully completed all testing for IMO and USCG type approval for its proprietary UV-based ballast water management system (BWMS), Purestream™ under the independent laboratory of Lloyds Register, and testing facility of NIVA and FORCE.

    • World’s First Lumpfish Breeding Plant Uses Hydro-Optic™ UV for Water Biosecurity

      The world’s first breeding plant for lumpfish opened at Namdal Rensefisk AS in Flatanger, Norway, on March 27, 2019. Atlantium Technologies’ Hydro-Optic™ (HOD) UV was chosen as the primary disinfection technology at the breeding plant.

    • Ontario Power Generation Controls Invasive Mussels Using Using Hydro-Optic™UV

      The Ontario Power Generation (OPG) DeCew II Generating Station, a hydroelectric generating station, undertook an evaluation of innovative and cost-effective methods to control invasive mussels with the use of MP UV light.

    • Baseload Power Station Installs Hydro-Optic™ UV to Meet Boiler Make Up Water Dechlorination Needs

      With HOD™ (Hydro-Optic Disinfection) UV, a North American baseload generation facility will achieve effective dechlorination treatment of its boiler make up water, with using lights to protect its ion exchange demineralization process.
