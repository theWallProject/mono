About Us

Who We Are

Yad Hanadiv carries forward the long-standing philanthropic tradition of the Rothschild family in Israel, in pursuit of a better future for all its inhabitants. A Board of Trustees, composed principally of family members, governs the Foundation – with the current Chair representing its fifth generation of leadership.
