About Us

Above-IT international technologies was established in order to better monitor vendor’s programs, as well as to be able to increase profitability for ISP’s, IT Distributors & System Integrators.

Our vision is to become the leading worldwide partner program management platform, of choice, for our customers.

 

After five years of R&D, we developed “Rebates-On” a SAAS based solution platform that enables you to manage multi-vendor Programs and Rebates in a single integrated secured system.

 

Our system is a simple and “easy to use” tool that makes program management easier and enables to manage multiple vendors’ programs (such as HPE, DELL-EMC, IBM, CISCO, JUNIPER, HPinc, MICROSOFT, and others).

We invite you to use Rebates-On system and increase your captured incentives!

OUR SOLUTION

THE SERVICE WE PROVIDE

Rebates-ON is a Business intelligence tool, that consolidates multi vendor programs into a single dashboard. Our system is custom made for every customer within his Programs & Certifications levels.
Using Rebates-ON, our customers can easily manage their vendors’ programs, increase cash rebates and improve their profitability.
During the past few years, the profitability of Systems Integrators was being worn down. Therefore, there is a substantial need to carefully manage the incentive programs given by the vendor’s that offers significant bonuses and cash rebates associated with the selling of different types of solutions and products.

Leading IT vendors offer variable channel programs such as:

  • HP Inc – Partner First Program, MDF founds, specializations, payments
  • HP Enterprise- Partner Ready program, MDF founds, specializations, payments
  • IBM – Business Partners Incentives Program
  • CISCO – VIP Programs, Partner Plus, CSPP
  • DELL-EMC –Partner Incentive Program, MDF founds, competencies
  • VMware – Solution Rewards payments
  • Juniper – Juniper Partner Advantage
  • Vendors Distributor partner solution
  • Multi Region Vendor’s program Solution
  • And more…

ACCELERATE YOUR REBATES

THE BENEFITS

Since the average bonuses for the above mentioned programs range from 1% to 12% and sometimes even more, a Systems Integrator that runs the vendors’ programs properly can maximize their rebates and accumulate bonuses of hundreds of thousands of dollars per year.
The main challenge for a System Integrator, ISP or IT Distributor is to wisely choose the programs to specialize in, and thereafter to smartly lead the vendors’ business and sales markets accordingly. Rebates-ON’s execution capabilities and proven track record yields an immediate ROI for our customers!
