Who We Are?

We live in a world of constant noise and increasingly short attention spans. Great influence and impact aren’t about volume; they’re about meaning.

At Laniado-Shtrauchler, we don’t settle for visibility – we create presence. A presence that is thoughtful, valuable, and impactful.

For three decades, we've been at the crossroads of organizations, public figures, businesses, and Israeli society.

What Sets Us Apart?

Strategic Partnership

We take an active role - thinking together, moving fast, and communicating clearly across diverse sectors.

Impact

We don’t stop at awareness - every move is designed to shift perception, shape opinion, strengthen engagement, or drive action.

Decoding the Audience

We dive deep into who we’re speaking to—not just demographically, but emotionally and cognitively.

Narrative Design

We build meaningful stories rooted in values and purpose, designed for large-scale emotional connection and impact - not just marketing slogans.

Systemic Thinking

We connect internal and external communication, understanding that one always impacts the other, and together, they are more powerful.

Crisis Leadership

When things get critical, we’re there with clear planning, precise messaging, and fast, effective execution.

Metrics Driven

Everything we do is data - driven and measured by one question: does it create meaningful, tangible results?

How We
Create Impact

  • Strategy that Drives Results – We build tailored strategic communication plans aligned with vision and values to spark real movement.
  • Crisis & Reputation Management – We are experts in anticipating the unexpected and responding and managing wisely in real time.
  • Public Relations & Media Management – We don’t just react to media - we create it. Proactively, strategically and reliably.
  • Internal Communication – Employees are your most authentic media. We craft communication that engages them to meaning and mission.
  • Digital Presence with Depth – We build digital strategies that blend creativity, analytics, and emotional insight for extensive impact.
  • AI in Communication – We merge human intuition with data - driven artificial intelligence to enhance strategic decisions, monitor discourse, and sharpen impact.

Our Team

Our team comprises experienced strategic advisors, communication professionals (internal and external), and digital and creative experts. We operate with flexibility, depth, and clarity—always focused on outcomes.

People connect with honesty, not campaigns. We act with transparency, precision, and human understanding.

We turn messages into stories, crises into opportunities, and vision into reality – even when the screen goes dark.

Executional Excellence

Good enough isn’t good enough. Every word, headline, and slide is polished to perfection.

Strategic Boldness
