Churn

International private security company wants to reduce its churn

After carrying out a detailed analysis of their customer retention policies, a predictive model was developed using the company’s internal data. The model substantially reduced (approximately -40%) voluntary terminations through an alert system tailored to those behaviors that triggered termination requests.

Geo Marketing

Important retailer wants to know where its next shopping center should open (geo marketing)

Using internal company data and external data from the credit bureau, different options were mapped to maximize the traffic of individuals with a suitable profile for their target population. With this objective, we developed an index that included geo referenced variables of risk, ability to pay, indebtedness and geographic accessibility. The deliverable was a ranking of the most suitable geographical areas for its territorial expansion.

Customer Experience

International Bank wants to better manage its customer service efforts (customer experience)

Faced with the challenge of adequately serving customers who called their call center, a model was developed that ranked the flow of incoming calls to the call center. The objective was to decrease waiting times for those clients who showed better business opportunities for the bank and to provide the operator with a current vision of the client and of those other products or services that might interest them.

Sales/risk tool

Telecommunications company needs a sales tool that reduces risk (Sales/risk tool)

Given the need to assess the risk of new prospects and to offer them the best available service plan, a predictive model was developed that not only rated new customers on a risk scale, but also recommended the best set of products and services in each case. The result was reduced risk exposure without affecting the sales pipeline.

Customer value

International financial company wants to develop a customer value score (Customer value)

Using the institution’s own data, a customer value index was developed which took into account all its historical list of products and services. The construction of the index included risk variables, profitability by product, etc. This development was then mounted on a front end tool in order to increase the effectiveness and efficiency of their marketing campaigns with the consequent increase in profitability.

Credit scoring / portfolio manager

Microcredit group wants a tool for granting credit and managing its portfolio (Credit scoring / portfolio manager)

We developed a web application which was parameterized by credit demand markets in unbanked sectors or in need of microcredit. The system analyzes borrowing capacity, risk, and instantly offers the right credit to each individual. The application also makes the input of the credits to the internal books of the financial institution and made the payment available to the clients.

Text mining

Government entity wants a document indexing and search tool (text mining)

An intelligent ingestion and indexing tool was designed that enables the handling of a large volume of documents in PDF, DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX formats. The tool provides the possibility of complex information searches disseminated throughout multiple concurrent users.

Online monitoring

An important telecommunications company wants a tool that allows it to manage the performance of its network in real time (online monitoring).

A system for collecting logs of network assets, applications, operating systems, net-flow traffic was developed, centralizing and making available performance metrics and graphs in real time. Likewise, machine learning functions and alerts on time series were incorporated with the aim of detecting atypical events that affect the performance and security of the network.
