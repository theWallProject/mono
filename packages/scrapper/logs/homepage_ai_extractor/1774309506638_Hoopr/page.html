Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on Copyright & Royalty Free Music

What does non-copyrighted music mean?

Though the term "copyright-free music" can apply to any music not covered by copyright laws, it is more frequently used in the creative community to refer to music that won't give rise to copyright disputes on YouTube and other social media platforms.

How can you find copyright-free/royalty-free music for YouTube?

YouTube has its own Audio Library where you can find music and sfx that are free to use in your videos. There are several websites where artists upload their music under licenses that allow for free use. However, the options may be limited and come with restrictions. Your best bet is to subscribe to copyright free/royalty free music libraries like Hoopr.

What songs have no-copyright?

The only music that is completely copyright-free is public domain music. A composition becomes public domain when its creator grants permission for it to do so, when its copyright expires, or if it was made prior to the establishment of copyright laws.

Where can you use Hoopr's copyright-free music?

You can use Hoopr’s copyright-free music for all your content needs - vlogs, ads, movies, reels, gaming, podcast, tutorials, corporate videos and more. Choose from a wide variety of vocals, instrumentals and sound effects to enhance your content.

How many songs on Hoopr have no copyright music?

Every song and sfx in the Hoopr library is copyright free for Hoopr subscribers.

Is it legal to download copyright free music from any YouTube Channel?

Downloading copyright-free music from a YouTube channel depends on the permissions granted by the channel owner. If the music is explicitly labelled as copyright-free then it's legal to download and use it according to the terms specified by the creator. If the music is not explicitly labelled as copyright-free or provided with permission to download, it's best to seek alternative sources like Hoopr to obtain proper licensing and avoid potential copyright issues.
