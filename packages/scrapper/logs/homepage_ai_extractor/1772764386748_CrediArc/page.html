    How the platform works

    CrediArc Assist™

    AI that empowers underwriters — not replaces them

    CrediArc Assist™ is a multi-agent AI system designed to supercharge underwriting, while keeping the final credit decision firmly in human hands. It automates the heavy lifting, dives deeper into risk, and continuously monitors exposure — so underwriters can focus on judgment, not data chasing.

    Underwriter Copilot Agent

    Human in control. AI in support.

    Works side-by-side with the underwriter, surfacing insights, flags, and recommendations — while leaving the final underwriting decision entirely to the human expert.

    Data Onboarding Agent

    Zero friction data intake

    Automatically ingests data from emails, attachments, files, and documents. Structures messy inputs into clean, usable financial and risk data — without manual uploads or back-and-forth.

    Financial Deep-Dive Agent

    Smarter analysis. Stronger scoring.

    Analyzes financials at a depth traditional models miss. Understands trends, anomalies, and context — delivering more accurate, explainable, and resilient financial risk scores.

    Borrower Engagement Agent

    Faster onboarding. Better answers.

    Engages directly with each borrower to collect missing information, clarify inconsistencies, and request supporting data — dramatically reducing manual communication between underwriters and borrowers.

    Risk Monitoring Agent

    Risk doesn't stop at approval

    Continuously monitors portfolios post-approval, tracking behavioral signals, financial changes, and emerging risks — enabling early alerts and proactive action.
