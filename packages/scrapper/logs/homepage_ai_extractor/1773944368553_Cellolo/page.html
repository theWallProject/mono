    Get to Know Us

    At Cellolo, we provide cutting-edge, fully integrated development solutions to our clients, leading the charge in simulation, metaverse technologies, consumer electronics, and interaction design. Since our establishment in 2012 Cellolo has consistently delivered groundbreaking solutions, demonstrating our profound expertise across diverse fields. Our work has garnered accolades, including a Guinness World Record, a Vive X recognition and the prestigious Epic Games Mega Grant, reinforcing our commitment to pushing the envelope of innovation. 

    Services

    Cellolo's Full Turnkey Development Solutions

    Simulation & Metaverse

    From designing the world's most advanced road safety training simulators for e-bikes and e-scooters, to pioneering VR and AR solutions for driver training, we make experiences as close to reality as possible.

    Consumer Electronics

    Our vertical integration services encompass concept creation, business case analysis, software & hardware design, and implementation. We work closely with our clients, ensuring successful delivery every step of the way.

    Interaction Design

    From retail events to music festivals and sports events, we design state-of-the-art crowd interaction systems that bring the audience into the heart of the show. Our technology merges AI and advanced computer vision with proprietary hardware and software.​
