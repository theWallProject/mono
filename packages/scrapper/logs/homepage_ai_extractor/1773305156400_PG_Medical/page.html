PG Medical Systems is bridging the huge pain management gap by making personalized diagnosis and treatment widely accessible and by introducing it as early as possible into the workflow. 


Harnessing state of the art Machine Learning Algorithms and Telehealth tools, our platform allows for:

• Bringing together Patients, Primary Care Practitioners and Pain Experts

• Effectively scaling pain diagnosis and treatment regardless of geographical or   mobility challenges.

• Encompassing a multidisciplinary approach to pain treatment and   management

• Empowering clinicians with the pain-medicine toolbox


We envision our system will bridge an evident gap in pain-medicine practices and improve treatment outcomes while lowering the risks of addiction to prescription drugs.
