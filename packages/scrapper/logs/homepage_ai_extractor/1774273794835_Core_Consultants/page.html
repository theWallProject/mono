We are Core

Our primary objective at Core is to see each of our clients realise fair value for their operations. We do this by offering a world-class commodity market analysis and communications service to the natural resources and extractive industries.

Core’s quality team of analysts, industry journalists, marketing and communications specialists and financial experts enable us to serve our clients in the best way possible to assist companies with their decision making utilising our consulting services and to increase their online visibility with our communications efforts.

Since our founding in 2009 by Lara Smith, we have remained an independent, family-run business and have successfully supported clients across the globe.
