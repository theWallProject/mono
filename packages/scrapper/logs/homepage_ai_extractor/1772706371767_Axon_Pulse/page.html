Technology & Approach

Turning signals into action.

PERCEPTION

Perception at Axon Pulse detects targets early in noise and clutter, classifies them into reliable labels, and maintains continuous tracks through motion, occlusion, and sensor changes. Built for real-world conditions, it delivers low-latency, stable outputs with transparent reasoning for confident decision-making.

Decision & Integration

Our in-house command and control delivers clear tracks, confidence, sensor evidence, and next-step guidance. It manages timing, audit, and failover, connects via clean APIs, and includes tools for analysis, training, and reporting.

Super Resolution

Super resolution clarifies radar, acoustic, and signals data — recovering structure, separating sources, and estimating direction so operators see real targets. Physics-aware methods enhance detection, classification, and tracking without inventing features.

Sensor Fusion

Sensor fusion delivers a single, trustworthy picture from diverse sensors and data streams. Our AI learns each source’s strengths and limits, fusing at measurement or track level as the mission demands. We align timing, handle dropouts, and adapt weights to reliability, geometry, and SNR. Cross-cueing requests the next best look. Consistency checks and deduplication keep one identity. Output: unified track and class with calibrated confidence and provenance.
