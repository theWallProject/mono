AKT Launches Qualifications Management Solution

EQM (short for “Employee Qualification Management”) is an extension to SAP-SuccessFactors, developed on BTP. It helps manage employee qualifications throughout the employment lifecycle, covering all types of qualifications such as certifications, licenses, internal and external training, OJT, vaccinations, medical tests and even accidents and disciplinary log. EQM supports complex business rules, integrates with external systems, and is designed to be flexible and adaptable across industries.

Papaya Global is a single global platform for workforce payments – built for the enterprise. Papaya Global gives you total control over how you manage and pay every type of worker, employees, contractors, and contingent talent — across 160+ countries. 

AKT Global partners with Papaya Global to bring Papaya’s global payroll and workforce management solutions to SAP SuccessFactors and SAP Fieldglass customers. Through this partnership, organizations using SuccessFactors can extend their HR capabilities with seamless global payroll, compliance, and payments, fully aligned with their existing HR core.

Together, AKT Global and Papaya Global help customers simplify international workforce operations and accelerate global growth.
