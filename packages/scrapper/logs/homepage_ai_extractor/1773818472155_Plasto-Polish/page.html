About Us

Seeking innovative household cleaning products that are trusted by some of the world's leading companies?

Plasto-Polish specializes in developing, manufacturing, and marketing top quality household cleaning products. Its high-quality product range includes 2 in 1 scouring sponges and cleaning cloths, as well as magic erasers, scourers and sponges. All Plasto-Polish's products are available in a wide range of sizes, colors, and materials.

With more than five decades of experience in manufacturing household cleaning products, Plasto-Polish's expertise enables it to develop innovative new products to meet market needs.
