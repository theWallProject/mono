SPICE-accurate full-clock analysis and power optim...

ClockEdge

ClockEdge delivers advanced-node clock verification and optimization that transforms clock margin into measurable performance, power, and yield gains. From clock tree synthesis (CTS) through final tapeout, its technology provides full-clock electrical visibility, enabling teams to identify and resolve clock risk early in the design cycle.

ClockEdge replaces approximation with full-clock, SPICE-accurate analysis across timing, power, jitter, and aging, allowing safer margin reduction, improved frequency headroom, lower clock power, and reduced respin risk.

At 5 nm, 3 nm, and below, where clock behavior increasingly defines product competitiveness, this level of accuracy enables semiconductor companies to reclaim recoverable margin, protect schedules, and deliver first-silicon success.

Trusted across hundreds of advanced-node tapeouts, ClockEdge helps customers reach true silicon limits with confidence.
