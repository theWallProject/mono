Golden Rules of Publisher SPO

  • Ads.txt is here to present your inventory to buyers. Misusing it can hurt your revenue.
  • Help buyers understand the shortest path to your inventory. Only you can be listed as DIRECT.
  • Cross reference ads.txt and sellers.json. Find out who is tapping into your inventory.
  • Clean up. Remove lines of partners you are no longer working with.
  • Run quarterly overall checks. Make sure there are no loopholes.
