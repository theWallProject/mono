What we do

Our law firm has extensive knowledge and expertise in the cross border transactions, companies laws, securities laws, guidelines of the regulatory authorities also applicable to the issuance of Digital Currency

We advise our clients on regulatory matters, including compliance with privacy and securities regulations, financial operation licensing, Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti Money Laundering (AML). 

We believe that the future of our planet in our hands and we are active with international organizations to promote innovation which can impact the world and progress the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

governance issues

My clients are corporations, individuals and regulators. I advise my clients on all legal aspects of Digital Asset technology, including securities law, privacy, and the prohibition on money laundering. I have extensive experience in working with Fintech companies. 

I’m a member of the Blockchain Association, Interim COO for Ownera, legal lead for the GoodDollar Foundation and LibraCamp Bootcamp.

I’m GDPR certified, received an M.Sc. in law from the Interdisciplinary Center Herzliya and graduated from the Microsoft Israel Entrepreneurship Program.

I’m a known thought leader, lecturer, and author in the Digital Assets and Blockchain technology space.
