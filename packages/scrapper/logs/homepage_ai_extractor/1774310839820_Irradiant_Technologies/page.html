Light is a uniquely information dense medium

The fundamental properties of light make it possible to sense, encode, and move vast amounts of rich information with a speed and efficiency that touches every aspect of life. Light is non-interacting and information dense, a unique combination that makes it the primary means by which we send information and perceive our world today.

Irradiant is the world’s first 3D Nano-optics & Photonics Foundry.

Discover our Technology

Full 3d

We can create any possible geometry with our fabrication platform.
Structures do not need to be self-supporting or contiguous.

Nanoscale

We create structures with features down to tens of nanometers, a deeply sub-wavelength resolution that is ideal for optics, photonics, and microelectronics.

Gradients

We pattern true gradients of material density.
For example, we can control refractive index continuously between 1.05 and 1.6, achieving a maximum contrast comparable to glass in air.

Multi-material

We have developed a large library of functional materials that can be patterned in 3D, including polymers, high refractive index dielectrics, quantum dots, highly conductive metals and even biomolecules.

Rapid prototyping

Digitally controlled 3D Lithography enables us to rapidly prototype components in hours. This dramatically shortens the development cycle for optics/photonics and nanotechnology at large.

Ultra-high throughput

We achieve throughputs thousands of times faster than existing 3D lithography systems by patterning entire planes at a time, rather than scanning a single point. This enables us to use the same tool for creating a single prototype as creating millions of parts per year.

Irradiant unites all the optical platforms

Solving the biggest industry pain point of getting light into and out of photonic components
Our innovation allows free-space optics, fiber optics, and photonic integrated circuits to be intimately combined. This is possible because lithographically aligned and monolithically fabricated 3D optics enable light to travel seamlessly across components, while drastically reducing the cost of component integration.

Volumetric optics & Multi-layer Metalenses

The ability to create multi-layer metalenses and volumetric optics at scale allows for entire optics trains to be collapsed down to 3D structures thinner than a human hair. This means we can achieve previously impossible feats, like eliminating the cellphone lens bump, miniaturizing hyperspectral imagers, and more.

Integrated Free-space Optical Microsystem

Lithographically aligned and monolithically fabricated Integrated Free-space Optical Microsystems unlock the next generation of photonic integration. This enables entire optical assemblies/modules to be fabricated as a single component. Essentially, it is now possible to fully miniaturize your optical table setup while eliminating the vast majority of active alignment steps – substantially driving down costs while improving yields.
