FAQ

Answered questions.

Everything you might want to know—up front.

1
Who can work with Stork?
For Suppliers: Stork works with verified distributors, brands, and wholesalers who want to expand their digital reach and move inventory faster, without losing pricing control or brand integrity.

‍For Retailers: Stork partners with online retailers, marketplaces, and digital sellers looking to grow their assortment with live, ready-to-sell inventory — without purchasing stock upfront.
2
What does it cost to work with Stork?
There are no setup or subscription fees.Stork operates as the merchant of record — purchasing products directly from suppliers and reselling them to retailers. Our revenue comes from a dynamic margin, automatically adjusted by AI based on product category, demand, and market conditions.
3
What types of integrations do you support?
Suppliers: Stork supports multiple integration formats — including API, SFTP, Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, and CSV — making it easy to connect your catalog and start selling globally.
‍
Retailers: Integration is equally flexible. Stork can connect via API or data feeds, syncing product information, pricing, and availability in real time.
4
How quickly can we get started?
Suppliers: Integration and catalog onboarding typically take just a few days, depending on data format and catalog size. Once connected, your products are instantly visible to Stork’s network of global retailers.

‍Retailers: Setup is even faster. Integration can go live within 48–72 hours, allowing you to access live, brand-authorized inventory immediately.
