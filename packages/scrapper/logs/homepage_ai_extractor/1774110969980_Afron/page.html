About us

We offer the most up-to-date methods for pruning with our comprehensive range of mechanical pruning machines used by growers across the globe. Our 40 years of experience in the design, manufacture and operation of hedging, topping, and skirting machines has enabled us to develop the variety and scope of models to meet your needs. Our team is ready to give advice on the best models for your specific requirements on your completion of the Pruning Machine Adaptability Form which can be sent to us by fax or email. Designed and Manufactured to Last

Durability

Our products are tested in tough conditions to ensure a long and trouble-free service.  Our designers work to produce machinery with the highest safety standards, ease of operation and convenience.

Safety

Our top priority is safety for the operator and any other personnel in the working arena throughout all maneuvers and during maintenance. We take pride in our high safety standards which have been maintained over five decades through our many models.

Quality

We take pride in the detail built into our machinery, which are designed with easy and fool-proof maintenance schedules and high level service.  We focus on feature controls that are easy to use together with excellent all round vision.
