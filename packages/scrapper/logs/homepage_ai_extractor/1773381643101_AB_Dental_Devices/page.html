AB Dental is an Israeli and worldwide leader in the development, manufacture, and marketing of dental implants, prosthetic products, and surgical tools. The company provides dentists and implantology experts with a comprehensive end-to-end solution – including advanced digital implant design, laser-printed custom implants, 3D-printed surgical guides, CAD/CAM solutions for successful and precise treatment, surgical tools and kits, and more.

Committed to Innovation

AB Dental is committed to the highest standards of manufacturing and service, while striving for continuous improvement, technological leadership, and the creation of new opportunities for dental professionals and patients alike. Ever since the company was founded in 2000, we have been working ceaselessly to expand our product lines, develop new types of implants, and integrate groundbreaking technologies.

Custom Products and Solutions

One of AB Dental’s advantages as a leading dental implant manufacturer is the company’s unmatched ability to provide individually customized solutions and patents. The combination of a broad offering and advanced technologies enables us to rapidly deliver an optimal solution to any clinical scenario, tailoring it to the precise needs of the patient and/or dental professional – including custom implants using laser sintering technology, custom surgical guides, and more.

The Human Touch

AB Dental is a family company, founded on the values of human connection and personal touch. Our success is based on a unified, dedicated team and on warm personal relationships with our clients. We do more than provide dental implants and smart dental solutions – we address all of our clients’ needs, anytime, anywhere, with genuine attention and all the resources needed to deliver a solution. We are always here for you.

Helping You to Smile

As a leading dental implant brand, our job is not only to address current market needs, but to lead the entire industry forward by creating more effective, easy-to-use, and convenient dental solutions. AB Dental’s implants are endorsed by dentists and implantology experts worldwide, who use them to provide their patients with a pleasant experience and optimal results – both functionally and aesthetically.

Strictest Standards

AB Dental’s dental implants, prosthetic products, surgical tools, and accessories meet the strictest international standards. Among others, the company holds the following regulatory approvals: MDSP, FDA (US), CE (Europe), Russia, AMAR (Israel), FDA (China), FDA (India), FDA (Taiwan), FDA (Australia), TGA (Ukraine), and more.

From Practice to Theory – All Under One Roof

AB Dental’s unique model provides dentists and implantology experts with a comprehensive end-to-end solution. In addition to smart technologies and products such as digital implant design, surgical guides, custom implants, CAD/CAM solutions, and more, we also lead the field in professional education.

AB Dental has created an advanced education center for learning and practicing on its products and using the latest implantology procedures. In addition, each year we produce numerous local and international events, including conferences, workshops, and meetups with world-leading professionals.
