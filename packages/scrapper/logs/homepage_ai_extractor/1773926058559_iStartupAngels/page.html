The iStartup Angels Group

INVESTING IN DISRUPTIVE INNOVATION STARTUPS
iStartupAngels is an Angel Investment Group that invests in the most promising startups. Managed by Alex Ackerman, we possess deep experience in identifying disruptive innovation startups across various sectors. Our group's angels aim to attract a small group of highly motivated entrepreneurs who offer unique and disruptive solutions across a broad range of industries. These solutions should appeal to mass international markets. We work with these entrepreneurs to build sustainable ventures. Most often, we invest in startups at the pre-seed, seed and Series A stages, providing co-funding, mentoring, and business development support.

Here is How We Help Startups

Funding Assistance
We'll work with you closely to understand your venture's required budget, then we'll map out the most suitable funding options and assist you along the way to get funded

Startup Package
If you have an idea or a startup in its early stages, we offer you a complete kick-off package including market research, business plan, a package of investor materials and more

Startup Mentoring
Mentors are the secret weapons of successful startups. We’ll give you a toolset of knowledge to develop business strategy and winning materials for the investor pitch deck

Business Development
If your business does depend on deals with big companies for success, you better come into that arena with more than your stones and sling. We’ll be guiding you step by step
