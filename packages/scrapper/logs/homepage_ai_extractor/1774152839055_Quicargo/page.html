Answers to your questions

Why should I use Quicargo?

  • Digital customer portal – Easily register, book and track your transport online. No endless emails or phone calls, but everything clearly organised in one place.

  • Flexibility for every type of shipment – Whether it’s a pallet, part load or full load, with our extensive network of carriers, we always find the right solution.

  • Competitive rates & transparency – Immediate insight into prices and deliveries, so you’ll never be faced with any surprises.

  • Reliable network – Thanks to our extensive network of certified carriers, you are assured of quality and delivery reliability.

Can i ship only in the Netherlands or also abroad?

You can ship pallets in Netherlands but also abroad.

Send your pallets to more than 29 countries in Europa.

Check all transport destinations –> 

What are the maximum dimensions for pallet shipments?

Pallets:

Mini Pallets – 80×60 – max weight: 350 kg
Europallets – 80×120 – max weight: 800 kg
Blok Pallets – 100×120 – max weight: 1000 kg
Custom Size Pallets (Non-standard pallets) – max width 200 cm – max weight: 1000 kg

*900+ kg? Then you need a forklift at the loading and unloading address.

How does the payment work?

If you are a new customer, we ask that you pay for your first order in advance.

After that, we will invoice you per order after delivery of the shipment. We apply a payment term of 14 days, or SEPA direct debit is also possible.

You have the following payment options:

  • SEPA direct debit
  • Bancontact
  • Bank transfer
Can I also have ADR goods and temperature-controlled goods transported?

Currently we do not  support ADR, temperature-controlled goods we can only do for full truckloads. Groupage/LTL shipments of temperature-controlled goods we can not do. 

Can I place orders as a private individual?

No, only companies with a valid VAT number can use Quicargo. 

How long does freight shipping take?

Depending on the destination and type of transport: groupage/LTL/FTL.

What is a freight forwarder?

A freight forwarder is a logistics service provider that organises and coordinates the transport of goods on behalf of a customer. The freight forwarder does not usually transport the goods themselves, but engages reliable carriers for this purpose and ensures that everything runs smoothly.
