Smart Power: A tool that adapts to people

Each person is different. Every job is unique. Built on AI and machine learning, Exia uses billions of anonymized data points from real work environments to understand how people move — and to support them accordingly. It adapts to the individual and the task in real time, delivering powerful assistance where it’s needed most.

Key benefits:

  • Up to 38 kg (84 lbs) of lifting support per movement

  • Relieves strain during lifting, carrying, loading, and bent-over work

  • Helps prevent injuries and musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs)

  • Learns with every use and continuously improves through AI

  • Regular (Over-the-Air) updates available

  • Connects to digital workflows and data platforms

  • Rugged and fully dust- and waterproof

  • Proven in real-world industrial and care settings
