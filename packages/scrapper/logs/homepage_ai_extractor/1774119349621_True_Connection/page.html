Our Workers

Proficient English Speakers

At True Connection, we guarantee proficiency by exclusively selecting native English speakers who have successfully passed the IELTS exam. This internationally recognized assessment, authorized by Canadian, US, and British authorities, ensures our workers possess impeccable communication skills to effectively engage with their dependents.

Our Experienced Talent Pool

True Connection boasts access to a diverse pool of experienced professionals, spanning roles such as caregivers, farmers, drivers, hotel staff, restaurant personnel, and beyond. With years of expertise in their respective fields, our workers are ready to seamlessly integrate into your operations with minimal training or adjustment needed.
 

Ready to Start ASAP

True Connection offers access to tens of thousands of skilled workers from the Far East who are ready to kickstart their relocation process swiftly and seamlessly. Say goodbye to unnecessary bureaucracy like the POEA process, as our streamlined approach ensures a hassle-free transition. Plus, with minimal need for additional training, you can onboard our workers with ease and efficiency.
