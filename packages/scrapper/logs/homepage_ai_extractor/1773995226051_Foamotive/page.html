About Us

Foamotive is an international manufacturer specialized in foam parts for the automotive industry, with headquarters in Israel and production facilities in Romania and in Mexico. The company is part of the Palziv Group, a global leader in the production of cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) foam materials.
 
Foamotive produces thermo-compressed XLPE and other foam parts for automotive industry original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) as a Tier 1 supplier. We also supply seals, gaskets and acoustic foam parts to other Tier 1 companies in the industry.
