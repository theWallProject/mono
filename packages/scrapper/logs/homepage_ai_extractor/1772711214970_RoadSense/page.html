Edge Sensor Layer

 High-Fidelity Capture

The foundation begins with our custom mmWave radar hardware. Unlike cameras, our sensors perceive the world as high-density 3D point clouds. By performing Edge AI processing directly on the device, we filter out noise and classify objects at the source. This ensures that only anonymous, metadata-level information ever leaves the sensor.

Privacy-by-design, all-weather reliability, and sub-centimeter resolution.

Secure Cloud Gateway

Encrypted Data Orchestration

Our cloud infrastructure acts as the secure backbone for global device management. Using low-latency 5G/LTE connectivity, the gateway aggregates metadata from distributed sensor networks. It handles over-the-air (OTA) firmware updates, sensor health monitoring, and ensures that data remains encrypted and sovereign throughout its entire lifecycle.

Scalable device telemetry, multi-region hosting, and end-to-end encryption.

Deep Analytics Engine

 Cognitive Intelligence

This is where raw data becomes strategic foresight. Our deep learning models perform Micro-Doppler signature analysis to recognize complex movement patterns over time. By correlating historical trends with real-time events, the engine generates predictive modeling, automated reporting, and API-ready insights for third-party integrations.

Pattern recognition, predictive trendlines, and automated decision-support.

Global Scalability

 The Cloud Gateway allows for a “plug-and-play” deployment model- new sensors are recognized and integrated into the global network instantly.

Continuous Learning

The Deep Analytics tier constantly refines its models; as the sensor network grows, the intelligence becomes more nuanced and accurate.

Minimal Bandwidth

 As the Sensor Layer processes raw signals locally, we transmit only 1% of the data volume compared to video-based systems.
