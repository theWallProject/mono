FAQ

Why should I choose Loora as my AI English tutor?

Loora is the most advanced English language learning app built specifically for conversation. It uses AI to create a judgment-free environment where you can practice speaking English until you reach full fluency.

Is there a free version of the app to learn English?

You can download Loora for free and start learning English with our AI today. We offer a trial period so you can experience having a dedicated English speaking partner before committing to a subscription.

How does the AI tutor help me speak English more naturally?

Unlike a standard English learning app, Loora acts as a real-time conversation partner. The AI tools listen to your speech and provide instant feedback, helping you improve your English pronunciation and flow.

Can I practice Business English with the app?

Absolutely. Loora is a powerful app for learning Business English, featuring specific modules to help you practice speaking for interviews, meetings, and professional presentations.

Is it safe to talk to an AI English speaking partner?

Yes. When you speak English with our AI, your data is kept secure and private. We use your interactions only to customize your learning journey and ensure the English tutor provides relevant feedback.

What makes Loora a good app for advanced English learners and for preparing for exams like TOEFL or IELTS?

Most English apps focus on basic vocabulary, but Loora is designed for deep conversation, and our AI can discuss complex topics, making it ideal for advanced or professional learners. How does Loora help me sound more like a native speaker?

Our app features use advanced speech recognition to analyze your accent. The AI identifies exactly which sounds you’re struggling with and provides specific coaching to help you improve your speaking clarity and confidence.
