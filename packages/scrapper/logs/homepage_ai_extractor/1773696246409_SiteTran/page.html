Frequently Asked Questions

  • How easy is it to use SiteTran?

    SiteTran is designed to be user-friendly for everyone, regardless of technical expertise. With intuitive in-app guidance and step-by-step tutorials, you’ll have all the help you need to set up and use SiteTran with confidence.

  • Will SiteTran alter my original website?

    No, SiteTran only adds a customizable language dropdown for visitors to select their preferred language, ensuring brand and design consistency. SiteTran’s architecture is designed to operate independently, making it technically impossible to alter your original site’s content or structure in any way.

  • What types of websites, platforms, and languages does SiteTran support?

    SiteTran works with websites across industries like Retail, E-Commerce, SaaS, and more. It integrates with popular platforms like WordPress, Squarespace, Shopify, and Webflow, and supports over 187 languages, including right-to-left (RTL) languages like Arabic and Hebrew, multi-byte character languages like Chinese and Japanese, and even specific dialects like Spanish for Mexico (es-MX).

  • How does SiteTran help with SEO?

    SiteTran boosts your SEO by ensuring translated pages are fully indexed by search engines. Our system creates optimized subdomains (e.g., fr.example.com) or subdirectories (e.g., example.com/fr/) and ensures all content, including dynamic elements, is translated and ready for global audiences. Plus, tools like Google Analytics work seamlessly on these pages.

  • What makes SiteTran different from other translation tools?

    SiteTran combines enterprise-grade performance with exceptional usability. Its intuitive in-app guidance and user-friendly interface make it easy for both technical and non-technical users to translate every part of a site, including dynamic content, images, links, and interactive elements. Designed to support global enterprises, SiteTran ensures seamless operation at a highly competitive price.

  • Can SiteTran translate dynamic content and images?

    Yes, SiteTran automatically translates dynamic elements like pop-ups and search filters, and it translates image alt text and swaps images to display the correct language version for each visitor.

  • What happens to my translations when I update my website’s content?

    SiteTran automatically syncs new or updated content, saving you time. Previous translations are retained, so you won’t need to re-translate everything. For maximum control, you can manually review and override any suggested updates to ensure they align perfectly with your needs.

  • Can SiteTran redirect visitors to their preferred language?

    Yes, SiteTran automatically redirects visitors to the correct language based on their browser preferences. This feature is enabled by default but can be turned off if desired. Additionally, translated pages can attract more backlinks, improving your SEO performance globally.
