Who We Are

TPredict will predict your patient’s hormonal and thyroid treatment for you. TPredict changes with your patient’s changing medical status as evaluated by ongoing questionnaires and diagnostic medical testing including blood, stool and saliva. You’ll be able to track your patient’s supplement/hormone orders to grow your residual income.  

Impressive Features

Lab Interpretation

Blood, saliva and stool test lab data interpretation with treatment recommendations integrated with your patient’s medical data.

Internal Medical Feedback (IMF)

The doctor gets accurate feedback about each patient's progress, according to changes in symptoms, and learns which treatments work best.

Interactive Questionnaire

Patients and physicians appreciate brief interactive questionnaires for getting accurate clinical baselines and updates as their medical health improves.

Auto Reminder

Auto Reminder helps make sure your patients fill out follow up questionnaires and order hormone/supplement refills as needed.

All in one place

Save time by having all the data in one place. Patient notes, interactive questionnaires, test results and analytic patient recommendations.

Patient Optimizer

Your patient recommendations are based on their medical profile, along with our vast data bank of treatment protocols and results.

Increase your income

Make money from specialized, branded supplements generated for your patient’s individual needs. Easily track your patients’ orders.

Educational materials

Videos, charts and research-based handouts to share with your patients boosts their compliance through motivation and the power of knowledge.
