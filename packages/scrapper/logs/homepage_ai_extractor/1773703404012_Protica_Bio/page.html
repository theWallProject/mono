Cutting-Edge Proteomics meets AI to Rejuvenate Cancer Immunotherapy

We discover novel drug targets and IO response markers by combining proteomics and computational biology

About

Protica Bio develops predictive biomarkers based on actionable proteomic insights into tumor cell biology to make cancer treatments more effective, personalized, and accessible. Unlike genomics, our proprietary platform, developed by world-leading proteomics expert Prof. Tami Geiger from Weizmann Institute, delivers unmatched protein coverage, meaning we can see what others cannot, revealing real-time biological signals that predict treatment response and uncover new therapeutic targets.

The Need

Treatment with immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICI) has revolutionized oncological therapy for selected patients with melanoma and lung cancer. However, the response rates in these and other solid tumors are still significantly lower. Therefore, it is critical to identify predictive markers of response and to identify novel drug targets for combination therapies with ICI.

The Solution

Protica Bio aims to tackle these challenges by combining state-of-the-art proteomics technologies and supporting methods with computational biology. As proteomics allows identification and quantification of the tumor proteome in an unbiased manner, it's combination with machine learning can identify novel cancer regulators and achieve breakthroughs in this domain
