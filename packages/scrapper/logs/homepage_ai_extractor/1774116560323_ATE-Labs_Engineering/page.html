ATE - Labs Engineering was founded in January 2014. ATE - Labs Engineering is an Engineering and Integration company with vast experience in design, development, manufacture and integration of customized Multidisciplinary Systems  (Full Turnkey solutions: Software, Hardware, Mechanics and System Integration).

We help our clients improve mass production processes and reduce operating costs.
Our clients are startup companies, high-tech companies and industrial factories.

ATE - Labs Engineering  provides automation solutions, ATE (Automated Test Equipment) robots and Cobots (Collaborative Robots) systems, monitoring, measurements, and control systems for the ​industries of semiconductors, telecommunications, medical, automotive, defense, renewable energy, consumer electronics, security, applied physics, research, Food-Tech, IoT and Industry 4.0.

Our clients :
A fully automated testing system for electronic boards (PCBA), integrating a Fanuc collaborative robot (COBOT), an Ingun automated test fixture, and ATE-Labs Engineering's software and equipment.
The system enables reliable, precise, and efficient end-to-end electrical and functional testing (FCT) with no human intervention. It is capable of replacing up to three operators per day on the production line.
Optional system extensions include a barcode reader for scanning PCBA data, automatic reporting of test results to the ERP system, automated sorting of tested PCBA boards (PASS / FAIL), and a PCBA Loader or PCBA Conveyor.

We demonstrated the system at the NEW-TECH 2025 exhibition in Tel-Aviv.
