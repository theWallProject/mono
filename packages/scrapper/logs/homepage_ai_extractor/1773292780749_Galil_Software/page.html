Our
work
process

Exploration

We start with an initial meeting — typically with your VP of R&D or Quality Director — to fully understand your organization's challenges, requirements, and goals.

We define the necessary technologies, workflows, and objectives for the project or team.

We also discuss the type of service you need: development teams, automated testing teams, DevOps teams, or a customized combination.

01
Presenting Pre-Built Teams

We assemble ready-to-go dedicated teams, complete with names, CVs, and technical profiles.

You can fine-tune and select specific candidates according to your preferences.

02
Defining the scope

Together, we define the scope of work, SLA commitments, and working methodologies.

03
Kick off

The team is integrated into your company’s workflows, tools, and methodologies.

We provide continuous support through project managers and technical experts.

04
Track and scale

We conduct regular meetings to assess performance, satisfaction, and any necessary adjustments.

We can scale or reconfigure your team based on project needs.
