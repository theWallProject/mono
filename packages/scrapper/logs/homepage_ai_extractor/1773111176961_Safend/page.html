DATA PROTECTION SUITE

Safend Data Protection Suite (DPS) protects sensitive data before its transfer via comprehensive data encryption, port and device restriction and control and content inspection.

Answering major compliance requirements like EU GDPR, SOX, HIPAA, PCI, FISMA or UK DPA, DPS has already been successfully integrated into critical industries, government agencies, municipalities, educational facilities, financial services, healthcare organizations, law enforcement and countless other organizations worldwide.
