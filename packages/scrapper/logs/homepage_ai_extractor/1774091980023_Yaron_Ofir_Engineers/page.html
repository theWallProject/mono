About Us

The firm began operating under the name Kadmor Ofir Engineers Ltd., and was established in October 2003 by engineer Yaron Ofir and the owners of Kadmor Ofir Engineers Ltd., with a vision to serve as a knowledge base in the field of earthquake engineering, which was in its infancy in the State of Israel. From 2008, under the management of Yaron Ofir and Alex Shohat under the name Yaron Ofir Engineers Ltd. The firm promoted the field of earthquakes, while developing advanced engineering in Israel. The office based in Haifa expanded and opened a branch in Tel Aviv as well as in Kiryat Gat, in order to improve service to customers in the center and south.
