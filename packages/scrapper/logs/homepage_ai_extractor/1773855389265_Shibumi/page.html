    AGO

    As with computer programming in the 80s, companies stand in front of this new discipline and struggle to understand it: how to start, how to build a team, how to plan a project, and more.

    This is why data science operations are many times accompanied by frustration: from managers, who don't get the business values they were promised, and data scientists, who feel the organization doesn't understand/provide them with the right environment.

    This is even more eminent in small organizations since the resources are more scarce and the cycles are shorter.

    We in Shibumi provide a safe landing in the data science field. With dozens of successful projects behind us in numerous business fields, we provide the best data science project practices. closed scoped projects & long-term planning.

    ABOUT

    Shibumi.AI was founded in 2016 by Gidi Shperber.

    With long years of experience as a data scientist and data science manager, Gidi founded Shibumi with the vision of helping organizations using AI technologies.

    In such projects, the line between great success and colossal failure is sometimes very thin. And a little bit of guidance may do a great difference.
    In the beginning, we worked with small startups on very specific features. But going onward, we engaged in the more significant projects, in fields like medicine, agriculture, transportation, and more.

    Through the years, we added more experts to the team, each with his own AI related specialty: vision, text, personalization, AI project management.

    “During my years in organizations, I've found that even highly technological organizations have trouble in becoming data-driven, or even applying small scale data solutions.”

    Gidi Shperber, CEO
