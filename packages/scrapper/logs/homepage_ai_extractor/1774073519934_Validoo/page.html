    Product

    Unblocking digitalization

    Validoo unblocks digitalization of paper documents. Due to the cost of manual verification, most organizations are obliged to store the original paper records. By automating image verification, Validoo enables the secure transition to digital records.

    VA

    The transition from paper to digital records:
    • Reduces the costs and risks of paper records
    • Enhances findability of data
    • Integrates digital and paper-based business processes  

    Legally-admissible record

    In order to create a legally-admissible record, regulatory standards require verification of the scanned image against the paper original. Today, the verification process is manual and the cost prohibitive. Validoo automates this process, eliminating the need for manual verification.

    LI

    • True – image integrity
    In line with international standards, Validoo verifies that the image is:
    • Exact – image quality
    • Complete – image completeness

    Auto-verification

    Validoo enables automated verification of scanned images. By verifying image capture, Validoo creates a legally admissible digital record of the paper document. This “digital original” serves as the document record, eliminating the need to store the physical paper document.

    Reduce costs
