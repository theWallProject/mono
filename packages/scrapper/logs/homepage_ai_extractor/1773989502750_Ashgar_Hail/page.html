About us



investment and development corporation



Over the past 20 years, Ashgar has focused on the purchase, development and marketing of diverse real estate projects throughout Israel. Ashgar's real estate projects bring unique building and design vision. The company's success can be attributed to the professionalism, experience and knowledge of its management team. Experience﻿ !



Ashgar, is led by highly experienced and seasoned professionals, with experience and successful track record of establishing new business and developing successful businesses across diverse industries such as real estate, retail, insurance, finance and telecommunications.﻿ Ashgar was founded by Mr. Eli Cohen, former CEO of Israel Discount Bank and and former CEO of IDB Holding Corporation. Over the last 50 years, Mr. Cohen has led a variety of business ventures across diverse sectors including: real estate, telecommunications, banking, insurance, financial markets, food retail and consumer products.﻿ With our knowledge and expertise, we can usually find a solution for different business and financing needs leveraging our strong relationships with all leaders of the business community in Israel.
