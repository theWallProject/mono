74% of all electricity

In the United States, buildings consume approximately 74% of all electricity. Thermal end uses represent approximately 50% of building energy demand and are projected to increase. Controlling HVAC load is therefore key to avoiding this waste.


Only 1% of commercial buildings install energy storage

Energy storage is the ideal tool to manage loads: It is reliable and can be automated, while the consumer continues to get energy from the onsite storage. However, currently, only 1% of commercial buildings install storage.


Enhancing resilience - Preventing blackouts

By discharging your storage when the grid is overloaded, you can help prevent the next blackout and be financially rewarded for doing so, all while continuing to enjoy your air conditioning, even during the hottest days.
