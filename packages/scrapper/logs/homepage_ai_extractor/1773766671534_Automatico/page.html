About

Welcome to Automatico, your premier destination for cutting-edge testing automation and performance expertise.
At Automatico, we pride ourselves on being pioneers in the field, offering unparalleled expertise in test automation and performance testing. Our team comprises seasoned automation and performance architects, each boasting over 20 years of hands-on experience. With a wealth of collective knowledge, we stand ready to tackle any automation or performance challenge you may encounter.
In today's fast-paced technological landscape, finding experienced automation and performance engineers who possess the skills to configure sophisticated infrastructure is akin to finding a needle in a haystack. Many professionals with development backgrounds aspire to transition into full-time development roles, while those who transition from manual QA often lack the requisite development expertise to make informed decisions regarding test infrastructure and tool selection. Consequently, organizations may find themselves grappling with inappropriate tooling choices and suboptimal programming languages.
At Automatico, we bridge this gap with our deep-rooted expertise and unwavering commitment to excellence. Our team not only possesses the technical prowess to navigate complex automation and performance challenges but also the discernment to select the most suitable test infrastructure and tools tailored to your unique needs.
Partner with Automatico today and experience the transformative power of precision-engineered automation and performance solutions.
