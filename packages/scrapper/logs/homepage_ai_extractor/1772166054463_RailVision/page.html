    Learn more about our two core products

    MainLine

    Our MainLine system provides an extended visual range of up to 2 km (1.2 mi), even in challenging weather and light conditions, improving the safety of train operations, preventing collisions, and reducing downtime.

    The system meets stringent railway inspection requirements adheres to the following standards:

    • EN 61373
    • EN 50121-3-2
    • EN 50153
    • EN 50155:2022
    • EN 45545-2: HL3
    • EN 50126:2017
    • SIL 0 compliant

    Learn More

    ShuntingYard

    Combining advanced vision sensors with AI and deep learning, our cutting-edge ShuntingYard system automatically detects and classifies objects within a range of 200 m – in harsh weather or light conditions.

    The system meets stringent railway inspection requirements adheres to the following standards:

    • EN 61373
    • EN 50121-3-2
    • EN 50153
    • EN 50155:2022
    • EN 45545-2: HL3
