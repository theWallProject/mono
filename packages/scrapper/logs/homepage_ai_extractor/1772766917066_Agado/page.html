Agado Live is a digital health company designed to support physical and occupational therapists by enriching in-clinic patient care with digital, at-home assistance through an intuitive treatment platform.

Contact Us

How Agado Live Works

Our platform helps clinics and hospitals to maximize their resources, allowing one therapist to manage multiple patients simultaneously while still providing an individualized treatment experience.

One To Many Video-Enabled Evaluation

Our high-performance computer vision and AI algorithms detect patient conditions, assess optimal treatment strategies through easy-access real-time video analysis, and enable personalized care management for many patients simultaneously, all without the need for special equipment.

Validated, Personalized Treatment Plans

After the initial evaluation, the platform produces a customized, practitioner-reviewed treatment plan to be followed by the patient at home individually or in a clinical group setting.

Ongoing Monitoring

Agado Live tracks the functional and cognitive changes in a patient over time, updating the treatment plan as needed and keeping caregivers in the know. 

Engaging Treatment  Features

The platform engages patients in a number of gamified tasks to ensure adherence to their treatment plan and make it an enjoyable experience.
