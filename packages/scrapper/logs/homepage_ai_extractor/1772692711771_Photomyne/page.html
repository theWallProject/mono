This is my parents’ wedding photo from July 4, 1942, in Manchester, UK. My mother was always very smartly dressed even though they were ordinary working people. She was very proud of her wedding outfit which she said was a beautiful pale blue color. They were married just before my father shipped out in the British Navy to Bombay, India for the duration of WWII. He didn’t get to return until 1946. My parents have never separated again and they both passed away in 1992 within months of each other. This photo was always the most special family photo to me as it signified the start of our family. That’s why this photo was the first photo I scanned with Photomyne so I could preserve it forever.
