FAQ

Can I customize YOOBIC for my industry?

Yes, YOOBIC is designed to be configurable and tailored for different industries and use cases. The YOOBIC platform works well for various categories across retail and hospitality, including grocery, convenience, fashion, luxury, pharmacy, discount, specialty and QSR.

What are some of the use cases that YOOBIC supports?

YOOBIC supports a wide range of use-cases for store teams such as store visits & audits, visual merchandising, promotions/marketing, micro-learning, social learning, image-recognition, incentives, culture & community building and many more.

How is YOOBIC priced?

YOOBIC customers pay a monthly fee based on number of locations, number of users, modules selected.

Does YOOBIC support multilingual teams?

Absolutely. YOOBIC supports multilingual frontline teams by offering a wide-ranging list of supported interface languages — including Arabic, Bulgarian, Czech, German, Greek, English (UK/US/CA/AU), French, Hebrew, Hungarian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish (ES/LatAm), Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Vietnamese and more. YOOBIC is a great fit for global organisations with diverse frontline teams operating in multiple languages, regions or countries.

What kind of analytics does YOOBIC provide?

YOOBIC offers rich analytics and insights across learning, tasks, operations and frontline execution. Highlights include fully customizable operational analytics, learning analytics dashboards, and powerful BI / Reporting capabilities.
