Workcify Marketing

Our initial product, Workcify Marketing, helps companies and marketing agencies to manage & continuously improve their marketing teams’ performance and productivity. It gives holistic view of team, project and performance, lets them set and track goals, and gives them customizable alerts, so they are never surprised by performance changes. It also detects the strengths & weaknesses of their team members, identifies & highlights top priority issues & opportunities, and actionable insights to improve their performance & skills.

In line with our win-win philosophy, the marketers get tools for continuous professional development, allows them to be better aligned with their teams, as well as a community of practice.
