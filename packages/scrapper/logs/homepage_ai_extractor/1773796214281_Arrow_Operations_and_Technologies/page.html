Our Services

What we offer?

Interdisciplinary environmental tailor-made solutions for complex hazardous and non-hazardous sludge and sediment applications. This includes industrial dredging, cleaning, separation, and reuse.

Arrow is part of the AGH Group, which brings together a diverse range of companies, each specializing in innovative industrial solutions.
As part of AGH, Arrow benefits from the combined expertise of companies that focus on industrial processes, smart solutions for operational efficiency, and environmental sustainability.
The group’s collaborative approach allows us to deliver comprehensive, interdisciplinary solutions to complex challenges across various sectors.

Our Goal

We strive to be the leading provider of innovative and sustainable solutions for hazardous and non-hazardous sludge and sediment handling and treatment. We create tailored, cost-effective solutions that meet each client’s needs while minimizing environmental impact and encouraging a circular economy.

Our clients are able to continue operations while we work as a result of a NO-SHUTDOWN policy. Our integrative approach, cutting-edge technologies, and committed team of professionals aim to meet each client’s specific needs.

Our competitive advantage

The combination of our no-shutdown policy and our unique, innovative robotic cleaning and dredging solutions for removing hazardous sludge and sediments from highly complex confined space structures in the chemical, petrochemical, metal, fertilizer and mining industries is our main competitive advantage. As the industry pioneer and leader in online industrial confined space cleaning, our primary goal is to provide our clients with a productive, cost-effective, and environmentally friendly solutions. By avoiding costly shutdowns, our online cleaning solutions save time and money while also improving safety and productivity.
