About Coverty

Coverty develops and manufactures AI mobile and cellular surveillance systems, enabling discrete, real-time face recognition, high quality recordings and transmissions of video, audio, and data over wireless or cellular networks. Coverty is an all-in-one solution, providing you with everything needed to facilitate remote command-and-control operations, in a wide range of tactical surveillance scenarios, using a single platform.
