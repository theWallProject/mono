about us

 Thermal Beacon Ltd. delivers advanced infrared IFF solutions and powerful laser technologies, spanning Visible to SWIR, MWIR, and LWIR spectrums.
Our systems meet the most rigorous military and industrial benchmarks, delivering exceptional field performance.
Driven by innovation and precision, we support global defense forces and industries with reliable, mission-ready solutions.

  • Patented thermal identification systems
  • High-power laser pointers, illuminators, and dazzlers
  • AS9100D with ISO 9001 certified for excellence
