The world goes electric and calls for fast-charging and long-lasting batteries.

The green shift pushes the electrification of society forward and to keep up, batteries must meet new requirements. Electric transportation and energy storage need batteries with high power capacity, which charge fast and last long, without compromising on safety.

Our solution is the brand-new
anode material TitanB™

TitanBTM accelerates the electrification of society because it is an ideal battery material for electric trains, buses, ferries and hybrids, which depend on fast charging. By extending the lifetime of batteries, the material brings down costs and reduce the environmental footprint of electric transportation. High safety will make batteries with TitanBTM the preferred choice for critical industrial applications. The long lifetime of the material makes it ideal for storage of renewable energy and for smart power grid applications – TitanBTM is made for a sustainable future.
