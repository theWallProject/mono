From Field to Market

About Us

In March 2016, after many years of exporting our fresh herbs via different export companies, we thought to ourselves: why don’t we do it ourselves? Why don’t we send our product directly to our clients? We will give each customer a customized solution for their needs, directly, without any middleman. And we did just that! Seven of the biggest fresh-herb growers in Israel now formed a company, and there is no better name for it than The Growers. How are we different? We don’t need to go looking for fresh herbs to buy; we grow them especially for you in our fields! We can guarantee delivery of all fresh-herb varieties in all seasons.
