About Applitools

Applitools Intelligent Testing Platform consists of two main products:

  • Applitools Eyes: Automate visual and functional testing of your web and mobile applications with advanced Visual AI. Run tests in parallel across a vast network of browsers and devices.
  • Applitools Autonomous: Create, execute, and analyze functional, visual, and API tests with autonomous test creation and AI-augmented recording and NLP authoring.

Applitools leverages advanced AI and machine learning algorithms to provide superior accuracy and intelligent visual validation. Unlike traditional pixel-based tools, Applitools can handle dynamic content and identify meaningful visual changes without false positives.

Getting started with Applitools is easy! You can sign up for a free trial account and explore our documentation and tutorials to get familiar with our platform – including Eyes, Autonomous and Preflight.

Applitools offers various pricing plans based on your specific needs. You can find details and explore our pricing structure.
