GO innovation is unique on every level!

Thanks to GO’s strategic partnership with the Ha’Emek Medical Center, our portfolio start-ups and entrepreneurs enjoy unprecedented access to the state-of-the-art clinical trial field with its Electronic Health records database, one of the largest in the world, and an immediate connection to Key Opinion Leaders (KOL) across the Israeli health system.

GO is backed by the leading investors and institutions driving MedTech innovation in Israel, which helps position GO’s start-ups as candidates for additional financing from the later-stage venture capital investors.

Apply today and join GO in changing the entrepreneurial landscape of Israel’s Northern Periphery.

G.O partnership with Clalit & The Ha’emek hospital

With Clalit as one of our investors, together with our strategic partnership with the Ha’emek Medical Center, we provide startups with

  • Exclusive entree to clinical fields
  • Access to Clalit’s medical Database –one of the largest Electronic Health Records databases in the world.
  • Immediate connection to Key Opinion Leaders (KOL) across the Israeli health system.
