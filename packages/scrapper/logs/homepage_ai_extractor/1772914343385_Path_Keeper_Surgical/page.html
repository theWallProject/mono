FDA Cleared Medical Device
The PathKeeper™ Full Spine Navigation System is cleared by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for marketing in the United States.

The PathKeeper system utilizes proprietary ultra-high  resolution 3D optical technology and Artificial Intelligence (AI) to provide dynamic, real-time navigation with no intraoperative radiation.

Radiation-Free, Efficient Surgical Navigation

Pathkeeper is designed to provide surgeons with a streamlined workflow, eliminating the need for intraoperative scans. This simplifies the procedure, improves efficiency, and provides a completely radiation-free experience.

Accuracy

Synthesizes preoperative anatomical information and planning with intraoperative intelligence and submillimeter tracking for precise spine surgery performance.

Safety

Improves surgical safety by creating a 3D optical view of anatomy registration and tracking without unnecessary exposure to radiation.
