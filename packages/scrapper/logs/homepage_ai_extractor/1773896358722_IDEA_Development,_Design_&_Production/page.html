IDEA Launches a New Manufacturing Facility in Israel

IDEA has launched a new manufacturing facility in the industrial zone of Shiller group, Israel, about 5 km from its currently site in Rehovot. IDEA’s new state-of-the-art Facility comprises 1,000 square meters of manufacturing space. Expanded manufacturing capacity will allow IDEA to efficiently serve customers with unique technologies through the different market segments.
