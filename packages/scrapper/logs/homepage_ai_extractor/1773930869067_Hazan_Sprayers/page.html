About Hazan

Hazan Sprayers Ltd. is located in the old city of Acre in the North of Israel. Founded by Mr. George Hazan in 1961 on the ideals of innovation and vision. The company started operating as a pest control operator contractor, and as an importer of pesticides and insecticides. As of 2006, the company has been managed by the second generation of the Hazan Family – Mr. George Hazan's  sons, whose vision has been to further develop and expand the company into the areas of manufacture and export.

Hazan Sprayers Ltd. imports and represents exclusively European and American leading brands of pumps, engines and accessories for Crop Protection machinery. Within less than five years after launching the first in-house designed power sprayer, Hazan Sprayers Ltd. became a leading player in the market for crop protection and pesticide sprayers in Israel. Hazan Sprayers Ltd. creates its own designed sprayers, based on 50 years of accumulated expertise and experience in the crop protection and pesticide field. Today, Hazan Sprayers Ltd. produces a wide range of garden, urban and field power sprayers from 80 to 1,200 Liters.

Hazan Sprayers Ltd. has been going from strength to strength and the success has been achieved thanks to product reliability, durability, user friendliness, unique features based on self-experience, short delivery times and unmatched after-sales service.

Hazan Sprayers Ltd.  is licensed by both the Israeli Ministry of Environmental Protection and the Israeli Ministry of Agriculture to import and distribute pesticides and insecticides. The company holds ISO 9001:2008 Quality Management Certificate from The Standards Institute of Israel. All Hazan products are manufactured in accordance with European Standards and carry CE mark.
