Have a question?

What is NLPearl?

At its core, NLPearl introduces Pearl, a speaking and reacting AI phone call agent, autonomous like a human on the phone, crafted for sales, support, and any type of phone interaction. Trained on millions of phone conversations, Pearl is the future of contact and call centers, embodying the next step in customer communication technology.

How can I customize Pearl for my business?
NLPearl offers an intuitive platform for creating and customizing your Pearl. You can add company-specific materials, making Pearl perfectly suited to your requirements.
Is Pearl available in my language or region?

You can use Pearl from anywhere in the world. Pearl is available in about 20 languages on the platform. Each plan includes a free phone number in the US and Canada, and numbers for other countries are available upon request. Pearl can also integrate with most VoIP providers. For any inquiries, contact us at support@nlpearl.ai.

How can I try Pearl?
You can try Pearl for free by accessing our platform and signing up. Once you’re registered, you can easily and intuitively create and customize your own Pearl.
Is there an option for larger enterprises?
Yes, NLPearl offers customized solutions for larger enterprises at special rates. Enterprises looking for a tailored AI strategy can reach out through this link to book a demo and access special rates.
How many concurrent calls can I make with my Pearl?

The number of simultaneous calls is unlimited but depends on your subscription tier (1 to 3). You can add agents starting at $100/month each, with no cap. Costs may vary for businesses based on specific needs, model additions, or higher server usage, ensuring flexibility and performance for high call volumes.

Does Pearl also handle inbound calls, not just outbound?

With Pearl, you can handle both inbound and outbound calls effortlessly. When you subscribe, you receive a phone number that is automatically linked to your Pearl. Setup takes just a few minutes, and you can use your number wherever you need.

Can I integrate Pearl with my existing tools?
Absolutely, with PearlVibe, you can integrate Pearl with almost anything.

PearlVibe connects your AI voice agents to your entire stack through APIs, webhooks, and native integrations.
Whether you use CRMs, support systems, booking tools, payment platforms, or internal services, PearlVibe plugs them in with a single prompt.Popular integrations include:
 
  • CRMs: Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive
  • Customer support: Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom
  • Messaging: WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger
  • Telephony & VoIP: Twilio, Aircall, Plivo
  • Calendars & bookings: Calendly, Google Calendar
  • Custom tools: Any internal or external API
 
If your tool has an API, PearlVibe can connect Pearl to it, fetching data, updating records, creating tickets, booking appointments, processing orders, or triggering workflows automatically.
What are the benefits of using a voice AI agent like Pearl for my business?

Implementing a voice AI agent like Pearl offers numerous advantages:

  • 24/7 Availability – Pearl handles inquiries anytime, ensuring seamless customer service without downtime.
  • Cost Reduction – Automating customer interactions with Pearl significantly lowers operational costs compared to traditional call centers.
  • Scalability – Pearl can manage thousands of calls simultaneously, eliminating wait times and improving efficiency.
  • Improved Customer Experience – Fast, accurate, and personalized responses enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty.
  • Lead Qualification & Conversion Boost – Pearl can pre-qualify leads and direct high-intent prospects to sales teams, increasing conversion rates.
  • Consistency & Compliance – AI ensures standardized responses and compliance with company policies, reducing human errors.
  • Multilingual Support – Pearl supports multiple languages, enabling businesses to serve a global audience effortlessly.
  • Omnichannel Integration – Seamlessly integrates with voice, SMS, chat, and CRM systems to provide a unified customer experience.
  • Data-Driven Insights – Pearl collects and analyzes customer interactions, offering valuable insights to optimize support and sales strategies.
  • Increased Productivity – Automating repetitive tasks frees up human agents to focus on more complex or high-value interactions.
How does NLPearl ensure data privacy and security?

We take security extremely seriously.

NLPearl is built from the ground up to protect every conversation handled by our AI phone agents.
We comply with GDPR, follow industry-standard security practices, and implement end-to-end encryption for data in transit and at rest.

NLPearl also offers secure hosting options, granular permission management, and role-based access controls to keep sensitive information protected. Our infrastructure and internal processes are built to meet SOC 2 requirements, ensuring your data is handled according to the highest standards of security, availability, and confidentiality. Whether you deploy Pearl for support, sales, or operations, your conversations and integrations stay fully protected.
