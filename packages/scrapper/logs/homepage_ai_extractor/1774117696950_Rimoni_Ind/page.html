About Rimoni

From engineering to mass production under one roof 

  • Industrial Design

    Industrial design is the foundation of a successful product. Our design process balances aesthetics, usability and manufacturability, ensuring that every product is optimized not only for appearance, but for efficient, scalable production.

    Read more +

  • Engineering & DFM

    Our engineering and DFM expertise bridges the gap between concept and production. We focus on cost optimization, reliability, serviceability and long-term production stability, reducing risks before tooling and serial manufacturing begin.
    Read more +
    Read more +

  • In-house Tooling

    Molds are designed and manufactured in-house, giving us full control over quality, timelines and performance. We produce hardened steel molds for high-volume production, including multi-cavity, hot runner, over-molding and micro-precision applications.

    Read more +

  • Injection

    We specialize in precision injection molding for regulated and high-demand industries. Our processes are designed for consistency, traceability and long-term repeatability, ensuring stable quality throughout the entire product lifecycle.

    Read more +

  • Advanced Assembly

    We provide advanced assembly solutions integrated with our injection molding operations. This allows us to deliver ready-to-use products while maintaining full responsibility for quality, efficiency and supply continuity.
