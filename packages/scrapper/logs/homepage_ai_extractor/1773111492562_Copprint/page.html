Breakthrough Additive Copper Inks that Outperform

Copprint enables low-cost, high-conductivity, onshore, and environmentally friendly printed electronics using copper inks, unleashing printed electronics.

 

Our Nano Copper ink technology supports additive fabrication of conductive patterns for a variety of applications, including PCBs, photovoltaics, RFID/antennas, and more, across a range of substrates.

 

Out with expensive silver inks and hazardous PCB etching. In with Copprint for sustainable additive electronics, printed with copper.

Lowest Cost

5-10X price performance compared to silver inks. Up to 90% material savings compared to etching.

Copper vs. Silver

Highest conductivity for any printed pattern.

Green Circuits

Fully compostable. Non hazardous. On-shore, sustainable manufacturing.

Substrate Freedom

A variety of substrates, including paper, PET, FR4, PI, glass, PV cells, with versatile printing methods — screen printing, dispensing, flexo, gravure, LIFT, inkjet.

Rapid Self-Sintering

Rapid self-sintering conductive copper inks in air environments for oxidation-free, high-throughput, high-yield printed electronics.
