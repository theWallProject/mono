CBD-based treatment solutions

STERO Biotechs Ltd., founded in 2017 and headquartered in Israel, is a clinical-stage company committed to the research and development of novel and improved Cannabidiol (CBD)-based treatment solutions that will potentially benefit millions of patients by reducing the side effects and the need of steroid therapy.

The company was established in partnership with ‘Mor Research Applications‘
the technology transfer unit of the Largest HMO in Israel – ‘CLALIT’ Health Services.
