Welcome to Shani Shenhav's ever-expanding technological galaxy.

Since 1986, we've emerged as a prominent technology distributor, serving over 4,000 clients across diverse sectors. Our holistic approach offers integrated solutions throughout the supply chain, enabling a seamless transition to digital, automated, and Industry 4.0-compliant environments.

At our core is a close-knit team of over 150 professionals, spanning diverse fields such as technology, logistics, sales, and marketing, both nationally and globally.

We foster a positive and collaborative environment, leveraging our expertise to drive innovation and success.

FinTech
