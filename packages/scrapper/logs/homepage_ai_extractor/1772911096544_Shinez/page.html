Shinez is proud to be part of Team Internet Group PLC (AIM: TIG, OTCQX: TIGXF), a leading internet solutions provider transforming the way businesses, brands, publishers and consumers connect online.
For nearly 30 years, Team Internet has been at the forefront of the digital economy. With customers in 230 countries and territories, our pioneering omni-platform approach is underpinned by a philosophy of providing every customer with world-class online presence and online marketing services. We create meaningful and successful connections: businesses to domains, brands to consumers, publishers to advertisers and beyond, enabling everyone to realise their digital ambitions. Discover more on the Team Internet website.
