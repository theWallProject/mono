WHAT WE CAN DO

OUR OFFER

 

01

PORT flat games to VR

We transform PC and console games into fully immersive VR experiences by seamlessly adapting each title while preserving its original essence. Our process ensures optimized performance across platforms such as Meta Quest, SteamVR, and PS VR2, delivering smooth and responsive gameplay.

 

We implement intuitive VR mechanics, including natural movement and hand tracking, to create engaging interactions tailored to virtual reality. Our team enhances visuals for maximum immersion and provides end-to-end support—from the initial assessment through development, launch, and ongoing post-release updates.

 

02

CREATE games from scratch

In addition to porting existing titles, we specialize in creating original VR games based on well-known IPs. By combining our technical expertise with a deep understanding of virtual reality design, we reimagine iconic worlds and narratives for immersive, interactive experiences.

 

Our goal is to honor the spirit of the original IPs while delivering fresh gameplay tailored to the strengths of VR—ensuring both long-time fans and new players can experience these franchises in an entirely new way.

 

03

SUPPORT other studios to succeed in VR

We cover the entire production process – from pitch deck to post-release support. we handle the entire game release process and support sales after launch.
