A Place of Encounter

Discover a unique experience in the Holy Land

Experience Magdala, a unique place of encounter in the Holy Land. Located on the shores of the Sea of Galilee, it is known as the crossroads of Jewish and Christian history and the hometown of Mary Magdalene.

Discover our first-century synagogue and archaeological park, Duc In Altum, guesthouse, Media and volunteer programs, events and more.

Hospitality Encounter
Guesthouse
The Magdala Guesthouse provides guests with a serene and immersive experience, blending natural beauty with a rich historical heritage and spirituality.
Learn more
Historical Encounter
Archaeology
Renowned for its first century synagogue and significant discoveries such as the Magdala stone which attract scholars and tourists intrigued by their historical and biblical importance.
Learn more
Biblical Encounter
Duc In Altum
Inviting visitors to deepen in their faith through prayer and reflection Duc In Altum encourages ecumenical dialogue and is inspired and centered around the public life of Jesus
