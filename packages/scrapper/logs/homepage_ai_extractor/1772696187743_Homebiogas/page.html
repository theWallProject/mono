Projects and Implementation​

Bio-Toilet

Supplying hundreds of waste-to-energy systems to schools in El Salvador

Providing national sanitation solutions in hundreds of El-Salvador schools with limited infrastructure. The systems include HomeBiogas Bio-Toilets, which turn human waste into renewable energy that can be used for clean cooking. These off-grid toilets save up to 80% of water with every flush.

Carbon Certified

HomeBiogas Expands Its Carbon Project to more than 7,000 households in Kenya

For thousands of farmers in Kenya, HomeBiogas is transforming daily life. This shift is not just about cooking—it’s about healthier homes, reduced carbon emissions, and financial savings. As we scale up, our goal is to bring this impact to 10,000 families by 2025—with a record 420 installations in February alone.

Farmers System

UNDP and HomeBiogas Empower 500 Rwandan Farmer Families

In 2023, a UNDP project introduced HomeBiogas technology to rural farmers in the Ngoma and Rwamagana districts. Our systems, which convert animal manure into cooking gas and biofertilizer, in order to save 14.6M kg of firewood and fueled 9M hours of clean cooking in 10 years.
