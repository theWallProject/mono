Our Spring Summer 26 Collection

The Spring/Summer 2026 collection is inspired by the quiet beauty of the coast, where natural landscapes and warm light, shape the rhythm of the day. From the soft tones of sand and stone to the sun-faded hues of earth and sea, the collection draws on a palette of natural, grounded colors that reflect the simplicity of coastal environments. Each piece is designed with love and care of the Baci team. Capturing the ease of summer and the timeless appeal of nature-inspired dressing.

Fluid silhouettes, breathable fabrics, and thoughtful details create garments that move comfortably through the day, from slow mornings by the water to evenings in the city. With Spring/Summer 2026, Baci brings the spirit of the coast into everyday life. From the coast to your closet, the collection celebrates sophisticated style rooted in nature.

Blending Fashion, Legacy & Innovation


At BACI, tradition and modern design come together. We draw from the past - classic techniques, natural materials, and careful craftsmanship - and bring them into the present with fresh shapes and thoughtful updates.

Each piece is made with care, using sustainable fabrics and low - impact processes whenever possible. The result is clothing that feels both timeless and new - designed to be worn often, loved deeply, and kept for years to come.

Boho Flow - New

Boho Flow is inspired by the effortless movement and spirit of bohemian style. The collection embraces fluid silhouettes, airy fabrics, and soft draping that create a natural sense of flow with every step. Grounded in earthy, sun-washed tones and organic textures, each piece reflects a relaxed, free-spirited aesthetic that feels both timeless and modern. Designed for comfort, movement, and individuality, Boho Flow brings the beauty of bohemian ease into everyday dressing

The Designer Behind The Brand

Our head designer, Batia Rutenberg, started the brand from humble beginnings. She was inspired by her grandmothers, who were seamstresses and embroiderers by trade, and also skilled in knitting, crocheting, sewing, and clothing design. Their creativity and craftsmanship had a lasting influence on her path.
