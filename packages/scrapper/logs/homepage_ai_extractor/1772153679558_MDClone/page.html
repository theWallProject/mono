How We’re Making a Difference

Our innovative healthcare analytics platform offers the potential to turn data into better outcomes for your patients. Here’s how we’ve been empowering healthcare institutions:

86% Avoided Hospitalizations for New Patients with Kidney Disease

Intermountain initiates proactive care management and deploys high-value therapy to improve the management of kidney disease. All patients who were identified at earlier stages of disease progression avoided dialysis and 86% of newly enrolled patients avoided hospitalizations.

Read the Press Release

Using AI Detection to Reduce Mortality Rates for Patients with Intracranial Hemorrhage

The Center of Stroke and Neurovascular Disorders at Sheba Medical Center participates in a multicenter study to evaluate artificial intelligence for the detection of intracranial hemorrhage.  The 30- and 120-day all-cause mortality was significantly reduced in the post-AI group when compared to the pre-AI group.

Read the Press Release

Utilizing Deep Learning Models to Predict Long-Term Mortality Rates for Patients with Liver Cirrhosis

Washington University explores data for predicting mortality among patients with liver cirrhosis.  Deep learning models can be used to predict longer-term mortality among patients with liver cirrhosis using common EHR data variables.

Read the Press Release
