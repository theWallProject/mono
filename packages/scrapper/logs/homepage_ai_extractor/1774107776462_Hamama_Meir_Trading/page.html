Hamama Meir Trading (1996) Ltd.

Hamama Meir Trading (1996) Ltd. is a leader in the import and distribution of raw materials for the food industry to the Israeli market. Publicly traded on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange under the symbol HMAM, Hamama has its origins as a family-owned business, with generations of hands-on experience in food-related global trade and commerce.
Today the Company employs 60 professionals, with broad-ranging experience and education in operations, logistics, quality assurance and compliance, procurement, sales, accounting and general administration.

Corporate Responsibility

Hamama follows the highest corporate standards and practices, with a focus on good corporate citizenship and strong business acumen.

  • We offer products that are healthy, reasonably price and make a difference to our customers.
  • Our products are of the highest quality, and we continually strive to improve ourselves and enhance the business of our customers.
  • We are committed to preserving the environment, and do our utmost to maximize material recycling and minimize our impact on the environment.
  • We support and are active in the local community, donating to an array of charitable causes and participating in a variety of events to benefit local citizens.

Logistics

Hamama imports its high-quality foodstuffs from some 200 suppliers in more than 50 countries spanning the globe, providing the local Israeli market access to the very best quality and value-for-money that can be found in the world today.

The Company imports some 4,000 containers per year. This translates into some 80 million kilograms of goods, or some 20% of the local “dry goods” food import market!

Hamama has a state-of-the-art logistics center in Kiryat Gat, Israel, where the goods are palletized and then delivered to its customers around the country, from Kiryat Shmona to Eilat. More than 95% of deliveries are made within two days.

Customers

Hamama has some 400 customers, ranging in size from Israel’s largest food conglomerates, wholesalers and distributors, to small and medium-sized natural food stores and health food shops. Hamama understands that each customer is unique, and spares no effort to meet the constantly changing needs and requirements of each and every one.

The Company offers its customers a full array of logistic services, including storage, sorting, packing, delivery and quality assurance. Hamama and its customers work together hand-in-hand to forecast demand accurately, interfacing with the logistics departments to ensure accurate, on-time delivery. By implementing JIT delivery, Hamama customers increase efficiency and decrease waste by receiving goods only as they are needed in the production process, thereby lowering inventory costs.

Quality

Hamama complies with the ISO 9001 quality management system, demonstrating its ability to consistently provide goods and service that meet customer and regulatory requirements.
The Company works with its customers who require supply in accordance with stringent standards such as the British Retail Consortium (BRC) and the ISO 22000 Food Safety Management System.
