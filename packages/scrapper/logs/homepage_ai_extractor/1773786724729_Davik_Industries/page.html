DAVIK - Your partner for adhesive solutions on a roll!

Innovative Manufacturer of Adhesive Solutions

Davik is an innovative adhesive solutions provider, serving global customers, offering a wide range of specialty tapes for various industries. Davik plant was established in 1982 and it includes two coating lines: hot melt/synthetic rubber adhesive & acrylic water based.

Placing a strong emphasis on R&D, Davik continually upgrades its technology, materials, and know-how. Most of our products are based on our innovation and our ability to find the exact solution to meet our customers’ demands.

One of Davik objectives is to impact positively our environment, thus focusing on sustainable solutions and manufacturing our products based solely on solar energy.

  We always look for new ideas and suppliers.
