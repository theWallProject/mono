Smart Cities.

SolView provides Smart Cities massive scale data analytics to support sustainability and emission targets that improve both the environmental and economic health of your community. 

The collaborative platform analyzes and creates a register of the solar potential and financial return for every rooftop in the community while incorporating time-sensitive and market-specific filters such as weather, tree shading, roof direction, and even local and regional financial incentives.

This newly created data is used to join residents, businesses and city resources to local installation options and seamlessly drive solar adoption to the economic benefit of the community. 

The result is reduced energy costs for residents, increased profits and job creation for local installers as well as increased renewable usage and positive economic impact for the city.

SolView is working with Smart Cities to create profitable programs that drive local renewable energy and economic growth around the world.
