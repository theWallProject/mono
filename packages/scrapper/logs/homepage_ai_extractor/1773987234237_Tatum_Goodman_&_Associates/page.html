About Tatum Goodman & Associates

Tatum Goodman & Associates is a boutique investment banking and financial brokerage based in Tel Aviv, Israel that specialises in global and strategic financial investments. Over 20 years in the brokering industry, as well as her extensive network of global strategic partners, has given Tatum the insight and intuition into how to create unparalleled opportunities for existing businesses, investors, and start-ups.

We deal with our investors’ needs according to their strategy and their focus, to provide appropriate opportunities. At Tatum Goodman & Associates we are about breaking boundaries and disrupting the norm when it comes to finding solutions for our clientele.
