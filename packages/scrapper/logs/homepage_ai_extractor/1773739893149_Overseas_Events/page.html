    About Us: Overseas - Destination Event Planners

    ABOUT US

    Overseas is an event planning company that produces one-of-a-kind company retreats in Europe. We aim to bring the retreat of your dreams to life at an affordable price, allowing you to focus only on forming memories with your employees that will last forever.

    ​

    Our team produces tailor-made events, unique to the customers' preferences. We believe that companies and start-ups can improve productivity by investing in a company retreat. Simply getting away from the stressful atmosphere of the workplace can do a world of good for management and employees.

    ​

    Overseas was founded by Oran & Hagar Kochavi. They have organised over 300 destination events (conferences, corporate events, LP meetings, Hackathons and more).

    Hagar Kochavi

    CEO

    Hagar is the Co-Founder & CEO of Overseas. Before founding 'Overseas', she worked for Glilot Capital Partners,
    a venture capital fund, ranked as the #1 performing VC in the world.

    Start-ups & technology have always been an integral part of Hagar's life and her passion for planing high quality events is what led her to found Overseas.
