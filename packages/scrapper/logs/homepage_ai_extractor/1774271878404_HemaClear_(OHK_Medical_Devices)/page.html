HemaClear®: SUPERB EXSANGUINATION

  • Bloodless, dry surgical field
  • Improved anatomical visibility
  • Reduced incidence of post-op DVT
  • Reduced incidence of pulmonary embolism

Ref: Nondrainage Decreases Blood Transfusion Need and Infection Rate in Bilateral Total Knee Arthroplasty. Demirkale I, et. al. J. Arthroplasty, 2013, (Table 5)

PARTIAL EXSANGUINATION with a Pneumatic Tourniquet = COMPLICATIONS

Others: PARTIAL EXSANGUINATION

  • 45-67% limb exsanguination
  • Intravascular clotting of blood left behind
  • Pulmonary embolism of migrating clots to heart & lungs when tourniquet is released
  • Risk of Brain Infarcts that may result in Post TKA Cognitive Dysfunction

Ref: Exsanguination of Lower Limbs in Healthy Male Subjects. Blond L, et. al. Acta Orthop Scand 2002; 73 (1): 89-92 Ref: Incomplete Leg Exsanguination: A Possible Cause of Post Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKA) Cognitive Deficit (CD). Gavriely N, OHK Archive – (Fig. 4)
