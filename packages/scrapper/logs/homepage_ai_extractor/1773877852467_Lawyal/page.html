Save time, increase profits

Backed by our extensive work with law firms, Lawyal is built to save you time and boost profitability – by turning best practices into an advanced, all-in-one practice management platform.

With Lawyal, you can easily manage your finances, control documents from anywhere, track tasks, and automate processes that were previously done manually.

 

Join other leading law firms who have upgraded to Lawyal

MOBILE & WEB

Any time, any place.

Enter billable hours and manage your firm from the office, on the road or from home. Our secure platform uses top security features and protocols to ensure your client’s privacy and protect your data.

BILLING LIST

Save time, save money, save the planet.

Our automated, paperless billing process allows you to assemble your billing reports and send them off to all relevant coworkers in one click.

BEST PRACTICES

Everything you need in one place.

Our intuitive and well-designed Visualized data dashboard provides all the essential data you need to make informed decisions at critical junctions.

DOCUMENTS MANAGEMENT

Smart, secure, searchable.

Upload, organize, and retrieve documents instantly-even scanned files. With powerful search and full cloud access, your firm’s knowledge is always at your fingertips.

TASK MANAGEMENT

Stay on top of it all.

Assign tasks, track progress, and streamline collaboration across teams. Lawyal's task manager keeps everyone aligned - on time, every time.
