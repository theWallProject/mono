Comprehensive Robotic System

The XACT ACE® Robotic System’s unique combination of features can overcome many challenges in accessing percutaneous procedures, so you can get exactly on target, every time.

Experience ACE

S-Drive™ Technology

Real-time, ongoing adjustments of the instrument trajectory to overcome tissue or target movement.

When a moving target throws you a curve.™

In percutaneous procedures a target can shift from its original location due to various reasons.

Remain exactly on target despite motion or obstacles with our proprietary S-Drive technology which enables the XACT ACE Robotic System® to quickly adapt from a linear to a non-linear trajectory with its robotic insertion and steering of instruments.

Precise Robotic Insertion

Robotic insertion and advancement of instruments to follow the user’s clinical input.

Remove manual inconsistencies.

The XACT ACE® Robotic System provides the opportunity to access difficult-to-reach tumors, lesions, or tissue samples far smaller than possible by manual insertion.

1. The user makes all the clinical decisions for the procedure. 
2. The ACE Robot precisely inserts instruments, removing inconsistencies that can occur manually.
3. Physician completes the procedure with or without the robot attached to the body.

Image-Guided Planning

The physician creates and downloads a procedure plan to the robot for execution.

Your clinical expertise; executed.

The XACT ACE Robotic System relies on the user’s clinical expertise to create a procedure plan for execution.

XACT’s planning software allows a user to select an entry point, target, and any obstacles to calculate a trajectory for the procedure.

Real-Time Monitoring

Adjust the trajectory throughout the procedure to remain on target.

Plan. Monitor. Adjust. Confirm.

Because a lesion can move, it’s important to verify throughout the procedure that the instrument remains on target.

The XACT ACE® Robotic System allows you to identify movement at any checkpoint that may occur during instrument insertion.* You can leverage its S-Drive™ technology to adjust the trajectory and reach the target with unparalleled accuracy. 

*Using real-time CT images taken during checkpoints

Synchronized Patient Mounting

Quickly and securely attaches to the patient to compensate for movement.

Safe. Easy set-up. All-in-one.

Hands-free and patient-mounted, the XACT ACE® Robotic System adapts to varying body sizes and target locations.

  • Quickly attaches directly on the patient
  • Moves with the patient, making it easier to remain on track despite breathing and movement
  • Maximizes the IR real estate with the registration element integrated directly into the ACE Robot

Unparalleled Accuracy. Unmatched Consistency.

Impressive clinical experience with unparalleled outcomes in multiple organs.*

  • <1.7mm average tip-to-target accuracy
  • 1 insertion-to-target
  • <8.5 minutes average skin-to-target time

*Overall system average accuracy based on system specifications is defined as 3mm. Average of 1.7mm accuracy is obtained from company clinical studies for biopsy procedures

  • Lung

    Lung nodules are particularly difficult to access because they are highly mobile and the needle path can be obstructed by anatomical obstacles. The XACT ACE Robotic System enables the user to plan and follow deep and complex trajectories, while the proprietary S-Drive™ technology compensates for respiratory and target movement to ensure the accurate placement of instruments.

    Small Lung Lesion Procedure
    Read Case Study
  • Liver

    The liver is mobile and adjacent to several vital organs and blood vessels.  Safely accessing lesions, especially those in the dome, can be problematic.  The XACT ACE Robotic System can accurately reach lesions with long and steep trajectories, while the proprietary S-Drive™ technology follows the user’s ongoing adjustments to the trajectory due to movement during the procedure.

    Liver Dome Procedure
    Read Case Study
  • Renal

    Renal lesions can be difficult to access with manual techniques due to tissue density. The XACT ACE Robotic System utilizes mechanical drivers with constant insertion speed to reach targets consistently and accurately with a single insertion.

    Renal Procedure: 0.62mm Tip-To-Target Accuracy
    Read Case Study
  • Lymph Nodes

    Lymph nodes can be small, surrounded by vital organs or vascular structures, and difficult to access.  With direct patient mounting, the XACT ACE Robotic System provides unlimited options for instrument insertion points to achieve specific and safe trajectories with unparalleled accuracy.

    Obturator Lymph Node
    Read Case Study
  • Omentum

    Lesions of the omentum are often long and narrow, making it challenging to find an appropriate insertion point that ensures the instrument remains tangential to the lesion. With direct patient mounting, the XACT ACE Robotic System provides optimal angulation that allows the advancement of instruments to reach the diseased tissue and spare non target anatomy.

    Renal Procedure: Robot Reached the Moving Target in One Shot
    Read Case Study
  • Adrenal

    Located near the kidney and diaphragm, the adrenal gland is a small and highly mobile structure. The XACT ACE Robotic System’s proprietary S-Drive™ technology leverages its ability to chart non-linear trajectories to successfully navigate through intercostal spaces to reach targets in the adrenal gland.

    Renal Procedure: Robot Reached the Moving Target in One Shot
    Read Case Study
  • Prostate

    The prostate can be extremely challenging to access due surrounding bowel and blood vessels.  The XACT ACE Robotic System enables physicians to make staged adjustments to the trajectory throughout the procedure, while the proprietary S-Drive™ technology compensates for movement to ensure unparalleled accuracy when placing the instruments.

    Prostate Drainage
    Read Case Study
