Platform Engineers

Reproducible at Scale

Why Flox?

  • Eliminate "works on my machine" errors organization-wide
  • Centralized visibility and control across the software lifecycle
  • Fast security updates and compliance with complete traceability
  • Integrate with CI/CD for instant, reliable pipelines
  • Enable teams to extend base environments while maintaining standards

“Flox removes the risk of environment drift by letting you replicate your exact production environment during development, regardless of architecture differences between OS'es.”

Priya AnanthasankarPrincipal Software Engineer at Microsoft

Developers

One Command, Zero Friction

Why Flox?

  • Skip the README archaeology and get productive in seconds
  • Work across the full stack without version conflicts
  • Share identical environments with your team via Git or FloxHub
  • No more "works on my machine" anxiety in CI/CD
  • Switch between projects without breaking your setup

“Flox takes the friction out of onboarding PostHog team members and contributors. Before, our local dev guide comprised 16 steps with 14 caveats. Now, it's just a universal `flox activate`.”

Michael MatlokaSenior Product Engineer at PostHog
Learn about Flox for Developers →

AI Agents

Build with AI, Ship with Flox

Why Flox?

  • AI assistants work with Flox environments natively using MCP
  • Rapid prototyping with batteries-included environments
  • GPU-accelerated development with CUDA, PyTorch, and more
  • Deploy runtime environments to K8s without container images
  • Perfect for hackathons and experiments with zero cleanup mess

“When you ask me to build something, I don't just write code—I create the entire environment. With Flox, I can search packages, install dependencies, and build and run your app.”

ClaudeAI Coding Agent
