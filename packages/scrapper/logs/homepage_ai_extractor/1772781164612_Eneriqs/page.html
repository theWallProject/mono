    MEET ENERIQS

    Experience the future of chiller optimization technology with our patented, predictive-AI solution, which is revolutionizing the HVAC industry. Our game-changing software employs a proactive management approach to deliver highly accurate and reliable HVAC optimization, reducing energy consumption without compromising building comfort. Our autonomous system is designed for easy, non-intrusive installation, and offers businesses a quick return on investment in their efforts to reduce carbon emissions and energy costs.

    OUR VISION

    Our goal is to establish a global presence by partnering with hundreds of distributors and deploying our technology to thousands of sites worldwide. As we look 5-10 years into the future, we see our cutting-edge technology becoming a vital component of building infrastructure, empowering businesses to make significant strides in reducing their carbon footprint, energy consumption, and costs.

    OUR MISSION

    We are dedicated to supporting businesses in their journey towards achieving net-zero emissions without sacrificing occupant comfort. We believe that sustainable practices can be integrated into building operations without compromising on the quality of indoor air. 

    OUR VALUES

    Innovation, initiative, and accountability are the cornerstones of Eneriqs. We're dedicated to pushing the boundaries of HVAC technology, taking the initiative to provide cutting-edge solutions, and holding ourselves accountable for delivering exceptional performance and savings to our customers. Join us in creating a sustainable future, with energy-efficient HVAC systems that make a real difference.
