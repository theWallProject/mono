Our mission
To build the world’s oracle network for human credentials

Velocity Network Foundation® is a non-profit membership organization, leading technology vendors and and solution providers, on a mission to build the next-gen data utility layer underlying mission-critical processes across finance, employment, work, business and collaboration.
We are reinventing how personal records are shared across the global market; empowering individuals, businesses, and institutions through transformational blockchain technology – public, open, trusted, and self-sovereign.

Introducing
Velocity Network

The most advanced, battle-tested decentralized infrastructure for verifiable credentials. Built from the ground up, Velocity Network is designed to meet the needs of institutions operating at scale in regulated environments. A game changing, blockchain based, decentralized utility layer, that replaces the outdated, fragmented way people represents personal and professional credentials. The Network is a public utility, compliant with W3C standards, governed by Velocity Network Foundation®, powering mission-critical processes across finance, employment, work, business and collaboration.

Individuals

Own your identity and personal records

Claim proofs of your identity, financial records, employment history, educational background, skills, and qualifications via Velocity Network. Store and manage them privately and share them exclusively with whomever you decide to.

Organizations

Turbocharge finance, employment, work, collaboration and compliance

Governments, employers, schools, license issuers, and other organizations use Velocity Network to issue verifiable digital credentials to their constituents, and instantly verify data shared by applicants, students, and employees.

Applications

Connect your application to enable your customers and users

Finance, employment, work, business and collaboration software providers connect their platforms to Velocity Network to offer their customers and users seamless issuing and verification functionality, create corporate value through the monetization of their participation, build new business models, and enhance existing ones.

How it works
One frictionless experience to exchange trusted personal records, using the power of blockchain. 

The App

Individuals use a credential application installed on their device to connect to their relevant authorities to claim digitally-signed proofs of their identity records, employment history, educational background, skills, or qualifications. These records are stored privately on the device and the individual controls who they share their data with.

Click here to see how individuals use their credential apps to navigate their careers.

The Credential Agent

Organizations use the credential agent software to connect to the Network, issue verifiable credentials, a digital career credentials, to their constituents, and instantly verify career data shared by applicants, students, and employees.

The Blockchain Network

Each credential issued on the Network has a verification key stored on Velocity’s high-performance blockchain. While the keys do not hold the credential data, or any personal information for that matter, they are used by organizations to verify credentials shared with them by individuals.

$VELOCITY The Token

Velocity utilizes the native $VELOCITY token to secure the decentralized network through Reputation Based Consensus and settlement for transaction fees.

The Foundation

The Foundation acts as the steward of the Velocity Network, hosting all involved parties, continuously guiding the building the rulebook, a common framework that ensures operational consistency and legal clarity for every transaction, and guiding the development of the decentralized protocols.
