Welcome to EGMO – Your Partner for Stainless Steel Fittings, Valves & Actuators

EGMO develops, manufactures and markets stainless steel fittings and flow products, including valves, actuators, manifolds and vessels for a range of industries, including semiconductors, solar energy, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics and food & beverage. Dedicated to turning innovative ideas and breakthrough technologies into leading products, the company provides services and products that increase productivity, product quality and profitability for customers.
As a member of the global NEUMO Ehrenberg Group, one of the world's largest privately-owned manufacturers and distributors of stainless steel products and metal cutting tools, EGMO operates a worldwide network of representatives and distributors.
