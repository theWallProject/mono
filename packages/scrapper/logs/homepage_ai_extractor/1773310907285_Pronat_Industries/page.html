Welcome to PRONAT

Pronat Industries is a world class converter of flexible materials. Founded in 1985, the company specializes in the production of die-cut parts for electronic applications. Our specialist areas are die-cut parts for EMI/RFI shielding, heat dissipation, electrical insulation, gasketing & sealing.
Pronat Medical produces die-cut consumables including complex patches, wearables, sensors and electrodes as specified by medical device manufacturers.
Pronat Aerospace Pronat is a specialist producer of light weight airframe parts using Rohacell® high density foams, Nomex® honeycomb,  pre-pregs, and other composite materials. Applications include flap and wing sections, ribs and mounting plates for UAVs, radar and radome mounts. With our engineering experience, CNC routing equipment and large stock of sheet materials, Pronat has the capability to produce parts to your specification.

 

Are you searching for Kapton® die cut parts, optical films, thermal management, EMI/RFI shielding? We can help with your product development.
