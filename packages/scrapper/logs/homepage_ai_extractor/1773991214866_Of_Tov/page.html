Why Us

 

Your Health is Important.

Our customer’s health and safety always come first! Which is why all of our products include all-natural ingredients, raised without added hormones and are in accordance with ISO 9001, HACCP and USDA approval.

100% In-House.

We oversee each step of the process to produce our 100% natural products, including direct supervision of the farms, self-preparation and the compliance of the most stringent of standards.

Less Oil, More Baking.

You don’t need to pull out the deep fryer to get the great taste of our chicken. Just bake them! Of Tov products are delicious without the oil.

Sustainability.

As a leading provider in the industry, Of Tov operates in a systematic manner, allowing us to improve our environmental performance and minimize our ecological footprint.
