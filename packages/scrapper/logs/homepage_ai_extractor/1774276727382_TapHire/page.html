    About

    ABOUT

    building the WORK network

    Our mission is to enable the next workplace. We think of it as "The Work Network". A safe place for consumers and providers to easily connect and conduct business. We are excited to share our humble first take on it and invite you to try it out.

    ​

    It all started at Google...

     

    While helping to make people in organizations work more efficiently, we realized that "work" means a lot more than just the inter-workings of the corporate world.

    ​

    "Work" fills our everyday lives. We search for providers to hire, we check for references, we negotiate, we make hiring decisions, we collaborate, we coordinate, and we do this over and over, and with many. 

    ​

    While it's a reality we learnt to accept, we think that working with providers still involves way too much friction. Friction that's holding back consumers and providers from fulfilling their employment potential.

    ​

    Think of yourself needing a provider - How do you find? how do you hire? how do you communicate? how do you pay? how do you evaluate? - TapHire brings both the framework and the network that makes finding and working with providers simpler and safer.

    ​

    We have pretty big plans for TapHire, aiming at being the next workplace for all people. Feel free to give it a try and let us know what you think.
