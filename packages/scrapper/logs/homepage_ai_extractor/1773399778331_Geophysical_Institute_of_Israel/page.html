The Geophysical Institute of Israel

The Geophysical Institute of Israel (GII) specializes in the geophysical mapping and characterization of the sub-surface. We provide services around the globe to private, public and governmental bodies.

The GII was founded in 1957 and has since then grown to service the Oil & Gas, Mines and Minerals, Sub-Surface Engineering and Homeland Security industries.

With Seismic, Magnetic, Gravimetric, Electro-Magnetic and Seismological services we provide a full suite of geophysics for your exploration programs on the way to discoveries...
