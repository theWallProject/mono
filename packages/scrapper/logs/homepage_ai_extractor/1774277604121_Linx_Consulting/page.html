Linx Consulting Market Reports and Conferences Now Part of SEMI

We’re excited to announce that Linx Consulting’s market reports and conferences have been acquired by SEMI.

By combining Linx’s trusted semiconductor and electronics materials reports and events with SEMI’s global reach and expertise, we’re well-positioned to provide unparalleled market insights and connections to members and the broader semiconductor industry.

Electronic Materials Insight for the Semiconductor Industry

At the crossroads of electronic thin film processes and the chemicals industry, Linx Consulting offers unique insights of electronic materials and processes for the semiconductor industry. As the premiere electronic consultants to the industry, Linx develops reports and services to help materials suppliers succeed in a volatile industry. In addition to reports and services, Linx is also involved in many industry conferences and events.

Stay abreast of semiconductor industry changes and their impact on your business with Linx reports, services and conferences.
