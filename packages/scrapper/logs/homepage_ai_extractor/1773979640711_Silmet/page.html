About Silmet

Silmet Ltd was founded in 1969 as a leading manufacturer of certified ADA amalgam alloys and over the past 20 years
has developed a full line of high-quality restorative
materials, including glass ionomers, composites,
and bonding agents.
Silmet Ltd has now been granted  EU MDR Certification for our complete portfolio of dental restoratives  materials.
Our state of the art facilities include a multi chambered manufacturing plant for amalgam production, a computerized quality control laboratory, automatic amalgam capsule production lines and a modern production line for Non Gamma dispersion amalgams. Special emphasis is put on particle size distribution, particle shape and heat treatment.
Silmet's strength lies in our commitment to extensive R&D combined with use of cutting edge technology in the dental industry. Our amalgam products offer excellent marginal integrity, superior carve-ability and easy condensation versatility.

Silmet Ltd Receives EU MDR Certification

We proudly announce that Silmet Ltd has been certified according to the EU Medical Device Regulation MDR 2017/745 . This latest certification means our complete product portfolio of Amalgams, Composites, Adhesives and Cements have now obtained EU MDR approval. Achieving EU MDR certification is a significant milestone for Silmet and marks our commitment to providing safe, effective, and cutting-edge dental restorative solutions.
