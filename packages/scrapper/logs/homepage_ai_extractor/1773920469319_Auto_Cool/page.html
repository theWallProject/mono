A/C

Auto Cool Ltd. is Israel's leading manufacturer of custom, heavy-duty air conditioning (A/C systems), automatic sliding, pivoting and tailgate door operations, and retractable electrical and mechanical steps for LHD & RHD vans, mini/midi-buses and vehicles for disabled persons. Established in 1982, Auto Cool develops, produce, installs and services all its systems in-house.

A right and left-hand applicable mechanism that electrically operates the sliding door on both sides of the vehicle. Designed for heavy-duty use, long operation hours, and extreme climate and road conditions, while maintaining the highest safety standards.

A right and left-hand applicable mechanism that electrically operates the vehicle’s front axial door. Designed for heavy-duty use, long operation hours, and extreme climate and road conditions.

A mechanism that electrically operates different commercial vehicles and vans’ tailgate rear doors. Designed for safe heavy-duty use, long operation hours, and extreme climate and road conditions

Auto Cool supplies a wide range of A/C systems for multiple applications, vehicle types, and models: rooftop, split and integrated systems that include different air distribution solutions. The systems can be operated with the vehicle’s own and/or an independent compressor.
