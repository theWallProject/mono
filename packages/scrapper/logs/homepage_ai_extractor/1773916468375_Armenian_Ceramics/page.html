Makers of Fine Hand Painted Artistic Ceramic Tiles, Tile Murals & Pottery

Since 1922, for over 100 years, our family has crafted exquisite Armenian ceramics, tile murals, and pottery in the heart of Jerusalem. Balian Armenian ceramics are crafted with Mediterranean tile elegance and deep cultural heritage. Our creations are designed to be timeless. Each piece is hand-painted using traditional techniques passed down through generations, making every tile a true work of art.

From detailed hand-painted wall murals to timeless artisanal pottery, our collections celebrate the rich heritage of Armenian craftsmanship and the elegance of Mediterranean tiles. Each tile and ceramic piece is a reflection of cultural storytelling, passed down through generations and brought to life with bold colors, floral motifs, and historic symbolism. Whether you're seeking authentic Armenian tiles for your home or decorative Mediterranean ceramics to elevate your space, our collections offer unmatched artistry and character.

Hand Crafted for Every Space

Our Armenian tiles and Mediterranean ceramics are crafted to bring timeless beauty to every corner of your home. In the kitchen, these hand-painted tiles serve as bold backsplashes and colorful accents, creating a vibrant focal point. In the bathroom, they add a touch of elegance and intricate detail to both walls and shower spaces. Outdoors, their durability and vibrant charm transform patios, fountains, and garden walls, infusing your exterior spaces with a unique, lively appeal.

Why Choose Balian Ceramics?

With over 100 years of ceramic artistry, Balian Armenian Ceramics stands as a testament to craftsmanship and tradition. Our authentic Armenian ceramics and Mediterranean tiles, handcrafted in Jerusalem, carry the rich cultural heritage of the region. We offer worldwide shipping and custom design services to bring our timeless pieces to your home, no matter where you are. As a family-owned heritage studio, we take pride in every detail, and our work is trusted by architects and collectors around the world for its quality and artistry.
