WHAT IS CYBERBULLYING

Cyberbullying includes social exclusion, verbal abuse, shaming, sexual harassment, pornography and pedophilia. Resulting in a variety of effects such as discomfort, deterioration in grades, depression, and at extreme cases even loss of life. Cyberbullying causes lifetime damage.
