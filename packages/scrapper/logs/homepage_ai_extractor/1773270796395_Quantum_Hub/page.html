Who are we?

Quantum-hub is an industry and tech platform that facilitates collaborations between industry leaders and startups. With our end-to-end value chain approach, we enable connections between these two worlds through the practical methodology of POCs testing. Our partners consist of five holding companies with 300+ subsidiaries worldwide, including Taavura-Livnat Group, Talcar Group, VDL Group, Hyundai Motor Group, and Bazan Group.

 

INDUSTRIES WE COVER.

TECHNOLOGy WE SEEK.

At Quantum, our main focus areas revolve around Automotive, Logistics, Industry 4.0, and Energy technologies. We actively scout for innovative solutions that align with the needs of our partners, leveraging our extensive ecosystem knowledge and expertise. But we don’t stop at making connections; we take pride in executing end-to-end projects and serving as local beta sites – Our Playground.

DISCOVER MORE

Setting up innovation for mutual success

At Quantum, we have created powerful methods for success. By gaining a deep understanding of our partners’ needs and designing the POC together, we reduce risks and bridge the gap between tech and industry, benefiting both startups and industry leaders.

COMPREHENSIVE OFFERINGS

Quantum is a consortium of partners that scouting, exploring and testing technologies at our local beta sites to create a meeting point between core-industries and startups to create mutual impact. Through a seamless end-to-end value-chain approach, we support throughout the entire journey—from conceptualizing and designing the POC to its execution and eventual commercialization.

FOCUS ON MARKET NEEDS

Due to our close relationships with our partners, we are guided by their core needs and actual pain points. Our in-depth familiarity combined with the ability to identify technologies, allows us to locate those relevant established startups and ensure the development of viable, market ready solutions. This Commercially focused approach sets up apart and leads to higher success rates.

TECH VALIDATION

Our innovation playground takes the technical assessment to another level by allowing us to provide the designing, planning and the execution of POCs in-house. By understanding the business potential of relevant technologies and having the partners with which to experiment, we deliver optimal results without interfering with the companies’ daily operations.

TRUSTED KNOWLEDGE

Our expert technical team closely escorts and supports the startups and the industries we work with in every step of the process. This enables us to reduce risk and empower industry partners to adopt innovative technologies. By building trust and facilitating innovation, we make it easier for our partners to navigate the ever-evolving tech landscape, and for startups to navigate the challenges on the way to commercialization.

MUTUAL IMPACT

Although corporations and startups potentially could create a powerful synergy, eventually they communicate differently. We translate the language of corporations to startups and vice versa. Speaking each other’s language, they complement one another, leveraging their respective strengths to create impactful outcomes.

SPARK is a PoC runway program designed to connect startups with our partners – world-class leading corporations, and help them expedite business collaborations and commercialization processes.
The program is focused on MVP+ startups who have the vision and potential to bring cutting-edge technologies to market and shape the future. The program is equity-free.
