Frequency Asked Questions

How does Cherrypick typically work with its clients?

Cherrypick offers two main services: 1) Full project ownership: We help product managers in defining the "features scope", design the software architecture, and handle development from ideation to launch. 2) Joining an existing R&D team: we take active role in implementing coding tasks, help the existing teams to increase capacity, quality and speed. On top of that, we help with optimizing cloud infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines.

What technologies does Cherrypick specialize in?

Cherrypick has a vast experience in Fullstack development (React, Node.js, Python, Django, Flask, etc.), Cloud Infrastructure, for example running containers and managed data-basese on AWS, GCP. Setting up CI/CD pipelines. We also have experience in mobile development (iOS, Android).

How can I determine if Cherrypick is the right fit for my project?

Cherrypick is a boutique software development firm with a reputation for delivering high-quality products. While we may not be the lowest-cost option, if you seek a dedicated team to help with critical product and tech decisions and deliver top-tier results, we are likely the perfect fit for your project.

What is the typical duration of a project?

Many of our client relationships extend beyond one year, a testament to our commitment to excellence and client satisfaction. However, we also cater to shorter-term, scoped projects to meet specific needs.

How can I learn more about potential collaboration?

We are always eager to engage with new potential clients. To initiate a conversation, simply click on the 'Contact Us' button at the top right corner of the screen, fill in your details, and we will promptly reach out to you.

Is Cherrypick available to start a new project immediately?

Our team availability may vary. Please reach out to us to ask about our current capacity and discuss your project timeline.
