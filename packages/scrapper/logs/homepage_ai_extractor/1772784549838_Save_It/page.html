Who Are We?

Save It was founded by Omer Mosenzon Ilan & Chen Halfon, driven by a shared passion and deep commitment to creating a better world. From a young age, both were actively involved in environmental initiatives, exploring the field and launching independent projects.

In 2015, Omer and Chen left their conventional jobs, joined forces, and embarked on an extraordinary journey with a bold vision of-Saving It!

Today, Save It has grown into a company with dedicated employees. We manage waste operations for more than 10 leading organizations in Israel, oversee waste solutions for more than 15 towers across the country, and provide services for large-scale events every year.
