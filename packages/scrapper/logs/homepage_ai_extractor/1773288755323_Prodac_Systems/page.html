Who We Are

PRODAC Systems is a leading international software company with two decades of successful experience in pioneering Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) and Enterprise Manufacturing Intelligence (EMI) solutions for filling and packaging operations, focusing on the food, beverage and brewing industries.

For the last twenty years, PRODAC has been installed and used at leading plants around the world.
