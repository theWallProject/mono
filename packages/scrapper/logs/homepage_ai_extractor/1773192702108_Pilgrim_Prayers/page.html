The Hill that faces the Temple
The Mount of Olives hillside, facing the Temple Mount, has drawn believers for millennia seeking burial in the hope of resurrection. Sacred to Jews and Christians alike, it recalls Jesus’ final days and promised return. Even from afar, pilgrims can send Mount of Olives prayer requests and share in this faith.

Purim in the shelter: how faith kept moving
During Purim in Jerusalem, believers continued the ancient story of Esther despite uncertain circumstances. Gatherings were smaller, some even in shelters, yet the megillah was still read, and prayers reached the Western Wall. The moment reflected a timeless truth: faith moves forward, acting and praying even when conditions are far from ideal.

A quiet morning at the Garden Tomb
At Jerusalem’s Garden Tomb, a woman burdened by unanswered prayers finds peace not through words, but stillness. In sacred silence, she senses God’s presence beside her. This quiet place reminds believers that faith isn’t demanding answers; it’s resting in God’s nearness, knowing they are never alone.
