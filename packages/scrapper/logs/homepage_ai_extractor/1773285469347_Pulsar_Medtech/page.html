Product​

Meet the FHP - a truly Smart & Wearable device​

  • Our FHP is designed as a flexible, thin electronic patch, which uses the same PEMF treatment modality as existing BGS devices, but with superior technology
  • Instead of big and cumbersome devices, the FHP is a small, single-use, silicone patch that can be placed comfortably anywhere on the body
  • Can be placed under cast.
  • It works 24/7 delivering continues treatment to the tissue and can be worn even during showers
  • Once placed on the body, the FHP is fully automatic and does not require any action by the patient – not even recharging
  • High treatment compliance.

Smart device

The FHP incorporates a verity of sensors and interacts with a designated cellphone app

​

Sensory systems such as movement, temperature and pressure, help monitor the treatment, improve functional outcome and can reduce risks

Technology

The well-known PEMF treatment

  • Pulsed Electro-Magnetic Fields (PEMF) is considered by the FDA to be a safe and effective way to stimulate bone growth​
  • Our patent-protected Energy Saving Circuit (ESC) technology, enables the FHP to generate the same PEMF energy, even though it is a thin flexible patch
  • Pulsar’s technology is protected by 16 patents
