We are GTH Software, a unique and experienced software development company who has developed one of the most successful online gaming platforms with more than 60 in-house games for the online gaming industry. Founded in 2005 and located in the heart of Tel Aviv , our company includes more than 30 employees in our Frontend, Backend, Creative studio, Product and QA departments, who are enjoying a fun and family environment with working on cutting-edge technologies, such as Java, Elastic Search, Apache Kafka, Vue.js and Pixi.js.
