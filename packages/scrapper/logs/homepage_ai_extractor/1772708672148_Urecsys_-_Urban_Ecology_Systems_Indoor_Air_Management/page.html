We spend 90% of our lives indoors, relying on buildings’ air handling units (AHUs) to provide clean, fresh air. While bringing in outdoor air is essential, it becomes a problem when that air is polluted.

Air pollution is a growing issue in urban areas, with sources ranging from vehicles and factories to construction and wildfires. Its effects are serious—at best, it hampers our productivity, and at worst, it poses severe risks to our health and longevity. Managing and mitigating air pollution is challenging and costly, yet crucial.

Our patented technology offers a smarter solution. Effortlessly protect your building against harmful pollutants while reducing costs, ensuring compliance, and creating a safer indoor environment for everyone.

Intelligent Ventilation for Healthier, Certified, Cost-Efficient Buildings

Certified Healthy Environment

Our solutions ensure your building meets ASHRAE ventilation standards and supports your efforts to earn LEED points, comply with BREEAM, and achieve WELL certification. By improving air quality, we help boost employee productivity, reduce sick days, and enhance overall well-being—all while keeping costs down.

Energy Efficiency

Improving ventilation often leads to higher energy consumption. Our AI-driven solutions strike the perfect balance, enhancing indoor air quality while simultaneously reducing energy use, helping you achieve both better health and greater efficiency

Carbon Neutrality

By optimizing ventilation and air quality management, our system reduces energy consumption and minimizes greenhouse gas emissions. This accelerates your sustainability goals, lowers your building’s carbon footprint, and supports your ESG reporting objectives.
