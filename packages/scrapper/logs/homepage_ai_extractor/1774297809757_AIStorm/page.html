About AIStorm

AIStorm has won many awards for its foundational technology.  These include FAST COMPANY AWARD, FROST AND SULLIVAN AWARD, EETIMES TOP100 STARTUPS, MISTY AWARD, etc.   What is being recognized is that AIStorm is different.  We are a new kind of plumbing that can co-exist with the old plumbing & can improve existing solutions.  Major chip vendors and model makers are our customers, not our competitors.  We retrofit solutions such that our technology exists along with their technology, improving performance, power, and size.  AIStorm has hundreds of millions of dollars in design wins with major companies where we have modified existing silicon, models or software compilers.  Our solutions include AI solutions, dataconverter solutions, image signal processing solutions (ISP), audio solutions, memory implementations and AIStorm has fundamental solutions for block chain, LLMs, and on device training, and software tools.  AIStorm sells IP blocks compatible with leading foundries like TSMC, custom development using its IP blocks, models, model development, labelling, semiconductor IC products, software development and complete solutions for specific applications.
