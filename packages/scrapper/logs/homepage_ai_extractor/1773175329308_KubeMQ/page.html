For regulated & controlled environments

Messaging under compliance constraints

In regulated, controlled, or air gapped deployments, compliance rules make messaging hard. No egress policies and cloud isolated networks block external access. External control planes and broker sprawl add heavy ops overhead and vendor lock in. The impact is real: higher audit risk, slower delivery, higher TCO, and fragmented governance. Teams need reliable, self managed messaging they fully control.

The solution

How KubeMQ solves regulated-environment messaging

Kubernetes native, runs anywhere
Kubernetes-native messaging that runs wherever you do—from air-gapped, on-prem estates to modern cloud Kubernetes. Deploy with Helm/YAML, plug into GitOps workflows, and support hybrid/multi-cluster topologies with one operational model.
Self-managed
Run KubeMQ fully inside your perimeter with zero egress and no external control plane. Get built-in tracing, replay, retention, and audit trails plus RBAC/SSO/LDAP—so you meet regulatory demands and pass audits without bolted-on systems.
All messaging patterns
One backbone for all patterns: queues, streams, pub/sub, and request/reply. Eliminate multiple brokers, reduce integration sprawl, and give teams a single, consistent way to build and operate messaging.
Lower TCO
Consolidate brokers, cut infrastructure and ops costs, and still scale. KubeMQ’s small footprint and high throughput, proven HA/DR patterns, and native integrations/connectors let you standardize messaging without custom glue code—lowering total cost of ownership.

Enhancing service mobility in the 5G edge cloud and beyond

Researchers from Ericsson recommend KubeMQ as the enabler for creating a multi-edge cloud, Kubernetes-native, in which latency is a critical factor.

Like a cloud that follows you wherever you go, edge cloud services need to match the mobility of terminals to deliver latency-critical 5G and future 6G. Part of this challenge may require moving the terminal’s state into the new edge cloud.
