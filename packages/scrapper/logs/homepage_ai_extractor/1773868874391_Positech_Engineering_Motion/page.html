Taking your motion control forward

Positech, an ITS company, is an industry leader in complex challenges, designing top-of-the-line, high-performance motion control systems for military and civilian use.

Expertise and experience

We are leaders in engineering high-performance, advanced motion control systems. We work relentlessly to create cutting-edge solutions that provide the absolute highest standards in motion. Our only boundary is physics.

Resourcefulness

Our talented team of highly inventive engineers creates products that raise the bar, every single time. We ambitiously seek solutions to challenges, leaving no stone unturned until we achieve a breakthrough.

Better together

Positech’s engineers are an integral part of the client’s team, striving together to achieve success.

Positech is an ITS company

Our partnership with ITS, a leader in end-to-end development and manufacturing solutions of fully integrated machines, complements our abilities and provides you with a streamlined Design to Manufacturing service.

The teams at ITS and Positech share the same passion for engineering excellence. This unique synergy allows us to design cutting-edge motion systems, then manufacture them at scale while meeting the highest technological standards.

We call this an ecosystem for engineering excellence.
