FAQs

What are real-world assets?

Real-world assets are digital assets backed by tangible or financial assets, such as securities, that are issued and traded using blockchain technology.

How is this different from other platforms?

INX offers regulated real world assets and digital securities, not just cryptocurrency.

Do I control my assets?

Yes. Assets are held using secure custody infrastructure designed to give investors control and protection.

Who can trade cryptocurrencies and real-world assets on INX by Republic?

Availability depends on asset type, investor eligibility, and jurisdiction. Some assets may be limited to eligible investors.

Can I fund my account with fiat?

Yes. You can fund your account using fiat, cryptocurrency, or stablecoins, depending on availability.

Is trading available 24/7?

Yes. Markets operate continuously on blockchain infrastructure, though availability may vary by asset.
