Have questions? We have answers.

Is ehouse is the right fit for you?

ehouse is the place for Businesses and entrepreneurs that operate and sell physical goods, typically through online channels and with the support of logistics providers. If you need support on these fronts, we are the right fit for you, even if you pre-launch!

What kind of storage unit are available at ehouse?

We offer a variety of storage unit sizes – from shelves, pallets, and lockers to full studios – and the cost varies depending on the type and size of the space. Just leave us some specific details in the form, and we’ll get back to you with a tailored offer.

‍

What is the monthly fee at ehouse consist of?

ehouse monthly fee consists of a subscription fee and a payment for the storage volume. The subscription includes access to our proprietary software, serviced facilities and common spaces, photography studios, multi-purpose rooms, community events, and more. Additional services, such as logistics services, are billed monthly according to our published services rates.

What’s the membership duration?

Tiered membership can be upgraded, downgraded, or even terminated within a month notice.

Do I have to be onsite to receive my incoming or outgoing shipments?

No, our staff will accept shipments on your behalf. We take care of all your daily shipping needs so you can travel abroad and be confident that your business is in good hands.
