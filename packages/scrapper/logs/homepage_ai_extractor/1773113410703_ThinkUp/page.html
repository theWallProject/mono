What is ThinkUp?

ThinkUp is an AI-powered platform designed to help early-stage entrepreneurs refine, validate, and scale their business ideas. It provides tools, insights, and expert guidance to help you succeed in your entrepreneurial journey.

How do I get started?

Getting started is easy! Simply sign up, receive a 10-day free trial, and start using our platform to refine your business idea and prepare it for investor readiness.

Who can benefit from ThinkUp?

ThinkUp is perfect for anyone with a business idea, whether you're just starting out or looking to take your startup to the next level. Our platform is tailored for entrepreneurs who want expert mentorship, actionable insights, and a clear path to funding.

How does ThinkUp help with market research?

ThinkUp’s research tools allow you to analyze competitors, understand market demand, and refine your business strategy based on real data, not assumptions. This helps you identify opportunities and gaps in the market.

What is the Idea Validation Overview?

Our Idea Validation Overview provides you with a snapshot of how your idea performs in terms of market fit and viability. It gives you insights into strengths and areas for improvement, helping you refine your idea for success.

How can I connect with a mentor?

If you need guidance, simply reach out through our chatbot, and we’ll connect you with a mentor in marketing or entrepreneurship. Our mentors are here to offer advice and support as you grow your business.

Can I try ThinkUp for free?

Absolutely! We offer a 10-day free trial, allowing you to explore the platform, test all the tools, and kickstart your entrepreneurial journey with no commitment or credit card required. After the trial ends, you will be asked to choose one of our paid plans that best suits your needs.

What makes ThinkUp different from other business tools?

Unlike other tools, ThinkUp combines AI-powered insights, expert mentorship, and funding opportunities in one platform. It's designed to guide you every step of the way, from idea validation to securing investment.

What industries does ThinkUp support?

ThinkUp is designed to support a wide range of ideas across various sectors, including technology, software, retail, fintech, and more. If you have a scalable and innovative idea, ThinkUp can help you refine, validate, and grow it into a successful venture.

Is my privacy protected on ThinkUp?

Your privacy is extremely important to us. We don’t have access to your ideas unless you choose to share them with us for feedback or to apply for funding. Your information is kept secure, and you’re in full control of what you share. We’re committed to safeguarding your data while helping you grow your business.

How can I get up to $100K in funding?

To qualify for up to $100K in funding, you need to complete all the steps on the ThinkUp platform and finalize your business dashboard. Once your dashboard is ready, share the link with us after agreeing to our terms and conditions. Our expert investment committee will review your application and select the most promising startups for funding.

What are the investment criteria?

Our investment committee evaluates startups based on factors like market potential, business model, uniqueness of the idea, and your ability to execute. We prioritize innovative and promising ideas with strong growth potential. Learn more about the full investment criteria on our platform.

Can I apply for funding if my idea is still in the early stages?

Yes! ThinkUp is designed to support early-stage entrepreneurs. As long as you complete the necessary steps on the platform and provide a clear, promising business plan, you’re eligible to apply for funding.

Are you an accelerator?

No, we’re not an accelerator. We’re a platform designed to help founders, including those in accelerators, build and validate their startups. We offer a structured framework with AI-powered insights to guide you through every step of your startup journey, from idea validation to strategy development.
