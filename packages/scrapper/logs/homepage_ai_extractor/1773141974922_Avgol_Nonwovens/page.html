What we offer

Avgol is a global leader in the manufacture of high-quality, high-performance spun melt nonwoven materials for a wide range of market sectors including hygiene, medical and functional materials.

Innovations

In order to anticipate and fulfill the needs of consumers while creating high performance technologies that meet the demands of modern commerce, Avgol developed the unique Forward Innovative Thinking (FIT™) suite of fabric solutions.

Who is Avgol

Since 1988, Avgol® has led the global hygiene market with the most comprehensive range of ultra-lightweight spun-melt nonwoven fabrics for the hygiene products market.

We are committed to sustainability

Our global pledge: Environmental, social, and economic responsibility is core to all that we at Avgol® are. It is no small challenge for Avgol to take on the role of being the definitive leader in polypropylene spunlaid fabrics to the commercial brands around the world.
