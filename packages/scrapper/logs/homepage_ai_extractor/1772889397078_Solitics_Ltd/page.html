Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is Solitics?

    Solitics is a customer engagement platform that turns all your data into real-time, hyper-personalised experiences. Brands can respond to anything their customers do — or don’t do — in 0.8 seconds or less.

    That means smarter visitor activation, stronger retention and built in gamification that feels native to your brand. Businesses use Solitics to optimise engagement and make data-driven decisions that fuel growth.

  • What types of businesses can benefit from Solitics?

    If your users expect more, Solitics is built for you. Whether you’re in iGaming, Trading or Fintech, our platform helps you create real-time, data-driven experiences that drive engagement, loyalty and growth. Fast.

  • What makes Solitics different?

    Solitics is built for speed, reacting to every user action or inaction within 1.8 seconds. You can connect 90+ tools out of the box and go live in weeks, without heavy dev work. The platform handles massive volumes of data from every source – including market and sports feeds – and keeps it all live, all the time.

    Where most platforms focus only on retention, that’s just the beginning for Solitics. Our platform delivers real-time, personalised engagement across the entire lifecycle – from activating anonymous visitors to re-engaging your most valuable customers. Predictive AI is fully integrated and trained on your data, so you can forecast churn and loyalty – and act on it in real time. And with native gamification, you can launch on-brand missions and interactive popups without extra tools or effort.

  • What support and training does Solitics offer?

    Solitics is more than a platform. We’re a partner. Every customer gets hands-on onboarding, technical support and a dedicated success manager. You’ll also have access to our full knowledge base, with tutorials, walkthroughs and best practices. Everything you need to consistently hit your KPIs. And keep growing.

  • Is Solitics compliant with data privacy and security standards?

    Yes. Solitics is fully GDPR-compliant, SOC 2 compliant, and ISO/IEC 27001 certified – built to support the data privacy, security and operational transparency required by regulated brokers and financial platforms. From consent management to audit logs and role-based access, everything is built for compliance. Without compromising performance.

    Read more about these security standards on our Security & Compliance page.
