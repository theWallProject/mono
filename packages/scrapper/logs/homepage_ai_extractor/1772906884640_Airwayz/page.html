Airwayz enables organizations responsible for complex, high-value environments to control their airspace, secure their assets, and defend their skies.

What We Enable

Control

Continuous, real-time planning, control, and deconfliction of mixed low-altitude airspace

Security

Asset-centric protection that adapts dynamically to operational needs

Defense
