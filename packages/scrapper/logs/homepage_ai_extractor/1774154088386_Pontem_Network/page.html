Why deploy on Move VM?

Move language and Move VM enable a safe, scalable, and incredibly fast infrastructure for mass Web3 adoption

Secure

Enables automated formal verification

Resistant to re-entrancy & other common attacks

Built-in access control & bytecode verifier

Efficient

Optimized for parallel execution & horizontal scaling

Account-based and with a clear “asset as resource” structure

Up to 160k TPS and sub-second finality

Flexible

Large ecosystem with L1s and L2s and hundreds of dApps
