Your Questions Answered

What Currencies Does Cedar Money Support?

Cedar supports a variety of currencies, including USD, EUR, GBP, and many African currencies.

What Is The Cost of Using Cedar Money? 

Using the Cedar platform is free and there are no subscription or set-up fees, however, transaction fees apply, which are reflected in the FX cost.

How Long Does It Take to Receive Payments from Africa? 

Cedar offers fast payment processing, with most payments being processed within 24 hours (T+1 settlements).

How Do I Open a New Account? 

To open a new account, simply register here, or download Cedar Money App on your IOS or Google Play Store, and follow the on-screen instructions. You will need to provide some basic information about your business.

What Does the Onboarding and KYC Process Entail? 

The onboarding and KYC process involves verifying your business identity and ensuring they comply with regulatory requirements. This may include providing documents such as your Certificate of Incorporation, National ID, Proof of Business Address, etc.

What are Stablecoins, and how do they work in Cross-border Payments?

Stablecoins are digital assets pegged to a stable asset like the US dollar. They empower businesses to bypass traditional inefficiencies in cross-border payments, offering speed, cost savings, and reliability. When integrated with user-friendly platforms like Cedar Money, Stablecoins can revolutionize global B2B transactions. Cedar leverages stable coins to facilitate faster, cheaper, and more secure payments.

How does Cedar Money assist Exporters in the US, UK, China, and Globally, with Foreign Currency Collection in Africa?

Cedar streamlines cross-border payments, making it easier for exporters to collect and disburse funds in African currencies. With Cedar, you can receive payments from African businesses directly into your bank account, eliminating the need for complex banking procedures, reducing transaction fees as well as offering quick T+1-T+2 settlement time.Cedar understands the problems African businesses face and hence can facilitate a much smoother collection process.

What Is The Timeline for Reviewing My Onboarding Documents Once They are Submitted?

We strive to review and process onboarding applications as quickly as possible. Typically, the review process takes 2-7 business days.
