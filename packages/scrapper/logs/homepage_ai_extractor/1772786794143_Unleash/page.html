Customizable deployment options

Providing the flexibility to deploy AI exclusively in your preferred cloud environment.

Shared-tenant

Best for: Cost, faster deployment.
Unleash will provide a dedicated instance for your company on a server shared with other customers.

Single-tenant

Best for: Secured SaaS deployment.
Unleash will provide a dedicated server for your exclusive use of the Unleash platform.

Self-hosting

Best for: Maximum security, cost savings.
Use Unleash’s platform in your company’s cloud as well as your existing LLM tokens.
