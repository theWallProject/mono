FAQs

Who is Blix for?

Blix is designed for market research agencies, customer insights teams, and anyone looking to turn open-ended feedback into actionable insights quickly and efficiently. Whether you're analyzing survey responses, online reviews, or customer support tickets, Blix helps you get the job done without the hassle of manual coding. It's perfect for professionals who need fast, accurate insights while keeping full control over the process.

How accurate is Blix’s coding?

Blix is designed for professional-grade accuracy. Our AI delivers precise coding that can match or even surpass manual coding, saving you time while maintaining high quality. You can always review and adjust the results to ensure everything meets your standards.

Can I customize the coding process to fit my needs?

Absolutely! Blix gives you full control over the coding process. You can adjust categories, review results, and fine-tune the analysis to fit your unique needs, ensuring the insights are relevant to your business.

Is Blix suitable for large datasets?

Yes! Blix is built to scale, whether you’re working with a small survey or massive datasets. It can handle large volumes of data efficiently without compromising speed or accuracy.

How is Blix different from other coding tools?

Blix stands out by offering a quick, easy-to-use platform with full control and customization options. Unlike other tools that may require extensive setup or manual effort, Blix is designed to be effortless while delivering professional, accurate results—without the grunt work.

Is there a trial period?

Yes, we offer a free trial period so you can experience Blix firsthand before making any commitment. Ready to see it in action? Book a demo today and our team will walk you through the platform and help you get started.

Do I need a subscription?

Not necessarily. We offer both subscription plans and flexible pay-as-you-go options, so you can choose what works best for your needs.
