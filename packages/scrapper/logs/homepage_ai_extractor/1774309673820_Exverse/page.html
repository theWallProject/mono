Millions of humans and animals died, powerless against this mysterious and sudden calamity. Forced to flee from death, humanity abandoned their home planet, finding refuge in orbital stations and spacecraft beyond Earth’s atmosphere…

Players use $EXVG to enter matches, purchase premium items, unlock exclusive content, and participate in special events. It is integral to the in-game economy, enhancing the gaming experience and enabling progress. $EXVG can also be traded, creating a dynamic marketplace within Exverse.
