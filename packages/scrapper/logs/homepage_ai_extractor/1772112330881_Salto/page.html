Own your Deployments

Enhance the quality and speed of your deployments

Easily deploy all your changes including customizations and scripts so you won't have to waste time on manual deployment tasks.

Environment Alignment

Keep your environments in sync at all times

Align your environments whenever needed and reduce team conflicts and deployment errors. Choose the components you would like to align and never lose your Sandbox work again.

Documentation

Version control your configurations and roll back

Push your configuration changes to a source control system of your choice to easily track the history of changes. Troubleshoot faster, rollback to previous versions, and collaborate with team members.

CI/CD

Improve collaboration between admins and developers

Establish a unified deployment pipeline for your configuration changes, seamlessly integrating the efforts of both admins and developers. Automate testing and deployment tasks to expedite the delivery of new business requirements to production and minimize the risk of deploying malfunctioning customizations.

Streamline approvals for your configuration changes

Automatically generate a pull request in your Git repository for every Salto deployment, allowing your team and all relevant stakeholders to review, discuss, and approve configuration changes before they go live. Gate your deployments with PRs to ensure that only approved changes are deployed.

BACKUP & RESTORE

Enable flexible configuration backup and fast recovery

Automatically back up your configurations and quickly restore previous versions in case of an emergency or human error.

CHANGE PLANNING MADE EASY

Search all your customizations as easy as Ctrl+F

Use Salto's free text search feature and see the results in the right context. Find hardcoded values and use a simple Find and Replace to change them in bulk.

Understand the impact of every change

Search for any element in your environment, identify references and dependencies, and understand the impact of a change before making it.

Collaboration

Keep your team on the same page

With Salto's SaaS solution, your team can access the most up-to-date version of the configuration, compare it to their own, sync it, make changes, and share them with the team.

Get notified about changes

Stay on top of the changes you care about the most. With Salto you can receive Slack or email notifications when something changes in your environment.

SALTO AI

Get instant and complete insight into every configuration element

With the power of AI and our NaCl, Salto can generate a user-friendly description of every element in your configuration, making it really easy for everyone on your team to understand how it’s built and what exactly it does.
