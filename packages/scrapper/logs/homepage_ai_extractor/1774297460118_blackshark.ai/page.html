From Pixels to a Planetary World Model

Blackshark.ai transforms raw sensor imagery into structured digital representations of the physical world. This process is powered by a multi-layer platform architecture:

01 Sensing

Imagery

Global sensor inputs from satellites, aerial systems, drones, and ground sensors.

02 Perception

HUNTR™

Analyst-driven AI for rapid detection and extraction of infrastructure and terrain.

03 World Model Compute

VEOS™

Distributed processing that converts trillions of pixels into structured world models.

04 World Generation

REPLIKA™

Reconstruction of the physical world into simulation-ready 3D environments.

05 Operational Environments

Integrations

Interactive digital worlds for exploration, planning, and spatial analysis.

06 Infrastructure

Deployments

Deployable across cloud, sovereign on-premise and edge infrastructure.

One Platform. Multiple Operational Domains.

Autonomous systems, disaster response, digital twins all run on the same geospatial Al platform.

High Frequency Mapping

Continuously update digital models of the world.

Physical AI Training

Rapid Disaster Response

A different origin story.

Most AI companies started with data.
Blackshark.ai started with worlds.

Before building geospatial AI, our team built large-scale simulation environments in the video game industry.

We solved how to transform massive geospatial datasets into coherent digital worlds.
Eventually we realized something.

The same technology used to generate virtual worlds could teach machines to understand the real one.

From pixels to intelligence.

Satellite and aerial imagery become structured models of terrain, infrastructure and the human environment, allowing humans and machines to explore, analyze, and train on the world before acting within it.

Blackshark was founded in 2021 and is backed by top-tier investors including M12, Point72 and IQT.
