About us

Established in 1991, IPU Industries Ltd. has built a strong reputation for innovation, quality, and out-of-the box solutions. With more than forty years of expertise in advanced plastic injection technologies, ongoing research & development, superior engineering, IPU has designed, developed, and manufactured complex products for the medical devices, water flow system, automotive, and high-end technology markets.
