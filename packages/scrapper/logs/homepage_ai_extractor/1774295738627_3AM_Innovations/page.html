The gold standard in team tracking.

Engineered to keep your team safe, connected, and in control no matter what you face.

Available as an app on iOS and Android, FLORIAN® tracks every move in real time on a dynamic 3D map, giving you full visibility of every person and resource, wherever they are.

Maximum capability. Maximum impact. Transform chaos into clarity with FLORIAN®.

Deployable kits provide quick and easy set-up you can establish full situational awareness. Equipped with FLORIAN enabled devices, coordinate your team and supporting agencies from a single map.

With different sizes to choose from, these kits were made to be versatile. Whether you are coordinating a large event, or sending out an emergency rapid deploy team, we have an APL option for you.
