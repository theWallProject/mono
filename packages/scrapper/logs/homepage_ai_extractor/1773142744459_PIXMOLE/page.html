The ultimate solution to Track, Protect, and Monetize your visual content

PIXMOLE is the world’s leading copyright management and protection Platform for Visual Creators. We provide visual creators and media agencies the tools to monitor, analyze and safeguard visual content across the web and social media.

PIXMOLE Safeguards Your Visual Legacy!

The PIXMOLE Platform​

PIXMOLE provides the most efficient, comprehensive and flexible platform for digital media copyright management and protection. PIXMOLE platform helps photography agencies, professional photographers and other visual creators protect their works from unauthorized use across the web and on social media as well as streamline workflow and generate additional revenue opportunities.

Streamline your workflow, protect your images, and generate revenue

Learn more

PIXMOLE Search Platform

Our advanced search engine constantly scans the web allowing identification and specific location of your content anywhere anytime including on social media networks. Integrated with an intuitive dashboard, each user from an individual creator to a large agency can have a plan that is tailored to its needs.

PIXMOLE Fully Managed Service

In addition to our powerful search engine, we offer expert analysis services and a global network of legal partners to enforce your copyrights, resolve claims and provide our customers with an end-to-end solution for copyright enforcement effortlessly.

Mission

At PIXMOLE, we are driven by a shared passion for artistry, innovation, technological advancement, and intellectual pursuit. We firmly believe that visual creators deserve due recognition and compensation for their diligent efforts as well as empowering them with the most accurate data as to their content’s usage. Therefore, we are dedicated to developing the preeminent global search platform for copyright protection and establishing ourselves as the definitive one-stop shop for creators seeking to monitor and enforce their copyright. Our advanced technology provides valuable insights into your content’s usage, empowering you to take informed action and protect your intellectual property

Learn more

Social Media

PIXMOLE operates worldwide, helping photography agencies, professional photographers and other visual creators protect their works from unauthorized use. We believe that visual creators deserve to get recognition and payment for their hard work and that is our goal.

Learn more

AI-Powered

Our sophisticated AI-based algorithm continuously scans the digital landscape to identify uses of your media content as well as its different variations and modifications and gather pertinent information from associated social media accounts and websites. By automating this process and eliminating the need for manual tracking, you can effectively monitor and analyze data, thereby enhancing productivity and gaining valuable insights.

Learn more

Archive Integration

PIXMOLE is designed to accommodate even the most extensive image archives, regardless of their physical location on-premises or in the cloud, and irrespective of their file formats. Our platform is engineered to seamlessly integrate with a diverse array of digital asset management platforms, thereby ensuring a streamlined and hassle-free setup process.

Learn more

Who are we?

Established in 2019 by a team of seasoned professional photographers and technology experts, PIXMOLE leverages a deep-rooted expertise in cutting-edge technologies, web intelligence, and professional photography to provide an unparalleled solution for safeguarding the copyrights of visual creators and enforce online media rightful use.

At PIXMOLE, we share a profound passion for artistry, innovation, technological advancement, and intellectual pursuit.

Driven by this shared passion, we are committed to developing the most comprehensive and effective service for tracking, protecting and monetizing creators’ copyright.
