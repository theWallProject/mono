About Us:

Jerusalem Media Group is a full-service multimedia production company located in Jerusalem, Israel. We produce content and events for some of Israel’s top artists, companies, organizations and institutions.

In addition to producing compelling content for television, internet and live playback, we are also a top provider of live internet streaming and satellite broadcast services and have brought large and small events to worldwide audiences.
