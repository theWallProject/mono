Our Services

Strategic Purpose Formation

This structure will set the standards for the company’s strategy and operations, all while becoming the backbone to brand integrity, establishing company values, and creating innovation that will inspire audiences.

Business Continuity Planning

A Business Continuity Plan is the capacity your company has in maintaining the output of its products or services with optimal efficiency, in the event of a disruptive or unforeseen circumstances such as cyber-attacks, pandemics, and natural disasters.

Scouting For Opportunities

The Start Up Nation is renowned for spearheading technologies and innovations that consistently bring cutting-edge solutions to all sectors. Our scouting services allows for a deep look into the crowded yet remarkable Israeli ecosystem.

Company Review

A company review is an evaluation process of the company and its assets. The process itself can focus on a single business operation or the holistic posture of the company.

Due
Diligence

A Due Diligence (DD) is an audit of a target company, normally requested by a third party, before an investment or acquisition, aiming to highlight its projected values and risks.

ESG
Services

We guide companies and organizations in integrating and measuring Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) aspects and standards into their core activities as a primary value, which has proven to attract investors, customers, and employees.

Business Strategy

Our Business Strategy consultancy is designed to assist your company through significant decision-making points, always personalized to your company, its status, culture, the current condition of the market in which you compete, resources, and operating environment.

Next Generation Audit

Auditing is an on-site verification operation, such as an inspection or examination, that can be done at any resolution. An audit can be conducted on a company’s department, process, or specific project or initiative to ensure compliance both internally and externally.

International Expansion

International Expansion is a growth strategy that enables companies to increase revenues and profitability by taking business operations, products, or services from local market into target markets abroad.

Digital Transformation & Strategy

Digital Transformation is the operation of using digital technologies to modify, improve or create new business processes or solve traditional problems with technology.
