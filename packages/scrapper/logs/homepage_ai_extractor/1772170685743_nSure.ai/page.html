Extract More from Your Declines

SoftApproval® leverages behavioral economics to distinguish fraud-suspected buyers from actual fraudsters.

Customers whose transactions have been flagged for SoftApproval® are presented with a challenge unique to their buyer profile and products are released to those who pass.

Massive waste of time. Worth every penny.

nSure.ai’s patented StingBack® protocol gives fraudsters every indication their transaction was successful, even going so far as to deliver a zero-value key or download code.

The transaction is refunded to ensure the cardholder isn’t billed for undelivered goods, but not before wasting the fraudsters time and delivering a lethal blow to their reputation on the black market upon trying to resell the goods.

StingBack® packs a punch in systemically dismantling the scalability of payment fraud and serves as a proactive deterrent for those who might otherwise try to defraud your company in the future.
