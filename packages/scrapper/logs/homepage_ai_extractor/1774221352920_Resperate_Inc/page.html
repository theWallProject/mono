Does RESPeRATE’s Device & Program really work?

Yes. The RESPeRATE device has been validated in over 20 published clinical studies in hypertension and has an explicit FDA indication for lowering high blood pressure (see FDA clearance 510K# K020399).
To help you stay on track and see results within 8 weeks, we provide RESPeRATE together with the LowerPressure™ Program. It includes personal support, educational videos, and BP tracking to help you get consistent, guided use of the FDA-cleared device.
Your program continues for 12 weeks at no additional cost, so you can feel confident results are maintained. And with a 90-day money-back guarantee, you’re fully covered the whole way through.
Check RESPeRATE reviews from real people who have used the device and shared their experiences.

Is RESPeRATE covered by Insurance?

As an FDA-cleared medical device RESPeRATE is eligible for reimbursement by Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA) and Health Saving Accounts (HSA) plans. RESPeRATE is not yet covered by Medicare but might be covered by your medical insurance plan.

Does the American Heart Association recommend RESPeRATE?

The American Heart Association statement on non-pharmacological options for blood pressure (BP) reduction says that “The overall evidence from clinical trials and meta-analyses suggests that device-guided slow breathing [RESPeRATE] can significantly lower blood pressure.” Please note: American Heart Association does not specifically recommend or endorse use of any products or brands.

See the AHA statement detailed and over 50 clinical studies validating RESPeRATE safety and efficacy in the Clinical Proof page

How much can RESPeRATE lower blood pressure?

RESPeRATE lowers high blood pressure by an average of 14 points systolic and 8 points diastolic, with top 10% reductions of 36/20 points, according to a published review of its initial 7 clinical trials . The review also demonstrated that the more you use RESPeRATE the larger the blood pressure reduction.

How fast does RESPeRATE Work?

Experience a significant reduction in BP within a few weeks. It continues to accumulate into a sustained blood pressure drop by week eight. Your program continues for 12 weeks – at no additional cost – so you can feel confident your results are maintained. And with a 90-day money-back guarantee, you’re fully covered the whole way through.

Do doctors recommend RESPeRATE?

Physicians around the country recommend RESPeRATE to their patients as a clinical proven blood pressure reduction method that can be used alongside medication and/or lifestyle modification such as diet or exercise. Download a clinical information package for your doctor

Can any adult use RESPeRATE?

All adults can use RESPeRATE. The FDA cleared it for use without a prescription saying that “RESPeRATE can be safely used in conjunction with medications and lifestyle modifications such as diet and exercise.”
RESPeRATE has no contraindication which means anyone can use RESPeRATE, however, people who suffer or have suffered in the past from the following conditions should consult with your physician before using RESPeRATE:
Active ischemic heart disease/unstable angina, bradycardia, severe congestive heart failure, chronic atrial fibrillation, stroke resulting in permanent impairment, chronic renal failure, severe asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), chronic bronchitis and emphysema, major organ failure, sleep paralysis, have a pacemaker, or if you are pregnant.
It is advisable not to use RESPeRATE while driving or operating heavy machinery since people may fall asleep during sessions.

Please note: RESPeRATE, should only be used as a part of an overall health program for achieving goal blood pressure, as recommended by a doctor. RESPeRATE can be safely used in conjunction with medications and lifestyle modifications such as diet and exercise.
