Online & in-person payments, powered by AI

Unipaas powers a fully branded payment solution across every touchpoint - giving platforms and their merchants a seamless, unified way to transact.

AI is embedded throughout the payment lifecycle, from accelerating onboarding and improving conversion to recovering declined transactions, personalizing payment messaging, generating reports, and automating revenue recovery - so platforms grow payment adoption efficiently.

Purpose-built payments tailored to your vertical

Unipaas goes beyond generic payment solutions by building tools tailored to your industry. From Tax-Free Childcare for nurseries, to an AI Copilot for invoice follow-ups, POS Terminals, and QR codes for field teams – we build features that match how your business works.
