Fees shouldn't eat into your investments

How Freetrade keeps investing free
Investing should be accessible and transparent. That’s why we don't charge account fees or trading commissions.

Instead, we make money through:

  • FX fees when trading non-GBP investments
  • A portion of interest earned on uninvested cash
  • Optional paid plans offering lower FX fees, higher interest on cash, and more benefits.


Now you can access essential investing products, including self-invested personal pensions, at no extra cost.
