      Welcome to Etesian Semiconductors!

      Founded in 2008, Etesian is a leading Fab-less foundry specializing in manufacturing design and development of tailored silicon process mainly for CMOS Image sensors and other non-std devices. Our unique Foundry model allows us to provide full solution for managing all product life cycle, from development to qualification and production, offering our customers a seamless experience.

      Our Vision

      Our vision is to become the premier destination for manufacturing of image sensors wafers using tailored silicon fabrication process. We aim to provide wafer production including design, optical and TCAD simulation, production and yield management. By leveraging our expertise using cutting-edge flexible technology , we aspire to be the preferred partner for businesses seeking excellence in image sensor manufacturing, delivering unmatched quality and efficiency at every step of the process.

      Product and services

      At Etesian Semiconductors, we specialize in design and production complex silicon wafers tailored to our customers' specific requirements, particularly in the realm of image sensors as well as other sensors. The process we’ve established for image sensor chips comprises several key stages, ensuring the delivery of high-performance, custom solutions.

      ETesiAN Semiconductor

      is serving as a one-stop-shop for the EMEA semiconductor industry by promoting and offering services and products to fabless and IDM companies for successful, cost effective, reliable and high performance design and manufacturing.
