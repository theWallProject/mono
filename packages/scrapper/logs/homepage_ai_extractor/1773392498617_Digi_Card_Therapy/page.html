About us

Digi Card Therapy is an innovative website providing digital tools and content for therapists and coaches from all approaches. The site was built especially for the community of therapists for the challenging period when many of the treatments take place outside the clinic space, and are conducted remotely. In addition, the site is also suitable for anyone interested in self-improvement.
