About Us

Established in 2002 by Prof. Adi Armoni, K.B.I.S has become a market leader in Knowledge-Based Information Systems that efficiently manage complex business processes within an organization.

We consider ourselves as long-term true partners of the leading insurance, health care and defense organizations in Israel and abroad.

We focus on development and assimilation of innovative BPM (Business Process Management) and services-oriented applications.

Telem

Bridges the gap between the back-office relevant (automated) paperwork and the on-site related work such as taking pictures and GPS position-stamp (using any standard smart device), filling on-site inspector/appraiser reports, etc

Our advanced forms-generator is used for standardizing checklists, inspections, field reports and documentations – automatically adjusted to smart mobile devices and other field related challenges

Nesicha

Complete status-driven control and management of cross-organization business processes, providing a one-stop-shop interface for handling the process even when other legacy or external information systems are involved “behind the scenes”

Procurement-related BPM platform shared by both organization stakeholders and involved suppliers

Hawk

End-to-end insurance claim handling and management solution

Includes on-line interfaces with all relevant systems (such as policy, underwriting, financial, optical archive, etc.) for control and management of the claim handling process from FNOL to payment

Use of advanced complimentary technologies such as rule-engine, BI and analytics embedded as OEM in the solution

Athena

Unique solution for handling the arbitration or subrogation processes

Supports the full life-cycle of such processes found in the Insurance and Legal fields, including – appointing an arbitrator, preparing rulings, submitting appeals, inter-party payments balancing and accounting, etc

Unique automatic mission and task generator to involved parties, while keeping track of schedules defined in respective regulations and providing process monitoring

Apollo

Complete control and management of external service providers involved as front-end of organization business processes

Aristo

Unique solution for on-line submission and management of applications, or reports of a structured format, different fill-in forms, tables and attachments of various types

Advanced features include enforcement of pre-defined submission format with administration flexibility, processing and auditing managed workflow, built-in chat capabilities between team of editors, unique printout capabilities embedding fields, free-text, and attachments as a continuous structured report

Paqadu
