Intro to iGaming
Intro to iGaming

iGaming refers to digital entertainment offered through regulated online platforms. It includes online casino, sports betting, live play, and other interactive games of chance and skill. By combining advanced technology with strong customer protection, iGaming delivers safe, accessible, and enjoyable entertainment to players around the world.

The Work Behind iGaming

Technology, data, creativity, and customer insight come together at Betsson Group. Our teams shape digital entertainment across product, tech, analytics, marketing, legal, and more.

A Place Where Every Talent Belongs

Our people come from diverse backgrounds and skill sets. Whether you’re technical or creative, early-career or experienced, you’ll find space to learn, grow, and make an impact.

We Play Fair and Put Responsibility First

iGaming is a regulated industry built on trust. At Betsson Group, we combine innovation with strong safeguards to deliver safe and sustainable entertainment.
