Technology & Solutions

Patented solar thermal collectors

TIGI’s Honeycomb Collector revolutionizes the solar thermal industry. Using proprietary transparent insulation, it minimizes energy losses and generates more heat than traditional collectors. Designed for high-temperature industrial applications, it features overheating prevention and low maintenance, setting a new industry standard. The Honeycomb Collector has received multiple industry awards and certifications, underscoring its innovative design and efficiency.


Learn More

Heat Pumps

TIGI revolutionizes industrial heating with advanced heat pump systems. Our technology offers superior efficiency for high-temperature applications, integrating multiple heat sources and smart controls. These heat pumps can achieve temperatures of up to 150°C, making them ideal for industries like food processing and chemical manufacturing. This innovation promises significant cost savings and sustainability for commercial and industrial users, with proven implementations in various sectors.


Learn More

Thermal Storage

TIGI leads in advanced thermal storage systems for sustainable heating. Our technology seamlessly integrates with renewable sources, offering efficient energy management and significant cost savings. With expertise in system planning and diverse applications, TIGI drives the decarbonization of heat in commercial and industrial settings, positioning us at the forefront of sustainable energy solutions.


Learn More

Control and Cloud Service

TIGI leads in advanced control and cloud services for renewable heat solutions. Our technology offers real-time monitoring, system optimization, and remote management for industrial and commercial heat systems.  Key features include comprehensive plant management, data analytics, and robust data security measures. TIGI’s innovative approach enhances efficiency, reduces costs, and improves sustainability. By transforming heat strategies with cutting-edge technology, TIGI positions itself at the forefront of modern energy management solutions.
