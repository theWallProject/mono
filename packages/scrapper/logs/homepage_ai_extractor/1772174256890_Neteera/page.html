Heart Rate & Pattern Analysis

Accurately monitors heart rate and uses pattern analysis to identify trends over time. Current measurements and trends are easily visible to help you detect potential cardiac issues sooner.

Respiration Rate
& Depth

Accurately and automatically measures and tracks respiratory rate and depth to help you leverage this leading indicator of deteriorating condition.

Inhale/Exhale Ratio

Provides deeper insight into respiratory function to help you identify the onset or worsening of conditions, such as COPD, and gain a holistic view of respiratory function over time.

Sleep Analysis

Detailed information on sleep duration and patterns, along with detection of REM sleep phases, helps you monitor recovery and overall well-being.

Position Change & Motion

Motion data supports behavioral and recovery monitoring, while position change notifications help reduce risks associated with sedentary positions. 

Exit/Frequency/Occupancy

Timely notifications of exits, in as little as 30 seconds, and for exit rates, along with tracked occupancy time, can help you tailor care and intervene proactively.

More Health Insights, Fewer Transfers

Neteera’s mission is simple: Innovate technology that improves healthcare quality for patients, providers, and facility operators.

Our precision sensing technology brings a more efficient, accurate, and compassionate approach to patient monitoring.

And we’re just getting started. Join us as we transform healthcare delivery and accessibility worldwide.

How It Works

The Neteera System device mounts unobtrusively on the ceiling or wall, and the detection radius centers on the bed location.

The Neteera System continuously monitors vital signs and activity through clothing and bedding using radar-based sensors. Subtle skin movements from the patient’s heartbeat are detected, measured, and tracked around the clock. This data is sent to a centralized dashboard, giving your team equal access to patient insights, analysis, and alerts.

Where It Works

No matter the healthcare setting, our touch-free, passive patient monitoring system helps ensure that patients aren’t disturbed, workflows are optimized, and healthcare workers can focus on delivering patient-centered care.
