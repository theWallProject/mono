Innovative Card Solutions

For Modern Businesses and Families

Jassby’s comprehensive solutions provide businesses with the tools and expertise they need to establish and maintain successful debit and credit card programs. By utilizing innovative technology, we empower businesses to tailor their card programs rapidly and effectively, ensuring they align seamlessly with their unique business models.

Key Features and Benefits

Jassby’s features and benefits will set your card program apart.
Program Customization

Our technology platform allows for the swift and efficient customization of card programs. Businesses can tailor various aspects of their programs, including card designs, reward structures, spending limits, and more, to align with their brand identity and target audience.

Rapid Implementation

We understand the importance of time-to-market. Our streamlined implementation process ensures that businesses can launch their card programs quickly and seamlessly. From program design to card issuance, we work diligently to minimize disruptions and ensure a smooth transition.

Data-Driven Insights

Our robust reporting and analytics tools provide businesses with valuable insights into their card program performance. These insights enable informed decision-making, allowing businesses to optimize their programs, identify growth opportunities, and enhance customer engagement.

Fraud Detection and Mitigation

We employ advanced fraud detection and mitigation systems to protect businesses and cardholders from unauthorized transactions. Our real-time monitoring and risk assessment capabilities help identify and prevent fraudulent activities, safeguarding the integrity of card programs.

Exceptional Customer Support

Our expert team of dedicated customer support professionals stands ready to assist businesses and cardholders with any inquiries or issues. We prioritize prompt and efficient resolution, ensuring that customers receive the support they need, when they need it.

Solutions

Jassby offers end-to-end debit and credit card program solutions. Powered by the Jassby Card Program Platform, each solution is complete and customizable. We issue the cards, provide the technology, manage fraud and compliance, and provide support, all so you can focus on maximizing the benefit our solution brings to your business.

Debit Cards for Group Travel

The Jassby Trip Card is a specialized debit card backed by tools and technology that allow you to manage every aspect of providing allotments to your travelers. It gives you amazing flexibility and complete peace of mind as you handle the complexities of enabling purchases by travelers. You can fund your program, schedule your allotments, and manage the surprises that arise while groups are traveling. Contact us today to learn how the Jassby Trip Card can solve your allotment management challenges.

Credit Card Programs

Jassby specializes in full service management of branded card programs. We handle all aspects of getting your program up and running, and managing it once it takes off. In addition, we partner with you on branding and optimizing the user experience in ways that align your program seamlessly with your business. If you are looking for a card program to generate additional revenue and build brand loyalty, we can help.

Debit Card for Families

The Jassby Debit Card for Families is packed with features that promote financial learning through experience. With built-in support for digital wallets paired with optional physical cards, the card gives teens, kids, and others who require assistance with money management the perfect balance of independence and support, and is available for all family members, regardless of age. Jassby’s innovative financial literacy tools reinforce sound, lifelong money habits. The Jassby Debit Card for Families is available direct from Jassby, or as a branded and integrated solution for your business.
