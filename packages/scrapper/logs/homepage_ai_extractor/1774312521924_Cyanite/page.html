Why Cyanite?

Speed

Cyanite customers see a 6x increase in licensing requests within the first few months, and cut search and curation time by 55% on average. Comfortably rely on precise search results while accessing your entire catalog, not just a portion. 

Reliability

Cyanite’s algorithms are developed in-house without reliance on ChatGPT etc., ensuring full control over your music, data, and search inputs. We uphold the highest data security standards, with EU-based servers and full GDPR compliance.

Innovation Lead
