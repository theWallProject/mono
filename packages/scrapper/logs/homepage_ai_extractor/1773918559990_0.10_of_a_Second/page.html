The Prelight System

Makes traffic predictable

It analyzes driving behavior, generates intelligent indications, quickly expos driving intentions of both- humans & autonomous systems and helps to avoid risk by improving road users response time capabilities

* For example:
When lowering foot from the accelerator pedal,
Some vehicles are showing red lights as like pressing the brakes
even though there’s only a deceleration process. 
We solved this issue, with our-
“Acceleration / Deceleration indicator

Autonomous systems behave different, 
they react immediately, faster then human road users!
The Prelight System reduces this growing gap of response time, 
especially with the two-wheelers. 

This is how we prevent accidents or reduce their severity and in some cases even saving lives

Since autonomous systems starts integrating, 
Human response time had started to grow.
Today, standard vehicle indications suffers from lack of information and late exposure.
On highways, those differences become even more significant.

Autonomous- are they also safe for the other road users around them?

Is this growing gap in response time
between autonomous and the other road users, along with the two-wheelers continue to grow?

Will visual indications for vehicles still remain a regulatory demand even in the autonomous and V2V era or will they become redundant and disappear from our landscape?!

There will always be human road users-
Two-wheelers or autonomous with breakdown that have to be driven by human drivers and more…
therefore, the language of lights will always be decisive, important to adjust and a regulatory demand
