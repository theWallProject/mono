ABOUT US

Greeneye utilizes artificial intelligence and deep learning technology to revolutionize the pest control process in agriculture, transitioning from the current practice of broadcast and wasteful spraying of pesticides to precise and selective spraying.

WEEDS ARE ONE OF THE MOST SIGNIFICANT THREATS TO GLOBAL CROP PRODUCTION

Farmers address this challenge by indiscriminately spraying herbicides across the entire field.

ECONOMIC

Globally, weeds result in an estimated USD 100 billion in annual crop production losses.

RESISTANCE

Herbicide-resistant weeds are making it increasingly difficult to control weeds effectively.

ENVIRONMENTAL

Millions of gallons of herbicides are sprayed every year in vain, causing severe soil and water contamination

HEALTH

The overuse of chemicals in agriculture poses significant health risks to society
THE SOLUTION

Greeneye utilizes artificial intelligence to transition from broadcast to precise spraying of herbicides

GREEN-ON-GREEN

Differentiating weeds from crop to spray precisely only the weeds and reduce up to 90% of herbicide usage.

GREEN-ON-BROWN

Apply precision spraying on pre-planting and post-harvest spraying application

SPECIES-LEVEL ANALYSIS

Identify weeds down to the species level to fight herbicide resistance weeds
OUR TECHNOLOGY

Our proprietary Selective Spraying (SSP) system seamlessly integrates to any agriculture sprayer to enable the precise spraying of chemicals in real time only where it’s needed
