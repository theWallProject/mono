Frequently Asked Questions

Is it VM or Bare Metal?

You get both options. You can choose to lease either bare metal or VM instances directly from the dashboard.

Will I get a dedicated IP address?

Yes. Each machine comes with a dedicated IP address. You'll be able to SSH into your VM or bare metal server with full root access.

Can I run containers on it?

Absolutely. It's your VM, you have full root access, so you can run containers or any other workloads as you see fit.

Is InfiniBand supported?

It depends on the provider. Some offer InfiniBand, while others don't. If it's available, it will be clearly mentioned on the dashboard before you lease the machine.

Will I get 100% uptime?

No GPU setup can guarantee 100% uptime. However, since our machines are sourced from Tier 3 and Tier 4 data centers, you can expect significantly higher availability compared to typical setups.

How do I get support?

If you're leasing just a few GPUs, most of it is plug-and-play. For larger scale deployments (e.g., 100+ GPUs), we can offer dedicated support via Slack or Discord, depending on your preference. We'll also assist in sourcing the best deals for your requirements. Please book a meeting with our team to discuss your requirements.
