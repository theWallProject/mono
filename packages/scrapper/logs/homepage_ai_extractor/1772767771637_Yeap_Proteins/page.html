OUR

STORY

“Every common protein from any source comes with an asterisk next to it. Animal protein tastes good but is not sustainable. Alternative proteins such as soy proteins are inexpensive, have a good nutritional profile and great functionality but have allergens, estrogen, and lack the taste of animal-based proteins. Pea-based proteins do not generate these health concerns but are expensive, lack nutritional values and generate significant off flavors.

Is there a sustainable protein in nature that has the nutritional value and taste of animal-based protein?

We’re happy you asked, because we just found it. Yeast, yes, yeast. The best nutritional protein that doesn’t require extensive agriculture land usage, is resilient to extreme climate events and has no production limits.

YEAP is the world’s first company that produces protein from yeast, incorporating a circular economy model.

A protein that can do it all; flavorful, clean, nutritious, sustainable, and last but not least, inexpensive.”

OUR

COMMITMENT

Our world is facing the great
challenges of climate change
and the scarcity of resources –
we believe Yeap can be part of
the solution.

Our unique production process by taking downstream yeast and converting them into upcycled food, leads to a signiﬁcant reduction in environmental impact compared to animal protein production, and realize circular economy solution.


We are committed to the environment and to constantly work to reduce our environmental impact and will be transparent on improvement.
