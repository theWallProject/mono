Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ's)

Why do high growth tech companies in Israel choose IAMOPS as their DevOps consulting services partner?

IAMOPS is a full-suite DevOps company headquartered in Israel that supports tech companies from early development through scale. We offer expert-led DevOps, DevSecOps, FinOps, QA Automation, and 24/7 NOC services — all designed to accelerate product delivery while ensuring scalability, cost-efficiency, uptime, and security. With a deep understanding of the tech ecosystem, we help tech teams focus on innovation while we take care of infrastructure and operations.

How does IAMOPS help accelerate delivery while managing growing infrastructure complexity?

Our DevOps services are designed to help tech teams build scalable cloud architectures, automate deployment workflows, and ensure reliable CI/CD pipelines. Whether you’re building on AWS, GCP, or Azure, IAMOPS brings proven expertise in Infrastructure as Code (IaC), monitoring, and developer tooling — enabling rapid, secure, and consistent releases with reduced downtime and technical debt.

How does IAMOPS help ensure uptime and proactive incident response?

IAMOPS provides 24/7 NOC services that include real-time infrastructure and application monitoring, automated playbooks, and fast response to incidents — all aligned with your SLA. We identify and resolve issues before they impact users, ensuring continuous operations in fast-paced cloud environments.

How does IAMOPS help embed security without slowing down development?

With IAMOPS, security is built into your CI/CD workflows from day one. Our DevSecOps services secure infrastructure, harden pipelines, and protect applications against vulnerabilities. We also help tech companies meet compliance standards like ISO-27001 with robust governance and incident response plans — without compromising delivery speed.

How can I reduce cloud costs for my product without compromising performance?

Many tech companies grapple with escalating cloud expenses as they scale. IAMOPS’ FinOps services provide real-time cost monitoring and hands-on cost reduction strategies tailored to high growth tech companies. We guide you through leveraging AWS credits, saving plans, and reserved instances, while also implementing right-sizing, architectural changes for cost reduction, storage cost optimization, and traffic-cost optimization. As an AWS Reseller, we offer access to private pricing — ensuring your cloud spend scales efficiently with your business.

What are the benefits of implementing QA automation in my product development process?

Implementing QA automation can significantly enhance product stability and user satisfaction. IAMOPS offers dedicated QA automation services in Israel, including test script development, CI-integrated testing pipelines, synthetic monitoring, API testing, and performance validation. This reduces manual QA effort, speeds up release cycles, and ensures that every release is reliable and bug-free.
