ABOUT MAPPO
Advance AI technology
Mappo has developed an Exclusive AI technology that Extracts and creates endless pieces of content and adds millions of POI’s (Points Of Interest) on the map, Worldwide.  
Our NLP algorithm scans texts and automatically creates short snippets from literature, movies, music, art, history and more, about the place you are in. 
We combine our technology with UGC (Users Generated Content) - we offer users to upload original content 
ABOUT MAPPO
In car experience
The in car experience is going through a gaming revolution, and Mappo is the first to develop an In-car culture-inspired app that matches the car Infotainment system, which is also location-based. 
We offer a full in car experience that changes the way we explore a destination - Through stories, music, Trivia games, Fun Facts, Riddles, and more
Moreover, The content is customized and personalized.

Technology
Cultural science
Our Groundbreaking AI Technology is Based on state-of-the-art NLP algorithms, Deep learning and Large language models. Combining books, public data, and expert knowledge, our technology produces content that is interesting, engaging, and playful for everyone. Our scalable technology scans millions of items everywhere on earth to create summaries of points of interest and events in multiple domains (history, sports, architecture, art, cuisine…), while linking them to the place they happened. For presentation, we employ gamification techniques that produce fun-facts, trivia questions and riddles.
Technology
Full automation
By leveraging the power of machine learning, Mappo's innovative platform curates cultural content from across the globe, bringing the world's rich and diverse History, Literature, Pop Culture, Music, and local traditions to your fingertips. Mappo scans your surroundings and presents you with relevant information about the cultural significance of your location.
Technology
Anytime, anywhere
With Mappo's Android Automotive application, you can explore the world around you like never before, immerse yourself in the history and culture of any location, and experience it in a way that's never been possible before.
