Frequently Asked Questions
1. How does the Hologram work?

The Hologram uses cutting-edge technology to create and broadcast life-size 3D holograms. Users can create these holograms from anywhere in the world, providing immersive experiences for customers.

2. In what industries can the Hologram be used?

The Hologram can be used in various industries such as retail, education, hotels, and events. It enhances engagement and provides awe-inspiring experiences for customers. 

3. How does Eyefeelit's platform integrate retail chains with the online world?

Eyefeelit’s platform seamlessly connects physical stores with e-commerce, allowing businesses to gain deep insights into customer behavior through analytics. It helps optimize product displays and increase sales.

4. How do Eyefeelit's interactive screens enhance the shopping experience?

Eyefeelit’s interactive screens enable products to be showcased in an interactive and engaging manner, creating memorable shopping experiences.

5. What services does Eyefeelit offer when partnering with businesses?

When partnering with Eyefeelit, businesses can expect a comprehensive process that includes creating a tailored strategy, developing captivating content, seamlessly installing and connecting displays, and providing ongoing maintenance and updates.

6. What sizes of screens does Eyefeelit offer?

Eyefeelit offers screens of various sizes to cater to different needs and preferences. They can provide screens of any size required by the business.

7. How can businesses schedule a demo or get more information?

Businesses interested in Eyefeelit’s innovative solutions can reach out to them directly to schedule a demo or get more information. The Eyefeelit team will be happy to showcase the exceptional opportunities their technology presents.

Do you have any other questions or do you want to learn more about our Holobox experience? Reach out to Eyefeelit team directly for the most up-to-date information and specific details about our products and services.
