The New CR-400

The Cellocator CR-400 is Cellocator’s next generation fleet and security management device, and is based on the LTE Cat M1 network.

The CR-400 is a high quality, yet cost-effective and easy to install device, with built-in BLE connectivity and LED indicators. It is equipped with a rechargeable backup battery (1000mAh) and includes basic driver behavior capabilities and built-in motion sensors that enable movement and towing detection to ensure improved compliance with vehicle security requirements.
