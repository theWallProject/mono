The Founder
From Homelessnes to Empowerment: 
My Journey of Resilience
Growing up in the small town of Bat-Yam, near Tel Aviv, I was surrounded by the stories of immigrants—stories of struggle, resilience, and hope. My family’s narrative was shaped by the harrowing experiences of my parents, Holocaust survivors who bore the weight of their past while striving to build a brighter future. My mother’s mental health struggles only added to the complexities of our household, creating an environment where love often felt overshadowed by challenges.

PROPHECY Series
The PROPHECY formula has been scientifically proven to deliver hyaluronic acid deep into the skin, even from the final product. This unique formula is the first in the world to successfully preserve the micronized hyaluronic acid in its reduced state after being incorporated into a cream, allowing for deep skin penetration upon application.

Remicronized Series
The new Remicronized product line by HAVA ZINGBOIM is the first in the world to enable the topical delivery of fibrillar collagen and micronized hyaluronic acid deep into the skin. This unique combination diminishes the appearance of fine lines, enhances skin health and functionality, and provides elasticity, firmness, intense hydration, radiance, and glow.
