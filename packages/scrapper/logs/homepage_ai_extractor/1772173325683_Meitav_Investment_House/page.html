About Meitav Investment House

Meitav is a publicly traded company specializing in investment management since 1979, controlled by Mr. Eli Barkat (BRM Finance) and Mr. Avner Stepak.

Meitav is a professional, experienced, stable, and well-recognized investment house in the Israeli capital market. With decades of proven expertise, Meitav's track record reflects not only the milestones it has achieved, but also its deep understanding of the capital markets. Meitav's employees are seasoned professionals who combine rich experience with a passion for innovation, leadership and the pursuit of the next opportunity.

Meitav employs approximately 1,000 skilled and dedicated employees, led by CEO Ilan Raviv. As of Sep 30, 2025, Meitav manages assets totaling around NIS 383 billion on behalf of more than one and a half million clients. Each client enjoys a broad range of tailored products and services designed to meet individual needs.
