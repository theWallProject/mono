Expertise

UI/UX

It all starts with smart planning of a product’s flow and a wise interface mapping.  Those are essential for creating a magnificent experience for your users while caring for your business goals.

Design

Memorable and unique design will make your product stand out. We are proud of the emphasis we put on the design in the process, and it has been proven to make a difference in the products.

Client Side Development

Whether it is mobile apps or web technologies, we keep various experts in house to deliver the best working products in the market.  We ace in jQuery, HTML 5, CSS, Java Script, AngularJS.

Server Side Development

The brain. Making any digital product work well means that the structure and DB must be carefully assembled. Our team knows how to support the simplest app, as well as to build a super-complex data systems.

Quality Assurance

Our reputation is on the line, and so we ensure that each and every release goes through a substantial testing and debugging process.  Our QA staff holds the most pertinacious, detail-oriented and caring people.

Who We Are

YKM was founded in 2000 and since then has been focused on creative technology. We specialize in custom-made development of products for a wide range of digital platforms, and provide in-house end to end solutions for our clients.
We consider ourselves to be strategic partners with our clients, who are often leading companies from various fields in the industry: Finance & Banking, Healthcare & pharmaceuticals, Retail, Tourism and more. Together we design innovative, ground breaking solutions and shape the digital market.
