How It Works

The Member Engagement Platform Featured on BrightSpots in Healthcare

Our Medicare member engagement platform is designed to address the complex evolution of Stars measures. As these measures shift away from administrative mechanics and toward clinical outcomes, pharmacy performance, and CAHPS, plans need to rethink how to prioritize, engage, and translate insight into action. Every member is different. So is every measure. We leverage real-time alerts, AI and advanced analytics and Stars measure logic to create individualized outreach and coordinated care that move the member to next best action. Solutions

One Platform, Real Impact Across All Measures

Drive stronger quality results, consistently. 

Member Experience

MedOrion helps improve CAHPS ratings by aligning outreach with each member’s preferred tone, timing, and channel. By removing friction and building trust, our platform reduces communication fatigue and ensures members feel seen, supported, and valued.

HEDIS | Clinical Quality

MedOrion helps improve Star Ratings and close care gaps faster by reaching the right members with the right message at the right time. Plans reduce wasted effort and drive measurable gains across key preventive and chronic condition measures.

Pharmacy

MedOrion helps drive stronger Part D performance by increasing medication adherence across key measures. Health plans improve refill rates, reduce abandonment, and raise adherence thresholds—leading to higher Star Ratings and more efficient pharmacy engagement.

Risk Adjustment

MedOrion helps Improve RAF accuracy and protect revenue by engaging members when they’re most likely to complete risk-relevant encounters. Health plans increase condition capture, reduce missed opportunities, and make better use of in-home, PCP, and vendor-led programs—without increasing burden on provider or outreach teams.
