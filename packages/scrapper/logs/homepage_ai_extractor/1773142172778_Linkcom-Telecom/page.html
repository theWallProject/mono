ABOUT US

Elie Levy - Founder & President, Linkcom Telecom Ltd.

Elie Levy is a visionary leader, Pioneer in bringing satcom technology in Israel  and a prominent figure in the satellite communications (satcom) industry. With decades of experience spanning aerospace engineering, satellite technology, and business management, he has made a lasting impact on global telecommunications and defense systems.

Zak Levi - CEO/CTO

Zak Levi joined Linkcom-Telecom as a System Engineer in 2007, bringing a wealth of multidisciplinary expertise and insights gained from his experience in the high-tech industry, academia, and the Israeli military. He holds both a B.Sc. and an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Tel Aviv University, graduating at the top of his class with honors.
