    our Vision

    “Mens sana in corpore sano”– a healthy mind in a healthy body is the Latin phrase from which Sano’s name is derived.
    Sano, means health in Latin, and it is this idea which is at the forefront of Sano’s vision: caring for consumer health, convenience, and well-being.

    Sano adapts a wide range of affordable products for every need by researching and understanding the consumer, and adapting products to local lifestyles and climate condition.
