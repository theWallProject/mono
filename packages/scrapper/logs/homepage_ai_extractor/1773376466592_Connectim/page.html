Our Story

A human-centered approach to tech leadership

Founded by industry expert and head-hunter Tchia Alona Altar, Connectim has been a trusted growth partner to tech companies across Israel, the U.S., and Europe since 2009.

We’ve built a reputation for going deeper: understanding a company’s mission, culture, and direction — and placing the kind of leaders who move the needle. We don’t stop at recruitment. Through hands-on workshops, strategic consulting, and a network-driven approach to development, we help companies build leadership ecosystems that thrive from within.

Because we don’t just hire for today we help you grow for tomorrow.
