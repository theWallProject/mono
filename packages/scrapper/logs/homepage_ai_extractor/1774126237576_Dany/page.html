FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dany?

Dany is a multi-channel AI sales and marketing agent that helps businesses grow by driving traffic, engaging leads, scheduling appointments, and boosting sales across all platforms – website, social media, SEO, email, SMS, and phone. With its advanced AI capabilities, Dany interacts with customers in a human-like manner, providing a personalized and efficient customer experience that drives unprecedented business growth.

How does Dany work?

Dany uses AI to identify the best methods to convert your website traffic into leads. It can follow up with leads via phone calls and use sophisticated sales techniques to turn leads into valuable appointments. Additionally, Dany creates and publishes tailored content to drive more traffic to your website.

How can I integrate Dany into my website?

Integrating Dany into your website is simple and straightforward. All you need to do is add one line of code to your website, and Dany will be ready to start interacting with your visitors and converting them into leads.

How does Dany help with lead generation and conversion?

Dany is trained to think like the world’s greatest salespeople. It understands complex requests, asks clarifying questions, and fully converses with prospects to turn them into booked meetings. Dany also builds trust with prospects by showcasing your web reviews and completed projects.

Can Dany handle multilingual customers?

Yes, Dany is capable of detecting a customer’s language and can converse in any of the 95 languages it supports.
