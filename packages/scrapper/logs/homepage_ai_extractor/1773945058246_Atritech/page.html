About Us
ATRITECH is a leading manufacturer and marketer in the field of Air filtration in Israel. It was established by a 30 years experienced engineers, designers, inventors and problem solvers team in the fields of turn-key clean room projects and ventilation systems for textile, health, food and beverage and pharmaceuticals sectors. ATRITECH offers standard filtration, as well as customized product that are geared to fit individual needs. We use the most current fabrication methods and high end technologies.
