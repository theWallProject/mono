About company

Kroosh Technologies Ltd. (Israel), founded in 1996, is a technological company focused on manufacturing of unique technological solutions for the process of screening both for wet and dry ones.

By developing a unique multi-frequency vibrating screeners, the company has created the only solution that provides high levels of acceleration over a wide frequency range directly on the screening surface (sieve) using a reliable mechanical device.

KROOSH screeners provide benefits for a wide range of industrial processes. They provide the ability to effectively screen wet and sticky bulk materials, as well as slurries with particle sizes from 15 microns to 30 mm.
