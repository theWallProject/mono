The Jewish Agency, writing our shared Jewish story

We are here to strengthen the Jewish people, in Israel and around the world—by promoting Aliyah, deepening connections in our global Jewish family, supporting the resilience and security of Israeli and Jewish communities, and encouraging every Jewish person to engage with Israel.

The Jewish Agency At Home The Jewish Agency Worldwide

  • Aliyah

    Dreaming of immigrating to Israel or new immigrants to the country? We are here for Jews every step of the way before, during, and after their Aliyah journey with crucial resources, guidance and support services.

    ABOUT ALIYAH →

  • Shlichut

    Our Shlichim (Israeli emissaries) foster meaningful connections to Israel through personal relationships by serving in communities worldwide, on college campuses, at summer camps, and more.

    ABOUT THE SHLICHIM →

  • Fund for Victims of Terror

    After tragedy strikes, we distribute critical immediate financial assistance to help survivors and also offer ongoing rehabilitative support to get individuals and families on the road to recovery.

    ABOUT THE FUND →

  • Partnership2Gether

    Empowering and developing deep connections through city-to-city and region-to-region partnerships, strengthening Jewish communities in Israel and worldwide.

    ABOUT PARTNERSHIPS →

    For three years, Elah served as a Campus Israel Fellow at an Ohio university, supporting Jewish students and helping them feel connected to Israel and their Jewish identities.

  • An Unforgettable Experience at Camp

    Through Campers2Gether, Ofer, 14, from Merhavim, fostered meaningful relationships with his American peers and strengthened his connection to his Jewish identity.

    Read about his amazing summer →

  • Our 2024 Impact Report

    In 2024, The Jewish Agency responded to the urgent challenges of a nation at war, a global surge in antisemitism, the determination of Jews to make Aliyah, and the need to strengthen Jewish unity.

    Explore our far-reaching impact →

October 7th changed us forever. It brought our global Jewish family great pain–but it also revealed our shared strength.

 

Over the past two years, The Jewish Agency has created impactful and inspiring projects through which Jews in Israel and around the world can remember, commemorate, and connect together, and from this dark time build a promising future for the Jewish people.
