About Us

Magnifica creates interactive simulation platforms using exceptional AI technology and high quality graphics.
Our products allow Military & HLS teams to innovate their training methods, reduce costs and gain data driven insights.

Since its foundation, Magnifica set on a mission to revolutionize the simulation industry. Customized platforms are developed through collaboration between our R&D team and world-renowned experts using the latest methods and technologies.

Our C.H.A.T.S. training platform was awarded single-source status from the Israeli MoD to train special canine units in the IDF using AI based Motion Capture data and immersive VR technology.
