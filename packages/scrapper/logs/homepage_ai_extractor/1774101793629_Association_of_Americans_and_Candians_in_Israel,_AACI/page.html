Connecting the
Jewish People to Israel 

Who we are

The Association of Americans & Canadians in Israel (AACI) utilizes the strengths, talents and passions of Israel’s English-speaking Olim community to initiate, develop and implement projects that involve, serve, and benefit the Jewish people.

About

Join our Mission

Partner with us

AACI invites organizations and foundations, both in Israel and abroad, to join with us in strategic partnerships to aid us in advancing and achieving our goals.

Volunteer and lead

AACI is a volunteer driven organization. We urge our volunteers to initiate, develop, and implement creative projects which are in line with our mission statement.

Donate

Your generous donations enable AACI to accomplish our mission both uniting Israel and the diaspora as well as assisting local communities in need.

 Stay connected

Keep up-to-date with the latest news, events, and initiatives that bring Israel and diaspora Jewry closer. Join our eNewsletter and follow us on social media to stay connected. Together, we can make a meaningful difference.
