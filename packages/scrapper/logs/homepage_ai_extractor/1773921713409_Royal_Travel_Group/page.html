Who are we ?

Welcome to Royal Travel Group Ltd. - Israel Incoming Tour Operator

Israel is a fascinating and unique country, the homelad of many civilizations and peoples, the land of three religions as well as the Biblical Promised Land.

Every year Israel attracts growing number of tourists from around the world and it is not surprising, because it boasts a huge number of historical attractions.

As a matter of fact, Israel has more history per square mile than any place on Earth !

Our goal

Royal Travel will take you on an amazing holiday journey across Israel:

Jerusalem:

Many call it "A heart of the world" - A city which almost everyone in the world is dreaming of visiting at least once in a lifetime.

Jerusalem is considered by many to be one of the holiest places on Earth.

It is a site of major significance for the three largest monotheistic religions : Judaism, Islam and Christianity.

 

Red Sea / Eilat:

Boasts the most beautiful beaches in Israel. Great for relaxation and world famous for Scuba diving and Snorkeling.

A rich kingdom of fabulous underwater world hides under the surface and in the debths of the Red Sea.  

 

The Dead Sea:

Where you can float on healing waters of the highest salt and mineral concentration.

The Dead Sea surface is at 430.5 meters below sea level, which is the deepest point on eatrh !

 

The Mediteranean Sea:

From the urban Metropolis Tel Aviv, to the villages and remote beaches of the Western Galilee, this diverse and beautiful coastline is home to most Israel's population.

It is a site of economic, cultural and leisure activities, including some of the country's coolest and most unique sites as well as gorgeous sandy beaches.

 

The Sea of Galilee:

Historical place where Jesus walked on water and the Baptismal site for Pilgrims on the Jordan River south of Tiberias.

 

Nazareth:

Childhood town of Jesus, with the Basilica of the Annunciation and where the house of Virgin Mary once stood.

 

While in Israel, enjoy comfortable Hotels as well as entertainment centers, huge number of unique shops, cafes and restaurants as well as some of the best clinics in the world offering therapeutic and cosmetic treatment programs based on salts and minerals.
