      About Prospec

      • Expertise

        20 years of extensive experience in recombinant protein production result in proprietary purification technologies of low production costs, high yield, production efficiency, utmost pure products and uncompromising biological activity at competitive prices.

      • RELIABILITY

        Having an indisputable record of reliably supplying proteins to scientists, ProSpec has become a well-known worldwide life-science leader, providing a vast array of proteins for research, development, and further manufacturing.

      • QUALITY

        Our research team and scientists rigorously test each batch to meet the research and development demand for excellent quality, uncompromising biological activity and consistency.

      • SERVICE

        Our goal is supplying many recombinant proteins which serve the academic and R&D industry. We have been able to assemble more than 6000 recombinant proteins, peptides and antibodies which are crucial to the research community.

      Our Customers

      ProSpec’s agenda includes being up-to-date with scientific developments through the collaboration with researchers & scientists in the academic and bioindustry fields. Researchers propose us important & crucial information which enables us to contribute to life science research from one end and increase our protein portfolio from the other…

      Our proteins, services and technology license are provided to a comprehensive range of customers around the globe from academic and government research institutions, biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies, as well as hospitals and reference laboratories.
