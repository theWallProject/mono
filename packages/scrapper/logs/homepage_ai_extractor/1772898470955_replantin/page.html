Technology

We are proud to introduce : PICI
Produce Integrated Cultivation Instrument

PICI is a fresh produce vending machine – fully autonomous and fully monitored, from seeds to a packed produce.
An instore growing unit to be located inside retailers’ stores and food services facilities.
Providing safe, healthy, and fresh produce, 365 days a year.
