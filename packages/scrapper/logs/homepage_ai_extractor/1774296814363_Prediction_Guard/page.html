Enforce Security Across Your Sovereign AI Systems

Manage, govern, and deploy your AI systems without compromising security

Operational Control & Standards-Aligned Governance

Stop treating AI as a collection of fragmented, external APIs and start operating it as a unified, sovereign system.

Prediction Guard is a security-first AI control plane that allows you to embed standards-aligned governance directly into the operational fabric of your AI system.

This shift allows you to move beyond symbolic governance into active authority, ensuring every handshake between models, tools, agents, and data is secure.

slide 7 to 10 of 11
 

Manage

Control is the prerequisite for AI Security. Goveranance is symbolic unless the enterprise owns, tracks, and manages the connective tissue of the AI systems it adopts.

  • Compose sovereign AI systems that contain models, MCP servers, and connections to application

  • Utilize Prediction Guard’s self-hosted control-plane, giving you control as you manage your systems

  • Move from consuming AI components (controlled by external vendors) to operating AI systems

Govern

System-level security requires embedding policy directly into the operational fabric of your AI systems rather than relying on point solutions or external, "firewall" filters.
  • Proactively analyze AI assets for vulnerabilities and obtain detailed AI BOMs for each of your AI systems

  • Audit and roll back AI system changes or updates

  • Integrate AI security events with your existing monitoring, alerting, or logging infrastructure

  • Apply AI governance policies globally or per AI system, API key, or agent

  • Align with NIST and OWASP standards out-of-the-box

Deploy

Accelerate ROI by deploying secure, tool-enabled AI agents that automatically inherit your system-wide governance and integrate directly with your sovereign AI systems.
  • No-code agent development via Prediction Guard’s Agent Forge application
  • Code forward integration via an OpenAI-compatible API, with access to all your unified AI assets (self-hosted models, cloud endpoints, MCP tools, etc.)

  • Granular telemetry across API keys, agents, and users

Secure AI at scale

Roll out AI functionality to the enterprise without losing control

Prediction Guard unifies fragmented AI assets (including AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, MCP tools, and agents) into a single, secure environment. We transform decentralized sprawl into a controlled, governed enterprise system.

 
Predictable Costs

Manage the TCO of your AI application sprawl

Prediction Guard reduces your Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) by 4X. Instead of struggling to integrate fragmented "AI security" point solutions, deploy a single control plane that ensures full compliance while you scale.
