A community of impact-driven CEOs, financiers and executives committed to Israel and Jewish communities

AlmaLinks is a global professional network that fosters meaningful relationships between altruistic CEOs, founders, financiers and seasoned executives

We nurture our members’ professional success and personal development by connecting them to each other - Outstanding business leaders in Israel and the Jewish communities worldwide. While all of our members are of Jewish heritage, our network is not religiously or politically affiliated.

Our Values

Intellectual generosity

When successful people share work and experiences in the right context, everyone benefits – our members and their families, businesses and communities

A culture of curiosity

Every member should seek to learn and be willing to share their knowledge

Mutual respect

Our network is non-hierarchical. Everyone is seen, heard and valued

Openness

Our members meet one another with no pre-conditions or specific expectations. The intention is to explore the connection and see the potential
