HERBAL PRODUCTS

Rekah specializes in the production of steroidal tablets and has a successful track record and extensive expertise in developing, registering and manufacturing steroids.

Steroid therapy is one of the most common therapies in the Western world for a variety of diseases.

Clinical importance:

  • Steroids are on the World Health Organization (WHO) essential drugs list (EML), which includes the most effective and safe drugs needed in any health care system.
  • Steroid treatment is one of the most commonly used in the Western world to treat a variety of medical conditions.

Main medicines:

  • Dexamethasone, hydrocortisone, cortisone acetate, prednisone and prednisolone.
  • Glucocorticoid drugs, synthetic steroids similar to the natural cortisol hormone.
  • Have the strongest anti-inflammatory activity and immunosuppressive activity - suppression of the immune system.

Diverse clinical use:

  • Steroids are clinically used for a huge variety of diseases:
    Respiratory system (mainly asthma), Musculoskeletal system (especially rheumatoid arthritis, myasthenia gravis), Gastrointestinal tract (especially Crohn's disease), Blood system (mainly hemolytic anemia), Autoimmune diseases, Allergies, Oncology, Cardiology (pericarditis), rejection of transplanted organs, and a wide variety of skin diseases.

Steroids are a common and effective treatment in:

  • Serious infections - such as inflammation of the membrane of the heart.
  • Autoimmune diseases - the use of corticosteroids causes suppression of immune system activity, reducing the harmful effect of the autoimmune response and significantly improving patients' condition. Such as lupus, psoriasis, Crohn's disease, rheumatoid arthritis.
  • Severe allergic reactions such as anaphylactic shock.
  • Many diseases respond well to steroid boost after which the dose can be reduced and still maintain the desired effect.

Steroids and Cancer:

  • Some steroids help destroy certain types of cancer cells and can improve the effectiveness of chemotherapy.
  • Some cancers are treated with aggressive chemotherapy combined with steroids.
  • The most common steroids in the treatment of cancer: hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, methylprednisolone, prednisolone.
  • Dexamethasone is also used to reduce edema that is caused by metastases in the brain.
  • In some cancer patients, steroids are used to improve overall feeling and increase appetite, reduce nausea and vomiting, and reduce allergic reactions to chemotherapy.
