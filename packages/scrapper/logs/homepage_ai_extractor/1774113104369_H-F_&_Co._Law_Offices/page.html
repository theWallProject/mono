About

UNIQUE

H-F & Co. is not your standard law firm, nor are we run-of-the-mill attorneys.

We bring to the table a unique team approach, combining commitment to excellence, top-notch expertise, professionalism and creative thinking.

We have a proven track record of delivering unparalleled value to our clients and bringing them to the finish line, achieving significant successes in some of the largest-ever transactions in the Israeli technology industry.

TECH FOCUS

Our in-depth understanding of companies’ business and the relevant legal environment has made us the go-to lawyers for some of the best tech companies in Israel. We represent tech companies at all stages, from entrepreneurs and startups to Unicorns and multi-billion dollar global technology companies. We also represent some of the largest international investment banks and underwriters, a selection of reputable venture capital and private equity funds and major multinational tech corporations, in their Israeli-related M&A transactions, investments and public offerings, all in the tech sector.

Our tech industry coverage includes: Software and SaaS, Cyber Security, Internet, Mobile Apps, Big Data, IoT, FinTech, AdTech, EduTech, AgriTech, CleanTech, Medical Devices, HealthIT, and MobileHealth Automotive and Aerospace e-Commerce HW and Semiconductors.

EXCELLENCE

We deliver to our tech clients top-tier 360˚ legal service. Our interdisciplinary methodology combines exceptional expertise in a full range of legal practices, including corporate finance, M&A, commercial and technology transactions, taxation, intellectual property, licensing, cyber, privacy and data protection, employee benefits, retention and compensation. We are known for our deep commitment to our clients, emphasis on highest quality legal advice and tailor-made creative solutions. Our team acted as lead legal counsel in the most prominent M&A, IPOs, private financings and other key transactions in the Israeli technology industry in each of the last several years.
