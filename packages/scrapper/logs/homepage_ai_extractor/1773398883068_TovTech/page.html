TovTech's Vision

Integrating people with disabilities into high-tech professions.

We believe that by building a tailored program and appropriate training, we can realize the potential of many people with disabilities by integrating them into high-tech professions.

We strive to enable as many people as possible to participate, which is why our programs are subsidized, online, and operated with adapted learning methods.
