Leo is Real’s 24/7 AI-powered concierge, designed to provide real-time support and insights for your business. From breaking down transaction payouts and automating document reviews to generating custom creative assets for your latest listings, Leo is your go-to assistant for streamlining workflows and enhancing productivity. Fully integrated with reZEN, Leo leverages your personal transaction data to answer questions about your deals, commissions, equity programs, and more. Whether it’s day or night, Leo is always on hand to help you stay ahead, optimize your business, and focus on what matters most.

Real Signature, Real’s proprietary e-signature tool, is seamlessly integrated into reZEN to simplify your transaction process—completely free of subscription fees. Its intuitive interface lets you quickly create templates or customize existing documents, saving you valuable time. You can easily manage transactions with folders, locate documents with advanced search features, and even edit sent documents without starting over. Real Signature also allows you to save drafts at any stage, offering flexibility at every step. With this complementary tool, you can streamline digital signatures and focus on closing deals and growing your business.

WealthPlan, Real’s customizable wealth calculator, allows you to set and track your earnings goals while taking full advantage of Real’s wealth-building opportunities, such as revenue sharing and stock incentives. With WealthPlan, you can visualize how your production goals, like GSV and unit targets, will impact your financial growth over the next five years. You can adjust your goals and instantly see how increasing your production or participating in Real’s Stock Purchase Plan (SPP) will influence your wealth at various stock prices. Additionally, you can set agent attraction goals and track how growing your network contributes to your long-term financial success. By monitoring your progress and allowing you to refine your approach, WealthPlan keeps you on target for financial success!

Revenue Share Insights gives you a clear view of your earnings through Real’s Revenue Share program, providing a detailed breakdown of your financial growth. With this tool, you can easily track your monthly earnings and compare them to previous months, view your full payment history, and see your progress in unlocking tiers. You can also monitor your entire network, including how much you earn from each tier, and access individual network members’ contact details for quick outreach. Plus, sharing your unique referral link with prospective agents is just a click away. Revenue Share Insights keeps you informed, connected, and empowered to maximize your earnings potential.

Secure Your Financial Future with Real

Real is dedicated to supporting your financial future, both during your career and in retirement. Our Real Retirement program allows agents with a minimum of three years of service to continue earning monthly revenue share payments after retirement, as long as they maintain an active real estate license. Additionally, with our Willable Revenue Share program, agents can designate a beneficiary (subject to program requirements) to receive up to 100% of their revenue share after five years with Real, ensuring their legacy is secure.

02
Integrated Tools to Elevate Your Brand

Real equips agents with customizable, MLS-integrated templates that enhance your production and elevate your brand. Quickly generate flyers, brochures, social media assets, and more to promote your latest listings. Plus, benefit from our Real Partners program, which offers exclusive discounts on industry-leading vendors, tools, and services to help you succeed.

03
Expert-Led Training to Enhance Your Skills

Real Academy provides agents with hundreds of hours of live and on-demand training each week, led by industry experts. Whether you want to sharpen your business acumen or discover innovative marketing strategies, our trainers are dedicated to your success. Additionally, Real President Sharran Srivatsaa shares his decades of industry experience and extensive network to deliver invaluable insights, helping you reach new heights in your real estate career

04
Competitive and Accessible Health Insurance

At Real, your health and well-being are our top priorities. We provide agents and their families with access to affordable healthcare benefits, ensuring you get the coverage you need without overspending. Agents throughout the US and Canada can access convenient medical, dental, vision, and specialty plans at rates significantly lower than public market options. Explore our exclusive benefits by visiting your country’s Real Agent Benefits website (US | CAN).

Supporting Our Agent Community

One Real Impact is Real’s agent assistance fund designed to provide financial support during challenging times. Any Real agent can apply for a grant funded by both Real and fellow agents. Grants from One Real Impact are never loans, meaning agents are not required to repay the funds received, and all grants are tax-exempt. Agents can apply for assistance during natural disasters, personal hardships, or other significant challenges.

Why Choose Real?

At Real, we empower agents to take control of their financial journey by providing access to cutting-edge technology and exceptional support. As a non-franchise brokerage, our low overhead allows us to offer competitive commission splits, eliminating franchise fees and streamlining operations through innovative solutions. With Real, you can:

  • Maximize Your Earnings: Take advantage of our competitive commission plan to enhance your wealth.
  • Grow Your Investment: Invest in Real stock and receive generous bonuses as you hit key milestones.
  • Leave a Legacy: Build wealth for future generations through our 5-tier revenue share and referral programs.

Additionally, our proprietary AI-powered technology platform, reZEN, simplifies your workflow and boosts productivity. With reZEN, you have everything at your fingertips to manage your deals, documents, and due diligence. Its AI-driven real-time support provides insights, ranging from transaction payout breakdowns to generating custom creative assets for your listings. By leveraging your personal transaction data, reZEN ensures quick answers to questions about your deals, commissions, and equity programs, keeping you ahead of the competition.

How do I learn more about joining Real?

Reach out to us at onboarding@therealbrokerage.com. Our team is ready to guide you through the process or connect you with a growth leader who can share what it’s like to be an agent at Real. Ready to join?

How do I bring my team to Real?

Real offers ideal solutions for your team! Our ProTeams solution provides unmatched flexibility, allowing you to customize your business model down to the agent level. From splits to caps to fees, everything is configurable to fit your team’s unique needs and maximize performance. Contact onboarding@therealbrokerage.com to learn more. Ready to join?

Can I keep my brand when I move to Real?

Yes! With Real’s Private Label program, eligible independent brokerages can continue to shine under their brand while operating as a Real-powered brokerage on the backend. You can offload administrative work and focus on what you do best. Solo agents and teams can also leverage Real’s high-end templates and brand guidelines for legally compliant co-branded marketing materials.
