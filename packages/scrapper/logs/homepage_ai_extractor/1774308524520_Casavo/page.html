Why don't you buy my house?

Every situation is unique. Sometimes, if you want to sell, it is not convenient for us to buy your house ourselves. That is why we put you in touch with potential buyers to help you maximise your profit quickly.

Can I sell and buy at the same time?

Casavo supports both buyers and sellers. It starts with the valuation of your home. When talking to a Casavo agent, specify that you also want to buy a house. To buy with Casavo, you will get a 4% commission. If your house is worth less than EUR 125 000, your commission will be EUR 5 000.
