About Us

 

Our Mission

Our status as a market leader can be directly attributed to our efforts to recruit and retain a quality workforce. Our team is dedicated to making easy shopping to supply your workplace.

Who We Are

For more than 10 years, L1-systems has partnered with global businesses, Education organizations, Industrial businesses to provide IT solutions to achieve their business goals.

We deliver communications, infrastructure, and consulting solutions that unlock innovation, drive efficiency and accelerate growth.


Our expert IT professionals focus on your business opportunities as they assess, design, building, and manage solutions that meet your strategic objectives. With a wide variety of certifications and years of experience across many technologies and industries, the  L1-systems team can help solve even the most complex challenges:

  • Streamline and simplify your IT processes
  • Offload time-consuming tasks so your IT team can focus on business initiatives
  • Implement new technologies that support innovation and collaboration
  • Access IT talent with a broad range of experience
  • Scale your technology solutions to adapt to the seasons of your business
  • Protect your sensitive business and customer data
  • Keep your business up and running in the event of a natural disaster or cyber attack

Our Approach

 

L1-Systems specializes in representing and promoting advanced technological solutions from Israel’s leading Homeland Security (HLS) companies. With deep industry expertise and strong global relationships, we serve as a strategic bridge between world-class Israeli innovators and international government agencies, security organizations, and private-sector clients.

Our company delivers end-to-end support for technology manufacturers, including market development, product positioning, business intelligence, and direct engagement with qualified customers across Latin America, Europe, and other key regions.

L1-Systems partners exclusively with top-tier Israeli companies in areas such as intelligence systems, cyber solutions, secure communications, threat detection, advanced scanning technologies, and operational security platforms. We ensure that our clients receive cutting-edge, field-proven technologies tailored to their operational needs.

Driven by professionalism, integrity, and long-term vision, L1-Systems empowers organizations to enhance their security capabilities through innovative Israeli technologies and trusted commercial representation.
