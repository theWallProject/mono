Frequently Asked Questions

Keeping our customer’s convenience in mind, we have answered all the most frequently asked questions here:
Full FAQ List
Is your handyman, plumbing and electrical work guaranteed, and if so what is your warranty period?

Yes, we offer a worry free guarantee – if we worked on it and it’s not working right, we will come back no questions asked. The guarantee on workmanship is one year unless otherwise stated.

Are your handymen plumbers and electricians trustworthy to be working in our home or business?

Our employees are thoroughly trained and screened before they are allowed to work for us at the HandyMench. We wouldn’t send someone to you who we don’t trust with our livelihood.

What form of payment do you accept for services?

We accept all major credit card brands due upon completion of the work.

 

Do you bring materials or do I need my own?

You can provide the materials, but it isn’t necessary. Our Technicians carry an inventory of materials needed for most jobs around the house, and whenever necessary, they are happy to go shopping for you at local home improvement centers.

* We mark up materials to compensate for shopping time.

How does your billing work?

We have a transparent billing structure. We charge by the item, not by the hour. We have a Price List available. We feel that this is the most honest way to do business. We suggest that you speak to us to understand more. Visit our Contact page for details.

Do you have references?

Yes, we do - and our customers are happy to tell you all about our amazing service. Just ask via the Contact page, and we will connect you with someone in your neighborhood.
