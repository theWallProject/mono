Frequently Asked Questions About Synerion

What is Synerion?



Synerion is a cloud-based time and attendance software provider offering employee scheduling, absence management, labor costing, and mobile solutions. We serve businesses of all sizes with both turnkey (Agile) and customizable (Enterprise) workforce management solutions.

How does Synerion prevent buddy punching?



Synerion uses biometric time clocks with facial recognition and fingerprint technology to ensure only authorized employees can clock in. This eliminates buddy punching and improves time tracking accuracy for better payroll management.

What integrations does Synerion support?



Synerion integrates with over 100 popular payroll, ERP, and HR systems including ADP, SAP, Workday, Sage, Ceridian, BambooHR, QuickBooks, and Gusto. We offer 3,000+ integrations to seamlessly connect with your existing technology stack.

What's the difference between Synerion Agile and Enterprise?



Synerion Agile is a pre-configured, turnkey solution perfect for small to mid-sized businesses. Enterprise is fully customizable for complex workforce structures, accommodating intricate labor rules, union requirements, and unique scheduling needs.

Does Synerion offer customer support?



Yes, Synerion provides world-class in-house customer support via email and phone, plus our AI chatbot SAI built into the system. SAI helps with common tasks like adding employees and fixing time entries.
