With a multidisciplinary team and global reach, we empower businesses to test, simulate, and optimize electric motors with unmatched accuracy—ensuring compliance with international standards (IEC, IEEE, CSA) and future-proofing operations for the era of electrification. Electric 2-Wheeler

Ensure Performance, Reliability & Compliance in Every Ride As electric two-wheelers rapidly evolve, precise motor testing is critical for both motor manufacturers and OEMs building e-motorcycles and scooters. Whether you produce motors in-house or rely on external suppliers, validating performance, durability, and efficiency is essential to avoid costly recalls, ensure regulatory compliance, and deliver a superior rider experience. Motomea’s advanced testing systems — including our patented Inertial Dynamometer (IDS) — offer fast, accurate, and real-world simulations tailored for compact, high-torque e-mobility motors, empowering you to guarantee quality from R&D to production.

Electric 3- & 4-Wheeler

Powering Precision for Urban and Utility EVs Electric 3- and 4-wheelers play a crucial role in modern mobility and logistics — making motor reliability and performance non-negotiable. For OEMs and motor suppliers alike, rigorous testing ensures optimal torque delivery, energy efficiency, and extended lifecycle under real-world conditions. Motomea’s dynamic testing systems, including our 4-Quadrant Regenerative Dynamometers and patented IDS technology, provide rapid, accurate evaluations of motor behavior during acceleration, braking, and drive cycles — helping manufacturers deliver vehicles that meet today’s demands for sustainability, safety, and performance.

Electric Vehicle 2WD & 4WD

Test with Confidence. Drive with Certainty. As EV powertrains become more sophisticated, especially in 2WD and 4WD configurations, the need for precise and synchronized motor testing is greater than ever. Whether you're developing dual-motor architectures, e-axles, or full 4WD electric platforms, ensuring seamless coordination, torque distribution, and system efficiency is essential. Motomea’s modular test benches with integrated HiL (Hardware-in-the-Loop) simulation and patented IDS technology deliver unmatched insight into powertrain dynamics — enabling OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers to validate performance under complex driving conditions, optimize efficiency, and accelerate development cycles.

E-Motors for Industry, Aerospace, Logistics, Transportation, Testing Labs

Mission-Critical Testing for High-Stakes Applications In sectors where precision, safety, and durability are paramount — such as aerospace, industrial automation, logistics, and transportation — electric motors must perform flawlessly under demanding conditions. Whether for mission-critical aerospace components or high-efficiency conveyor systems, comprehensive motor testing is essential for quality assurance, compliance, and innovation. Motomea’s versatile testing platforms, including our Regenerative Dynamometer Systems (RDS) and patented IDS technology, empower manufacturers and test labs to simulate real-world load scenarios, capture dynamic performance data, and validate motor integrity with absolute confidence — ensuring that every motor meets the highest global standards.

A China-based appliance manufacturer turned to Motomea for a solution to test their high-speed, high-torque electric motors used in advanced home appliances. Their R&D team faced unique challenges due to high power densities, wide torque and speed ranges, and the need for robust, repeatable testing.

Motomea delivered a tailored solution combining our Regenerative Dynamometer System (RDS) with the patented Inertial Dynamometer System (IDS). This setup enabled precise, real-time testing of motor performance parameters like torque ripple, friction, and back EMF, all without the need for mechanical loading.

As a result, the manufacturer was able to streamline development, ensure thermal and performance stability, and accelerate time to market—all while maintaining high testing accuracy and efficiency.
