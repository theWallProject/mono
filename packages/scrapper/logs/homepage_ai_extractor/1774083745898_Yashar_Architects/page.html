At Yashar Architects, we work in a professional and creative environment to deliver innovative design solutions that are built perfectly on time and on budget. Equipped with strong design skills and sound professional expertise, we create dynamic spaces in Israel and abroad for working, living and interaction. Our projects vary in scale and type from urban planning to interior design, with programs that range from retail, commercial, residential, offices, and hotels.

We work to create spaces for human experience, carefully considering the aesthetic and functional aspects in every scale of the design process. Our aim is to create buildings that meet the needs of the client and enhance the well-being of the intended users, by designing conceptually clear and unique solutions for each project.
