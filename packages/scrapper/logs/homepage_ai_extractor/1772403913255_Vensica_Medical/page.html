Discover Our Cutting-edge neurotoxin Therapy

Vensica is developing ViXe, a product that combines Xeomin®, a commercial neurotoxin, and Vibe, a breakthrough, needle-free, ultrasound drug delivery device.

Xeomin®

Uniquely purified Botulinum Toxin A, FDA-approved, and licensed from one of the major players in global neurology-focused specialty pharma

Vibe

The first needle-free Botulinum Toxin A delivery device

Meet the Next Generation in Urology Therapeutics

Vibe is a breakthrough platform designed to deliver drugs directly into the bladder wall, providing uniform, controlled and localized treatments for urology applications.

Vibe leverages a disruptive ultrasound technology to deliver drugs through the natural barriers of the urinary bladder. By generating cavitation bubbles, Vibe precisely microinjects the drugs into the bladder wall, ensuring a controlled and uniform delivery of medications and biologics.
