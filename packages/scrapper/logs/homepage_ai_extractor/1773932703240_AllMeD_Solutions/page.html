Minimally invasive solutions

Allium Medical Solutions Ltd. is a publicly traded company (TASE: ALMD) which develops, manufactures and markets minimally invasive products internationally in various medical disciplines. These innovative products serve the need for minimally invasive interventions benefiting the patient by improving their recovery process and shortening it. Allium Medical Solutions’ products are used by physicians to treat a wide range of diseases and patients worldwide. We invest in our people, processes and products in order to give our users the confidence they need in order to operate and ensure their patients are getting the best treatment possible.

Our business currently includes the following distinct products

Allevetix Medical is a leading developer and manufacturer of innovative medical devices focused on minimal invasive therapy for the treatment of type 2 diabetes in obese and overweight patients.

For more information

Allium Medical through its True Leaf division is vigorously developing a solution to the mitral valve regurgitation problem, affecting millions of people around the globe.

For more information

The ProCatid system (formerly known as WIRION by Gardia Medical) is a distal embolic protection system comprised of an independent Filter Unit that can be delivered, locked and deployed on any commercially marketed guide wires.
