V2X or vehicle-to-everything technology revolutionizes transportation safety and mobility

It all starts with real-time, direct communication that includes:

 V2V

Vehicle to vehicle

 V2I

Vehicle to road infrastructure

 V2VRU

Vehicle to vulnerable road users

Cellular network connectivity complements these time-critical communications to make entire transportation systems smarter.

Learn more

Qualcomm Technologies is creating the next generation of V2X products with a unified global solution that:

Supports safety-focused solutions for road users

Sees beyond line-of-sight to accelerate reaction time of ADAS and automated vehicles

Improves mobility with real-time information

Enables services such as tolling and other secure transactions

V2X offers proactive safety alerts

V2I, V2V and V2VRU facilitate the sharing of data between road users and traffic infrastructure to provide actionable alerts. And soon, these technologies will trigger key ADAS features. V2X is important because, according to the World Health Organization (WHO):

Approximately 1.19 million people die each year as a result of road traffic crashes.

Between 20 and 50 million more people suffer non-fatal injuries, with many incurring a disability.

Our V2X is purpose-built for ADAS

With the ability to “see” around corners and past obstacles, V2X technology is ideally suited as an information-rich sensor to assist ADAS features such as auto emergency braking (AEB).

V2V

Enables vehicles to send and receive information.

Critical data such as speed, position, direction, and more are exchanged to promote a safety-focused, more optimized traffic flow.


This becomes especially important near busy intersections where the ability to communicate with vehicles around corners and obstacles proves highly effective.

V2I

Facilitates real-time data exchange between vehicles and infrastructure.

Roadside units can be connected to most any device, from traffic signals to streetlights to tolling systems.

 

The infrastructure transmits information to roadway users about traffic conditions, signal timing, road hazards, construction and road work, pedestrians present on roadway and more.

V2VRU

Helps vehicles see vulnerable road users (VRUs).

VRUs are individuals who are at a higher risk of injury or death when involved in traffic collisions, and account for more than half of all road traffic deaths according to the World Health Organization.

 

They consist primarily of cyclists including e-bikes, motorcyclists, motorized scooter riders and in the future pedestrians. Direct communication between VRUs, infrastructure sensors and other vehicles helps improve the detection of hard-to-spot, often obstructed VRUs.

V2X saves time

Real-time data shared through V2V and V2I communication enables safety applications critical for future automated vehicles.

Additionally, shared information would inform speed, route and maneuvering suggestions to optimize flow, leading to less congestion, and overall improved system and personal mobility.

China mass produces smart vehicles equipped with C-V2X technology

Nearly 90 cities have deployed tens of thousands of roadside units (RSUs) to demonstrate intelligent, networked roads to align with the mass production of smart vehicles equipped with C-V2X technology.

Under the C-Roads Platform, a joint initiative of European Member States and road operators, Cooperative Intelligent Transport Systems (C-ITS) utilizing V2X technology have been installed across the EU to improve road safety, traffic efficiency and decision-making.

More V2X applications

Heavy machinery

In industries such as agriculture, mining, construction, manufacturing and more, heavy-duty vehicles often operate in very challenging environments and in close proximity. Enabling vehicles to communicate with each other, the surrounding environment and workers helps heighten safety and improve productivity.

Heavy vehicles

Commercial and government fleets such as heavy trucks and transit vehicles that operate on tight schedules can benefit from centralized V2X data that informs purpose-built applications. Utilizing V2X technology is expected to streamline operations and support more efficient movement of goods, services and people.

Discover our V2X suite of products

Qualcomm® 300 V2X Chip Series

Qualcomm® V2X 350 Chipsets

A complete, fully integrated sensor that supports V2X global radio technologies including DSRC, LTE-V2X and the latest 5G-V2X (NR-V2X). It’s capable of advanced use cases and includes an embedded hardware security module (eHSM) and a line-rate verification engine.

Learn more

Qualcomm® V2X 300 Chipsets

Embeds two radios operating concurrently, either LTE-V2X or DSRC and 5G-V2X, which supports global V2X protocols. Includes an embedded hardware security module (eHSM) and a line-rate verification engine.

Learn more

Qualcomm® V2X 350 Demo Kits

Two fully integrated V2X 3rd generation SoC evaluation kits and reference design for vehicles and bicycles, e-bikes and e-scooters. They feature built-in V2X firmware for cost optimization, reducing development integration efforts and enabling fast time to commercialization. A tablet application allows users to view warnings related to other road users and configure the radio parameters for radio performance field tests.

Other V2X products

Qualcomm® V2X 250 Chipsets

A communication Linux processor specifically designed for connected automated driving applications integrating enhanced-range mobility-optimized IEEE802.11p DSRC and C-V2X direct communication modems.

Learn more

Qualcomm® V2X 200 Chipsets

A global V2X hardware add-on that integrates an enhanced-range mobility-optimized IEEE802.11p DSRC and C-V2X direct communications modems.

Learn more

V2X Software

The V2X stack is optimized for the Qualcomm® V2X 350 chip, enabling fast execution. All received messages from the concurrent dual channels are processed at low latency. The safety applications include local hazards for V2I, and crash warnings for V2V and V2VRU. The software can be optimized for ADAS integration, as well as fitting two-wheelers or aftermarket devices.
