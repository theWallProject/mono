about us

We are inspired by Science, but always driven by Business.

BDC is a consulting firm specialised in corporate strategy and business development for life sciences companies.

Founded in 2002 by a group of forward-thinking experts, all former executives of the healthcare industry, our initial goal was to help innovative start-ups developing their business through strategic agreements and partnerships with industrial players.

BDC’s extensive international network and physical presence in Europe, Israel and USA enable and facilitate worldwide support to its clients.

Since its inception, BDC has advised more than 200 companies, and has grown into an international consulting firm. Following the expansion of its client portfolio BDC advises today a large spectrum of companies including start-ups, mid-size companies, big pharma, private and public organisations, VCs and financial institutions.

BDC is active in several fields such as biotechnology, pharmacy, diagnostics, platforms, instrumentation, e-health …

Why Us?

  • Global offer: from science to business, from ideas to strategic recommendations, up to negotiation and deal closing…
  • Multidisciplinary workforces
  • Scientific, industry and business knowledges: all senior partners were formerly executives from big pharma and created many biotech companies
  • Extended worldwide network, including executives in the industry, experts and KOLs
  • Advising, implementing and fighting by our clients’ side
  • Customising all our missions to clients’ needs
  • Strengthening of BDC’s offer by integration of our experienced advisors in legal and intellectual property

Because we are inspired by Science, but always driven by Business.

Corinne SZILAGYI

Corinne has garnered deep expertise in R&D and Project management along with Strategic Alliances identification and Business Development in the health sector. She has more than 25 years’ experience in pharma – mainly at Pfizer – and biotech, at operational and strategic levels and more than 10 years in strategical consulting in healthcare and more largely in the biotech sector. Her area of expertise includes drug candidate development (NCEs and biologics), cell and gene therapy, biomarkers, nutraceuticals and bioprocesses.  She has also strong experience in change management, R&D processes harmonization, leadership of integration programs following M&A operations and leading matrix structures.

Corinne holds a PhD in Biochemistry from the University of Sherbrooke, Canada, a MSc in Human Physiopathology from the University Paul Sabatier, Toulouse, France, and an Executive MBA from the French business school HEC Paris, France. She also completed training with the French Navy on the Management and Leadership in Crisis Situations.

Farid BOUZIDI

Former Head of Business Development at AC Immune. Before, he was at Institut Pasteur and Roche. Certified in critical reading and interpretation of clinical trials for the medical practice.

Farid holds a Ph.D. in Biophysics and Biochemistry in Immunology from the French Atomic Agency and Joseph Fourier University in Grenoble, MBA from EM-Lyon, France.

Bastien LAFARGUE

Biotechnology MS degree with a dual training in science and business. Bachelor degree in Biology from University Pierre et Marie Curie (Paris VI). Trained at BDC since 2013, Bastien is now an experience strategic consultant and project manager with a good experience in market analysis, business development, financial, and other subjects gathered through the numerous and varied missions he worked on with BDC.
