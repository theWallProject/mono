Vectria Technologies specializes in RF to mmWave systems, components and technologies
to deliver state of the art cost effective solutions.

Vectria Technologies offers a wide range of products among which are:

  • Transmitters
  • SSPA
  • Tuners
  • Transceivers and up/down Converters
  • Exciters
  • Signal Sources
  • T/R Modules
  • Beam Forming Networks
  • High Power low loss control components (Phase Shifters, Switches, Limiters etc.)
  • Low loss Power Dividers
  • Specialty passive components (highly selective, linear phase and multiplexed Filters)
  • Very low noise Amplifiers
