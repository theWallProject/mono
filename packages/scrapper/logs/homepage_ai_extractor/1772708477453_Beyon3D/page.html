inspiring innovation

Founded in Israel in 2011, Beyon3D is a rapidly growing public company, scalable in products, markets, and territories. Beyon3D’s unique business model and increasing product demand, enables brand success, deep market penetration, and maximum profitability.

Beyon3D is first and foremost multidisciplinary building technology and industrial robotics company. Beyon3D has been the spearhead of ingenuity in the construction industry, through the development of new technologies and production processes that allows the creation of new age series of superior concrete building products that is unparalleled in the industry.

Following years of intense R&D, Beyon3D has completed its pioneering technology, reinventing the process of design and manufacturing of innovative constructive and decorative building products, redefining quality and aesthetics in the field.

where are we now?

Construction is underway on the first Beyon3D’s factory in Israel with production scheduled to begin early 2022.
Beyon3D’s first of its kind factory will be an advanced manufacturing facility with significantly more automation
including a fully automated robotics manufacturing line which provides concrete end products.

FAQ?

Where are Beyon3D’s end products manufactured?

In 2019 Beyon3D broke ground on the first factory in Israel, in Haifa port.

How long are the Beyon3D’s building products expected to last?

Beyon3D’s products are expected to last at least the same time as standard concrete building products or longer.

When will the local factory’s production operations begin?

Our factory was established in March 2022, and it is now in its final stages of preproduction tests. The factory will begin its serial production by the end of the year.

Can I customize Beyon3D’s end products?

Yes. Our series of products can be customized by shape, dimensions, colors and surface finishing.

When will the Beyon3D’s series of products be available to anyone interested?

Beyon3D is currently involved in various large projects, working with leading construction companies. Yet, we would love to learn more about your project. Contact us for details.

Are Beyon3D’s series of products build to code?

Beyon3D’s products are designed to meet all the local standards and regulations for every territory in which they are sold.

technology

Beyon3D’s off-site manufacturing process is an advanced, holistic solution to automatic fabrication of a large spectrum of concrete building products. With minimal capital investment, Beyon3D’s game changing technology is reaching micron-level precision at unprecedented production speed and capacity, compared to traditional methods. Combining a controlled environment with in-process QA/QC procedures, Beyon3D’s new fabrication process is revolutionizing the construction industry, eliminating the inefficiencies caused by common outdated construction processes and human labor.
