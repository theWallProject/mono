Eon Data Protection

Automated Cloud Infrastructure Protection

Secure your environment with cloud-native, agentless architecture. Eon automatically backs up your cloud, slashing costs via deduplication. Featuring built-in cloud backup posture management (CBPM) and log archiving, you can instantly recover anything down to a single database row or file.

Eon Data Lake Infrastructure

Managed Zero-ETL Data Lake

Transform cloud resources into a high-performance managed data lake, 100% agentless and with zero-ETL. Eon ingests and deduplicates your data into open formats like Iceberg and Parquet. Query directly via Snowflake, Databricks, or BigQuery to eliminate complex pipelines and cut Spark compute costs by 30%. Use AI to run natural-language queries across your backup and archived data, or connect directly to your LLM through our MCP agent.
