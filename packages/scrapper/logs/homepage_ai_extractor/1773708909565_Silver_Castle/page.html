About

  • Meet our team

Silver Castle is a leading Israeli institutional-grade investment house, specializing in investment management and the development of financial solutions in the digital asset sector

Established in 2018 by senior executives from Israel’s banking and capital markets industries, Silver Castle became a publicly traded company on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange in January 2022. With a strong commitment to transparency and reliability, our mission is to provide secure and seamless access

to digital assets for a wide range of investors.
In addition, we actively collaborate with regulators to design and implement innovative financial frameworks for digital assets, while leveraging proven performance to develop advanced trading strategies

Our Services

Advanced Investment Management for Accredited and Institutional Investors

Silver Castle provides innovative investment solutions designed for the dynamic digital financial market, combining advanced risk management, intelligent algorithmic trading and the development of structured investment products.

What We Offer?

  • Hedge Fund Management – Development and management of hedge funds based on Bitcoin as a core asset
  • Smart Trading Strategies – Utilizing AI and big data for investment management
  • Advanced Crypto Investments – Building research and development infrastructure for NFT and DeFi investments
  • A unique, accessible, and secure asset management platform – in collaboration with leading entities in the financial and insurance sectors.

How We Operate?

  • Crypto Investment Management for Accredited and Institutional Investors
  • Institutional Grade Asset Management Platform – Registered in the Cayman Islands under CIMA (Cayman Islands Monetary Authority) regulation
  • Secure Asset Management with Comprehensive International Insurance
  • Fully transparent, monthly external reporting by an independent administrator
  • Easy access through traditional banks with ISIN
