How It works

Historically, inventory forecasting has relied on static projections—looking at historical data to estimate future demand. While this is useful as a baseline, the reality is that there are countless factors that contribute to how a product will perform over time. Predictive AI allows us to leverage a multitude of insights and adjust forecasting in realtime, ensuring you are making the best inventory decisions for your business every day.
