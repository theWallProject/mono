Accelerate ROI by discovering deeper influences and capitalizing on opportunities.

InsFocus BI’s industry-specific tools and built-in insurance expertise deliver clear, detailed analysis and focused information to insurance business executives and management at all levels and in all insurance business domains.

A broad range of actuarial data management and calculation tools, including claim development (triangulation) with chain ladder extrapolation, policy and sum-insured exposure calculations & claim behavior pattern analysis: Accident, Reporting, Payment analysis.

InsFocus frees the IT team from the development and maintenance of the BI platform, allowing them to concentrate on the most urgent and core developments of the insurance company. The entire model is open, and all definitions reside in simple SQL databases.

Boost sales and retention with InsFocus BI’s marketing analysis features. Monitor sales and analyze distribution channel performance. With InsFocus you can supervise marketing campaign effectiveness, control lapse and attrition, identify and capitalize on cross-selling / up-selling.
