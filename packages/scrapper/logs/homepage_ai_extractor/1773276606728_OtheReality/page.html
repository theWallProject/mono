OtheReality has developed a personalized empathy gym for medical centers. Leveraging VR, staff are invited to walk a mile in the shoes of their patients and colleagues through individual workouts designed using our research-backed methodology.

​

We provide our clients with a comprehensive and complete solution - from analysing the organisation's needs, through filming and production, to developing a tailored training program.

HOW DOES IT WORK?

We have developed a unique empathy and communication training methodology, backed by academic research and real world experience:

 

1.  VR Experience - Our in-app training modules present the trainee with

     a VR experience presenting specific provider-patient interaction (for                   example: delivering bad news). The engaging multi-perspective VR                     experiences propel the viewer to feel the other person's emotions. 

 

2. App-Based Workshop -The emotional empathy achieved is later elaborated      via a personal app-based workshop that processes cognitive and                      motivational empathy and improves communication skills.

 

3. Team-Based Workshop - Following the personal reflection, a team-based         workshop is then initiated allowing colleagues to learn and improve                   together.


4. Data analysis - The data collected from the workshop is later analyzed by        senior academic researchers and processed into “bottom-lines”, giving the        organization a detailed understanding on their empathy and                                  communication  KPI’s
