  • Our Services

    Product Strategy Consulting

    It's always the Strategy to make-or-break your business

    Without a strong and clear strategy, you're playing the terrible game of chance. And we're sorry, but by our book 'giving it your best shot', is just not enough.

     

    Harnessing tens of years of experience in strategy, product, and goal-driven execution, we'll partner with you to define the best winning strategy, crystallize the plan to achieve it, and help you put it to action.

    Analytics Consulting

    To truly become Data-Driven, you must align your data to your business and Product needs

    When planning and strategizing your Analytics implementation, you must focus on we need to understand, before want we need to measure.

     

    The Product Alliance is globally recognized as one, if the top, Analytics experts. Whether it be GA, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Heap, Fullstory, Pendo or any platform, our vast experience and recognized methodology, will help guide and empower your team to truly own your data.

    Startup / CEO Coaching

    Been here, done that (and failed too)

    We've walked in your shoes, far more than once. We know the deep loneliness feeling and we also bear the failure scars.

     

    Our task is one - cut your learning curve and push you to perform beyond your expectations. From mentoring to coaching, 1-on-1 or in groups, from C-level execs to product managers, we help you catch the right waves and ride them like a pro.

    Courses and Workshops

    Because knowledge is key

    Every now and then, you have to 'sharpen your tools'.

     

    We provide a variety of education and training sessions, publicly or custom tailored, such as:

    - Analytics courses, workshops and seminars. Globally recognized and certified

    - The productMBA - Product Leadership Course

    - Product Strategy workshops for non-product executives (CEOs, COOs, CMOs, CTOs, CFOs)

    - Product Marketing workshops

    Interim CPO

    Because a Product Leader is a crucial asset of a Product-oriented company

    You may not yet need a full-time Chief Product Officer (CPO) but you must have someone strong in that leadership role, and you need it yesterday. And if you have your leader, you surely wish they had an extra brain and another pair of hands...


    We fill those gaps with you right now. And yes, it can be done part-time (some say it's even better).

    New Frontiers Exploration

    "He who moves not forward, goes backwards"

    When everyone is drawn to working heads-down on customers' critical tasks, no one has the attention to look after future innovation anymore.

     

    We get you 'back on the horse' of innovation. We'll be your scouts, do the research and analysis, and help you plan future evolution. Like a drone you send up and forward to see the waves developing ahead so you can make sure you'll catch them.

    AI & ML Expertise

    AI that Elevates Experiences

    We are proud to be at the forefront of AI/ML product evolution. We offer a rare hybrid expertise in Product Strategy and core AI.

     

    Having successfully deployed AI solutions across retail, IoT, tech, entertainment, and more, we are fully familiar with both customer pain points and the challenges of building the scalable back-end AI platforms that power delightful real-time experiences.

    Product Leaders Mentoring

    Because the right guidance can make a good PM into a great Product Leader

    There's a point in time you know you need someone experienced to guide you. Someone who knows your challenges and can help you optimize your actions.

     

    From mentoring to coaching, 1-on-1 or in groups, we offer an accelerator for product professionals and their bosses who understand the need.

    Go-To-Market Planning

    It's where the rubber meets the road

    Having a great product that fits its market can only succeed if it's introduced to the right customers at the right time with the right value proposition.
