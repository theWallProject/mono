Simnoa – Simulating a Universe Beyond Reality

Simnoa Technologies is a world leader in designing and manufacturing technologically advanced entertainment solutions. Simnoa offers 4D, 5D, 7D and XD cinema and motion effect seat simulators; virtual reality (VR) rides; and SkyRide Flying Theater elevated immersive viewing mechanism. Because Simnoa solutions are all based on cutting edge technologies, we can supply the most advanced, full turnkey solutions to the 4D theater industry world-wide. As a result, theme parks, malls, 3D, 4D, 5D and 7D cinemas, museums, planetariums, expos, visitors centers, XD Theaters, and Flying Theaters around the globe use Simnoa solutions. With Simnoa’s theaters offering the widest range of special effects, VR seats, and more, the sky and beyond are the limit to the creative, thrilling and immersive entertainment offerings that we can provide.
