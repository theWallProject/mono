Transform Connected Mobility Data into Cybersecurity and Quality Insights, Powered by AI

Upstream’s cloud-based agentless platform ingests data from connected vehicles (telematics), OTA, diagnostics, dealerships, and APIs, delivering agility and scalability with zero in-vehicle footprint. Powered by advanced AI and ML, the platform detects known and emerging anomalies and trends, accelerating investigations and response. The platform transforms traditional analytics into proactive and strategic insights that drive cybersecurity and quality at scale.

Proactive Quality Detection

Reduce warranty and recall costs of connected and software-defined vehicles with a purpose-built solution, powered by ML models and advanced AI.
Upstream empowers automotive quality teams to monitor component failures earlier, predict scale and severity, and help quality engineers and forensic experts with Root Cause Analysis (RCA).

Mobility Cybersecurity Detection & Response (XDR)

Ensure trust and safety by securing passenger, commercial, electric, agricultural, and modern connected vehicles and IoT devices. Upstream's cloud-based cybersecurity detection & response platform (XDR) is purpose-built to leverage connected vehicle and IoT data delivering near real-time monitoring, advanced ML-based detection, and GenAI-powered investigations for effective threat mitigation.

API Security

Misuse Detection

Identify and monitor automotive misuse activities targeted to bypass subscription fees or manipulate vehicles for warranty-related claims. Leveraging a unique understanding of automotive cybersecurity and the Upstream AI-powered platform's robust vehicle digital twin and detection, the Misuse Detection solution flags anomalies in vehicle data to indicate feature or service misuse.
