From Boundaries to Breakthroughs

Unlock your Coordinate Measuring Machines’ (CMM) true potential with a robust, flexible solution integrating frame, controller, and software. Upgrade in just one day using our easy-to-install retrofit controller, matching all major CMM brands – no inspection software upgrades needed, while multiplying compatibility. Enhance future proof technology through hassle free retrofitting – no upgrades, no rewiring or full calibration needed, using existing controller error map.

Chameleon Controller

A plug-and-play retrofit CMM controller compatible with all major brands. No cable cutting or rewiring required, and no inspection software changes needed.

Mediator Software

Connect your existing CMM controller with the inspection software of your choice. No hardware changes or part-program rewriting required. Available in Direct and I++ version.

Mapper Software

Import, modify, and export error maps from supported CMM controllers, without relying on OEM software. Compatible with Hexagon, Mitutoyo, Renishaw, Zeiss and more.
