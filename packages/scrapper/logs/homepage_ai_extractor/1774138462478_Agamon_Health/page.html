Agamon Health Coordinate Platform

Fully Automated, End-to-End Closure of Care Gaps

Our platform leverages AI to provide a comprehensive, automated workflow solution. Identify patients in your EHR that require follow-up across service lines (radiology, cardiology, pulmonology, oncology, urology).

Learn More

The Coordinate platform automatically:

Triages patients according to site-specific clinical criteria.

Tracks patient follow-up status and benchmarks it against clinical guidelines.

Communicates patient recommendations to referring providers and patients.

Monitors the status of and facilitates follow-up order creation.
