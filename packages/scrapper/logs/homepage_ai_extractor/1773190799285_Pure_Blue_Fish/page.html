Pure Blue Fish

The world’s oceans are in crisis. Soaring demand for seafood has led to overfishing and a fragile global supply chain dependent on imports.

Pure Blue Fish was founded to solve this—to save the ocean & feed the world.

We developed a revolutionary, land-based aquaculture system that is 100% independent of the sea. Our proprietary Zero Water Discharge (ZWD) technology is a breakthrough that allows us to raise clean, healthy, and sustainable marine fish anywhere, delivering an ultra-fresh, local product that is free of microplastics, heavy metals, and antibiotics in hours, instead of days.
About Us

Innovation

Our innovation is simple: total independence from the ocean. While 95% of marine fish are still wild-caught , our Zero Water Discharge (ZWD) system allows us to build farms on land, anywhere.

This land-based breakthrough perfectly emulates the ocean’s natural biological processes to recycle 100% of its water and nutrients. We create a clean, controlled environment that produces zero pollution and gives our oceans time to regenerate. It’s the key to a new, sustainable food system—one that provides clean, ultra-fresh, sushi-grade, local marine-fish without depleting our seas.

Our Products

We produce a new class of seafood: clean, healthy marine fish free of microplastics, heavy metals, and antibiotics. Grown in our controlled, ocean-independent farms, our fish are harvested on-demand and delivered ultra-fresh to local chefs, restaurants, and markets in less than 12 hours.
We offer a variety of sushi-grade species, all with full traceability and year-round availability.

Sustainability

Our Mission – Saving the Ocean & Feeding the World – is powered by Zero Water Discharge (ZWD) innovation. By completely disconnecting the the sea and eliminating pollution, our land-based farms protect marine ecosystems while providing clean, sustainable protein. Discover our full environmental impact.
