Introducing BioCir® - A game changer in the durable consumer goods industry.

BioCir® is a material solution that can be processed seamlessly like conventional plastics, taken back to a compost facility at the end of use, and broken down through biodegradation into renewable biomass, leaving no waste or negative impact on the environment. BioCir® is designed to replace traditional fossil-based plastics which have no environmentally safe end-of-life solution.
