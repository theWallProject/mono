asked questions

How fast can we launch?

Most migrations ship in 4–6 weeks; full-site builds in 6–8.

Do you work with our existing design?

Yes. We'll match your brand exactly or collaborate with your designer's handoff.

How do you measure success?

We set KPIs (demo bookings, trials, speed, SEO) and track them via GA4/PostHog.

Who owns the code and components?

You do—upon final payment. Reusable components are documented for your team.

Support after launch?

That's what retainers are for: fixes, experiments, and new pages/components.
