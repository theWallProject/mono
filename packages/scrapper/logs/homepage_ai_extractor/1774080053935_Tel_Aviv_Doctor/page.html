English Speaking Doctors

Our experienced native English speaking doctors provide quality medical care at our Tel Aviv clinic, or place of your choosing (please contact us to check availability). We pride ourselves in catering to the needs of tourists, foreign workers and Israeli’s alike.

Easy Insurance Handling

We work through direct billing with many major travel insurances. Alternatively, we accept self-payment via credit card or cash, and provide an itemised bill for easy reimbursement. We are happy to give you all information needed and assist with the process.

High Quality Care

Tel Aviv Doctor is the preferred medical provider of embassies, insurance companies, hotels and tour operators. Our clinic is recommended by Lonely Planet – and most importantly by our patients with consistent high ratings and reviews.
