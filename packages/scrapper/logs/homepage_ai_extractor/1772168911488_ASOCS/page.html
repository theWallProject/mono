About Asocs

ASOCS connects the dots between physical objects in the industrial universe and digital twins to assist immediate operational actions with AI-driven decision-making in a split second, millions of times a day.

ASOCS delivers on this vision by employing both industrial-grade connectivity with CYRUS® and its Generative Physical AI technology for precise positioning Hermes©
