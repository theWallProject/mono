Scale Your App with STARKs

Our scaling solutions use STARK-based validity proofs to ensure an Ethereum-secure, fast and seamless user experience. They support a range of data availability modes. Learn More

Starknet

A Permissionless Decentralized Validity-Rollup (often referred to as ZK-Rollup).
Any developer can deploy any dApp, using smart contracts, and achieve unlimited scale.

StarkEx

A Standalone Validity-Rollup SaaS
dApps can build on the StarkEx service. StarkEx supports a range of use cases out of the box: NFT minting & trading, derivative trading, AMM, spot trading, payments, and DeFi Pooling.

Cairo

Cairo is a language for creating STARK-provable programs for general computation. Cairo powers Starknet and StarkEx, scaling applications on Mainnet. Cairo 1.0 is the upgraded Rust-inspired version of Cairo.
