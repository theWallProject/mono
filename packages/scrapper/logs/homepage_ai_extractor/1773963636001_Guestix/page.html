Frequently asked questions

What is GUESTIX?

Guestix is the new solution for B&B/Short Term Rentals TVs.
Hotel TV is a tailored solution created for hotels.The system works by having multiple TVs within the same building. The centralized system controls all units. Thus, being an expensive system, it is more suitable for hotels rather than B&Bs.
B&Bs are dislocated.
In order to evolve to a better and tailored system, we created a portable stick that simply overcomes the barriers within the B&B industry. The Guestix Stick (Guestick)  connects the TV to channels, applications, welcome dashboard and much more. It’s plug&play so you don’t need any tech skills to run it.

How does it works?

Super Easy! Connect the Guestick Device to the TV through the HDMI, plug the USB power behind the TV and connect the device to the WiFi, that’s it! Then download “Guestix Host App” from the App Store or Google Play, where from there you can manage your Guestick Devices.

What is the added value?

Imagine the following situation: your guest enters the apartment for the first time and finds the TV ON with his name on it and the welcome, you are already halfway to a positive review.  The first impression is very important and is our business card.  Today, in order to differentiate yourself in a market that is increasingly expanding, you need to know how to innovate and be able to amaze your guests with the famous “WOW effects” and this situation is certainly one of these effects, obviously not according to us but based on  feedback received from people who have already installed Guestix in their apartment.

Where can I download the app?

The APP is available on both Apple Store and Google Play! Just search for “Guestix”

What can I do with the app?

With Guestix Host APP you can manage all of your guest’s needs. You can check the ratings in real time, welcome them before their arrivals, note Wi-Fi details as well as host’s contacts & relevant information and personalize Guestix TV with your logo. With the New update you can also send Messages to your guests!

Are there minimum requirements?

Nothing special, just a WiFi connection and a HDMI input on the TV ( HDMI is a standard since 2002 so probably your TV has it)

How can I subscribe?

Easy! If you have less than 20 TVs, just choose if you want to pay monthly or yearly, select the amount of devices you need and pay with your credit card.
If you have 20 TVs or more, contact us and we will be happy to offer you a special price and a tailored solution!

What does the subscription include?

The subscription include 3 products: Guestick device( with air mouse remote), Guestix TV app, Guestix Host app; and of course lifetime Support!

I manage multiple properties, do you offer special prices?

Sure! Do not hesitate to contact us in order to receive a tailored solution and a Special offer.

Is there a minimum order quantity of devices?

No! You can start with one and we will grow together!
