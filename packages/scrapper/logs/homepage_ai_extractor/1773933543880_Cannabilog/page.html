Planning and Regulation

When shipping cannabis, it is crucial to adhere regulations and required permits. Select safe routes, based on risks analyses, timeline and cost.Prevent illegal transshipments for smooth delivery.

Quality

Medical cannabis must meet GAP, GDP, and GMP standards with proper lab tests. Use validated packaging and correct labeling. Ensure temperature control per GDP standards during shipping.

Execution

Coordinate with cargo agents, airport authorities, and government agencies. Secure all necessary licenses and documents. Meet GDP requirements and ensure shipment security with adequate insurance. Confirm clearance with authorities before shipping.

Security

Shipping cannabis must adhere to GSP standards and regulations (international, regional, or domestic). Cannabilog makes sure it happens by using secured transportation, warehousing, and distribution (vehicles and facilities.)

Compliance

With ever-evolving regulations in the medical cannabis industry, it is crucial for businesses to stay up-to-date and fully compliant. Cannabiog equips you with knowledge and tools to navigate this dynamic regulatory landscape.
