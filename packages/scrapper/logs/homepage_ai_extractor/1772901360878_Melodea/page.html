Frequently asked questions

  • Can Melodea's Eco-Friendly Packaging Be as Effective as Traditional Plastic?

    Absolutely! Melodea’s eco-friendly packaging provides the same high level of protection as traditional plastic packaging. Our innovative barrier coatings create recyclable packaging solutions that offer superior protection against oxygen, moisture, and oils. This sustainable packaging keeps products fresh while being cost-effective and seamlessly integrating into plastic films or paper packaging, enabling manufacturers to transition to 100% recyclable packaging with ease.

  • What Are the Benefits of Using Melodea's Packaging Solutions Over Traditional Plastic?

    Melodea’s paper packaging is a greener alternative to plastic, offering a recyclable, water-based, and repulpable solution. Unlike plastic, it supports a circular economy and helps meet eco-friendly packaging goals. Our paper solutions provide a superior water vapor, grease, and aroma barrier (WVTR), ensuring optimal protection while being PTS and CEPI certified for sustainability. Transitioning from plastic to sustainable paper packaging has never been easier!

  • How Can Advanced Barrier Technologies Extend Shelf Life While Supporting Eco-Friendly Packaging?

    Melodea’s advanced barrier technologies protect against oxygen, moisture, and contaminants, effectively extending product shelf life. Our eco-friendly coatings, including water-based oxygen barriers (OTR) and water vapor, grease, and aroma barriers (WVTR), provide high-performance protection comparable to EVOH and metalized alternatives—but in a 100% recyclable and environmentally friendly format. This ensures your products stay fresh while significantly reducing environmental impact.
