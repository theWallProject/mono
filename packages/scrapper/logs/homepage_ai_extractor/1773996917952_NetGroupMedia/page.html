About Us

Founded in 2007 by an international team of creative, passionate and marketing-savvy women, Net Group Media provides high performance, global digital marketing solutions to Gaming, Fintech and E-commerce clients.

Fluent in English, German, Spanish, Italian and French, our unique combination of international insight and business knowledge empowers our customers with a distinct advantage for worldwide business growth.

With extensive, strategic connections, Net Group Media ensures the perfect match between advertisers and publishers looking to grow their businesses. From Online Media buying to Affiliate Management and Mobile Advertising, our approach is always innovative, targeted and effective.
