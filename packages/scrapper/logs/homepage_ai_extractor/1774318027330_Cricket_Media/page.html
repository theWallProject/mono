Cricket Media creates trusted, age-appropriate experiences for kids and families.

For over 50 years, we’ve sparked curiosity through award-winning magazines. Today, with Sensical, we unite print, streaming, audio, and interactive play into one safe, purpose-driven ecosystem.

Grounded in child development science, our content grows with kids from babies to teens, building creativity, confidence, and critical thinking.

We serve families and educators, and partner with brands and platforms to scale safe, meaningful engagement.

Children's Magazines

Inspiring Curiosity Through Reading.

For over 50 years, Cricket Media’s award-winning magazines have sparked kids’ curiosity and imagination, encouraging them to explore, discover, and dream big. 

Every issue builds confidence, creativity, and a lifelong love of learning through stories, art, and ideas that make the world feel full of wonder.

Parents and educators trust our magazines to deliver screen-free learning that inspires imagination and nurtures lifelong curiosity.

Sensical

Free Streaming for Curious Kids.

Sensical is the completely free, kid-safe streaming platform made just for ages 2–12. Every show is handpicked by experts—not algorithms—to spark curiosity and inspire learning. With 50+ channels built around kids’ favorite topics, Sensical makes screen time smart, safe, and fun. Stream smarter today!

Reading Stars App

Building Reading Confidence Through Interactive Stories.

The Reading Stars app helps kids build reading fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension—one story at a time. 

The app features thousands of Cricket’s Lexile®-matched children’s books, interactive quizzes, and daily rewards that turn reading into a joyful habit. From quick bedtime reads to epic story adventures, Reading Stars makes reading fun, personalized, and measurable for every child!
