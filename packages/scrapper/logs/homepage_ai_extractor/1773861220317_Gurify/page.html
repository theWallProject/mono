About Us

Who We Are

The software development arm of a company that knows talent

Software Geniuses is a subsidiary of Geniuses Recruiting, an agency that helps companies find outstanding software developers, QA engineers, and tech professionals in Georgia and Eastern Europe.

Founded by a former R&D Director who established and managed development centers for international communication companies across Georgia and Eastern Europe, we bring deep industry insight to every engagement. We don't just write code — we understand how high-performing tech teams are built from the ground up.

Our recruiting team brings 5+ years of tech industry experience and a proven track record with international companies and startups. This unique combination of recruiting expertise and engineering capability means we deliver not only great software, but the right people to maintain and grow it.
