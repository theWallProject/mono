Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use my FSA or HSA funds to purchase the Annabella Pump?

You can pay for your breast pump and certain accessories with money from an FSA or HSA. Simply pay with your FSA/HSA card at checkout, or pay with another credit card and submit your receipt to your plan's administrator for reimbursement.

To use your HSA or FSA debit card, add products to your cart as usual. At checkout, select “Flex | Pay with HSA/FSA” as your payment option, enter your HSA or FSA debit card, and complete your checkout as usual. If you don’t see “Flex | Pay with HSA/FSA,” you may be in Shop Pay. Select “checkout as guest” to view more payment options.

Can I buy the Annabella Breast Pump through my health insurance?

The Annabella double breast pump can be purchased on Babylist Health through insurance. See if you qualify for coverage through your health insurance provider. Check your eligibility.

What’s Annabella's return policy?

Annabella products are covered by a 1-year warranty for the massage and pumping units, and 45 days for washable parts. The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, general wear and tear, or use by multiple users. You can check out our full delivery and return policy here.

Is Annabella really more efficient than the other breast pumps?

Yep — it's a fact. We've conducted a controlled study that demonstrates that with the Annabella Breast Pump, there is an average increase of 50% in milk expression, with increases ranging from 30 to 60%.*

*Based on a clinical study comparing the Annabella Breast Pump to a leading brand. Results may vary and should not be generalized to all breast pumps.

What’s Annabella's warranty policy?

We have a one-year warranty for the Annabella single electric breast pump (45 days for the silicone and plastic washable parts).

Does the Annabella breast pump meet U.S. safety and health standards?

Of course! Annabella is an FDA-approved, 5(10)k class II medical device.
