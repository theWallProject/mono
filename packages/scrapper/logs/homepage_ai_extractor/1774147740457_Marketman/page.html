Frequently Asked Questions

Say goodbye to time-consuming inventory management tasks.

1. Do I Need To Buy Any New Hardware To Use MarketMan?

+

Nope! MarketMan works on your existing Windows and Mac devices as well as Android and Apple mobile devices. Download our mobile apps on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

2. What is available For Customer Support?

+

Marketman is committed to providing you with the best possible experience. Based on your subscription, we offer a range of options to help you succeed. Our support team is available to assist you with any questions or issues you may have. Our customer success team is dedicated to ensuring that you get the most out of our platform. We also offer training services to help you and your team get up to speed quickly. And if you need more advanced assistance, our professional services team is here to help.

At Marketman, we believe that your success is our success. We are committed to providing you with the tools and resources you need to achieve your goals. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don’t hesitate to reach out to us.

3. Can I Use MarketMan With Multiple Locations?

+

Yes, you can. MarketMan supports a wide range of foodservice business models, including restaurants, bars, and similar businesses ranging from one location up to hundreds. We have a unique account feature called "HQ" to manage all of your locations in one place centrally.

4.  What POS Systems Do You Integrate With?

+

MarketMan’s all-in-one restaurant management platform offers integrations with the most widely used, best-in-class POS platforms. Check out our POS partners here. Learn the benefits of integrating your POS with MarketMan here.

5. What Distributors Do You Integrate With?

+

Integrating with your distributor will allow your rep to automatically update prices, product codes, and add new items into MarketMan. Orders placed through MarketMan will go directly to your distributor, and all invoices/delivery notes will automatically flow into MarketMan. Current distributors include Performance Foodservice, Sysco, Fresh Direct, Gordon Food Service, LaBatt Food Service, US. Foods, The Bread Factory, and Brown Foodservice.
