Our Ventures:

Sparkling Up Business Opportunities

Innovative, Dynamic, Voltaic straight Out of the box

Scouring the world, we have committed to finding new and alternative companies that are working in tandem to spread the light of diamonds in new and intriguing ways. At InLight we take pride in sharing this different companies, their take on diamonds light, presence and vision.

Diamond Gifts Are Forever
This venture aims to bring diamonds into the world of the gift market. Unfortunately, today, diamonds are not purchased as a product that is for everyone. With it’s over publicized elite status, most think that the diamond is something that is only for special occasions. It’s likely that none of us have ever given anyone a diamond as a holiday, housewarming or birthday gift because we have been programed not to do so. Thus, diamonds have “imprisoned” in the world of jewelry, where purchases take place almost exclusively in the context of romantic relationships. With this venture, we strive to create a change in perception by offering innovative packaging for diamonds that will greatly enhance their gift-appeal among larger audiences and boost sales accordingly.
OrYahalom.com
A diamond venture targeted to people of faith that draws on the connection between the diamond’s light and the ethereal light. We believe that the diamond’s energy connects us to the all-seeing eye of God, regardless of one’s religion. Therefore, diamonds deserve a special place in the world of religiously tailored products and to have an immense ability to radiate a renewed interest among believers of all religions.
Masaor.com
Combining diamonds with the latest products and events in the world of accessories and fashion, this venture emphasizes diamonds, lifestyle, and everything in between. Bracelets, purses, organizers, pens, eyewear, and more- anywhere you look, you’ll see fashion, fashion and more fashion that incorporates a unique statement and of course, an innovative spot for placing diamonds.
Human Diamonds
More than any other venture, Human Diamonds expresses the true spirit of Inlight and our belief in human excellence. Human Diamonds is a groundbreaking venture that focuses on technology in advanced laboratory grown diamonds. This venture draws on the understanding that the world of diamonds stands at a historic turning point where the demand for lab grown diamonds is increasung significantly. Human Diamonds has begun to develop innovative products backed by a world-first B2B2C trading arena and ecosystem.
Blessed In Israel
