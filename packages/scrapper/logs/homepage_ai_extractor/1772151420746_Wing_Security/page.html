One Platform. Unified AI and SaaS Security. Total Enterprise Protection.

Wing provides a single platform that unifies AI and SaaS security by discovering all tools, agents, and integrations, managing risk and access controls, enforcing policies, and detecting threats, delivering complete visibility, control, and protection across the entire enterprise.

  • Full AI Discovery
  • An Actionable Inventory
  • AI Risk Management
  • AI Observability
  • AI Supply Chain Attacks

Wing automatically discovers every AI tool, agent, and embedded feature, analyzing integrations, identities, and API connections, to reveal all AI usage across your environment without any agents, proxies, or manual input.

Learn More

Wing’s Actionable AI Inventory gives you a complete, enriched view of every AI tool, agent, and integration, revealing usage, data access, and risk so you can confidently approve, restrict, or block AI activity in real time.

Learn More

Wing’s AI Risk Management continuously analyzes every AI tool, agent, and integration to detect hygiene issues, misconfigurations, excessive permissions, abandoned agents, and unauthorized usage, providing real-time insights that help prevent incidents before they escalate.

Wing’s AI observability provides continuous insight into how AI tools and agents behave by monitoring and analyzing their activities. It detects risks and emerging threats in real time using Wing’s dedicated threat intelligence feed.
 

By automatically discovering AI tools, agents, and embedded features, mapping their connections and permissions, detecting risky integrations or compromised identities, and using continuous monitoring and threat intelligence, Wing helps enterprises identify and respond to Supply Chain threats before they spread.
