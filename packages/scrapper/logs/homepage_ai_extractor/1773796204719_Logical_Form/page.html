Security, Privacy and Tranparency.

AUTHENTICATION Without IDENTIFICATION

We tag items, and use the abilities of Block Chain to track, authenticate and prove the sources.

Block Chain uses strong cryptography and decentralized ledger to track transactions of digital tokens. This enables combination of important security elements:

  • Mitigate "Double Spending".
  • Enable authentication without identification.
  • Ability to verify conditions on cryptographic hashes and digital signatures.
  • Decentralized ledger that easily combines trusted and non-trusted parties.

We focus on utilizing the Block Chain algorithm and protocol between trusted parties, without the complications required when trust is eliminated. We use the Block Chain open source protocol and code base as much as possible.
