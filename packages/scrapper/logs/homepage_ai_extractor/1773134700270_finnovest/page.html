Bridging the Gap Between Investment Professionals and Retail Investors
Finnovest is at the forefront of a transformative shift in retail investing, addressing the most significant wealth transfer in modern history.
Powered by proprietary technology, Finnovest enables investment professionals to scale personalized investment advice across the mass-affluent retail investment market and provide a next-gen non-discretionary advisory experience to the next generation of investors.
Next-gen investors want to act independently - yet, still seek trusted, professional and personal investment advice. Until now, scaling non-discretionary advisory services wasn’t feasible. Finnovest is first to make it possible to deliver the same hyper-personalized, traditional ‘one-to-one’ advice ,now at scale. Through a true ‘one-to-many’ platform, Finnovest enables investment professionals to create actionable and tailored recommendations simultaneously for each client’s unique portfolio, holdings and risk appetite. Investors receive proactive, real-time advice that can be authorized and easily executed with a single tap.
Finnovest sets a new standard in retail investment — enabling professionals to preserve the depth of personalized advice while achieving the scale and seamless experience today’s market demands.
