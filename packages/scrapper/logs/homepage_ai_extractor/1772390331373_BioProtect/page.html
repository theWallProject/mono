About

Lift your patients to the apex of care with the first non-hydrogel spacer for modern prostate cancer radiation treatments.

  • Creates Uniform Space

    The BioProtect Balloon Spacer has been shown to create consistent and symmetric whole-gland spacing with minimal variability along the midline.1,2

  • Positions Predictably. Fills Adaptively.

    With a preshaped balloon visible on ultrasound, MRI, and CT,3 BioProtect’s saline-filling technology enables controllable space creation.

  • Protects Quality of Life

    BioProtect demonstrated reduced long-term rectal and urinary toxicity versus control in preliminary data.4* Patients reported improved quality of life with significant benefits across bowel, urinary, and sexual domains over 48 months.4*

The products discussed herein may have different product labelling in different countries. The BioProtect Balloon Implant™ System is intended to temporarily position the anterior rectal wall away from the prostate during radiotherapy for prostate cancer. In creating this space, the BioProtect Balloon Implant™ System intends to reduce the radiation dose delivered to the anterior rectum. The BioProtect Balloon Implant™ System is a balloon made of a biodegradable material that maintains that space for the entire course of prostate radiotherapy treatment and is completely absorbed by the patient’s body over time.
