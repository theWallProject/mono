Exposures are on the rise

Today, employees are facing more pressure than ever to meet financial targets in an environment of surging complexity, creating more exposures for your business.

More human errors

Honest mistakes happen all the time. And more data means more of them.For example, creating multiple records for the same entity, which inevitably lead to costly duplicate payments.

More non-compliance

Some employees knowingly or unknowingly bypass protocols to work faster. And with workloads on rise, it’s only getting worse, costing firms nearly 3X as much as being in compliance.

More fraud

Whether committed by employees or state-sponsored cyber criminals, fraud is growing fast. It’s already responsible for the staggering loss of 5% of annual revenueof businesses worldwide.
