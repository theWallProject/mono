Key benefits

We are Building and Operating the best Flex-Grid Modular Data Centers Globally

Sustainability

Flex-Grid power from Grid through Natural Gas to HYdrogen, no Diesel generators, minimal to zero emission, zero water use, PUE under 1.1

Modularity

The ECL Block design was created to maximize Modularity. This fundamental 1 to 2 MW building block allows ECL to deliver a consistent experience in all locations. This applies to any type of location and use cases brown and greenfield properties.

Best Customer TCO

The ECL novel solution improves the customer’s overall TCO by reducing the cost to build and opeatare and improving by that the power and MRR cost.

Built to Order

ECL builds a disaggregated site according to the customer’s specification one MW at a time. The customer defines any rack criteria, set power requirement per individual rack, the availability and redundancy modes, and the data center internal temperature.

Time to Delivery

With the Flex-Grid ECL offers industry-leading time to delivery from Purchase Order to IT-ready site including on-demand network and server integration services.

Availability and Efficiency

ECL provides an Exceedingly efficient cooling system with up to 150KW per rack, and superior space utilization (kW per sqft). Additionally, On site power generation with integrated BESS and, minimal transmission loss, eliminates the need for a UPS system
