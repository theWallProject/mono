Comprehensive Fraud Prevention Solutions

Secure High-Risk Transactions

High-value transactions are at risk from deepfake attacks, such as synthetic voices and facial reenactments, which can grant unauthorized access. Dtect’s Motion ID Biometrics verifies users based on their unique movement patterns, ensuring secure authentication and preventing fraud.

Fraud-Proof Document Verification

Traditional verification methods can miss AI-driven document forgeries, allowing fraudulent documents to slip through. Dtect’s High-Frequency Neural Networks detect subtle manipulations in documents, ensuring that only genuine files are processed, providing stronger protection against document-based fraud.

Authentic Video Conferencing

Video conferencing is vulnerable to deepfake attacks like face swaps and voice alterations, which compromise communication integrity. Dtect’s High-Frequency Neural Networks detect these manipulations in real time, ensuring confidential meetings remain secure and trusted.

Simplified Password Resets

Password reset processes are vulnerable to deepfake manipulations, like voice cloning and eSIM takeovers, compromising user security. Dtect’s Motion ID Biometrics reauthenticates users with their behavioral data, offering an advanced layer of protection alongside existing methods like MFA.

KYC and ID Verification

Current KYC processes are vulnerable to advanced deepfake techniques, such as face swaps and reenactments, which allow fraudulent identities to pass as legitimate. Dtect’s AI seamlessly integrates with your systems, detecting these manipulations and ensuring only genuine identities are onboarded.
