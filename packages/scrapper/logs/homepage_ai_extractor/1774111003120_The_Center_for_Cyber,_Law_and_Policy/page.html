Welcome to The Center for Cyber Law & Policy

The Center for Cyber, Law and Policy (CCLP) at the University of Haifa, in partnership with the Israeli National Cyber Directorate, focuses on advancing research to inform public policy on cyber and biometric challenges. By integrating law, technology, and social sciences, the CCLP develops interdisciplinary tools for advancing cyber technology and cyber policymaking. National and individual security, innovation, democracy, and civil rights in the digital ecosystem, as well as the roles of biometric technology and artificial intelligence, are critical areas of focus.
