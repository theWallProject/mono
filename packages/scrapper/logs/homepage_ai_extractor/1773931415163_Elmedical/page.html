          BWT (UniThermia) for Bladder cancer: 
          Bladder Wall Thermochemotherapy (BWT) treats non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) by combining uniform  bladder wall hyperthermia with chemotherapy instillation.

          DuoThermia for Prostatitis: 
          Treats chronic prostatitis/chronic pelvic pain syndrome (CP/CPPS) by simultaneous hyperthermia to the prostate and bladder.

           
           Combining heat and drugs induces a synergy creating a healing phenomena:

          a) Heat selectively damages the cancer tissue (neoplastic tissue)

          b) Heat increases tissue drug uptake by 3 mechanisms:

          • Heat increases blood flow (more drug available to the tissue)
          • Heat increases cell membrane permeability to the drug
          • Heat accelerates drug metabolism (with every one centigrade over 37 centigrade metabolism increased about 50%)
