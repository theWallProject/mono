Our Products

At Invocap we are passionate about helping you achieve your financial goals. We partner with experienced property investors to finance the growth of their portfolios. Whether you are purchasing a property or refurbishing one already in your portfolio, we have a solution for you.

All of our products are secured with a 1st charge, lending up to 75% of the value of the property. With a focus on residential, mixed use, and conversions of commercial property to residential property. Our facilities range from £100k to £5m.

About Us

Since 2014 Invocap has provided over £200m in funding to businesses across the UK. Since inception, we have been blending technology with a human touch, to deliver an exceptional service that makes us stand out. In 2020 our efforts were acknowledged by the British Business Bank and Invocap was trusted to become an accredited CBILS (Coronavirus Business Interruption Loan Scheme) lender. This endorsement demonstrated our dedication to providing unwavering support to UK businesses. Our aim is to become your funding partner, taking the hassle out of the process of securing funds and letting you focus on running your business. Whether you already own a property or are looking to purchase one, apply now to get connected to one of our account managers.
