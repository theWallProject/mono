OUR SOLUTION

Multi-Layered Wildfire defense

Proactive Peripheral Firebreak

Smart capsules with eco-friendly fire-retardant are launched and activated in precise locations to create a peripheral barrier around your property to stop encroaching wildfire

Preventive Asset Protection

Your most valuable assets are coated in a eco-friendly protective layer as the smart capsules are activated precisely where they’re needed - making the assets spot fire resilient

Reactive Real-time fire suppression

AI sensors detect ember-driven spot fires and automatically extinguish them within seconds, stopping fires before they can establish and spread

End to End Services

Ongoing expert support, system maintenance, and year-round readiness, tailored to the specific property and always primed for protection

OUR TECHNOLOGY

How it works

At the heart of FireDome is an AI-powered mechanical launcher that detects fire and calculates the optimal response. Within seconds, it releases smart capsules, providing rapid, reliable fire protection even when access is restricted or evacuation is underway

CORE COMPONENTS

FireDome

Launcher

An AI-guided electromechanical mechanism that rotates and adjusts to rapidly release retardant or water capsules with pinpoint accuracy

Smart

Capsules

Eco-friendly, safe, and highly effective capsules designed to activate and disperse water or fire retardant within seconds, precisely where needed

Sensors

Multiple type of sensors, such as thermal and visual, continuously scan to detect fire, movement, and changing conditions in real time

AI Decision

Engine

Proprietary algorithms assess incoming data, prioritize threats, calculate optimal capsule trajectories, and initiate launches with precision

Connectivity

& Control

Remote monitoring, real-time alerts, safety features, and a mobile app give you full visibility and coordination with external fire services

Autonomy

& Safety

Autonomous operations with multiple safety layers before and during each launch, external connectivity and environmentally safe materials
