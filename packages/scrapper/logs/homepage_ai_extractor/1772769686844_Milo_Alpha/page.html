DIGITIZATION

Milo Alpha aims to modernize traditional operations in hotels by digitizing them with the goal of automation. Currently, housekeeping in most hotels operates with no, or at best, little use of technology.

We see an opportunity to optimize performance, increase efficiency, and to enhance the guest experience.

Housekeeping departments are seen within the industry as being a basic and traditional sector. We see an opportunity to transform housekeeping, bringing it into the next century through a process of digitization and automation, thus reducing costs, and increasing ROI. Our solution will greatly improve housekeeping performance and enhance the guests’ satisfaction.

Milo Alpha supports a “Digital Trolley” suit; an elegant IOT based solution utilizing an AI engine combined with a sensor array designed to ensure optimal performance by housekeeping staff while upholding the highest hotel standards. All of this technology is integrated into the traditional housekeeping trolley with incorporated software. This solution is projected to have a significant positive impact on hotel operators’ bottom line, whilst significantly improving and optimizing the guests’ customer experience.

INTEGRATION

Milo Alpha is developing an in-house software solution to integrate critical high value cross-departmental activities within the hospitality industry.‎

We provide a solution that integrates the different IT systems in your organization, such:

  • Reservations
  • Check-in / checkout
  • Procurement
  • Housekeeping
  • Food and beverage
  • Concierge
Data fusion from those systems supports decision making, by providing real-time actionable insights, focused for the various use-cases. There is an urgent need for smooth integration of management systems within the hotel industry.
This poses a major challenge across different property types and geographies. Milo Alpha is busy developing a suite of software solutions that will disrupt the industry and revolutionize the process of critical high value decision making. Our software solutions are largely compatible with existing IT products on the market and can easily be integrated, providing a unified and flexible solution that enhances the ongoing operational activity of hotel employees.
