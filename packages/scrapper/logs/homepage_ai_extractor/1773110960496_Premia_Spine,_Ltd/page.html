    Why Consider TOPS™

    There are many advantages to the TOPS™ System. Patients can now avoid fusion surgery altogether. With the TOPS™ System, you can feel better, reduce the risks associated with surgical procedures like fusion, and get back to your daily routine faster!
    With the TOPS™ Lumbar Motion Device, you can achieve normal movement in rotation, bending, extension, and flexion.
    On top of that, the procedure can stabilize your spine to reestablish a more controlled range of movement.
    These two benefits go hand-in-hand.
    So now spinal stenosis and spondylolisthesis patients can get spine stabilization without rigid fixation thanks to the TOPS™ System. This means that patients can confidently go back to normal activities.
    Clinical FDA evidence shows that TOPS alleviates persistent low back and leg pain better than fusion for those with moderate/severe spinal stenosis and spondylolisthesis.
