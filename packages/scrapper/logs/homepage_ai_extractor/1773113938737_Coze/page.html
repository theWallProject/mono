    What makes Coze different from all the other QA companies?

     #Accurate documentation for all products sampled:
    Many other companies present false inspection reports. They report a complete test, but the test was only performed correctly on a very limited number of products. Our system documents every item test in writing with a clear picture. This gives you a complete and accurate presentation of the quality examination findings.
    #Reliability and transparency:
    Some standard inspections use a "waiting period" that allows the results to be biased by an agent or supplier. Coze's inspection results are published in real time so that the customer can see updates and changes from minute to minute. This leaves no time for hustling.
     
    #Real-Time Results:
    While most of the test companies deliver the final report up to 3 days from the date of the inspection, Coze will provide the results of the inspection in real time and without unnecessary delays in the delivery of the goods.
     #Efficiency:
    The traditional test process involves recording the results of the test on a form, photographing items examined with a physical camera and matching the written data to the photographs. This process takes more time.
    Coze uses a much faster and more efficient process. Our inspectors check and photograph the product simultaneously, and feed the data directly to the summary report. Yes. It's that simple.
