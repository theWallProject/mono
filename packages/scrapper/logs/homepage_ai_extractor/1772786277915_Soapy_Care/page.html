Use Cases In Healthcare

Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU)

NICU units house newborns and infants who are particularly vulnerable to infections due to their underdeveloped immune systems. Implementing Soapy PRO in NICU units ensures that healthcare workers practice proper hand hygiene before interacting with these vulnerable patients. This reduces the risk of healthcare-associated infections (HAIs) and helps protect the health and well-being of newborns and infants.

Explore Other Use Cases

Soapy's systems have not only proved to be efficient in increasing adherence, but they have also been effective as a training tool for new healthcare providers, ensuring they learn and adhere to proper hand hygiene practices from the start of their careers. This has a long-term impact on the immunocompromised patients.
