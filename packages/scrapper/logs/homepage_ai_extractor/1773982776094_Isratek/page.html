ISRATEK is a leading manufacturer of complex Electro-Mechanical Assembly, wire harness assemblies, rugged electronic systems, RF Cables, RF Assembly, Circuit Card Assemblies,  Automatic Test Equipment (ATE) used in the testing of clients products and numerous other specially engineered products. We’ve manufactured products for military contracts directly and as subcontractors to a number of customers in the defense, aviation, communications and aerospace industries.
 
Our wide experience and flexible approach enables us to provide our customers with qualitative design within a short time frame, while maintaining low costs and committed to quality, optimal prices and customer satisfaction. To date, ISRATEK has successfully completed numerous projects with prominent customers in a variety of industries. View our products, customers and testimonials.
