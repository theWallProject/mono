AMBOYA

Amboya Investments is a privately held global technology company representing leading solution providers across  Telecommunication, Cyber Security, IT, Power, Homeland Security, and Solar Energy sectors.

With a proven track record spanning over seven years, Amboya has successfully delivered advanced equipment, software, and integrated solutions from top-tier international vendors to clients worldwide.

–
Our Global Reach

We operate across strategic markets in APAC, EMEA, Africa, and the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) region – and beyond. Our project portfolio includes successful deliveries in:

  • Asia-Pacific (APAC): Korea, Thailand, Indonesia, Singapore, Philippines, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands, Fiji, Vietnam, Myanmar
  • Gulf Countries (GCC): United Arab Emirates (UAE), Oman, Bahrain.
  • Eastern Europe: Various countries across the region
  • Africa: Morocco, Sudan, Ethiopia, Algeria, Mali, Ivory Coast, Cameroon, Somaliland, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Zambia, Kenya, Angola, Namibia, Botswana, Mozambique, Madagascar, South Africa

Our reach continues to expand, serving governments, enterprises, and service providers in emerging and frontier markets that demand scalable, reliable, and secure solutions.

Amboya: Your Partner in Advanced Technological Solutions

At Amboya, we offer a comprehensive suite of technological solutions across various sectors, including defense, security, enterprise, and telecommunications.
