Leaving the security of their day jobs, they took a leap of faith, determined to turn their vision into reality. When they approached us to create their Kickstarter campaign video, we knew their story was the key. We created a narrative that resonated with their audience – An origin story of two dreamers turned pioneers, defying the odds to build something extraordinary. The result? A $500K campaign in just one month, a captivated community, and a company that continues to lead its category today.

Tel Aviv Stock Exchange Opening Bell Ceremony Video

Establishing a meaningful relationship in a meaningful moment.

What started as a client requirement (and a challenge for us), using stock footage to tell a story, became this project’s greatest strength through thoughtful curation and editing. When Tel Aviv Stock Exchange asked us to create their Opening Bell Ceremony video, we knew it had to leave a lasting impression, as it is viewed weekly by IPO attendees. Our concept was “Time.” With TASE’s diverse companies, the cluttered nature of stock footage transformed into a richness, weaving industries into one rhythmic narrative. The result? A powerful message: No matter your industry or vertical, when your time to shine comes, TASE is the perfect partner for future growth.

SaaS Made Sexy

When a platform tells a tale - Asterra

How do you make a SaaS platform engaging without actors, props, or staged scenarios? You let the product do the talking. That’s exactly what we did for Asterra. Using only their platform’s interface, we built a clean, dynamic video that showcased their core features in action. Smooth motion design, sharp editing, and a pulse-pounding soundtrack turned static screens into a clear, compelling story. To give the video structure, we used VFX to craft a distinct beginning and end—framing the experience with context and focus. The result? A polished, professional video that helped their product stand out while clearly delivering its value to their audience.

From Boring to Awesome in 100 Seconds

Can a company that manufactures and distributes cables stand out as a creative force? That’s what we set out to discover with Ray-Q. Finding the Soul of the Digital Product

UXV - A relationship course you'll actually want to attend.

Featuring Tal Florentin as its frontman, UXV stands as the premier Israeli UX certification program. Together, we created a narrative that draws a humoristic parallel between personal relationships and interactions with digital products, highlighting how both rely on communication and meaningful connection. The video effectively explains the role of a UX designer while showcasing the program’s value. Years later, it remains a powerful tool for recruiting students and promoting the brand.
