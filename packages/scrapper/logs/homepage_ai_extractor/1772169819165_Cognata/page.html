Autonomy Built for the Real World

Mission-level autonomy for off-road and defense systems, from development to field operations.
CONTACT US

Two Products.
One Autonomous Advantage.

Cognata’s autonomy offering spans from simulation to field deployment. OneSim provides the simulation-first foundation used to train, test, and validate autonomous behaviors at scale. AVBox brings those validated capabilities into real vehicles, delivering mission-level autonomy in off-road and defense environments. Together, they form a continuous loop where simulation drives deployment, and field experience feeds back into system improvement.

CONTACT US

AVBox – All-in-one autonomous retrofit box

Cognata AV Box brings complete autonomous mobility in a single rugged unit, combining all required compute, sensing and autonomy logic into one ready to deploy system. It installs quickly on off road defense platforms and delivers reliable, simulation driven autonomy across harsh terrain and changing mission conditions.

Simulation-First Platform for Autonomous Systems

Dual-engine simulation platform for training, testing, and validating autonomous systems at scale.
By combining real-world data with high-fidelity synthetic environments, OneSim enables faster development cycles and higher confidence before deployment to the field.

Gen-AI Data Augmentation Platform

DriveMatrix uses supervised GenAI to expand real-world driving data into controlled variations across terrain, weather, lighting, and sensor conditions, enabling scalable and repeatable autonomy training.

Features

Mission-Level Autonomy Execution
AVBox enables vehicles to execute autonomous missions end to end, including navigation, task sequencing, and operational decision-making in complex and unstructured environments.                  
Real-Time Perception and Decision-Making

AVBox processes live sensor data to perceive the environment, assess conditions, and make autonomous decisions in real time, supporting safe and reliable operation in dynamic off-road scenarios.

               
Multi-Sensor Runtime Fusion
AVBox fuses data from multiple onboard sensors at runtime to create a robust and consistent understanding of the vehicle’s surroundings, improving performance across varying terrain and visibility conditions.                  
Field-Ready, Scalable Deployment
AVBox is designed for rapid integration and deployment on existing vehicle platforms, enabling scalable autonomy upgrades across fleets without extensive vehicle redesign.                  
GPS-Denied Operation
AVBox maintains autonomous operation in environments where GPS is degraded, denied, or unavailable, using onboard sensing and intelligence to continue missions reliably without dependence on external positioning signals.                  
Mapless Autonomy Architecture
AVBox operates without reliance on high-definition maps, enabling autonomous navigation and decision-making in unknown, changing, or unprepared environments where prior mapping is not available.
