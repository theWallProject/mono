Welcome to Tammuz Family

Tammuz Family is a global leader in surrogacy, fertility, and egg donation services. Founded by Doron Mamet-Meged in 2008, we have proudly helped build over 2,000 families across five continents and more than 32 nationalities.

With offices in over 15 countries, we offer accessible and affordable surrogacy and IVF programs in the United States, Mexico, Georgia, Armenia, and Colombia—tailored for same-sex couples, heterosexual couples, single men, and single women.

Tammuz Family is a full-service, centralized surrogacy agency providing a responsible, well-managed journey for intended parents. We handle every step with expertise and care—from the initial consultation to your baby’s homecoming.

Our multilingual team of specialists understands the surrogacy process from both personal and professional perspectives, ensuring you feel guided, supported, and cared for every step of the way.

Join the Tammuz Family, and let us help you transform your dream of parenthood into reality.

Our donors

At Tammuz Family, we offer a diverse selection of egg donors to meet your unique needs. Each donor undergoes thorough medical and psychological screening, ensuring the highest standards of care. We provide comprehensive profiles for every donor, detailing their genetic profile, medical history, educational background, employment history, interests, hobbies, and family medical history. Additionally, each profile includes photographs for your consultation, helping you make an informed and confident choice. With Tammuz Family, you’ll find the perfect donor to help you build your family.
