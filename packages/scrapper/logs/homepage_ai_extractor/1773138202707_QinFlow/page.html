BLOOD & IV FLUID
WARMING SOLUTIONS

The Warrior and Warrior lite Modular Product Lines:
Gold Standard blood and IV fluid warmers for the entire continuum of care, delivering superb performance at an affordable per-use price. Our solutions are portable, simple to use, easy to maintain, and extremely safe (e.g., dry warming technology, aluminum free fluid path). All this is complemented by uncompromising levels of customer service to our end users and partners.

THE HUFFINGTON POST​

Keeping Trauma Patients Warm Is Critical!

Thermal management is a key component in the survivability rates of trauma patients. Our mission is to completely eliminate the challenges of warming blood and IV fluid from the entire continuum of emergency care so that emergency care professionals will be able to focus on what they do best- saving lives.
