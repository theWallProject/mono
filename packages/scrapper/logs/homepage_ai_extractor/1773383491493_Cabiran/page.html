Who We Are

Cabiran is the one-stop shop for innovative aluminum investment casting solutions. Since 1969, the company and its multidisciplinary team of experts has provided its customers with very high quality complex cast parts and finished components for use in diverse industries, including commercial aerospace. All Cabiran products meet strict industry and aerospace standards.

The Cabiran Advantages

From concept, design and advanced engineering to rapid prototyping, to casting and post-casting, to testing, Cabiran offers these services all under one roof. The company’s integrated workflow brings together design, casting, machining assembly, coatings, product testing and resources, resulting in —

  • proven quality and efficiency (Quality Assurance Requirements),
  • faster turnaround and
  • greater cost-effectiveness.
