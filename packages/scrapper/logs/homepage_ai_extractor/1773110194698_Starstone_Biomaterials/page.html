WHAT GOT US HERE CAN TAKE US NO FURTHER

As our population rapidly expands, the demand for building materials is soaring. Traditional Cement & Concrete, cannot support this growth sustainably.

Existing production processes have not significantly changed in the past 200 years. Concrete is the second most consumed material on Earth and is generated in a complex and polluting process using massive quarries and energy intensive kilns all over the planet.

HARNESSING LIFE FORMS THAT BUILD MOUNTAINS

We create a biogenic cement replacement using natural biomineralization and cementation processes, similar to those that made the sedimentary carbonate formations in our mountains.

Leveraging the power of microscopic life, we replace the old, unsustainable methods, and in doing so, unlock a circular economy that puts industrial byproducts to use.
