    Welcome to MORYAM

    For over 25 years, Moryam has been a leading food importer in Israel specializing in U.S. national brands and specialty foods from around the world. Moryam has been pioneering the way for many of the world’s global trends – from sports bars and functional foods to organic and gluten-free products. With a name that has become synonymous with quality, service and customer care, we welcome both new and veteran food suppliers. Accordingly, we work closely with each supplier to tailor a marketing strategy that best fits the company and its products.
