About our company

Since 1986

Over 35 years ago, VST delivered its first thin film, high vacuum deposition/coating system. Our engineering team offers custom- made, thin film deposition systems to meet our clients' exact application specifications and requirements including: nanolayer sputtering, EB gun, thermal, ion beam, plasma, dielectric, organic low temperature and more. VST produces equipment for deposition, etching, or testing in high and ultra-high vacuum environments that are proudly manufactured in Israel.
