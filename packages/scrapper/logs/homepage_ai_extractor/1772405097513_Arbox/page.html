Frequently asked questions

What is Arbox and what services does it offer?

Arbox is the leading business management platform that provides a comprehensive solution for small and medium-sized businesses.

‍

We offer features such as membership management, class scheduling, payment processing, CRM, and marketing automation tools. Whether you are a gym owner, personal trainer, yoga instructor, or team coach, Arbox enables you to focus on what matters most - your clients! We help businesses streamline their operations, increase efficiency, and grow their businesses.

‍

Our platform is cloud-based, which means users can access it from anywhere with an internet connection. Arbox is known for its user-friendly interface and ease of use, making it a popular choice for businesses of all sizes.

Does the platform have to be installed?

Arbox is a cloud-based platform and does not require installation. All you need is an internet connection. Creating and setting up your account is easy, quick, and convenient. Arbox can be used on a variety of devices: mobile, tablet, PC, and Mac computers.

Can existing data be transferred from another platform?

Definitely. We know the process of changing management platforms can be excused, that's why we’ve established an easy process to help you transfer all your existing data to Arbox.

Can I manage my staff with Arbox?

Yes. Arbox supports the option of managing a number of staff members with selected access and permissions according to the business’s needs.

Can I manage payments on the platform?

Yes. Arbox offers a variety of integrated payment options for easy and secure payment processing.

Can I manage and send invoices through the platform?

Definitely. You can produce digital invoices and receipts on the platform and send them to your client automatically.

Does Arbox have a check-in interface?

Yes, Arbox has a check-in system. With the check-in system, you and your staff can easily track and monitor attendance for group sessions and appointments.

Does Arbox have an Access Control system?

Yes, Arbox’s access control Software provides an easy-to-implement and convenient way for members to access your facility giving you added security and peace of mind. Our network-based reader is designed as a low-cost and reliable way to provide access control for your members.
