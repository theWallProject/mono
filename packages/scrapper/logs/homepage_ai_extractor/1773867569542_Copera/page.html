One Integrated Physical Security Platform

Verkada’s One Integrated Physical Security Platform brings every layer of your site’s protection into a single, cloud-native solution—no more juggling multiple interfaces or legacy consoles.

 

From high-resolution cameras and smart access control readers to environmental sensors and intrusion alarms, every device connects seamlessly to a unified browser-based interface that you can access anywhere, on any device.

AI-powered analytics automatically surface critical events—whether it’s unusual motion detection, tailgating at secure doors, or sensor alerts—so you can respond instantly and with full context.

And because it’s built on a hardened, zero-trust architecture in AWS GovCloud, you get enterprise-grade cybersecurity, automatic firmware updates, and unlimited scaling without the need for on-prem servers.

With Verkada, you don’t just manage security—you harness visibility, insights, and control across your entire organization, all from one platform.
