Happy partners create strong partnerships

Peachtree Corners, GA, USA

Peachtree Corners, GA, USA

Peachtree Corners exemplifies professional excellence in smart city implementation, particularly in sophisticated traffic management systems. Their proactive approach in using real-time data optimizes traffic flow and enhances urban mobility, establishing them as a model for innovative traffic solutions in the U.S.

Netivei Israel (National Transport Infrastructure Company)

Netivei Israel (National Transport Infrastructure Company)

Netivei Israel manages Israel's national road network overseeing over 8,000 kilometers of roadways and more than 700 signalized intersections. It utilizes advanced technology to optimize traffic flow and enhance safety, playing a pivotal role in national transportation projects.

Tel Aviv Municipality

Tel Aviv Municipality

Tel Aviv Municipality is at the forefront of urban transportation planning, emphasizing the prioritization of public transit and pedestrian traffic to enhance city mobility and reduce congestion. Their commitment to sustainable urban development showcases their dedication to creating a more accessible and environmentally friendly cityscape.

Technion Transportation Research Institute

Technion Transportation Research Institute

The Technion Transportation Research Institute is a leading research facility in Israel, dedicated to advancing transportation science and engineering. The institute collaborates on projects that focus on improving road safety, optimizing traffic flow, and enhancing public transportation systems, reflecting a commitment to developing innovative solutions for modern transportation challenges.

European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT)

European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT)

The European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT) enhances Europe’s ability to innovate by fostering integration among leading companies, universities, and research institutes. Focused on advancing sustainable transport solutions, EIT plays a crucial role in shaping smart city and transportation policies across the continent.
