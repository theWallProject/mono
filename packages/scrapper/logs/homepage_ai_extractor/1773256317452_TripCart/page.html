More features to
power your tour business

Offline bookings

Add phone, email, or walk-in reservations directly into your Tripcart system. You keep 100% of everything you make, no commission fees.

Traveler fees

Add local taxes, payment gateway fees, or service charges automatically to the booking total. You keep 100% of this. Tripcart takes no cuts.

Discounts on full payments

Encourage guests to make payments in full while booking with discounts. This not only improves cash flow but also reduces the risk of cancellations.

Abandoned cart recovery

Automatically send reminder emails to guests who abandon their booking. You can reach out to them further via email or phone calls to help finish their booking.

Customer dashboard

Give guests a personal dashboard to view their bookings on. This makes their experience smoother and increases repeat business.

Travel insurance

Sell travel insurance with every tour. You can make it optional or required, giving travelers peace of mind while increasing the value of every booking.

Customized private tours

Offer customized tours that match each guest's schedule and interests. You can create private booking links for them and receive payments directly.

Cost includes & excludes

Clearly show travelers exactly what’s covered in the tour price and what’s not. This helps set expectations, reduces questions, and avoids surprises later.

Tour difficulty rating

Add a difficulty rating to each tour so guests know what to expect physically. Perfect for hiking, adventure, or activity tours.

Altitude chart

Show a visual altitude profile for trekking and climbing tours. Helps guests plan better and feel confident before booking.

Currency switcher

Let travelers see tour prices in their own currency. This removes confusion and makes it easier for international guests to book with confidence.

Maps

Let travelers visualize your offerings by embedding a map or uploading map images directly onto your tour pages.

Tour labels

Highlight tours with special labels like “Best Seller,” “New,” or “Limited Spots.” Catch attention instantly and drive urgency to book.

Webhooks & API keys

Connect Tripcart to your other tools and systems. This allows you to sync bookings, payments, or customer data with your existing workflow.

Customer management

You get a complete view of every traveler’s details. Track bookings, notes, and preferences all in one place.

Custom Scripts

Want to add live chat, Google Analytics, or other tools to your website? You can easily connect external tools without changing anything on your site.

Checkout Form Editor

You choose what information to collect during checkout. This keeps your booking process clean and only shows what’s relevant to you and your guests.

Tour Search

Your guests can easily find the right tour using filters like destination, date, or price. No more endless scrolling.

Upcoming tours

Stay on top of every departure. Your dashboard shows what’s coming up, so you're always prepared.

User access & role-based permissions

You decide who sees what. Set roles for your team to keep everything secure and running smoothly.

Pricing & Trial

  • How does the free trial work?

    We offer a 30-day free trial that gives you full access to all of Tripcart’s features—no credit card required. This allows you to explore our platform thoroughly and see how it can benefit you. If you decide to continue using our services after the trial, you can easily upgrade to a paid plan that suits your needs.

  • Do I need to choose a plan now?

    No, you don’t need to choose a plan immediately. You can start with our free trial and explore all features before deciding on a plan that works best for your business.

  • What payment methods do you accept?

    We accept all major credit cards and bank transfers. Our payment system is secure and processes transactions quickly and safely.

  • Are there any contracts or cancellation fees?

    No, we don’t require long-term contracts, and there are no cancellation fees. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time without penalties.

  • Does Tripcart charge commission fees?

    No, Tripcart doesn’t take any cut from your sales. You keep 100% of every booking. That means no commission fees, no hidden charges, and no surprises.

Platform Setup

  • Do you offer onboarding help?

    Absolutely. Our support team and resources are here to help you launch faster and easier. We provide comprehensive guides, video tutorials, and direct support to get you up and running quickly.

  • Can I use my own domain with Tripcart?

    Yes. You can use your own domain to make your tour booking site feel fully branded. Connecting your domain is quick and simple. But if you need any help, our support team is always ready to assist.

  • Do I need to pay for hosting separately?

    No. Hosting is already included in every Tripcart plan. You don’t need another hosting service or pay extra. Your website will be fast and secure without having to worry about hosting fees.

Use Cases & Growth

  • How does Tripcart help grow my revenue?

    You can grow your revenue with Tripcart by giving travelers more ways to book and spend.

    You can ask for upfront payments to reduce no-shows and cancellations. You can also offer add-ons like airport pickup/drop-offs, private guides, or extra tickets.

    You can also add multiple packages for the same tour with different services and prices. These can be packages such as Budget, Standard, VIP, etc. You can also add accommodation upgrades such as 5-star hotel rooms, tea houses, or hostels.

    Moreover, you can create group discounts and promo codes to attract more bookings and keep seats filled. These options help increase the average order value (AOV) of every booking, helping grow your revenue.

  • Is Tripcart good for selling single-day tours, or is it better for multi-day tours?

    Tripcart works well for both single-day and multi-day tours.

    If you run single-day tours, Tripcart makes it easy to create and sell activities like walking tours, day trips, or local experiences. Create hour-by-hour itineraries to help travelers visualize the trip. You can set clear pricing, manage capacity, and accept bookings right on your website.

    If you offer multi-day tours, Tripcart helps you design daily itineraries with images, maps, FAQs, and more. You can show travelers what’s included each day and manage bookings across dates. You can even add accommodation upgrades and multiple packages to each multi-day tour.

Payments

  • Can I accept payments directly for bookings?

    Yes. You can receive payments directly on your website. Tripcart doesn’t hold any of your payments or take any commissions.

    You just need to connect your account with a payment gateway like PayPal, Stripe, Revolut, etc., to start receiving payments. What travelers pay is exactly what you receive.

  • What if Tripcart doesn’t support my payment gateway?

    If your preferred gateway is not available, you can submit a request. Our team looks at these requests closely and adds new gateways based on what users need most. Click to request a new payment gateway
