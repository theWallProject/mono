HOW CAN WE HELP?

  • Biologically based IPM

    BioBee offers a wide array of natural enemies including predatory mites, parasitic wasps, predatory insects, beneficial nematodes, traps, pheromones and bio-stimulants. The beneficials are geographic specific.

    Read More

  • Natural Pollination

    The bumblebee is an outstanding pollinator that enables higher yields and better quality. It is used in greenhouses and net structures; for tomatoes, sweet peppers and Zucchini as well as outdoor crops such as avocado, cherry, blueberry, pears, almonds and mango.

    Read More

  • Plant Protection

    BioBee offers a line of plant protection products that integrate with the beneficial insect’s activity and provide all encompassing solutions for above and below ground issues.

    Read More

  • Insect Feed

    BioBee offers quality insect feed for the early establishment of a “standing army” as well as for rearing.

    Read More

  • BioBee recognizes the uniqueness of each country, embraces and adapts the solutions to custom fit the needs with a hands-on approach.
