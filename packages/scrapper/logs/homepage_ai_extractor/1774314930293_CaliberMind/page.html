What is CaliberMind?

CaliberMind is the Multi-Touch Attribution, Marketing Analytics, and GTM Intelligence platform that transforms complex and disjointed marketing and sales data into actionable insights and provides a complete picture of go-to-market efforts, from campaign performance to revenue impact.
The platform includes agentic analytics in the form of an AI assistant called Agent Cal, which helps teams get answers to stakeholder questions quickly, generate audit-ready reports, and surface patterns and opportunities that might otherwise go unnoticed.

Who is CaliberMind best suited for?

CaliberMind was built for everyone on the go-to-market team: Revenue Operations, Demand Generation, Marketing Operations, Marketing Analysts, Sales Teams, and Marketing Leaders. It is ideal for large companies and enterprises characterized by:

  • Nuanced go-to-market motions
  • Mature MarTech stacks with ~12+ tools
  • A diverse mix of online and offline channels
  • Account-based marketing programs (ABM)
  • Complex CRM implementation (e.g., Salesforce with Custom Objects)
  • High level of accountability to leadership
  • Cross-functional collaboration and shared reporting
  • Need for data sovereignty, governance, and security
What does CaliberMind do?

CaliberMind helps large companies and enterprises understand how marketing contributes to pipeline and revenue, prove contribution without contradicting what’s in the CRM, prioritize accounts for sales, and make better decisions about where to invest their marketing budget.

How does CaliberMind work?

Once data is ingested into the warehouse, CaliberMind models how key entities relate to one another. Those entities include People (leads and contacts), Companies (accounts), Events (interactions, touchpoints), Channels, Opportunities, and Campaigns.

By resolving these relationships first, CaliberMind runs queries on top of your predefined business rules, attribution logic, and funnel structure rather than fragmented source data. Insights can then be explored through dashboards, queried through Agent Cal, or written back into CRM systems like Salesforce.

CaliberMind is not a black box. Every data relationship, query, and output can be audited. Plus, CaliberMind can be tailored to your business. While CaliberMind has plug-and-play attribution models, account scoring models, and funnel stages, they are all customizable to fit your business context.

What’s CaliberMind's time to value?

In most cases, you can expect your team to get valuable insights in two weeks or less — as soon as your data sources are connected.

For custom insights that require advanced data modeling and configuration, our professional services team can move as fast as you do.

I’ve heard there’s a learning curve. What should I expect?

CaliberMind isn’t a one-size-fits-all tool. It’s modular and designed to flex around your business — which means there’s a lot you can do with it. 

While most users get comfortable with the essentials quickly, many teams discover new capabilities as they start asking harder and harder go-to-market questions.

In other words, the more you explore, the more value you unlock. The best part? You don’t have to learn or explore on your own. Our customer success team (consistently praised on G2) will guide you every step of the way.
