Super Light All-Terrain Vehicle

One of the lightest 4x4s in the extreme category.
Engineered for riders who seek both power and agility, it combines a lightweight build with the strength and stability of a full 4x4. From city streets to demanding off-road trails, the MIA Four delivers freedom, control, and the ultimate thrill.

INNOVATION

A revolutionary, globally patented platform built for extreme endurance. The dynamic chassis features a patented tilting suspension system, allowing each wheel to tilt independently for superior stability and safety across city streets, off-road paths, and challenging terrains. ideal for true all-terrain performance.

OUR ELECTRIC PLATFORM

MIA Dynamics introduces compact technology that maintains the stability of a traditional four-wheel chassis while incorporating the width and movement of two-wheeled vehicles. This miniaturization makes the platform ideal for micro-mobility and enables the development of variou vehicles with the new design.

Safety First

The four-wheel grip on a small vehicle ensures an exciting, stable, and safe ride. on challenging roads, making the platform ideal for sectors like autonomous robotics, agriculture, tourism, and construction.
