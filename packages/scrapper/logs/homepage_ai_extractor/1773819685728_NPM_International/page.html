BEHIND THE

TECHNOLOGY

NPM Ltd. was established in 2005 in the vicinity of Israel’s northern shoreline, in a high tech industrial and technological park. NPM’s innovative products – like ORON 58s, ORON 60, GLOW 7 GLOW 10, and GLOW Unlimited devices – for permanent make-up and micro-pigmentation, are currently sold around the world and can be found at leading institutes.
