    NEW CAMPAIGN ON POLIO VACCINATION 

     In March 2022, poliovirus emerged again in Israel, nine children were infected, some meningitis cases, and one case of paralysis in a 4-year-old child. None of them was vaccinated.

    At the same time poliovirus was discovered in sewage in several regions: Jerusalem, Modiin Ilit, Bet Shemesh,  

    It seems that during the COVID era, vaccination schemes have been disrupted.

    In April 2022, the Ministry of Health launched a campaign to vaccinate with the OPV and check the vaccination status of all the children.

    ERANIM  undertakes to support the Ministry of Health's efforts with different actions toward the general public, the Healthcare professionals, and the school. 

    Inspired by the 2 heroes who are saving the world, Jonas Salk and Albert Sabin, we will use their narratives to sensitise the children and teenagers, using superheroes cartoons, slogans and t-shirts.   

     

    BE A HERO AND SAVE THE WORLD FROM POLIO WITH 2 DROPS

     

    The number of polio cases in the world decreased to 99.9% of its number since 1988 since the creation of the GPEI the Global Polio Eradication Initiative. Already 2 serotypes,PV 1 and PV3 of the wild polio have been eradicated, last effort is focused to totally eradicate the last serotype PV2.

    OUR MISSION 

    ​

    • To promote safe medication practices

    • To enhance awareness and  provide science-based information on vaccines

    • Through education, communication, and research

    • To be an actor in times of Public Health crisis and pandemic

    • To strengthen the collaboration between Israel and the international community 

    OUR VISION 

    We believe that

    • Everyone is entitled to free access to transparent and scientific information.

    • Medication and vaccine safety must be considered as a Public Health activity involving all stakeholders.

    In this objective, 

    • We welcome both healthcare professionals and the general public.

    • We are a multidisciplinary, innovative, and independent platform linked to a large scientific network.
