The problem

Ammonia is essential. Emissions aren’t.

Billions of humans rely on ammonia every day. From fertilizers and pharmaceuticals to cosmetics and cleaning products, it makes modern life possible.
→ Ammonia is the second most synthesized molecule, but its production isn’t sustainable.
The traditional Haber–Bosch method requires extremely high temperatures and pressures to transform high-purity nitrogen and hydrogen into ammonia.
N2​+3H2​ → 2NH3 (400°C, 300 atm)
→ Vast amounts of fossil fuels are burnt at massive, remote facilities adding transportation costs and complex logistics.

450M

450 million metric tons of CO2 emitted annually by traditional ammonia production methods.

2%

2% of global CO2 emissions are attributed to conventional ammonia synthesis.

Soaring Demand

As populations grow and the hydrogen economy matures, ammonia production is expected to triple by 2050.
