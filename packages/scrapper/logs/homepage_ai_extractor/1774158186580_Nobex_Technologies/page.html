Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build my app?

Most apps are built and ready for submission within 24-48 hours after you complete the setup process.

Do I need coding knowledge?

No! Our platform is designed to be completely no-code. You can build and customize your app through our intuitive dashboard.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees.

Do you handle app store submissions?

We guide you through the submission process and provide all necessary assets. You'll submit using your own developer accounts.

Do I need Google or Apple Developer accounts?

Yes, to launch your app you will need a developer account for each of the platforms you want to launch the app on. You'll grant our team access and we will take care of the whole submission process for you.

What stream formats do you support?

Nobex Partners apps support MPEG, AAC, WMA formats on both HTTP and HTTPS.

What kind of support do you offer?

We offer email support for all plans, with priority support for Plus and Pro plans. Pro plans also get dedicated support.
