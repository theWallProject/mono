About Sadovsky Ltd

With a rich history spanning nearly 50 years, Sadovsky Ltd has been a prominent manufacturer and supplier of cleaning products. Established in 1967 by Samuel Sadovsky, the company is currently managed by a dynamic team that includes the younger generation. Sadovsky collaborates with importers, large-scale distributors, supermarkets, and retail chains both in Israel and globally.
