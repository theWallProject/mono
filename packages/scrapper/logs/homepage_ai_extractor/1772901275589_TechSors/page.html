Our Services

We are a leading digital web agency, providing full-package services for businesses small to large.

Content Creation

At TechSors we know that great content marketing not only helps companies convey their expertise through written words, but it can also accomplish a wide range of goals. Whether those goals involve lead generation, social marketing, or brand awareness, our team of content creators is here to take your content to the next level.

Content Marketing

Our Content Marketing Portfolio will knock you off your feet - promise! We're experts at creating content for social networks and content distribution platforms such as Taboola & Outbrain. Our content creators have vast experience developing content that drives engagement and improves your overall site's KPIs.

UX & UI Design

To receive an ultimate product we put stress on the first stages, creating user stories and wireframes. Our UX team is here to focus on your needs while specializing in research, design & development. Backed by a team of UX specialists, TechSors tackles simple usability interfaces to complex enterprise platforms.

Website Development

Whether you want to develop a WordPress site or a complex eCommerce with an integrated content management system, TechSors is here to provide a complete solution. Together with our award winning web design team we create products designed to provide you with results you want.

App Development

Our team of experts are here to assist you with the development of your mobile app. Whether you want a sleek cross-platform product, mobile site, or even develop an app that integrates with third party software, we can create an entire package that leads to greater success for your business.

Software Development

TechSors employees a team of experienced software product developers to tackle all types of projects. We have expertise in PHP, .NET, JAVA, MySQL, HTML, Objective-C and many more languages. We build different solutions from enterprise to cloud-based products.
