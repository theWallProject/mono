REDEFINING EFFICIENT PERFORMANCE AT SCALE

Maverick-2 Intelligent Compute Architecture (ICA) represents a revolutionary shift in computing with the unveiling of the intelligent software-defined hardware acceleration that delivers real-time adaptability and unmatched performance. This breakthrough technology is designed to deliver superior performance and efficiency for HPC, AI, and vector database applications.

MOVE BEYOND THE PAIN OF PORTING

Maverick-2 ICA pioneers a new era in HPC computing for developers. Paired with a user-friendly and powerful developer toolchain that natively supports popular languages, such as C/C++, FORTRAN, OpenMP, and Kokkos, Maverick-2 eliminates the need for domain-specific languages and the burdensome application porting effort they create. With Maverick-2 you can avoid extensive code rewrites, and accelerate your innovation while bypassing the time-consuming porting process. This software compatibility and ease of use saves months in development effort, enabling faster time-to-science and insights. NextSilicon is dedicated to providing comprehensive support for customer applications, with upcoming integrations planned for CUDA, HIP/ROCm, and leading AI frameworks.

THE FUTURE OF ACCELERATION IS INTELLIGENT

Maverick-2 is redefining the future of high-performance computing with Intelligent Compute Architecture (ICA), a new approach that moves beyond the status quo of traditional CPU and GPU acceleration by combining intelligent software-defined hardware with real-time optimization. Maverick-2 ICA can dynamically adapt and reconfigure its hardware to the specific needs of your HPC and AI workloads to provide efficient, workload-specific performance with the flexibility to adapt to any future demands.

ACCELERATING SANDIA'S VANGUARD PROGRAM

In 2023, Sandia National Laboratories, leading a tri-lab consortium with Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL) and Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL), announced their partnership with NextSilicon Inc. to deliver the next Advanced Architecture Prototype System (AAPS). NextSilicon's system was embedded into Sandia’s Vanguard program and was tasked with identifying and evaluating emerging pre-tested technologies, to determine their viability in supporting the National Nuclear Security Administration's (NNSA) Advanced Simulation and Computing (ASC) program. Learn more about NextSilicon and Sandia’s partnership.
