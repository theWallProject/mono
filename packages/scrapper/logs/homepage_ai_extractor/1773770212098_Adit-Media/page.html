About Us

With Adit-Media the only thing you need is a goal. Let us deal with the rest!

Adit-Media is an online performance based ad network. We work with hundreds of sites and apps worldwide, serving billions of ad impressions a month. Our expertise, cutting edge technology and great relationships with our partners enable us to deliver the best ROI and reach for our advertisers as well as the highest rates in the market for our publishers.

OUR VISION

Make every impression count! We understand our clients’ needs for quality targeting and Maximum ROI and our publishers demand for high rates and 100% traffic monetization. Using our vast experience, advanced analysis and optimization tools and direct relations with our partners, we ensure that every Ad impression will have the best value for both the client and the publisher.

LONG-TERM

Every partnership begins with the goal of establishing and maintaining a long-term relationship. We put a strong emphasis on good communication and full transparency so you always know where you stand. “where there is a will, there is a way” so if you want to make the most out of your traffic or product, put your trust in us and we will show you the way!

SERVICE

We are here for you! At Adit-Media we understand the importance of client service and are proud of our 100% attention, 24/7 availability approach. We are human so we have to sleep for a few hours a day, but other than that we are always available, very responsive and fully dedicated to making sure our partners are getting all they need and more.

EXPERIENCE

Knowledge is power. With more than 10 years of combined experience in this industry, we know a lot. We know what is most important for an advertiser and a publisher, we know all the DO’s and DON’T’s, and most importantly – we know what we are doing. You are in good hands!

FLEXIBILITY

Need special payment terms? Want fixed rates? Everything is possible! Every partner is different and has different needs. We understand that. As a boutique Ad network, we are able to be super flexible and this allows us to find a tailor-made solution for each partner.

INNOVATION

In this ever-changing environment, it is crucial to be able to innovate and reinvent yourself. we know that in every difficulty there is an opportunity, and our partnerships grow better and stronger every time the rules of the game change.
