Core Values

United Purpose

As a global team, we are united in our purpose to shape the future of Clean Energy. We embrace the diverse skills, teamwork and perspectives needed to achieve our goals.

Close to our Customers

We listen carefully to understand our customers’ and partners’ needs. We build trust through consistently meeting our commitments.

Breakthrough Innovation

We constantly create new, innovative ways forward. We are persistent and strive for continuous improvement and learning opportunities in every situation.

Courageous Honesty

We value honesty over personal interest. We bravely provide fact-based and respectful feedback, even if uncomfortable.

Excellence in all we do!

We commit ourselves to world class performance: Not only in our technology but in all of our daily responsibilities and behavior.
