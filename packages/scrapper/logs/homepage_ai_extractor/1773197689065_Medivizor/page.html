Some questions you may have

What do you do with my personal information?
We use the information you provide us to find medical information that is relevant to you (or the person you care for). When we find something that is relevant, we will email you about it.

We will never share your personal information with anyone without your explicit permission.

We are HIPAA compliant (if you're not sure what HIPAA is, learn more here).

We only ask for the least amount of information which is essential for delivering a personalized experience. That’s why we don’t ask for your name, phone number, ID, date of birth, or address. One thing we do need is an email address – and that’s so we can notify you when we find important updates that are relevant to you.

You can read our full privacy policy here.
Can I use Medivizor on behalf of someone I am caring for?
Yes. When you sign up, we’ll ask you whether you are using Medivizor for yourself or for someone you care for.
Is this service free?
Yes, this service is 100% free for patients and caregivers.
How do you make money?
We do not sell or provide any identifiable personal or medical information to anyone.

In the future, we may introduce relevant advertising to help support the free service. We may also introduce premium services which will not be free.
Who are you and why should I trust you?
Our mission is to improve the lives of people coping with medical conditions and those who care for them. Thus, our interests align with our subscribers’ interests.

We provide personalized information, not medical advice or diagnosis. We suggest discussing all medical information with your healthcare professional.

You can read more about our team here

Our Partners

The Chris4Life Colon Cancer Foundation is a national Colon Cancer non profit dedicated to permanently eliminating the threat of the disease through the discovery of a cure. Until there is a cure, the foundation is committed to significantly improving the treatment experience for patients and caregivers, and to dramatically raise awareness about colon cancer in general.

Go to Chris4Life
Diabetes Hands Foundation seeks to understand, connect and energize the millions of people living with diabetes. They are doing so by providing platforms where people with diabetes and their loved ones can connect and have an open dialog about their experiences with this chronic condition.

Go to Diabetes Hands Foundation
Melanoma Foundation of New England's mission is to reduce the incidence of melanoma in the New England region. They provide a variety of educational programs targeting the prevention and early detection of skin cancer and melanoma for both children and adults. The Foundation also assists patients and their caregivers struggling with melanoma by offering support groups, educational programs, and Billy’s Buddies, a one-on-one patient advocacy and support program. These programs are all free.

Go to Melanoma Foundation
Melanoma Girl's mission is to positively promote melanoma awareness through local and national fundraising, MG-branded products, and prevention education.

METAvivor is dedicated to the specific fight of women and men living with stage 4 metastatic breast cancer. At the time of METAvivor’s founding, no organization was dedicated to funding research for the disease and no patient groups were speaking out about the dearth of stage 4 cancer research. While more and more people have taken up the cry for more stage IV research, METAvivor remains the sole US organization dedicated to awarding annual stage IV breast cancer research.

Go to Metavivor

Desert Cancer Foundation is a nonprofit organization dedicated to assisting residents in the Coachella Valley and surrounding communities who need financial assistance in obtaining screening, diagnosis and treatment for cancer and allied diseases. Desert Cancer Foundation (DCF), a California 501(c)(3) corporation, was founded in 1994 to pay for cancer treatment of residents who lack insurance or sufficient funds to pay for their medical care by Dr. Sebastian George and Art and Cory Teichner. Desert Cancer Foundation works with the support of local healthcare professionals, who provide their medical services to our clients at a greatly reduced cost. DCF's Patient Assistance Program assists with the costs related to cancer care such as medications, insurance co-pays, deductibles, premiums, and more. In addition, through partnerships with local providers, DCF offers lung cancer screening, as well as breast cancer screening and diagnostic services at no cost to low income, uninsured or underinsured Coachella Valley residents. Please contact Desert Cancer Foundation at 760-773-6554 or visit DesertCancerFoundation.org for more information.
