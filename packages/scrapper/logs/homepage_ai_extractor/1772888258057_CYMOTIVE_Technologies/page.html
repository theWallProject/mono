Solving the automotive industry’s most pressing cybersecurity challenges

Today’s vehicles are more connected and more vulnerable than ever before. That’s why CYMOTIVE is building the next generation of automotive cybersecurity solutions to meet the needs of regulation, public safety, and the rapidly changing ecosystem.

  • An industry in transition

    The automotive industry is undergoing a tectonic shift with the rise of electrification, connectivity, software-first vehicles, autonomous driving, and more. To successfully navigate this shift, industry players must effectively cope with the evolving threat landscape.

  • The evolving threat landscape

    As vehicles evolve, they are increasingly exposed to vulnerabilities across the entire automotive value chain. A rise in cyberattacks puts the automotive industry at risk for financial and information loss and reputational harm, and poses a severe threat to public safety.

  • The safety context

    With the automotive industry's digital transformation, the stakes are high. Connected vehicles are cyberattack targets with devastating consequences. Automakers must protect public safety by prioritizing cybersecurity and compliance with evolving regulations like UNR 155, 156, and ISO/SAE 21434.

Safe, seamless, and trusted solutions

In-Vehicle Intrusion Detection System (IDS)

Real-time, in-vehicle security monitoring for fleet protection

IDS brings together cutting-edge technology and sophisticated threat analysts for end-to-end detection and mitigation of cyberattacks across the entire vehicle ecosystem. The singular, automotive-compliant solution is distributed throughout the vehicle and is completely flexible and adaptable. With a three-in-one approach combining detection, forensics, and response IDS is an unparalleled, bespoke system already on the road with 1M+ vehicles.

Connected Data Hub (CDH)

Fully customizable IoT fleet telemetry data collection

With CDH, seamlessly analyze, filter, and investigate fleet data to identify cyber and technical anomalies, and close the gap between IoT end-point devices and backend teams. CDH enables you to monitor changes in data streams and seamlessly manage these changes across teams, disciplines, and geographies. Powered by AI, CDH makes data accessible cross-company with our semantic query capabilities, so you can navigate data with ease. Finally, automatically setup backend systems in a customizable and cost-efficient way based on your specific use cases.

CyClarity

Automated, in-vehicle & end-to-end penetration testing

CyClarity is an advanced, singular framework for in-vehicle, fleet, and end-to-end security testing and penetration testing. It tackles the complexities of protecting connected mobility with comprehensive scanning, analysis, and vulnerability discovery for every IoT system in the vehicle ecosystem. CyClarity both automatically identifies vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and security issues, and is customizable, enabling users to easily and quickly generate their own new, complex test flows.

CarAlert

Robust vulnerability management

CarAlert provides robust, timely vulnerability management for maximum protection throughout the vehicle’s lifespan. It combines deep technology and automated management with human intelligence to match, prioritize, and swiftly resolve vulnerabilities. By doing so, CarAlert mitigates financial and reputational risks, saves time and money, and enhances road safety – all while enabling regulatory compliance.

Cybersecurity Management System (CyCSMS)

A single source of cybersecurity truth for speedy and streamlined compliance

CyCSMS streamlines cybersecurity operations throughout the vehicle's lifespan into one centralized management platform for effortless risk and regulatory compliance. With a systematic approach and seamless integration, CyCSMS oversees, monitors, and assesses all cybersecurity processes and activities to serve as the organization’s single point of truth for vehicle cybersecurity. With CyCSMS, automatically govern risk and pass audits with ease.
