We Make Water Safe

Protecting the world’s waters with proven scientific ingenuity and deep-tech solutions
It’s everyone’s problem
Safe water is not a given, but it should be.

Climate change, pollution, overuse, and waste are rapidly diminishing the planet’s safe water supply. But another deadly problem is hiding in plain sight.

Dangerous cyanobacteria (blue-green algae) are proliferating in lakes and oceans, swiftly dominating ecosystems, killing off thriving life forms, and hampering the environment’s delicate balance. 

Even more frightening, these harmful algal blooms (HABS) release hazardous toxins such as cyanotoxins that can sicken or even kill humans and animals. Worldwide, this destruction is also inflicting devastating economic damage on industries that rely on water.

Success Stories

Lake Okeechobee, Florida, USA

Lake Okeechobee, the eighth largest freshwater lake in the US and a wildlife paradise, was contaminated with aggressive toxic algae. A response team of BlueGreen field scientists arrived on site within 72 hours of the governor’s call and applied Lake Guard® Oxy. Within a matter of days, toxic algae were eliminated, leaving no trace in the water. BlueGreen improved Lake Okeechobee’s water quality and helped keep Florida’s waterways clean and fit for recreation, commerce, etc.

Lake Chippewa, Ohio, USA

Lake Chippewa was closed to the public every summer due to toxic algal blooms. In 2019, a 25-minute Lake Guard® Blue treatment reopened the lake within 24 hours, reviving the community and leaving the lake bloom-free for years.

6-hectare reservoir, Israel

The Lake Guard® Blue application took only a few minutes and achieved far-reaching results within 24 hours.

Nan Hu, China

Nan Hu is the beating heart of Yo Yang City and the site of its famous annual water festival. A proliferation of toxic algal blooms threatened to destroy the delicate environmental balance in and around the lake. After exhausting every alternative, government officials assigned BlueGreen Water Technologies to solve the problem. Within 24 hours of the Lake Guard® application, satellite images showed the lake’s exceptional recovery.

Mantua Reservoir, Utah, USA

BlueGreen's Net Blue™ technology removes CO2 from the atmosphere by treating harmful algal blooms. The solution, tested in Utah's Mantua Reservoir, sequestered 12,913 tons of CO2, demonstrating a major accomplishment in the fight against the effects of climate change.

Roodeplaat, South Africa

South Africa knows more than most countries how critical every drop of water is, which is why Roodeplaat Nature Reserve put its trust in BlueGreen. One treatment with Lake Guard® made billions of gallons of water in South Africa safe for human consumption within just a few days.
