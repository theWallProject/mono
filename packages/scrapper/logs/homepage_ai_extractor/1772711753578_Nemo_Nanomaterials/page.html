Better Composites.
Zero Process Change.

We create plastic additives with expertly dispersed nanoparticles, providing combined solutions that boost electrical and thermal conductivity, EMI shielding, and more – flexible, low-dose, and tailored to your needs.

Why NemoBLEND™
Added Electrical and Thermal Conductivity, EMI Shielding

High performance at low addition achieves strong property enhancements with minimal active material

More room for other additives: Flame retardants, colorants, UV stabilizers, impact modifiers or process aids

Reduced Environmental Impact
