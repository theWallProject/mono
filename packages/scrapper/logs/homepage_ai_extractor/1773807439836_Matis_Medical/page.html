Matis Medical, Inc. developed the Cone and Plate(let) Analyzer (CPA), a novel point-of-care system for real-time, whole-blood platelet function testing.
Matis’ products are state-of-the-art, yet simple and highly usable devices, designed to provide fast and accurate platelet function results.
The Impact-R is a research tool for platelet activity monitoring and research of platelet dysfunctions disorders.
The Impact system (in development) has an application mode for selective monitoring of anti-platelet drugs, allowing individual dose adjustment and drug selection.
Its features (rapid, physiological, simple, cost effective) will help in designing a more rational approach to cardiovascular therapeutics, improving clinical outcome.
After receipt of the FDA approval for marketing the Company expects that its unique device will generate great interest in the cardiovascular market.
