Meet Sage
Sage is always on your side

Sage, the always-accessible AI behavioral health assistant, works with providers as part of the care team. Sage is never a substitute for human clinicians.

Life happens when patients are not in session with their provider. Sage is there every day: before, after, and between sessions. Whether the patient needs support and reminders, needs to vent, or wants to practice a new skill they learned in session, Sage is here for them.

Developed by a world-leading team of psychologists, psychiatrists, and AI experts, with clinical rigor and safety at the forefront.

Sage both receives instruction from the clinical team to best support the specific patient needs, and also escalates necessary situations to them. Sage shares patient progress with the provider so they can best understand and care for patients.
