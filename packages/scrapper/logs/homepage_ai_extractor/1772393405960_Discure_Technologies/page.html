Turning Back Time

The Discure Approach

Reversing disc degeneration

Discure’s minimally invasive, implantable device is designed to treat and reverse degenerative disc disease, without need for hospitalization or rehab. By restoring the disc’s healthy fluid dynamics, Discure aims to restore cushioning and stability to the spine, revive disc cells, and accelerate tissue healing, to reduce pain and restore function
