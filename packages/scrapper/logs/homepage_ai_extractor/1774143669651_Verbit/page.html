Together, we can make words work

Verbal intelligence goes beyond capturing your words. Verbit deepens your understanding by providing actionable insights. We then apply those insights exactly where they’re needed to unlock the value of speech.

Capture

Our AI-based, Automatic Speech Recognition engine, Captivate™, is designed for speech-intensive industries. It’s fully customizable and continuously trained to capture your every word and deliver best-in-class accuracy on even niche subject matter.

Understand

Our Generative AI technology, Gen.V™, offers real-time insights on captured content within seconds. It makes your transcripts actionable and helps its users work, learn and share information more efficiently by generating quick summaries, keywords and more.

Apply

Verbit’s solutions and the insights they derive fit seamlessly into customers’ existing workflows. Leverage our helpful integrations to move from speech to action faster than with any other technology.
