The thick layer of skin beneath the outer epidermis, providing support & sensation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a good candidate for ULVERA AI™?

Any person with excess fat that wants to change and improve their body contour

How many treatments does it take?

ULVERA AI is efficacious after a single treatment but full efficacy is best after 3 treatments.

Is ULVERA AI™ safe?

ULVERA AI is a very safe procedure.

What does the ULVERA AI™ treatment feel like?

During the treatment there is a small sensation of pinching, but no significant pain.

Can ULVERA AI™ replace a surgical procedure?

Yes. In some cases is can. We had patients who were scheduled for  a tummy tuck and decided not to do the surgery after going through our treatment.

What are ULVERA AI’s™ clinical results?

The clinical results show excellent results with significant circumference reduction with no adverse events.

How is ULVERA AI™ different from other cosmetic procedures for Body Contouring?

ULVERA AI has the best clinical efficacy for circumference and excessive fat reduction while the pain during the treatment procedure is minimal.

Is there pain after the ULVERA AI™ procedure?

No, there is no pain after the procedure.

How long does it take to see results?

First results can be seen 1 to 2 weeks after the treatment with an onward gradual improvement. Full results are seen 60 to 90 days after the procedure.

How long is a typical ULVERA AI™ Treatment?

The treatment takes around 30 min.

How does ULVERA AI™ work?

Ultrasound waves generate heat inside the fat tissue while the surface is cooled.  This leads to gradual disruption of the fat cells in the treated area…

EMS pulses stimulate the muscles during the treatment leading to an even better body contour.

What is ULVERA AI™?

Ulvera AI™ is an Israeli startup company based in Tel-Aviv with the most advanced technology IN THE NON-INVASIVE BODY CONTOURING MARKET
