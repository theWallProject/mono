ABOUT US

We make your event &
conference memorable

We make your event &
conference memorable

Founded in 2006 by Professor Zeev Shoham and Professor Milton Leong, CME Congresses is the world’s leading boutique professional conference planning firm specializing in organizing and managing academic medical conferences.

CME Congresses has organized hundreds of medical conferences around the globe. Disciplines include oncology, hematology, gynecology, neurology, endocrinology and more.
