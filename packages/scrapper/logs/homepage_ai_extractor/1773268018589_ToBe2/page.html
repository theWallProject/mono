Introducing HiveMaster
Proactive Precise Beekeeping Platform
Saving over 50% of the Colonies at risk

01

In-Hive Precise Sensing & Automated Treatment Device

  • Releases precise treatment compounds in a gas phase
  • Holds a consumable cartridge providing one year of hive treatments using 90% less medicine
  • Monitors bee health, hive conditions & environment
  • Plug & Play; Compatible with all hive types
  • Self-Sufficient & IoT connectivity
02

AI-based Data Analysis and Decision Support System

  • Analyzes hive conditions and trends
  • Detects patterns to recognize hive events & recommend treatments
  • Adjust/design health treatment protocols
  • Generates valuable insights for all market stakeholders
03

Beekeeper Dashboard & Operations Interface

  • Presents real-time bee and hive conditions
  • Control and update of health treatment protocols
  • Optimizes apiary operations
  • Allow scheduling of hive worker visits based on hive status & condition
