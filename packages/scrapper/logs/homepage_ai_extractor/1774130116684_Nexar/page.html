Data Safety

Your safety is our mission.
Your privacy is our responsibility.

When you trust Nexar with real moments - accidents, near misses, family drives - that trust is earned through discipline, not promises. Here's exactly how your data works.

How your data is used.

Road SafetyAnonymized & Blurred

Your dashcam contributes to a safer road network. Before any road data leaves your camera, faces, license plates, and pedestrians are blurred on-device. This anonymized data helps improve maps, detect hazards, and advance driving technology. On by default. Opt out anytime in Settings → Privacy → Road Safety.

AI & Vision Training

We use footage (including non-anonymized content) to train our safety AI — improving collision detection, hazard identification, and our own anonymization technology. Access is restricted to AI development teams. When you delete your data, we delete the corresponding training data.

How we protect you.

Explicit Control

We never share identifiable footage without your consent or a lawful legal obligation. You choose which programs to participate in.

Privacy by Design

Faces, plates, and pedestrians are blurred on-device for Road Safety data. Identity is separated from analytics.

Encrypted & Protected

All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. ISO 27001 and 27018 certified. Access is limited, monitored, and logged.

You Own Your Footage

Export it. Delete it. Control it. When you delete, we delete — including AI training data.

Our commitments.

These are not aspirations. They are the boundaries we hold ourselves to — and the standard we invite you to hold us to.

We will never sell your personal video footage to advertisers, data brokers, or commercial buyers
We will never share identifiable footage without your consent or a legal obligation
We will never use your data for personal surveillance or tracking outside of lawful public safety purposes
We will always give you control: opt out of any data sharing program at any time

Note: Some data sharing may be considered a “sale” under U.S. state privacy laws (e.g., CCPA). You can opt out via Settings or at getnexar.com/data-sharing-opt-out.
