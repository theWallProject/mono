Frequently Asked Questions

What types of DevOps services does Naviteq offer?

We offer end-to-end DevOps services, including CI/CD automation, Infrastructure as Code, Kubernetes cluster management, and DevOps as a Service. Whether you’re building from scratch or improving existing pipelines, we tailor solutions to your team’s needs.

How quickly can we get started?

Fast. You can hit the ground running. Our engineers are ready to plug into your environment immediately, and we typically deliver a working solution or implementation plan within a short period of time. No hiring delays and no long ramp-up.

How can FinOps automation help reduce my cloud costs?

FinOps automation provides real-time visibility into cloud spend, usage patterns, and inefficiencies. We help teams right-size infrastructure, implement automated guardrails, and eliminate waste, especially in dynamic environments like AWS and Kubernetes.

Do you offer managed services for AWS environments?

Yes. Naviteq offers fully managed AWS services, including cloud migration, provisioning, scaling, patching, and ongoing optimization. Our DevOps-first approach ensures your infrastructure stays compliant, cost-efficient, and production-ready at all times, and we can start immediately.

Can Naviteq manage complex cloud infrastructure for AI or multi-cloud/hybrid-cloud workloads?

Yes. We support AI/ML platforms, data pipelines, and distributed systems across multi-cloud and hybrid environments. Using Kubernetes, GitOps, and policy-as-code, we help you manage complexity without compromising control or velocity.
Read our blog to learn more: Mastering Hybrid Cloud Architecture with DevOps: Best Practices for CTOs

Is Naviteq a good fit for startups and enterprise teams alike?

Absolutely. We support startups that need speed and flexibility, as well as enterprises that prioritize governance, compliance, and scalability, all without the wait or cost of building in-house teams.

What does AI-powered DevOps mean at Naviteq?

AI-powered DevOps at Naviteq means enhancing every stage of the DevOps lifecycle with intelligent automation. From zero-touch provisioning and real-time drift detection to automated policy enforcement and self-healing workflows, our AI-enhanced approach frees your engineers from repetitive tasks while improving speed, stability, and compliance. You keep full control, while AI ensures your infrastructure adapts in real time to changing conditions.

Can AI really help my team move faster?

Yes. AI-assisted workflows eliminate manual steps that slow teams down, from provisioning and scaling to compliance checks. That means faster deployments, fewer errors, and more time for your engineers to focus on product, not plumbing.
