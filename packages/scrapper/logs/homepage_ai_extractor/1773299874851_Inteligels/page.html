NEWS

Our unique technology is based on polyethylene oxide and polypropylene oxide segments and differs markedly from common hydrogels. Its reverse thermal response features allows Inteligels to redesign skin treatment regimens that improve ease of use,  accelerate healing,  reduce pain,  and have the added benefit of reducing environmental waste.  Applied as nontoxic, solidifying  gel, spray, or sheet, we program to “melt” at specified temperatures into a biodegradable liquid for efficient disposal
