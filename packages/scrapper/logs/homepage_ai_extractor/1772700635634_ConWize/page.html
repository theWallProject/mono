What Is Construction Estimating Software and What Does it Do?

Construction estimating software is computer software used by contractors, developers, and civil engineering professionals to accurately and efficiently gauge the cost of proposed construction projects.

Construction estimating software helps engineers to accurately calculate all construction project costs in order to build a commercial proposal for the customer. After they have won the project, they then have a budget ready for the construction project execution phase.

The best construction estimating software uses technology such as artificial intelligence, automation and cloud-based collaboration among team members to enhance the accuracy and speed of the cost estimation process.

What Are the Benefits of Construction Estimating Software?

The benefits of construction estimating software include:   
  • Precise estimates. Construction estimation software leaves little room for error. The digitization of project plans means calculations are computerized from the beginning. Additionally, the use of advanced technology such as predictive analytics helps account for market fluctuations to generate more accurate cost estimates. 
  • Improved team collaboration. Cloud technology makes collaboration a breeze. Multiple individuals and teams can conveniently access the same document and see changes in real time. This eliminates duplicate work and conflicts arising from different versions of the same document, which makes it easier to harness the potential of the team. 
  • Easier access to data. With construction estimating software, relevant data can be accessed from a central place. While reviewing an aspect of a project, data about quantities, measurements, and costs can be accessed at the click of a mouse and used to quickly make decisions. 
  • Improved chances of winning profitable work. With accurate cost estimates, you can generate more competitive bids and optimize profitability. 
  • Faster 3D takeoff. Instead of making manual measurements and calculations, or using spreadsheets where a wrong cell reference could mess everything up, construction estimation software automates and significantly speeds up the planning process. 

What Types of Companies Benefit from Construction Estimating Software?

  • General Contractors. Contractors stand to benefit the most from construction estimation software. They can use the software in the pre-construction phase to streamline the estimation and bidding process. They can also use it during construction for budgeting and cost control. 
  • Subcontractors. Subcontractors who specialize in services like HVAC, plumbing, and electrical works can use construction estimation software to create more competitive bids. 
  • Owners and Developers use construction estimating software to streamline the bidding process, choose eligible consultants and contractors and improve budget and schedule management.
  • General Contractors use construction estimating software to simplify construction pricing and quoting and reduce calculation errors to minimize tender losses. 
  • Real Estate Developers. Real estate companies, including residential and commercial builders, can use construction estimating software to assess the cost and feasibility of projects in the early stages. The software can also be of great help in budgeting and cost control. 
  • Fit-out Contractors can estimate the cost of building interiors, and prepare a competitive quote for the client.
  • Renovation and Remodeling Firms. Companies offering renovation services can use cost estimation software to more accurately estimate the cost of proposed projects and bid competitively. 
  • Facility Management Firms. Firms that manage facilities can use cost estimation software to analyze the effect of renovations and upgrades and distribute resources effectively. 

How Is Construction Estimating Software Done?

Construction estimation is done by factoring in everything that will affect the final cost of a project, including the cost of equipment, materials and labor. 

Construction estimation software digitizes and automates this process, from the digitization of the project plans and integration of relevant data to the calculation of project costs. 

Modern construction estimating software is available as a cloud-based solution that can be accessed on a subscription basis. 

What Are Some Important Features to Look For In Construction Estimation Software?

  • Cloud-Based Software. Instead of having offline software, go for cloud-based software. Members of your team can access it regardless of their location, which accommodates virtual work. Additionally, it makes collaboration breezy – you won’t have to worry about duplicate work. 
  • Predictive Analytics. Construction estimation software that’s capable of predictive analytics helps you factor in market trends and price fluctuations, which results in more reliable cost estimates. 
  • Easy access to data. The software you choose should make it easy for you to incorporate historical data, labor rates, land acquisition costs, and other data while making your analyses. 
  • Easy Integration with ERP software. The best software makes it easy to integrate with ERP software, which proves useful for cost control. Integration makes it easy to transfer figures without errors and streamlines the whole cost management process.
  • Mobile accessibility. Ideally, you should get software that team members can access while on the move. This can be web-based software that one can access through their mobile browser.
  • Scalability. The software you choose should be able to grow with you as you grow to serve more clients and handle bigger projects.

What Should Be Included in a Construction Estimate?

A construction estimate should include: 

  • The cost of the material to be used in the project, along with a breakdown of each material and the required quantities. 
  • Labor rates, including the cost of overtime and extra hours. 
  • The cost of the equipment to be used in the project. 
  • Indirect costs such as administrative expenses and land acquisition. 
  • Quotes from subcontractors. 
  • Contingency, which is typically expressed as a percentage of the total estimated cost. 
  • Profit margin. Once you have the total estimated cost of the project, you should add your profit margin to come up with the figure you’ll quote in the bid.
