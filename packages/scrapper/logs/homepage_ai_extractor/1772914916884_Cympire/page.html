Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cympire's platform?

Cympire’s AI-powered cyber range is a cloud-based and gamified cybersecurity training and assessment platform. It provides dynamic, hands-on training that immerses trainees in a lifelike SOC experience, where they will master the fundamentals of cybersecurity and gain critical real-world experience.

Who can use the platform?

Cympire’s AI-powered cyber range can be used by teams from private enterprises, government entities, higher education and also available for large-scale cyber war games and CTFs.

What does the platform offer?

Cympire’s training scenarios are aligned with industry-standard frameworks including MITRE ATT&CK, NIST-NICE, and OWASP. This ensures comprehensive coverage of relevant skills and compliance with recognized security standards.

Admins can keep track of their teams training progress to build up cyber resilience.

How does Cympire support large-scale cyber competitions?

Cympire’s platform can scale to support large cyber competitions with hundreds of participants. Our infrastructure allows for team-based exercises, individual challenges, and complex multi-stage scenarios. We provide comprehensive scoring, leaderboards, and detailed analytics for competition organizers.

Can I create my own content?
Yes, Cympire’s AI-powered cyber range is highly customizable. Training scenarios can be tailored to match your organization’s specific industry, infrastructure, and security tools in an easy and comprehensive way thanks to our “Cyber Studio”.
 
Custom training paths can be created for different roles and skill levels within your team.
