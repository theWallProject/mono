Pelvic Organ Prolapse affects millions.

More than half of women over 50 are currently living with Pelvic Organ Prolapse. And many of them are looking for options.

Symptoms of Pelvic Organ Prolapse.

When the connective tissue and muscles of the pelvic floor can’t support the pelvic organs, it can result in the dropping of the uterus and cervix from their normal position. The resulting pressure and bulging may affect exercise, intimacy, even going to the bathroom. And for many, a pessary, physical therapy, and Kegels aren’t enough.

EnPlace was successfully tested in human trials. It’s a same-day, minimally invasive approach that typically requires no overnight hospital stay. It may help women return to the activities they love quickly.
