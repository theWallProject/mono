Prism
Powered by the Brain.

An interventional psychiatry suite of solutions powered by
fMRI-informed digital biomarkers for personalized patient care. 

GrayMatters Health is leading a transformation - bringing evidence-based, accessible technology to psychiatry clinics to augment standard-of-care therapies and help improve patient outcomes and patient lives.

 

With a strong foundation in scientific research, Prism self-neuromodulation is being used in clinical practice to help patients get Back to Better™. This innovation enables patients to learn to regulate brain activity associated with their mental health condition in the clinic, gaining a skill for use in their daily lives.  

 

Prism is a comprehensive suite of solutions for scientifically leveraging the power of the brain, featuring protocols for PTSD and Depression.  

 

PTSD

Prism uses an amygdala-based biomarker and engaging computer simulation to help improve symptoms of post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD). Read the outcomes of our clinical study. 

​

Depression

Patients learn to up-regulate the reward-system biomarker (Ventral Striatum based) to help live better with depression. ​​Review the results of the pilot study on anhedonia.

Value for your patients and your practice

Leading-edge technology

​

The ground-breaking fMRI-Informed digital biomarkers that power Prism have a strong scientific and clinical foundation. The PTSD protocol uses an amygdala-based biomarker and the Depression protocol uses a reward-system biomarker. Learn more >>

Operational simplicity​

​

Prism integrates seamlessly with operational flow from intake to treatment and can be stored on a shelf or in a drawer when not in use or when administering other interventions. Most importantly, Prism sessions can be administered by a technician to optimize clinician time. 
Read a Case Study of a clinic in Texas.

Personalized care​

​

Prism reads the signal from sensors on the EEG cap and computes the relevant digital biomarker. These signals are the result of mental strategies the patient explores – this is as personal as a treatment can get. Watch the Prism PTSD mental strategy animation on YouTube.
