FAQ

Q&A

Learn about Cloudonix’s operational and business philosophy, and why we don’t believe in minutes based charges for communications services.

How do I create a Cloudonix account?

To create a Cloudonix account, simply click the “Signup” button at the top of the screen, or just click here. Once at the cockpit’s login page, simply click the green “Social Login” button to continue. No credit card is required to sign up.

Do you provide a trial period?

Yes. Cloudonix provides all accounts with a 14 days trial period, after which, in order to continue using your account to the fullest – you’ll need to enter your credit card information. If not entered, after 14 days your account will be limited to 1,000 sessions per month and 2 active calls.

Why don’t you charge by the minute?

Old-school minute billing is outdated. We charge by session—no hidden fees, simple pricing.

Can I use my own numbers or provider?

YES! Absolutely!
Cloudonix supports any SIP number or carrier. Bring your existing infrastructure—we plug right in.

Is my account limited in any way?

ABSOLUTELY NOT!
Once your account had been converted to a paid account, unless clearly requested, your account isn’t limited in any form. You subscription package will be billed based upon your selection. If you will exceed your package allotment, you will be charged based an associated overage fee, depending on the service. You can find out more information about our package in our pricing page.

I'm not a developer, how can I make use of Cloudonix?

No worries, being a developer isn’t really a must to use Cloudonix to its fullest. You can use Make™ to create your call-flows and control the platform, using a simple to use NoCode platform. Click here to learn more about our Make™ application.

I'm a developer, where should I start?
“Hallelujah, Brothers! I am here to comfort the sinner and return the stray lamb to the fold.”
Cat Ballou, 1965
Cloudonix was made by-developers, for-developers. As such, we recommend referring to our developer portal. Specifically, we recommend that you start with the Start Here Guide, then, complete the First Application Tutorial.
Do you provide volume discounts?

Cloudonix’s pricing is designed to fit as many use-cases as possible. However, if your use case differs from what we offer, for example, exceeding 500,000 sessions per month, or requiring over 150 active calls-it’s best to contact us directly to discuss your requirement.

Is Cloudonix an AI Company?

Cloudonix provides various voice AI technologies that are directly embedded into our platform. These include text-to-speech and speech-to-text, available via our <SAY> and <GATHER> CXML verbs. However, these are mostly designed for synchronous operations. Voice agent solutions, such as ReTell™ and VAPI™ require a most asynchronous streaming experience, which Cloudonix doesn’t provide.

I still have questions, who should I talk to?

If you still have questions, your best option will be to schedule a meeting with a sales engineer. Click here to schedule a meeting today.
