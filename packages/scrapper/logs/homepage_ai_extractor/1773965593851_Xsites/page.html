Xsites collaboration with the Hapoel Tel Aviv Football Club

Xsites, a leading Israeli Tech Company, teams up with Hapoel Tel Aviv Football Club. At Xsites, we strive to be an integral and contributing part of our local community. For us, it is an essential part of everything we do, and of who we are as a company. With this ethos in mind, Xsites has decided to partner with the Hapoel Tel Aviv Football Club. As a part of this collaboration, we are proud to announce that we will be directly supporting those in our community with disabilities & special needs. Through our cooperation with Hapoel, we will be providing financial grants and support to those members of our society in need. At Xsites we are proud to be a positive member of our community and continue to do great things.
