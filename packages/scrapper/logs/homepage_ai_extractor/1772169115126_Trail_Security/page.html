FAQs

What is data loss prevention?

Data loss prevention (DLP) is a set of technical controls designed to prevent sensitive data from being misused, exposed, or exfiltrated. It monitors how sensitive data is accessed, shared, or transferred across data at rest, data in motion, and data in use, and applies actions - such as alerting, blocking, or logging - based on defined policies. DLP helps reduce the risk of data loss that can lead to regulatory violations, financial impact, or loss of trust, while protecting proprietary data and personally identifiable information (PII).

How is Omni DLP different from traditional DLP?

Traditional DLP evaluates activity within individual enforcement points - such as email, endpoints, or web gateways - using static rules and isolated context. This often results in inconsistent decisions, high false positives, and constant policy tuning as data usage evolves.

Omni DLP introduces centralized, AI-driven decision intelligence by correlating signals across channels and enriching them with data sensitivity, access, and business risk context. This allows teams to prioritize incidents based on real risk, reduce alert noise, and focus investigations on what matters most.

Does Omni DLP replace existing DLP tools?  

No. Omni DLP is designed to sit above your existing architecture, providing centralized decisioning and prioritization. Teams continue using their current DLP controls while Omni improves consistency across them without changing enforcement points or workflows.
