The Somatix Difference

A passive, continuous, and non-invasive way to monitor those under your care

Leveraging our unique and patented wearable-enabled gesture detection algorithms, Somatix provides powerful clinical insights and predictive analytics on user health, safety, and wellbeing through our platform, SafeBeing.

SafeBeing™

AI-Powered Remote Patient Monitoring Platform

SafeBeing™ provides users and their caregivers with a passive, total solution. Bringing health and gesture data to life by alerting to changes in a user's health and activity trends to prevent hospital re-admissions, motivate the user, and provide peace of mind to loved ones.

SafeBeing™'s one-of-a-kind real-time gesture detection technology monitors a users hydration levels, sleep and activity data to provide insights into fall and ulcer risk, medication intake, and smoking habits.
