ABOUT US

For over 40 years, Shoham Machinery Ltd. is a leading designer and manufacturer of tooling and machinery, providing unique punching technologies for the Aluminum and PVC industries. Shoham’s products are mainly utilized by aluminum and vinyl fabricators handling profiles of windows, doors, skylights, solar installations, storefront and curtain wall profile systems. Our Manual and Pneumatic tools can be customized for diverse cutting and perforating applications for all types of metals and materials.
Shoham specializes in innovative and original solutions, by integrating top of the edge cutting techniques that are patent protected worldwide, for rapid and accurate punching results. Shoham is the largest manufacturer of punching tools in Israel and exports machines for Aluminum fabricators and extruders all around the globe.
