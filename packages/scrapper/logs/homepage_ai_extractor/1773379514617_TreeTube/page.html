Climate-Resilient Urban Environments

Trees are essential for urban spaces – they provide a shaded green canopy, reduce air pollution, and boost biodiversity, all of which help combat global warming and climate change. So why do so many urban areas still struggle with a lack of greenery?

The challenge lies in the cityscape itself. Urban trees require space, as well as access to healthy soil, air and water to thrive. Yet, urban environments often pose tough conditions for trees, with dense underground utilities, impervious asphalt surfaces, and compacted urban soils making it a hostile habitat for trees to grow. 

How can we create thriving nature-based, urban forests amidst these challenges?

We need innovative, efficient and sustainable solutions that address these age-old problems and seamlessly integrate into the demanding urban infrastructure through a holistic approach.

Meet TreeTube™: Pioneering Seamless Tree Integration for Green Urban Spaces

TreeTube™ offers the perfect tree-planting solution to harmoniously accommodate an urban space’s various underground utilities by steering the tree roots through the TreeTube™ to their desired sub-surface positioning. The customizable system is delivered on-site, offering a quick, easy-to-install, durable system, adjustable to the urban space’s various needs.

Constructing Urban Forests
  • The patented TreeTube™ systems offer a unique, modular plug-and-play solution, to accommodate for any belowground challenge
  • The comprehensive system is delivered on-site, guaranteeing quick, easy, same-day installation 
Optimal Design

The product’s circular design guarantees the required strength and durability to accommodate tree roots and allow them to coexist in complex underground infrastructures, while protecting neighboring utilities.

Structural Soil Cells

Structural soil cells are crucial, as they provide a stable, load-bearing foundation to support healthy root growth while preventing soil compaction and damage to infrastructure. TreeTube™ offers structural soil cells that are highly customizable, and can be designed according to the landscape architect’s local needs and specifications.
