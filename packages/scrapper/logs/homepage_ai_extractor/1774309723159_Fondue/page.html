Create a healthier incentive strategy with CashBack

Drive more conversions with bigger incentives

Unlike coupon codes that cut into your profits every use, CashBack allows you to offer high-value incentives that motivate order and list conversions while protecting your margins.

Let customers choose their own reward

After placing an order, customers choose the reward that matters most—store credit or a prepaid award card. Giving them the choice boosts conversions and keeps profits in your pocket. Customers get rewarded when it matters, and you make more money more efficiently.
