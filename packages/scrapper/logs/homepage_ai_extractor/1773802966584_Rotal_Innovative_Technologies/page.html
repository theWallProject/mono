Sense the Change

Rotal Innovative Technologies’ (RIT) Sensor Technological Layer (STL) is a cutting-edge solution for sensor developers and manufacturers that signifies a new chapter in sensor technology.

It streamlines development processes, minimizes time-to-market and supply chain operational costs, and can upgrade conventional sensors to smart sensors that are equipped for standalone IIoT solutions. The technology merges software and hardware to provide a comprehensive modular solution.
