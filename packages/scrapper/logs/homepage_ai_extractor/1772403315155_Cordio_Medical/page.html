Medical Speech Processing Labs

WHO WE ARE

CORDIO MEDICAL provides medical grade, groundbreaking solutions to monitor several health conditions by analyzing voice samples recorded in a simple smartphone app backed by sophisticated and proprietary algorithms allowing near real-time monitoring and early detection of condition deterioration.

LEARN MORE

BEYOND WORDS

Using only patient speech, sampled via mobile device, the Cordio HearO™ can sense fluid accumulation related to CHF and alert healthcare professionals in real-time. This is the only non-invasive, easy to use medical grade, CHF monitoring device that offers patients and caretakers peace of mind and a true sense of control.
