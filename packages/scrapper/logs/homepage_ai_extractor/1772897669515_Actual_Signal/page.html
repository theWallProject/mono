Frequently Asked Questions

How does ActualSignal work?

ActualSignal tracks Parkinson's symptoms and disease status using unique technology to analyze movements and to create your personal disease profile.
This dynamic data is continuously shared with your care team to support clinical monitoring. Our technology is currently being evaluated in an IRB-approved  clinical investigation.

Do I need a smartwatch or any special hardware?

Not at all! ActualSignal works with any iOS or Android smartphone manufactured in the last five years-no extra devices or wearables needed.

How much does it cost?

For patients, it’s completely free! We partner with healthcare organizations like ACOs to provide the app at no cost to their members. If your healthcare provider is a partner, you can use ActualSignal for free.

Can my doctor see my results?

Yes! We work with healthcare organizations like ACOs to ensure your entire care team has access to your up-to-date health insights for better decision-making. You can also generate an easy-to-read PDF to share your results with anyone outside of your care team.

How can I sign up?

Right now, ActualSignal is available to members of select ACOs in Florida, and we’re expanding across the U.S. If your healthcare provider is a partner, you’re eligible to join. Reach out to us to check availability in your area!
