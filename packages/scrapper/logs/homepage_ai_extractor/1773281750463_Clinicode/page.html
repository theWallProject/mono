Clinicode develops Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS) that improve patient safety related to electronic drug prescriptions errors.

Patient safety is at the heart of every healthcare organization. It increases the global demand for healthcare IT solutions that can add meaningful use to Electronic Medical Record (EMR) or Electronic Health Record (EHR) solutions by minimizing prescription errors.

Clinicode’s SafeRx™ solution enables hospitals, clinics and Health Maintenance Organizations (HMOs) to improve patient safety related to drug prescriptions and administration of medications. SafeRx™ Clinical Decision Support Systems alert physicians to potential prescription errors such as drug-to-drug interactions, wrong dosage, duplicate therapy, potential allergic reaction, etc. SafeRx software is integrated with EMR/EHR solutions, thus allowing physicians to view the alerts within their normal workflow.

SafeRx™ uses Clinicode’s own developed PharmaBase™ knowledge base as the clinical basis for drug prescription alert generation. PharmaBase is continuously updated by Clinicode’s professional pharmacists and physicians. Special effort and management attention have been invested throughout the years to minimize the alert fatigue phenomena related to alerting systems.

Why use SafeRx™?

  • Improves patient safety
  • Operates within the physician workflow – no need for training or assimilation processes
  • Protects the reputation of the healthcare organization
  • Employs sophisticated software tools
  • Adds value to EMR solutions
  • Designed for swift and easy integration with EMR
