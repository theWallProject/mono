SCREENEX® TRANSFORMS CONVENTIONAL WINDOW DOOR PARTITION WALLS INTO SOMETHING FAR MORE VALUABLE

New Windows of Opportunity

The future is here. Meet ScreeneX® by OSG – a slim and reliable passenger information system (PIS) fully embedded in glass units. Transform windows and glass areas into real-time on-board digital information displays.

ScreeneX® employs unique LCD-embedded glass technology. The surface on both sides of the screen is as smooth and clear as regular glass. This makes ScreeneX® the PIS solution of choice for train/bus manufacturers and fleet operators.

ScreeneX® by OSG. Powering new windows of opportunity.

Innovative glass-embedded passenger information and signage screen solutions. From the leaders in cutting-edge glass solutions.

ScreeneX® TRN transforms windows and glass partitions into vivid real-time digital information displays for passengers.  ScreeneX® TRN can be implemented in all public transportation vehicles at the OEM stage or as a retrofit. ScreeneX® TRN is also ideal for rail and bus terminals, stations and platforms.

On-Board

With ScreeneX® on-board solutions, your passengers are always in the know! ScreeneX® embedded glass technology can be implemented in side windows, partitions and other glass elements, transforming them to vivid digital screens. Ideal for on-board customer communication in railway carriages and busses! ScreeneX® links seamlessly to existing PIS systems.

Learn More

Off-Board

Provide real-time service status information when and where passengers need it. ScreeneX® can be installed in locations not possible with traditional screens such as platform screen doors (PSD), glass screen-walls, glass partitions and other glass elements. ScreeneX® systems are slim and low-weight. They are designed to handle rugged environments with zero maintenance.

Learn More

ScreeneX® RTL creates a range of exciting new solution for retail venues and commercial environments.
The ScreeneX® RTL  line of transparent products transforms vending machines, commercial refrigerators and showcases into compelling digital signage. Additional solutions include embedded screens in screen walls, totems and other decorative glass elements.

Learn More

Transparent Solutions

ScreeneX® empowers the retail experience! Consumers can now view products and eSignage simultaneously. Create relevant, seamless and engaging customer interactions by locating ScreeneX® on refrigerators, display boxes and vending machines. Screen sizes and designs can be customized to client requirements. ScreeneX® is also the solution of choice for transparent display walls.

Learn More

Embedded Screens

Captivate your customers with ScreeneX® glass-embedded screen walls and kiosks! The seamless integration of screen and glass unit enables a slim, low-weight, robust information systems that can be tailored to specific requirements. ScreeneX® embedded solutions feature a wide range of screen sizes. They also include software/hardware and optical effects.

ScreeneX® Features

Transform the passenger experience with fully-integrated glass-embedded digital signage solutions.

Next Generation Design

A new “clean” and sophisticated design concept where screens are an integral part of vehicle.

Saves internal space

Substitute traditional mounted digital signage systems by better utilizing existing glass areas and elements.

Vandal Proof

ScreeneX® is glass-embedded and inaccessible to vandals.

Durability

ScreeneX® is built to meet or exceed the most demanding industry standards.

Time Tracking

Low Weight - No External Mounting

There is no loss of cabin space or interference with line of sight or surveillance devices.

Best Performance

Plug and Play
