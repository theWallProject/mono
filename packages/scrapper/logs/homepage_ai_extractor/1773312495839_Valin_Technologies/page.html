About Valin Technologies

Valin Ltd. is a Biotechnology Company focused on research and development (R&D) of innovative biological therapeutics and of biosimilars. Our core competency lays in biopharmaceuticals development throughout the entire drug development value chain. We have extensive experience, from early discovery stages, through complete process development, CMC, pre-clinical and clinical development to approval. We develop drugs for markets with unmet needs and high business potential
