NeoMatriX®

NeoMatriX® is a collagen wound matrix uniquely derived from the extracellular matrix (ECM) of the axolotl, an aquatic species known for its remarkable regenerative abilities. This medical device is designed to support the management of partial and full-thickness wounds to support the wound environment.

Meet the Axolotl

The axolotl (pronounced ACK-suh-lot-uhl) is a rare aquatic salamander native to the lakes of central Mexico—and a marvel of regenerative biology. Unlike most vertebrates, the axolotl retains its larval features throughout its life and can regenerate entire limbs, spinal cord, heart tissue, and even parts of its brain without forming scar tissue. This extraordinary ability has captured the attention of scientists and medical innovators around the world.
