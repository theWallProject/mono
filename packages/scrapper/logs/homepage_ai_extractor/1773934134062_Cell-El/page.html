Cell-El, Using AI & Biomarkers to Improve Autism Care

A simple blood test, empowering results

Cell-El Therapeutics Ltd. created algorithms using AI technology to analyze blood-based protein biomarkers (proteomics). Cell-El’s test offers objective diagnostic support for children from age 2 with suspected Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).

Cell-El’s test identifies biological patterns of children with ASD differentiating them from their typically developing (TD) peers. The test is being evaluated as a precision medicine tool for guiding immune- and cell-based therapies in ASD.

About us

MISSION

Cell-El’s mission is to identify ASD in infancy and to advance AI‑based precision medicine. This approach may enable personalized therapeutic treatment options and improve outcomes.

About us

PROTEOMICS

Cell-El’s ASD blood test measures protein patterns. This test, together with psychological evaluation, could enable earlier intervention and is being investigated as a tool for monitoring treatment success.

The Cell-El Multi-Center Study

In a multi-center study, Cell-El examined the biomarkers of more than 400 children aged 2–12 years. AI tools were used to distinguish children with ASD from their typically developing peers.

Building on these findings, Cell-El’s study has now expanded to include high-risk infants (with a sibling diagnosed with ASD) from 10 months of age and their mothers. Cell-El’s technology is also being used to monitor children aged 2-18 years with ASD who are undergoing stem-cell treatment. The study has approvals from the Helsinki ethics committees and the Israeli Ministry of Health.

Inclusion Criteria
  • Cohorts of infants and their mothers: Mothers of high-risk infants and infants aged 10-18 months not diagnosed with ASD, but with a sibling diagnosed with ASD according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM)-IV (299.00) or DSM-V (299.00) or
  • Cohorts of children pre and post SCT: Male and female children age 2-18 years old diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM)-IV (299.00) or DSM-V (299.00) undergoing stem cell therapy.
  • For all cohorts: Informed consent signed by the parents
Exclusion Criteria

For all cohorts:

  • Child and/or mother completed treatment with systemic steroids or immune suppressants less than 4 weeks before the screening visit
  • Child and/or mother diagnosed with severe infectious diseases or sepsis over the last 6 months
  • Child and/or mother with hematological or malignant disorder
  • Child with ASD treated for a severe convulsive disorder

For children in the SCT cohort: 

  • No new planned immune-modulating treatment (other than SCT) for at least 6 months before or after planned stem cell transplantation date.
