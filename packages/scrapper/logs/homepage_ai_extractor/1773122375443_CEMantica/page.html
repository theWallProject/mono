End-to-end Customer Experience software from strategy to execution. 
Start with journey mapping as a first step and then manage the outcomes. Whenever you’re ready, connect your VoC customer data directly in your maps. And finally open the ROI module to calculate the financial impact of your CX projects.

Use the power of Artificial Intelligence in Customer Experience software to boost your productivity and support your decision-making. Cemantica´s AI Assistant “Alex” is a helping hand throughout the CX management platform as your additional CX team member. Whether it´s creating and enriching personas, building customer journey maps instantly, or automatically detecting frictions across customer interactions then suggesting solutions to overcome them. Alex is there every step of the way.

With Cemantica you can build solid CX fundamentals and deploy different modules we offer at your own pace as you go and grow.

Are you ready?

Create Personas

When used in journey mapping, personas give valuable insights into the Customer Experience. Start from a customizable Template to define the criteria of your customer personas, add your own blocks and swimlanes and organize / change the colors. Use the Cemantica AI assistant Alex to generate or enrich your persona´s profile.

Define your Personas

Create Journey Maps

Create your journey maps from Cemantica Templates, your own templates or start from scratch. Use AI to speed up journey mapping. Gather customer interactions, surface customer pain points at each Touchpoint, view Sentiment Analysis per Persona then define Opportunities and ideate Solutions to create a Service Blueprint.

Discover CX Journey Mapping

Journey Implementation

From your Opportunities and Solutions, create a detailed CX project plan to manage activities to optimize customer experiences. Prioritize projects using a cost / benefit matrix and align with business goals. Collaborate, track and execute CX projects and connect your own Project Management tools to manage tasks.

Deliver Impact

Data integration

With more than 70 connectors you can easily integrate and connect data in Journey Maps. Collecting and analyzing customer feedback in real-time via VoC data in a map helps compare your perception vs. real customer sentiment at each Stage and Touchpoint to optimize Personas and prioritize fixing frictions.

Data-driven Journeys

Journey ROI and analytics

Measure the financial impact of your CX program and align with business KPIs and objectives. Prioritize and focus on the projects that bring the greatest impact to revenues, profits and customer experiences. Out of the box dashboards can be customized and shared with management to prove ROI of CX.
