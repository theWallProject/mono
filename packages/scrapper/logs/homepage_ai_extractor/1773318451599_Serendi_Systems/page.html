Aloud Book Reading for Kids

Storyteller is an aloud book reading app for kids.
Any print book can be scanned and read aloud to kids by an AI narrator as an audiobook.
The app is based on an intelligent AI engine that turns the book's scanned pages into text and text into speech so it can actually tell any story and speak any book like an audiobook.

Hear Fetal Heartbeat

My Baby's Beat is the first app ever to hear a fetal heartbeat on a mobile device.
The app allows parents-to-be to listen to the fetal sounds in the womb, and to hear the baby's heartbeat using only their mobile device.
It passively picks up sounds from the uterus with the device's microphone, amplifies it, filters it, and makes it available for hearing with human ears.
