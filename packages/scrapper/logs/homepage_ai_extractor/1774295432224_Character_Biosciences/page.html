Transforming Eye Care With Precision Medicine

Character Bio is a precision medicine company unlocking the patterns of progressive polygenic diseases, with an initial focus on macular degeneration.

Age-related macular degeneration (AMD) impacts one in eight people over the age of fifty and is a leading cause of blindness globally.

Our mission is clear: Leverage human genetics to understand what drives disease progression so we can discover and develop targeted therapies to more effectively treat patients.

About Character
THE CHALLENGE

Addressing The Variability Of Polygenic Diseases

Patients who have polygenic diseases like AMD and Glaucoma exhibit different disease manifestations and progress at different rates due to their unique genetic and clinical profiles — but today’s therapies are blunt instruments that take a one-size-fits-all approach, failing to address underlying individual disease drivers.
At Character Bio, we’re leveraging proprietary world-class genomic databases to characterize these patient subtypes and discover genetic drivers of disease progression for more precise drug targeting.

Revolutionizing How Polygenic Eye Diseases Are Treated

Character Bio integrates genomics with longitudinal clinical data, imaging, and AI-enabled biomarker quantification for thousands of patients affected by AMD and POAG, leading us to unprecedented insights into the genetic drivers of disease progression.
This data platform is the foundation for Character Bio’s drug discovery and development pipeline.
Our Approach

Delivering The Promise Of Precision Medicine

By matching individual patients to specific medicines targeting key drivers of their disease, Character Bio can more effectively translate our work into therapies that reach the clinic.
