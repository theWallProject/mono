About us

LabOS delivers a scalable, AI-driven, and tailor-made Laboratory Information System (LIS) designed to streamline operations, ensure regulatory compliance, and maximize ROI for medical laboratories.

LabOS’ patient-centric SaaS platform seamlessly connects every lab department in all clinical fields, offering exceptional support in adapting to evolving workflows and cutting-edge add-on offerings.

For three decades, hundreds of clients worldwide chose LabOS to maximize their growth and achieve streamlined operations through automated, agile, and scalable solutions that drive efficiency and innovation.
