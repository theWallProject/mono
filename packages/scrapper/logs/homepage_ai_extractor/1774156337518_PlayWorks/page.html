Who are We? 

Play.Works develops and publishes games across a network of over 400m televisions via its proprietary technology platform. Play.Works can be found on Sky, LG, Samsung, ROKU, Fire TV, Comcast, Cox, Videotron, Rogers, British Telecom, Vizio, and most major pay TV platforms in North America and the UK

Delivering rich casual games and entertainment to the primary screen in the home, the company continues to grow exponentially and has become a must have addition for major pay TV, CTV and OTT operators. Play.Works’ extensive library of games and video content positions it firmly as the primary player in the explosive CTV market.
