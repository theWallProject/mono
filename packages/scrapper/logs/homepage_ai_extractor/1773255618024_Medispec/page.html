About medispec

Medispec is a pioneer and leading global manufacturer of non-invasive medical devices.

Medispec was the first medical device manufacturer to apply shockwave therapy to sexual health indications for both men and women. Established 30+ years ago to meet doctors’ needs, medispec embeds extraordinary engineering in each and every one of its technologies to provide effective medical treatments around the world – now in 80 countries. Medispec remains committed to high-quality, innovative, and versatile medical device designs. Doctors rely on medispec’s revolutionary solutions across multiple disciplines, including orthopedics, sexual health, and more.

TECHNOLOGY

Medispec Multi-Wave Is a steady, reliable stream of high-energy shockwaves, uniquely designed for optimal non-invasive treatment.

The multi-wave journey begins at a patented, proven reflector, or Ingenious head.  No other shockwave reflector is designed with multiple points of reflection to deliver a powerful set of healing waves in a scattering method, targeted to the treatment area.
