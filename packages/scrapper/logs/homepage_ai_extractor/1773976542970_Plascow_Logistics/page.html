Customs Clearing

Our well-skilled professional and experienced Customs Agent team has more than 88 years of expertise. They provide consultancy to set up customs clearing as smooth and cost-effecient as possible, besides import, export and transit handling, we also organize fiscal representation, inspections, cargo checks, surveys and etc.

[02]
Ocean Freight

Ocean freight is the core of the Plascow Logistics services. We have special partnerships in place with all global and local carriers. Additionally, we provide full reporting services and shipment follow-up.

[03]
Air Freight

For airport-to-airport we rely on the fastest and most qualitative possible airfreight solutions, offering both express and consolidation airfreight  services. If safety precautions are desired,we can offer  an on board courier (OBC) .

[04]
Road Freight

We offer full trucking service for all kinds of cargos within Israel. We are able to coordinate trucking to the Palestinian authority and Jordan as well.

Humanitarian Aid Operation to Gaza – End-to-End Logistics
Plascow Logistics Ltd, in collaboration with Rotem Logistics Ltd, continues to lead complex humanitarian logistics operations to support relief efforts for the civilian population in Gaza. In our latest project, we coordinated two full air charters on behalf of our client, Samaritan's Purse, utilizing two privately owned aircrafts – a Boeing 757 and a Boeing 767 – delivering over 60 tons of ready-to-use supplemental food.
Read more
HIGH-LEVEL LOGISTIC PLANS UNDER WAR
Plascow Logistics Ltd. proudly shares the successful completion of a time-critical logistics project for Plasan Reem, a specialist in civilian vehicle armoring, made possible through close collaboration with our Jordanian partner,we delivered 20 tons of armored glass from Israel to Poland, despite the temporary closure of Israeli airspace in mid-June 2025. The closure stopped the Israeli airfreight industry for over two weeks. During this period, Israel was almost isolated from international air cargo movements, severely impacting supply chains. The shipment was urgently required for protective installation on civilian vehicles in Poland, and the project could not be delayed under any circumstance.
Read more
International Transport of a Komet 2800 HP Industrial Recycling Machine
Plascow Logistics Ltd. has successfully managed the international transport of a Komet 2800 HP industrial recycling machine, a highly performing shredder, from Austria to Israel via the Port of Koper, Slovenia. Measuring 6,803 x 2,504 x 3,130 cm and weighing 29,195 kg, this complex operation underscores Plascow Logistics Ltd.expertise in handling large-scale industrial shipments with precision and efficiency.
