Trusting Utility Data Is Hard. That’s Why Industry Leaders Trust 4M

The Problem

Manual utility research is outdated, unreliable, and inefficient

Time Delays

Hours (even weeks  or months) wasted searching for utility data

Data Gaps

Data is often missing, incomplete, or in inconsistent formats

Team Silos

Teams are disconnected and data is siloed

The Result

Project delays, cost overruns, misalignment, and broken  client trust.

The Solution

Meet 4M—Smarter, Faster, Safer Infrastructure

4M is the first Utility AI Platform that compiles millions of scattered public  utility records into one easy-to-use map. Think of it as a comprehensive utility repository — one platform for all utilities powered by AI.
