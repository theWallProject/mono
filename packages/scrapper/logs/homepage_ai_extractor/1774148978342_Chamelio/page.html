Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get up and running?

Chamelio works out of the box — most teams are live within hours, with no heavy implementation required.

Can Chamelio handle more than contracts?

Yes. Chamelio supports a wide range of legal documents, including policies, playbooks, Q&A docs, and board materials.

Is our data used to train other AI models?

No. Your data is never shared or used to train any external or public models. Your legal knowledge stays yours.

Do I need to manually tag documents for Chamelio to work?

Not at all. Chamelio automatically extracts key terms, clauses, and obligations using its legal-trained AI — no manual setup required.

What if I want to start with just one use case?

You can. Chamelio is modular and flexible — start with redlining, search, or Q&A, and expand as you go.

How is Chamelio priced?

We offer flexible pricing based on your team size and needs. Contact us to receive a tailored quote.

Do you have a Word add-in?

Yes. Chamelio works directly inside Microsoft Word with a seamless sidebar experience.

Do I need to create my playbooks manually?

No. Chamelio automatically builds playbooks based on your previously negotiated contracts and redlines.
