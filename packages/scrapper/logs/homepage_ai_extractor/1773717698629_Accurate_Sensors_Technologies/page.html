Accurate Sensors Technologies

Accurate Sensors Technologies is a global leader in non-contact temperature measurement solutions, delivering precision, reliability, and innovation to the world’s most demanding process industries. Since 1994, we have specialized in advanced technologies for measuring temperature on challenging surfaces—particularly aluminum—with low, unstable, and variable emissivity. Today, our cutting-edge product range includes Infrared Pyrometers, Thermal Imagers, Furnace Monitoring Systems, and Black Body Calibration Furnaces, serving industries such as Steel, Aluminum, Cement, Glass, Non-metals, and a wide array of R&D applications. With a focus on accuracy under complex and fluctuating conditions, we empower our customers to achieve superior process control beyond conventional standards.
