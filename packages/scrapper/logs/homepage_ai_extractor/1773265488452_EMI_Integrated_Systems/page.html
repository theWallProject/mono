About EMI.

EMI offers close-tolerance contract manufacturing to medical device industry, fast and reliable delivery of special purpose machinery, highly-precise machine components, jigs and fixtures mainly for use in the medical device industry.

Our target is to deliver products in the shortest possible lead time and to offer outstanding value for money without ever compromising on quality.

QUALIFIED TEAM. ADVANCED EQUIPMENT.

EMI is trusted by medical, aerospace, and high-tech companies around the world to deliver complex, high precision parts and assemblies (delivery systems, optical parts, body invasive parts, structural parts etc).  Employing the most advanced techniques and equipment, we are committed to manufacturing at superior levels of quality.
