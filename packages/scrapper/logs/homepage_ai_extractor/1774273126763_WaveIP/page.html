Kauffmann Telecommunications, company with over 25 years in the telecommunications market, based in Chile and customers in various countries of the Southern Cone, has adopted as its partner to WaveIP on IP and E1 links for the reliability of their equipment and excellent results .

Our recommendation was to change a network with computers WipAir 3000, which has jumped powered by solar panels and now have 20 Mbps and 8 telephone lines which presented no problem.
