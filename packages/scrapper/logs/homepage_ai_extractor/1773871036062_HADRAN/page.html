Hand Labeling System

Manual Labeler

The manual labeler enables fast and easy sticking of various sized labels with tape width of 20,25,30 mm .

The built in micro controller utilizes a photoelectric sensor to accurately advance the tape and locate the labels in the optimal position under the sticking sponge wheel.

The unit is powered by rechargeable batteries, sufficient for 10 hours of operation between charging to charging.

The operation rate remains stable independent of the battery level.
