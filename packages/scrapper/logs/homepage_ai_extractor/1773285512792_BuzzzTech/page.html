FAQ

Discover more what do you get from the our system

What is BuzzzTech, and what does your system actually do?

BuzzzTech is a smart platform that manages cleaning and maintenance tasks in commercial buildings. It detects issues automatically and directs cleaning staff to the right place at the right time — even before complaints arise.

How does it work on-site?

We install (or you can install) touchpads with a simple interface for cleaning staff, alongside sensors and AI algorithms that track real-world usage. Cleaners receive voice instructions in their native language with clear tasks.

Who manages the system?

Your operations or property team manages it through our dashboard. Our team provides full onboarding, insights, and ongoing support — including performance tracking and optimization.

Do cleaning staff need training to use it?

Not much. The interface is visual, intuitive, and supports voice instructions. It’s designed for users with low literacy or language barriers.

Can the system work with outsourced cleaning contractors?

Yes. You can assign tasks, track completion, and monitor hours per contractor — creating transparency and accountability.
