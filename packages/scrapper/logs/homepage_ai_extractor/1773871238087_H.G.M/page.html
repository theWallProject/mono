About H.G.M

H.G.M is an independent environmental engineering firm with worldwide experience. Ranked among Israel’s most influential environmental engineering companies, over the past 70 years HGM has played a major part in the development of Israel’s groundbreaking water and wastewater infrastructure against a backdrop of perpetual water scarcity.
