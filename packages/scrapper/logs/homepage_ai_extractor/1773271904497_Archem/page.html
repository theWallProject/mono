WELCOME TO THE WORLD OF ARCHEM

Archem is the leading manufacturers' representation firm supplying packaging solutions, raw materials and machinery to Israel's industries (including the Palestinian Autonomy)

Archem is dedicated to delivering reliable, complete, and broad-based solutions to both global manufacturers and local customers. Our commitment to maintaining a supreme standard of service at all times has gained us a distinguished reputation, the trust of our industry partners, and earned us the status of a primary supplier.  


We manage an extensive portfolio of related, yet non-competitive product lines on an exclusive basis. This enables our customers and suppliers to benefit from the synergy created by the representation of multiple and inter-related products.

Archem is proud of its' role and accomplishments in Israel’s fast-growing economy, developing business for international suppliers and customers alike.
