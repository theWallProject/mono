Dan Hotels - Experience the Best

Welcome to Dan Hotels, where we invite you to experience the best of luxury hospitality. Our portfolio includes two members of the prestigious "Leading Hotels of the World" list - The King David Jerusalem, our crown jewel, and Dan Tel Aviv hotel. Other hotels include the Dan Caesarea Resort, three hotels in Tel Aviv, three hotels in Jerusalem, two hotels in Haifa, three hotels in Eilat, the Dan Accadia resort in Herzlia, and hotels in Nazareth and Safed. The Dan Hotels also own 2 hotels outside of Israel, the Momo-Soho hotel in New York city, USA and the Den hotel in Bengaluru, India.

Whether you're visiting for business or pleasure, Dan Hotels is your premier choice in Israel, you will love our full range of luxury guestrooms and suites, backed by our motto: "Experience the Best." We guarantee you warm hospitality and outstanding service. When you join our exclusive free E-DAN Club online membership program, you can earn points redeemable for a great choice of benefits.

Book now and experience the comfort and prestige of Israel's best luxury hotels.
