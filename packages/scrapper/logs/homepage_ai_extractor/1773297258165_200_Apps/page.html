When service gets smart

An advanced platform for smart field technician management
‍
Service.link is a new application developed specifically for field technicians, offering a fast, simple, and efficient solution for all daily needs.
The app enables PC technicians to view service calls, manage part pickups and returns, and use an integrated map that displays an optimal work route - allowing technicians to follow a smart workflow that maximizes the number of calls completed per day.
It's like an Uber for technicians 

The application supports users throughout every stage of the service process - from the initial call to job completion and payment - integrating advanced technology that delivers a more accurate, efficient, and intelligent work experience.

Education. Management. Technology.

A comprehensive system for managing learning and pedagogical content in educational networks.

Israel’s leading school network (according to Ministry of Education metrics) operates over 100 middle and high schools across the country.
At the heart of its technological activity stands an innovative LMS platform, integrating advanced AI services to support students and enable intelligent lesson planning.

The system includes an AI chat assistant for students and an AI-powered bot that helps content developers and teachers. It also features a standalone lesson Builder – a rich and flexible platform for creating lesson plans, exercises, and interactive learning materials.

Built with VueJS, hosted on Google Cloud, and integrated with Ministry of Education systems, the LMS creates a unified, advanced, and efficient technological ecosystem – driving meaningful learning in the field.
