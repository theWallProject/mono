Introducing the Anavi™ procedure

May Health has developed a treatment intended to restore ovulation. Through targeted ovarian ablation, this one-time treatment aims to lower androgen production, which has been shown to restore ovulation using other techniques. The Anavi procedure can be done conveniently in an outpatient setting — no hormones necessary.

NOTE: The Anavi System and procedure are currently limited to investigational use in the United States

More to Come

May Health’s Anavi System is CE Mark certified under EU MDR and is currently enrolling the REBALANCE study in the United States, a pivotal Investigational Device Exemption (IDE) trial designed to support submission to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for marketing authorization.
