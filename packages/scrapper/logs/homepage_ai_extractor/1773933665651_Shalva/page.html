Shalva Events Center

Shalva’s event center is a state-of-the-art facility designed to host a wide range of events, from conferences and seminars to performances and social gatherings. The center features modern amenities and technology, including a spacious auditorium, multiple breakout rooms, and audio-visual equipment. The center is fully accessible for individuals with disabilities, and provides the highest standard of service to ensure a successful and enjoyable event experience.
