Minimize Engineering Overhead

Security teams are using tools that find problems for others to fix.  Backline’s AI team changes that by adding unlimited remediation capacity to all teams. Most vulnerabilities are remediated automatically, reducing security’s reliance on developer time or requiring minimal effort with clear asks from AI for support. The security team gains more control over remediation velocity and SLA performance, while Engineers save valuable time to focus on building products.

Narrow Risk Exposure

The longer a known vulnerability lingers, the more likely it is to be exploited. Backline helps close the window of vulnerability faster: narrowing your attack surface, preserving SLAs, and mitigating risks before they escalate.
