The discovery of three major natural gas fields over the past two decades has turned Israel into a natural gas exporter, bolstered domestic energy security, and supported a significant reduction in emissions. The Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure works with the natural gas industry and neighboring countries in order to develop the East Med's potential as a world-class petroleum province. Israel has three key advantages in developing its exploration and production potential:

Proximity
Israel is located in close proximity to the European market, of major importance in light of the energy crisis and EU policy to diversify gas supply.		   Stability
As a stable, democratic nation, Israel is a good place to do business in these times of growing uncertainty.		   Security
Israel takes the security of its infrastructure very seriously, and energy installations are highly secured by the Israel Defense Forces.
