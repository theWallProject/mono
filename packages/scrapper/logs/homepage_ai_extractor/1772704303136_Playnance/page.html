FAQ
Playnance is a decentralized Web3 ecosystem that merges trading, gaming, and creatordriven experiences. It enables users to participate in real-time markets, prediction pools, and on-chain entertainment while maintaining full ownership and control of their assets.
Traditional gaming and financial platforms lack transparency, ownership, and fair economics. Playnance introduces a decentralized model built on audited smart contracts, real-time payouts, and scalable reward mechanisms - creating a fair, transparent, and aligned ecosystem for users, creators, and partners.
All core mechanics - including payouts, trades, and reward distribution - are executed and verified by audited smart contracts and stored permanently on the blockchain. Price feeds are sourced from independent, bias-free providers, ensuring zero manipulation or conflicts of interest.
Playnance operates with a non-custodial wallet model. Users never deposit funds into the platform - they interact directly from their own wallet, preserving full ownership at all times.
Playnance is designed with compliance-by-design principles, including transparent smartcontract execution and a utility-based architecture aligned with emerging Web3 regulatory frameworks such as MiCA and global guidelines.
Playnance operates on its proprietary blockchain, PlayBlock - a high-performance, gasless chain optimized for real-time transactions, gaming, trading automation, and instant settlement.
Partners receive their own branded platform with a 50/50 revenue-share model, daily automated payouts, access to a growing ecosystem, and the ability to scale through creators, affiliates, and content-driven engagement.
G Coin powers gameplay rewards, trading payouts, partner and affiliate revenue share, ingame promotions, and core ecosystem utilities. A portion of ecosystem revenue supports liquidity and buybacks, reinforcing long-term sustainability and demand.
All payouts - to players, traders, partners, and affiliates - are instant and executed directly on-chain to user wallets. No withdrawal delays, no requests, and no manual approval processes.
Playnance is modular and plug-and-play. Developers can add games, markets, bots, tools, and new experiences with minimal integration friction - enabling rapid expansion across gaming, prediction markets, sports, trading, social, and entertainment verticals.
Playnance uses a transparent, fully on-chain affiliate structure where performance-based rewards are tracked, verified, and distributed automatically. Affiliates and partners earn from activity, not promises - with blockchain transparency ensuring fair and provable outcomes.
Developers, studios, creators, and enterprises can build new products, automate trading, launch games, or integrate custom logic using provided APIs, SDKs, and smart-contract templates.
Yes - the ecosystem is driven by real activity, real transactions, and real value exchange, not speculation. Growth is supported by user engagement, partner revenue, game creation, trading liquidity, and content monetization.
Playnance is the first ecosystem to combine real on-chain trading, real-time entertainment, creator-driven content, and community-powered growth within a single unified infrastructure, all enabled through gasless transactions, instant settlement, and full asset ownership.
Partners can apply through the Playnance Partner Program, which includes technical validation, compliance screening, and commercial alignment for licensing, integrations, and long-term collaboration.
