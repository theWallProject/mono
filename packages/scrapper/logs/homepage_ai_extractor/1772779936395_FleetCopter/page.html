Introducing  FleetCopter!

Video coverage of outdoor events that span across large areas is a challenge. Using stationary cameras is too expensive, not to mention the complex infrastructure involved.

The natural choice is to use quadcopters (drones).

A fleet of commercially-available prosumer drones, controlled by FleetCopter’s cloud-based command and control software providing intelligent, autonomous, and continuous professional-grade video.
