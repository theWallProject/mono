A WORLD LEADER IN DESIGN AND MANUFACTURING OF HMI

Aeromaoz, with over 40 years of experience, is a world leader in designing and manufacturing rugged Human-Machine Interface (HMI) solutions for mission-critical Avionics, land and naval-based platforms. Our systems feature advanced internal light balancing, complete tactile functionality, and full compatibility with night vision imaging systems (NVIS).

As a trusted, high-quality supplier, we serve leading Tier 1 system integrators across military and commercial sectors, including aircraft, helicopter, Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV), combat vehicle and naval system platform manufacturers. Aeromaoz provides both built-to-spec and off-the-shelf products engineered to withstand demanding environments.

Clients across all market segments benefit from our exceptional engineering expertise and extensive hands-on experience. Our precision design process ensures products and subassemblies meet both functional requirements and the most stringent quality standards. With state-of-the-art production facilities, a dedicated team of professionals, rigorous product development processes and quality assurance protocols, we consistently deliver excellence in every project.

As a US Government QPL (AS7788) and AS 9100 certified company, Aeromaoz has established itself as a reliable, high-quality supplier to Tier 1 system integrators, leveraging four decades of industry experience to deliver superior products and solutions.
