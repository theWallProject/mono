Getting vendors to compliance in Jones

Here’s how requesting an updated COI from a non-compliant vendor looks like step-by-step.

STEP 1

Jones Dashboard

Gives you instant visibility into compliance and shows which COIs need your attention.

STEP 2

Chart Drilldown

Gives you a list of problematic (e.g. Non Compliant) COIs to review and address.

STEP 3

Vendor Report

Provides the exact coverage gap that caused non-compliance (e.g. inadequate limits).

STEP 4

Gaps Email

Allows you to trigger an email requesting an updated COI with detailed explanation of gaps.

STEP 5

COI Upload Portal

Allows vendors to easily submit their updated COIs through low-friction, no-login links.

STEP 6

Updated Status

Updates the vendor/tenant compliance status once the new COI has been uploaded.
