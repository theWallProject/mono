Smart Stethoscope

Simple operation

Our smart stethoscope is a fully integrated diagnostic tool that redefines how physicians listen, detect, and decide. At the heart of VoqX is Sanolla’s patented infrasound technology. Unlike traditional stethoscopes, VoqX captures low-frequency body sounds (below 30Hz) that the human ear can’t hear. These infrasound signals are rich in clinical information and offer vital clues to early-stage heart and lung conditions.

Hearing the unheard

VoqX is the ONLY stethoscope that shifts inaudible sounds into the range of human hearing. Leveraging infrasound enables deeper acoustic analysis, allowing detection of subtle, low-frequency sounds that cannot be heard with traditional stethoscopes. This technology enhances diagnostic capabilities, especially for early detection of heart and lung abnormalities.
