Who Are We?

J.Eilon Process Engineering company was founded in 1994 by Jacob Eilon who has over 40 years of experience in chemical engineering. Our company expertise branches into two main areas: Process engineering for the chemical industry and environmental consult for IPPC - integrative Pollution protection control regulation.
