About

WAYNERR® stands for sustainable innovation in the construction and interior design sectors. By merging functionality, aesthetics, and environmental responsibility, the company partners with leading architectural firms and scientific institutions shape the future of interior design.

WAYNERR® leads the way in environmental responsibility. Manufactured from recycled collagen, the panels contribute to waste reduction and CO2 mitigation. With an estimated carbon footprint of nearly 0 kg CO2 equivalent per m², WAYNERR® is among the most eco-friendly solutions on the market.
