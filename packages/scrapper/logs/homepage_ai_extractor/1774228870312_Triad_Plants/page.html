Introducing the Fast & Free Shipping Program

Triad Plants is excited to launch our new Fast & Free Shipping Program—designed to make re-stocking easier, faster, and more affordable than ever.

This program features a curated list of our most popular plants, available with quick turnaround and zero freight charges. Orders ship in as little as four days, making it an ideal option for quick re-stocks or urgent fills. Whether you're ordering a few cases or a full truckload, you'll enjoy competitive plant pricing, fast delivery, and no additional shipping costs.

How It Works:
To place your Fast & Free order:

  1. Log in to the Customer Portal

  2. Click the Fast & Free tab

  3. Select your plants and submit your order

Shipping Schedule:

  • Orders placed by Monday will ship Friday

  • Delivery will arrive as early as Saturday and no later than end-of-day Tuesday

The Fast & Free program was built for speed, reliability, and simplicity—helping you keep your store stocked without overpaying for freight.

Have questions? Reach out to your rep or explore the Fast & Free tab today.
