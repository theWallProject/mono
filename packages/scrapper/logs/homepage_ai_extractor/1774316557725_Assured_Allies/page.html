The urgent, unmet need

Long-term care costs have more than doubled, with 70% of Americans over 55 likely to need substantial care. Meanwhile, the collapse of traditional LTC insurance has left millions of families exposed.

The largest growth opportunity in decades

This historic coverage gap spells opportunity for innovative carriers: two million policies per year remain unsold, creating a massive opening for market leadership. But only if you can manage the risk.

Unlocking sustainable value

Seizing the opportunity requires reimagining LTCI from the ground up. The good news? Assured Allies has spent the past decade and $50M building proprietary technology and behavioral IP—a robust new foundation for successful aging—so you don’t have to.

A win-win for consumers and carriers

When people stay healthy longer, aging costs fall for individuals and insurers. By embedding analytics, behavioral insights, and prevention in every product, Assured Allies creates long-term care solutions that align everyone’s interests and make successful aging possible.
