What rollups have always
needed but never had

Rollups rely on infrastructure that wasn’t made with them in mind—slow settlement, siloed sequencers, and limited composability.

Espresso is purpose-built for rollups, not repurposed from legacy systems, so chains can focus on building user experiences that drive real adoption, not just compete for fragmented liquidity.

The confidence of BFT consensus without sacrificing instant user experiences

Compatibility

How Espresso works

1
A rollup's authorized sequencer(s) sends blocks of transactions to Espresso.
2
Espresso validators confirm blocks in ~6 seconds (subsecond confirmations on roadmap)
3
A protocol-level restriction ensures only Espresso-confirmed blocks will settle on any other L1 (e.g., Ethereum) with a bridge to the rollup.
4
The Espresso-confirmed block is available within seconds, offering a reliable view of that rollup’s final state.
5
Other chains, solver networks, messaging protocols, exchanges, etc., read from Espresso to gain real-time visibility into the state of integrated chains, improving interoperability.

How to work with Espresso

Building on Espresso

Developers of decentralized applications, from general-purpose rollups to app-specific chains, build on Espresso to provide users with speed, security, and defragmented liquidity.

Reading from Espresso

Bridges, crosschain DEXs, messaging protocols and more benefit from the speed and capital efficiency they derive from Espresso's decentralized confirmations.

Running Espresso

If you have experience running node infrastructure, you can support Espresso and the future of crosschain composability.
