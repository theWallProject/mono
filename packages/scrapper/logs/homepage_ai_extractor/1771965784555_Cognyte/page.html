Cognyte is a global leader in investigative analytics software that empowers a variety of government and other organizations with Actionable Intelligence for a Safer World™.

 Hundreds of customers in approximately 100 countries rely on Cognyte’s solutions to accelerate and conduct investigations and derive insights, with which they identify, neutralize and tackle threats to national security and address different forms of criminal and terror activities.

Addressing broad investigative challenges

Investigation

Speed and resolution
Investigative teams require solutions to shorten the investigation cycle and drive high quality conclusive outcomes.

Operational intelligence

Real-time insights
Operational teams seek solutions to generate real-time actionable insights to quickly adapt and complete missions successfully.

Threat intelligence

Detect and mitigate
Investigative and SOC teams require solutions for faster detection and mitigation to drive real-time response to threats.

Empowering security organizations with Actionable Intelligence

30 years of proven market leadership enable us to bring unique proven expertise to the development of our investigative analytics platform and solutions. Our solutions help organizations accelerate investigations by giving teams the tools to find the needles in the haystacks, connect the dots, and resolve investigations quickly and effectively.

Powered by a robust and open analytics platform, we offer a broad portfolio of investigative analytics solutions that address a wide range of security challenges.
