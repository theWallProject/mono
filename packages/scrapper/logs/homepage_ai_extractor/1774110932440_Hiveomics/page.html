* the toolbox

More than algorithms — a complete platform

Nexus is built for real clinical deployment, integration, and governance. Here's what's under the hood:

Workflow Editor

(drag & drop!)

Visual editor for composing AI algorithm pipelines. Build complex processing workflows as directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) without writing a line of code.

2D/3D Medical Viewer

(built right in)

Integrated volume rendering with segmentation overlays, mask toggle, and opacity controls. Review AI findings directly in the platform.

PACS Integration

(plug & play)

Connect to existing infrastructure via DICOM Query/Retrieve (C-FIND, C-MOVE). Seamless integration with your current radiology systems.

On-Site Deployment

(your data stays yours)

Fully containerized and air-gapped capable. Zero external dependencies. Patient data never leaves your facility.

Organization Management

(multi-site ready)

Support for multiple organizations and sites with role-based access control, department-level isolation, and centralized administration.

Audit & Compliance

(every action tracked)

Immutable audit logging with full action history. HIPAA-compliant infrastructure designed for regulatory requirements from the ground up.

* who's it for?

Built for your entire team

Nexus serves every role in the radiology workflow — from clinical practice to research and IT deployment.

Radiologists

Thoracic and oncologic radiologists who need AI-assisted detection and diagnosis with transparent, explainable results.

Key capabilities:

  • Review automated findings with segmentation overlays
  • Malignancy scoring with differential diagnosis (hamartoma, lymph node, TB)
  • Longitudinal nodule tracking with growth analysis and VDT

Department Leadership & CMOs

Leadership requiring governance visibility, quality metrics, and workflow orchestration across the department.

Key capabilities:

  • Concordance registries (algorithm vs. physician decisions)
  • Workflow orchestration and algorithm pipeline management
  • Audit trails for regulatory compliance

IT & Deployment Teams

Technical teams responsible for deploying and maintaining clinical infrastructure within existing hospital systems.

Key capabilities:

  • On-site deployment with Docker (air-gapped capable)
  • PACS integration via DICOM (C-FIND, C-MOVE)
  • Organization management and role-based access

Research & Clinical Teams

Researchers and clinicians who need advanced measurements, radiomic features, and structured data for clinical studies.

Key capabilities:

  • Automated measurements across 117 organ segmentations
  • Radiomic feature extraction (107 features per finding)
  • Clinical study data management and export
