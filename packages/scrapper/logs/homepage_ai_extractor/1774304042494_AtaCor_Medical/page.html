WHY
EXTRAVASCULAR?

Traditional transvenous treatment methods require leads to be placed in the vasculature and heart, compromising the heart for future cardiac care needs.
The longer physicians can leave the heart untouched, the better it is for a patient’s long-term health.
Extravascular solutions place leads outside of the vasculature and heart, maintaining the integrity of the heart while delivering much needed therapy.
DEFIBRILLATION
Life-Saving ICD Benefits...
No Transvenous Leads
Featuring a streamlined parasternal approach, the AtaCor EV-ICD Lead System is being designed to:
  • Deliver reliable defibrillation and antitachycardia pacing (ATP) without entering the heart or vascular system
  • Eliminates complications associated with transvenous leads and reduces the pacing discomfort of other extravascular ICD systems
  • Utilize commercially available ICDs in either a lateral or pectoral placement
