Features

Advanced optimization

  • High grade optimization based on an advanced route optimization algorithm
  • Multi drop routing
  • Multipurpose – supports transports, shipments, and delivery planning
  • Traffic loads based optimization
  • Comprehensive built-in library of routing rules
  • Skill-based assignments
  • Flexible development of custom optimization business rules
  • Time windows constraints
  • Support for various transport pricing methods
  • Configurable optimization target function – by cost/time/distance/custom
  • Real time rideshare (carpooling) optimization – coming soon
  • Asset management – users, transport companies, vehicles, drivers, pricing, orders…
  • Real time optimization based on the current assets status
  • Stations – pickup passengers from a nearby by station/corner  coming soon

Rule Engine

  • The algorithm injects the business rules (constraints) during the optimization processing in order to achieve high quality optimization results and reduce the calculation time
  • Pickup and  delivery time windows
  • Vehicle availabilities and capacities
  • Driver shift times and breaks
  • Max route time
  • Max shipment time
  • Shipment & Vehicle capabilities/requirements (skills) matching
  • Max shipment exception of its direct time (absolute time / percentage)
  • Custom cost calculator
  • Configurable target function: distance/time/price-list/custom
  • A built-in rule engine enabling quick development of custom business rules

Supply Chain Adjusted Optimization

  • Dynamic delivery time windows
  • Dynamic pickup & drop times
  • Driver skills
  • Cargo Requirements
  • Adjusted operational cost models
  • Contractor activation rules
  • Driver shift regulation

Scheduler

  • Complete solution for dynamic scheduling
  • Supports real-time last minute changes as well as long term orders
  • Submit your orders and get a constantly optimized scheduling
  • Transparently handles the re-optimization process of related rides upon submission of new orders
  • Push notification of updated (created/updated/deleted) routes upon order change

Enterprise class

  • Built-in integration to more than 80 systems including ERP, CRM, databases, cloud services, accounting systems and more
  • Available as a fully managed cloud service or as an on-premises deployment
  • Highly available and scalable by design
  • Advanced auditing
  • Ability to apply passenger’s data obfuscation coming soon

API

  • gRPC based over secure HTTP/2
  • Progress notifications – streaming (push) incremental solution improvements (optimized valid result)
  • Native SDKs for: C#, PHP, C++, Node.js, Java, Objective-C, Python, Ruby, Go
  • REST API coming soon
  • Connector SDK for development of custom integrations

About

Being involved in many transportation projects with our transport management products, we were constantly looking for a true optimization solution suitable for complex systems and complicated enterprise workflows. Our experience has been the basis for OptiWays, a true game changer that meets the need of large enterprises seeking for a suitable solution for their complex operational workflows. It took us more than three years to complete the research and release the first version which offered a multipurpose transport optimization solution with an advanced rule engine. However, it wasn’t enough – we realized that in order to offer an enterprise grade solution we need much more: we need to improve the calculation performance in order to support real time and last-minute changes, we need to include built-in integration in order to integrate with the enterprise’s operational systems, and we need smart scheduling engine that transparently handles the re-optimization process of related rides upon submission of new orders. We made it happen and we trust our solution and the algorithm behind it, so we offer several pricing models, including Shared-Savings model in which we offer our solution for free, getting only a small percentage of the proven cost reduction
