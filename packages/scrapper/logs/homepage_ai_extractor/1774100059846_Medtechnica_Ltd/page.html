Partners for life

Medtechnica develops and commits to long-term partnerships across the Israeli healthcare industry – with our partners, as well as with the clinical teams, which use the innovative products we promote. We support our suppliers and customers by learning the goals both sides’ and identifying and meetings their needs, throughout our relationships.

Expert team

With decades of experience in the healthcare industry to our name, at Medtechnica, we pride ourselves on our team’s clinical and technical expertise. Our strong salesforce is highly skilled and trained to maintain Medtechnica’s outstanding relationships with all of our partners and customers. We provide uncompromising support and service.

Comprehensive solutions

One of our key strengths is a unique multidisciplinary approach that combines medical devices from different disciplines into one comprehensive solution, witch gives us our competitive edge.

Financial strength

As a fully-owned subsidiary of Ilex Group(TASE:ILX), our size and reach enable us to devise complete market-leading medical equipment solutions and financial packages that support our clinical teams in Israel, so that they can provide exceptional healthcare to their patients.

We are passionate about providing world-class service experiences

Medtechnica’s highly skilled, trained, and experienced team provides ongoing services and support to our cutting-edge medical equipment users. We work closely with hospitals, HMOs and medical professionals, tailoring healthcare solutions that meet their specific needs.
