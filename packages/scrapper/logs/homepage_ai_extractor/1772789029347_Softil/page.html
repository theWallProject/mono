Products & Solutions

Create better digital communications faster

Softil, formerly RADVISION Technology Business Unit, is the world’s leading provider of enabling technologies for IP communications and offers proven solutions to rapidly add voice, video and messaging services to applications and devices for Enterprise, IMS/VoLTE, and Mission Critical Communication solutions.

BEEHD SDK Framework for Mission Critical Communications

BEEHD SDK Framework for Mission Critical Communications

Softil’s BEEHD is a cross-platform SDK framework designed for chipset vendors, device manufacturers, system integrators, application developers and service providers looking to accelerate development of IP-based mission critical communications solutions for first responders (MCPTT/MCX/MCPTX/FRMCS).

Read More

Multi-Platform VoLTE & RCS

Multi-Platform VoLTE & RCS

Softil BEEHD is a complete VoLTE/RCS based video communications client, allowing service providers, application developers, and device manufacturers to launch customizable IMS compliant VoLTE/RCS services for Android, iOS, Windows, or MAC OS-based devices.

Read More

Video Communication Solutions

Video Communication Solutions

The BEEHD Video Communication Framework is a state-of-the-art standard based VoIP client to add video and audio communication to any product and service. The BEEHD H.323 and SIP client SDK is available for all leading operating systems, enabling a fast time-to-market on all platforms.

Protocol Stacks & Frameworks

Protocol Stacks & Frameworks

Softil formerly RADVISION TBU pioneered the introduction of Voice and Video over IP (V2oIP) with its vast range of developer solutions combining its unique expertise in signalling and multimedia. They are used by chipset vendors, device manufacturers, communications infrastructure, and service providers worldwide, and have helped enable Voice and Video over IP deployments, including VoLTE, ViLTE, and MCPTT/MCX.

Read More

WebRTC Interconnect Solution

WebRTC Interconnect Solution

Softil WebRTC Interconnect Solution was created specifically to address the need to connect WebRTC clients to the enterprise communication solutions which are largely SIP-based. It utilizes best-of-breed protocol stacks and extends them to support required WebRTC transport and media capabilities.
