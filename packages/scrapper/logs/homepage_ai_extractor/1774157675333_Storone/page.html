The Ultimate Storage Solution for AI:
Optimized by Next-Gen Auto-Tiering

Maximizing Performance and Cost Efficiency for Evolving Data Storage Needs

StorONE’s AI – Optimized Auto-Tiering solution is designed to meet the evolving needs of AI systems and beyond. Our platform efficiently moves training data to high-performance All-Flash Arrays for rapid processing, then seamlessly transfers cold data to cost-effective media. This ensures that businesses benefit from both optimal performance and reduced storage costs without compromising immediate access to data. StorONE enables companies to scale AI workloads with precision, avoiding storage bottlenecks while future-proofing their infrastructure for next-generation AI advancements.

Trusted by Companies Worldwide

StorONE Reduces Drive Requirements by 50% with Per-Drive Pricing, Unlocking Exceptional Cost Savings

Get more out of drives while dramatically driving down TCO by maximizing disk capacity and performance for AFA and Hybrid configurations. This unique approach minimizes hardware, boosts efficiency, and cuts costs without sacrificing speed or scalability. Outdated technology in data centers leads to high costs and wasted capacity due to low drive utilization and long rebuild times. Migration to new systems is often complex and costly, with varied cyber capabilities increasing downtime risks and recovery times.

We Think Results

Fast RTO and minimal RPO with no performance degradation, storing 1 million immutable snapshots in a low-cost tier

StorONE ensures fast recovery with the capability to handle over 1 million auto-tiering immutable snapshots per volume for lower storage costs by moving cold snapshots and other cold data to low-cost media, while built-in anomaly detection monitors access to cold data and multi-admin approval protects the data from unauthorized changes.

Unparallelled data protection that addresses new challenges and threats

StorONE's data protection can handle up to a user-configurable number of simultaneous drive failures per volume with ultra-fast rebuilds, even on high-density drives. Our multi-replication ensures data is securely replicated across locations. By fully leveraging modern drive capabilities, StorONE maximizes performance and capacity for optimal data protection efficiency to deal with new challenges.

100% SDS with zero hardware lock-ins, enabling all drives and protocols; allowing hardware changes on the fly

StorONE abstracts hardware similar to virtual machines and SDNs, and offers ultimate flexibility with no hardware dependency, allowing for hardware replacements without migration or vendor lock-in. It seamlessly integrates across on-premises, hybrid, and multi-cloud environments, ensuring effortless scalability and adaptability to any infrastructure needs.

Cloud-like storage simplicity with automated processes and minimal management of fewer components

StorONE simplifies storage management with automated management and maintenance processes. It eliminates the need for complex manual interventions, integrates smoothly, and consolidates multiple storage workloads into a unified platform. This reduces administrative overhead and makes storage management more efficient and straightforward.

Next-Gen Auto-Tiering:
Tier ONE Storage Performance at Tier Two Costs

StorONE’s Optimized Data Placement with advanced auto-tiering stores your cold data without the heavy price tag. You can access the data you need – when you want, how you want – without spending on expensive drives to store cold data.

Our auto-tiering technology is ideal for use cases such as Backup, Archive, Video Surveillance, and PACS for healthcare. By combining the high write performance of SSDs with the ability to store cold and snapshot data on low-cost media, we reduce costs while maintaining maximum performance.

Software-Defined Storage Platform:
Virtual Storage Container™ Technology

StorONE built the storage stack from the ground up over eight years of deep R&D. The technology is 100% software with no hardware dependencies. We offer unmatched flexibility by abstracting both hardware and software as virtual machines and SDN.

Our innovative Virtual Storage Container™ technology works seamlessly with hardware from various vendors and cloud platforms using the same StorONE software. This abstraction allows you to tailor storage solutions for your specific needs, choosing the right media (flash, HDD, or both), protocols (block, file, or object), and deployment locations (on-premises or hybrid/multi-cloud) to meet your application requirements.
