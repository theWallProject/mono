Why PlainID? To scale
business processes

Trust breaks at authorization, especially with AI agents in the mix.

PlainID makes real-time access decisions for humans and AI, enforcing Zero Standing Privileges across apps, APIs, data, and agentic workflows.

To author policies once and enforce them everywhere

Centralized Management, Distributed Enforcement across applications, APIs, data platforms, AI systems, and infrastructure with flexible enforcement patterns close to each runtime system.

To authorize from end-to-end

Discover assets, manage policies, enforce in real time.

To standardize with one policy language

One policy language no matter where your data, identities, or APIs live.

For visibility with a Smart Decision Engine

Every authorization decision is evaluated with fine-grained precision and context. It is then recorded in business-readable language that security teams, auditors, and business owners can actually use.
