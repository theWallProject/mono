NHIDR™
Entro's proprietary detection and response engine continuously monitors AI agents and non-human identities for behavioral anomalies, surfacing real-time threats across your stack.
Idle Secrets
Identify secrets that haven't been used in a while. Entro tracks activity and flags idle or orphaned secrets so you can rotate them before they become hidden risks or compliance gaps.
Ownership Attribution
Every NHI and secret is mapped back to its human owner. The attribution model ensures accountability and fast remediation across security, DevOps, and engineering teams.
Secrets Scanning
See where your secrets are leaking. Entro performs deep, contextual scanning across code, cloud, CI/CD, and SaaS apps to discover exposed secrets and map each one to its source, owner and real risk.
Lineage Mapping
Visualize how AI agents connect to cloud, code, and SaaS tools. Entro maps every MCP, NHI, and secret across resources and environments to expose over-privileged access and risky connections.
NHI Security Posture
The platform continuously assesses every non-human identity’s privileges, usage, and activity to uncover overprivileged, idle, misconfigured or orphaned tokens for easy cleanup and improved posture.
Prioritizing Remediation
Entro flags live risks for investigation. Detect and fix unsanctioned agent deployments, rogue MCP servers, and other unwanted behaviors before they escalate.
