DURAM
Rubber Products

Duram is a rubber manufacturer with over 50 years of experience, specializing in a wide range of technical rubber products. the company has become an important source for the agricultural, electronics, construction, safety, and security industries.

View our products

Highest quality since 1968

Duram Industries is an ISO 9001 approved company using state-of-the-art technology. DURAM™ abides by a philosophy of maintaining close ties with its clients and paying attention to the feedback of its end users in order to continually develop and improve its rubber products line.

We use high quality materials, including Natural rubber, Nitrile (NBR), EPDM, Neoprene, Viton and Silicone. Production is made by modern injection machines and supervised by a fully equipped lab for rubber testing.  We produce parts from rubber compounds that meet international food and drinking water standards. 

Our products are exported to many countries’ worldwide, and are well known for their high quality and long endurance.

DURAM Mask

As DURAM’s subsidiary, DURAM MASK™ is a manufacturer of specialty personal escape masks.
