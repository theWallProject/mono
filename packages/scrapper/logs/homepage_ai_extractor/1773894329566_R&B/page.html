About Us

R&B prides itself on developing leading-edge technological CAMD (Computer-Aided Mold Design) solutions for the Mold & Die industry, consisting of a set of software products allowing engineers to generate complete 3D computer generated mold tool designs.
Our “industry proven” solutions have helped hundreds of organizations achieve their business objectives in a cost effective manner.

Our technologies provide engineers with powerful, intelligent functionality within 3D CAD, unprecedented ease-of-use, at a price affordable for every engineer. Thus providing our customers with a return-on-investment (ROI) as quickly as within 3-6 design projects.
