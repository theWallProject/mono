Powering the world one project at a time

Norway’s first step toward electrified BRT lanes

Electreon will equip Trondheim’s BRT with wireless charging in motion and at stops. Designed for tough terrain and weather, the system cuts battery size, improves efficiency, and supports AtB’s vision for shared electric roads and Norway’s zero-emission transport goals.

Learn more >
View all projects

Charge-as-you-drive on the world’s first large-scale charging highway

Electreon is deploying France’s first wireless Electric Road on the A10. The 1.5 km route features dynamic and stationary charging with next-gen coils, supporting the 2035 freight plan with vehicle testing and demos ahead of a September 2025 launch.

Learn more >
View all projects

Detroit’s wireless charging reaches UPS’s Xos stepvans

Electreon will deploy wireless charging at a UPS depot in Detroit and integrate its tech into an Xos stepvan—part of UPS’s last-mile delivery fleet. The project cuts downtime, lowers costs, and builds on Detroit’s first public wireless EV road.

Learn more >
View all projects

Israel’s first 24/7 wireless charging bus depot

At Electra-Afikim’s depot near Tel Aviv, Electreon’s tech wirelessly charges 23 buses during driver breaks and overnight parking. The system delivers 99% uptime, adds up to 60 km daily range per bus, reduces standby fleet needs, and includes smart remote monitoring software.
