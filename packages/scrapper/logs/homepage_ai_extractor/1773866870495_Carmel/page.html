About Us

Carmel, the commercial arm of the University of Haifa, scouts and evaluates innovative technologies developed by researchers and graduate students, secures intellectual property through patents, leads commercialization and licensing processes with industry partners.
Carmel fosters partnerships with industry, VCs, entrepreneurs, and government agencies to enhance the impact of academic innovation.

 

Vision: to become a central force in advancing innovation by bridging academia and industry.

 

Mission: to create significant economic and social value through commercialization of breakthrough technologies, strategic collaborations with industry, and the transfer of academic knowledge to the global market.

 

Values: Innovation and creativity in academia | Technological and academic excellence | Commitment to creating social and economic value | Transparent and ethical management

AccelUp – University of Haifa

Established in early 2024 in collaboration with the Ministry of Innovation, Science and Technology.
The program aims to accelerate early stage technologies and innovative applicable research projects in order to promote technologies and knowledge transfer from the university to industry. It focuses on identifying, guiding, nurturing, and POC financing of early-stage research projects developed in the University with the potential for commercial realization.
Carmel oversees the program and will lead the commercialization of its outcomes.
The third cycle of the program is now open, inviting submissions from all academic fields across the university.
