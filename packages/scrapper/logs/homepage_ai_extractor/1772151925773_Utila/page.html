Security at the Core

State-of-the-art secure MPC based key management system eliminates all unnecessary counterparty risks, protecting you from threats and mismanagement. Leverage our advanced MPC technology for unparalleled security in storing, issuing, and transferring digital assets.

Flexible Governance Framework

Highly configurable and robust policy engine gives you the freedom to manage your digital assets with granular control. Set roles, rules, limits and policies for every person in your organization. Create approval flows and easily whitelist addresses for streamlined approvals.

Unified Platform

Manage and track all your digital assets on one platform. Get a consolidated view of your digital asset holdings across wallets, exchanges and dApps in a unified dashboard. Track transactions, performance and export vault balances for a complete picture of your portfolio.

Multi Blockchain Support

Wide blockchain coverage including BTC, ETH, SOL, TRX, TON, all major EVM chains and Cosmos chains. Easily integrate your desired EVM compatible blockchain with our Bring Your Own (BYO) EVM feature.

Ecosystem Integrations

Connect to your favorite exchanges and thousands of dApps across any blockchain. Leverage accounting integrations for streamlined financial reporting, integrate with KYC/AML providers for security risk assessments and connect to leading backup and recovery solutions providers.

Robust Developer Offering

Seamlessly integrate Utila API with your existing infrastructure and effortlessly build your own solution on top. No training or expertise needed. Developers can leverage Utila API to create wallets and transactions at scale for various applications and payment flows.

All-in-one institutional MPC Wallet Platform

An out-of-the-box platform that lets you securely store, transfer and take complete control of your digital assets from day one, powered by MPC technology.

  • Intuitive & easy to use

    Onboard and create your account in less than 5 minutes. Effortless wallet setup, transaction management, OOTB reporting and monitoring.

  • Self-service

    Take actions autonomously with our fully self-serve wallet platform. Easily add, integrate, make policy changes, and react to market changes and clients needs on the spot.

  • Effortless Transactions

    Send, receive, and convert digital assets instantly – all within Utila. Transfers and trades can be triggered from your mobile device.

  • Direct On-Off Ramp

    Move funds easily on or off chain, anytime, between your Utila vault and any US bank account. Support for EU & UK coming soon.

  • Self-custodial solution

    With Utila’s Self-custody model, you remain in full control of your private keys. We offer multi-level backup and recovery support, at org and user level.

  • DeFi Connectivity

    Access and interact with any DeFi application seamlessly via our proprietary browser extension or DeFi integrations. Trade, lend, or stake with confidence.

  • Audited & Validated

    We are SOC 2 Type II compliant and our security audits are done by industry-leading blockchain security firms.

  • Track DeFi Positions

    Get an aggregated portfolio view of your DeFi holdings across platforms, track performance, and generate historical reports with our exclusive DeFi positions feature.

  • Integrated Compliance

    Streamline compliance with built-in integrations for AML, KYC, CFT, KYT, on-chain forensics, accounting, auditing and reporting.
