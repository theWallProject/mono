An Industry Leader Since 1959

Gadot has been a pioneer in the Israeli chemical field. Over the years it created a  unique home for management and operation services of the entire industry’s value and supply chain. Gadot was established in 1959 by the Johananoff family and since 2014 has been owned by the Tene Investment Funds. Six decades of activity have established Gadot’s status as a leading cutting-edge company in Israel and Europe (Gadot Belgium, Gadot Germany)
