Emergency war room

An organic, in-house, highly experienced multi-disciplinary cyber crisis management team with over twenty years of experience to handle all crisis vectors under one roof. This team operates with a unique methodology that proves to shorten crisis time and minimize damages.

PR and reputation management Our PR and Reputation teams oversee the entire operation; they will be the ones advising about the correct wording of a media release, the proper wording for the regulation report, building the media matrix during the crisis, providing various solutions for reputation issues during the crisis, briefing and training speakers from the organizations on what and how to deliver messages during crisis, they will operate the PR suppliers and coordinate media reports. They are the gateway and mirror of the organization to the world, and their intervention can sometimes make the difference between success and failure. Our team has extensive experience in cyber crisis reputation management and wide media and communication capabilities.
Cyber BCP recovery BCP recovery capabilities are critical during a cyber crisis. As a cyber crisis is a business continuity issue, knowing what needs to be done first and what needs to be done later is crucial for decision-making. Our Cyber BCP experts will assist in finding the correct compensation for various business dilemmas, helping our teams and the organization's management to prioritize during a crisis.
Privacy and regulation Most organizations will face privacy and regulations issues during a cyber crisis. Providing the organization and its executives with a solid legal defense makes a difference between bankruptcy and legal ramifications to nothing at all in some cases. Our privacy and regulation experts provide that unique approach and knowledge to the organization during the crisis, managing to minimize potential damages and restrictions; their vast knowledge of international regulations and privacy laws, with hands-on experience, provides Code Blue experts the ability to reach these goals.
Comms team Our communication teams coordinate media relations during crises, assist with reputation efforts, and work with management. Our team has extensive experience in cyber crisis media management and wide media and communication capabilities worldwide.
Organizational legal defense team Our legal and forensic experts provide that unique approach and knowledge to the organization during the crisis, managing to minimize potential damages; their vast knowledge of international laws, with hands-on experience, allows Code Blue experts to reach these goals.
IR Team Incident Response & Recovery teams are a crucial vector in the tactical efforts to find the cause of the attack, find the correct forensics, and make sure the organization's network is secure when it is most vulnerable. Code Blue's unique method prioritizes the organization's business recovery and provides not only the investigation teams but also Recovery teams; we start recovering the organization's network during the crisis and not after it – this is how you go back to business fast.
Social media monitoring Looking into the various relevant social media platforms during the crisis allows our teams to monitor the public pulse, allowing our analysis to foresee the reputational damages and possible trends. Being able to interact, to some degree, with the public on social media assists with lowering potential damages.
Financial rehabilitation Financial recovery is the backbone of crisis recovery, as it is a sign that the organization can function properly and return to routine. Code Blue will assist the organization in accessing its essential assets, prioritizing recovery efforts in accordance with the BCP, or adjusting the BCP to cyber crisis restrictions.
Cyber intelligence Gathering sufficient intelligence, analyzing it, and providing insights are significant factors when building the threat actors' profile. It allows the investigation and recovery team to understand better what they are facing and what could be the attacker's next move, and it assists in management decision-making.
Client and investor relations Losing clients or investors due to the cyber crisis might be a significant factor for the organization; the Code Blue communication team will be the connecting link between the management and the clients/investors during the cyber crisis. The team will coordinate efforts with the reputation team leader to deliver the responsible information that will assist in preserving the clients or investors.
Crisis management The crisis manager will be the single point of contact and will manage the operation from start to end, including status with the company’s management. Our executive cyber crisis managers, all ex-IDF special forces with years of experience managing special operations and cyber crisis operations, are certified as crisis managers. This team of crisis managers is responsible for the highly successful cyber operations Code Blue manages year-round.
Strategic advisory Our strategic executive members include our CEO and other experts with vast experience and knowledge in the business and the cyber world. Their unique foresight draws from their expertise in cyber crises within critical infrastructure and state-level organizations for over 25 years. Our strategic advisor will be available for management during a crisis to assist decision-making.
Forensic team Forensic evidence is a significant factor when investigating a cyber-attack. The relevant forensic evidence can prevent the organization and its executives from being negligent, and evidence can be destroyed if not collected properly. Code Blue forensic experts study each piece of evidence and prepare a professional report to support the entire investigation effort.
Cyber negotiation Our cyber negotiation expert will negotiate with the threat actor on behalf of the company to extract information, provide details of the attack, create the attacker’s profile, provide expectation reports, and advise regarding the progress of the overall efforts, which will affect the attacker’s point of view. His experience is with complex negotiations for commercial and state-level organizations.
Proactive defense Securing the network when it is highly vulnerable during a crisis is crucial. Code Blue teams will either use the client’s tools, if any, or deploy their tools to secure and monitor the network during the crisis and the supervised routine phases, alerting the IRR team of any suspicious activity occurring during the investigation/recovery.
Technology experts The technology experts will manage all technology efforts during the crisis, coordinate the forensic and recovery teams, prepare the reports, and provide technology insight to management. Our technology experts are certified CISOs with MSc degrees in Computer Science and extensive background in IT/OT networks and complex systems. Their knowledge assists the team by understanding the mechanism that operates the organization and helps the IRR team with their investigation and recovery efforts during the crisis.
