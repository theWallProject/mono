Entrepreneurship and Betterment

Development and Value-Add Strategies
Realya boasts professional expertise in acquiring and developing international assets, supported by decades of experience and proven execution capabilities. Our process begins with thorough research and precise data collection on the locations we choose to operate in, followed by meticulous evaluations, value-add improvements, and hands-on management of the properties.
The company focuses on acquiring portfolios using its own equity for a predefined period. Only after achieving set goals does Realya consider expanding operations and including additional investors in the same area. Through our U.S.-based operations entity, we establish local management hubs to oversee property acquisition, maintenance, value-add enhancements, and foster relationships with tenants and financial institutions.
Our expertise in the development process, combined with the ability to design a structured and precise cost framework that considers every detail, allows us to make well-informed decisions and maintain optimal control over profitability.

Management and Maintenance

Management and Operations of Income-Producing Properties
Success in real estate hinges on meticulous management and close monitoring of assets. At Realya, we maintain a tight control structure and provide close oversight whenever possible. Our operations begin on the ground, focusing on precise and tactical management that enables us to maintain stability, even during periods of market volatility, shifts in tenant preferences, or economic challenges.
Each property is examined thoroughly, down to the individual unit level, ensuring high standards of construction, value-add improvements, and routine maintenance. Accurate cost assessments for management and operations are central to our strategy. Realya integrates an advanced logistics system to support its properties, providing quick response times and high operational efficiency.
We understand that tenant satisfaction is a key factor in preserving property value. Therefore, we emphasize delivering high-quality, fast, and personalized service to all tenants. This approach not only enhances tenant satisfaction but also strengthens the value of investments, ensuring stability and growth over the long term.
At Realya, we are committed to creating a management and operational framework that adds value for all stakeholders – our investors, tenants, and the communities in which we operate.

Management, Supervision and Financial Control

Financial Management, Oversight, and Monitoring
At Realya, we view our investors as full partners and operate with maximum transparency and reliability. Providing a complete, accurate, and reliable picture is a cornerstone of our operations. We are committed to presenting detailed, comprehensive, and accessible reports on our financial investment performance every quarter.
Our investors benefit from a custom-developed digital portal designed specifically for their convenience. This platform provides detailed insights into investment performance, costs, and fees, as well as return calculations for each fund compared to the original business plan. The information is available at any time and is supplemented by structured and detailed quarterly reports, presented in a clear and user-friendly manner.
Our commitment to transparency and full disclosure enables investors to make informed decisions and stay consistently updated on their investment performance. We believe this approach strengthens the trust between us and our investors, many of whom continue to reinvest with us over time and recommend Realya to their network as a trusted investment partner.

Capital Funds

Equity Funds – A Professional and Tailored Investment Solution
Realya specializes in the establishment, management, and realization of equity funds, offering an efficient and tailored solution for collective investment. These funds operate within a dedicated legal and tax framework, specifically designed to facilitate the acquisition of asset portfolios and manage investments in a professional and focused manner.
Realya’s investment strategy through equity funds provides significant advantages for investors. The funds enable optimal risk diversification through a single investment unit, thereby reducing exposure to individual risks. The funds are managed as American funds, uniquely tailored to Israeli investors, whether private individuals or corporations. Additionally, the funds’ tax structure is carefully designed to prevent double taxation on the earnings of Israeli investors, while also allowing investment via various IRA entities in Israel.
The legal framework of Realya’s funds is clear and accessible, making it user-friendly and suitable for both experienced investors and those taking their first steps in the investment world. Realya’s expertise in establishing and managing equity funds has positioned the company as a provider of dedicated solutions for a wide range of investors, from private individuals to institutional entities and strategic investment sectors.
Realya’s commitment to transparency, stability, and professionalism ensures investors receive a comprehensive and high-quality investment solution tailored to their financial needs and aspirations.
