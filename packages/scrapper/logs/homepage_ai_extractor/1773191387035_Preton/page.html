    What Preton Does

    Preton is an enterprise software company that provides print cost optimization and print security solutions. Preton analyzes print jobs locally on the customer's infrastructure to reduce toner consumption, detect sensitive information, block unauthorized printing, and support regulatory compliance such as GDPR and HIPAA—without requiring internet connectivity or cloud-based processing.

    Core Value Pillars

    Cost Optimization

    Reduce enterprise printing costs through patented toner optimization technology, delivering measurable and proven savings across the print environment.

    Print Security

    Detect and prevent the printing of sensitive information by inspecting print jobs in real time and enforcing security policies at the print stage.

    Compliance

    Support GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, and other regulatory requirements with enforceable print controls, audit-ready logging, and reporting.
