Bankom Security Systems

Bankom Security Systems has developed a system for the protection and management of safety deposit boxes. The system, called SafeGARD, enhances security and reduces the operating costs of existing vaults containing personal safety deposit boxes.

SafeGARD can be used to convert an existing room to self-sevice operation without the need to open any of the safes. The system is operational in dozens of bank branches and in a diamond exchange.
