You’ve heard about Neve.
But… "Is it right for me?"

Neve enables women from less affiliated Jewish backgrounds to explore the breadth and depth of Jewish wisdom.

In a warm, nonjudgmental environment, you’ll discover who you are and what you really want out of life. We’d love to welcome you on your unique journey.

Why choose Neve?

World-Class Education

Study with world-renowned Jewish Educators

Personalized Mentorship

Get personal guidance as you explore your unique path

Life-long Friendships

Find your tribe and build life-time friendships

Caring Community

Tap into a worldwide network of alumni communities

Only in Israel Experiences

Explore Israel from the inside out with unforgettable tours and trips.

Varied Extracurriculars

Work in the organic garden, paint in the art studio, dance on stage, or just hit the gym
