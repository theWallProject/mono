Core Components

Each product combines semi-transparent stimuli in eyeglass lenses with a cloud-based personalization system.

Products are differentiated by stimuli characteristics (size, number, location) and personalization aspects. Personalization includes engine input collection, facial measures, performance tasks, and functioning domain measures.

Our advanced algorithm adapts to individual user profiles, continuously evolving for optimal performance enhancement.
