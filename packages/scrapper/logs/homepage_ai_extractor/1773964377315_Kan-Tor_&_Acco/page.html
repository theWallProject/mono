Who We Are

“Expert Solutions for Global Mobility: At the Heart of Kan-Tor & Acco.”

Established in 2007, Kan-Tor & Acco is a leading corporate immigration law firm based in Ramat Gan, Israel. Our mission is to build strong relationships with our clients and provide exceptional service that exceeds expectations. We place our clients at the center of everything we do and continue to uphold our reputation as a premier provider of immigration services.

With extensive experience in industries including hi-tech and biotech, pharmaceuticals, construction, energy, gas, and transportation, and with close ties to migration advisory bodies in Israel, Kan-Tor & Acco offers innovative and proactive immigration solutions for both international organizations and private individuals.

Our Division

Corporate Immigration Division
We support companies and organizations with all aspects of inbound and outbound corporate immigration, including:

  • Work visas and permits for foreign experts and employees (Israel and the U.S.)
  • Project quota management for companies
  • Corporate immigration compliance and labor law matters
  • Notary and apostille services, including document procurement
Private Immigration Division

We advise and represent individuals and families in all matters of private-based immigration, including:

  • Legal status for high-net-worth individuals
  • Aliyah for individuals of Jewish descent
  • Legal status for spouses of Israeli nationals
Professional Leadership

As a key player in the Israeli Bar Association (Foreign Employee Legislation), the Israel-US Chamber of Commerce, and various government committees on immigration, our partners are active members of professional organizations such as the American Immigration Lawyers Association (AILA), the International Bar Association (IBA), and the International Association of Young Lawyers (AIJA).
