    ABOUT US

    The Groisman Engineering Company provides multidisciplinary consulting and management services. It establishes its position in the top field of project management and delivery of integrated service and design/build projects. 

    Combining highly professional skilled staff with an advanced management techniques, Groisman Engineering Ltd. provides its clients with the most professional service, ensuring that their projects are delivered on time, to budget at the required quality. 

    DOMAINS AND SERVICES

    Groisman Engineering operates in three main engineering fields: Transportation, Energy and Construction.
    The company provides the following main services and more:

    Project Management

    Design Management

    Construction Management

    Schedules & Risk Management

    OUR PROJECTS
