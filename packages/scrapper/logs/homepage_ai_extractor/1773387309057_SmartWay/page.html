What We Do

Smartway offers unique expertise leveraging years of hands-on experience gained from complex development and integration projects for leading companies.

Professional Services

Our extensive experience with innovative and creative technological solutions enables us offer a complete set of services. From architectural design and consulting to integration, implementation, training and support, Smartway guides its clients decision making process through all stages, strategizing, planning, implementing, and setting up performance and maintenance for the complete solution.

Project Management

Smartway has a proven record of managing large scale and complex projects. Our project management office implements a structural project management program that takes care of the complete project life cycle based on best practices, and effectively facilitates streamlining our clients projects time tables with the necessary content as well as cost and resource limitations.

Software Development

Smartway specializes in developing an array of customized software applications that fit together and integrate with client’s organization systems. Our development abilities are cross layers from server side to client side, in either a Microsoft or Android environment per customer need.

Outsourcing and Personnel Placement

Smartway offers support for its clients by providing professional assistance either on a regular basis or during peaks. Our practical knowledge and experience enables us to recruit, allocate and place the most suitable experts for a position, ensuring customer satisfaction. All our personnel are highly skilled in their area of expertise and receive any necessary guidance and training from Smartway before being stationed at the client’s site.
