a little about our misson

RECONNECTING GENERATIONS

Grandparents are a family asset. But as they age, they don’t always manage to keep up with communication technology. My Grandma Bela, for example, found it physically difficult to operate touch screens. She also got confused by the multiple options, icons, and modern backgrounds that allure younger smartphone consumers.

Things that are intuitive to most technology users today are baffling or nearly impossible to many older adults. This makes them feel frustrated, lonely, and even left behind. The need to improve communications with the elderly takes on added urgency in times of trouble, such as the COVID-19 pandemic affecting the world in the early 2020's.

Our challenge is to help them reconnect with junior generations while keeping active and updated. We offer senior-friendly software that helps older adults keep in contact in a simple, direct, and independent way. Our products’ immediate benefits have a great impact on their overall well-being and self-esteem by empowering them and reducing their loneliness.

We believe in doing well by doing good, and we are proud to make communication technology accessible to elderly youngsters at heart.
