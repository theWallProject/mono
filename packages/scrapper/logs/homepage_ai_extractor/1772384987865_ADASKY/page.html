Our Solutions

Our Thermal Camera Technology

Our advanced thermal camera delivers unparalleled visibility, detection, and safety across multiple industries. With proprietary LWIR technology, automotive-grade reliability, and the industry's smallest form factor, it seamlessly integrates into both infrastructure and vehicle-based applications. Whether enhancing intelligent transportation systems or elevating automotive safety, our shutterless, high-performance thermal solution sets a new standard for all-weather vision.

Intelligent Infrastructure: Smarter, Safer Roadways

Our thermal camera transforms traffic management by providing real-time detection of pedestrians, vehicles, and obstacles, ensuring uninterrupted visibility in complete darkness, fog, glare, and other challenging conditions. Integrated into V2I (Vehicle-to-Infrastructure) networks, it enhances situational awareness, optimizes traffic flow, and reduces collisions. With a compact, low-power design, it’s the perfect solution for smart city deployments, connected corridors, and highway safety systems.

Automotive: The Future of Vehicle Safety

Designed for automotive-grade reliability, our thermal camera enhances both active and passive safety in V2V (Vehicle-to-Vehicle) communication and ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems). It provides early hazard detection beyond the reach of standard sensors, significantly improving pedestrian, cyclist, and wildlife detection. With FMVSS 127 compliance and seamless integration into existing sensor suites, it bolsters automated emergency braking (AEB), driver assistance, and night vision systems, ensuring safer mobility for all road users.

Revolutionizing thermal sensing technology for safer mobility

Our mission is to create life-saving technologies that will help people get to where they are going safely. Whether driving a car, crossing a street, or entering a public place, ADASKY’s thermal sensing systems pave a safe path by seeing what our eyes and other sensors cannot.

ADASKY, the leading developer and manufacturer of intelligent thermal sensing technologies, was founded in 2016 in Northern Israel by a team of industry experts spanning across a wide range of engineering backgrounds.

The company was established with the purpose of solving the problem of perception gaps left by other sensors in automated vehicles by way of thermal cameras, making cars function better and safer to drive 24/7, in all weather conditions, enabling all levels of automation. As the company evolved, ADASKY began taking on a more holistic approach to road safety, leading it to create a thermal smart city solution to safeguard all road users and complement it’s in-vehicle systems. The emergence of COVID-19 and the disruption to the freedom of movement that followed, prompted the team to find new ways to repurpose its powerful technologies to help mitigate the impact of the pandemic. By adapting its auto-grade thermal camera, ADASKY built a new sensor that alerts when detecting elevated body temperatures of individuals in a crowd, making public places safer, helping to bring life back to normal.

In just a few years, ADASKY revolutionized the thermal sensing industry by creating end-to-end, the smallest, most intelligent, energy-efficient auto-grade camera systems in the market, at a price that anyone can afford, making safety accessible to all, for safe travel and a safe return.
