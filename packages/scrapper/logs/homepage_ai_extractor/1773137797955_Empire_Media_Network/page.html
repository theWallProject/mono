Empire Media Network LTD.

Empire Media’s family of brands brings a user centric approach to the universe of financial and health websites and mobile apps. We make every effort to explain, contextualize and inform our readers about the complex subjects of finance and health.

Launched in 2011, FX Empire is a leading global financial news portal, delivering up-to-date market news and analysis, streaming quotes and charts, technical data and financial tools tailored for the financial markets.

FX Empire aims to provide well-researched, insightful news, data, and content that in order to empower our readers in making financial decisions. On top of the daily news and live market data, we take pride in our review sections covering CFD and Stock brokers, as well as Crypto exchanges and Wallets.
