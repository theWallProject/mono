Ground Water Modeling
Argus ONE GIS supports the largest number of ground water models in the market. The USGS develops and distributes Argus ONE intrefaces for its most commonly used groundwater models.

Surface Water Modeling
Argus ONE is used around the world for surface water and sediment transport modeling. Leading surface water model RiverFLO-2D is available to Argus ONE users

Land Use Analysis
Land use analysis with unlimited number of criteria can be easily conducted. Weights of criteria can be changed on-line to allow for dynamic sensitivity analysis and for considering different design goals.

Environmental Management and Design
