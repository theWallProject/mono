Cocoa Cooperation and Purchasing Manager

“This expansion in our relationship with Acclym is a critical next step in our ongoing mission to ensure sustainable cocoa systems to the cooperatives and farmers. Acclym’s technology gives us more visibility into growers’ sustainability practices and how our cocoa is being produced. This helps us improve EUDR compliance, but also enhance collaboration with growers for stronger sustainable outcomes”

Nestle

4C Traceability Manager

“Acclym’s system was implemented in 2021 to improve traceability and assessment for 4C. Once implemented, manual records weren’t necessary as the system is efficient and safe. Using Acclym made it easier to generate traceability information, such as the number of bags delivered per month, per producer, per trader. In audits carried out by 4C certifiers, coffee traceability done by Acclym is always praised. I would like to thank Acclym’s team for their commitment and dedication to our various demands. The team is always polite and willing to help. Thank you for the partnership.”

Alejandro Penagos Muñeton

Agricultural planner

“At McCain we are adopting the digital culture to monitor potato crops, Acclym is helping us take information storage to the next level to make decisions, allowing us to view data in real time, which will additionally allow us to increase the efficiency of the team. Acclym allows us to centralize information, view maps and photographic records, view georeferenced data, and view reports interactively. It is a platform that has all the tools to support the evolution towards digital agriculture.”
