STEM CELL MEDICINE LTD.

Stem Cell Medicine is a biotechnology company that develops second generation cell therapy products as stand-alone treatments or in combination with pharmaceuticals, with a focus on neurological indications, including multiple sclerosis, pain and neuromuscular injuries. The company also manages the production, registration and marketing of such products.
