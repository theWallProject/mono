    Both. SIRKL is available on iOS, Android and Web App. Try it now!

  • Do I need to install a wallet to connect?

    Not necessarily, you can also use the fast login and we will create a background wallet into the app for your use in chat. Use it only for 1 time or Keep it forever with your 12 passkey words or your mail.

  • What decentralized technology is used?

    We are using the most advanced protocol for secure and decentralized communication. More information in the whitepaper. https://docs.sirkl.io

  • Am I secure on SIRKL?

    Decentralized protocol is the best way to avoid the risk of attack on centralized server that traditional messaging app are using. Also the end-to-end encryption in Matrix is based on the Olm and Megolm cryptographic ratchets and provide the highest level of security in the market.

  • Why is it different from WhatsApp, Telegram or WeChat?

    First traditional messaging apps are using centralized servers. Second, on thoses you connect providing some personal information. On SIRKL, you just connect with your wallet.
