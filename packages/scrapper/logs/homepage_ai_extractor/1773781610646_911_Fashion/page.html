Nice Copy about our 20 years Heritage

drag to continue
1999

Uri Reiss started the business while traveling in Brazil, south America as he understood the abundance of international fashion is far from reach to the Israeli market.

2001

Official establishment of the company as a footwear wholesale distribution with the first showroom sales campaign.

2001–2007

Assemble of unique brands with incredible story behind them.

2007

Gidi Federmann joined the company as Chairman of Board, he serves as “Dan” Hotel chain’s Chairman, manager at Federmann Enterprises and director at Freiberger compound materials GMBH.

2008

1st  :story store opened in Shenkin st., Tel-Aviv hipster neighborhood and with it came alive the concept of a new premium multi-brand store in Israel.

2008 H²

Quickly arrived :story #2, in Dizengoff st., Tel-Aviv, another great neighborhood, which officially meant it’s a chain.

2009–2010

Ramat Aviv Mall, the no.1 prestige shopping center in Israel opened it’s doors to the 4th :story location, followed by Givatayim Mall.

2011

E-commerce joined the party, now established to be one of the leading multi-brand fashion and lifestyle platforms, offering over 100 brands, exclusive collections and high-tier collaborations.

2011–2019

A total of 12 :story stores, over than 20 local designer collaborations, wining every year the award to “BEST STREET STORE” from Time-Out Magazine.

2019

1st Mono-brand store for the company – Scotch & Soda, the Dutch brand has performed successfully in :story multi-brands leading to it’s own standalone.

2021
  • 1st AllSaints mono-brand store – the British cult brand is represented by the company since 2020 and quickly showcased demand for its full store.
  • Second :story – a second-hand department was born within :story multi-brand, answering the demands of our guests, as part of our “WE GIVE A DAMN” campaign, featuring our sales associates and team members.
2022

Private Equity firm “Green Lantern” has showed it’s belief in the company and joined forces with Uri Reiss and Gidi Federmann. The firm was founded by ex-McKinsey & co. consultants in 2014 and specializes in mid-market consumer segment, while managing today over $500M in capital.

2023
  • 1st alo mono-brand, which quickly was followed by the 2nd and 3rd all in one year! The L.A. wellness, fashion and lifestyle brand was received amazingly in the Israeli market, reaching phenomenal global sales records.
  • 1st step by :story – footwear concept store was designed.
  • 3rd Scotch & Soda mono-brand.
2024
  • 4th alo yoga store
  • 2nd AllSaints
  • 24th :story location
  • And more to come…

NATIONWIDE DISTRIBUTION OF FASHION & LIFESTYLE BRANDS

read more
close

911 FASHION specializes in nationwide wholesale of fashion and lifestyle brands. The company hosts a year-round showroom that is attended by all top accounts in every prime location in Israel.​​The company has a highly known and experienced in-house design and creative team; that adapts the look & feel to the local market, while maintaining the brand's DNA. Each brand has its own dedicated space with a full collection representation.​

The company sells to over 1,000 points of sales in Israel. Among its customers are the best fashion and lifestyle stores, boutiques and multi-brand stores from all over the country.

911 FASHION operates a self-owned retail chain, under the name :story since 2008.
​​:story is a curation of over 80 fashion, beauty and lifestyle brands, customized and combined to offer the BEST retail experience to our loyal and greatest customers. ​

Each store has a distinctive service model including fashion consultants and personal shoppers, that advise and educate on the story behind each brand.​

The chain is highly regarded for its commitment to excellence in terms of quality, design and customer service. The multi-brands are known to be a space where fashion, people and planet coexist and inspire individuals.

911 Fashion owns and operates 30 multi-brand shops, under the name :story. Altogether, there are 80 brands sold in :story, 60 of which story exclusively represents in Israel. :story is highly regarded for its commitment to excellence in terms of quality, design and customer service. Story's platform is often used in order to bring light on new brands and new products.

:story always strives forward with dedication to distinction and attention to detail regarding quality, design and customer experience. :story’s platform specializes in building the best foundation to launch new brands, initiatives and product innovations. Our stores are highly known to be the destination where ones come to seek inspiration and discover the upcoming advanced collaborations.

As part of the company's agenda to represent the finest fashion & lifestyle brands out there, a couple exclusive stores offer carefully selected premium brands, a few to mention are A.P.C, Filippa K, Saturdays NYC, Vagabond Atelier and more.

7 { our values

WE MAKE SURE EVERYTHING WE DO & EVERY BRAND WE REPRESENT IS ALIGNED WITH OUR VALUES:​

drag to continue
our messages

We are 100% committed to our community, therefore we represent only the BEST brands out there and do everything we canto make sure we leave the world better than we found it, because – WE GIVE A DAMN.

‍

our brands

911 Fashion's team travels the world to find firsthand stories that just must be told.
Each and every brand in the portfolio has a unique and distinct DNA, agenda, purpose and mission.

We are highly proud (and modest) to be able to say – WE REPRESENT THE BEST BRANDS IN THE WORLD.

BODY IMAGE​

:story is proud to be a home for pioneering brands doing their best to promote healthy body image and inclusivity for all. To name a few: Understatement Underwear, Girlfriend Collective and many more.

OUR PROJECTS

Border :story is a collection that was born after the 7th of October, the morning all borders were crossed.

A collaboration with local Israeli designers from the Gaza envelope which expresses their experience.

Revenues were donated to Advocacy organizations.

HER:story

Her:story is a collaboration with “HerAcademy”, a non-profit organization that facilitates a safe, empowering and accessible learning space, suited for woman exiting the prostitution and abuse cycle.

The collection is devoted to groundbreaking feminist activists women that changed our history.

Revenues were donated to the foundation.

NUDIE REPAIR

:story offers free repairs and alterations as we believe in fixing anything we can.

‍

SECOND STORY

:story is known to be a pioneer in sustainable fashion. Proud to be the first retail chain to offer second hand in our stores, since 2021. We called this project “SEcoND :story” and it was received wonderfully by our community.

Also, :story is the only multi-brand in Israel to receive the Vegan Friendly certificate.

COMMUNITY

911 FASHION is honored to take part of the "Greenhouse" scheme that promotes up and coming Israeli designers. Thanks to this initiative, new designers receive a financial grand and business guidance to make their dreama professional reality.

​​:story's platform sheds light on these interesting new creatives and assists by utilizing its well-known hub.​
