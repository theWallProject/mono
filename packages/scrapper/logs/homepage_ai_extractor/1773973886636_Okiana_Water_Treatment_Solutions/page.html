About Okiana

Okiana is a private end-to-end water treatment solution expert. We deliver over 30 years of experience in developing and delivering global customized water treatment systems and solutions that stand the test of time. We serve multiple markets. From industrial, mining, municipal, farming and agriculture to hotels, resorts and other services. Every single one of our projects is led by a senior decision maker which enables us to implement every project with the highest efficiency, experience and commitment.
