Electro Magnetic Navigation Sensor

Benatav's magnetic navigation sensor and mini coils are a critical part of the Cardiac ablation catheter. "Cardiac ablation" or "catheter ablation", is a mini-invasive treatment for cardiac arrhythmia that neutralizes the abnormal electrical activity that causes the irregular heartbeat. Most patients who undergo cardiac ablation experience a long-term reduction in the number of episodes of arrhythmia and the severity of symptoms

 

Electro Magnetic Navigation Sensor for Ear Nose and Throat Procedures

This sensor is an electromagnetically-navigated and suction device which inserted into the body for ENT (Ear -Nose- Throat) medical procedures. Benatav's magnetic sensor, assembled from our mini coils, is embedded at-the-tip of the device enabling advance navigation capabilities during the medical procedure and the Suction operation itself

 

Cryotherapy Ablation Heater Coil

Benatav's coil is a critical component in the cryoablation systems and needles for treating kidney, prostate and other types of cancer. This system provides minimally invasive temperature-based therapies for treatment of both benign and malignant diseases of the prostate and other urological diseases such as kidney tumors. Benatav's minimization advanced technology ensures success in this complex medical procedure.

Our Technology

Benatav designs and produces technologies that utilize ultra-fine insulated wires to manufacture micro coils and sensors for the medical field. Our products are based on in-house developments of an array of techniques for handling insulated copper wire at any diameter, down to the finest serially manufactured size of 59 AWG (9 microns). One of Benatav’s developments is a fine wire connectivity technology that enables the connection of ultra-fine insulated wires of any serially manufactured diameter, down to 0.009 mm, to other ultra-fine wires or to wires up to 5 times as thick.

About Benatav

Since its establishment over 40 years ago, Benatav’s activities have centered around winding coils, medical sensor assembly, and treatment of conductive fine wires. The company specializes in designing and manufacturing solutions tailored to fit clients’ needs, employing advanced in-house technologies to support production, thereby meeting the most complex technical requirements.

The company excels in designing and manufacturing coils for the solar industry, micro coils, sensor assembly, and using innovative technologies to connect ultra-fine wires for the medical device industry. We also offer special solutions for the traditional coil industry.
