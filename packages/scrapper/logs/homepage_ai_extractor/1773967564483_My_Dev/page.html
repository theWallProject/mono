KYC

Know Your Customer (KYC) has become a real challenge for Fintech companies. Regulation requirement is evolving frequently and differently across countries. Our experience is based on several projects where our team was deeply involved in third party validation integration together with the development of highly customized modules for hybrid implementation using internal and external validation.

Learn more

PCI DSS

PCI security standards are composed of technical and operational requirements set by the Payment Card Industry Security Standards Council to protect cardholder data. We focus on the technical aspects of the PCI DSS and we are masters in designing product architecture together with high security level of coding in order to make the certifications as seamless as possible. Our DevOps team specializes in deployment in cloud environment which meet the highest level of security and which are conformed to the most constraining PCI requirements.
