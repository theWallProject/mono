02 // Industry Recognition

Projects
awards

Toya was founded by female executives from the gaming and media industries, which were inspired to create diverse multiplayer media products for girls and boys, from the tender age of <4 through +18. The company achievements in innovation were recognized by platforms and brands alike, and includes:

  • First pro-studio to launch experience on Minecraft marketplace, in partnership with Microsoft
  • First branded media IP hit on Roblox (Miraculous Ladybug RP, 2021)
  • First teleportation brand activation (Netflix, 2021)
  • First to co-develop a branded Roblox + Fortnite concurrent activation (Dove, 2023)
  • First to introduce proprietary Deep Learning to Roblox brand analytics (2023)

Our teams are based in Europe and North America, and are using a mature game development pipeline which merges the high quality of a pro-studio with the fast iterations and community-first approach of a UGC creator
