Why Efcom?

MULTIDISCIPLINARY design-house


Efcom is not your typical run-of-the-mill software & hardware development company! We develop and integrate complex multidisciplinary solutions where the need might be known, but not the path to address it; where requirements are so stringent, they rarely make sense to ordinary thinkers; where accuracy, reliability and durability are so important, lives actually depend on them; where if not for our integrity and confidence, some of the most sensitive industries would not risk employing us.

Companies in a variety of industries reach out to Efcom when they require a high-level of creativity and support in the development of unique solutions. often in challenging environments and with constrained resources.

Our process begins with researching the need, and continues with establishing a feasibility test, building a prototype, manufacturing, supporting and maintaining.

Throughout the various stages of the process we maintain close contact with our customers from consultation, through product characterization and design, development, and Turnkey deployment.

what we do

SOFTWARE & HARDWARE SOLUTIONS

Efcom relies on a high level of creativity, on the extensive and diverse background
of its team members and on sound methodology that boasts over a hundred satisfied customers,
to deliver a wide range of top-quality software-based solutions and their integration with hardware,
within competitive time frames.

TURN-KEY PROJECTS

Owing to a diverse set of disciplines and experiences that characterize its team, Efcom is able to deploy
Turnkey projects that cover all software and hardware development and integration. The company is
often approached at the earliest stages of a project to design a creative, end-to-end and future-proof
solution from use-case analysis, through design planning, detail design, development and QA. Efcom
approaches its clients as true partners, and has demonstrated sound reliability, confidentiality and
commitment.

RAPID DEVELOPMENT

Undaunted by time limits, EfCom has immense experience developing pressing projects: POC development, demo systems, systems for exhibition, etc.
Similarly great experience in urgently eliminating bugs and adding critical features to existing systems, accelerating release to market.
Our software engineers work around the clock to ensure on-time delivery and results to satisfy the most demanding of clients.

ALGORITHM DEVELOPMENT

EfCom optimizes existing algorithms and develops new algorithms for real-time systems, maintaining strict performance standards. We have proven experience in algorithm development and implementation for commercial use. EfCom does advanced work in image/signal processing (for real-time platforms or PC based systems), machine vision, visual inspection system and statistical algorithms.

PROOF OF CONCEPT

EfCom gained vast experience and numerous tools for taking for bringing customers ideas to live. We assist in the initial brainstorming, characterize the needs, understand the market requirements, provide the technical solutions and help the customer to create his product.

CONSULTING & SYSTEM ENGINEERING

System Engineering enables the realization of successful systems and overlaps with both technical and human-centered disciplines such as control engineering and project management. EfCom uses Systems Engineering methods to manage demanding software engineering projects. Gurus in numerous areas, from urgent bug elimination and patches to effective architectural design and system engineering, EfCom is experienced in defining demands, analyzing use cases, performing risk analysis, and many other procedures crucial to software engineering.
