    About US

    Hi, we're MSBit, a software development company that provides custom tailored web, mobile, and AI solutions to meet the needs of its clients. Founded in 2008, MSBit has been dedicated to delivering top-notch software solutions for over a decade. With its headquarters located in Israel, the company is comprised of over 50 talented software engineers who are passionate about delivering the best possible results for its clients.

    ​

    At MSBit, we believe in utilizing the latest technologies to create innovative and cutting-edge software solutions. Our team is well-versed in a range of technologies including  .net, NodeJS, React, Angular, 
    Flutter and the latest AI / ML solutions ensuring that we are able to provide our clients with
    a comprehensive suite of services to meet their specific needs. Whether you're looking to develop a new web application or want to enhance your existing mobile app / web platform with the latest AI technology, We're here to help!

    ​

    Our team works closely with clients to understand their unique needs and requirements, and then leverages its expertise to create solutions that are both effective and efficient. With a focus on delivering results that drive business success, we've earned a reputation as a trusted and reliable software development partner.

    ​

    Whether you're looking to build a new product from scratch or enhance an existing one, we're here to assist.
