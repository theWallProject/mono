Solution Components

Smart Centralized Software

Our software enhances operational efficiency through intelligent algorithms, eliminating the dependency on various types and quantities of robots.

Workstations

Our workstations, tailored for eCommerce, retail, and omnichannel operations, maximize efficiency and can be customized for specific warehouse needs.

Robots

Caja uses two types of robots for vertical & horizontal optimizati. They optimize storage, picking, and transport, reaching up to 5.3m (17ft), and collaborate to efficiently fulfill orders.

Omnichannel Operation

eCommerce

The eCommerce station is used mainly for unit picking, when the forecast of the SKU is low for all open orders. This is common for eCommerce operations.

Retail

The Retail Station is used for bulk picking. This type of workstation handles highly demanded SKUs, which are brought by the robots that leave them in the station until they are no longer needed.

Hybrid

The Hybrid Station is used for a combination of high/medium/slow runners doing both unit and bulk picking. At this unique picking station, both types of robots bring bins and participate in the outbound process. This picking station is especially helpful for omnichannel operations.

Cutting-edge cloud-based software system

Cloud-based-software

Our software controls the entire operation and is based in the cloud. This allows scalable computational power and better control of the automated warehouse.

Swarm management

Our fleet management is handled by advanced algorithms. A unique 4D algorithm determines the fasted route in real-time.

Advanced Algorithm

Advanced trend algorithms optimize inventory and ordering activity, along with their priority and grouping. Unique navigation management is enabled by machine learning and AI.

WMS Integration

Caja’s software is ready to be integrated with any WMS system by using simple, user-friendly APIs.

Caja robotic systems can be installed in almost any warehouse since it adapts to the existing structure and infrastructure (e.g. different ceiling height, existing floor, and pillars). Caja’s system also easily integrates with the customer’s Warehouse Management System (WMS).

Our eGrocery solution supports FEFO/FIFO and adapts to different storage types and temperatures. Integratable with a pre-installed WMS, it implements fast and accurate picking processes and bin consolidation. With this solution you can support multi SKU bins, expand your warehouse with ease and add robots during peaks.

Built for multiple companies in one warehouse, our 3PL solution supports different SLAs for each company. It promotes efficient space utilization and makes returns easy and simple. With this solution you’ll enjoy fast replenishment and accurate picking processes as well as easy warehouse expansion.

Caja’s AI-powered warehouse automation

A large part of warehouse automation is the ability to configure hundreds of algorithms in parallel, we use AI to make the tough decisions and accurately calculate the machines’ next move.

Caja’s system learns its clients’ warehouse operations from end-to-end, including inbound and order fulfillment, inventory and much more, and uses AI to best route the robots, optimize traffic, orders and tasks, and bring the warehouse to its maximum efficiency.
