  • Hawk

    Hawk is a unique on demand Pharma Sales Force effectiveness tool, specifically designed to give your Sales Force the freedom to become more efficient, more dynamic ... more productive. Designed "bottom-up“ with the Pharma Medical Reps' needs in mind, Hawk enables and encourages the individual sales rep to increase productivity and effectiveness in the field.

  • Falconer

    REPtor enables Sales Management total online transparency of ﬁeld force operations, effectiveness, and productivity. It provides a dynamic interface between the Sales Force in the ﬁeld and Management, giving Management full hands-on control of reps' ﬁeld activities.
    Falconer allows the use of standard or customized reports to gain valuable, up-to-the-minute insight into critical issues such as detail calls, rep and team performance, and more. Falconer can export data to Microsoft Excel for additional analysis or integration with external information.

  • CLM

    Reptor CLM (Close Loop Marketing) is the integrated CRM and CLM solution designed specifically for the pharmaceutical industry:
    * Helps medical reps improve physician response rates and build lasting customer relationship interactions
    * Gives Management the power to evaluate sales and marketing efforts in real-time
    * Allows medical reps to capture important data early in the process
