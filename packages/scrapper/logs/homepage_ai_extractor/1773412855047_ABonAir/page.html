The best choice for Wireless Live Video productions: Sports, Live Events, News, Houses of Worship and Cinema

Wireless Live Video for Sports

Closed Arenas, Open Stadiums & Open Fields

Sports productions happen everywhere — from indoor arenas and open-air stadiums to vast outdoor fields. These events are dynamic, fast-paced, and filled with action that fans expect to experience from every possible angle, in real time and in broadcast-grade quality.
However, each venue type presents unique RF and operational challenges for wireless transmission — signal reflection in closed arenas, long-distance coverage in open stadiums, and unpredictable conditions in open fields.
The AB4000 and A612 systems are designed to overcome these challenges, using the multi-Zone FCE technology.

Wireless Live video for News Gathering

OB Vans, DSNG and DENG Vans

Bringing news to viewers as it happens requires instant live transmission, rapid setup and plug-n-play operation in the most unpredictable conditions.
Whether you’re working from an OB VAN, a DSNG, or a DENG unit , every second counts and mobility is essential. To capture breaking stories from every angle, camera crews must be free to move without the limitations of cables.
The AB4000 and AB612 systems are designed specifically for these scenarios. Featuring bi-directional communication, automatic link optimization, and built-in IP data channels, our systems ensure seamless connectivity and broadcast-quality video from field to studio.

Wireless Live video for Live Event Coverage

Stadium Concerts 

An open-stadium concert is a thrilling experience — not only for the audience on-site but also for viewers watching live broadcasts at home. Large screens inside the venue, or outside near the crowd, bring every moment to life — from the main stage to behind the scenes.
To meet these expectations, broadcasters must deliver crystal-clear video with perfect lip-sync and close to zero visiable delay. In live street events, such as parades or city celebrations, wireless systetms are essential, yet they often face limitations caused by changing environments, distance, and interference.
The AB4000 and AB612 systems overcome these challenges with its uninque system topologyy and groundbreaking end-to-end low delay technology – ensuring real-time, broadcast grade transmission without compromise.

Wireless Live Video for Religious Events

Live House of Worship Broadcast

House of Worship events are among the most emotional and inspiring live gatherings. The audience is not just watching — they are part of the ceremony, sharing in every word, song, and moment of devotion.For Production crews, the true challenge lies in traslating that emotion to the screen — bringing the experience to believers who are watching remotely, with the same sense of connection and presence. Covering live events in houeses of worship introduces several technical and logistical challenges:  

* Constant movement of sppeakers or clergy among the audience
* Physical obstacles such as columns, walls, and decor that can block RF signals
* The need for seamless indoor and outdo coverage as ceremonies often extend beyond the main sanctuary

The AB4000 and AB612 systems are designed with advanced bi–directional RF technology and exeptional range and stabbillity overcome these challenges effortlessly. Our systems deliver smooth, delay-free video and data transmission, enabling crews to capture every spiritual moment from every angle.
