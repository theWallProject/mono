AI-powered Platform DANNA Revolutionizing Private Market Investing

Your AI investment copilot, DANNA, provides clarity to private markets by enabling investment professionals perform screening, prospecting, due diligence, and validation of analyst findings for global startups across geographies and industries 10 times more efficiently than traditional approaches. It generates investment committee-grade investment memo drafts and produces portfolio-level analytics of groups of startups, forecasting performance and risk over time.

Private Markets are opaque, special knowledge is key, and DANNA provides the edge.

What We Do

Differentiated Solutions

DANNA transforms private market investing through its data-centric design meant for modern investors and investment professionals. DANNA’s AI-based analytics empower decisions, optimizing outcomes for investors.

With the DANNA copilot, VCs, PEs, family offices, and other adjacent functions conduct deep and thorough due diligence on global startups and quantify probable outcomes, such as probability of successful exit, probable exit multiple, expected time to exit, and other critical factors relevant to the investment decision process.

DANNA provides additional proprietary metrics relevant to grading key company criteria. The proprietary indicators include innovative elements such as momentum, sentiment, investor grade, and more.

Our Products

Vantage Market

The Vantage Market platform empowers private market operators (VC’s, PEs, WMs, etc.) to showcase investment opportunities to their investors / LP’s through a highly-customizable, modern, and secure  interface, allowing highly capable data rooms under sophisticated access controls. This enables a flexible, secure, and feature rich experience for investors (LPs), powering a frictionless digital experience for private market investments, end-to-end.
