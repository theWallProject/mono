Common Questions

Do I need to replace my existing cameras or VMS?
No. Liquid360 is an overlay solution. We integrate directly with your existing VMS (like Milestone, Genetec, etc.) and camera infrastructure. You keep your current recording backend; we just provide a revolutionary new frontend interface.
How long does it take to build the 3D Digital Twin?
Surprisingly fast. For a typical facility, we can build the initial model in a few days using standard 2D floor plans or CAD files. Once the model is built, placing cameras and calibrating them is a streamlined process.
Is this a cloud-based or on-premise solution?
Liquid360 is designed for high-security environments, so it runs fully on-premise within your secure network (air-gapped if necessary). However, we also offer hybrid options for multi-site organizations that need centralized visibility.
How much training do operators need?
Because the interface mimics human eyesight and uses intuitive ‘click-to-look’ navigation, most operators are proficient within 15-30 minutes. It’s designed to be as simple as a video game, removing the need for complex joystick training.
Can it handle multi-story buildings and outdoor areas?
Yes. Liquid360 excels at spatial context. You can seamlessly fly from an outdoor perimeter view into a 3rd-floor hallway in a single smooth motion, maintaining complete situational awareness of where everything is located.
