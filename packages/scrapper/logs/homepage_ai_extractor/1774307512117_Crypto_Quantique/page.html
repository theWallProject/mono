When off-the-shelf IP no longer fits your SoC

Teams come to us when off-the-shelf IP no longer fits the SoC they’re designing.

  • Security choices are affecting power, area, memory, or performance
  • Your architecture is non-standard (no CPU, FSM-based control, custom memory)
  • You need to protect AI models, firmware, or external memory (emerging trends)
  • Customer or OEM security questions are becoming architectural, not procedural

Design security that strengthens your SoC. Not constrains it

Security choices don’t live in isolation. Early decisions can introduce integration gaps that only surface later, affecting power, performance, area, memory footprint, and future product flexibility.

We work with silicon teams during the design phase to identify those system-level impacts early, advising on the right abstractions and architecture choices so security strengthens the SoC without creating unintended overhead or constraints.

Security without unintended trade-offs

We help silicon teams design security that fits their SoC by validating architecture decisions during the design phase. That includes surfacing hardware–software integration gaps early, where many system-level issues emerge.

We then provide the documentation, evidence, and technical support needed to streamline OEM engagement and certification processes.
