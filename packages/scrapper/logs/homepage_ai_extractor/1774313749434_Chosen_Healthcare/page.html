Welcome to Chosen Healthcare

Chosen Healthcare communities provide short-term and long-term residents with a warm secondary home environment for all residents and their families. We pride ourselves on delivering the highest quality medical care and rehabilitation wrapped in kindness and compassion to help our residents live their lives to the fullest. Our mission is to continue to serve as a continuum of care provider for our residents, families, hospitals, and physicians. Chosen Healthcare is dedicated to patients and their families by striving to promote smooth admission transitions into our Chosen communities. Our small intimate settings at each of our communities promotes healing and recovery.Senior residents from the best Indianapolis assisted living environments can feel at home while receiving personalized care and support.

Wound Care

Diabetes / Wound Care - More than 25% of the U.S. population over the age of 65 has some form of diabetes. The high prevalence of diabetes in older adults is due to age-related Physiological changes such as obesity, cardiovascular complications, cognitive impairment, depression, falls, polypharmacy, persistent pain, and vascular complications.

Fall Prevention / Balance

Falls are one of the leading causes of death in older adults. Determining the cause of balance impairment is very important, which is why each individual undergoes our comprehensive balance assessment to establish a baseline for treatment. Our care program contains specific therapy modalities and exercises that are prescribed according to the cause of balance problems.
