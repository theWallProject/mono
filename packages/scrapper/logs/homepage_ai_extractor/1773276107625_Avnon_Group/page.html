AVNON GROUP - DEFENCE & HLS TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS

Securing Nations
with Innovative Technology:
Join Avnon Group and
Shape the Future of Security

AVNON Group is a global provider of defence and homeland security solutions that protect borders, critical national infrastructures and perimeters from air, land and sea for governments, HLS agencies, law enforcement, special units and first responders in over eighty countries worldwide. We operate with integrity, versatility, and flexibility, harnessing in-house technologies, development and collaboration with dual-use technologies, and our access to top industry vendors to deliver exceptional solutions with synergy and cohesiveness.

Join the Avnon Group in shaping the face of modern security and innovative defence solutions that help keep nations safe.

OUR CORE TECHNOLOGIES:

MULTI-LAYERED C-UAS SYSTEMS →

Skylock develops modular, multi-layered C-UAS systems and countermeasures, delivering comprehensive solutions for airports, critical infrastructures, military bases and national events against evolving drone threats.
> More on Skylock

UAS PLATFORMS →

Manufactured from advanced composite materials, iSTAR delivers one of the most advanced, high-endurance, VTOL unmanned aircraft in the world.
> More on iSTAR

MULTI-SENSOR SYSTEMS →

Beesense Systems specialise in developing, designing and manufacturing advanced multi-sensor & radar-based integrated systems for land, air and maritime applications.
> More on Beesense

COMPOSITE MATERIALS →

Comparts is a full-service composites manufacturer. We engineer, analyse, design, develop, and fabricate composite materials, delivering an integrated, customised turnkey solution.
> More on Comparts

TECHNOLOGY INTEGRATION

Technology integration is at the heart of the Avnon Group. We dedicate ourselves to providing solutions that assist you in navigating the ever-changing threat landscape. The needs of our clients drive us to continually develop and integrate new technologies into our solutions portfolio, enabling us to deliver advanced platforms, systems, C-UAS and sensors that provide our customers with comprehensive security solutions tailored to mitigate their unique threats.
