WHO WE ARE

FIN AND TUBE HEAT EXCHANGERS
Lordan engineers and manufactures high quality custom-made fin and tube heat exchangers for the global HVAC&R market since 1958. Beyond thermal engineering, our heating, cooling and refrigeration coils: Condensers and Evaporators are designed to operate in extreme environmental conditions. We specialize in applications suited to the heavy duty, precision cooling and industrial cooling markets.
Since 1958, Lordan has built a reputation for supplying high-quality engineering and product design with a high standard of production process with an ongoing technical support to its customers.
