Wildfires are a $20 trillion crisis.

From devastating homes, to threatening lives and polluting our waters, wildfires are expected to impose nearly $20 trillion in damages over the next two decades — in the U.S. alone.

Confronting the crisis.

Fuel treatments are proven to reduce wildfire risk and promote forest health. The challenge is implementing treatments on the landscape in a timeframe and scale that matter.

Speed with precision.

Forestry tasks like manually painting trees and flagging project boundaries simply don’t scale. We help minimize time consuming and expensive project preparation to accelerate larger projects.

Guide in real-time.

Through precision sensors and an in-cabin tablet, Guide provides operators with maps, alerts, and tree-level insights to ensure prescriptions are met & boundaries observed — in real-time.

Scale with transparency.

Project administration often requires long distance drives to remote locations to collect data. We scale capacity by bringing field data to key stakeholders, helping to oversee more acres with confidence.

Admin through Portal.

Portal is a web application that automates reporting from the field. Through Portal, administrators can oversee exponentially more acres, while staying close to ground-truth.

We’re collaborating with innovative land managers to prepare healthy forests for future generations.
