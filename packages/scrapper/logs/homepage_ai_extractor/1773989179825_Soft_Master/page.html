SOFTMASTER is a company dedicated to implementing successful computer support, network maintenance and solutions. These solutions are firmly focused on the business' IT needs, not on gimmicks or purely on Technology. We consider all aspects of the business' current and future requirements in every solution. With our consultants help your company computer will be a profit making tool.

WHAT WE DO?

We offer a complete range of consultancy, network maintenance, delivery and support services, giving you the option to request a specific skill, technology or, more commonly, take a fully integrated IT end-to-end solutions. We can help you in defining strategy, choosing appropriate technologies, procurement, custom configuration, project management, security and installation. Once implemented us then provide a range of support services and network maintenance to ensure long-term cost-effective performance.

When considering a network design we take a multi-dimensional view ensuring the business' needs, growth and budgets are considered. Our consultants design networks that help highly motivated and focused companies achieve their targets. We will also introduce conventional companies into the world of computer networks, the internet and distribution of information. Trust and belief play pivotal roles in our ethos. We base our business on the fact that our customers will exceed their expected growth and continue to let us provide quality support and network maintenance to them.

Many companies require but can't afford an IT Manager/Engineer for their network. Companies from 5 to 50 people can't justify the costs of employing an IT manager, because they don't really have enough users for a full time position Soft-Master can fill this gap by offering regular network maintenance contracts from one day a month basis to perform tasks such as Virus updates and regular backups, add new users and monitor system performance. We will also keep networked machines up to date with the latest service patches. This includes email and telephone support so you have IT support at hand everyday of the week.

Website design is another part of the service. Soft-Master can produce not only an attractive and customer friendly website but optimize the site so that people searching the internet will find it. Part of this process involves optimizing the design.

A good informative website is almost as important in today's business environment as having a telephone. We employ innovative and talented designers that are capable of bringing your website to life. This website is just a sample of what's on offer from our designers. All levels of web sites can be catered for, from just a single page highlighting your business and contact details to a full online brochure giving customers a completed and detailed picture of what your business has to offer.

Your network may be several years old and require updating to the new Operating Systems and Desktop applications currently on offer. We can help you choose the applications and services that you need and also the hardware required to run them. Once this decision has been made then we will install and implement your new services quickly and efficiently and minimize the impact this transition has on your day-to-day business.
