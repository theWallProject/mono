SunDo’s platform connecting farmers with

Download the SunDo app and join Israel’s first Agri-Community!





Real People, Agri-Family

SunDo is Israel’s largest agricultural platform and partners with hundreds of farms throughout the country. With SunDo, the farmers of Israel can open their farms to to everyone to enjoy the experience of agricultural and access thousands of working hands .


Learn more

Working Together

SunDo offers many activities from the farming world. From picking, pruning and shaping, to work with sheep and cattle. Each and every one can find the activity that is appropriate to their character, pace and abilities, and thus enjoy the communal, valuable and uplifting work.

An Ocean of Experiences

In addition to the agricultural tasks, SunDo’ers enjoy social activities, local attractions, new friendships and unforgettable experiences!
