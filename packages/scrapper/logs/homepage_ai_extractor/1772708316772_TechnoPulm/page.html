How it works?

Currently, there is no available solution for pulmonologists in outpatient clinics to perform a full pulmonary function test (PFT).

Advanced lung function testing

Transforming the landscape of pulmonary healthcare

Today, the only diagnostic tool available to pulmonologists in outpatient clinics is the spirometer. However, spirometry alone provides a limited view of lung function, measuring only a subset of respiratory parameters.

For a more complete assessment, most patients are referred for advanced tests, such as body plethysmography, lung volume measurements, diffusion capacity (DLCO), and CO₂ monitoring, procedures typically performed only in specialized pulmonary labs.

The STS system transforms this process by bringing advanced diagnostic capabilities into the clinic.

STS - All in One

The STS is the only compact, lightweight handheld device that enables full pulmonary function testing (PFT) directly from the doctor’s office. It introduces a disruptive approach to diagnosing and monitoring respiratory diseases, eliminating the need for bulky equipment or hospital-based labs. Connected to a comprehensive digital platform, the STS will support respiratory telehealth solutions in future releases, allowing patients to engage with their physicians from anywhere, at any time.
