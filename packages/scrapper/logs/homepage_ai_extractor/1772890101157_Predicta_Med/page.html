Diagnosing and treating autoimmune diseases is complex and time consuming. Since these diseases affect multiple organs, the patient journey usually requires visits to many different specialists: primary care, rheumatology, neurology and gastroenterology. Common, non-specific, confusing symptoms often prolong the time frame for a definitive diagnosis and can lead to unnecessary procedures and tests, and patient stress. Delays in diagnosis can be critical in patient outcomes.
