FAQs

Do I need any machine-learning or coding skills to use Pecan?

Nope. If you can ask a clear question, you’re qualified. Pecan’s predictive agent is built for business teams, data leaders, and BI analysts alike. If you can ask a clear business question, the agent handles the predictive work end to end. No coding, no model tuning, no ML expertise required. Want proof? Book a live demo.

How long does it take to build a predictive model with Pecan?

Most teams spin up their first model in under a day, often within a couple of hours. Iterations happen in minutes, so you act on insights fast.

What data do I need to get started?

Just historical, event-level data from your warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks, etc.). PII is not required. We actually prefer messy, real-world data. That’s where the signal is. We handle data prep and feature engineering automatically. Let’s confirm your data fit on a quick call.

What business problems can Pecan solve?

Customer churn, lifetime value, demand forecasting, fraud detection, inventory optimization, campaign optimization or anything else – if you have past data and a future question, Pecan can model it. Check real-world use cases in our demo.

How does Pecan connect to my data sources and tools?

Easily! Secure connectors link directly to your cloud warehouse, then predictions flow into CRMs, marketing platforms, and BI dashboards via native integrations or API. You can opt to upload CSV or Excel files. We’ll map it to your stack during the demo.

How accurate are Pecan’s predictions?

Pecan benchmarks every model with metrics like AUC, lift, and forecast error. Our customers usually see double-digit performance gains over manual methods. Transparent dashboards show the drivers behind every prediction for maximum explanability.

How is my data kept secure and compliant?

Pecan is ISO 27001 certified, SOC 2 Type II audited, GDPR ready, and encrypts data in transit and at rest. You control what data is shared, and PII is never needed or requested. Your Information Security team will be smiling. We’ll walk you through our security on a call, and you can also read more here.

How much does Pecan cost?

Here is our easy-to-understand and no end-of-month-surprises pricing. This is far less than a single data scientist’s salary – but you’re still gaining enterprise-grade automation. Get a tailored quote at the demo.

How fast will I see ROI?

Because models go live in days and predict automatically, customers often report measurable revenue lift or cost savings within the first quarter. Hear their stories when we meet.

Is there a trial or proof-of-value program?

We’re so confident in Pecan’s results that every plan is commitment-free, and you can cancel anytime. See the value first-hand, keep us only if you love the impact.
