What you can do with Aiso

Two ways to understand why AI recommends your competitors instead of you.

See the searches behind the answer

ChatGPT doesn't just answer questions. It breaks them into sub-queries, searches Google for each one, and synthesizes a response. These are called fan-out searches.

You might rank #1 for your main keyword. But if a competitor owns the fan-outs, they get cited. You don't.

With Fan-Outs you can:

  • See every sub-query ChatGPT generates for any prompt
  • Check who ranks on Google for each fan-out
  • Run dozens of prompts simultaneously
  • Compare your fan-out presence against competitors
  • Track changes over time

Unlike browser extensions that show one prompt at a time, Fan-Outs gives you the full picture across your entire keyword universe.
