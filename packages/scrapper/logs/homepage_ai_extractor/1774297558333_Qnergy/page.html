Stirling Engine: Our Core Technology

The Free Piston Stirling Engine is at the core of our methane abatement technology. Designed to deliver reliable, grid-quality remote power to locations using low-grade or high-grade fuels. The PowerGen transforms waste methane gas and other gaseous fuels into useful energy.

Industry Solutions

Oil and Gas

Qnergy provides reliable, autonomous power and compressed air for onshore and offshore operations. We help upstream, midstream, and downstream companies cut methane emissions from gas pneumatics, electrify and automate well pads, and solve problems like vapor recovery, compressor leaks, and cathodic protection. Our proven technology turns methane management into profitable, low-emission solutions.

Landfills

Whether your landfill is closed, capped, or still active, uncontrolled methane emissions present both environmental and financial risks. Qnergy’s TORCH4 system offers a breakthrough solution: a mobile, autonomous, closed flare that destroys over 99% of methane emissions, even at low concentrations. TORCH4 is the only system on the market capable of reliable, unmanned operation using Landfill methane as fuel. It’s available for lease, sale, or performance-based deployment through carbon credit monetization.

Learn more about Methane Destruction

Carbon Credits

Qnergy’s carbon credit program is built specifically for closed landfills, transforming environmental liabilities into revenue-generating assets. Through the deployment of TORCH4, the program enables verified methane destruction and generates high-quality carbon credits. These credits are shared with landfill owners, creating a new income stream where none existed.

Learn more about Carbon Credits
