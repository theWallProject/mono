REDEFINING HOW MISSIONS ARE EXECUTED

XTEND’s mission is to extend human reach into the most challenging operational environments.By combining AI-driven autonomy with direct human control, XTEND built to enable operators to deploy and manage multiple platforms remotely, executing complex tasks with precision, speed, and confidence. From confined spaces to contested environments, XTEND’s systems are designed to give operators decisive advantage while keeping personnel out of harm’s way.

BUILT FOR REAL-WORLD OPERATIONS

Human-Centric Control
Designed around the operator, XTEND’s systems prioritize intuitive control and clear decision-making under pressure.
Human operators remain in command while AI supports awareness, precision, and execution across complex missions.
Rapid Deployment & Adaptation
XTEND platforms are built for fast deployment, minimal training, and seamless adaptation to changing operational conditions. Operators can execute missions quickly and adjust in real time as requirements evolve.
Modular, Open Systems
XTEND supports modular payloads and open software integration, enabling tailored configurations for diverse mission needs. This flexibility allows organizations to scale capabilities and integrate new tools over time.
