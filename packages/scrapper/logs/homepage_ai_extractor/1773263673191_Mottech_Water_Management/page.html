About

 

Mottech is part of the MTI Group and a worldwide distributor of Motorola’s IRRInet platform.

MTI specializes in wireless communication systems (especially radio frequency and microwave) for both the civilian and military markets. The company has offices and production operations in Israel, South Africa, India, Australia, and the United States. Mottech is headquartered in Rosh Ha’ayin, Israel.
