Common Questions

Who can benefit most from the MyLee?

MyLee is beneficial for all breastfeeding mothers, whether they're first-time moms or experienced parents seeking to optimize their breastfeeding journey. It helps monitor milk production, balance supply between breasts, and identify potential issues early on, providing personalized guidance for a successful breastfeeding experience.

When should I start using MyLee?

Ideally you’ll start using the device as soon as possible after birth, for gaining valuable insights early on. The App can be downloaded and used immediately. You can start using MyLee even before birth. We built the MyLee to work with just a few milk drops so it can be valuable early on with your early prenatal colostrum.

What is Mylee testing?

MyLee is sensing milk properties associated with milk transition from colostrum to fully mature milk, that reflect on breastfeeding establishment, breastfeeding efficiency, and milk supply.

Is MyLee scientifically validated?

MyLee was thoroughly designed and validated by our professional team for accuracy, repeatability and robustness. The tech behind the MyLee was peer reviewed in the scientific journal BMC pregnancy and childbirth. (link to scientific paper)

Can MyLee increase my supply?

MyLee helps you monitor and understand your milk production progress through early insights into milk maturation. The device provides valuable information and guidance to help you optimize your breastfeeding practices and effectively establish your milk supply.

Can MyLee help with clogged ducts?

Tracking your milk with MyLee can identify early trends and provide insights and recommendations, which might help you manage and prevent issues like milk stasis that when mismanaged may lead to clogged ducts or mastitis. If you suspect you have clogged ducts, you should consult with a lactation consultant or healthcare provider.

Is MyLee FSA/HSA eligible?

Yes. As a product directly aiding breastfeeding the MyLee is FSA/HSA eligible and can generally be directly covered by your FSA/HSA plan. Search for possible direct FSA payment at checkout. Please note specific plans may differ and direct FSA payment may not always be available.

How often should I use MyLee?

MyLee can be used multiple times daily, with each scan providing valuable information about your breastfeeding progress. Daily scans are especially beneficial during the first weeks of breastfeeding, and you can continue scanning at your preferred frequency to monitor your journey and track any changes.

How long will I need MyLee for?

MyLee supports your entire breastfeeding journey, from the very first drops of milk onwards. Starting with MyLee as early as possible after birth allows you to get the most benefits from personalized tracking and insights throughout your unique breastfeeding experience.

Can I use MyLee if I’m exclusively pumping?

MyLee can help any breastfeeding mother to track milk production and maturation, whether you're breastfeeding directly, exclusively pumping, or any combination thereof.

Can I use MyLee if I’m supplementing with formula?

For protecting your body’s ability to produce milk according to your baby’s needs, it is generally advised to delay any formula or bottle introduction after breastfeeding is well established. Reality is sometimes complicated and the MyLee can help any breastfeeding mother to track milk production and maturation, whether you're breastfeeding directly, using a bottle, exclusively pumping, or adding non breastmilk feeds.

How it all Began

Becoming Mothers, Facing Unexpected Challenges

As breastfeeding moms, we experienced moments of joy and connection, but also immense challenges. From low milk supply to pain and inflammation, we faced the unexpected frustration and anxiety that came with something we thought would be natural. These struggles ignited our determination to make a change.

Founding MyMilk Labs

As life science PhDs with extensive research experience, we understood that human milk holds invaluable data for supporting a proactive approach to breastfeeding. With this vision, we established MyMilk Labs—the first clinical-standard laboratory dedicated to the analysis of human milk.

The Birth of MyLee R&D

After five years and thousands of milk samples processed, we launched the development of MyLee: the first-ever at-home monitoring system designed to assess breastfeeding through advanced breastmilk sensing. This marked a major leap forward in supporting breastfeeding mothers worldwide.

Meet Mylee

Our hard work paid off with MyLee’s patent approval, CE/FCC certifications, peer-reviewed publications, and rigorous technology validation. Collaborating with lactation consultants and mothers, we tested and refined prototypes to ensure MyLee’s reliability and effectiveness.

Empowering Breastfeeding Mothers

Our goal is to unlock the data within breast milk to help mothers overcome breastfeeding challenges and feed their babies with confidence, empowering them on their unique feeding journeys.
