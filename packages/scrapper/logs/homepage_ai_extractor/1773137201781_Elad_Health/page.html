Digitalizing Hospitals

Broad Solution

Single EMR for all clinical units in the hospital. Patient chart adjusted for unique units' needs.

Clinical Depth

Chameleon EMR implements all the assessment and treatment tools used by medical professionals.

Configurability

A fully configurable and adaptable solution to streamline all clinical processes.

Flexibility

Patient chart easily and independently adjusted to changing clinical needs by means of customization, and without any code writing.

Interoperability

Complete integration with all medical devices in a clinical setting.

Integration

Interfaces with medical, paramedical and administrative platforms across the hospital to enable optimal patient management.

Agile Solution

Chameleon is a web-based Electronic Medical Record used by leading hospitals, ambulatory clinics and health organizations.

EMR in action

Leading Israel’s hospitals digital transformation

From paper to fully-digital, across hospitals and HMO’s, Chameleon EMR is leading the charge to digital across Israel’s healthcare landscape.

Sheba Hospital
Named as one of the world’s top ten leading hospitals, the Sheba Medical Center coordinates 120 departments, 1,700 hospital beds, as well as the care for over 1 million patients annually, with the Chameleon EMR.
Read more >
Ministry of Health, Israel
The Israeli Ministry of Health was looking to connect 11 public hospitals and upgrade their inter-connected record system between hospitals while also effectively monitoring hospital loads.
Read more >
Clalit HMO, Israel
Clalit is the largest HMO network in Israel with 14 hospitals and 7 million patients annually. Their goal was a completely unified digital record policy across all of the hospitals within their extensive network.
Read more >
Assuta Hospital, Ashdod
This medium-sized, fully digital hospital is a general, modern hospital made up of 300 beds. It opened in 2017. Assuta, Ashdod was built from the beginning with Chameleon EMR integrated into its operations.

Raphael Hospital
This small outpatient hospital provides specialized care, and performs around 1,000 medical operations a month. The hospital was built with fully digital capabilities from day one.
