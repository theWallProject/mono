What is AngelSense?

AngelSense is an assistive technology that includes a proactive monitoring & alerting system, an app for caregivers, and a wearable device with an auto-pickup speakerphone and SOS button.

Have a Safe School Year with AngelSense

  • School Bus Monitoring

    See live tracking map, updated ETA, speed, and detailed history for every ride

  • Emergency Detection Alerts

    Get notified right away for elopement, unfamiliar places, late arrivals, and more

  • Inclusion & Independence

    Let your child join activities, field trips, recess and daily routines

What Makes
AngelSense Unique?

AI-Powered Solution Proactive Alerts Assistive Speakerphone Emergency Search Tools Sensory Sensitive Wearing Customer Care by Parents

Utilizing AI to overcome the inherent limitations of GPS and other sensor technologies, for reliable proactive alerting and monitoring.

Automatically receive proactive alerts if your loved one leaves a designated safe zone, enters a new area, or deviates from a planned route—without opening the app. AngelSense automatically alerts potential at-risk situations when assistance may be needed, helping to prevent escalation.

Speak to your loved one with auto-pickup, hands-free speakerphone to provide remote support, or respond to call requests generated by a single click of an SOS button. 

Lifesaving tools to help you quickly locate your loved one, easily share live location with others, react to emergencies, and prevent them from escalating.

With our non-removable sensory-friendly wearing accessories, combined with personalized guidance, over 95% of individuals with sensory sensitivities are able to wear AngelSense daily.

Our customer care team is 100% staffed by special needs parents who use AngelSense with their loved ones daily and enjoy sharing their expert knowledge and experience.
