Tufin provides that control through a unified control plane built for complex multi-vendor networks.

A Control Plane for Your Entire Network Security Ecosystem

Tufin integrates seamlessly with the technologies powering your hybrid network—from firewalls to cloud, SASE, microsegmentation, and beyond.

Multi-Vendor Firewalls

Centralized visibility and unified policy enforcement across traditional and hybrid mesh architectures, including Cisco, Palo Alto Networks, Check Point, Fortinet, and more.

The control plane streamlines management, eliminates configuration silos, and ensures consistent, least-privilege policies across on-premises and cloud networks—helping security teams operate faster and reduce risk at scale.

Cloud Environments

Streamlines and automates security policy workflows across public and private cloud platforms—including AWS, Azure, and GCP to ensure consistent enforcement, reduce risk, and streamline operations.

The control plane delivers unified visibility across hybrid networks, automates cloud onboarding and change workflows, and enforces least-privilege policies to prevent network exposure.

Learn more

SASE & Edge Networks

Unifies and orchestrates security policies across edge networks by governing and managing SASE-driven network policies, ensuring consistent, least-privilege access everywhere your remote users are located.

The control plane provides visibility and control over Firewall-as-a-Service (FWaaS), Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA), and SD-WAN policies—ensuring users and workloads stay securely connected across the network edge and cloud.

Learn more

Network Device Topology

Delivers the most accurate network topology for complete visibility across routers, switches, and connected devices to improve troubleshooting, planning, and operational efficiency.

The control plane automatically maps every network access path and security policy, giving teams a continuously updated view of their network infrastructure and eliminating blind spots that may lead to outages or security gaps.

Microsegmentation

Centralizes visibility and policy management across microsegmentation technologies to enforce granular, application-level segmentation at scale. Tufin aligns microsegmentation controls with existing network security policy to eliminate gaps, reduce complexity, and prevent policy drift.

By unifying microsegmentation with traditional firewall and cloud policy management, the Tufin Control Plane ensures consistent enforcement across hybrid environments. Organizations can isolate critical assets, prevent lateral movement, and maintain continuous compliance with automated policy validation and change governance.

Learn more

Zero Trust

Operationalizes Zero Trust by enforcing least-privilege access across the entire network security stack. Tufin ensures that every connection request is verified, authorized, and continuously monitored through centralized policy orchestration and automated risk-aware change workflows.

By aligning identity-driven access controls with network and cloud security policy, the Tufin Control Plane eliminates blind spots and ensures consistent enforcement across hybrid environments. Security teams gain unified visibility, measurable risk reduction, and continuous compliance without increasing operational overhead.

Learn more
