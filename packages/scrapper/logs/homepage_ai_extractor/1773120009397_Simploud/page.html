More About Us

Simploud is a cloud-native compliance platform built on Salesforce that uniquely integrates Quality Management (QMS), Laboratory Information Management (LIMS), and Design Control into one streamlined ecosystem. Tailored for life sciences and biotech companies, it delivers ready-to-use, subscription-based features like CAPA tracking, customizable reminders, and digital forms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear answers to common queries about Simploud’s platform, features, security, and setup.

What happens after the 15-day free trial if I don't want to continue?

If you decide not to continue after your 15-day free trial, your account will simply become inactive—no charges will be made. You’ll still have the option to export your data or upgrade to a paid plan at any time if you choose to return.

Is Simploud secure? Will my data be protected?
Yes. Security and privacy are top priorities at Simploud. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, and we follow industry best practices for secure cloud hosting. Access is strictly role-based, ensuring that only authorized users can view or manage your information.
Can Simploud integrate with our existing systems like ERP or CRM?
Absolutely. Simploud is designed to work seamlessly with popular ERP and CRM systems. Our integration options (via APIs and connectors) allow you to sync data and streamline workflows without disrupting your existing setup.
What languages does Simploud support?
Simploud currently supports multiple languages to help teams work comfortably across regions. English is the primary language, and we’re continuously expanding support to additional languages based on customer demand.
Will Simploud be around in the future?
Yes. Simploud is built and backed by a dedicated team committed to long-term growth and innovation. We are continuously improving the platform, adding new features, and ensuring sustainability so you can rely on us for years to come.
