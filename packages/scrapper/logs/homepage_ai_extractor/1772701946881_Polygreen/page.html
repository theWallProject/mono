    Sustainability

    Polygreen's vision is to enhance sustainability in all aspects in which superabsorbent polymers are used or can be used, and particularly so in the hygiene and agriculture sectors. This helps these industries meet the Sustainable Development Goals regarding climate change, global warming, and waste management.
