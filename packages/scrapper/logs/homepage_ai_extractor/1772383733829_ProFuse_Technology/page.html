Drug Discovery

We discover innovative drugs for skeletal muscle preservation and growth using groundbreaking muscle modelling technology.

Skeletal muscle mass is essential for health and longevity, yet it diminishes due to weight loss (e.g., from GLP-1 drugs) and aging (sarcopenia).  At ProFuse we develop a unique in vitro high-throughput drug discovery platform for skeletal muscle loss and metabolic disease, that enables precise and efficient screening of novel therapeutics with high translational potential.

About Us

ProFuse is the leader in skeletal muscle growth, developing innovative drugs for muscle preservation and regeneration, as well as selling products for enhanced cultivated muscle meat production. The company is led by seasoned entrepreneur Guy Nevo Michrowski, CEO and co-founder, and Dr. Tamar Eigler Hirsh, CTO and co-founder, who developed ProFuse’s groundbreaking technology over six years based on science developed in the Weizmann Institute of Science. ProFuse’s team is uniquely focused on skeletal muscle biology, comprising 15 experts, including 6 PhDs and 7 MSc holders. The award-winning company won the 2021 Israel start-up competition and has received exclusive grants from the EU and the Israel Innovation Authority.
