Enabling LLMs to modernize architecture

LLMs are effective at upgrading code and containerizing workloads. But without runtime context, system-wide visibility, and business alignment, they cannot modernize the architecture of complex applications.

Meaningful cost savings, elasticity, and speed require architectural modernization.

By combining runtime and static analysis with data science, vFunction enables teams to design their target architecture. It generates a structured refactoring plan to guide code assistants in transforming Java and .NET monoliths into cloud-native services—15× faster than traditional methods.

Modernization beyond monoliths

Technical debt and complexity aren’t unique to monoliths. Distributed environments introduce architectural challenges that require modernization. vFunction uses OpenTelemetry to deliver deep architectural insights that remove complexity, enable migrations, and keep your systems scalable and resilient over time. 

Once monoliths are broken into modular services, vFunction provides ongoing oversight across distributed applications, monitoring drift and preventing sprawl.
