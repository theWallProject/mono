About Nanomedic

Nanomedic Technologies Ltd. is a bio-medical therapeutics technology company specializing in the development and manufacturing of its innovative Spincare System platform for advanced wound treatment.

Founded in 2018 as a spinoff of Nicast, a pioneer in electrospinning technology, Nanomedic concentrates on its unique Portable Electrospinning technology platform, the Spincare system.

The company, situated in the heart of a renowned microenvironment of innovative companies, has set a goal to revolutionize wound care treatment, envisioning the ultimate solution of 3D printing artificial skin directly on patients’ wounds.

The Spincare system was first introduced to the market in Q3 2020 and is now distributed and sold in more than 40 countries across five continents.
