About Us

Sustainability

At ETTO, sustainability is at the core of everything we do. We believe that art and design should not only be beautiful but also responsible and respectful of our planet. This belief drives our commitment to transforming plastic waste into something meaningful, functional, and personal. By using our patented Free Molding technology, we give new life to recycled plastics, turning them into unique pieces of art and design that resonate with individuality. Our process is not just about recycling; it’s about redefining the material itself. We aim to change the way people perceive plastic, shifting the narrative from it being a symbol of waste and pollution to one of creativity and possibility. Every product we create is a testament to this vision, carefully crafted to minimize environmental impact while maximizing artistic expression. Through our workshops and educational initiatives, we strive to inspire others to see the potential in what might otherwise be discarded. We believe that sustainability is not a trend but a necessity, and we are committed to contributing to a more sustainable future by making thoughtful, environmentally-conscious choices in every step of our production. .

About Us

Our Story

One of the key principles that distinguishes one person from another is the individuality that is instilled in us all, from the moment we are born. No matter how similar two people may look on the surface, they are inherently unique and generally express this through the things that they do, the things that they say, the things that they wear, and the things that they own. In order to meet this need for individualism in the world of art and industrial design, our company was founded with the same guiding principle of uniqueness which is something that is reflected in our name. ETTO, a word of Italian origin that is used to represent exactly 100 grams of pasta, is meant to reflect a personal portion or serving that is defined to be just for you. Taking on a similar philosophy for our product lines, ETTO us represents a piece of art or design item that is just for you. Our innovative production method, hands-on care, and threads of thought that are present in each of our designs ensure that every single piece that we produce across a series may look similar to another but is in fact unique and personal… just like their owners.

Our Vision

To change the collective concept of Plastic from a cold and alienated material to a friendly & artistic substance.

Our Technology

01

Free Molding Technology

A computer-guided robotic arm, combined with a plastic extruder generates a unique method whereby molten plastic is allowed to fall freely, producing one-of-a-kind, artistic creations. Despite being a technological robotic-guided process, Free Molding Technology veers from standard template production by allowing random elements to affect the resulting object.

02

Unique Features

Eco-Friendly | High Efficiency | Fully Customizable | Low Production Cost.

03

3D Free Molding Machine (3DFM)

Our groundbreaking 3D Free Molding Machine (3DFM) sets us apart in the industry, enabling us to produce customizable 3D products in large series – a capability unmatched by other manufacturers. Unlike traditional 3D printers that build in layers, our technology creates products in one piece, significantly reducing production costs and time.   Through our user-friendly software application, customers can easily intervene in the production process without needing expertise in 3D software. This innovative approach allows for real-time, online customization and manufacturing of products.    Moreover, our commitment to sustainability shines through in our “green” production process. We work with recycled plastic, create 100% recyclable products, and maintain a comparatively low energy consumption. With 3DFM, we’re not just revolutionizing product customization – we’re doing it with environmental responsibility at the forefront.

How it works

01

Create a Mold

Design and create a three-dimensional mold using materials like polyurethane foam, cardboard, or silicone-coated fabric.

02

Prepare Design Pattern

Develop a customized design pattern with specific design parameters using a computer interface.

03

Fit Mold to Support Unit

Attach the mold to a movable support unit that can rotate and move in multiple directions.

04

Extrude Material

Attach the mold to a movable support unit that can rotate and move in multiple directions.

05

Shape the Design

The extruder and support unit work together to apply the material to all faces of the mold, forming the design.

06

Separate and Finalize

Once the design is complete, separate the mold from the finished product, allowing it to cool and solidify.

Our Team

Investor & Major Partner
Hilik Dery
Yaron Elyasi, born in 1971 in Israel, is an industrial designer and artist from Israel specializing in the area of practical art and design with an emphasis on the emotional touch and individualism. He studied at the Shenkar/ Vital College of Design and Engineering where he received his degree in Product Design in 2001. Today Yaron is passing on his knowledge and experience to students at the same institution where he teaches Industrial Design. In his studio at ETTO Design Lab, Yaron realized his design visions by creating dynamic and intriguing art using a multitude of recyclable materials. Yaron’s designs, which range from lighting and home styling pieces to jewelry and commissioned items, use an approach where each product has its own unique character that cannot be reproduced, and as such is a “one of a kind” piece of art. The creations are produced using an innovative technique that Yaron developed himself, which has been patented under the name Freemolding.
Yaron Elyasi
