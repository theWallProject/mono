How UTU pays you for building trust online.

UTU Trust Token (UTT)

UTT is your reputation on the internet. It’s a non-transferable token that can’t be bought or sold, only earned connecting your socials to UTU protocol and stak on your feedback and endorsements on UTU protocol. Staked UTT pay rewards in UTT and $UTU. When your endorsements help others find things they love.

UTU Coin $UTU

$UTU is the native currency of UTU Protocol. It allows users to access content and signals from their network on UTU protocol and is freely tradeable. Staked UTT endorsements on UTU protocol pay rewards in SUTU from the Ecosystem Pool and user fees..
