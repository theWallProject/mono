Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about comparing money transfers

How do I compare money transfer rates?

Simply enter the amount you want to send, select your sending and receiving countries, and click "Compare Rates Now". We'll instantly show you quotes from over 30 FCA-regulated providers, displaying the exact amount your recipient will receive and all associated fees.

Is it safe to use a money transfer comparison site?

Yes, absolutely. We only compare providers that are regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Your money never passes through our site - you go directly to the provider to complete your transfer. We've been helping users find better rates since 2014.

How much can I save using a comparison site?

Savings vary depending on the amount and currency corridor, but our users save an average of £247 on a £10,000 transfer compared to using their bank. For larger transfers like property purchases, savings can reach into the thousands. The key is that rates and fees vary significantly between providers.

Which money transfer providers do you compare?

We compare over 30 FCA-regulated providers including Wise, Western Union, Moneycorp, CurrencyFair, OFX, WorldRemit, and many more. We regularly add new providers to ensure you're seeing the most comprehensive comparison available.

Do I have to pay to use this service?

No, our comparison service is completely free to use. You don't need to sign up or create an account. We're funded by referral fees from providers when you choose to use their service, but this never affects the rates you see or which provider appears first.
