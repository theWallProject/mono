    // About us

    Founded in 2008 by Adar Shafran & Roni Abramowsky, we specialize in cinema-grade video productions for international startups & tech companies, TVC and film.

    Our mission is to bring creative ideas and visual concepts that are fresh, original, and align perfectly with our client's marketing strategy, production requirements, and target audience.

    We take care of every aspect of the video production - from storyboard to final product and leverage over a decade of experience to create videos that make an impact, ranging from low to high budget productions.

    // Services

    / Creative

    Whether your goal is to position your brand, explain what you do, drive sales, or draw traffic to your website - FIRMA aligns the script to your video strategy and helps define the project’s scope and specifications to meet your KPI’s.

    ​

    / Animation

    Having experience producing complex videos for all markets, FIRMA can do it all - whether it’s classic animation, 2D, 3D, or live & animation combined.
    In cases where HW/SW is still in development, our animators are skilled to accurately render any technology and bring it to life on screen.

    ​

    / Production & Shoot

    Firma is ready to provide everything you need. We work with award-winning cinematographers, directors, & writers - and take care of all aspects of the video production from casting to crew, locations, studios, equipment, and more.  

    ​

    / Post production

    While keeping your goals in mind,  FIRMA’s motion artists tend to all your post-production needs: video editing, visual effects, color correction, graphic design, animation, sound design, voice-over (narration), subtitles, and more.
