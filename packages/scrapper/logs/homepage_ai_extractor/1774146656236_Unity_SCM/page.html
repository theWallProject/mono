Solutions
Solutions

Discover smart solutions for your shipment platform that streamline logistics, optimize inventory, and reduce costs. Enhance efficiency with real-time tracking, automated processes, and seamless integrations to meet your business needs.

Eliminate D&D Costs

Leverage real-time container visibility, accurate vessel schedules, customizable rates, and integrated exception management tools to minimize detention and demurrage fees, driving operational efficiency and cost savings.

Capture Revenue on Schedule

Accelerate invoicing and enhance cash flow by integrating real-time shipment updates, order-level ETAs, and delivery confirmations across your systems, ensuring timely revenue capture and smooth financial processes.

Reduce the Value of Inventory in Transit

Improve inventory planning and reduce stockout risks by gaining full visibility into in-transit inventory, linking order data to shipments, and tracking inventory flow seamlessly across the entire supply chain.

Align Supplier Payments with Deliveries

Streamline supplier payments by gaining full visibility into delivery schedules, Cargo Ready Dates, and customs updates, while leveraging integrated exception management to reduce payment errors and improve cash flow accuracy.

Improve Customer Experience

Enhance customer satisfaction by providing real-time order and shipment updates from creation to delivery, empowering customer service teams with proactive, accurate information and seamlessly integrating with external systems to address delays.

Facilitate Hassle-Free Exports

Simplify export operations with complete visibility into shipment details, including booking creation, vessel schedules, ERDs, cut-off dates, and rail tracking, enabling efficient risk management and on-time export deliveries.

Ensure Timely Import Deliveries

Ensure timely imports by tracking shipments end-to-end, from port departure to destination, with updates on customs holds, available pickup notifications, and destination rail ETAs to mitigate delays.

Improve On-Time Delivery Performance

Boost on-time delivery performance by integrating comprehensive shipment data, including rail container tracking, customs updates, and real-time visibility, while using exception management tools to mitigate risks and ensure timely deliveries. Provide Exceptional Customer Service

Equip your customer service teams with high-quality supply chain visibility, keeping customers informed on the status of their orders from creation to delivery. Use reliable predictive insights to help customers anticipate risks and shared views for easy self-service updates. Equip your customer service teams with real-time order and shipment data to boost efficiency and resolve issues faster.

Empower your sales team

Empower your sales team with real-time supply chain visibility to quickly identify on-time customer deliveries, track what customers are ordering, and pinpoint delayed orders or SKUs along with the reasons behind them. Gain insights into how long customers retain transportation assets, helping assess and optimize customer-requested free time requirements. Boost efficiency and customer satisfaction with data that drives smarter, faster sales decisions.

Enhance Transportation Planning

Enhance Transportation Planning with high-quality, granular shipment, order, and inventory data. Assess carrier and lane on-time performance, and monitor dwell times for high-volume lanes. Lead data-driven carrier performance reviews to assess utilization vs. contracts and make smarter carrier decisions. Additionally, improve free time allocation by analyzing transportation asset usage, reducing costs, and enhancing overall efficiency.

Leverage Rich Historical Data

Leverage rich historical order and shipment data to analyze inventory flow down to the SKU level. Balance key metrics like utilization, freight costs, on-time performance, lead times, and inventory value, ensuring smoother, more efficient operations.

Optimize warehouse management

Optimize warehouse management with high-quality, reliable transportation updates at the order level, from cargo ready dates through delivery, including frequently updated ETAs. This keeps warehouses informed on inbound cargo, improving receiving processes and cross-functional collaboration. When cargo is delayed, warehousing teams can reschedule or reallocate slots efficiently. Flagging high-priority shipments enables teams to quickly identify and prioritize unloading. Additionally, tracking containers at risk of detention fees allows warehouses to prioritize returns, avoiding extra costs and streamlining terminal or depot operations.

Improve Production Planning

Improve Production Planning with real-time visibility into orders from creation through delivery, down to the SKU level. Stay up-to-date on shipment statuses and proactively identify delays as early as the cargo ready date, allowing you to adjust production schedules and avoid downtime. Leverage this data to develop cost-effective strategies that minimize risks, ensuring smooth production flows and preventing costly disruptions.

Enhance financial operations

Enhance financial operations with timely updates on customer order deliveries based on Incoterms, enabling automated invoicing and reducing payment delays. Finance teams can accurately track supplier deliveries to avoid early payments or overpayments, ensuring better cash flow management. Additionally, monitor supplier deliveries against commitments to enforce late delivery penalties, improving contract compliance and reducing financial risks.
