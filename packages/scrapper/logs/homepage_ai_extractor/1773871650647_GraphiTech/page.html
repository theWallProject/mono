GraphiTech

Established in 1991, GraphiTech designs, develops and markets a range of high-accuracy software and engineering solutions for inspection, scanning, artistic CAD/CAM, and delivering PAS-CMM inspection solutions. For over two decades, GraphiTech systems have provided the world’s leading manufacturers with high-accuracy design, manufacturing, scanning and measurement tools. 

Based on years of experience in motion control hardware, high-precision laser probes and next-generation software, GraphiTech solutions include Quality Control (QC) & In Process Inspection (IPI) applications, Scanning (Reverse Engineering & CAD Compare), Computer-Aided Inspection (CAI) tools, and market-leading CAD/CAM software.
