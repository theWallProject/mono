Services

Seamless Event Production

At Pike Productions, we turn your ideas into exceptional events. With expertise in event production, audio-visual solutions, and innovative tools, we create seamless, unforgettable experiences tailored to your needs. From concept to execution, we ensure every detail reflects your vision.

Live Event Production

For fully in-person events, our Live Event Production services cover every aspect to ensure a flawless execution. From audiovisual setups to event logistics, we handle all the details so you can focus on engaging with your audience.

Hybrid Event Production

Most all events nowadays have an in-person and virtual attendee Hybrid production. With our years of experience specifically with Hybrid Events, we are able to provide a cohesive experience for all participants, regardless of their location.

Virtual Event Production

Engage your audience remotely with our Virtual Event Production solutions. We offer comprehensive services, including live streaming, interactive platforms, and technical support, to ensure your virtual event runs smoothly.

Web Streaming

Whether it’s a corporate conference, town hall meeting or investor pitch. Or even a wedding, live performance or graduation, you can focus on your event while we take care of the seamless live video feeds to reliable technical support. We handle all the details to ensure your virtual attendees enjoy an engaging and high-quality experience.

Audio Services

Achieve crystal-clear sound quality with our professional Audio Services. Having worked at nearly every venue in the DMV, our experienced technicians will ensure to meet all your audio needs, ensuring your message is heard loud and clear.

Visual Services

Ensure your guests can see all the visuals that you and your team have worked so hard on to bring to life. From TVs and LED Screens to Projectors and Screens, we have your event covered.

Lighting Services

Set the perfect ambiance with customizable, tailored solutions to elevate your event. From stage washes, custom gobo lighting and uplighting to table pin spotting and string lighting, we ensure every detail contributes to your vision.

Event Planning

The visuals, videos, audio, and stage is set, now let our production planning meetings come to life. Our talented team handles every technical detail of the “run of show” to ensure your event is both stunning and engaging and most importantly, on schedule! We work with your inhouse or 3rd party planner to create a cohesive team.
