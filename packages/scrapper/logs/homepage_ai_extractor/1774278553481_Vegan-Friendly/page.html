New Companies

  • Hillfarm Oils

    Hillfarm Oils crafts premium cold pressed extra virgin rapeseed oil, grown, pressed, and bottled on their Suffolk family farm. As the UK’s original producer of cold pressed rapeseed oil, Hillfarm has been leading the way since 2004 with a commitment to quality, sustainability, and traceability. Their golden oil is rich in Omega 3, 6, 9, and Vitamin E, with a smooth, nutty flavour and a high smoke point—perfect for everything from drizzling to frying. 100% British, natural, and made using solar power, Hillfarm is a delicious and responsible choice for modern kitchens.

    Oils and Vinegars

  • Bomonde

    Bomonde is a premium mineral makeup brand founded by beauty expert Essjay, offering 100% authentic, no-nonsense products that are gentle on both skin and the environment. Created for confident, conscious women, Bomonde blends sustainability with style, empowering individuals to look and feel their best. Essjay, with over 20 years of experience in the beauty industry, designed the brand to meet the needs of those who value natural, cruelty-free, and effective makeup. The product range includes palettes suitable for all skin tones and types, with a focus on minimizing waste through thoughtful packaging. With Bomonde, the only thing made up is you.

    Makeup & Nail Polish

  • Presteez

    Meet Presteez® – a range of snacks that bring you a world of delightful flavours and textures all pressed together to form a unique snacking experience. Presteez bars are crafted with unique technology that eliminates added sugar, sweeteners, and dates as binders, offering delicious, minimally processed, eco-friendly bars.

    Snack Bars

  • Dalgety

    Dalgety Herbal Teas crafts strong, exotic blends using 100% natural ingredients sourced from small farmers, creating over 500 jobs in the process. Known for their bold flavor and vibrant packaging, each teabag delivers up to two cups of rich, herbal goodness. Founded by Mark Dalgety with just a credit card and a passion for Caribbean tradition, the brand has grown into a household name across major UK supermarkets. Their wide range of teas caters to all tastes, staying true to natural, authentic brewing methods. As an award-winning Caribbean-owned business, Dalgety continues to blend heritage, quality, and innovation in every cup.

    Tea

  • Taste of Nature

    Taste of Nature brings gourmet vegan spreads to your table, inspired by Mediterranean and Middle Eastern flavors. From rich pepper creams and tangy chimichurri to luxurious artichoke mousses and savory aiolis, every product is crafted with plant-based ingredients and bold seasonings. Whether you're elevating a sandwich, pairing with a 'cheese' board, or enhancing a cooked dish, these spreads offer both depth and creativity. 100% vegan, flavor-forward, and made to impress.

    Savoury Spreads

  • Tivall

    Tivall reveals a new world of vegetarian cuisine! As more people reduce meat and add vegetables and legumes, Tivall offers satisfying, protein-rich dishes with real bite. From the start, Tivall’s goal has been to make vegetarian food accessible - better for you, better for the planet.

    Vegan Deli Slices,

    Vegan Burgers,

    Vegan Chicken,

    Vegan Nuggets

  • Clean Living

    Clean Living sets the gold standard in sustainable cleaning, combining probiotic-powered formulas with refillable aluminium bottles to protect both your home and the planet. Their innovative system eliminates single-use plastic and reduces carbon emissions through concentrated refills and long-lasting packaging. Every product is thoughtfully designed to deliver a deeper, longer-lasting clean—without harsh chemicals or unnecessary waste. Unlike conventional cleaners, Clean Living takes a closed-loop approach that transforms daily cleaning into an act of environmental care. It's not just cleaning—it’s a commitment to a healthier home and a cleaner planet.

    Cleaning Products

  • Keeko

    Keeko is a family-run business creating wholesome, screen-free play experiences for children through their fun and engaging colouring products. Established in 1998, they’ve become a trusted supplier to schools, restaurants, hospitals, and more across the UK and Europe. Keeko proudly manufactures their crayons in their own UK-based facility, ensuring quality and fast turnaround. They also offer bespoke designs with a fully carbon-neutral production process. Committed to creativity, sustainability, and smiles, Keeko makes playtime better for kids and kinder to the planet.

    Toys & Games

  • GLYDE

    GLYDE Condoms are truly vegan, made from natural rubber latex with only natural and vegan colors and flavors. GLYDE® Dams are thin, silky latex sheets designed for added protection and comfort. They come in a variety of fun fruity colors and scents like vanilla, strawberry, wildberry, mint, grape, and cola. Lightly scented to enhance the experience, GLYDE® Dams are available in packs for clinics or smaller retail packs. They’re easy to use and help provide peace of mind during intimate moments.

    Sexual Wellbeing

  • The Right Moods

    The Right Moods show that candles have a great potential that goes beyond aesthetics and scents. Their candles help create a complete experience to immerse you or your loved ones in the right mood. Founded by four young entrepreneurs, the brand is driven by creativity and a passion for building something meaningful. They aim to explore all it takes to create, build, and grow a unique candle brand. The Right Moods invites you to experience candles in a new and inspiring way.

    Candles & Essential Oils

  • W'ZIS

    W’ZIS is on a mission to revolutionise dog treats with bold shapes, bright colours, and zero boring bones. With their playful mantra “Ban the bone,” they toss out dull, brown snacks and pack in a whole lot of fun instead. These are treats designed to delight both dogs and their humans, creating a stronger emotional connection. W’ZIS delivers fun bombing snacks infused with love and free from unwanted ingredients. Who says dog treats can’t have personality?

    Pet Treats
