Scalable, extreme-bandwidth optical interconnects

Fast and energy-efficient data transfer is a must for modern data centers and AI clusters. Enlightra uses state-of-the-art photonics to build scalable, extreme-bandwidth and low-power optical interconnects for the future of computing and communications.

Optical interconnects

Optical interconnects are photonic highways that use light to route data. They link processor units, boards and racks with bandwidth, reach and energy efficiency unmatched by copper cables. As AI training and inference workloads surge, optical interconnects must scale in bandwidth while staying cost- and energy-efficient. This is only possible utilizing multiple laser colors.

Enlightra’s comb laser engine delivers dozens of high-power laser colors from a single chip. It is ready to power terabit-class links today and is uniquely capable of addressing the rapidly growing needs of AI infrastructure in the future.
