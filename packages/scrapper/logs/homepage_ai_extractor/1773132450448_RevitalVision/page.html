How It Works: The Science Behind RevitalVision

The quality of vision depends on the image transferred from the eye and the brain’s neural processing. Blurred vision can be improved by training the brain to process visual information more effectively. RevitalVision uses special visual patterns called “Gabor patches” and a unique stimulation method that activates specific brain cells in the visual cortex, helping to form new connections for lasting vision improvement.
