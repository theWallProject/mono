Telsys Ltd. Was Founded In 1963 And Since Then, Has Become A Leading Force In Israel’s Components Market. Today, Telsys Represents More Than 20 Major Electronics Manufacturers, Including Some Of The Biggest Names In The Industry.

The Company Also Acts As A Stocking Distributor For These Manufacturers And Others, And Prides Itself For Keeping A Large Stock.

Telsys, One Of Israel’s Largest Electronics Distributors, Focuses On Semiconductors, Subsystems, Passives, Connectors, Microwaves, Telecom / Datacom Products And More.
