CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference) defines six levels of English proficiency. A1 and A2 are beginner levels — the speaker can handle basic phrases and simple interactions. B1 and B2 are intermediate — B2 is typically the minimum required for professional work in English. C1 and C2 are advanced levels, required for academic study, senior professional roles, and near-native communication. SmallTalk2Me's AI speaking test maps your spoken English directly to one of these six levels.
Improvement timeline depends on your starting level and practice consistency. Learners who practice 30 minutes daily typically move up one CEFR level in 3–6 months. Moving from B1 to B2 — the level most employers require — takes most learners 4 to 6 months of structured daily practice. AI platforms accelerate this by providing instant feedback after every session, removing the delay between making a mistake and correcting it.
