The AutoCropter Advantage

The AutoCropter is a fully automated, unmanned system that operates with minimal turbulence and at half the speed of an airplane. This allows for best-in-class precision and productivity compared to other aerial application methods. The large capacity and fuel-efficient gas engine give it the ability to seed and apply crop protection across large acreages.
