Yondor Diamonds. Fine Diamond Manufacturers

Driven by Our Passion for Natural Large Diamonds

Yondor Diamonds, led by the Serrouya Family, is a third-generation diamond manufacturer and trader with over 40 years of expertise. Known for our passion for large natural diamonds, unmatched professionalism, and commitment to excellence, we offer one of the industry’s most extensive inventories of high-quality natural diamonds. Specializing in natural diamonds ranging from 2.00 to 60.00+ carats and above, we feature a diverse selection of fancy shapes, fancy colors, and a full spectrum of color and clarity options.

With operations in key diamond hubs— USA, Israel, Hong Kong, and India—Yondor Diamonds combines global reach with an extensive and diverse stock to meet the unique needs of our clients worldwide. Our dedication to professionalism, flexibility, and exceptional service has made us a trusted partner in the natural diamond industry.

Manufacturing: Sourcing with Integrity

Yondor Diamonds was built on the vision and passion of the late Mr. Eli Serrouya, whose commitment to rough sourcing and diamond manufacturing continues to guide us today. Now led by his son, Mr. Doron Serrouya, Yondor Diamonds remains dedicated to upholding the highest standards of craftsmanship and quality.

Every diamond we manufacture is sourced, cut, and polished in Israel or New York, where our expert craftsmen transform the finest natural rough diamonds into exquisite polished diamonds. Each rough diamond undergoes meticulous analysis, planning, cutting, and polishing. By combining vision with advanced technologies, the brilliance and beauty within each diamond is unlocked, setting a recognized industry standard for cutting excellence. Ethical sourcing is ensured, with full compliance to all industry standards, including the Kimberley Process, Responsible Jewellery Council (RJC) guidelines, maintaining integrity and transparency throughout the entire supply chain.

Our Diamonds

Yondor Diamonds offers an extensive and diverse selection of high-quality natural diamonds, designed to meet the varied needs of a global clientele, catering to different market segments and cultural preferences. Ranging from 2 to 60 carats and beyond, our diamonds are available in a broad spectrum of shapes, colors, and clarities. Each diamond is GIA certified and traceable to its rough origin, ensuring consistent quality and full transparency.

Renowned for an exceptional inventory of large diamonds, Yondor offers a wide collection of diamonds from 10 carats and up, including both fancy shapes and rounds. The collection also features an extensive range of fancy-colored diamonds and D-flawless diamonds, offering tailored solutions for the diverse demands of the market.

With over 40 years of industry experience, Yondor diamonds is dedicated to providing tailored solutions to meet the specific requirements of professionals in the diamond trade.

Social Responsibility

Yondor Diamonds is committed to observe community values and is actively promoting human rights, help communities and protect our natural environment. We are a committed equal opportunity employer and abide by all fair labor practices. We ensure that our activities does not directly or indirectly violate human rights in any country. We do not tolerate child labor nor forced labor, We maintain the highest standards in matters of health and safety, and make sure to donate and aid to alleviate those in need, advance education and community events and encourage our employees to volunteer.

Responsible Sourcing

Yondor Diamonds practice Responsible Sourcing policy which is incorporated into agreements with all suppliers. Our Diamonds are sourced in line with the requirements of our Code of conduct, covering a wide range of environmental & social criteria. We support the Kimberley Process and sourced only from mines & other manufacturers that adhere to the strictest labor, trade, and environmental standards.Yondor Diamonds practice a demonstrable due diligence procedure, including clear identification of our own suppliers, “Know Your Customer” (KYC) details, and terms of business and any proof of provenance of materials and undertake risk assessment in order to identify any risks in our supply chain which may be contrary to the company’s Responsible Sourcing policy, Our tightly-controlled diamond supply chain also ensures we never sell any treated or synthetic diamonds.

Business Ethics

We are striving to be a responsible business that meets the highest standards of ethics and professionalism. Yondor Diamonds complies with applicable laws and regulations and ensures that all its business operations are legitimate. Our company does not tolerate any kind of corruption or money laundering and promotes Anti-bribery and anti-corruption practices. We conduct our business with integrity, Respect toward the consumer and respect to human rights. We make sure to provide fair labor practices for our employees as well as the employees of our suppliers, this includes equal pay for equal work and living wage compensation initiatives. Ethical labor practices for suppliers include the use of products that have been certified as meeting fair trade standards.
