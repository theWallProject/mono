Recommended bytravelmyway

Hotel Zenith

Sunny Beach, Bulgaria - Zenith Hotel in Sunny Beach is a small but beautifully renovated and very modern hotel. Everything is clean, tidy, and comfortable. The rooms are spacious, with a separate shower from the toilet, and each room has a terrace with chairs and a table—perfect for relaxing. I highly recommend the all-inclusive option because the food is fantastic. There’s a great variety: fresh salads, plenty of main courses, delicious desserts at dinner, and a very rich breakfast buffet with many choices. There’s one pool, but the hotel is right next to the beach, so you can walk straight from the hotel to the sea. The evening shows aren’t very impressive, but there’s a lovely outdoor area where you can enjoy a drink by the pool and listen to cozy music after the animation program. Overall, it’s a small, modern, beautiful, and very comfortable hotel that offers great value and a relaxing stay in Sunny Beach.
