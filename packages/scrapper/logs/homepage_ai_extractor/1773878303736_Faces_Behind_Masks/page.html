Faces Behind Masks - About Our Initiative

Faces Behind Masks is a global non-profit initiative born in Tel Aviv, Israel. Our mission is to provide medical staff in hospitals all over the world with sets of FREE personalized Smiling Face stickers.


A photograph of each staff member’s face can be worn over protective clothing, so that patients see faces instead of just masks. This simple act helps patients and medical teams feel more connected.


 

Rafi Albo

Founder & “Makes Things Happen” Officer
