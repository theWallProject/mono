Affogata: The PX Infrastructure

Affogata unifies fragmented customer touchpoints into a single, structured data layer. The platform transforms unstructured feedback from community, social, and support channels into real-time insights consumed in three ways:

Website / Dashboard

A dedicated interface for Product, Marketing, and CSX teams to visualize Customer Insights and Business Impact.

Data Warehouse (DIL / API)

Direct integration with Snowflake, Databricks, and BigQuery, providing structured tables ready for internal BI and data storage.

MCP -> AI Chatbots

A direct connection to Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT, allowing teams to query customer data through AI copilots without a dashboard.
