A versatile brain interpretation platform that can be used across a broad range of applications.

  • Parkinson’s
  • Alzheimer’s
  • Cognitive decline
  • Epilepsy
  • Stress/anxiety

Brain health is
medicine’s final frontier

The brain is still almost totally opaque to modern medicine, the only organ in the human body for which we have inadequate tools and diagnostic tests, no satisfactory metrics for assessing healthy and unhealthy brain function, poor early detection, and limited intervention options. In addition, we’ve historically been unable to test brain functionality during a standard daily routine, with real rather than simulated sensory stimulation and activity.


Neurosteer is changing all that!

A wireless, pocket-sized, medical-grade,

brain monitor

Neurosteer uses an adhesive forehead strip connected to a pocket-sized sensor that wirelessly transmits data to the cloud for signal processing. Advanced algorithms then decompose the single hdrEEG channel for real-time analysis and display on a web-based dashboard.


This will allow more affordable screening, continuous patient monitoring, and optimized neurostimulation and drug selection, all leading to improved neurological treatment and outcome of clinical trials.

A comprehensive
brain health platform

Neurosteer brings the century-old EEG into the 21st century by developing the world’s first wearable, medical-grade brain activity interpretation platform for a wide range of medical, wellness and lifestyle applications. 


The company’s intends to obtain clearance for the early detection of cognitive decline and other neurological disorders, and the management and mitigation of stress, anxiety and pain at home.

Decomposing the symphony of the brain

Just as a symphony is played on different musical instruments, the symphony of brain wave activity plays out on a collection of functional neural networks.

﻿

Neurosteer’s single-channel hdrEEG aims to distinguish the activity of the different networks in the same way a single human ear can distinguish between the instruments of an orchestra, and in both cases, based not only on the fundamental frequency but also on the harmonics.

Delivering a new standard
of care in brain health

Neurosteer intends to deliver a new brain activity representation that can be used to screen, assess and monitor a large number of neurological conditions. Just as blood pressure maintenance went from the specialized purview of the cardiologist to the GP with the now ubiquitous blood pressure monitor, Neurosteer is offering an off-the-shelf product that can become a standard general-purpose neurological diagnostic tool for use not only by neurologists but also by anyone in the medical community.

Initial screening to continuous monitoring

Compared to a functional MRI – the current “gold standard” for brain activity screening and assessment – Neurosteer is inexpensive, portable and unobtrusive. It can be used in a clinical setting for initial assessment and then as part of a patient’s normal daily routine for up to continuous monitoring. It allows the patient to perform cognitive or physical tasks while being monitored and delivers interpreted results in real-time.
