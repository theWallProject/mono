What do we do for the climate?

Approximately 30% of greenhouse gas emissions (GHG) are tied to Hard-to-abate sectors, including aviation, shipping, and other heavy industries such as Chemical and Metal fabrication.

In these sectors, today's inefficient processes are the obstacles to creating sustainable carbon-based products. 

Our mission at Carbonade is to surmount these hurdlesand pave the way for a climate-conscious future.
