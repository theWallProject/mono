Microgil is Israel’s leading manufacturer of industrial minerals with over 50 years of expertise in the industry, producing a market-leading range of materials including white calcium carbonate powders and fine accurate marble aggregate.

 
The company is renowned for its uncompromising commitment to quality – sourcing the world’s best raw materials, producing coarse, fine and ultra fine powders & granulated accurate aggregates of consistent quality, and offering unrivalled service. We provide the industry’s most qualified technical support, whether it’s advice on choosing the right filler, or maximizing its benefits to customer specification during the manufacturing process.


Our AVGIL calcium carbonate powders are used in the manufacture of paper, plastics, rubber, caulks & sealants, adhesives, paint, herbicides and insecticides whereas GRANULITE accurate aggregate is designed for applications that range from playground surfacing to desalination systems.

All of our processes are certified compliant with international standards of quality assurance management, and our dedication to quality encompasses worker safety and environmental protection at the highest level.
