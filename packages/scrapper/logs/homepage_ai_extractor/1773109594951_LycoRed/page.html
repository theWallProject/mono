Lycored helps brands transition from artificial to natural color solutions with clean label, plant-based colorants sourced from tomato lycopene and beta-carotene. Designed as effective alternatives to synthetic dyes, our natural food colors deliver vibrant, stable performance in real-world applications while meeting increasing regulatory and consumer demand for transparency. Backed by scientific validation and formulation expertise, our portfolio combines natural color stability with clinically supported health ingredients, helping brands replace artificial additives and future-proof innovation with confidence.
