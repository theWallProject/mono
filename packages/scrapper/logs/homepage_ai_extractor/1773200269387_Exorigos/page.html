Exorigos


Exorigos comes from the Latin words Ex and Origos meaning from the origins, we bring the original content to the people
Learn, laugh and get excited with our fresh content

Politically Corrects

Here at politically corrects we are trying to be different than the traditional news outlet, we are striving to bring you the latest political news that won’t always show up in the mainstream media. With the help of our very talented and creative writers, we find those inside stories that are hidden but still make an impact. Come join us while we try and find a different point of view on the latest news and updates from the political world.

Health And Wellness News

Health and Wellness News was born out of a passion for sharing ideas about healthy living, eating, and exercise, and as a means to bring into light new finds in this ever-changing area. We are committed to bringing you the highest -quality content to have you feeling and looking your absolute best.

Traveler Door

Traveler door was born out of a passion for traveling, and as a means to bring some of the wonderful places this world has to offer. We at the traveler door are committed to bringing you high-quality content on the most interesting places in the world. We have gathered amazing content writers, all passionate about traveling, and they are here to share with you their experiences, and their recommendations for places to visit that will take your breath away.

The Rockets Science

The Rockets Science caters to its mainstream audience by sharing the latest tech news ranging from topics about AI to new technologies. Our team focuses on delivering up-to-date news to keep our readers informed on the rising growth of tech products and how they’re being integrated into almost any industry.

Doc Journals

We created Doc journals because we felt there was a need for a new and original content website that is all purely medically related. Our writers, who are well versed in diets, sickness, and exercise, will keep you up to date on all the latest technologies, procedures and medicines out there. We pride ourselves on our talented and knowledgeable writers who find the latest news and trends the medical field has to offer.

Careers

PPC User Acquisition Manager

Responsibilities

  • Create, monitor and optimize campaigns on different platforms
  • Conduct market research to identify new growth opportunities. Plan, launch, test and optimize new marketing channels and initiatives
  • Collaborate closely with the content team as well as the tech team to improve user experience and results
  • Continually provide creative best practices and feedback to drive high-performing advertising campaigns
  •  Help develop an in-house system to automate the buying process

Requirements

  •  At least 1 year of experience in running large-scale, performance-oriented PPC campaigns for a large publisher – A must
  • Exceptional analytical and quantitative skills, Strong Excel skills
  • Ability to perform in a fast-paced, start-up environment
  •  Bachelor’s Degree in Economics, Business Administration, Industrial engineering or an analytical field
  • Fluent English
