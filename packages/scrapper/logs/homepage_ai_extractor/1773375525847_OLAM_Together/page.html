What in the world is OLAM?
WHO WE ARE

OLAM is a network of Jews and Israelis committed to global development and service - practitioners, philanthropists, leaders, and young adults.

Together, we strive to grow our collective impact, raise awareness within the Jewish community about the importance of supporting vulnerable populations around the world, and live out our most deeply held values.
