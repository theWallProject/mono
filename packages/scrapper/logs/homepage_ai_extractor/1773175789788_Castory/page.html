What we do


Castory dramatically cuts down rework costs.

Castory is a seamless progress tracking and documentation platform, allowing you to control every square meter of your Jobsite throughout the construction, and prevent costly errors.

Using a simple wearable camera, we capture imagery from the construction site and automatically map it to the floor plans. This way the site can be inspected remotely anytime, anywhere, while viewing identified issues within our collaborative platform.

How it works

Collect Data

Data is seamlessly collected from the construction site, in a straightforward process. Put the camera on the hardhat, start recording the video, and simply walk the Jobsite.

React On Time

Your project's blueprints are automatically linked with up-to-date data from the site. You can now know the project is on track, and gain actionable insights on time.

Get The Full Picture

View the project's progress and updates in real-time. We provide dashboards showing 360° documentation, open tasks, important upcoming events and more.

Progress Tracking


Easily assess completion rates

Captured data is converted into measurable site progress, enabling you to respond promptly to potential delays.

Tracking progress manually can be time-consuming. Castory automates this process, allowing you to easily compare completed work with your schedule, helping to minimize delays.
