ABOUT

PIC-PLAST DESIGNS, MANUFACTURES, AND DISTRIBUTES SOLARIG® AGRICULTURAL AND AQUACULTURE COVERS AND STRUCTURES GLOBALLY.

YOUR SUCCESS IS OUR PRIORITY. WHEN YOU SUCCEED, WE SUCCEED!

OUR CORE VALUES

DURABILITY

Our commitment to durability ensures reliable, long-lasting solutions for commercial greenhouses, tunnels, net houses, and aquaculture structures, delivering years of dependable performance.

Sustainability

We offer innovative structures and agrotechnical solutions with a holistic approach to sustainability. Our protected cultivation systems are designed to optimize natural light, utilize environmentally friendly materials, and minimize the consumption of valuable resources such as water, minerals, and energy. consumption.  

COST EFFECTIVENESS

Our cost-effective Solarig covers deliver optimal agronomic solutions for a wide range of crops, including flowers, vegetables, berries, cherries, grapes, and aquaculture farming, ensuring maximum efficiency and value.
