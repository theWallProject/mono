360 Degrees of Freedom

Based in Israel, Sharon Tuvia is a family owned global manufacturer of mechanical assemblies, parts and components. Combining creativity with accuracy, we have served hundreds of customers worldwide over the past six decades, providing them with advanced solutions for a wide range of applications. Established in 1959 by Tuvia Sharon, and now managed by the second generation, the team brings with it a strong legacy of innovation, craftsmanship, integrity, and personal care for each customer, as well as vast experience in game changing technologies such as additive manufacturing.
