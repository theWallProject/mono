Our Story

Let's Bench started out in 2011 as a small personalized bencher company and now includes a full range of products that transforms events into memorable experiences. Our brand DNA is clean and modern, and our goal is to bring contemporary design to your family simcha.

Perfect for Every Event

Create a unique, one-of-a-kind bencher for any event. Let’s Bench custom Grace-after-Meal booklets are the perfect wedding bencher, bar mitzvah bencher, bat mitzvah bencher, anniversary bencher and school bencher. Especially great for for fundraising, synagogue dinners, Israel missions, group events and more.

Several Nusach Options

Let’s Bench custom benchers currently include Ashkenaz, traditional egalitarian, edot hamizrach (Sephardi), Chabad Nusach Ari, Sfard (Chassidic Ashkenaz), French Ashkenaz, Yemenite (Temani Baladi), Italian (nusach) and Reform text. If you are interested in a different text, please let us know and we’ll do our best to accommodate you. We also have the latest version of the Anim Zemirot bencher nusach (created by Leah Solomon) and the Kolot bencher with gender positive text that includes both Hebrew and English and a combination of masculine and feminine terminology.

Additional Packages

Our basic custom bencher includes Birkat Hamazon (Grace after Meals), Sheva Brachot (the seven wedding blessings) and Brachot Achronot. Upgrade options can include zemirot, Shabbat and holiday additions, extra photo pages as well as Friday night prayers.

Unique and special

Special care was given to every detail of creating our custom laminated benchers, siddurim and cards. We use a modern, classic font that is clear and easy-to-read. This is a high quality birkon that is functional as well as a highlight of any event.

The perfect gift for your event

Consider gifting Let's Bench custom products. Customized and personalized benchers, siddurim and ketubot are the perfect present. Let's Bench customized items are a great personalized keepsake for any simcha.
