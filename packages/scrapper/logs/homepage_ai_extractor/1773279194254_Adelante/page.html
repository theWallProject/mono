How it works

1

Resolves real support flows end-to-end

The agent isn't just answering FAQs – it's completing real ecommerce flows, in any language your customers use.

  • Where Is My Order (WISMO) – Tracks orders, delivery status, delays and exceptions
  • Returns and exchanges – Checks order details, applies your policy, initiates returns
  • Coupons and promotions – Explains discounts, checks eligibility, fixes checkout issues
  • Delivery issues and damaged items – Captures evidence, guides resolution
  • Subscriptions and login issues – Helps with login problems and subscription changes
2

Acts as a sales assistant when the time is right

Once the immediate problem is solved, the agent can gently move into revenue-driving mode.

  • Product search and discovery – Helps customers find the right product by size, style, use case
  • Smart recommendations – Suggests products based on preferences, stock levels and cart
  • Intelligent follow-ups – Stays with the customer until they complete their journey
3

Less handovers and more human-in-the-loop

The agent works with your team and partners, not instead of them.

  • Human-in-the-loop workflows: Waits on feedback from your team or third parties and continues responding to customers
  • Managed Handovers: Ensure each request really needs a human, and transfer the request with full context.
