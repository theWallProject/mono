Established in 1992, VIM Technologies, Inc. has become the market leader in providing quality products and supplemental services for environmental compliance. Our flagship product, CEMLink 6, offers cutting edge data acquisition and reporting solutions for our ever-changing industry. We developed COMPAS to assist with your quality assurance programs, audits, training, reporting, analysis, and other compliance needs. When you choose VIM for your air compliance solutions, you will find a partner, not just a vendor.
