Lightbits Enables Cloud Hyperscale Efficiency Without Compromises

Unmatched Scalability and Flexibility

Seamless adaptability without the limitations of legacy systems, enabling independent scaling of compute and storage resources while supporting a single cluster for CSI, Cinder, KVM, and bare metal simultaneously, with the added control of Quality of Service (QoS).

Effortless Resolution of App Time-Outs

Lightbits delivers the industry’s best performance and consistent low latency at scale, ensuring seamless data access for critical workloads, from databases and AI pipelines to OpenStack and OpenShift environments, while reducing the risk of application time-outs.

Unrivaled High Availability & Resiliency

Seamless storage server failover that ensures continuous service, safeguards business continuity, and protects your business from data loss.

Exceptional Cost-Efficiency

Reduce CapEx and OpEx storage overhead without compromising application performance. With Lightbits, no proprietary hardware is needed and you get the industry’s best hardware efficiency.

Cloud-Native Integration

Seamless integration with popular orchestration environments such as Kubernetes, OpenStack, OpenShift, VMware, and others. Lightbits simplifies the deployment and management of persistent storage across private and public cloud platforms enabling a unified, or hybrid, approach to data center infrastructure.
