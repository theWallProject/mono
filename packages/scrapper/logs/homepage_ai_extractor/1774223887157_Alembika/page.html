Alembika offers beautiful, innovative clothes in ageless styles, available in a wide range of sizes for Missy and Plus proportions, and designed to fit and flatter the 50+ woman. Comfort is key for both younger and older women who embody a diverse range of body types, and Alembika's mission is to provide meticulously designed clothing for everyone, including women over 50, that is both comfortable and chic, that is easy to care for and machine washable, and that builds your wardrobe with timeless, go-to designer fashion you'll wear throughout the seasons, year after year. Mature women who like classic style, modern edge and everything in between will find so many different collections and capsules to shop from! You'll find dreamy soft jersey knit essentials that create the foundation to every look, Urban style that's dripping with Tel Aviv street flair, retro-inspired designs with bold patterns and colors that pop, wrinkle-resistant styles that travel like a pro and feature built-in UPF50 sunscreen, dressy evening wear that adds elegance to every event and special occasion—and so much more! Discover high quality dresses, tops, tunics, sweaters, jackets and pants—and be sure to check out Iconic Stretch Jeans and best-selling Punto Pants, so you can discover why these amazing designs have a serious cult following. Embrace your creative side and treat yourself to unique fashion for women with discerning taste. Enjoy shopping!
