Get Started

Create a plan without compromising your vision

From configuring the plan to onboarding the first group, Yuzu has worked relentlessly to streamline the process and help you launch more groups.

Step 1

Build The Plan

Design with our Benefits Builder

Add tiers, accumulators, and benefits custom to your plan.

Integrate with partners

Add PBM, Stop Loss, and other high-priority vendors through streamlined connections. Enable a single source of truth and efficient data flow.

Create materials intelligently

Create ID card, welcome and support materials from scratch or use pre-existing templates.

Step 2

Launch The Plan

Launch group entirely on web-based portal

Add the group and connect banking and eligibility in minutes.

Add the partner stack

Update vendors and fees in one click.

Seamless document handling

Auto-generate custom SPDs and SBCs and eSign in the portal.

Step 3

Run plans with confidence

Once the plan is live, Yuzu provides unmatched transparency and usability.

Payment Infrastructure

No latency and maximum transparency into where dollars are spent. Custom auto-ledgering system to track every penny in real-time.

In-house claims engine

Every line of code is owned by Yuzu. Timely and reliable adjudication in accordance to plan rules. A single owner for all claim types and remittances.

Human-centered portals

Portals and communications for employers, members, and vendors in one place, built from ground-up.

Support tools

Custom call routing, workflow alerts, case management software, proactive tools, and more built into the portal

Monitor performance with best in class reporting

No black boxes. Raw data to drill into the details and analytics to see the big picture, available at your fingertips. In real-time so you can catch things early.

You are the face of the plan

Yuzu’s white-labeling ability means everything is tailored to your identity. Fully branded portals and communications at launch.
