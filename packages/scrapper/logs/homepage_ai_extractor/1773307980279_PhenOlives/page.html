PhenOlives

Transforming the olive oil industry’s byproducts into high-value ingredients and components.

Get in touch

Did you know that only 15% of olives become olive oil? The rest, a staggering 85% , is considered by product.​

At PhenOlives, we are revolutionizing the industry by transforming 100% of these byproducts into valuable, sustainable products, ensuring full utilization within a circular economy model.

PhenOlives was born from a shared mission within Innovalley, an innovation hub nestled in Israel’s Valley of Springs. Several years ago, alongside our sister company, the Assi Olive Oil Mill, we faced a challenge that affects the entire olive oil industry: the overwhelming volume of waste left behind after pressing olives.

Learn more about our solution

Transforming Olive Oil Byproducts into Value

PhenOlives’ patented Total Solution Impact (TSI) system seamlessly integrates within an existing olive mill infrastructure, efficiently capturing and processing the mill’s wastewater and pomace. This continuous process breaks down all olive mill byproduct types into their core components, unlocking new opportunities for sustainability and profitability.
