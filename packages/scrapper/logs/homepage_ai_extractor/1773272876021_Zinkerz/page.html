How Do I Get Started

Take the first step by scheduling your consultation. Whether your student is a high school freshman, senior, transfer, or postgraduate applicant, we create a personalized roadmap to guide them every step of the way.

1

Book a Consultation

Meet with a Zinkerz advisor to discuss your goals — academic improvement, test prep, or admissions strategy.

2

Diagnostic & Profile Review

We evaluate where you are today:
Tutoring: Skills check to spot strengths and areas to improve.
Counseling: Review of your admissions profile, courses, activities, and application timeline.

3

Meet & Plan Together

We sit down with you to design your personalized roadmap — setting up your counseling plan, test prep schedule, and overall academic preparation plan so every piece works toward your long-term goals.

4

Begin Your 1:1 Program

Start tailored tutoring sessions or counseling guidance, with ongoing check-ins and access to our student platform to track progress and stay organized.

College Admissions FAQ

When should I start college admissions prep?

At Zinkerz, we believe the sooner you start admissions prep, the better! Our personalized approach to college counseling is designed to meet you where you are. Whether you’re ready to jump in early or just want to make sure you’re staying ahead of the curve, our dedicated team will work closely with you to craft a tailored plan that fits your goals and timeline.

Your journey to college success begins with the support of a community that truly understands your needs. Schedule a consultation with us today, and let’s explore the path that’s right for you!

Why do families choose Zinkerz?

Families choose Zinkerz because we offer a highly personalized experience, tailoring our approach to each student’s unique goals and needs. We take the complexity out of the college application process, guiding families through every step with clarity and care. Our commitment to fostering a supportive, community-driven environment ensures that students feel confident and empowered throughout the journey.

What truly speaks to our impact? Parents who work with us for their first child often return for their younger ones—it’s the ultimate testament to the trust and satisfaction we build with every family we support!

What GPA do college admissions look at?

GPA requirements differ across colleges, and understanding the difference between weighted and unweighted GPAs is key. At Zinkerz, we know grades are just one part of a student’s application. Our team will help you navigate both types of GPAs and guide you toward schools that align with your strengths.

Schedule a consultation, and let’s find the best fit for you!

How much does Zinkerz’s college counseling cost?

Schedule a FREE consultation at zinkerz.com/consultation to chat about our personalized counseling plans.

What does a typical Zinkerz session look like?

Each session with your personal counselor is tailored to your unique goals. Whether it’s building your resume or crafting an application plan, we focus on understanding who you are and guiding you toward your college aspirations every step of the way.

How do you help international students apply to U.S. colleges?

We assist international students in finding the perfect U.S. college fit through personalized, one-on-one sessions. Our counselors guide you through researching schools, completing the Common Application, and meeting U.S. deadlines, ensuring your path is clear.

With extensive experience working with international students, we’re familiar with all the extra requirements, from TOEFL and IELTS to navigating the unique challenges of the U.S. application process. We’re here to support you every step of the way.
