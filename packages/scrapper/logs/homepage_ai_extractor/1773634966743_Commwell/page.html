Legacy envisioned in 1972

“Almost all the sophistication of modern medicine is located in the hospitals however we spend most of our time at home, an environment that is totally deprived of any medical sophistication. This paradox of modern health care has to change.
The aging of population, the prohibitive cost of medical care (mainly hospital driven), the development of sophistication physiological sensors as well as the expected boom of the telecommunication industry will allow to move sophisticated health care out of the hospital into the natural environment“ (1972).

Telepsychiatry
By keeping psychiatric patients connected and providing continuous support and guidance with reminders and instructions Commwell's platform is enabling a gradual re-introduction to society with a holistic approach that enhances patient outcomes and promotes mental health and well-being.
Home hospitalization
Developed by Doctors for Doctors - Commwell empowers doctors with innovative technologies, liberating them from mundane tasks. This enables quality care, reducing hospitalizations and improving patient outcomes. With a wide range of compatible vital-sign measurement devices, Commwell ensures professional continuum of care for discharged patients in the comfort of their homes.
Chronic patient management
Our digital health assistant enhances chronic disease management, enabling caregivers to detect and address health issues early, preventing hospitalizations. It improves compliance, patient satisfaction, and quality of life by offering a user-friendly, flexible solution for condition management.
Aging care
Commwell is transforming the care in nursing homes and aging facilities, improving health outcomes and enhancing elderly patients' quality of life. With user-friendly remote care, even at home, patients receive personalized guidance for routine activities, enabling proactive care from dedicated teams.
Rural & remote care
Our autonomous digital clinics provide triage, screening, and care plan monitoring in remote areas lacking healthcare workers or nearby hospitals. Remote clinical teams review patient data, utilize video conferencing for further investigation, and provide instructions to the onsite team.
Rescue & emergency care
Commwell's technologies enhance patient outcomes in emergencies or remote/dangerous settings. They enable rapid, accurate diagnosis and treatment, regardless of location. With fast access to vital signs and clinical consultations, rescue teams obtain crucial data, make assessments, and communicate with remote hospitals for guidance.
Clinical trials
Health eCare facilitates clinical research with comprehensive protocol design, including medication monitoring, reminders, clinical/survey questions, symptom updates, vital sign measurements, and care team visits (in-person or online), ensuring treatment management, patient compliance, data collection, and analytics generation.
Concierge medicine
Giving health care professionals the exact tools to offer their patients experienced personalized, convenient, and unhurried healthcare with our concierge medicine service, enabling unparalleled access to their dedicated physician and comprehensive care tailored to their unique health needs.
Remote rehabilitation
Transforming rehabilitation by offering personalized plans accessible on personal devices, enabling active patient participation and seamless tracking of vital signs. Healthcare professionals remotely guide patients through their rehab programs, ensuring independent progress with exceptional care and support.
eTriage
Streamline walk-in patient assessments with our cutting-edge triage solution. Our automated, voice-guided system allows multiple patients to complete questionnaires simultaneously, ensuring quick data upload to EMR and empowering healthcare providers to deliver efficient and high-quality care while minimizing wait times.
