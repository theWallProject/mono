abioSCOPE & PSP Biomarker: True enabler of early sepsis detection

PSP runs exclusively on the abioSCOPE®, an award-winning rapid diagnostics platform that uses nanofluidics to deliver lab-quality results from a drop of blood within minutes. Seamlessly integrated into routine clinical workflows, our PSP assay delivers fast, accurate, and actionable results across Emergency & Critical care settings.  By measuring PSP levels, which inform a patient’s risk of sepsis, healthcare professionals can activate Sepsis bundles earlier and improve patient outcomes.
