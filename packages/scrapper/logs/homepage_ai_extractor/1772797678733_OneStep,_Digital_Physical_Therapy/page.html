Turn real-world movement into clinical action

OneStep transforms any smartphone into a clinical-grade motion lab. Detect risk and progress, drive proactive care, and deliver better outcomes with measurable financial and operational impact.

Book demo
Watch video

Trusted by Leading Organizations

Every step tells a story.

Falls. Chronic pain. Neurological conditions. Heart health. Even cancer. Mobility-related health challenges impact 1 in 4 people. Gait (the way that we move) is one of the strongest indicators of these conditions, yet it is rarely measured in routine care.

OneStep makes real-world gait analysis easy, accessible, and scalable - using just a smartphone. By turning movement into a measurable health signal, we help solve some of healthcare’s biggest problems and improve quality of life for millions.

Bringing objective outcome data to every level of healthcare

OneStep empowers everyone to understand movement, act earlier, and deliver better care.

Simple by design

OneStep uses the smartphone your patient already carries - no extra sensors, no calibration, no hassle. Just slip the phone in a pocket and get clinical-grade data in seconds (also captured passively).

Insights that drive care

Our AI turns every step into actionable, clinically validated insights - fall risk, balance, surgical recovery, cognitive function and more - delivered in real time and directly into your workflow.

Patient trust and engagement

When patients see their own Walk Score in real time, they understand their care - and take action. That simple moment sparks motivation and drives better outcomes over time.

Strong and streamlined documentation

OneStep standardizes assessments, delivers clean, defensible documentation, and saves valuable time by enabling clinicians and non-clinicians alike to measure with confidence.

Measurable financial and clinical outcomes

Organizations use OneStep to guide care transitions and power hybrid care models - increasing revenue per patient while improving outcomes and efficiency across the continuum of care.

Category defining solutions across the care continuum

Fall Prevention

Eliminate falls

One in four adults experiences a fall each year, resulting in $50B in healthcare related costs and countless lives lost. OneStep is the only tool that makes fall risk visible early, allowing for timely intervention and prevention.

Orthotics & Prosthetics

Empower every patient to move naturally

Every fitting matters. OneStep shows how orthotics and prosthetics impact real-world mobility - revealing asymmetries and imbalances, so you can adjust earlier, with confidence, and achieve better outcomes.

Joint & Bone Health

Enhance bone and joint health

Joint surgeries cost billions, and aren't always necessary. OneStep tracks real recovery patterns, flags when progress stalls, and helps clinicians intervene early or avoid surgery altogether.

COGnitive decline

Assess cognitive function seamlessly

Cognitive decline often manifests through changes in movement. OneStep tracks subtle shifts daily - rather than every six months - empowering earlier, more informed, and proactive care.

Longevity, balance and Pain

Inspire healthy aging

Movement is key to independence. OneStep helps individuals stay steady, strong, and pain-free, providing valuable and motivating insights into mobility for a healthier lifestyle.

Research & PHarma

Turn movement into evidence

Gather motion analysis directly from any smartphone, enhancing subjective results with objective data to support your pharmaceutical and research product development.
