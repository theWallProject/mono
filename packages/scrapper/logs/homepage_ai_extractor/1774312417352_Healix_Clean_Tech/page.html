We rescue our planet from plastic pollution by returning it to the economy

Our blue planet is facing one of the biggest disasters in human history. Plastic pollution has become a significant threat to the health and survival of our oceans, wildlife, and ourselves. It is a global crisis that requires immediate action.

Our recycling services

Turning the Tide

At Healix, we are determined to solve the plastic problem that is destroying marine life and polluting our planet. We help break the plastic wave by creating a circular future for plastic waste from fishing and farming using our advanced purification technology. We transform used ropes and nets into certified circular plastic for the global manufacturing supply chain.

Retrieve

In close partnership with waste management companies and NGOs, we retrieve plastic waste from traceable sources, mainly from the fishing and farming industry.

Recycle

Healix transforms these waste streams into certified circular plastics using our advanced purification technology.

Return

We keep plastic out of the environment by keeping plastic in the economy. This reduces plastic pollution and creates a circular solution that helps our customers reduce their carbon footprint and use virgin plastic.

Our Message

Plastic is here to stay; what will and needs to change is how we use and dispose of it. Companies of the future know that their success depends on their relationship with society and the environment. As a father of two sons, I am deeply inspired by the proverb, “We do not inherit the Earth from our ancestors; we borrow it from our children.” I am convinced we can change our industry for a more sustainable future. However, circularity is a team effort. We cannot do it alone, and we need the help of like-minded trail blazers to push the boundaries and spark change.

“The greatest threat to our planet is the belief that someone else will save it.”

Robert Swan
