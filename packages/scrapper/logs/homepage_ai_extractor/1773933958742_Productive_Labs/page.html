Single Page Application

SPA is a web application, meaning that the content on the web-page is managed dynamically by an app, rather than being managed by a server, that has to send full web-pages on every user interaction.

In your day to day routine, you use SPAs more than you realize, some examples include: google maps, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

The cutting-edge technologies that all the biggest companies are adapting to - you already have them!

SPA is an extraordinary UX before everything else. By creating an application that runs naturally inside your browser - you will never wait for pages to load ever again!
