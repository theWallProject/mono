Everything you need to know about working with us

Palmidos is a technology consulting and development company based in Tel Aviv. We help businesses build custom software solutions, integrate AI into their operations, and transform their digital presence. Our team combines technical expertise with creative thinking to deliver solutions that actually work for your business.

Most of our projects are delivered within 90 days or less. The exact timeline depends on the scope and complexity, but we're known for moving fast without cutting corners. We'll give you a realistic timeline upfront and keep you updated throughout the process.

We offer four core services: AI Integration (smart automation and data insights), Custom Software Development (web and mobile applications), Creative Studio (UI/UX design and branding), and Tech Consulting (strategy and problem-solving). We also have our own AI products - DocBrain AI for document management and Whatsi AI for WhatsApp customer support.

Three things: we actually listen (100% customer satisfaction isn't accidental), we move fast (90 days or less), and we stick around (we build lasting partnerships, not one-off projects). Our team combines Israeli tech expertise with real business understanding - we're not just coders, we're problem solvers.
