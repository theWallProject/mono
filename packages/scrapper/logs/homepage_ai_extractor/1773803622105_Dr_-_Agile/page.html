WHO WE ARE

The leading Israeli Agile consulting group

Our coaching spirit

Dr. Agile LTD is an industry leader in Agile transformation, consulting and mentoring. Since 2004, we have consistently improved the business results of a variety of organizations in the Hi-Tech, Fin-Tech, Medical, IT, Insurance & Defence. As the largest and highest-impact player in Israel, our group consists of 25 coaches with multidisciplinary skills and vast and diverse industry experience.

Responsible Agile Transformation

It is our strong belief that “Being Agile” requires more than following ceremonies.  Agile transformation is a powerful accelerator to your business, that touches the core of your enterprise culture. Such a transformation must be carried out responsibly, with ongoing watch-out on business outcomes. 

 

Throughout the years, we’ve developed a holistic coaching methodology with careful attention to supporting the transformation with our Change Leadership Team. 

Let us find your agile rhythm!

Tell us what your challenges are. We will match you with our best experienced coaches: we’ll listen to you, jointly tackle your pains, and construct a plan and lead you through the path of execution. 

 

Our coaches will hand-hold you as long as you need, but will also remind you that we’ll not be with you forever – we will gradually teach your staff to steer and hand you over the steering wheel when you feel ready!
