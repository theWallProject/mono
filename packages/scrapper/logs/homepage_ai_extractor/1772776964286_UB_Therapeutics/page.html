About Us

Learn More >>

NGT HealthCare II (“NGT”) is an early-stage medical device and life sciences venture capital fund, investing in promising technologies.

NGT enjoys a very significant risk reduction mechanism , by means of high non-dilutive leverage via Israel’s Innovation Authority (“IIA”).

Close relationships with many of Israel’s leading universities, hospitals and research institutions provide NGT with a rich flow of investment opportunities.

Wide network with multinational corporations enables NGT to create value to its portfolio companies. 

NGT is structured as an impact fund, located in Nazareth, aligned with 3 UN SDGs.

At NGT we are always aiming beyond our horizon, expanding our network,  please be in touch.

Livnat Ben-Zur

Chief Business & Technology Officer

Livnat Ben-Zur

Ms. Ben-Zur brings over 20 years of experience in the medical device and healthcare industries, holding executive positions in management, business and marketing, in the United States and in Israel.
Livnat has an extensive knowhow in spearheading global product development projects, and leading global business development efforts in various sectors as well as leading development of complex product portfolios from design & development through clinical and production phases up to commercial launch.

Prior to joining NGT, Livnat was the CEO of Corolabs, a deep-lung inhaled therapies, and prior to that the General Manager of Regentis Biomaterials, a tissue regeneration company.
Livnat served as VP Business Development & Director of Women’s Health Program at Galil Medical, where she spearheaded product development processes related to the breast and gynecology applications, as well as managed the clinical program of these applications. Before that Ms. Ben-Zur served as a Director of Marketing of Medinol Ltd, and as Business director of Medical Technology group of Datamonitor Inc.
She holds MBA in marketing and International Business from Tel Aviv University and Bachelor of Sciences, in Biology from Tel Aviv University.

Social Impact

Good Health and Wellbeing

By accommodating unmet medical needs, quality of life improves, lives are saved, patient welfare is improved, and medical costs are reduced. NGT portfolio companies are all in the medical fields, aligned with SDG #3.

Equity, Diversity & Inclusion

By promoting equal opportunities for Arab entrepreneurs within Israel’s vibrant medical startup ecosystem, Israel will become more economically and socially resilient. At the fund level, NGT Healthcare 2 actively seek entrepreneurs from the Arab society: researchers, doctors, scientists in the medical fields. In addition, great efforts and attention are given in promoting employment of Arab men and women ensuring integration of Arab academics in the employment market.

Geographically Underserved

Cultivating an entrepreneurial ecosystem in the Northern periphery of Israel, and catalyzing economic growth is a national mission. Working in Nazareth gives employment opportunities for talented people from the region, jumpstarting their careers. With salaries higher than the national average, social mobility can be achieved.
