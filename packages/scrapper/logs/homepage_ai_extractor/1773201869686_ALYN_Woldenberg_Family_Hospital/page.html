Nearly a Century of Support

American support has been vital to ALYN Hospital's mission of serving children with disabilities in Israel for nearly 100 years.


Since 1932, ALYN Hospital has stood as Israel's premier pediatric and adolescent rehabilitation center, offering hope and transformative care to children facing a wide range of physical challenges.

OUR SUPPORT. THEIR SUCCESS.

Our goal is to help each child with a disability reach their highest possible level of mobility and independence.

HOW YOUR DONATION HELPS

Make a BIG difference in a child’s life.

$36 - One Session Physical Therapy

$180 - Two Weeks of Counseling

$720 - One Month Hydrotherapy

ALYN’s experienced physical therapists focus on improving motor skills and respiratory function, enhancing mobility, stability and endurance.

As part of ALYN’s holistic approach to maximize the child’s social and emotional development, counseling is offered to guide their parents, caregivers, and educators.﻿

ALYN’s hydrotherapy-based rehabilitation for children who are respirator-dependent is the only one of its kind in Israel.

$1800 - One Month Occupational Therapy

ALYN’s experienced occupational therapists focus on helping patients develop life skills.﻿
