Corporate Profile

We strive to generate return on our investment through active board participations and our operational and managerial expertise. Portfolio

ICL Group
(c. 44%) | Fertilizers & Specialty Chemicals

ICL (NYSE: ICL, TASE: ICL) a global manufacturer of products based on unique minerals, fulfills humanity’s essential needs, primarily in three markets: agriculture, food and engineered materials. ICL benefits from a number of unique advantages, including: its vertically integrated activities and complementary and synergistic downstream operations for the production of unique end products; its balanced and varied product portfolio in growing markets; and its worldwide presence with proximity to major global markets in developing regions. IC holds c. 44% of ICL.

Visit the ICL website
AKVA Group
(c. 18%) | Aquaculture

AKVA Group (OB:AKVA), a unique partner with the capability to offer both Sea based and Land based aquaculture operations with complete technical solutions and service. IC holds c. 18% of AKVA Group.

Visit the AKVA website
Prodalim
(c. 23.3%) | Natural Ingredients

Prodalim (TASE:PRDM) is a global company, incorporated in Israel, which is engaged in creating natural solutions for various industries. Its products include concentrates, flavors and fragrances, beverage bases and other natural functional ingredients. In addition, Prodalim is developing innovative activities focused on the emerging world of the dealcoholization of alcoholic beverages.

Visit the Prodalim website
