Manam 3DBIA

Drone-based Asset Inspection Software
Manage infrastructure, assets and related inspections

YOUR BRIDGE TO INNOVATION

Drone's (small UAV) capabilities are the quantum leap in surveying and inspection technology, enabling the collection of highly accurate field data, using a wide range of sensors for data acquisition. Loyal to our promise “Advanced engineering software solutions”; Our team developed in the last five years new "deep technologies" for the civil engineering industry.


 "Manam 3DBIA" is an online inspection software solution, which provides “one-stop-shop” for survey teams and inspectors of Assets such as bridges/dams/building facades/PV-solar-fields/cellular-towers to provide "automated condition reports".

Drone based Asset Inspection & Remote 3D Defects Analysis- Manam has developed a breakthrough technology that integrates automated drone (UAV) asset surveys, support digital twins, a remote 3D defect analysis studio, and engineering insights for maintenance, repair, and more. 

This innovative platform enables efficient, intelligent data processing and provides powerful tools to support the effective maintenance of bridge structures.

Main features include: manage unlimited number of assets and related surveys, track teams progress, upload to cloud drone images and 3D models (digital-twin, 3D mesh, BIM, point cloud), perform inspection  using prepared templates (US/EU bridge/ PV/ cellular towers/ dams/building facades, safety during construction, airplanes, more), use AI assist tools to detect and quantify defects, automatic 3D/2D condition reports (custom templates support, place your own and client logos), data collaboration, give online access to your clients, engineering insights (decision -trees, treatments, costs, deterioration, LCCA), BI charts and more.

Manage efficiently big-scale asset/bridge networks, thus to reduce significantly maintenance costs, and increase network capacity, life span and safety.

The technology has been registered with the US Patent Office "US Patent 10,902,671 B2".

Manam PMS

Click the Tabs below to read more

  • Manam PMS | Pavement Management System
  • Manam Strider

Manam™ PMS assists road network managers in evaluating road pavement condition, planning pavement maintenance and repair.

Manam™ PMS includes a rich GIS environment and allows users to easily manage road surveys, import survey data, analyze current pavement overall condition, predict network future performance, and define expected needs for pavement network maintenance.

The PMS system (also named sometimes RIMS or RAMS) optimizes and applies treatment options over multi year periods - using engineering-mathematical algorithms including PCI (PAVER, ASTM D6433), HMG sections (AASHTO App. J), IRI, Rut, SN etc. and applied budgetary considerations. 

Manam™PMS outputs the results in a variety of formats appropriate to different levels of decision-making.

Manam™STRIDER is a powerful Road Survey Unit, enables the conduction of video surveys in any networks scale and collect data of road condition. 

Manam™STRIDER offers digital imaging at a HD resolution taken from up to 5 cameras from the moving survey vehicle. The cameras are mounted in adjustable housings above the vehicle for easy access, best viewing angle. Typically, the images are captured at 5 meters intervals. the system is offered both portable / fixed installations and includes GPS, DMI, IRI (optional), rut (optional), geometric sensors.

Manam™STRIDER vehicle software package includes a GIS environment, CAD file and aerial photo support, the capability of feeding data into prepared data forms, and the input of photographs with GPS locations. The system includes an exact GPS coordinate location together with additional information including altitude, velocity, DMI, Geometry parameters and more.

MANAM DCSA

UAV Construction Safety Analyzer

Keep your construction sites safe

UAV Construction Safety Analyzer - a quick and professional solution using drones to evaluate the level of safety in construction sites:

  • Fast "automatic flight plan" production onsite, we take hi-res images, we operate the drones in "low signature" (high altitude, so the workers cannot hear the drone) we deliver a true and updated view of construction sites.

  • Trained PE/engineers perform the safety analysis using our proprietary studio software and use the assist of AI technology (use of helmets);

  • Safety Report- We produce a forensic detailed report (date, time, image) that include all hazards to be found in the inspected construction site.

  • Analyzed data is uploaded to cloud and available to our clients via secured web-portal; automatic feedbacks send to our clients about hazardous sites.

About

Manam Applications Ltd located in Caesarea, Israel is a software company. We provide advanced solutions and services for condition inspections and data analysis and economic- engineering insights for infrastructures and road networks:

  • Bridge Condition Inspections- drone based inspections, digital twin creation, 3D studio analysis, Insights, recommendations 

  • Road condition Inspections- road survey unit, data and network analysis, rehabilitation plans

  • Quality Control Management Systems- smart recording (2D/3D) of your project during construction (both QC/QA).

Our team owns unique capabilities and consists experts in various fields: Civil Engineering, Code/Algorithmic- Software engineers, Mathematics, GIS (Geographers), IT (Information Technology), Field Surveyors. 

Manam has been doing bridge and road and surveys since 2004, using state of  the art equipment: RSU (road survey units)  with sensors (location based- DGPS, DMI, multi HD cameras, Lasers,), advanced drones, LIDAR, thermal imaging in the collection of big data to asses condition of road and bridge networks, for both state/city level.
