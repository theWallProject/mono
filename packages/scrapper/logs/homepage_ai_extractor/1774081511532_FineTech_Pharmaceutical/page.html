API Development and Manufacturing

Focusing on development and production of low volume, high value specialty Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients.

We develop novel patented processes, find “by-passes” to existing patents, develop new polymorphs, prepare DMFs and manufacture complex APIs.

Established in 1990 by Dr. Arie Gutman, FineTech has a proven track record of successfully developing and manufacturing low volume, high value complex APIs.  Our International team of highly qualified scientists and engineers work in our state-of-the-art FDA approved facility to meet our customers’ needs.
