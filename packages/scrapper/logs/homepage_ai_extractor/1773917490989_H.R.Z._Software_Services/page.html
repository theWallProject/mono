H.R.Z.

Connecting Healthcare Information Systems

Innovative Medical Imaging and Healthcare IT software solutions based on DICOM® and HL7® industry standards.

Book a Demo

Since 2002 H.R.Z. helped hundreds of companies and organizations to meet their healthcare IT goals and create excellent medical device  and medical IT products.

H.R.Z. products are installed worldwide both as OEM and as end product solutions.

Our Products

MODALIZER-SDK™

DICOM SDK

Add DICOM connectivity to your product with high level DICOM API that hides the complexity behind comprehensive service level commands.

MODALIZER+™

DICOM Converter

Healthcare Imaging Workflow Wizard MODALIZER+™ Converts Images, Video and Documents to the DICOM Standard and Imports them securely to your PACS.

Cirrus-OIA

Cloud Workflow

A cloud-native imaging workflow solution. fast, secure, Mobile ready, offline capable, and fully integrated with existing PACS/RIS systems.

ZiKiT™

PACS Server

A DICOM Archive (PACS), A Modality Worklist Server and an HL7® Message Broker, all sharing the same database and communicating with each other.
