who we are

DigitalC is delivering the internet experience you deserve. Headquartered at the MidTown Tech Hive in Cleveland’s Hough neighborhood and serving communities in both Cleveland and Detroit, DigitalC is the only internet provider built for communities, by communities. Our vision is to change the world one connection at a time by delivering superior internet, offering accessible community space, and providing tailored digital skills training to ensure opportunity in a digital future. We’re on a mission to deploy a premier, state-of-the-art network that’s reliable, sustainable, and bold enough to bridge the digital divide—for good.
