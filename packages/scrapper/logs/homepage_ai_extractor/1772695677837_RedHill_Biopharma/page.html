Innovation in GI, Infectious Diseases and Oncology

RedHill is a specialty pharmaceutical company on a mission to help people live extraordinary lives through the development and commercialization of drugs that address significant unmet medical need.

Learn More

Pandemic Response and Medical Countermeasures

RedHill is at the forefront of pandemic preparedness and medical countermeasures efforts, developing two novel, potentially broad-acting, host-directed, late-stage, oral, small molecule therapies, with intrinsic viral mutation-resistant properties, suitable for stockpiling and easy distribution and administration as a medical countermeasure in the event of biological, chemical, viral or radiological exposure due to pandemics & disease outbreaks, accidents, warfare or terrorism.
