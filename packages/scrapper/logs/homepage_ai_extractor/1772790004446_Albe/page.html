How does Albe work?

Current credit rating models and technologies have very limited capacity to predict borrowers and applicants future behavior.
Albe is different in using:

  • Behavioral psychology and game theory
  • Albe builds a comprehensive digital behavioral profile – no credit history required
  • Effortless and real-time generation of behavioral credit profiles
  • Prediction accuracy validated with over 20,000 applications and monitored loans
