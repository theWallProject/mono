Introducing the IGI 2.0 Hybrid Surgical Navigation and Robotics System.
IGI features the first intelligent dental drill with Spatial Awareness™. A revolution in dental implantology and precision surgeries. Overcoming workflow and clinical challenges, IGI 2.0 streamlines procedures with a user-friendly interface, reducing chair time. It enhances dentist motor skills and achieves improved patient experiences: minimized pain, accelerated recovery, and enhanced aesthetics.

TRAX™

TRAX real time tracking system offers best-in-class, high-accuracy tracking with no observable onscreen lag (20 millisecond refresh rate).” with zero onscreen lag.


TRAX gives precision in confined spaces with complex geometries. Moreover, it seamlessly captures rotational motion, crucial for dentistry.


With each procedure, Trax’s accuracy empowers the navigation system, guiding the dentist to a precise trajectory, angle, and depth.

PRECISE

Robotic Autostop

Prioritizing safety, we have implemented a robotic autostop allowing dentists to receive robotics only where they really need it – at the end-user tool.

The autostop acts as a safeguard, preventing off-target drilling during osteotomy.

Importantly, it achieves this without any forced movements of a robotic arm, preserving the tactile sense and feedback of freehand drilling.

SAFE

MiniLOCK™

Adoption of navigation in dental implantology requires efficient workflow and unwavering accuracy.

MiniLOCK implements a proprietary design that achieves: i) effortless chairside attachment to the patient, ii) steadfastness (no shifting or dislodging during surgery), and iii) easy removal.

This revolutionary device forms the cornerstone of upholding accuracy throughout dental implant procedures.

Accuracy – Key to dentistry

IGI 2.0’s TRAX real-time tracking system offers best-in-class, high-accuracy tracking (0.35 mm +/- .14mm)  with no observable onscreen lag (20 millisecond refresh rate).   Accuracy is maintained across the workflow, even with patient movement, with the Company’s proprietary and patented MiniLOCK patient-to-tracker connection.

Based on an analysis of published accuracy statistics, IGI offers best-in-class accuracy.

Spherical accuracy

Even a seemingly small change in linear deviation (e.g., 1.0 mm vs. 1.4 mm) considerably broadens the potential for errors in the spherical target zone where the dentist is operating because the volume is a function of the radius cubed.  The example below is included to help visualize the IGI version 2.0 target zone:

The IGI systems’ edge over competitors is amplified in three dimensions, shown in the graph below.

Mean Volumetric Precision (mm^3)

This data was calculated using the formula to calculate the volume of a sphere (V=4/3 πr³) to reflect real-world dental anatomy, using the linear accuracy data as the radius of the sphere. Standard deviations were removed in the calculation for visual aesthetics.

MiniLOCK for accurate patient tracking

Enables precise synchronize the on-screen digital twin with the in-chair patient.
MiniLOCK’s ability to reposition to 50 microns allows a CT scan, diagnosis and planning to occur on either the same day or different days. This also ensures patient comfort since MiniLOCK can be  replaced without any reduction or diminution of accuracy.  Results in a truly collaborative process with patients!
