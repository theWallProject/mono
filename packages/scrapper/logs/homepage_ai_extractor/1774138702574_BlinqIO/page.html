    How it works

    01

    Define your test

    Simply click through your application flow to show the AI what you want to test. Our AI instantly captures your actions and generates a business-level test prompt (in BDD/Cucumber format).

    02

    AI writes complete test automation

    You never write a line of code. BlinqIO autonomously generates a full Playwright project with parameterized data and reusable functions—not disconnected snippets. Real code under the hood ensures stability, scalability, and no vendor lock-in.

    03

    AI maintains tests intelligently

    Because BlinqIO understands the underlying business logic it built, it handles significant maintenance automatically—adapting tests to major UI changes, not just trivial tweaks. While not every edge case is caught instantly, the AI autonomously resolves most changes that preserve test intent.

    04

    AI analyzes and routes failures intelligently

    BlinqIO performs deep analytics on comprehensive data from all sources—network, API, automation steps, and product changes. The AI not only identifies root causes but intelligently routes issues to the right team (Product, DevOps) or resolves test automation maintenance issues autonomously.
