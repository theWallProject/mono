Biologics Beyond Boundaries.

Developing first-in-class therapeutics to treat
brain cancers and CNS diseases.
contact us
With a goal to extend the reach of biological therapeutics to the brain to tackle CNS diseases head-on, Nanocarry is pioneering a multidisciplinary approach that enables biologics to access the brain, overcoming the Blood Brain Barrier (BBB).

The Challenge

Our proprietary technology platform’s Plug & Play approach offers infinite possibilities. The rise of biologics over the last 20 years has revolutionized disease treatment; however, 1 in 6 people worldwide are living with a neurological condition and patients with brain diseases are being left behind, as existing therapeutics cannot access the brain, due to the restrictive Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB).
