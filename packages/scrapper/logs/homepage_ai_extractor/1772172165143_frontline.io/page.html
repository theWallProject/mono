    OUR CLIENTS

    Digital Twin

    A light virtual comprehensive representation of a mechanical system, created within hours out of a standard CAD. Used for simulations, it eliminates the need for physical models

    Learn More

    Interactive parts catalog

    A full list of all the parts including metadata and spare parts accessible through the Digital Twin. Used for quick parts identification and ordering. The Parts Editor enables simple editing and sharing

    Learn More

    Remote Support

    An expert communication that guides the field personnel using AR, MR, video, annotation, and chatting tools. The remote training software saves travel time, improves first-time fixes, and increases machine-up time. The Remote Support is seamlessly integrated with the Virtual Training Room

    Learn More

    Interactive flows and procedures

    Interactive step-by-step 3D flows used for training, support, and troubleshooting. Eliminating learning curve, always updated, and performed on any device, flow runs are recorded into frontline.io analytics. Flow and procedures creation are done by the Flow Editor and Animation Builder

    Learn More

    Real-time Analytics

    Real-time monitoring and recording of flows and remote assist sessions, enabling immediate response and continuous improvement process. Includes steps performed, the decisions taken, and the user’s feed. The data is compatible with leading AI and BI systems
