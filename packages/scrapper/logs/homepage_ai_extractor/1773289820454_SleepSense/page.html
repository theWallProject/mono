Sensor Solutions That Make Sense

SLP has been a global manufacturer of sleep diagnostic sensors since 1993. The SleepSense® line of sensors provides high quality signals, are designed for optimal patient comfort and are easy to use and apply.

SleepSense® Sleep Diagnostic Sensors

SleepSense® Snore Sensors provide a clear and artifact free indication of snoring and respiratory sounds.

SleepSense® Thermal Airflow Sensors employ a high thermal mass design, providing improved correlation with flow values as well as temperature values.

The SleepSense® ThermoCan™ System monitors oral/nasal thermal airflow and nasal pressure simultaneously.

SleepSense® Pressure Flow Sensors generate a small voltage in response to pressure changes and acoustical input.

The SleepSense® Disposable Inductive System features Pre-Cut Disposable Belts in various sizes and Inductive Interface Buckles.

SleepSense® offers Reusable or Semi-Reusable Inductive Belts compatible with nearly any system.

SleepSense® Respiratory Effort Sensors provide a clear, qualitative indication of respiration. Choose between PVDF or Piezo technologies.

SleepSense® Limb Movement Sensors provide a clear, qualitative indication of movements.

SleepSense® Body Position Sensors provide a signal that is directly proportional to the patient’s sleep posture.

SleepSense® SpO2 Sensors are available in disposable or reusable options with high quality LEDs for accurate measurement and fast response.

SleepSense® Pressure Cannulas are soft, flexible, designed with no-kink technology and are specifically tailored for sleep studies.

SleepSense® Accessories & Adapters are designed for ease of use with SleepSense® products.
