Created By Parents Trusted By Millions

We are Pazu, home to a library of 50+ games trusted by millions of children and parents around the world. We offer a wide variety of high quality and ad-free mobile games.

Our goal is to create fun games that spark the imagination, promote creativity and give children the space to express themselves.

Pazu+ is an all-in-one subscription that gives unlimited access to all of the content in our game library; with no ads, no in-app purchase, on both Apple and Android devices.
