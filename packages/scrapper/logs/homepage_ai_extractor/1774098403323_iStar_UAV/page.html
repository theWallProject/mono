ADVANCED C2 PLATFORMS

The iSTAR C2 Mobile Command & Control provides real-time information enabling the operational team to make fast and accurate decisions.

Our Unmanned Vehicle Control System is a field-proven C2 system based on the iSTAR architecture and used to control the UAS remotely. The relay system facilitates the control of multiple systems.

Powered by our flexible approach and unique modular platforms, we provide customised solutions, with an option to add additional consoles and modules. The vehicle command and telemetry displays and configurations, telemetry displays, and range maps are designed according to customer preference.

ADVANCED DESIGN

The iSTAR C2 is designed and manufactured in-house, specifically for our aircraft. With large HD displays, integrated storage, build-in antenna connectors, the iSTAR GCS is comprehensive Command and Control station.

COMPACT & RUGGED

Built from lightweight carbon composite and aluminum materials, industrial-grade components sealed in the world’s best travel case, the iSTAR C2 is compact, easy to transport and deploy.

RELIABLE POWER

With two power sources, the iSTAR C2 allows for reliable, continuous operation in the field. Hot-swappable battery packs together with AC power, which switches over instantly in a power cut.

LONG RANGE

The standard iSTAR C2 features a 2.4Ghz data & video link which offers up to 10km range and up to 250km range with our optional advanced Antenna Tracking systems.
