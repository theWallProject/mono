Kalderon Atelier

Glass artist Dan Kalderon has lived many lives as an explorative, curious creative. Born and raised in Jerusalem, he began his career following in the footsteps of his father, a well-known ceramicist.

With a whole lot of charismatic determination, a deep connection to glass art, and serendipitous circumstances, Dan paved his own, distinct path.

Together with his companion in life and work, Michal, the business engine and CEO, the Kalderon family has propelled glass artistry into an international enterprise.

Kalderon’s multi-faceted glass atelier produces otherworldly, handmade and customized dishware sets for Michelin-starred restaurants and luxury hotels around the globe.

In this awakened place, set in a nature-filled Mediterranean pocket where fresh mountain air meets lush forests and sea views, all of the inspired elements that go into Dan’s glass-making come together; a constant movement and evolution of technique, pattern, color, texture, and flourish of painting by hand. No two pieces are the same.
