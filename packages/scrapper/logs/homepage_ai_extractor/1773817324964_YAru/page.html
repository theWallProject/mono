The goal of the game is to eliminate your opponent.
8 x 8 x 8 x
Each player gets 3 types of pieces.
Each player in their turn moves one piece of their choice.
A piece can move one square forward, left, or right – to an empty square or to a square with an opponent piece.
A piece may only move to a square with an opponent piece if it is able to slay it.
A piece cannot move back to where it was one turn ago.
The only exceptions are if you have no other possible moves, or if your last move was from, to, or on the last row.
Once you are at the last row with one of the pieces, you can jump to any square on the board on which there is an opponent piece of the same type - and slay it.
If the opponent ran out of pieces of the same type, you can do this to any of their other pieces.
If you have no possible moves, the opponent gets another turn.
That’s it! You are now ready to play Yaru.
