Why travel with Israel My Way

Traveling with Israel My Way means enjoying a unique and truly personalized experience. With 18 years of expertise in luxury tourism, we create tailor-made itineraries that blend sophistication, authenticity, and exclusivity.

We offer bespoke, out of the box activities and experiences, many of them specially designed for you, and unavailable online. From unforgettable celebrations like bar/bat mitzvahs to unique family moments, pilgrimages, or incentive trips, every detail is carefully planned to exceed your expectations. With Israel My Way, your journey to Israel will be transformed into a memorable adventure filled with discoveries and emotions, always delivered with the highest standards of quality and care.
