General Physical AI through Doorstep Delivery

RIVR builds General Physical AI by deploying autonomous legged robots for real-world doorstep delivery. Each delivery generates data that improves our AI, combining large-scale reinforcement learning in simulation with real-world self-supervised learning. By owning the full stack from software to hardware, we design for reliability, safety, and cost efficiency from day one, enabling Physical AI to scale.
