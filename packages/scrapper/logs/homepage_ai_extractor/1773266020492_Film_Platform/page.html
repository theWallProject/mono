Spotlight
Made to Measure
In this chilling exploration of digital surveillance, filmmakers attempt to reconstruct a complete stranger’s identity using only her Google search history. Five years of personal data become the foundation for creating a living replica, performed by an actress who embodies this digital ghost. When the real woman finally encounters her data-driven double, the meeting exposes how intimately technology companies know us. This provocative documentary reveals the unsettling accuracy with which our online behavior can predict our deepest desires and fears.
