KNOW YOU ARE ONAIR

The Media Forest Group specializes in audio, image and video signal processing and analytics.
We provide Automatic Content Recognition (ACR) and Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) solutions and added-value services utilizing our unique AI, cloud-based, SaaS platform that monitors TV channels, radio stations, online streams and content archives, and generates real-time, accurate, actionable and value-creating insights for the wider music and advertising industries.

We convert free speech into searchable text with our automatic real-time transcription engine and analytics platform, enabling audio searches within TV/radio broadcasts, audiovisual media clips, and podcasts based on the mentioned keywords.

Media Forest automatically tracks content airplays on broadcast emissions and online streams, and keyword mentions on spoken media, providing artists, songwriters, record labels, publishers, brands, advertisers and organizations around the world with valuable real-time data.
