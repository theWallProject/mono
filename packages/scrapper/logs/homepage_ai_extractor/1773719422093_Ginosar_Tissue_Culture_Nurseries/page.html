Ginosar Agro - Tissue Culture Company Since 1980

Ginosar Agro is a leading force in the tissue culture laboratory industry, backed by over 40 years of experience. We are recognized for our extensive expertise and our ability to produce millions of high-quality seedlings each year while adhering to the highest industry standards. With advanced capabilities, our tissue culture laboratory can propagate any unique genetic material to meet the specific needs of our clients.
