KAST Silicone Ltd

Kast Silicone specializes in the development and the manufacturing of high-quality silicone components and silicone finished products.
Kast is a privately held company founded in 2001 and located in Har Tuv, Beit Shemesh in Israel

The company is a leading developer and manufacturer of silicone products (membranes) for the irrigation industry.

Kast specializes in the development and manufacture of high-quality silicone tubing, profiles seals and gaskets, all made of high-quality silicone rubber.
