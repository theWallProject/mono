Optimedia Pvt. Ltd.

Optimedia Pvt. Ltd. founded in 2008 is one of the biggest "Out of Home Outdoor Media Agency"
 of Pakistan. It aims to invent and execute unique out of home experiences – creative
ways for consumers to interact with brands and  influence consumer behavior along the 
customers' journey out of his home.Working for past 5 years, Optimedia aims to invent and
execute unique out of home experiences for consumers to interact with brands. From April 2011
Optimedia Pvt. Ltd. Affiliated with Kinetic worldwide which is world’s largest planner and buyer
of Out of Home media.

Optimedia Pvt. Ltd. is working with the expertise in just about every type of Out of
Home media available, including exciting alternative outdoor media. It aims to
invent and execute unique out of home experiences, creative ways for consumers
to interact with brands and influence consumer behavior along the customer’s
journey out of home.

Optimedia Pvt. Ltd. also enable clients with smaller advertising budgets to take advantage
of opportunities to advertise at discounted rates.
