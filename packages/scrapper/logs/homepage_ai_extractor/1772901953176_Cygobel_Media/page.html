Inspired by “Cybele”,
Mother of the gods in Anatolian mythology, later adopted by many ancient cultures.

Same as Cybele, Cygobel will always strive for growth, facing any challenge towards leading the industry with our cutting-edge technologies along with a dedicated & professional team.

Video

who we are

Cygobel Media started its journey in 2012 and was one of the first agencies to recognize the importance of targeting the accurate user to the relevant content! From its early days, Cygobel media was technology-oriented. The combination of high quality, personal and sophisticated advanced technologies was always at the forefront of the company. 

Over the years, Cygobel developed in-house platforms,  performance measurement, and anti-fraud tools, In order to make sure our clients receive the best results. Our company is a leading force in the industry, helping mobile developers, publishers, and well-known agencies to fulfill user acquisition and monetization strategies by using Cygobel’s in-house technology solutions. 

By using our mobile app marketing platform, our team of experts maintains both publishers and advertisers by managing the mobile user acquisition and keeping the positive ROI, and greatly increases mobile user acquisition and app usage based on targeted goals and KPIs.

services

our expertise

Cygobel is a performance-based advertising agency of Mobile apps, Video & Display. One of the largest agencies in the Industry with an end-to-end mobile advertising platform that helps the world’s top brands reach their most valuable users with the widest range of traffic sources available today. 

Cygobel’s partners enjoy its proprietary technology solutions including RTB engine, advanced LTV analytics, and performance prediction algorithms which were developed by our own developers, based on the vast experience we have gained over the past few years.

mobile

Due to the widely increasingly mobile users and a fast-growing environment, mobile advertising became a challenge. fortunately, Cygobel acknowledged the importance of this medium a long time ago and started to develop special technologies and automatic tools in order to enable both advertisers and publishers to reach the highest potential results and maximize their revenues. As this fast-growing market poses many challenges, our experienced team of experts knows how to guide our clients toward their goals and beyond.

Our unique technologies let our partners combine all models (CPA/CPI/CPC/CPM & more). This smart in-house solution allows reaching the highest possible ROI while keeping the quality users engaged with the app.

Monetization
One of the industry’s largest in-app ad networks leveraging all available ad units to drive revenue and user engagement

User acquisition
The UA platform based on user-level revenue data to ensure that you scale smartly and campaigns stay ROI positive.

Programmatic media buying 
Our advanced and unique in-house technologies evolved through the years due to the experience we achieved over the years. The automatic tools we have created allow our partners reach to the best and most targeted traffic for their brand by using our PMB system.

video

Cygobel provides full video advertising solutions, through innovative, advanced technology-driven optimization tools, and real-time reporting. 

Our team of dedicated managers uses the most advanced technological capabilities to deliver the full monetization package, leading you to the top of the video advertising arena.

Cygobel’s Video’s player allows you to choose between web, mobile & OTT, across all Platform and verticals, support all browsers, provides the statistics in real-time, leading the users to the one of the best viewing experience

display

Cygobel is a leading force in the Display advertising industry. We have noticed the importance of this medium and have begun to develop core technologies to support billions of ad requests.
Nowadays, Cygobel continues to develop its proprietary technology to be leading in the advertising arena and continues to support media acquisition and RTB. Our unique and advanced forecasting algorithms allow us to effectively optimize to the client’s satisfaction.

advertisers

Cygobel leads its partners to reach the targeted goals and KPIs, by planning, executing, and promoting their apps in the most accurate way to achieve the highest performance, user engagement, and maximizing revenues. 

PERFORMANCE AND SAFETY

publishers
