Phononics - Innovative overheating solutions

Phononics leverages its rich academic and industrial diamond growth experience to enable differentiating performance in power delivery and high power communication systems.

Technology

Diamond layers are characterised by heat conductivity over fivefold that of metal. When combined in semiconductor electronic systems previously unattainable higher power densities and lower operation temperatures are enabled.

Business

We are currently engaged with several customers and are seeking segment exclusivity based partnerships with systems companies that have benefit in our process.

Phononics technology in leading industries

TRANSPORTATION

Enabling the next generation of active sensors and RADARs that transmits in real time the vehicle location and its interaction with the surrounding.

COMMUNICATION

Increasing the communication throughput of cellular base stations will reduce the density of cellular networks while improving access and quality. The heat management of end-user devices will reduce their heating and improve the reliability.

LIGHTING

Phononics will remove the heating burrier of LED and LASER lighting and will significantly increase light source efficiency and reliability.

RENEWABLE ENERGY

Phononics-based energy converters will save significant energy losses and will increase efficiency.
