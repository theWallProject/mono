    About

    Relcom Components Ltd was established and operates since 1979 and is oriented to the Military, Space, Avionic & Medical Industries.

    Relcom Components is a representative and Distribution organization of Electronic, Electro-Mechanic and Servo & Motion control Components Manufacturers from USA, Canada, and Europe.

    Supported by our Hi-Technology innovative principals we provide wide range of products to our customers in the Israeli market.
