About Artisan Sonics

Established in 1996, Artisan Sonics LTD gave the OBGYN ultrasound practitioners a solution to an unmet need, namely, a useful tool to create, store, archive, and share multimedia information from medical devices, combined with advanced structured reporting. Today, Artisan Sonics provides the OBGYN practitioners a complete suite of affordable web services which further enhance their working environment. The reports and their multimedia are stored in the cloud for an unlimited time, providing the user secure and immediate access to his works. Artisan is in Hod-Hasharon in Israel. Artisan Sonics is well-reputed by the most significant Israeli KOL’s in this field who took part in our advisory board to assure that our R&D efforts coincide with the ever-changing needs of the OBGYN community.

Our Products

Rapporteur

This reporting service allows the OBGYN sonographer to document any detail during a scan. Exclusive templates are created to support Gynecology, NT, Comprehensive, and others. We calculate the GA, GG, EFW, and present growth charts of the entire pregnancy. The screens are intuitively ordered to help you reporting any detail. With the Rapporteur you will get:
• Easy Access to your work, mobility in using your medical records all in a paperless environment straight to your cell phone or tablet.
• Secured Material without dongles or detachable discs. Everything is up in the secure cloud that is the industry standard.
• Quality of Care to your patient by sharing exam results with your referring colleagues.
• Data for Research, once you accumulate a significant library of findings, the data can serve you for further analysis and Research.

Aligned Rapporteur

While the Rapporteur requires typing of data, the Aligned Rapporteur receives data from your ultrasound. The data collected automatically populates relevant measurement fields in the Rapporteur. Then all related GA, GG, and EFW are automatically computed. The Aligned Rapporteur also stores images, clips, and session recordings, all displayed in the exam's gallery. In addition to the Rapporteur's advantages, with the Aligned Rapporteur you will:
• Save Much Time by not retyping the measurements into your report.
• Have Exam's Gallery with all its images, clips, and recordings.
• Print Selected Images that will come within your printed report;
• Share All Imagery and Report of the examination. Your patient's smartphone will display the report, the growth charts, the images and clips gallery, and even the entire video recording of your exam.

ART
(Artificial Reproductive Techniques) Assistant

This dedicated service enables the practitioners to follow up, in an orderly fashion, the admission process of the patient and her spouse. It documents all given treatment cycles and allows printing and emailing all the relevant protocols. The phase of assessment and investigation before treatment cycles consists of medical history with previous pregnancies, tests, medications, and diseases. The infertility investigation consists of menstruation, previous treatment cycles, surgeries, and contraceptives. The mechanical workup includes of HSG, hysteroscopy/laparoscopy, and ultrasound exams. The treatment sections consist of preferences and general data with present and previous protocols. The patient is getting shared with the Detailed protocols and her referring doctor.

Pregnancy Follow Up Assistant

This extensive service gives the OBGYN practitioner the ability to document any detail that affects and relates to the ongoing pregnancy. It includes the patient's medical data, her spouse's data and mainly tracks the fetus's development. Besides the demographic data, the entire medical history of the woman like diseases, surgeries, and family history are registered. Previous pregnancies, medications, sensitivities, medical tests are recorded. Genetics of the woman and her spouse, lab tests like serology, coombs, glucose, and karyotype. All fetal screenings like NT, 2-nd Trimester, and follow-ups. Clinical exams include the mother's weight, blood pressure, proteinuria, glycosuria, uterus, and Cervix. On the fetal side, it consists of fetus gender, presentation, movements, breathing, tonus, pulse, weight, detailed measurements of the fetus, and pregnancy closing data.
