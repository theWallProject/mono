Learn Hebrew Online with Native Teachers

If you want to learn Hebrew before your visit to Israel? Sign up for our Hebrew online lessons to get started. We have a diverse student base from different parts of the world, including Australia, Canada, SA, UK, and even Israel who study Hebrew from our experienced and highly competent

Hebrew language instructor.

At Ulpan-Or, we give you a platform to learn Hebrew online with native teachers who provide you guidance at every step during the course to help you learn the language in the most efficienct way.  Our Hebrew language instructors will help you learn the Hebrew alphabet, grammar, and vocabulary to have conversations with locals without any trouble.

Before admission, we evaluate your proficiency in Hebrew to assign you with the right classmates to allow a positive learning outcome.

We believe learning is creation, not consumption.

Our accelerated learning technique allows you to learn faster and retain the knowledge for a longer duration, helping you become proficient in the language with ease.

We offer a range of options, including online learning, self-study, school program, and Israel immersion. Sign up with the option that aligns with your learning goals. If you want to learn more about our courses, get in touch with us today!
