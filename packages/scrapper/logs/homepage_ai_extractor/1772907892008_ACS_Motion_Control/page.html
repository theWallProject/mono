The Universal Control Platform for High Precision Motion Systems

The SPiiPlus Platform is an EtherCAT-based control platform consisting of motion controllers, servo drives, interfaces, and I/O built to meet the needs of OEM engineering teams developing equipment with precision motion control systems: 

  • Performance to meet challenging precision motion system requirements  
  • Flexibility to control stages and motors from best-in-class suppliers 
  • Scalability to manage increased complexity with each machine generation 

The SPiiPlus Platform enables the OEM motion control design team to build a tailored control solution for each machine configuration. Unique control algorithms and performance optimization tools enable the team to maximize motion stage performance. Comprehensive application development tools and programming libraries enable the team to bring more machine features and functionality to market, faster. 

SPiiPlus Platform

DS402 EtherCAT Servo Drives for Precision Motion Stages

Unlike most DS402 EtherCAT servo drives in the market, IDM high performance servo drives includes a suite of optimization tools uniquely engineered to achieve the highest levels of performance with precision motion stages. This performance is achieved through unique & powerful servo control algorithms, very fast servo sampling/update rates, and advanced power electronics techniques. Advanced features include flexible real-time programming and powerful position event generation capabilities. The IDM series are offered in 1, 2 and 4 axes modules with a variety of voltage (DC, AC input) and current (up to 60A) selections.

IDM Series

Entry-Level Precision Motion Controllers with Integrated Servo Drives

Built on a cost-optimized architecture for precision motion systems, ECM series provides high performance in for cost- and space-sensitive OEM machines.

This performance is achieved through unique & powerful servo control algorithms, very fast servo sampling/update rates, and advanced power electronics techniques. ECM features include advanced motion profile generation, flexible real-time multi-threaded programming, motion optimization tool, motion-to-process event synchronization and many other advanced controller features. The ECM series are offered in 1, 2 and 4 axes modules with a variety of voltage (DC, AC input) and current (up to 60A) selections.

ECM Series

Advanced motion control capabilities for advanced equipment manufacturers

ACS Motion Control has built a strong foundation of advanced motion control capabilities over decades of solving the most demanding precision motion control challenges. This suite of capabilities includes a comprehensive application development software package, unique servo control algorithms, powerful servo drive techniques, sophisticated motion profile planning, and advanced motion-to-process synchronization.

Capabilities that enable high-tech OEMs to build “best-in-class” machines by:

  • Achieving the highest level of performance from precision motion systems & stages
  • Increasing machine throughput
  • Creating scalable & flexible control solutions for a variety of machine configurations
  • Decreasing development time (improving time to market)
