LASER TECHNOLOGY

Laser is an acronym for “Light Amplification by the Stimulated Emission of Radiation” which produces a sharp, focused light beam that melts a very small area of metal. The benefit of this technology is that very little heat is generated at the weld point, allowing easy weld just microns away from the most complicated and intricate component parts without damaging heat sensitive materials. Micro laser welding is used for joining high value, miniature components in a range of industries such as electronics, telecom, automotive, the medical industry and more. Typical medical applications include catheters, delivery systems and implants, surgical robots, hermetic seals and many others.

LaserLux offers a manual micro laser welding and CNC welding. We offer micro laser welding services within a clean room and outside a clean environment.
