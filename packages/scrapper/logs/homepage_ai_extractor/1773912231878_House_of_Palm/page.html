    A Historic Gem in the Heart of Tel Aviv

    The House of Palm owes its name to its unique and wonderful architectural style, revealing a majestic palm tree motif. Located in the heart of Tel Aviv, just steps from Nahalat Binyamin street, this building combines Biblical and Israeli elements like the Star of David and Menorah with seven candleholders.

    Our residence is close to classic and authentic neighborhoods like Kerem Hateimanim, Rothschild Boulevard, Neve Tzedek, and the vibrant center of Tel Aviv.
