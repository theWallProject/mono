Advanced AI Capabilities

Smart Technology for Field Management

Our platform uses advanced AI to optimize every aspect of your field operations

AI-Based Dynamic Polygons

Dynamic service territories (Polygons) based on season, day in a week and density rather than relying on fixed zones.

Smart Time-Window Calculator

AI algorithm that calculates precise time windows for customers based on location, travel times, expected service duration, and workload.

Fully Automated Booking

Self-service booking system that allows customers to schedule appointments 24/7, with instant confirmation and automatic calendar coordination.

Fully Automated Dispatching

Smart dispatch engine that automatically assigns tasks to technicians based on skills, availability, location, and business priorities.

Routes Optimization

Advanced algorithm that calculates the most efficient routes in real-time, saving fuel, travel time, and maximizing daily visit capacity.
