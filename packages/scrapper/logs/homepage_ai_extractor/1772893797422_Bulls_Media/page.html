Who we are

Since 2013, Bulls-Media has been providing businesses of all sizes with powerful digital solutions. We strive to ensure our clients receive the highest quality services and the best customer experience.

Our core values lie in our ability to understand all aspects of digital marketing. We provide the knowledge and solutions necessary to create a successful online presence.

We offer custom-built systems for generating traffic and sales, KPI reporting, visualization tools, and guidance for businesses to reach their full potential.

Our blend of proprietary systems seamlessly automates business processes, saving clients time and money. Our experienced professionals are here to provide you with all the expertise you need for success.

Research & Development

Our R&D teams are capable of creating a website or landing page, automating processes, and setting up tracking and monitoring tools to ensure that your site functions correctly from a technical perspective. We can also configure alert notifications based on specific triggers, such as real-time site availability checks.

Business Operation

Our business operation team is dedicated to helping your company succeed. We provide you with project management, scheduling, resource allocation, and support, so your business runs smoothly and efficiently. We help you develop strategies to maximize profits and streamline operations.

Analysis

The analytics team determines what KPIs and other metrics need to be monitored. Then our developers put together a custom-built monitoring system to gather the information needed. Our advanced algorithms create custom reporting tools to gather and compile in-depth reports.

Legal & finance

Our finance team can provide assistance with contract management, compliance, and financial reporting strategies. Our team is committed to helping you navigate the complex legal and financial landscape regardless of which country you are in.

Creative & Design

Our UI/UX design team is responsible for creating user-friendly, intuitive, and visually appealing designs for our clients. They create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes to test user experience and flow. Our UI/UX design team can add reviews, articles, promotional text, and SEO-optimized images.

Digital Marketing

We create and manage campaigns tailored to each client's needs to get the highest possible ROI. Our media buyers negotiate with publishers to secure the most cost-effective and strategically placed ad to drive traffic to your site.
