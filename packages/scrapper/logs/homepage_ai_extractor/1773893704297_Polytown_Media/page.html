This is a 3D interactive canvas where you can add and remove colored blocks. Move your mouse and left-click to add a block. Right-click or press Delete/Backspace to remove a block. Press F to toggle auto-revolve mode. Press H to toggle UI visibility. Drag to rotate the camera view.
Note: This 3D canvas requires mouse or touch input for interaction. The canvas is primarily a visual experience and may not be fully accessible with keyboard-only navigation.
