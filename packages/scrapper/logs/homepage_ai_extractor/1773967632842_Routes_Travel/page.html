WHAT WE DO

RoutesTravelServices

Assisting you with all of your Israel travel needs such as Highly Specialized VIP Groups, Family Tours, Community Missions, Synagogue Trips, University Programs, itreks, Mega Missions, Youth Programs, Summer Camps, Youth Groups and School Tours.

Family Tours

Renowned for our boutique family tours in Israel, we will work closely with you and your family to design a personalized trip that will provide the best experience for your group.

Community Missions

We are experts at planning community missions that will connect your Jewish community to the land of Israel through any avenue you wish.
Learn More

Synagogue Trips

Synagogue Trips with so much history to explore and so many amazing sites to visit, Routes Travel is committed to providing the right experience for your congregation or community.
Learn More

Israel Treks

We coordinate educational trips to Israel for graduate students, mid-career professionals, and influentials worldwide. We partner with itrek to bring the richness and complexity of Israel to life through Israel Treks that combine education, culture, and fun - and leave a lasting impression on participants.
Learn More

Bar and Bat Mitzvah Tours

Allow us to create a personalized tailor made Bar and Bat Mitzvah tour in Israel for your group and family. Combine a world-class vacation with a meaningful ceremony and celebration of your Bar/Bat Mitzvah in one of the most meaningful places in the world.
Learn More

Teen Travel, Schools, Youth Movements & Camps

We believe that an Israel experience should be an integral part of any day school, camp, or youth group’s curriculum. A peer-group Israel travel program will enrich students’ educational experiences and strengthen their Jewish identities and Zionist values.
Learn More

Conferences

Israel provides an amazing backdrop for a conference, convention, or event. Steeped in history, Israel is also the ‘Innovation Nation’ full of modern amenities. Let us plan your conference, convention or event in the most professional high level way possible.
