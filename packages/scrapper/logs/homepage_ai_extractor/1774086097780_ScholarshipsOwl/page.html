What is ScholarshipsOwl?

We are a leading scholarship database designed to simplify and personalize your scholarship search. No more tiresome hunting for scholarships that suit you. With ScholarshipsOwl, create a profile and receive personalized scholarship recommendations.

But ScholarshipsOwl isn’t just about scholarships. We provide students and their parents with valuable information to help navigate the journey from high school to college, graduate school, and beyond.

How ScholarshipsOwl Works

Create Your Profile

After signing up, customize your profile by answering a few questions about yourself.

Receive Scholarship Matches

Based on your unique profile, you’ll get a list of scholarships you qualify for, tailored to your strengths, interests, and skills.

Apply and Secure Funds for Education

Each time you log in, find new scholarship matches and the total value of the scholarships you qualify for. Filter your list, save those you’re interested in, and start applying for scholarships.
