Unlocking the Future of Delivery:

Shaping Tomorrow with LDS's Advanced Formulations - Projects and Collaborations

LDS, a leading biotech company, specializes in revolutionary drug delivery technology for pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, and cosmeceuticals. 

Our innovative and proprietary delivery system, developed through years of extensive research, offers solutions for insoluble and poorly permeable active molecules, enhancing their bioavailability and efficacy. 

The platform technology can be tailored to a wide range of molecules—both small and large, generic or innovative—and customized for various routes of administration, including oral, ocular, dermal, parenteral, nasal, and more. 

With a comprehensive patent portfolio of over 37 granted patents, we’ve established global partnerships, collaborated on commercial products, and advanced multiple clinical studies. 

LDS welcomes collaboration with industry leaders, innovators, and researchers, offering unparalleled access to our groundbreaking delivery technology.

Wide Range of Solutions for Multiple
Applications

LDS’s molecularly engineered liquid-structured nano-domains are platform technology designed for numerous applications, various dosages, and different administration routes.

Oral

LDS’s projects are based on the embedment of generic or innovative bio-actives (APIs) designed and prepared to achieve enhanced bioavailability for oral uptake. The active molecules are safeguarded from the harsh conditions of the gastrointestinal (GI) tract, improving their release, absorption, and transport to the bloodstream.

Ophthalmic

LDS’s unique liquid nano-domains provide extensive surface contact with the eye’s epithelium, demonstrating increased stability against the pH and electrolyte levels of the tear film. This enables enhanced permeation to both anterior and posterior eye segments.

Topical and Transdermal

LDS introduces groundbreaking novel topical delivery systems with nano-liquid structures in soft gels or thin films. These enable superior transport of insoluble active molecules (APIs) to the skin layers (epidermis/dermis) and nails, targeting skin diseases, as well as transdermal delivery for high absorption

Parenteral

LDS liquid domains are specially engineered to significantly improve traditional emulsion and suspension formulations in parenteral applications, enhancing chemical, physical, and microbiological stability.
