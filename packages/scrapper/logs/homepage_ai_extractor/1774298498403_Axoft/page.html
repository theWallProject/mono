Biocompatible
implant

Our material mimics the soft tissues of the brain, improving device stability and communication — far exceeding malleability and resilience of conventional implant materials.

Signal processing electronics

The precise data collected by the implant is processed by an integrated circuit. Once compressed, the data is wirelessly sent to the software interface for interpretation.

Surgical implantation tools

Today’s rigid devices act as their own insertion tool, damaging the soft tissues over time. Our surgical tool guides the soft implant in brain tissues with precision for seamless integration.

Neural data decoding software

Axoft is developing its unique software and decoding algorithm to meet the demands of larger, more stable data streams.
