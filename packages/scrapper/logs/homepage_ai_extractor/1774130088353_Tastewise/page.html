FAQ

01.What is a food intelligence platform?

A food intelligence platform is a system that helps brands understand what people are actually buying, ordering, cooking, and eating, in real time. It brings together consumer research, market data, and AI analysis to show not just what is happening, but why. That way, food and beverage teams can make decisions with proof they can stand behind.

02.What is Tastewise and how is it different from traditional market research?

Tastewise is a human- and AI-powered food intelligence platform built specifically for food and beverage brands. Traditional market research often means waiting for static reports or survey results. Tastewise works differently. It combines live consumer panels, retail and foodservice performance data, and AI agents to reveal demand as it unfolds, so teams can move faster and act with confidence.

03.Is Tastewise research qualitative or quantitative?

Tastewise combines large-scale quantitative market data with behavioral and consumer signals to generate statistically significant insights and explainable demand drivers.

04.Who is Tastewise built for?

Tastewise is built for retail, foodservice, marketing, and product innovation teams in food and beverage companies looking for scalable research software and actionable growth insights.

05.Does Tastewise offer services in addition to software?

Yes. In addition to its research and insights software platform, Tastewise offers expert-led services to support high-stakes growth, innovation, and sell-in decisions.

06.Does Tastewise support execution?

Yes. Tastewise goes beyond consumer insights. It includes AI-powered execution tools like Product Visualizer, Recipe Creator, and Blog post or Sales story creators to help teams move from idea to activation faster.
