PIONEERING STRUCTURAL HEART INNOVATION

Cardiovalve is a Novel Transcatheter Valve Replacement System that fits both Mitral and Tricuspid anatomies. The flagship Cardiovalve system is designed for transfemoral delivery, avoiding the need for open heart surgery. The Cardiovalve system offers significant benefits, including a short profile for minimal protrusion to the ventricles, minimizing the risk of LVOT obstruction, a robust anchoring mechanism, and an enhanced sealing solution to prevent paravalvular leaks.
