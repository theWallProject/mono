    Holistic energy solutions

    enshift develops integrated solutions that are individually tailored to each property. An in-depth analysis with consideration of all factors listed below, result in future-proof sustainable energy concepts.

    The roof becomes a powerful source of energy – integrated, efficient and emission-reducing

    enshift combines technical expertise with holistic implementation: from grid integration, roof renovation to energy sales – for maximum efficiency and stable returns or fixed interest rates.

    Scalable battery systems for the best possible energy autonomy

    enshift seamlessly integrates high-performance battery storage systems into existing infrastructures – tailored to consumption profiles, grid topology and regulatory framework conditions. This creates an economically viable system for peak load capping, self-consumption optimization and integration into flexibility markets.

    Flexibility Trading for efficient grid stability and yield

    enshift integrates all building technologies into flexibility market pools. This enhances the assets yields and provides needed stability to the grid. A team of dedicated energy specialists trade and optimize the frequency response loads.

    High efficiency heat pump systems for all kind of properties

    Whether reversible air-water, water-water or geothermal heatpump solutions, enshift develops carbon free, efficient and building specific heating solutions.

    Integrated, intelligent, scalable and future-proof

    enshift develops and operates charging infrastructure as part of integrated energy concepts. The intelligent connection to PV systems, storage systems and building management creates economical and grid-friendly charging solutions – from individual locations to fleet solutions.

    Load management, billing and subsidy management are seamlessly integrated.

    Energy from our community, For our future.

    By generating renewable power on-site and sharing it with your community, you create lasting value — for yourself, your tenants, and the entire region.

    Whether you’re looking to future-proof your property or a business seeking affordable, sustainable energy, our solutions connect producers and consumers in a way that lowers costs, reduces emissions, and strengthens local independence.

    From assessment to optimization:

    A value-creation Roadmap

    We begin with a comprehensive property assessment, leveraging advanced data analytics to uncover opportunities for energy optimization.

    This includes a detailed analysis of consumption patterns, geographic factors, and accurate simulations to quantify potential savings and earnings.

    Our expert team runs an extensive environmental, technical and economic feasibility study, minimizing risk in the development phase as well as during operations.

    enshift helps you manage your CAPEX through a variety of financing options, including green energy service agreements (EASY) and energy contracting (PLUS).

    enshift manages the end-to-end delivery of the approved energy upgrades—coordinating certified contractors, overseeing installation, and ensuring minimal disruption to building operations.

    This phase is driven by precision, safety, and quality control, ensuring that all technologies are implemented on time, on budget, and to the highest standards.

    enshift stays engaged after implementation to secure and enhance the value of your investment.

    Ongoing system optimization ensures sustained cost reductions, compliance, and improved ESG performance.
