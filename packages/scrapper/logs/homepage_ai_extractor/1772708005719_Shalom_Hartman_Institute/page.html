Young Jewish Leaders

The Shalom Hartman Institute’s Wellspring initiatives are changing the way young Jewish leaders connect to Judaism and Israel. We offer a growing suite of educational initiatives for young Jews ages 15–25 as well as the professionals, educators, and institutions who serve them on college campuses, in days schools, at summer camps, in congregational schools, and in youth groups.
