What is Apos®?

Apos® is a targeted innovative approach to addressing knee osteoarthritis, chronic hip and lower back pain. By considering each patient’s unique anatomy, biomechanics, neuromuscular responses, and medical history, Apos® delivers personalized treatment tailored to individual needs.

As easy to use as putting on a pair of glasses, it results in significant improvements in pain relief, functional ability, and quality of life.

Non-invasive approach

Imagine eyeglasses: personalized precision medicine that instantly improves life—no pills, injections, or surgery. Apos® offers the same for knee, back, and hip pain. Complex science made simple.

Personalized treatment

Each patient’s condition is unique, so why treat it with off-the-shelf solutions? Apos® Physical Therapists evaluate each patient’s unique anatomy, biomechanics, neuromuscular responses, and medical history and provide a personalized foot-worn medical device and treatment plan tailored to their individual needs.

Technology driven solution

After treating more than 150,000 patients and based on 15 years of research, we’ve made Apos® as easy as wearing reading glasses. Simply wear Apos® for about an hour a day, and the science does the rest.
