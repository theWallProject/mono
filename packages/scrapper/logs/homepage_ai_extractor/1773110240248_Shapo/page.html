What is testimonial software?

It’s a tool that lets you collect, manage, and display customer testimonials. Shapo gives you one dashboard to grab text or video feedback and publish it on any channel—no coding skills needed.

How do I add Shapo testimonials to my website?

Grab the widget’s HTML code from your Shapo dashboard, paste it into Shopify, WordPress, Webflow—anywhere—and hit publish. Any changes you make in Shapo update on your site automatically.

Can I import my existing reviews from Google or social media?

Absolutely. Connect your accounts and Shapo will import reviews from Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter (X), G2, Capterra, and other sources—then auto-sync any new ones.

Is there a free plan and what’s included?

Yes. On the Free plan you can gather up to 10 testimonials, use unlimited collection forms and widgets, and launch a Wall of Love—all for $0 and no credit card.

Can I collect video testimonials with Shapo?

Yes. Share a collection link, your customer records on their phone or laptop, and the video lands in your Shapo dashboard ready to publish.

Does Shapo work with Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, and other builders?

Yes. Shapo widgets are pure HTML, so you can drop them into Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, Bubble, or nearly any site that accepts embed code.
