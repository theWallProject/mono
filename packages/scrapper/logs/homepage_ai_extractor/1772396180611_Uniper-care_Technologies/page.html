Discover Uniper

Their Life, their home, their way.

We can help your members age in place, together. With support for TV, web, tablet and mobile, however your members choose to join, it has never been easier for your members to improve their health, pursue their passions, make new friends, and stay connected, from wherever they call home, or anywhere else life’s adventure takes them.

With Uniper, your members can join the community, contribute at their own pace, and Connect to Life!

ways to connect

Connect your members to what matters…

a world of well being, content, and community directly from their
TV, smartphone, tablet, or web browser…

Improving Health

Whether it’s video visits with their doctor or live activities and recorded classes carefully designed for senior health & wellbeing, Uniper helps you deliver meaningful results.

Connecting to Community

Uniper is more than our equipment and services— it’s a door to a community of peers that makes it easier than ever for seniors to meet new people and stay connected with friends & family.

Expanding Horizons

Our ever-growing library of informative, enlightening, and inspiring videos, classes, and live activities, empowers your members to discover new passions, learn new skills, and pursue their goals.

Staying Active

Physical activity is a key part of human health. With Uniper, members can join LIVE physical activities from the comfort of their own home, improving flexibility, stability and energy.
