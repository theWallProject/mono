    The SCENTIFIC Device

    Where elegant design meets validated science.

    SCENTIFIC is a Class II medical device designed to provide respiratory biofeedback during periods of rest and sleep. Placed conveniently at the bedside, it operates as a fully contactless system - no masks, no wearables, and no invasive components.

    By working with nasal breathing-related sensory pathways through biofeedback, SCENTIFIC offers a comfortable, non-invasive approach to respiratory biofeedback during nighttime use.

    Contactless Biofeedback

    Experience the convenience of a fully contactless system - no wearables, no masks, and no physical contact. SCENTIFIC is designed for comfortable bedside use during rest and sleep. Advanced radar-based sensors monitor respiratory patterns and body movement from the bedside, enabling continuous respiratory biofeedback without physical contact or intentional interaction.

    Respiratory Biofeedback Guidance

    Patented olfactory biofeedback technology uses brief, precisely timed scent cues as part of a respiratory biofeedback process. These scent-based cues are designed to interact with natural sniffing responses and support breathing awareness during periods of rest and sleep, while operating below the threshold of intentional arousal.

    SCENTIFIC: How It Works

    A non-invasive respiratory biofeedback process designed to support nasal breathing awareness during periods of rest and sleep.

    01

    Setup

    Place the SCENTIFIC device on a stable surface, such as a nightstand, with the scent dispenser positioned approximately 50-75 cm (20-30 in) from your head, according to the setup instructions. The system is fully contactless - no masks, no wires on the body, and no wearables - designed for comfortable bedside use during periods of rest and sleep.

    02

    Monitor

    Using integrated radar-based sensor technology, SCENTIFIC continuously monitors respiratory patterns and body movement throughout the session. This low-energy, contactless sensing technology is designed to detect breathing-related motion through bedding, enabling ongoing respiratory biofeedback without physical contact.

    03

    Guide

    When variations in breathing rhythm are detected, the system may deliver a brief, precisely timed scent cue into the surrounding air as part of the respiratory biofeedback process. This olfactory cue is associated with natural sniffing responses related to nasal airflow and is processed without requiring conscious user action.

    04

    Support

    Through repeated biofeedback cues, the system is designed to support awareness of nasal breathing during periods of rest and sleep. The process operates automatically and quietly, below the threshold of intentional arousal, and is not intended to intentionally modify normal sleep architecture.

    Smart Reports

    **Clinical Insights for Users and Physicians** Automated reports summarize objective respiratory biofeedback data, including breathing patterns, detected respiration events, and sleep-related indicators, to support informed review by users and healthcare professionals.

    **Objective Progress Monitoring** Respiratory biofeedback session data can be securely synchronized to the cloud, enabling longitudinal review of respiratory patterns over time and supporting data-informed adjustments, as guided by the user or their healthcare provider.

    Dual Functionality

    **Relaxation Mode (Day)** Guided respiratory biofeedback sessions, typically 10-20 minutes in duration, designed to support slow, nasal breathing patterns commonly associated with relaxation during wakeful use.

    **Sleep Mode (Night)** During sleep, the system monitors respiratory patterns and may provide olfactory-based respiratory biofeedback designed to support nasal breathing awareness during periods of rest and sleep.

    Key Research Foundations

    Clinical evaluations conducted by Appscent Medical describe the system's capability to monitor and characterize respiratory patterns during rest and sleep as part of a respiratory biofeedback framework.

    Peer-reviewed research indicates that precisely timed olfactory stimulation can be processed during sleep and influence respiratory-related sensory responses without intentional arousal or purposeful modification of sleep architecture.

    A prospective pilot study published in 2024 in the Journal of Sleep Research describes respiration-triggered olfactory stimulation as a non-invasive approach to delivering respiratory biofeedback during sleep under controlled study conditions.

    Natural Biological Mechanism

    SCENTIFIC uses brief, momentary odorant cues as part of an olfactory-based respiratory biofeedback process designed to guide awareness of nasal breathing during rest and sleep.

    This patented approach leverages the body's natural sniffing response, which can be elicited by scent detection even during sleep, without requiring conscious effort or voluntary action.

    The biofeedback process is designed to support nasal breathing patterns during periods of rest and sleep while operating below the threshold of intentional arousal.
