Beach Bum was created in 2015 by industry leaders out of a genuine passion for gaming and technology. The global gaming company is focused on classic hit board & card games played everywhere. In October 2021, Beach Bum was acquired by hypercasual gaming leader Voodoo, opening the door to countless new opportunities. With famous hit titles like Lord of the Board Backgammon, Spades Royale, Gin Rummy Stars, Domino Go and Rummy Rush - Beach Bum entertains millions of players worldwide!
