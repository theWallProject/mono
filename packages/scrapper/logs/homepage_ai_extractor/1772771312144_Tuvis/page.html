#Security

Monitor and protect communications on messaging apps

Integrate your company’s messaging apps with security systems such as firewall, proxy, SSO, and endpoint. We ensure complete control and monitoring of interactions, with advanced protection against data leaks (DLP). Our AI technology enables the application of custom rules, detection of data breaches, and real-time alerts, keeping your company always protected.

#Compliance

Capture and archive your company's communications

With Tuvis, all interactions on messaging apps are captured and archived directly into your existing data storage system. Ensure compliance with privacy regulations and effectively monitor corporate messages, maintaining protection and full control over communications.
