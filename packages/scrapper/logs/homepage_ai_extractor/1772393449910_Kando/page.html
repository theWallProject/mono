Do more with your wastewater data

We are Kando, a wastewater intelligence company dedicated to optimizing the wastewater ecosystem by harnessing the power of Artificial Intelligence and data analytics.

Our Mission: Collaborating to protect and improve the quality of the world’s most precious resource – water.

Introducing Kando’s Solution:
A comprehensive wastewater intelligence platform designed to optimize the wastewater ecosystem and enhance source control. Combining hardware, software, and expert services, our solution is now accessible through an innovative voice assistant for seamless operation and insights.

Learn more about our hardware component here 

Using advanced IoT devices, AI, and LLM, Kando enables precise identification of upstream wastewater quality discharges, preventing recurrences and improving long-term quality. Real-time data, delivered via button or voice command, prioritizes critical issues, helping teams enhance compliance, optimize operations, and protect WWTP processes.

Kando’s Wastewater Intelligence platform includes an Assessment map, offering in-depth wastewater quality insights. This tool helps prioritize daily operations, pre-treatment activities, and strategic objectives, guiding targeted efforts to improve influent quality to your WWTP.
