Our Vision

Transforming foam into solutions that matter everywhere – from the everyday to the extraordinary.

We empower a world where every step is lighter, every journey safer, and every space performs beyond expectations – transforming raw materials into life-changing foam solutions that our global partners and customers depend on.

Who We Are

With over 50 years of expertise, Palziv is a global pioneer in expanded foam materials. Operating from production sites in Israel, Romania, Mexico, and the United States, we deliver innovative foam solutions that power industries and enrich lives.

What We Do Best

At Palziv, we don’t just sell materials, we co-develop solutions tailored to your needs. Combining deep expertise in material science with cutting-edge manufacturing, we co-develop precision-engineered products.

Powered by a vertically integrated model, global reach, and an innovation-driven culture, we’re here to shape smarter, more effective material solutions for your world.

Sustainability

Our Mission​

Innovating foam solutions that protect both performance and the planet
Palziv is dedicated to making environmental sustainability a core priority, ensuring our business practices align with global sustainability standards. We strive to harmonise our operations with environmental responsibility, meeting stakeholder expectations with effective and accountable stewardship. Our commitment reinforces the foundation for long-term success and positive impact.

Sustainability at Palziv

At Palziv, sustainability is more than a goal—it’s how we operate. From powering our Israeli facilities with solar energy to conducting in-depth Life Cycle Assessments (LCA) of our products and monitoring carbon emissions in real time, we’re driving measurable change. Explore how our environmental, social, and community actions are shaping a more sustainable future.

Governance​

Guided by a robust sustainability policy, we continuously identify key environmental challenges, develop innovative solutions, and implement actionable strategies to minimize
our impact. Our commitment extends to upholding rigorous standards, Including ISO 14001 for Environmental Management, ISO 45001 for Occupational Health & Safety Management System and ISO 9001 for Quality Control. ensuring excellence in both practice and results.
