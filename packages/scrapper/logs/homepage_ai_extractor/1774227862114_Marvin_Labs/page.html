Key Features

AI Analyst Chat
AI-powered investment research chat for professional analysts. Ask questions about company financials, earnings, and strategy and get validated answers grounded in filings, earnings calls, and press releases.
Deep Research Agents
Automated AI research agents for equity research workflows. Deploy agents for earnings analysis, guidance tracking, and company primers that update automatically with new filings, calls, and press releases.
Material Summaries
Summaries built for professional investors. They highlight only material and new information, such as changes in business drivers, strategy, or guidance. Linked straight back to the source.
Guidance Tracking
Extract and track all forward-looking statements made by management. Analyze whether companies deliver on what they told investors, assess accuracy and management discipline over time.
Sentiment Analysis
Condenses company communications into a 0–100 score. Scores are updated daily, comparable across peers, and track shifts in management tone across earnings calls, press releases, and presentations.
Automated Data Import
Automatically ingests filings, calls, presentations, and other primary financial content from companies worldwide within seconds. From lengthy text documents and dense PDFs to slide decks and audiovisual content, every source becomes AI-ready for fast, reliable analysis.
