How Jackpot.com works

As a licensed lottery courier service, we make it easy to order tickets for national and state lottery games and scratch offs from your device! Just follow these simple steps:

Step 1

Choose Your Game & Numbers

Choose your favorite lotto game and pick your lucky numbers or use our quick pick option to generate random numbers.
We offer Powerball, Mega Millions, Cash4Life, and numerous state-specific games such as New York Lotto, New Jersey Pick 3 and Pick 4, Arizona The Pick, etc.


Step 2

Place Your Order

Once your order is confirmed, we will purchase the lottery or scratch tickets on your behalf from an authorized retailer.
We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience and security.



Step 3

Get a Scan of Your Lottery Ticket

A scanned copy of your physical ticket will be uploaded to your account.
This digital copy serves as proof of your purchase, but we securely store the original physical ticket.




Step 4

Win & Collect

If you’re a winner, you will be immediately notified and 100% of your prize will be transferred to your account. When you win big, we will securely deliver your ticket.

Frequently Asked Questions


  • 01 How do I order a ticket?

    Ordering a ticket is simple:

    • Choose the state lottery game you want to play.
    • Select your numbers manually or use the Quick Pick option to have them generated for you.
    • Pay for your ticket. After your purchase, your orders are stored electronically, and you can view a scan of your physical tickets in your account at any time.

  • 02 How soon will I receive my ticket scan?

    After your order is placed, we purchase your ticket from an authorized retailer and upload a scan to your account as soon as it’s ready. Most scans are provided promptly after purchase, though exact timing may vary depending on when your order is placed and the volume of orders being processed.

  • 03 How will I find out if I win?

    If you win a prize, you will be notified via an automated email. You can also view the details of any winnings in your lottery account. The latest winning numbers are also posted on the Results pages for each lottery shortly after the draws take place.

  • 04 What happens if I win a jackpot?

    For jackpot wins, we'll contact you immediately and arrange secure delivery of your physical ticket. We'll also provide guidance on claiming your prize from the lottery commission, though you'll always receive 100% of your winnings.

  • 05 How do I receive my winnings?

    Winnings are deposited directly into your account with no fees. For smaller prizes, typically under $600, funds are automatically credited. For larger prizes, we'll securely deliver the ticket so you can undergo the claims process with the state lottery commission.

  • 06 Is there a fee for using Jackpot.com?

    We charge a small service fee that is clearly displayed before you complete your purchase. There are never any hidden charges or fees taken from your winnings.
