Who We Are

Our Mission

At CiNet, we aim to significantly improve the success and lives of Tech CEOs, their families and their companies. We achieve this through curated network opportunities, high-impact development meetings, and by building a strong ecosystem of best-in-class professionals that support our network members in critical decisions and in complex situations.


Our goal is to leverage each other's success stories and provide our members with unparalleled support, knowledge, and tools to navigate challenges and achieve their aspirations.

About Us

The CiNet network was founded and is chaired by Yishai Amir, an experienced executive with a proven track record of leadership and growth. Yishai previously served as a Senior Vice President at HP in the US where he led large-scale global businesses and organizations.


Later he served as the CEO of Landa Corporation, a high-growth tech company with over 600 employees. As a CEO, Yishai recognized the critical need for such a network that could provide leaders like himself with opportunities to share experiences, access best practices, and gain support for both business and personal challenges. This insight became the driving force behind CiNet — a network built by a CEO, for CEOs.
