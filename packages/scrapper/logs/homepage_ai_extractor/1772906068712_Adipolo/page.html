FAQ

What does Adipolo do?

Adipolo is a powerful monetization platform that seamlessly connects advertisers (demand) with publishers (supply), optimizing revenue across three key digital segments: websites, mobile apps, and games.

Designed for maximum performance, Adipolo empowers publishers to unlock premium ad demand while providing advertisers with high-quality, brand-safe inventory.

How does Adipolo work?

Adipolo connects publishers to premium demand sources, maximizing revenue through direct demand deals and OpenExchange access.
This includes Google’s SSP and top advertisers worldwide, ensuring high eCPMs, optimized fill rates, and seamless monetization.
With advanced technology and real-time optimization, Adipolo helps publishers unlock the full potential of their ad inventory while providing advertisers with high-quality, brand-safe placements.

What Makes Adipolo Different from Other Monetization Platforms?

Adipolo sets itself apart with industry-leading technology, exclusive demand access, and seamless integration:

  • Top Google Certified Publishing Partner (GCPP) – A trusted, high-performance solution.
  •  Direct Advertiser Access – The largest portfolio of MENA region demand for premium revenue opportunities.
  • Advanced Optimization – Intelligent matching technology that connects advertisers with the most relevant traffic for superior performance.
Who can use Adipolo?

Adipolo is designed for a wide range of digital publishers and advertisers looking to maximize monetization and access premium demand. It’s ideal for:

  • CTV Publishers & Streaming Platforms – Unlock high-value ad placements and premium demand.
  • Mobile App Developers – Seamless in-app monetization with top-tier advertisers.
  • Website Owners – Boost ad revenue with direct access to Google SSP and global demand.
  • Ad Networks & Media Buyers – Gain exclusive access to premium, brand-safe inventory.

Whether you’re a publisher seeking higher eCPMs or an advertiser looking for quality traffic, Adipolo provides the ultimate monetization solution.
