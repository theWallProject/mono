WHO WE ARE

We are technologists, educators, and instructional designers with over 30 years of experience developing advanced learning and training solutions for millions of people in 50 countries and 35 languages. 

  

We help publishers to transform their content into engaging, AI-powered, immersive learning experiences.

​

​We are developing custom educational and training programs for publishers in STEAM, healthcare, cyber security, quantum security, and more. 

  

Compedia applies technologies like visual computing, augmented reality, virtual reality, advanced systems, AI, instructional design, and UX in its learning solutions. 

​

We collaborate and partner with leading universities, such as Cambridge in the UK, Karolinska Institute in Sweden, where the Nobel prizes are awarded, Hebrew University in Jerusalem, and Bar Ilan University in Israel. 

  

Among the companies and organizations we developed education or training software for are Siemens, Intel, National Geographic, the European Union, and Albert Einstein Hospital in Sao Paulo.
