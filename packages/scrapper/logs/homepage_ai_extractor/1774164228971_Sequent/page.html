Sequent’s three layers of confidence

Sequent, a next generation online voting platform, is purpose-built to address the imperative at the core of online elections: voter confidentiality, end-to-end vote verifiability, and complete system transparency. These three layers are critical to the integrity of any online election.

Confidential Voting

End-to-end ballot encryption and state-of-the-art cybersecurity measures guarantee voter privacy and tamper-proof elections

Verifiable Election Results

Advanced cryptographic techniques provide verifiable proof that every vote is cast, recorded and counted correctly throughout the election process

Complete Transparency

Unique to the Sequent platform, Sequent’s core technology is open source, increasing the system’s reliability and trustworthiness through academic and election expert review and vetting

The software in an E2E-VIV system must be trusted to fulfill the system’s requirements. Making all development artifacts freely available as open source is an important step toward such trust.

The Future of Voting – E2E Verifiable Internet Voting, The US Vote Foundation and Galois, July 2015

Online voting with confidence, and ease

Simple and fast voter experience

Voter gets a unique personal link to a user-friendly, web-based interface. Compatible with any mobile, tablet or PC. No app download necessary.

Trackable and verifiable ballot

After casting his/her vote, the voter gets a receipt to track the vote throughout the election and verify that it is tallied correctly.

Easy-to-use SaaS platform

The Election Manager interface allows officials to easily configure, simulate, control and analyze the entire election cycle.

Seamless 3rd party integration

Comprehensive API easily integrates with other election systems including EMS, IAM, ballot creation software, and election-night results websites.
