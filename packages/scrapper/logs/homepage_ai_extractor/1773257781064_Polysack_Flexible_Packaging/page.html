About Us

Meet Value Chain Requirements

  • Recycling One of the most sorted and reprocessed materials in a circular economy is PE, a ubiquitous flexible packaging material. Mono-Material PE structures can be recycled and reused worldwide.
  • Consumption Sustainable and recyclable packaging is in high demand; however, convenience, performance, and shelf life are critical factors. Polysack MOPE barrier films prioritize all these features.
  • Retail Polysack MOPE grades are available in various formats, including high clarity, matte, and metalized finishes to draw consumers’ attention on shelves and through e-commerce platforms.
  • Distribution Flawless pouch integrity is critical from packer to retailer. Polysack MOPE films make high-quality seals possible, guarantee durable performance, and enhance drop resistance.
  • Design Design for Recycling considers the whole supply chain specifications and processes, hence it’s a crucial step in developing mono-material recyclable packaging.
  • Production Polysack MOPE films are made by co-extruding a unique formula and implementing an inline machine direction orientation to create a PET film alternative.
  • Conversion Polysack MOPE films are durable, productive, and efficient on converting lines. This process includes printing, laminating, and sealing which creates an appealing pouch that ultimately protects the product.
  • Packing Polysack’s high efficiency MOPE films are similar to PET films in that they perform well in the form, fill, and seal (FFS) process as it relates to temperature, pressure, and dwell time, among other specifications.
