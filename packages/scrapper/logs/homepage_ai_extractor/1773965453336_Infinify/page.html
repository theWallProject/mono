About Noa Ganot

Noa is the Founder & CEO of Infinify, a product leadership academy. For over 20 years Noa led the creation of market-leading products and built effective product teams in a variety of industries, company sizes, and business models.

Based on this experience Noa developed training programs, lectures, and consulting methods that coach product leaders and executives to success.

Product management is one of the most impactful roles for tech companies. It can drive a company towards success when done right, or failure when done wrong.
