The DC magic touch

Hair extensions are the ideal way to achieve a look of longer, fuller, and richer hair – easily and quickly. Women who suffer from thinning hair, varying degrees of baldness, or thin & short hair can use hair extensions to make their hair look full, rich, beautiful and longer. There are two things that will determine your satisfaction with the results: The hair quality – DC hair offers one of the highest quality hair available on the market.

Professional hair stylists can feel very convenient when offering their client’s the DC Hair extensions to fulfill the look they always dreamt about, thanks to the very high quality of the DC Hair. Beyond the hair quality, our professional developers have developed very uniquely, strong, and safe keratin and tape to ensure a strong grip on the client’s hair that is very easy to release when it’s time. This combination will determine customer satisfaction.
