O-1 Visa
For startup founders, engineers, scientists, and artists

This is a non-immigrant visa, also known as ‘temporary residence’, that will authorize the candidate to legally work and live in the U.S. for a period of 3 years, with potential extensions and progress towards permanent residence.

Learn More
EB-1A Green Card
For experts with exceptional professional standing
This is one of the fastest options for getting a Green Card. It is reserved for those individuals who can demonstrate extraordinary ability. An advantage of this option is that employer sponsorship is not required.
Learn More
EB-2 NIW Green Card
For individuals working in an area of national interest

The EB-2 NIW category makes it relatively easy for many startup founders and researchers to obtain a Green Card. With the ability to self-petition, flexible criteria, no labor certification requirements, and recent expansions of premium processing, it is one of the fastest options for permanent residency in the U.S.

Learn More
B-1/B-2 Visa
A visa option for travel of a business nature
The B-1/B-2 Visa is a temporary, non-immigrant visa that allows the holder to travel to the United States for either business or tourism purposes. B-1/B-2 Visa holders are permitted to stay in the U.S. for up to 6 months at a time, with potential to extend.
Learn More
Recommendation Letters
Letters explaining your achievements that play an important role in the approval of visa petitions
Recommendation letters are an important part of any successful O-1, EB-1A or EB-2 NIW application. They can be used to confirm or support many different aspects of your achievements, such as working in a critical role for your employer.
Learn More
