Digital Construction Management 

Locusview's DCM Streamlines Utility Construction 

Locusview's Digital Construction Management (DCM) platform automates data capture and streamlines the construction close-out process. By linking planning, execution and project closure, it maintains data accuracy across all systems.

Designed for construction fieldwork, Locusview simplifies complex mapping workflows and makes them accessible to crews, bridging the typical technology gap in the workforce. This enables field workers to gather high-quality data, ensuring compliance and enhancing operational efficiency.

Product

LocusviewBUILD

Paper-based job packets in construction are a major source of frustration for utilities and often cause delays in meeting project deadlines. Back-office staff have to spend a lot of time interpreting handwritten reports, forms and notes, and then enter this information into financial, asset management and GIS systems.

This repetitive and error-prone process can now be streamlined with Locusview's solution, which digitizes the job packets. This allows data to be transferred smoothly and quickly from the field to the back office in just minutes.
