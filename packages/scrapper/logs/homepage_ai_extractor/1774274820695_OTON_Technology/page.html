WHICH CHALLENGES DOES THE OTON BLOCKCHAIN COVER?
Partners' security
Public blockchain guarantees the transparency and control of all financial flows.
Transparency of transactions
The technology works via smart contracts that make all transactions between the participants transparent and make it impossible to leave any data out of the network.
Consistent statistics
Any information transferred via the network is guaranteed to be valid by the principles of the decentralized system.
Complete ownership of the funds
No one controls access to funds in a decentralized system. Distribution of rewards happens automatically and each partner has the right to choose the way of cashing-in the income, including the OTON Wallet.
A possibility to reach international markets
Blockchain not only makes transactions between participants transparent, it also allows them to make cross border payments, growing the geography and customer base of a company.
