Why IPClock?

Cost Efficient

Reduce your total BOM cost without compromising on clock synchronization accuracy, taking advantage of our support of low-cost oscillators.

Flexible Implementation

Choose the implementation method that best suites your needs:

  • Chip on FPGA – Load file for dedicated standalone FPGA
  • Off-the-Shelf System on Chip (SoC) – Bundle of FPGA & load file
  • IP Core – Netlist for FPGA

Unmatched Accuracy

How accurate are we? Under ITU-T G.8261 conditions, we provide:

  • ToD accuracy < ±1µsec
  • Frequency accuracy < 16ppb

Typical lock < 10sec
