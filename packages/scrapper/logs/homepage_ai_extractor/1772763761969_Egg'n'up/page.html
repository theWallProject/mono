    About Us

    We are a FoodTech startup that develops revolutionary plant-based, multifunctional, clean-label egg replacement solutions for the food industry.

    Thanks to novel technology, proprietary know-how and unique plant-based formulations, our innovative egg replacers perfectly emulate the versatile functionality of eggs while maintaining 100% clean-label products.

    This breakthrough presents the gold standard the food industry requires to remove eggs entirely from products.

  • Egg'n'Up’s Technology Delivers the Gold Standard for the Industry

    Perfectly mimicking eggs in multiple food applications

    Achieving the multifunctionality of eggs successfully in alt-egg products is a huge challenge. Egg’n’Up egg replacers perfectly substitute the multifunction of eggs: they bind, gel, foam, emulsify, texturize, and thicken. All the while delivering taste, texture, mouthfeel, and nutritional profile. Just like a real egg.

    Egg’n’Up’s versatile functionalities, sensory properties, and nutritional profiles make it the ideal choice for replacing eggs in various applications, thereby satisfying the diverse needs of food manufacturers.

100% Clean-Label - Meeting Consumer Demand

With a decisive consumer shift toward healthier, better-for-you products and growing consumer demand for transparency and simplicity in food production, maintaining clean-label products that exclude undesired “E numbers” is critical.

Egg’n’Up plant-based egg replacers are, by definition, clean-label products –

  • No E-numbers
  • No Methylcellulose
  • No Hydrocolloids
  • No Transglutaminase
  • Non-GMO
  • Non-Allergens
  • No Cholesterol
  • Short ingredient list

  • Meeting the Strategic Needs of the Food Industry

    The food industry relies heavily on eggs for their multifunctionality in various applications. As a result, the global egg processing market is expected to grow to $40bn by 2027.

    However, this dependency comes at a high price and entails many drawbacks for food companies. The reasons behind the surge of food manufacturers’ need to remove eggs from their products include availability & price volatility, food safety issues, egg allergies, a high environmental footprint, vegan trend, and ethical implications.

    Using Egg’n’Up solutions delivers cost-efficiency, scalability, and improved business processes.

We are passionate about our mission of delivering 100% egg replacers that are 100% clean-label, making better business processes and improving the world for the manufacturers, consumers, and animals.
