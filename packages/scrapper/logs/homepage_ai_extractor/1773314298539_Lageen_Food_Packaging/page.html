LAGEEN Food Packaging LTD  has been manufacturing tinplate packaging since 1939 for the country's leading food industry.

Our expertise is food packing such as vegetables, meat, pickles, coffee, fish and more. We also supply packaging for the paint manufacturing industry.

Advanced technologies allows us to print directly onto the tinplate according to customer specifications.

We provide services, tinplate cans in a variety of sizes, closing machines including spare parts, and technical support to our Customers
