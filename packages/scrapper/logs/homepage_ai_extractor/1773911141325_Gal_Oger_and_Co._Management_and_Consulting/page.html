About Us

& Our Company

Since its inception in 1990, our firm specializes in local taxation and accounting for international corporations conducting business activities in Israel from first contact to full business operation.
We provide an extensive package of services, all designed to provide a ‘Smooth landing’ and effortless compliance with all Israel’s, accounting and tax regulations.
Supporting projects across diverse industry sectors, we provide fiscal representation, controlling & accounting, professional assistance with the banking system and trustee services.
