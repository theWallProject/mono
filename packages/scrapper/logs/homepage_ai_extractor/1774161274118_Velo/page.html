Frequently Ask a Question

Got a question? We've got the answer. Check out our frequently asked questions below.

What does Velo actually protect?

Velo protects the transaction itself —
the data, the APIs, and every external system it touches. We secure sensitive customer information, monitor risk signals in real time, and prevent exposure across carriers, 3PLs, and third-party platforms.

How does Velo know which orders are risky?

Velo connects transaction signals across payments, customer data, devices, IP, carriers, and fulfillment systems — then analyzes them in real time using AI. Because all data flows through a secure, centralized database, Velo detects risk, exposure, and anomalies before they scale.

Will Velo slow down my good customers?

No. Velo operates at the infrastructure layer — scoring, encrypting, and governing transactions in milliseconds. Legitimate orders move faster.
Risky ones are controlled before they create loss or exposure.

Do I need to change my current tools or carriers?

No. Velo integrates with your existing e-commerce platform, payment provider, and carriers — operating as a secure transaction layer on top of your current stack. No replacements. No disruption.

What revenue impact should I expect?

Most merchants see measurable reductions in fraud, disputes, and operational losses — while improving approval rates and reducing unnecessary friction. Stronger security. Higher net revenue.

What integrations do you support?

Velo connects with Shopify, WooCommerce, major payment providers, and leading carriers. Our secure API layer sits between systems — centralizing and encrypting data without requiring workflow changes.
