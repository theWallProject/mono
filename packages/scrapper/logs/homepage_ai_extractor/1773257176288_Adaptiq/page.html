Where we operate.

More about the markets we work in.

Ukraine

One of the largest IT hubs in Eastern Europe, with a market capacity of around $5 billion, a rapidly expanding talent pool of over 300,000 professionals, and up to 30% annual growth.

See open positions
Poland

The country with over 250,000 IT talents and a strong focus on dedicated software development teams. Market capacity of Poland is over $7.5 billion and an annual growth rate of around 5-7%, making it one of the fastest-growing IT markets in Central and Eastern Europe.

See open positions
Portugal

The country has a talent pool of over 50,000 IT professionals who are dedicated to digital transformation and innovation. Portugal's IT industry has been growing steadily, with a market capacity of $8.8 billion and an annual growth rate of 6-8% over the past few years.

See open positions
Spain

Spain is an established and steadily growing IT market in Southern Europe, employing over 450,000 professionals. Its tech ecosystem is driven by strong demand in software development, cloud computing, and digital transformation services. The Spanish IT sector is valued at over $60 billion, with consistent annual growth of around 4–6%.

Romania

One of the most dynamic IT markets in Central and Eastern Europe, Romania is home to over 200,000 IT specialists. The country has a thriving software development industry, driven by innovation and a strong STEM education system. With a market capacity exceeding $6 billion and an annual growth rate of 10%, Romania remains a key player in the European tech landscape.

Bulgaria

A rapidly growing IT hub in Southeastern Europe, Bulgaria boasts a talent pool of over 100,000 IT professionals. With a strong emphasis on software development, cybersecurity, and AI, the country's tech sector continues to expand, contributing to a market capacity of approximately $3.5 billion and an annual growth rate of 5-7%.

Israel

We’re headquartered in Israel together with the majority of our customers. With over 6,000 active startups and an economy dominated by industrial high-tech and entrepreneurship, Israel certainly earned its nickname 'The Startup Nation'. The Israeli tech sector has set a new record with 33 unicorns and $25B in funding.
