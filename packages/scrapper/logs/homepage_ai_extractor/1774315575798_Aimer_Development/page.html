Farming made smarter

Every feature in AIMER is designed to save you time, reduce guesswork, and help you get the most from every paddock.

AIMER Vision and AI-assisted partial farm walks
Use your smartphone to quickly scan pasture covers with 90% accuracy using AIMER Vision, and save hours by measuring only part of your farm while AIMER estimates the rest.
