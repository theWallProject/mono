Technology Intro

Eye-Net offers a real-time, multi-user software solution built on a patented architecture that utilizes existing cellular networks without requiring additional hardware.

Operating seamlessly on smartphones and smart devices, it enables precise collision prediction and near-zero latency. The system incorporates cutting-edge algorithms, dynamic protocols, and a globally distributed cloud network to optimize accuracy, reduce, and minimize latency.
