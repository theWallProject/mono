                Resmetrix Medical

                Building a Personalized Breathing Analytics Platform


                OUR MISSION

                Resmetrix wishes to improve the home evolution and care of patients with cardio-respiratory insufficiency.
                We develop pulmonary functions remote monitoring, easy to use, without patient intervention, to optimize the course of care and quality of life.

                The Problem 
                 
                Currently we cannot continuously and quantitively measure breathing pattern and respiratory symptoms under a natural physiological condition. 
                Indicators like respiratory rate Blood oxygen saturation, heart rate or spirometry pulmonary test, are unable to capture breathing pattern and its translation into symptoms.

                Our Solution
                 
                Continuous Monitoring Pulmonary Functions and Breathing Patterns 

                Adding those quantifiable measurement parameters provides a unique capability to accurately define breathing activity in healthy and ill people.
                Accurately measuring the severity attenuation of the breathing symptoms, in different diseases, enables to adjust treatment and objectively follow the response to therapy.

                Clinical Need: The growing problem of heart failure management 

                 Every heart failure hospitalization increases a patient’s risk for re-hospitalization and further symptom deterioration.

                Symptoms control Heart Failure

                Dyspnea, the subjective experience of breathing difficulty or discomfort, is a hallmark symptom of chronic congestive heart failure (HF). 
                The goal of therapy for CHF is to improve symptom management and quality of life, decrease hospitalizations, and decrease overall mortality. 
                An accurate assessment of Dyspnea is especially important. 

                Challenges in Managing Heart Failure
                Quantitative monitoring CHF symptoms and effective adjusting treatment can prevent re-hospitalization and improve the patient's perception of the disease.
                 
                Remote Monitoring in Managing Heart Failure
                Resmetrix is the only Quantitative Symptoms Monitoring System for breathing  and Pulmonary Functions worsening in CHF.

                Resmetrix Technology

                Monitoring respiratory situation from Hospital-to-Home – 24/7 - 365

                The Resmetrix innovative wearable respiratory monitoring system is a comfortable chest strap that continuously and accurately monitors respiratory patterns of the patient, whether in the hospital, at home, exercising, traveling, or in a living facility. The device wirelessly connects to a smartphone app that displays the respiratory parameters and vital signs in real time and can communicate episodes (exacerbations) early to enable quick attention and rapid clinician response.
                The device is seamless to the patient, requiring no interaction or management to enable its continuous monitoring of lung performance and other related health indicators.

                The Resmeterix System features a  chest-strap powered by a proprietary, patented sensor enabling continuous, and accurate monitoring of breathing-patterns and detection of deterioration and improvement in respiratory trends. The strap includes heart rate measurements, temperature, activity and position sensors. The system utilizes a unique AI-Powered algorithm that assesses disease progression, based on the breathing patterns and trends, predict and alert on exacerbations and response to treatment.
                The system’s smartphone/tablet app provides continuous, interactive real-time access to respiratory and vital signs for the patient and clinicians. The UX not only delivers health performance, but also encourages patient compliance, and utilizes an array of alarms for early signs of exacerbations. All the data is managed securely through a cloud-based platform.

                Clinical Validation

                Clinical study at Rambam Medical Center:

                • Resmetrix clinical results show high correlation between respiratory signals from Resmetrix’ strap and a spirometer/flow meter tidal volume signals.
                • In asthmatic patients, the system detected successfully changes in respiratory patterns during mild attack and response to treatment.
                • Studies in exercise, the system demonstrate the ability to continuously and accurately monitor the respiratory signals in various levels of exercise.

                Value Proposition

                Home Monitoring: With early detection capabilities of patient deterioration and alerts for timely interventions, the Resmetrix system can provide clinicians the ability to manage and treat patient health at any time and in any setting – importantly preventing further deterioration, and unnecessary ER visits, hospitalizations, and re-admissions. The result is a financial savings to the system alongside more efficacious and safer care for patients.

                Hospital: Continuous monitoring of breathing patterns throughout the in-facility care spectrum, will enable rapid intervention and response to patient’s deterioration giving providers greater capacity to dealing with a moment of crisis. Additionally, anywhere monitoring can alleviate ICU placement/admissions and allow facilities to potentially discharge of patients earlier with the security of real-time health data during transition to home or long-term care facility. The result is far more flexibility in time, cost, and patient management for the hospital.
