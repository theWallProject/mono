About Us

Forging the Future of Peppers

CapsiBreed is on a mission to revolutionize pepper breeding through cutting-edge molecular technologies, enabling us to meet the rapidly evolving needs of growers, consumers, and the food, pharmaceutical, and cosmetics industries.

We envision a future where precision-bred peppers unlock new possibilities—from thriving in challenging climates to powering functional foods and natural health products—driving a more resilient, nutritious, and sustainable global food system.

At the core of our innovation is PepGEM™, our proprietary molecular breeding platform. PepGEM™ overcomes the genetic bottlenecks of pepper breeding by integrating bioinformatics, advanced molecular biology, genome editing tools, and optimized tissue culture techniques.
This platform enables the simultaneous development and introduction of multiple high-value traits, unlocking the full potential of peppers.

Technology

The Engine Behind CapsiBreed

Peppers (Capsicum annuum) are among the most popular vegetables worldwide, yet their breeding still relies heavily on traditional methods. Although seed
companies have invested decades in developing germplasm and elite lines, they often face genetic bottlenecks that limit further improvement and hinder the
successful commercialization of new varieties.

To overcome these challenges, we developed PepGEM™ (Pepper Gene Editing and Modification) — a powerful, proprietary platform that brings
advanced molecular breeding to both hot and sweet peppers. PepGEM™ enables the precise, efficient introduction of multiple traits—whether recessive or
dominant—dramatically accelerating the development of novel elite varieties in both speed and cost-effectiveness. The platform supports gene editing and
genetic engineering, and also serves as a powerful tool for trait discovery. 

By integrating AI-driven bioinformatics with deep expertise in plant science, genetic engineering, gene editing, and molecular biology, we are redefining the
future of pepper breeding. In close collaboration with seed company breeders, we unlock the full potential of elite germplasm and deliver breakthrough varieties
tailored to the demands of a rapidly evolving market. 

In addition to peppers, our team brings extensive experience in the genetic modification and editing of other major crops—including tomatoes, lettuce, and
ornamentals—applying cross-crop insights to deliver innovation across horticulture.
