Dynamometers

Dynamometers

If you’re looking to weigh objects while conducting lifting operations, or if you need to measure force or tension in a horizontal application, a crane or tension dynamometer is just the tool for the job.

Crane Scales

Crane Scales

Crane scales are a specific type of industrial weighing device and are designed to be used with cranes or other overhead lifting equipment. By combining precision and accuracy with portability and durability, our Ron Crane Scales are regularly used in countless weighing and lifting applications around the globe.

Try now

Load Cells

Load Cells

Load cells, or more specifically hanging or crane load cells, are devices used to measure weight, force, or tension in various industrial applications.

Try now

Multi-Point

Multi-Point

Multi-point load monitoring systems are advanced solutions designed to measure and display the weight or force from multiple load cells simultaneously, providing real-time data for safe and precise load distribution in complex lifting and rigging operations.

Try now

Additional Options

Additional Options

Additional options are a range of accessories and enhancements designed to expand the functionality, connectivity, and usability of load monitoring systems, allowing for customized solutions to meet specific operational needs.
