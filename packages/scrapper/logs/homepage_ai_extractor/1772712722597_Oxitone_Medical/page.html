The Future of Blood Pressure Monitoring is Cuffless.

Introducing the Oxitone 2000M – The next-generation wrist-worn monitor for continuous blood pressure and vitals.

Discover Oxitone 2000M
 

Mind and Data Against Disease

The Oxitone 2000M is a next-generation medical device that delivers a holistic view of patient health through continuous, automated monitoring.
 
 

Cuffless Blood Pressure:

Frequent systolic and diastolic measurements.

Cardiac Health:

Pulse rate, Heart Rate Variability (HRV), and Cardiac Output.

Respiratory:

Blood Oxygen (SpO2) and Respiratory Rate.

Wellness:

Skin temperature, Sleep patterns, Activity/Steps, and Dehydration monitoring

Who We Serve
