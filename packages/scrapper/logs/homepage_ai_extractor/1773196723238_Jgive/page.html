Your Place for Giving

Jgive is a nonprofit platform that connects donors worldwide to meaningful causes in Israel.

Whether you give once, set up a donor-advised fund, or contribute stock, you can make a lasting difference—while enjoying tax benefits in the U.S., U.K., and Israel.

We invite you to be part of the change—because together, we can build a better future. Want to learn more about us?

The Giving Place of Israel

The Giving Place of Israel—Made Simple for Global Donors
  • Full U.S. Tax Deduction: Donations are processed via Jgive Friends of Asor Fund USA Inc. (EIN: 81-0757923), just like giving to any U.S. nonprofit.
  • Safe & Transparent Giving: Every charity is a vetted Israeli nonprofit, meeting standards equivalent to U.S. 501(c)(3) organizations.
  • Maximize Your Impact: As a nonprofit ourselves, we keep fees low so more of your gift goes where it’s needed.
  • Donor-Advised Funds (DAFs): Centralize your giving, donate stocks, and invest funds to grow your charitable balance.
  • Quick Receipts: Receive your U.S. tax-deductible receipt instantly through our U.S. 501(c)(3) entity.
