Uplifted, Just How We’d Explain to a Friend

You know how your creative ends up spread across drives, editors, ad accounts, and old folders, and it becomes almost impossible to see which parts of your ads actually drove results? Uplifted solves that. It gives you creative asset management, creative analytics, and an AI brief in one place. Every asset is organized, broken into the moments that matter, and linked to performance, so you can instantly see what worked, reuse it, and build the next winning ads without guesswork.
What does Uplifted actually do?

It connects to your ad accounts, Google Drive, Dropbox, and other sources, or you can upload directly, to bring all your creative into one place, including raw footage. It then automatically breaks every asset into key moments like hooks, CTAs, product shots, and scenes. Each piece is deeply tagged and linked to ROAS, CTR, CVR, or any metric you care about.

You can search for things like "best hooks from last quarter" or "product demos that converted" and get the exact clips with the performance behind them. Think of it as next-gen creative asset management that already understands your performance history and surfaces what you need in seconds.

What makes Uplifted different from traditional Digital Asset Management?

Traditional DAMs store files. They help you keep assets organized, but they do not understand what is inside the content and they do not know how that content performed. Uplifted goes much further. It analyzes every scene, hook, CTA, and visual, tags each one automatically, and connects it to real performance data from your ad accounts.

Instead of a folder system, you get a creative intelligence layer that tells you what worked, why it worked, and how to use it again. It is built for teams that need to move fast and produce high performing creative at scale, not just store assets.

How will it change how I work?

No more digging through folders, chasing links, or guessing why something performed. Everything is fully tagged, searchable, and connected to results the moment you log in. You can pull up your best clips instantly, drop them into a Board, and collaborate with your team or the AI Brief.

The brief shows you which hooks, visuals, and messages drove performance and turns them into a clear plan for your next ads. You can build iterations faster, spot fatigue early, and focus your time on producing creative with a higher chance of winning, all inside one platform.

An AI Creative Strategist That Writes, Tags, and Thinks Like You

Uplifted’s AI Creative Strategist helps you brainstorm, tag, analyze, and brief — all from one place. Ask what’s working, get new ad ideas, surface top clips, or generate a performance-backed brief in seconds. It’s like having a strategist, editor, and analyst on demand.

Find Winning Content Without Digging Through Folders

Uplifted makes your entire creative library searchable and performance-aware. Whether it’s a testimonial clip, a high-performing hook, or a forgotten CTA — you can find what works in seconds and reuse it in your next ad.

Remix Top Creatives Into High-Performing New Ads

Uplifted uses performance data to identify the best clips, auto-slices them, and helps your team build new variations in minutes - not days. No more guessing what to test - we surface what works, so you spend less time cutting and more time scaling.

Manage Creative Globally Without Slowing Down

Uplifted tags, transcribes, and analyzes creative in any language — making it easy to find, reuse, and learn from what works. Whether you're in New York, Berlin, or Tokyo, your team sees the same data, speaks the same creative language, and moves with the same speed.
