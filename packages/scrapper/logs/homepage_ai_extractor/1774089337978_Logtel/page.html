Official Training Center of AMD in Israel

Logtel serves as the exclusive training and instruction center for multinational companies leading in their fields, such as AMD Xilinx and Doulos. As a result, we are at the forefront of technology, exposed to the most innovative development tools and products available today in the industry, and updated on the latest advancements and stringent standards.

We invite you to join the many leading organizations in the industry that work with us and the thousands of students from across the country. For over 40 years, Logtel has been leading the field of technological knowledge for engineers in Israel. Over the years, we have trained and educated over half a million engineers. We possess knowledge, experience, and a team of technological experts leading in their fields both domestically and internationally.

Logtel Experts – Technological Consulting

The Pinnacle of Technological Knowledge, Perfectly Tailored to Your Project

Logtel Experts is a consulting company specializing in providing expert advice and solutions in hardware, cyber, communications, and software. Our goal is to offer companies quick and accessible access to the knowledge and experience we’ve accumulated, advancing specific technological projects.

We offer highly skilled personnel through focused consulting models, per project or hourly. These models allow targeted leverage for specific projects, efficiency, and budget flexibility. In essence, Logtel Experts serves as a natural extension of our training, providing joint accompaniment after the training, during work, or within a specific project.
