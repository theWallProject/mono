Powering better batteries through next-generation dry coating

How it works Work with us

Our mission is to decarbonise battery cell manufacture by accelerating the industry’s shift to dry battery electrode coating.

With a proprietary technology platform to make dry coating precursor powders (DCP®) and extensive knowledge in dry coated electrode production, we unlock the process for different customers in the battery market. Together, we can make high-performance, lower cost batteries, that are better for the planet.

Our technology

Anaphite’s technology platform combines all the key ingredients of a battery electrode into a single engineered material, optimised for high-speed dry-coating lines.

We use a chemistry focused approach. Our proprietary compositing processes, combined with low-energy processing, produce tailor formulated dry powders – we call this Dry Coating Precursor powder (DCP®).

How it works

Solving the challenges of industrial dry coating

Switching to dry battery electrode manufacturing, unlocks huge cost and CO₂ savings for OEMs and cell makers, but achieving it at scale is an enormous challenge. Our technology platform is a flexible and scalable solution that eliminates the barriers to cut costs and emissions, while enhancing electrode performance.

Formulation

Electrode active materials, conductive additives and binders are selected. These can be from customer’s existing bill of materials or formulated by Anaphite for specific performance outcomes.

Chemical Compositing

Our proprietary chemical compositing processes disperse the materials and attach them together for optimal microstructure.

DCP®

We use low energy processes to produce an engineered powder, ready for dry coating – DCP®.

Working with us

Work with us

Anaphite works closely with the battery industry as a trusted dry coating development partner. With our in-house formulation process and coating capabilities, we co-develop DCP® dry coated electrodes customers.

We then rigorously test their mechanical and electrochemical performance, validating the DCP ® for pilot line testing and GWh-scale deployment.
