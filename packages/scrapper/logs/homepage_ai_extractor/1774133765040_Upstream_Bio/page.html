Driving Innovation for the Treatment of Respiratory Diseases

We are advancing verekitug, the only known antagonist currently in clinical development that targets the TSLP receptor, with the goal of transforming the daily lives of patients with chronic respiratory diseases.

About Us

Targeting Inflammation at its Source

By potently inhibiting the receptor for TSLP, the upstream driver of pro-inflammatory signaling, verekitug can disrupt the pathway implicated in many inflammatory diseases.

Science

A Unique Mechanism of Action

Our clinical trials are evaluating verekitug’s potential to treat respiratory diseases driven by chronic inflammation where TSLP signaling plays a key role, including severe asthma, CRSwNP and COPD.

PIPELINE

A Driven and Dedicated Team

Our experienced team works together every day to improve the lives of patients with respiratory diseases. We invest in and empower our employees so we can uplift patients.
