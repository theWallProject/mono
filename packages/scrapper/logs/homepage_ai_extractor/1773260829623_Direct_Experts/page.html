This is us

Eldad & Idan Cordoba, The founders of Direct Experts (DEX).

Brothers Eldad and Idan Cordoba founded Direct Experts (DEX) in 2012 to raise the bar in Cloud, IT, Data, AI, and Cybersecurity. Both served in the elite computer units of the IDF, bringing unmatched technical expertise and discipline to the company’s DNA.

They saw the need for a partner that delivers world-class IT, led by top technical managers, a company built by experts, for experts.

Today, under their leadership, DEX is a trusted SI and MSP, relied on by enterprises, startups, and digital-native companies to design, secure, and manage their most critical technology environments.
