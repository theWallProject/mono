Intraocular Implant for Sustained drug release

MicroSert develops an easily implantable and re-fillable, reservoir-type device, known as the “BlueDOT”, for long lasting ocular drug release.

 

The device is implanted in a private office procedure and does not require incision.

 

The implant can be configured to various release profiles, and may be refilled within two years after being implanted.
As a drug delivery device that is capable of sustained and controlled release of biological medications, Microsert intends to introduce this versatile platform for the treatment of Age-related Macular Degeneration (AMD), and Diabetic Macular Edema (DME).
