Meet the AI Wizard™ - The Heart of the Experience

At the core of this innovation is the AI Wizard™, a sleek, compact device designed to revolutionize the way consumers discover and connect with fragrances.

Elegant Interface

In less than a minute, shoppers engage with an elegant digital interface - a short, guided questionnaire that decodes their mood, preferences, and personality.

Personalized Fragrance Match

The AI Wizard™ then curates a personalized fragrance match from the store’s featured collection, along with three tailored recommendations to explore.

A Seamless Experience

Once a scent is chosen, the AI Wizard™ delivers a precise, touch-free spritz - with the actual fragrance, no exposed testers, no waste, no hygiene concerns. Just a pure, immersive scent experience.
