MicroStone: contemporary stone finishes without compromise

MicroStone is our innovative microcement designed to bring a natural stone-like aesthetic to interior and exterior spaces. With 15 different tints and oxides, its versatility allows a wide range of finishes, from smooth, polished surfaces to more rustic textures.

Proudly made in Australia, MicroStone is easy to apply, long-lasting, and offers a lower environmental impact than conventional surface finishes.

Elevate your space with our Designer Range

Rockcote Designer Range draws inspiration from nature to deliver beautiful interior and exterior finishes. Bringing together our Natural Materials and Specialty Finishes, this collection blends earthborne materials with modern textured renders to create surfaces rich in texture, warmth and character.

Each finish is crafted to elevate architectural spaces with a natural, timeless appeal without compromising the environment.

Built on a commitment to sustainability and community

Made on Queensland’s Sunshine Coast, our products are developed to perform in harsh climates while taking a considered approach to environmental impact. Our approach is shaped by the resilience of the natural world and the communities we work within. 

We focus on thoughtful formulation, energy-efficient production and the use of quality raw materials to reduce reliance on harsh chemicals. Across our range, we continue to support low-VOC, non-toxic finishes and help create spaces that are durable, naturally beautiful, and built with care.
