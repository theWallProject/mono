Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Spekit

Spekit is an AI-powered revenue enablement platform that delivers coaching, content, and deal intelligence to sales reps inside the tools where they already work. It combines a governed knowledge engine, agentic coaching, personalized buyer experiences, and analytics into a single system designed to accelerate rep performance across the entire deal cycle.

Rep Acceleration is Spekit's approach to enablement: instead of sending reps to a portal to find what they need, Spekit brings coaching, content, and actions directly into Salesforce, Gmail, Gong, and every tool in the selling workflow. The result is faster ramp, higher capacity per rep, and more consistent execution across the team.

Spekit is built for high-growth B2B revenue teams, typically mid-market to enterprise companies with 1,000 or more employees. The platform serves CROs, sales leaders, sales enablement teams, product marketing, and the reps themselves. Customers include Southwest Airlines, iHeartRadio, Employment Hero, Q4, and Pacvue.

Most enablement platforms are content repositories with training bolted on. Reps have to leave their workflow, search a portal, and hope what they find is current. Spekit is built the opposite way: a governed knowledge engine that delivers content, coaching, and actions directly inside the tools where selling happens.
