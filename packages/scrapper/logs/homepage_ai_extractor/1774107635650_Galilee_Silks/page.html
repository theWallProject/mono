Bar Mitzvah Tallit Size

In general, there are two styles of a Bar Mitzvah tallit. The most popular one is worn as a Shawl, and comes in a standard size of 20"x72". The design pattern appears one side of the tallit. The second style is a full size design, also referred as "size 60" tallit, which measures 55"x72". This design is worn folded over the arms and shoulders, and in superior designs the pattern appears on both sides of the tallit. We at Galilee Silks can make in both styles any design listed on our website . We can also make a Bar Mitzvah tallit in any other size - making it a custom tallit based on each individual's preferences. To learn more about the different Bar Mitzvah tallit sizes contact us using the chat box or the Contact us form.
