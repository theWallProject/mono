THE factory

We lead innovation

At Hagor, development and production go hand in hand — from the ideas conceived within our R&D Department to fully field-ready products.

All manufacturing is carried out in Israel and supplied to defense and tactical clients around the world, with strict adherence to timelines, superior craftsmanship, and the use of the highest-quality materials available worldwide.

Our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art machinery and advanced production systems, enabling precise execution of complex, large-scale projects.

Quality control is implemented throughout every stage of production, ensuring that every product delivered is accurate, reliable, and built to perform over time.
