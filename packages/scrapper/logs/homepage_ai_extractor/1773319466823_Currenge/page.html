The Currenge Transaction:

How it Works?

1

REGISTER: FREE, NO CHARGE

Complete your Know Your Customer form by answering few simple questions and uploading a few documents.

Filling out the Know Your Customer form is expected to take less than ten minutes.

In order to make the transaction fast, we will update the bankers handling the process on our behalf in advance with the details of your transaction.

At the same time, we will ask you to update the bankers who serve you with these details. This dual coordination is intended to achieve a smooth and fast process for you.

2

REQUEST A TRANSACTION

You would fill out the details of your requested transaction.

Requested transaction details will be electronically sent to both of us.

Filling out the transaction request form is expected to take less than five minutes.

3

TRANSACTION EFFECTUATED

Transfer funds to the Currenge bank account. We will convert the received money, from which a small fee has been deducted, and transfer proceeds to the beneficiary.

We strive to transfer proceeds to beneficiaries, that hold a bank account in Israel, on the same business day we have received your money on, given it’s a day foreign currency is traded at.
