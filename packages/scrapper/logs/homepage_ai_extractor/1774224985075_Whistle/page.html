Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can we get started?

For Hire SDR service, we can typically identify a good candidate in under 24 hours and place the SDR within five days (provided there's no delay from the client).

Can we choose how many outsourced SDRs we need?

Yes. You decide how many reps you need and can scale up or down as your pipeline demands.

Do you only do outbound?

As SDR providers with inbound and outbound solutions, we specialize in outbound, inbound and PLG. We handle everything from cold outreach to following up on inbound MQLs that are sitting in your CRM getting cold. If leads are going unanswered or unqualified, we're here to help.

Who handles the messaging and strategy?

Our team does. We craft custom messaging, build sequences, and align with your go to market motion — you just review and approve.

Do you integrate with our CRM?

Yes. We optimize and plug into your CRM so every lead, conversation, and meeting is fully trackable.

Are you responsible for closing deals?

No. We create qualified sales opportunities and book meetings. Your sales team is responsible for closing deals.

What makes Whistle different from other SDR agencies?

Unlike a traditional SDR agency, we don't throw reps at the problem. We offer a range of services to fill your unique gaps - from a simple offshoring model with optional add-on services, to fully managed outbound sales development (in which you get vetted SDRs, data, messaging and a full tech stack). We're one of the few B2B sales development agencies that combines people, process, and technology under one roof.

How fast can we get started?

For our fully managed outbound sales development services, we go live in 10 business days — from onboarding to first outreach, without sacrificing quality. For Hire SDR service, we can typically identify a good candidate in under 24 hours and place the SDR within five days (provided there's no delay from the client).

What kind of results should we expect?

While outcomes vary by segment and volume, most clients see their first meetings within 2–3 weeks of launch and a consistent flow of qualified opportunities after month one.

What does pricing look like?

Pricing varies based on scope — whether you're hiring SDRs, running full-service outbound, or need RevOps support. We’ll walk you through a clear quote on your intro call.

What is SDR as a service, and is it right for my business?

SDR as a service is a model where a specialist agency provides fully managed Sales Development Representatives on your behalf - without you needing to hire, train, or manage them internally. It's ideal for B2B companies that need qualified pipeline quickly and predictably, without the overhead of building an in-house function. Whistle's SDR lead generation programmes are designed to plug directly into your go-to-market motion.

What should I look for when comparing B2B sales development agencies?

When evaluating B2B sales development agencies, the most important factors are: proven results in your industry, transparent reporting, speed to launch, and whether the agency handles both strategy and execution. Many B2B outbound sales development agencies will promise meetings but fall short on consistency. Look for case studies, clear onboarding processes, and a team that functions as a true extension of your business - not just a vendor.

How does Whistle handle B2B lead qualification?

Our B2B lead qualification services go beyond basic list building. We define your Ideal Customer Profile, verify contact data, and use multi-channel outreach (cold calling, cold email, and LinkedIn) to engage and qualify leads before they ever reach your sales team. Every meeting booked is a qualified opportunity, not just a cold intro.

Do you work with companies that need inbound and outbound SDR support?

Absolutely. Whistle is one of the few SDR providers with inbound and outbound solutions. We not only generate net-new pipeline, we also respond to and qualify inbound MQLs, ensuring no lead goes cold. This makes us an ideal partner for companies running both paid and organic demand generation alongside their outbound motion.

Can I trial Webflow before paying?

Sure! You can test out Webflow on our free plan where you can experiment with 2 projects. Your unhosted projects will have a two-page limit, but you can purchase a site plan on a per-project basis to unlock up to 100 static pages and additional CMS pages.

What is a project?

A project is a website that you build in Webflow. You can publish projects to a webflow.io staging subdomain for free, export the code on a paid plan, or add a site plan to connect your custom domain and unlock hosting features.

What can I white label?

Pro accounts can add their own logo to Client Billing forms and the Editor. Pro accounts can also remove references to Webflow in the source code and form submission emails, and hide the Webflow badge from their staging sites.

Webflow hosting scales automatically to handle millions of concurrent visits. All site plans serve sites through our Amazon's Cloudfront CDN and accelerated using Fastly, loading sites in milliseconds.
