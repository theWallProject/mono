Why Use an Indoor Positioning System?

  • Visitors, who can conveniently find what they need, feel their time is valued – increasing satisfaction and customer spend
  • Optimized operations and marketing, using location-based actions, result in efficient service
  • Data-driven decision-making improves space utilization, traffic management and layout performance

Why Use Oriient's Geomagnetic Indoor GPS?

  • No hardware or installation makes implementation exceptionally quick, simple and scalable
  • A cost-effective SaaS model solution – removes the need for CAPEX investment
  • Unparalleled accuracy of 1 meter (3 feet) ensures the highest quality user experience, and delivers definitive data
