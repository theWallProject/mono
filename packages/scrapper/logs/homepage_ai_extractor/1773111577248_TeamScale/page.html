Datagen
The Challenge
Datagen, a dynamic startup specializing in 3D technologies, grappled with recruitment and retention challenges to fuel their goals. Seeking a solution, they turned to TeamScale. With years of expertise in building and engaging exceptional technology teams, TeamScale offered a tailored recruitment process perfectly aligned with Datagen’s unique requirements.
The Solution
Employing comprehensive technical and personal assessments, TeamScale ensured that only the most outstanding candidates were presented to support Datagen’s growth. Notably, their swift recruitment capabilities enabled Datagen to respond to project demands, even during peak periods. Within a remarkable three-month timeframe, they successfully onboarded 80 highly skilled professionals—a feat made achievable through TeamScale’s unwavering support and professional expertise.
The RESULT
TeamScale’s invaluable contribution propelled Datagen’s go-to-market strategy and granted them a competitive edge in their endeavors. By meticulously curating a pool of top-tier talent, TeamScale empowered Datagen to thrive and excel as a company and attain its dev milestones.
Bits Of Gold
The Challenge
Bits of Gold, the crypto fintech startup, encountered significant obstacles in their pursuit of assembling and retaining a highly skilled team of full-stack developers. Overcoming challenges such as high labor costs and the scarcity of specialized local talent proved to be challenging. As a company in the financial sector, Bits of Gold had an added need to ensure that all developers involved would apply to regulatory rules and regulations.
The Solution
Recognizing the unique needs and aligning with Bits of Gold’s company culture and values, TeamScale emerged as the perfect partner. They employed a thorough talent recruitment and onboarding process, meticulously identifying and screening candidates based on qualifications and cultural fit.. TeamScale ensured stringent compliance with IT regulations, specific to fintech clients, allowing Bits of Gold to become the first crypto firm to obtain a capital markets license. This pivotal achievement enabled them to forge partnerships with global banks and financial institutions.
The RESULT
Overall, TeamScale’s exceptional recruitment and onboarding services played a crucial role in helping Bits of Gold overcome challenges related to high labor costs, talent scarcity, and compliance issues.

WHY US

Our Story

We built TeamScale out of our own need to work with top outsourced talent.
TeamScale was founded by Amir Zeltzer and Yaron Zeltzer, two Israeli brothers from the leading startup nation, who were working on their own venture and ran into the challenge to develop better and faster. In search of premium outstaffed development support, they forged a relationship with India’s top talent developers and the rest as they say is history.

TeamScale has been operating globally since 2010 working with top Israeli startups and tech companies. We pride ourselves on the “no bench” methodology and find the best candidates suited for your needs. TeamScale founded the Israel India Innovation HUB, a collaborative space where researchers, scientists, and entrepreneurs from India and Israel can come together to work on innovative projects and ideas. We are committed to fostering a culture of collaboration and continuous learning and are eager to support the development of innovative projects and ideas.
