    ICT Sandbox is an innovative digital platform utilising digital tools to enhance the education and development of young children, whose reading skills are still developing during the early years of school (ages 4 - 8 years).
    ICT Sandbox prepares young children for success in an ever-changing world of search engines, emails and social networking. ICT Sandbox equips young learners with age-appropriate digital tools compatible with their environment and level of development, enabling them realise their full potential.

    ​

    ICT Sandbox can be incorporated into the daily learning process and is compatible with the school’s curriculum.
