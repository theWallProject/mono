Welcome to Programa

Programa is an international management consulting and engineering company specializing in supply chain, operations, logistics, materials handling design, analytical modeling & simulation.

Our journey as a boutique and unique knowledge based company has led us to work with some of the world's leading companies. We constantly strive to add value to our clients' business by developing and implementing significant, cost-effective, tangible benefits, thus achieving a competitive advantage in their market place.
