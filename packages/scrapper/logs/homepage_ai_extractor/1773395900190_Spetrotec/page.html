We Are Spetrotec

Spetrotec is an Israeli tech company that designs, develops and sells IoT electronics, online tracking systems and monitoring solutions. Our systems combine cutting-edge software and hardware to enhance car, truck and motorcycle communication and diagnostic capabilities, while also greatly improving vehicle and asset protection.
