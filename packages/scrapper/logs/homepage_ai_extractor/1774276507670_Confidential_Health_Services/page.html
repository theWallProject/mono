Welcome to Confidential Health Services, LLC.

Confidential Health Services, LLC. is bonded and insured to give you the peace of mind that a trustworthy caregiver will be in your or your loved one’s home. We assure families that their loved ones are well cared for and continue to live healthy and happy.

We monitor our caregivers through one-on-one contacts with our clients via telephone check-in and home visits. It is our main priority to make sure that our care recipients are confident and comfortable with the level of care they are receiving from Confidential Health Services, LLC..
