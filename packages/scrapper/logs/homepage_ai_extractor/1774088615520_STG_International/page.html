COMPANY PROFILE
STG International, a public company traded on the Tel-Aviv stock Exchange, is a leading Value Added Reseller of electronic components, equipment and systems in Israel. The Company is engaged with all market segments of the high-tech industry in Israel with an impressive track record, offering optimal solutions to customers to which it is a valuable partner. STG’s expertise is in the fields of Defence, Communications, Security, Medical, Industrial and Semiconductor industries. The company’s long-standing involvement with government electronic business makes it an integral part with most major Avionics and Defense programs. STG has a staff of 60 employees most of whom are engaged with field technical marketing and sales. The Company takes pride in its operational excellence and high customer service level, supported by “best in class” computing systems and warehouse capabilities.
