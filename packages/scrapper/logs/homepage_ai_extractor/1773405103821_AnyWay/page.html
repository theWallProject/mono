Who We Are

AnyWay Solutions is a consulting engineering firm specializing in climate-resilient transport infrastructure. With over 20 years of experience across Africa, Asia, and the Pacific, we combine fit-for-purpose engineering with advanced geospatial technologies and deep cultural understanding to deliver roads that last.

Solutions

Planning to Delivery

Explore more

AI-Driven Route Optimization & Planning

Route alignment using AI combined with satellite imagery, DTM/DEM models, and geotechnical overlays. Utilizing earth observation for advanced engineering to deliver cost-effective and timely data-driven designs.

Optimized Engineering for Rural Roads

Designing and rapidly delivering resilient low-volume rural roads by combining remote technologies with targeted local engagement that builds workforce capacity and uses locally sourced materials.
 

Digital Road Asset Management

Automated road surveys, pavement distress detection, and maintenance optimization tools. Utilizing AI for cost-effective and timely data-driven decision making.

Construction Supervision

Field presence, QA/QC, and stabilization expertise from feasibility to final handover.
