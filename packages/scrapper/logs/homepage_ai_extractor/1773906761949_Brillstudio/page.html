About Us

BrillStudio was founded by Nadav Brill, A Creative Director, 3D Artist and Technical-Director with substantial experience in the AR/VR, simulations and post production industry. Nadav has held various positions in major film and post-production studio’s around the world such as MPC,Rainmaker, Gravity(israel) and more and has finally opened his Studio in Jaffa’s old city, Tel-aviv. Nadav’s work rests on a solid familial tradition of artists, which he incorporates in his studio.
