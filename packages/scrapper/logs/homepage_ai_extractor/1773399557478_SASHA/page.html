PROVIDING INDEPENDENCE MINIMIZING THE NEED FOR CAREGIVERS

SASHA stands for self ability supporting home activities. As a provider of robotic and artificial intelligence integrated solution, SASHA supports performance of all daily activities, virtually eliminating the need for care givers and unprecedentedly reducing the cost of care for its recipients, their families, assisted living facilities, insurers, and society.
