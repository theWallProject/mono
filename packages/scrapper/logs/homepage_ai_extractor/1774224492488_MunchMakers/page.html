Common Questions

Custom Smoke Accessories FAQ

What is the minimum order for custom grinders?

Our minimum order for custom weed grinders is just 6 units. This makes it easy for small businesses, dispensaries, and brands to get started with custom branded products without a large upfront investment.

How long does it take to produce custom rolling trays?

Custom rolling trays typically ship within 3-5 business days after artwork approval. Rush orders may be available for time-sensitive projects. Production includes full-color UV printing that is scratch and fade-resistant.

Can I customize the packaging for my branded products?

Yes! We offer custom packaging options for all products including custom boxes, pouches, and retail-ready displays. Custom packaging is available for orders of 100+ units and helps create a premium unboxing experience for your customers.

What printing methods are available for custom vape pens?

Custom vape pens and dab pens can be customized with laser engraving for a premium etched look, or full-color UV printing for vibrant multi-color designs. Both methods are durable and won't wear off with regular use.

Do you offer bulk discounts on custom ashtrays?

Yes, all products including custom ashtrays qualify for our volume discount program. Save 10% on 6+ units, 20% on 24+ units, 30% on 48+ units, and 40% on orders of 100+ units. Discounts apply automatically at checkout.

What file formats do you accept for custom logos?

We accept AI, EPS, PDF, PNG, and JPG files for custom printing. For best results, provide vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) with at least 300 DPI resolution. Our free online design tool also allows you to create custom designs directly on our website.
