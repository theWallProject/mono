OUR

SERVICES

International Forwarding

We specialize in international forwarding to any point across the globe - both by sea and land. Our company offers the most attractive prices for weight, size of baggage, the type of products in it and the date when it should reach its destination.

Supply chain

Supply chain management assistance: The way a product goes overseas is not easy. Starting with picking up the product from your warehouses, planning the packaging for optimal delivery, shipping the necessary taxes and comprehensive planning of the route through which the goods will move. We assist all our clients in supply chain planning from the time they separated from the bag to the moment it reaches its destination.

Customs brokerage

Israel has complex trade agreements with some countries around the globe. With other countries there is a customs charge that depends on the nature of the goods, the quantity shipped and its weight. For years, we have been handling customs payments at various destination points to ensure smooth and rapid passage of packages and containers and avoid paying fines due to non-compliance with the tax laws of one state or another.

About Us

KCI LTD have been serving customers since 1988!

With our over the international agents, we can provide services to every shipment, of any size, to every location, of any kind of goods!.

Kelner Cargo Israel (KCI) LTD is a leading and trusted logistics company in Israel that offers a wide range of integrated logistics services that can support every single aspect of a supply chain for all types of companies.

 

Our company has over 30 years of experience as a freight forwarder as well as in importing and exporting, cargo transportation, customs clearance, packing, drop shipments, courier and door to door services, and insurance just to name a few of the services that we expertly offer.

 

By combining our experience with the expertise of our highly trained staff, we can offer personalized supply chain solutions to clients to ease them of the burden of meeting increasingly intricate foreign trade requirements.
