What is Slauth.io?

Slauth.io is a security platform that utilizes advanced Language Learning Models (LLM) and traditional scanning methods to generate least-privilege Identity and Access Management (IAM) policies for machines, services, and workloads.

What data is going to be shared with Slauth.io?

Slauth.io does not have access to any of your code. The CLI scans your code, sends parts of it to OpenAI and returns the least-privilege policy to you in your terminal or CI/CD. Similar to Github Copilot, OpenAI will read parts of your code.

What are the prerequisites to use Slauth.io?

To fully utilize Slauth.io's capabilities, you'll need:
- An app deployed on AWS or GCP
- Languages: All languages are technically supported but Typescript has shown the most success from our experience to date

How does Slauth.io support multi-cloud or hybrid cloud environments?

Currently, Slauth.io is focused on AWS and GCP environments. However, we have plans to extend our support to multi-cloud and hybrid cloud environments in the future.
