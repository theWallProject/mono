Everyday moments of joy

Bring creativity and fun into your home

It’s never too late - or too early - to bring a new creative hobby into your daily life. Whether you've always wanted to play the piano or guitar, express yourself through drawing, or sing your heart out, Simply makes it simple and fun to dive into your creative interests.

START YOUR JOURNEY

Let your journey begin

Pick up a new creative hobby or take one you already enjoy to the next level.

Have the time of your life learning

Make it a time for your creative expression or a way to connect with loved ones.

Find your own rhythm

Pace yourself or dive right in. We’ll be by your side every step of the way.

Whatever your level, gain the skills you need to play the songs you love and make your piano dreams come true.

MORE ON PIANO

Whether you’re a seasoned strummer or just starting your musical journey, Simply Guitar makes learning an absolute blast with personalized tutorials and feedback.

MORE ON GUITAR

It’s time to let your voice shine! With your favorite songs adjusted to your vocal range, no note is out of reach.

MORE ON SING

Dive into the captivating world of pencil drawing with our step-by-step instructions and personalized courses.

MORE ON DRAW

Keep your guitar, bass, or ukelele in perfect pitch with our fast, easy, and 100% free tuner.

MORE ON TUNE

Wherever you are, step into the music, build real skills, and experience your piano journey like never before with the power of Augmented Reality.

DISCOVER XR

Stay tuned for new additions to the Simply family.

Find joy, growth, and meaning in the pursuit of creative hobbies.
