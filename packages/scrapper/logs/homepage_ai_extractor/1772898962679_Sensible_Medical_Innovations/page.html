The ReDS™ System

ReDS™ (Remote Dielectric Sensing) is a noninvasive, easy-to-use system for monitoring and management of pulmonary congestion in patients with heart failure and other chronic diseases requiring lung fluid level monitoring. ReDS measurement takes only 45 seconds and can be performed in any point of care.
