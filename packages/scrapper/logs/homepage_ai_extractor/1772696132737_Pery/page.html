Your behavioral health happens in between appointments.

Pery is a SaaS platform that seamlessly integrates mental health into clinical workflows, automating patient-reported outcomes (PROs), AI-driven analytics, and care coordination. By continuously monitoring patients between visits and integrating directly with EHRs, Pery enhances provider decision-making, improves patient engagement, reduces administrative burden and drives better outcomes.

How it works

Intelligent BH Monitoring

Pery integrates with a clinic’s EHR system, allowing physicians and therapists to activate mental health monitoring without disrupting existing workflows. The clinic decides whether to monitor all patients or focus on a specific group (e.g., at-risk patients, diagnosed patients, or those starting medication).

Pery’s user-friendly app ensures continuous personal tracking, with patients receiving questionnaires based on predefined settings. It includes customized, validated assessments, short check-ins to track mood, sleep patterns, and daily activity – and smart reminders to increase patient engagement.

Pery analyzes collected data and generates real-time insights for the therapist. It detects patient condition changes, enabling measurement based decision-making and adjustments to treatment plans. Therapists get structured summaries with aggregated data, comparisons and AI-driven recommendations.

Frequently asked questions.

  • Does Pery Integrate with my EHR?

    Yes, Pery integrates seamlessly within over 20 major EHRs!

  • How will I know which patients qualify?

    We use AI to scan your diagnosis codes and identify which patients meet the qualification requirements for BHI.

  • What if we are part of a larger healthcare system?

    No problem! We work with clinics of all shapes and sizes.

  • Does it matter what insurance our patients have?

    Nope! BHI is covered under both CMS and commercial policies. It is however important to note that reimbursement rates may vary based on location, carrier and patient.
