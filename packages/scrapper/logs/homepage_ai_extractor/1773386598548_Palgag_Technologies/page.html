About Palgag Group

Palgag, established in 1990, is a privately owned group specializing in Design and Build projects, as well as in the full range of roofing and cladding solutions, steel and wood structures, from the initial planning stage to final construction.

News and updates

  • PALGAG Awarded Prestigious 5-Year HVAC IDIQ Contract

    PALGAG is proud to announce that we have been selected, alongside a few distinguished contractors, for a 5-year HVAC Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract.

    Contract Highlights:

    • Client: U.S. Department of State – OBO Facilities
    • Scope: Design, engineering, and construction services for the replacement of central HVAC systems, including chiller and boiler plants.
    • Location: U.S. Government facilities worldwide

    Under this significant contract, PALGAG will deliver top-tier services in strict compliance with the Statement of Work and specifications.

    We are honored to contribute to the enhancement of critical infrastructure at U.S. Government facilities across the globe.

  • PALGAG has been awarded by AECON the design, fabrication and installation of metal cladding systems at the new L.F. Wade International Airport in St. George, Bermuda. All the systems and its substructures were designed, tested and approved under the Florida Building Code for High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ).

  • PALGAG has Completed the NAMRU-SOUTH (Naval Medical Research Unit) Revitalization Project

    PALGAG is proud to announce the successful completion of phases 1, 2, and 3 of the revitalization project for Building 1 at NAMRU-SOUTH, formerly NAMRU-6.

    NAMRU, a biomedical research laboratory of the US Navy, is located in Lima, Perú. The 25,000-square-foot, two-story building, housing research laboratories and administrative support areas, was delivered to the owner and received its BOD (Beneficial Occupancy Date) on September, 2024.

    A detailed description of the project scope and achievements is available under the “Projects” section.

  • New Project Spotlight: U.S. Embassy Santo Domingo

    PALGAG has been awarded by the U.S. Department of State – OBO, a project under the HVAC IDIQ contract to replace two (2) existing air-cooled chillers at the U.S. Embassy in Santo Domingo.

    Under this contract, PALGAG is to conduct a detailed survey to verify existing conditions, which will be followed by a complete HVAC and Electrical design, with PALGAG serving as the Designer of Record (DOR) for both disciplines. The construction phase will include the installation of two (2) 355-ton 1248 kW air-cooled modular scroll chillers (N+1 configuration) and all related electrical work.
