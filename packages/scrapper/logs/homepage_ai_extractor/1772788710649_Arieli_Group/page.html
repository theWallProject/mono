Prosperity through Innovation

Arieli Group partners with visionary entrepreneurs to transform bold ideas into groundbreaking technologies.

Arieli Group is a global investment firm committed to creating enduring prosperity. With a forward-thinking approach, we invest in early and growth-stage companies across industries including cybersecurity, defense, deep tech, healthcare, energy, fintech, and agritech. Rooted in a third-generation family office, we are dedicated to building the next generation of technologies and driving them forward.
