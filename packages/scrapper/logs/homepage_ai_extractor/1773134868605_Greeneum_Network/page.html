CARBON Emissions Reporting and DISCLOSURE

The Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR) is a European regulation introduced to improve transparency in the market for sustainable investment products, prevent GREENWASHING, and increase transparency around SUSTAINABILITY CLAIMS made by financial market participants.

The European Union has finalized the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (“CSRD”) that will introduce more detailed sustainability reporting requirements for EU companies, non-EU companies meeting CERTAIN THRESHOLDS for net turnover in the EU, and companies with securities listed on a regulated EU market.

The SEC's NEW PROPOSAL from 2023 would require US publicly traded companies to disclose ANNUALLY how their businesses are ASSESSING, MEASURING, and MANAGING CLIMATE-RELATED RISKS. This would include the DISCLOSURE of GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS (GHG) as a measure of exposure to CLIMATE-RELATED RISK.

The Challenges

The renewable energy industry has challenges that need to be addressed. Greeneum has advanced and creative solutions to address these challenges.

 

The Market

The carbon and clean energy market are currently lack a one stop shop marketplace for organizations and companies that would like to verify, certify and trade their emission and renewable energy and to ensure trust in the global markets. 

 

The Technology

Greeneum is a blockchain and AI-based platform focused on promoting green energy and sustainability. It utilizes smart contracts and decentralized technologies to track, certify, and reward the production and reduction of carbon emissions and renewable energy. By providing real-time data and transparency, Greeneum incentivizes stakeholders with digital certificates and tokens (GREEN) and fostering a more eco-friendly energy ecosystem and supporting global carbon reduction efforts.

 

The Data

Currently, data is fragmented throughout centralized players in the marketplace using data and artificial intelligence (AI), Greeneum’s platform drives decision-making for the production, planning, and implementation of a more efficient and cost-effective energy grid.  

 

The Solution

 
 

Greeneum solves the challenges of transitioning from central production and distribution to regional and local communities through an integrated proprietary and secure solution.

Greeneum’s offers software-as-a-service (SaaS), application programming interfaces (APIs), and Secure Internet of Things (IoT) Technology that enables management, smart predictions, optimization and trade of carbon emissions and renewable energy.

Using real-time machine learning and advanced multiple validations, Greeneum monitors and rewards the reduction of the carbon footprint throughout the macro and micro-network.

Based on blockchain technology and smart contracts, Greeneum creates a P2P trusted framework for recording, management, and trading of products, data, and energy.

Greeneum provides end-to-end solutions for energy challenges and fosters social impact.
