Market Spotting

Automated market intelligence platform

MarketSpotting is a platform that provides extensive coverage of various markets by analyzing data from social and open sources.

It is primarily designed to cater to market analysts by serving as a one-stop-shop for processing intelligence alerts, receiving AI-driven recommendations, and generating management reports.

This platform enables businesses to identify untapped market opportunities, analyze target industries, and monitor competitors. Using advanced AI technology, irrelevant data is filtered out to bring forth relevant insights, resulting in increased efficiency and the creation of proprietary tagged data.

The platform is highly customizable to meet the unique requirements of each end-user and is trusted by top vendors worldwide, covering an unmatched amount of content.
