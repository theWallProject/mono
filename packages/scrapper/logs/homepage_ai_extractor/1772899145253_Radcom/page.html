Agentic AI

Transform network operations and service management with customer-centric, end-to-end data, driving efficiency, reducing costs, and enabling automation.

Automation

Automatic testing and verification for on-demand instantiation, healing, and updates deliver intuitive scaling processes for network optimization.

Cloud-Native​

A scalable set of microservices in containers—deployed on public, private, or hybrid clouds—provides flexible cloud-native functionality.  

RAN-to-Core​

By correlating real-time data across the RAN to the core, the platform enables packet-level analysis and actionable insights.  

Open

A universal, vendor-agnostic approach offering full support for seamless integration, provisioning, and management of infrastructure and network resources.
