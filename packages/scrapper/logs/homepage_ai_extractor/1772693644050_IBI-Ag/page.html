How Single Domain Antibodies Help Crop Production

Single domain antibodies are becoming increasingly popular as reagents due to their small size, stability, high-aﬃnity, high-speciﬁcity, ease of manipulation and production.

How It Works

  • Produce a new type of insecticide for selective bio-insect control.

Solution

Single Domain Antibody
Platform and Knowledge Hub

IBI-Ag develops customized biological pest control solutions based on thorough testing, selection and extensive learning processes.

Methodology
