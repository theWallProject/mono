About us

Arad Measurement Technologies is the development center of the Arad Group, A world leader in the development of IOT smart water measurement systems that are marketed and sold through the Arad Group’s global subsidiaries in over 50 countries.

More About Us

About ARAD GROUP

Arad Group is a world leader in the field of water metering. Millions of Arad’s water meters are installed worldwide and the company manufactures over 500,000 units a year, which had made Arad into one of the leading companies in the global water measuring industry.

What we do

Beside a lot of fun, happy hours, good time and social activities, our multidisciplinary R&D center develop and design for the following solutions

Hardware for advanced water metering technologies

Embedded software’s based on low power consumption and multiprotocol connectivity interfaces

Advanced water metering technology involves precise performance and high reliability under extreme environmental standards

Customized electromagnetic solutions and advanced antennas design

System engineering and integration to customize maximize the performance from our products

Data management platform dedicated enterprise water metering systems

Complex algorithm for big data, science and machine learning techniques

Data science and advanced analytic algorithm development, to maximize the benefits from our solution

Cloud applications to support our End-To-End solutions

Connectivity infrastructure based on IoT platforms to support data transfer and device management tools
