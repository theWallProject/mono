Pharma Sept

Pharma Sept manufactures sterile surgical equipment and robotic covers, with about 30 years of experience.

Pharma Sept’s products are used in leading medical centers worldwide. The company produces and distributes its products with numerous variations offering customized solutions to meet customer needs.

Pharma Sept products include Microscope Covers, C-arm Covers, Ultrasound Probe Covers, various protective covers, Diathermy Pouches, and Video Camera Covers.

The company’s products adhere to strict international quality regulations (FDA, CE) and are manufactured in accordance with EN ISO 13485.
