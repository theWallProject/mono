VANGUARD

The Vanguard is a formidable offensive and defensive weapon with great damage and good hitpoints for 5 Mana. Its charge ability grants it more speed and allows it to deliver double damage to a target when it arrives, allowing it to annihilate opponents or accelerate slow units.

SPLASHY

A high-damage, medium-ranged splash dragon with 2 mana. It is decent against medium to large swarms and can deliver great damage behind a tank for its price, despite being frail and only targets ground.

IRON FIRE

For 4 Mana, you get a powerful punch packed into a middling health package. It's a quick single-targeting dragon that can wipe out most support dragons or destroy tanks and towers with just a few hits, but it can be easily countered by swarms.

SHARPEYE

It is a reliable support or defensive unit with enough damage to take out small tanks but enough speed to rapidly handle tiny swarms. It is a constant 4 Mana single-target shot targeting both air and ground with rather average stats.
