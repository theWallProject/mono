Micron3DP

Micron3DP a subsidiary of MicronDP Ltd specializes in the design manufacturing and assembly of high-end extruders, hot-ends, and accessories for the hobby and professional 3D printing market. Micron is committed to improving printing quality and supports future materials that require relatively high temperatures, thus all our extruders are integrated with patent-pending “all-metal” hot end.

The Micron3DP team is made of multidisciplinary, technology-minded members with extensive backgrounds in additive manufacturing processes.

Arek Banach

Arie is the founder and general manager. A one-stop-shop for mechanical design, manufacturing, and assembly, from concept through prototyping up to final production. Arek has over 20 years of mechanical engineering experience, specializing in products involving precision optics and motion design.

Erik Gaag

Erik is the founder of Studio and an Industrial Designer. Driven by curiosity, he designed and built the largest 3D ceramic printer in the world, and continues to invent new ways to 3D print various exotic materials. During the competition in London, Erik won the Design Prize.
