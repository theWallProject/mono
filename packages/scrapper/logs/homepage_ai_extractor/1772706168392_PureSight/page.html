      Cutting Edge Protection

      In an environment as dynamic as the internet, we need to keep updated with the latest trends - new social applications, communications combining text, images and video, network games - as well as those emerging trends encouraging kids to do dangerous challenges. Keeping on top of all these threats challenges us to push our R&D to be at the technological forefront. Our deep technology combines Machine Learning via our Active Chat Inspection (ACI) implemented in over 11 languages and Artificial Intelligence (AI) - data-oriented Active Content Recognition (ACR) for content filtering, which classified millions of web pages.
