About us

A legacy of craftsmanship.
A future of excellence.

GLD Diamonds was built on the decades of experience
and reputation of it’s founder, Haim Giladi. long before the company’s creation, Haim had already earned recognition
in the diamonds trade through his integrity, precision, and commitment to innovation.
When his three sons joined him, they expanded on that foundation together, carrying the family legacy forward and shaping GLD Diamonds into a global leader in
finely calibrated diamonds.
Today, the tradition continues – where craftsmanship consistency, and excellence define every detail.

What sets GLD apart is our full control over the value chain. We own and operate our manufacturing from start to finish, allowing for rapid turnaround absolute confidentiality, and a tailored experience for every client, from luxury brands to specialized manufacturers.

At GLD, precision is more than a metric, it's our promise!

Manufacturing

GLD Diamonds goes far beyond precision calibrated cuts. Our Chaing Mai atelier is a laboratory of creativity and cutting-edge innovation, where artisanal skill meets bold R&D ambitions.

In 2012, we established RGA, our state-of-the-art factory in Chiang Mai – Thailand, built on advanced diamond cutting technologies and lean manufacturing principles. Today, RGA stands as a global standard for modern, ethical production. Every diamond is precisely cut. calibrated, and inspected under uncompromising quality controls, ensuring seamless perfection from concept to final polish.

Cutting & Polishing Excellence

GLD has evolved into a trusted international diamond house, serving distinguished clients across Europe, the U.S., and Asia. With advanced manufacturing in Israel and Thailand, we deliver precision at every scale from flawless cuts to intricate layouts and bespoke assortments. Every process is guided by strict calibration full traceability, and unwavering commitment to excellence, ensuring each stone reflects the highest standard of craftsmanship.

Precision at Scale

GLD brings together over 200 specialists, expanding to 350 by 2026, to deliver precision at every level. We combine artisanal craftsmanship with the capacity to serve the world’s foremost watchmakers and haute joaillerie maisons worlwide. From bespoke client requests to large scale production, our work is defined by uncompromising accuracy, consistency, and trust.

The Art of Precision

Our team of master cutters and engineers in Chiang Mai redefines what’s possible in the world of fine craftsmanship. From bringing the boldest design visions to life to refining the smallest mechanical details, GLD transforms innovation into artistry. With an unwavering pursuit of excellence, we shape creations that set new standards and ensure your designs remain timeless and ahead of the curve.
