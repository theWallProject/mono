FAQ

How is Smartico priced? 

Our pricing is based on monthly active users, offered in tiers. The package includes both CRM automation and gamification, together with a dedicated Success Manager who supports you from day one - coordinating the integration, providing training, and offering daily support via Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Does the price include both CRM and Gamification?

Yes. With Smartico, you get both CRM automation and gamification as part of the same platform. No hidden fees, no separate modules - it’s all built-in and designed to work together seamlessly.

Is there a trial version or demo available?

We don’t offer a trial version, but we’re happy to provide a detailed demo to show you exactly how Smartico works. Instead of generic presentations we prefer to dive straight into our real back office, using real examples to give you a transparent, hands-on experience of the platform.

What is the onboarding process like with Smartico?

Integration is usually handled by us and takes about 3 weeks. We map all transactions into events in our system with minimal effort required from your side. The heavy lifting is our job.

If you prefer to handle the integration yourself, our APIs are flexible and easy to use. We’ll set up a group on Slack or or Microsoft Teams to provide direct support to your tech team and ensure a smooth process every step of the way.

Do I need my own e-mail or SMS gateway?

If you already have a gateway you like, perfect. We’re integrated with many options and can easily add new ones free of charge if integration is needed. If you don’t have one, we can provide a gateway for a competitive price. Either way, it’s your call.

Can Smartico support multiple brands in one system?

Yes! Smartico's casino CRM software allows you to operate and manage multiple brands within a single instance. You can run unified campaigns across all brands or create tailored setups and gamification for specific brands, all while keeping everything organized in one place - at no additional cost.

Is Smartico really that simple to use?

Absolutely. As a leading CRM software for casino, Smartico is designed to make life easier for iGaming operators. From setup to daily use, everything is straightforward. Plus, you’ll always have your Success Manager to guide you.

What is the onboarding process like with Smartico?

Our onboarding process is straightforward and hands-on. It begins with a live demo to showcase Smartico in action. Before signing any agreements, we conduct a technical feasibility call to confirm integration possibilities and address any blockers. Once the contract is signed, we focus on integrating essential data like users, deposits, and casino bets, followed by front-end setup. During this phase, our Success Manager guides your team, ensuring seamless progress. After integration, we provide training, grant system access, and continue integrating non-critical data like sports feeds. From start to finish, we’re with you every step of the way.
