Sustainable agriculture solutions through utilization of plant-based compounds for crop protection and productivity

Learn more about us

OUR
EXPERTISE IS

In the discovery and development of plant-based compounds for crop protection and productivity.
Our mission is to find breakthrough solutions of innovative compounds and bring them to the agriculture market.

OUR UNIQUE
POSITION

The company has developed a new technology platform for screening, development and understanding of plant metabolites for crop protection.

The Company is an initiative by few leaders in plant metabolites exploitation. The Company collaborates with Yeda, the Commercial Arm of the Weizmann Institute of Science (WIS) (www.weizmann.ac.il), the lab of Prof. Asaph Aharoni, head of Plant Metabolic engineering and a world leader in plant science.

THE MARKET NEED

Over $55 Billions are spent each year in crop protection
The conventional chemistry based compounds are facing drastic drop in innovation, increased regulatory pressure and prohibitive cost of development. As a result, new ag-chem compounds introduction dropped to merely 5 compounds/year in the last decade vs. 14 in the previous decade

Bio-based pesticides are recognized as one of the future solutions. They can have shorter TTM, lower development cost and healthier environmental profile. While the total market CAGR is 4%, the bio-based solutions are expected to have CAGR in the range of 15-20%.

Within bio-based solutions, those of plant metabolites origin are the most promising.
