    Total Human Field of View XR Enablement

    From 2026, VR/MR developers of applications requiring situational awareness for flight/driving simulators, remote vehicles/robots supervision or Location Based Entertainment (e.g. paintball game) will get access to the technology by Hypervision

        For complete human field of view (FoV) stimulation, Hypervision is offering VRDOM architecture supporting up to 270°x130°@90°stereo-overlap. This architecture is intended for enterprises as a low cost alternative to the "Dome Projection rooms" or "Six wall caves". Considering VR Community feedback that horizontal FoV of 140° to 180° could be good enough while preserving the huge vertical FoV has more importance, we introducing the PanoVR1.RDK, that was demonstrated on AWE-USA in June 2024 and is previewed in podcast of Artur's tech tales.

     

       The PanoVR1.RDK is based on the HO140.Rev2 visual engine that is compatible with 2.56" fast LCDs (2.16K BOE or 2.73K TCL) and provides up to 130° monocular FoV (horizontal & vertical). The PanoVR1.RDK is configured to supply 160°x120°@80°stereo-overlap. The architecture allows to configure the horizontal FoV from 140°x120°@100° of stereo-overlap to 180°x120°@60°stereo-overlap, depending on user preference. There is also possibility to shift fast LCD orientation by 45° Z-axis rotation to add 2x10°= 20° for each dimension, so max FoV configuration could be 180°x130°@80°stereo-overlap or 160°x130°@100°stereo-overlap.

    ​​

        To make the PanoVR1.RDK affordable we are working on mass production processes of HO140.Rev2 Visual Engines and  release of the PanoVR1.RDK is planned to H1-2026. It will be PC-tethered solution with Thunderbolt 4 connection and 3DOF with possibility to add off-the-shelf trackers to extend to 6DOF. For the MR functionality, the high-PPD wide FoV pass-through will be offered in H2-2026. As for VRDOM architecture an expansion with peripheral Visual Engines is planned as PanoVR1.X.RDK during H2-2026 to provide 240°x120°@90°stereo-overlap. Want to learn more - see granted patent US12411342B2.
