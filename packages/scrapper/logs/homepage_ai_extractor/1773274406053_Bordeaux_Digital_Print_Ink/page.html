About Bordeaux

Bordeaux Digital PrintInk is an industry leader in developing, manufacturing and distributing high quality premium digital inks and solutions for almost every inkjet technology and application available.

Products

From our revolutionary Latex ink to our Eco solvent inks and UV curable inks, our products deliver superb printing quality exceeding original ink and present outstanding features in terms of wide color gamut, durability, quality and low cost. View Bordeaux’s new catalog
