Welcome to AIDA

Association Internationale de Droit des Assurances

The International Insurance Law Association

Asociacion Internacional de Derecho de Seguros

Internationale Vereinigung fur Versicherungsrecht

Associazione Internazionale di Diritto delle Assicurazioni.

Since 1960 AIDA has been a non-profit making international association successfully promoting and developing collaboration between its members and related institutions worldwide to help increase the study, knowledge and understanding of international and national insurance laws and their application in the changing world of insurance.

Through its global membership of approximately 50 national associations, its two regional groupings, AIDA Europe and CILA, its affiliated ARIAS Societies and Young AIDA groupings and nearly 30 Working Parties and convening at events and activities throughout each year, AIDA engages and can draw upon the resources and experience of nearly 10,000 people concerned in a variety of ways with the development and application of insurance laws.

Young AIDA

After the wonderful World Congress held in Melbourne, organised by the Australian Insurance Law Association (AILA), Aida World enters this new phase, which will focus on organisation, content production, consolidation of the commitment of its members and directors, and openness to new generations.

All of this within the framework of the objectives set out in our statute: “...promoting and developing at an international level collaboration between its members and increasing the study and knowledge of international and national insurance law and of related matters and proposing measures for adoption by the insurance industry at a national or international level which may lead to the harmonisation of insurance law or of the means for resolution of insurance disputes”

It is necessary to adapt to new environments and forms of communication in order to achieve the transcendence to which we all aspire for the Association.

I have nothing but words of gratitude for all those who support us and especially for those who have taken on responsibilities in the organisation, contributing the most precious thing a person can have, which is their time.

A word of thanks to our predecessors who, by their example, encourage us to work for a better AIDA W.

Carlos Estebenet - AIDA World President

GET INVOLVED with AIDA

There are many ways to be involved with AIDA...

You may join an existing AIDA national association or, if eligible, any recognised association for younger members.

Alternatively, combine with others to help establish such groups.

You may participate in a Working Party or similar activity or simply register for an AIDA event, which is not confined to AIDA members.
