Standard EHR Inbox

Standard EHR Inbox work requires redundant clicking, navigation between multiple windows, and utilizing outdated flows for each task. Droxi's centralization of patient information reduces time spent searching and clicking between multiple windows, freeing up more time to practice medicine.

With Droxi™

Each task is displayed with the relevant information. You sit down, relax, and apply medical thinking. Then, with one click everything takes care of itself: the forms are filled out and a patient response is drafted based on the choices you make. Droxi does not make any medical decisions, it just streamlines the process based on your decisions.

Main Features

Prescription Refills

AI reveals the most relevant patient data for the specific prescription, eliminating the need to search for it manually. Additionally, everything that can be automated in the prescription process is indeed automated. Whenever an appointment is needed for a prescription the patient receives an SMS to schedule it without any need for phone calls.

Inbox Message Management

Droxi displays the patient message with all its most relevant data and all follow-up actions the provider can take. It is all available with a single click so there is no need to switch between screens. Follow on actions are fully streamlined.

Lab Results

There is no need to search the patient record manually to understand why a lab test was ordered. Droxi's AI presents results in context alongside the objective and other relevant parameters.
