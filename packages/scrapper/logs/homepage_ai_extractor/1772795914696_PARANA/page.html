A company like no other

Parana has set itself to become one of the best skill-based gaming companies by providing unique games and content, focusing on great experiences across all our games. We are developing games we are passionate about creating – Cash 8Ball Pool being the first on our platform as we have additional unannounced projects in development.

Our games

Skill-based cash-enabled games allow our players to enjoy a meaningful experience of competitiveness and fair play while using their abilities to earn real- life money in live matches and tournaments, playing against LIVE players.

Our Team

We are a team of dedicated and highly experienced game and platform developers passionate about bringing foundation-changing concepts to the skill-based growing market.
