    The Challenge

    Commodity markets are shaped by non-linear interactions between climate, supply chains, policy, geopolitics and macro forces. Traditional models struggle because:

    Critical data is scattered, unstructured, and delayed

    Regime shifts are driven by physical-world events, not price alone

    Linear signals fail under volatility and structural change

    As a result, traders are often late, overconfident, or mis-positioned during the most profitable periods.

    Viridian Solution

    Viridian is an agentic intelligence platform purpose-built for commodity hedge funds and CTAs. Instead of relying on static models, Viridian deploys dedicated AI agent swarms per commodity that replicate the work of hundreds of domain analysts.

    Our Unique Capabilities

    For each commodity, Viridian orchestrates specialized agents across all major price drivers:

    Supply Agents

    Production, yields, inventories

    Demand Agents

    Consumption patterns, substitution effects

    Climate Agents

    Weather anomalies, seasonal stress, extremes

    Policy Agents

    Regulation, trade rules, sanctions, subsidies

    Logistics Agents

    Freight, ports, bottlenecks, disruptions

    Macro Agents

    FX, rates, capital flows, correlations

    Each Swarm:

    • Generates hundreds of forward-looking scenarios
    • Traces each scenario to observable real-world signals
    • Continuously updates probabilities as new data arrives

    Multi-Scenario Intelligence

    Viridian explicitly models uncertainty, not single forecasts. For every commodity, the platform:

    • Simulates multiple plausible futures
    • Scores scenario likelihood and expected price impact
    • Flags early indicators that shift regime probabilities

    Earlier positioning • Better risk sizing • Reduced false confidence

    Machine Learning Layer

    On top of the agent intelligence, Viridian runs ML models that:

    • Predict price direction (up / down)
    • Estimate confidence and likelihood
    • Adapt dynamically as regimes evolve

    Crucially, models are conditioned on agent-identified fundamentals, not just historical prices - improving robustness under stress.
