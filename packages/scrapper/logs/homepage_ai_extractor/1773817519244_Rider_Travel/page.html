Shuttles / Shared Transfers

Are a shared ride option and are a cheap, fast and effective way of travelling to and from your accommodation. You pay for a seat in a vehicle that you share with other passengers travelling on the same route.

Private Sedans

The best alternative to a local taxi. This is a price driven category, where we have selected the best local suppliers for reliability on an affordable price.

Executive Cars

Our recommended service for superior reliability and peace of mind. Our certified local suppliers provide you with executive grade private cars and drivers that will make your ride completely worry - free.

Minivan

The best choice for traveling with a group, or lot’s of luggage. Our minivans also drive “long-haul” rides, making them a convenient alternative to trains and even flights for a group transfers.

Flat Rates

You know the price of your ride before you book, and pay online with either PayPal or your favorite credit card, in your currency before you ride. You don’t need to worry about surge pricing or foreign currency.

Headache Free Travel

Don’t worry about waiting for a taxi or having enough cash for the ride. With our advanced reservation transfers, your driver is waiting for you at your destination, not the other way around.

Online and Real-time

Our systems provide you with a transfer booking reservation that is confirmed both online and in real-time. We will email you a confirmation of your booking and you do not need to worry about reconfirming your reservation.

Safe & Secure

Our travel partners are properly licensed, with insured cars and drivers. We keep you safe every step of the journey.

About us
