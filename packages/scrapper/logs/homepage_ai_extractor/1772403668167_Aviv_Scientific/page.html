Reignite Your Vitality With Science

At Aviv Clinics, we don’t simply treat symptoms — we address the root causes of cognitive and physical decline. Our team of medical professionals can incorporate a combination of therapies, including the world’s most advanced hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) protocol, cognitive exercises, physical training, and nutritional coaching to craft a physician-directed program tailored to your medical needs and individual health goals.

Our approach stems from clinical studies that have shown the Aviv hyperbaric protocol (Hyperoxic-Hypoxic ParadoxTM) can improve symptoms of a variety of health conditions. This includes cognitive decline, long COVID, post-stroke, traumatic brain injury, and more. 

Aviv Clinics is the only UHMS-accredited medical facility in the nation to primarily treat these emerging conditions. The Undersea & Hyperbaric Medical Society accreditation is recognized by The Joint Commission and represents the gold standard in hyperbaric medical care.

The Aviv Medical Program

Aviv Clinics offers a personalized program of evidence-based therapies designed to address your specific health challenges. This scientifically-backed approach maximizes your performance and helps you perform at your best.

  • Assessments

    Guided by our physicians, we leverage a comprehensive health assessment to establish a solid foundation for your treatment plan.

  • Treatments

    A multi-week journey prescribed by our medical and clinical professionals. Your therapies may include our exclusive HBOT protocol, cognitive exercises, physical training, and personalized nutrition guidance.

  • Post-assessment

    At your request, we can quantify your physical and/or cognitive improvements after the program using detailed analytics.

  • Follow-up

    Your personalized plan can also include up to five years of monitoring follow-up appointments.
