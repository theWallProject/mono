On the Front Cover: The Red Tutu

On the Back Cover: Awaiting Transformation,
Oil on Linen, 29 x 44 inches, 2023.
Arturo Garcia © All rights reserved.

In this month’s 2025 Final Edition, we have the pleasure to feature fantastic artists and photographers from around the globe, exhibiting unique projects. Alex Sher, known globally for his outstanding Underwater figurative artistic Photography is exploring the relationship between vision and emotion.
Arturo Garcia is exhibiting colourful figurative and still-life artworks and shedding light on the process of The Alchemist of Self. Xavier Ponce captures the solitude in urban spaces that rarely pause for breath through artistic black-and-white street photography. The well-known Croatian painter Alfred Freddy Krupa exhibits Contemporary Ink Painting, created in the process of exploring a psychological and emotional seismograph. Internationally award-winning photographer Melanie Haberkorn shares her latest project, Alternative Film Stills. One representative frame leads the viewer to resolve the plot only in their own imagination. Jun Gueco Cruz presents his latest artworks from the fantastic Transformation series, which continue to explore biological processes at the cellular level. David Moješčík creates contemporary sculptures influenced by two main sources, separated by place and time: East Buddhist spiritualism and the romanticized simulacrum of former bourgeois fin-de-siècle cultures. His work frequently blends real life with fairytale worlds, as is common in contemporary literature and fantasy video games, and Sergio Camporeale shares his magnificent series, The Babylonia Show, a body of work based on appropriations, juxtapositions, combinations, and fusions of different styles and ideas.
Enjoy! Best wishes for a blessed 2026.
