Zero Carbon,
Sustainable Farming

Bringing the next generation of
biogas production and nutrient recovery solutions

SGTECH leverages advanced AI to revolutionize livestock waste-treatment with our patented Integrated Ecosystem Solution (IES).

By harnessing data from multiple plant sensors, laboratory analysis, DNA sequencing & functional gene expression, our AI-driven technology optimizes the anaerobic digestion process, ensuring maximum biogas production and efficient nitrogen reduction.

Our innovative nutrient recovery system, powered by AI, processes biogas digestate to manage and utilize nitrogen and phosphorus effectively, promoting sustainable reuse.
This results in three key outcomes compared to conventional solutions:

  • Produces as much as 30% more energy
  • Removes as much as 70% of nitrogen from digestate
  • Recovers as much as 50% of phosphorous from digestate
  • Reduces as much as 50% of greenhouse gases
