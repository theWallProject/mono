For more than 60 years, Moked Precision Aerospace Components has produced superior parts and subassemblies of unparalleled quality at competitive prices. The company takes great pride as a leading global manufacturer of high-precision, complex parts, and for its reputation in the aerospace industry for its ability to meet — and solve — engineering challenges, its excellent service and its consistent on-time delivery.

Moked’s goal is to maintain its reputation in the aerospace industry in three ways: through ongoing improvements in equipment and technology, with a commitment to high standards, and through its highly qualified and professional team.

Moked provides its customers with complete support. Starting from concept and design, continuing to prototyping and on to production, Moked is dedicated to its customers’ success.
