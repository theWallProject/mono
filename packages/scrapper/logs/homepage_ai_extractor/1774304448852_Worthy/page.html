FAQs

What type of jewelry can I sell with you?

Our business model is built and optimized to facilitate the sale of different kinds of diamond jewelry, such as diamond rings, bracelets, necklaces and earrings. We also help sell loose diamonds and branded luxury watches.
Visit our accepted pieces page for more information.

How much does your service cost?

Our fees are subject to a successful auction and are a percentage of the final sale price. So we share a common interest. We also only get paid when you do.

How long does the selling process take?

The process typically takes 10-14 business days from the time your jewelry arrives at Worthy headquarters to getting your payment issued. For more on getting paid, see here.

Can I back out of the process at any time?

Prior to auction there is no obligation of any kind. Once you agree to a reserve price and start your auction, you are committed to selling your valuable for that price (at least). If the highest bid falls below the reserve price, you can decide to decline the offer and have your jewelry sent back to you fully insured, free of charge.
