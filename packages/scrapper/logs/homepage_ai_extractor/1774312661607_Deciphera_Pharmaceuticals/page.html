A targeted approach with extraordinary potential

Kinases are enzymes that help regulate cellular function. When they do not work properly, these enzymes contribute to the growth and spread of many cancers. We design switch-control kinase inhibitors with the goal of potentially improving patient responses to treatment and, ultimately, patient outcomes.
