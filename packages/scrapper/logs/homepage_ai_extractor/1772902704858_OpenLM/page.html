Enter smart software license management

Do what matters the most

Real-time consumption views

Get immediate visibility into license availability by user and machine. This helps identify idle sessions and monitor details such as license type, tokens consumed, and borrowed status.

Granular application and process monitoring

Track the usage of applications, specific features, and DLLs to gain insights down to the application add-in level.

Dongle and offline tracking

Monitor physical USB dongle license keys connected to PCs to track their location and usage frequency. The system captures usage data even when the device is disconnected from the network, providing an accurate view for remote workers.

Historical usage and allocation auditing

Track logs of every license check-out and check-in. This allows analysis of historical usage patterns to determine who used what license, when, from where, and for how long.

Denial tracking and management

Tracks and analyzes failed license requests (denials). This provides instant visibility into the cause, user, and lost productivity duration.

Strategic and financial analytics

Get high-level insights for optimizing contracts. It also offers Cost and Chargeback capabilities to accurately align software expenses with specific projects, departments, or cost centers for transparent financial governance.

Automated license harvesting

Reclaim idle licenses automatically to make them available for other users. Thus, you can prevent wastage of expensive licenses and prioritize efficiency.

Subscription optimization (SaaS Management)

Automate reassignment of SaaS licenses (named-user subscriptions) based on actual usage.

License Access Control (LAC) and policy enforcement

Manage and restrict access to licenses within the organization. Customize license access policies for specific users and groups to ensure fair usage.
