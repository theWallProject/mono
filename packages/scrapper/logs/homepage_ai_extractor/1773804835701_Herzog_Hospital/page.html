Herzog Medical Center

Our Divisions

Celebrating our 130 anniversary, Herzog Medical Center, established in 1894 in Jerusalem is the oldest hospital in Israel. Today, it has grown to a 550 bed hospital that is Israel’s foremost center for geriatrics, physical rehabilitation, adult and children chronic respiratory, mental health, psychotrauma care and research. Herzog Medical Center with its staff, friends and supporters around the world, constitutes a family of individuals who are dedicated and passionate about providing the best possible medical care for all with dignity.

Forged with the same energy and pioneering spirit of the first settlers of the modern State of Israel and coupled with the staunch dedication to the Jewish ideals of championing the down-trodden and uplifting the weak, Herzog Medical Center has blazed a trail of compassionate care and world-class pioneering research to not just treat conditions but to enhance lives.

We invite you to partner with us and join our growing family. Together, we will move the needle for excellence in medical intervention and quality research on the world stage. Make your lasting impact on Israel and its residents today by partnering with Herzog Medical Center.

Click on the following to find out more about Herzog Medical Center's Impactful work:

Geriatrics

treats the effects of advancing age and mental instability with the best medical technologies available, combined with expertise and compassion.

 

 

Rehabilitation

helps patients regain a maximum level of functioning so that they may return home as quickly as possible. 

 

 

 

Chronic Respiratory Support

treats patients from infancy to old age representing a wide range of etiologies – ranging from acute events (such as anoxic brain damage) to slowly progressive diseases (such as ALS).

Mental Health

Offering both hospital and community-based programs, Herzog Medical Center focuses on improving the mental health and well-being of the individual.
