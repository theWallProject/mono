Raicol Factory

Raicol manufacture a variety of different crystals, including RTP, KTP, LBO, CLBO, BBO, PPKTP, PPSLT and PPLN. Our cutting-edge manufacturing facility is a one-stop-shop, consisting of all the equipment required for high quality production, including proprietary growth systems, cutting and polishing machines, X-ray measurement systems, clean rooms, optical assembly equipment, state-of-the-art testing stations, and a coating facility.
