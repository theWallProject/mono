About

BunnyBearXYZ is an early stage startup. We are building games and educational applications for kids and their parents. Our apps are ads-supported, but the ad-placement is picked carefully so that it will not alienate users, but rather complement their experience.

We believe that apps must be simple and intuitive, without any learning curve and have a self-explanatory user interface. If there is more to it, than users will learn as they go, and not by a coach-marks. And instead of one app ‘to rule them all’ it is better to do few small specific apps.

Flashy

Flashy is a flash cards slider app for toddlers. It will help you teach your kids numbers, letters, colors and animals. Learning is easy and fun with Flashy. It features child-friendly colors, images and voices. Also features natural animal sound recordings. It is fully responsive and will run properly on any screen size, either in portrait or landscape mode. To use the app choose one of four options: Animals, Colors, Numbers or Alphabet. Than simply swipe right and left. To go back use a semi-transparent back button at the top left corner. To quit the game press phone's back button twice, or just use phone's home button. Happy swiping!

Slay the Dragon

Slay the Dragon is a casual multiplayer online role-playing game. It is designed to be intuitive, so you won’t need to study the game before you can play. This does not mean that the game lacks complexity, rather that complex aspects are simplified and split into clear, obvious flows. It features cute and fun vector graphics and rich social interaction model. Public beta is scheduled for Q2 2016.
