Meet Us

Company profile:

HandyMann was founded in 2010. HandyMann supplies engineering services for transition from development to production of multidisciplinary products, with emphasize on medicinal products.

HandyMann provides wide range of engineering capabilities, like components engineering, documentation, creating product file (DMR) and production files, design, V&V, QA & QC, building engineering infrastructure, project management, NPI, and more.

There are 30 employees in Handyman. These employees bring experience, profession, and devotion. Our team blend with customer activity and enriches the customer ability to produce fast quality solution and success.

HandyMann activity is adjusted to customer needs and its methods. We also supply other services for development, engineering and operation on demand.

Our vision:

HandyMann believes in synergy working with its customers.
Our team is embedded in customer activity and becomes integral part of day to day activity, for thriving to customer goals. The vast experience of our team enables us to provide quality services and provide a fast response. The customer success in its mission is our mission.

Our added value is:” we were in your shoes, we sat at the same position as you, we did the same role, we faced the challenges, the missions and difficulties you face, we will know well to guide and help”.
