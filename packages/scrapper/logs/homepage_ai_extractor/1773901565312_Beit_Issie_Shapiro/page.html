Our Mission

Our mission is to create a more inclusive society by advancing opportunities and rights for people with disabilities.

For over four decades, Beit Issie Shapiro has served as Israel's pioneering leader and innovator in the field of disabilities; providing services and sharing models that improve the lives of children and adults with disabilities across the globe.

Thank you

Binaya, A CHILD'S LIFE IS CHANGED
Binaya has Cerebral Palsy and was told he would never be able to walk. At 9 years old, the impossible became possible as he started to walk independently for the first time. This incredible breakthrough is thanks to therapy at Beit Issie's gym where we loves to work with the adapted equipment and machines which helped strengthen his muscles.

Thank you

Alison Scott, A partner of Beit Issie
Our journey with Beit Issie Shapiro began with a visit to watch Issie Senses in practice... The Beit Issie team provided support in every way, including encouragement to ’step out’ and be the first school in Africa to do an installation and in-depth training. It is now an integral part of our school offering and a powerful tool to assist the children.” -Alison Scott, Principal, Bellavista School, South Africa
