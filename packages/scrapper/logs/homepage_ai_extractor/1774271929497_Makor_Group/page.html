We have developed a respected range of quantitative research products specifically designed to add value to our clients’ investment decisions, notably through a focus on quantitative systematic analysis that assists valuation judgements.

Our team offers a deep pool of knowledge, with dedicated coverings of strategy, risk arbitrage and special situations to deliver rigorous insight.

Expertise

Our clients include Pension Funds, Mutual Funds, Quant-driven Funds, Hedge Funds, Risk Arbitrage specialists, proprietary trading desks and Private Banks, located across more than 20 markets worldwide.

We pride ourselves on the diversity of our client relationships. Each one not only enhances our access to liquidity, but also deepens our insight into trading flows across borders and strategies.

Equities

We provide our clients with a single point of access for over 90 execution venues spread throughout all global markets in Europe, the Americas, Asia, Africa and the Middle East.

Derivatives

We offer our clients execution in global listed options (single stock, index, sector) and futures (single stock, index, dividend), with enhanced returns and prudent risk management.

Fixed Income

With a wide range of expert research and insights, we pride ourselves on our ability to identify relative and absolute-value opportunities in sovereign, credit and emerging markets, in addition to hybrids and swaps.

Foreign Exchange

Our trading desk is fully dedicated to end-user clients, offering best-in-class execution in G10/EM currencies with a strong emphasis on cost efficiency, no-conflict agency-based operation, anonymity and transparency.

Investment Banking

As a decentralised and digital finance leader, the Makor Investment Banking Division creates the most cutting-edge financial innovations for our clients, designed to add value based on unbiased, extensive deal-making experiences.

Structured Products

Drawing on a set of specialised, globally connected teams across multiple asset classes, we address our clients’ specific needs for capital protection, guaranteed yield, diversification and hedging strategies.

Our Principles

Pure Client Focus

Our priority is putting our clients’ interests first. In order to achieve best execution for their chosen trading strategy, we hold neither trading books nor positions of our own.

Best of Breed

We attract the best and brightest professionals from a range of disciplines, including engineering, finance and trading, and data analysis. Our focus on recruiting and nurturing new talent ensures Makor consistently remains an industry leader.

Complete Integrity

Makor’s rigorous Legal and Compliance Department assures the firm only adheres to the highest ethical standard. Continuous monitoring of the regulatory environment further ensures our full compliance, while positioning us to best assist our clients in meeting their regulatory obligations.

Efficient Collaboration

Makor’s dynamic structure is non-hierarchical and non-bureaucratic, allowing for minimum friction and maximum efficiency in the workflow across markets and disciplines. We emphasize communication across borders and ranks, which leads us to develop multidisciplinary expertise and bring forth more creative solutions.
