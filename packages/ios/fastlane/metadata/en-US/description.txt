The Wall is a Safari extension that helps you make informed ethical choices while browsing. With a database of 19,000+ companies, The Wall identifies websites associated with humanitarian violations and suggests alternatives.

FEATURES:

• Automatic Website Checking
Browse Safari normally while The Wall checks every website against our comprehensive database.

• Searchable Company Database
Explore 19,000+ companies directly in the app. Search by name or domain to learn about flagged companies before you visit.

• Suggested Alternatives
When a flagged website is detected, see recommendations for ethical alternatives.

• Extension Status Monitoring
Check if your Safari extension is working correctly with real-time status updates.

• Privacy-First Design
All checking happens locally on your device. No tracking, no data collection, completely private.

• Free & Open Source
Transparent, auditable, and always free. View our source code on GitHub.

HOW IT WORKS:

1. Enable the Safari extension in iOS Settings
2. Browse the web as you normally would
3. Get notified when you visit a flagged website
4. See detailed information and alternatives

ABOUT THE DATABASE:

Our database includes companies flagged for:
- Humanitarian violations
- Operations in occupied territories
- UN violations
- BDS (Boycott, Divestment, Sanctions) list

Companies are continuously researched and verified by our community.

BUILT FOR PALESTINE:

The Wall was created to support the Palestinian cause by making it easy to participate in ethical boycotts. Every browsing decision can make a difference.

APP PERMISSIONS:

• Safari Extension: Required to check websites as you browse
• All Websites: Needed to identify flagged domains across any site you visit

Your privacy is paramount. The Wall never collects, stores, or transmits your browsing data.
